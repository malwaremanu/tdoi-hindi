# About the Author

## About the Author

Jawaharlal Nehru (1889–1964) was an Indian nationalist leader and politician who played a significant role in the struggle for India’s independence from British colonial rule. He went on to serve as the first Prime Minister of India for seventeen years. An accomplished writer, his other works include Letters from a Father to His Daughter (1929), Glimpses of World History (1934) and An Autobiography (1936).
