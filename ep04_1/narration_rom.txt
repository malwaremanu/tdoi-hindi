Sochiye. Isi nadi ke kinaare Mohenjo-daro ki deewarein uthi thi. Isi ko hal wale wo kisaan paar kar rahe the. Aur yahi nadi ek din poore desh ka naam ban jaati hai. Ek aisa naam, jo desh ne khud nahi rakha.

Phir ek Chini yaatri aate hain. I-tsing — ek Chini tirthyatri, jo is Tantrik pothi se kuch pehle Bharat aaye.

I-tsing apne safar ki kitaab mein likhte hain ki "uttar ke kabile", yaani Madhya Asia ke log, Bharat ko "Hindu" kehte hain. Unki zubaan mein, "Hsin-tu".

Par wo saath mein ye bhi jodte hain. Ye koi aam naam nahi hai. Is desh ke liye sabse theek naam hai Aryadesha. Yaani shreshth logon ka desh.

To "Hindu" naam aaya kahan se? Bahar walon ki zubaan se. Nadi ke us paar khade logon ki nazar se.

Aur ise ek dharm ke naam ki tarah bolna? Wo bahut baad ki baat hai. Bahut, bahut baad ki.

Nehru ek behtar naam sujhaate hain: Hindi. Iran se Misr tak, poora pashchim Asia Bharat ko "Hind" kehta aaya. Iran, Turkey, Iraq, Afghanistan — in sab jagah aaj bhi Hind hi chalta hai. Aur us naam ka dharm se koi rishta nahi.

Aaj sochiye. Jab hum kisi ko "Hindi" kehte hain, to hum ek zubaan ka naam le rahe hain. Par Nehru ke zamane mein ye shabd zubaan se pehle ek desh ka naam tha — bina kisi dharm ke.

Nehru isliye kehte hain ki shabd chahe jo chuniye. Indian kahiye, Hindi kahiye, Hindustani kahiye. Asal baat shabd nahi. Asal baat wo cheez hai, jise ye shabd pakadne ki koshish karte hain.

To us cheez ka apna, purana naam kya tha?

Ek chhappar sochiye. Beech mein ek khamba. Khamba hata dijiye, to chhappar gir jaata hai.

Purane Bharat mein us cheez ka naam tha dharm. Aur Nehru batate hain ki ye shabd ek aise mool shabd se bana hai, jiska matlab hai thaam kar rakhna. Jod kar rakhna. Us khambe ki tarah.

Kisi cheez ka dharm uski sabse andar ki banawat hai. Uske andar ka niyam, jisse wo wahi hai jo hai. Isliye dharm sirf puja-paath nahi. Isme naitikta hai, sahi aur galat ki samajh, aur insaan ke saare farz aur zimmedaariyan.

Aur "Hindu dharm" khud dhundhla hai. Nehru likhte hain ki uski koi seema nikaali nahi ja sakti. Usme sabse oonchi soch bhi hai, aur sabse neechi bhi. Wo lachakdar hai, bahu-mukhi hai, har aadmi ke liye alag matlab rakhta hai. Bhav ek hi hai: jeene do.

Sab raaston ke liye ek purana, bada naam tha: Arya dharm. Isme wo saare panth aate the jo is desh mein paida hue. Jo Ved maante the, wo bhi. Jo nahi maante the, wo bhi. Bauddh bhi ise apna kehte the, aur Jain bhi. Buddha to apne raaste ko hamesha "Arya maarg" kehte the.

Ek chhota ghera bhi tha: Vaidik dharm. Isme wo log aate the jo Vedon ka adhikaar maante the.

Aur ek teesra naam: Sanatan dharm, yaani purana dharm. Ye kisi bhi purane Bharatiya panth ke liye bola ja sakta tha. Bauddh aur Jain ke liye bhi. Par Nehru likhte hain ki unke zamane tak kuch kattar log is naam ko lagbhag sirf apna maan baithe the.

Ab Nehru ka tark. Bauddh aur Jain dharm na Hindu dharm hain, na Vaidik dharm. Phir bhi wo isi desh mein paida hue. Ek Bauddh ya Jain sau feesadi Bharatiya soch ki paidaish hai. Par dharm se wo Hindu nahi.

Nateeja? Bharat ki sanskriti ko "Hindu sanskriti" kehna bhatkaane wala hai.

Jab tak purana dharm ek jeene ka dhang tha, wo is sanskriti ke barabar tha. Jab wo reeti-rivaazon mein kas gaya, to wo kuch zyada ban gaya. Aur kuch kam bhi.

Aage chalkar Islam aaya, aur is sanskriti par uska gehra asar pada. Phir bhi ye Bharatiya hi rahi. Aur Pashchim ki karkhanon wali sabhyata is par sau tarah se asar daal rahi thi. Uska nateeja kya hoga, Nehru nahi jaante.

Ek Isai ya Musalmaan bhi is jeevan-dhang ko apna sakta tha. Aksar apnaya bhi. Wo apne dharm mein pakka raha, par Bharatiya ban gaya. Dharm badle bina.

Nehru ko is poore itihaas mein jodne ki ek andar ki khinchaav dikhti hai. Har baar koi bahar se aaya, to ek chunauti aayi. Har baar jawab ek naya milan tha, aur har milan ke baad ek nayi bahaar. Neev wahi rahi.

Ek angrez lekhak ne isi ko duniya ke liye Bharat ka khaas tohfa kaha: anek se ek bana dena.

Teesra jawab ab dikhne laga hai. Ek aadat. Sab ko apne andar sama lene ki.

Par isi ghar ke andar, chupchaap, kuch aur bhi ban raha tha.

Yaad hai wo kisaan, jo bailon ki jodi lekar Sindhu paar aaya tha? Jo hal chalata tha, puja bhi karta tha, ladta bhi tha, vyapaar bhi karta tha?

Ab uske saath kya hota hai?

Arya aaye, aur ek nayi samasya bhi laaye — nasal ki, aur raaj ki bhi. Doosri taraf Dravid the, jinke peechhe ek lambi sabhyata thi. Teesri taraf jangal-pahaad ke wo kabile, jo peeche reh gaye the.

Nehru likhte hain: dono taraf ke beech ek chaudi khaai thi, aur Arya khud ko bahut oopar maante the. Yahin se jaati paida hui.

Us zamane mein jeetne wale haarne walon ko mita dete the, ya ghulam banate the. Greece ki sabhyata ghulamon par khadi thi; Bharat mein aisa ghulam-khand nahi tha.

Bharat ne teesra raasta chuna. Sabko neeche rakh lo — par andar rakh lo.

Nehru kehte hain: jaati na poori taur par Aryon ki thi, na Dravidon ki. Wo alag-alag kaumon ko ek samaaj mein baithane ki koshish thi, jo aage chalkar ek bojh ban gayi — par hum use baad ke paimana se nahi naap sakte.

Shuruaat ek sakht lakeer se hoti hai: ek taraf Arya, doosri taraf wo, jo Arya nahi the.

Ye deewar doosron ko alag rakhne ke liye uthi thi. Par ek baar uth jaaye, to andar bhi kaatne lagti hai.

Kaam bantne lage: koi sirf puja, koi sirf ladai, koi sirf vyapaar. Jaise-jaise kaam khaas hote gaye, varg jaatiyon mein badalte gaye: Vaishya — kisaan, karigar, vyapaari. Kshatriya — raaj karne aur ladne wale. Brahmin — pujari aur sochne wale. Shudra — mazdoor aur be-hunar log.

Jo deewar Aryon aur baaki logon ke beech uthi thi, wo ab Aryon ke apne beech uth gayi.

Shabd bhi badal gaya: "Arya" ka nasl wala matlab mit gaya — ab matlab tha bhala, shreshth; aur "anarya" ka matlab hua neech.

Yunani darshanik Plato ne apni ek kitaab mein aadarsh samaaj ka wahi bantwaara socha tha; madhyakaal ke Catholic Europe mein bhi wahi.

Aryon ne samaaj hi nahi, insaan ki zindagi bhi chaar hisson mein baanti: vidyarthi — seekhna, sanyam; grihasth — ghar, kaam, duniya; phir ek buzurg, bina apne fayde ke samaaj ka kaam karne wala; aakhir mein sanyasi — duniya se lagbhag alag.

Isme ek samajhdaari thi: zindagi ko poora jeene aur use chhod dene, dono ko jagah mili.

Is desh mein, jaise China mein, vidya ka darja sabse oopar rahi. Raja aur yodha bhi vidwan ke aage jhukte the; daulat unse neeche.

Brahmin logon ne ek suvidha-prapt varg ke saare aib dikhaye — bahut se aise, jinke paas na vidya thi, na sadgun. Phir bhi izzat kam nahi hui: wo izzat pad ki nahi, gun ki thi.

Aaj bhi is paise ke zamane mein Gandhi, jo Brahman nahi hain, desh ke sabse bade neta hain. Isse Nehru ek kasauti nikaalte hain: dekhiye, ek desh kaisa neta chunta hai.

Purane Bharat ka ek bada vichaar tha: dharm, yaani farz — apne prati, aur doosron ke prati. Ye dharm Rita ka hissa tha, us naitik niyam ka jisse poora brahmaand chalta hai. Farz theek se karo, nateeja apne aap aayega.

Adhikaar ki baat kam hoti thi, farz ki zyada. Aaj ka zamana ulta hai: aaj har koi adhikaar maangta hai — insaan bhi, samooh bhi, desh bhi.

To is desh ki pehchaan kahan se shuru hui?

Shehar se nahi. Shehar ret mein so gaya. Naam se bhi nahi. Naam doosron ne diya, aur der se diya.

Ek aadat se. Sab ko apne andar sama lene ki aadat. Aur usi ke saath ek doosri aadat bhi: andar deewarein uthane ki.

Ret aati rahi. Aur deewar oonchi hoti rahi.

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, "Hindu" shabd. Nehru ke liye ye pehle nadi ke us paar ke logon ka naam tha. Phir ek sanskriti ka. Aur bahut baad mein ek dharm ka. Aaj ye shabd kabhi sabse sankare arth mein bola jaata hai, kabhi sabse chaude mein. Dono baar ek hi sawal poochhne laayak hai: ye shabd jodne ke liye bola ja raha hai, ya baantne ke liye?

Doosri, Gandhi wali kasauti. Nehru ne poocha tha ki ek desh kis tarah ke neta ke peeche chalta hai. 1944 mein jawab tha: ek aadmi, jiske paas na pad tha, na paisa, na fauj. Aaj ye sawal humare saamne hai. Hum kisi neta ke peeche kyun chalte hain? Uski taaqat ke liye? Uske paise ke liye? Ya uske kirdaar ke liye? Jawab humare baare mein utna hi batata hai, jitna neta ke baare mein.

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapke aas-paas aaj kaun si deewar sabse oonchi hai? Aur wo aapko bacha rahi hai, ya qaid kar rahi hai?

Mohenjo-daro ki likhaai aaj tak koi padh nahi paaya. Wo shehar chup hai.

Par jo log hal lekar aaye the, unke paas ek cheez thi jo Mohenjo-daro ke paas nahi bachi. Aise shabd, jo aaj bhi padhe ja sakte hain. Geet. Duniya ki sabse purani kitaabon mein se ek.

Aur us kitaab mein, pehli baar, koi poochta hai: ye sab aaya kahan se?

Agli baar, wahi pehle padhe ja sakne wale shabd.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
