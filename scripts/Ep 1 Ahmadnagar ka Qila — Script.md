## Cold open

Ek aadmi tapti dhoop mein zameen khod raha hai. Mitti kharab hai: pathreeli, malbe se bhari. Wo phoolon ki kyari banana chahta hai.

Phawda kisi sakht cheez se takrata hai. Mitti hatane par ek purani deewar ka hissa nikalta hai. Us par pathar mein tarasha hua ek kamal hai.

Ye jagah Ahmadnagar ka qila hai. Saal 1944. Aur ye maali ek qaidi hai, jiska naam Jawaharlal Nehru hai.

Is qile ne bahut ladaiyan aur darbari saazishein dekhi hain. Ek naam aaj bhi yaad kiya jaata hai: Chand Bibi, jisne talwar haath mein lekar Akbar ki shahi fauj se is qile ko bachaya. Uski jaan uske apne hi ek aadmi ne li.

Nehru aur gehra khodna chahte hain. Jail ke afsar ijazat nahi dete, aur auzaar bhi nahi hain. To wo phawda rakh dete hain aur kalam utha lete hain. Zameen ki khudai band, itihaas ki khudai shuru.

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 1: Ahmadnagar ka qila.

## Part 1: Chaand wala calendar

Tareekh hai 13 April 1944. Nehru ko is qile mein bees mahine se zyada ho chuke hain. Ye unki nauvi jail yatra hai.

Jis shaam unhe yahan laya gaya, aasmaan mein naya chaand tha. Tab se har naya chaand unhe batata hai ki ek mahina aur nikal gaya. Wo chaand ko jail ka purana saathi kehte hain. Wo ghat-ta hai, badhta hai, aur yaad dilata hai ki andhere ke baad roshni aati hai. Kisaan ke liye to ye hamesha se sabse aasaan calendar raha hai.

Shuru ke teen hafte bahar ki duniya se rishta poori tarah kata raha. Na akhbaar, na chitthi, na radio, na mulaqat. Sarkar ke hisaab se ye bhi raaz tha ki ye log kahan band hain. Nehru is par halka tanz karte hain: aisa raaz jo poore desh ko pata tha.

Phir akhbaar milne lage, wo bhi censor hokar. Kuch hafton baad kareebi rishtedaron ki chitthiyan, sirf ghar-parivar ki baaton tak. Mulaqat bees mahine mein ek bhi nahi.

Akhbaaron se jo tasveer bani, wo darawni thi. Dasiyon hazaar log bina mukadme ke jail ya camp mein. Hazaaron goli se maare gaye. Dasiyon hazaar students school-college se nikale gaye. Poore mulk mein martial law jaisa mahaul.

Nehru apni haalat ko behtar maante hain. Baaki qaidiyon ko na chitthi milti thi, na akhbaar, kitaabein bhi mushkil se. Kharab khane se kai beemar pade. Kuch apne log theek dekhbhaal na milne se chal base.

Ek tulna unhe chubhti hai. Bharat mein hazaaron yudh-bandi bhi rakhe gaye the, zyadatar Italy ke. Unke liye Geneva Convention ke niyam the. Bharat ke apne qaidiyon ke liye koi qanoon nahi tha. Bas wo ordinance, jo angrez hukumat jab chahe nikal de.

## Part 2: Akaal

Phir akaal aaya. Malabar, Bijapur, Orissa, aur sabse zyada Bengal, jo upjaau aur ameer sooba maana jaata tha. Roz hazaaron mard, auratein aur bachche bhookh se mar rahe the. Calcutta ki haveliyon ke saamne, gaon ki jhopdiyon mein, sadkon aur kheton mein laashein thi.

Nehru yahan ek fark karte hain. Jung mein bhi log mar rahe the. Par wo maut aksar tez hoti hai, aur kisi maqsad ke liye hoti hai. Akaal ki maut mein na maqsad tha, na majboori. Ye insaan ki banayi hui aafat thi, laparwahi aur nikammepan ka nateeja. Aisi maut jo dheere-dheere aati hai, jisme aadmi zinda rehte hue hi marne lagta hai.

Aur hukumat ka rawaiya? Is par baat karna hi bura maana gaya. Kaha gaya ki log baat ko badha-chadha rahe hain. Jhoothi reportein nikli. Nehru ki baat ka saar ye hai ki laashon ko andekha karna aasaan nahi hota, wo raaste mein aa jaati hain.

Bahane bhi sunne layak the. Pehle kaha gaya ki jung ki kamai se kisaan itne khush-haal hain ki unke paas zaroorat se zyada khana hai. Phir kaha gaya ki ye sooba sarkar ka maamla hai, aur Delhi ya London constitution ke khilaaf dakhal nahi de sakte.

Yahan Nehru ka gussa saaf dikhta hai. Wahi constitution roz Viceroy ke ordinance se toda ja raha tha. Asal mein poori taaqat ek aadmi ke haath mein thi, jo Bharat mein kisi ko jawabdeh nahi tha. Afsar aur police Governor ki sunte the, mantriyon ki nahi. Mantri tab tak the, jab tak upar wale chahein.

Aakhir kuch rahat pahunchi. Tab tak das lakh log mar chuke the, ya bees, ya tees. Sahi ginti kisi ko nahi pata. Jo bachche bach gaye, unme se laakhon tan aur man se toot chuke the. Aur akaal ka dar abhi gaya nahi tha.

Usi waqt President Roosevelt chaar azadiyon ki baat kar rahe the, jinme ek thi abhaav se azadi. Nehru poochte hain ki ameer England aur usse bhi ameer America ne Bharat ki bhookh par kitna dhyan diya. Jawab ye mila ki paise ki zaroorat nahi hai, aur jahaaz jung mein lage hain.

Par wo ehsaan bhi ginate hain. England, America aur doosri jagahon ke aam logon ne madad bheji. Aur do sarkaaron ne, jo khud gareeb thi: China aur Ireland. Dono ne akaal jhela tha, isliye dard samajhti thi. Nehru kehte hain ki Bharat ki yaaddasht lambi hai, aur ye dosti wo nahi bhoolega.

## Part 3: Democracy ki jung, aur democrat jail mein

Ab ek ajeeb sthiti dekhiye. Duniya mein fascism aur nazism ke khilaaf jung chal rahi hai. China mein lagbhag saat saal se. Europe aur Africa mein saadhe chaar saal se. Poori duniya ki jung ke roop mein do saal chaar mahine se. In saalon mein se kareeb teen saal Nehru ne jail mein kaate hain.

Aur ye wahi aadmi hai jo shuru se fascism ka virodhi tha. China par Japan ka hamla, Abyssinia par Italy ka kabza, Czechoslovakia ke saath dhokha, Spain ke republic ka girna. Nehru likhte hain ki Spain ki haar unke liye niji dukh thi.

Virodh sirf hamlon ka nahi tha. Asli dikkat un usoolon se thi jin par ye hukumatein khadi thi. Aur Bharat ko ye usool kisi kitaab se samajhne ki zaroorat nahi thi. Ghulam desh ne wahi tareeqe, thode saaf-suthre libaas mein, khud par jhele the.

Do kisse hain. March 1936 mein Mussolini ne milne ka zordar nyota bheja. Nehru ne mana kar diya. Us daur mein Britain ke kai bade neta Mussolini ki tareef kiya karte the.

Do saal baad, Munich se pehle ki garmiyon mein, Nazi sarkar ne Germany bulaya. Kaha ki humein pata hai aap humare khilaaf hain, phir bhi khud aakar dekhiye. Mehmaan bankar ya niji taur par, apne naam se ya gumnaam, jahan chahein wahan. Nehru ne shukriya kehkar phir mana kiya. Germany ki jagah wo Czechoslovakia gaye.

Munich se pehle wo Britain ke kuch mantriyon aur netaon se bhi mile aur apni baat rakhi. Baat pasand nahi ki gayi. Jawab mila ki aur bhi kai cheezein dekhni padti hain. Prague, Sudetenland, London, Paris aur Geneva mein jo unhone dekha, usse wo hairaan aur khinn hue. Unke hisaab se "appeasement" chhota shabd tha. Wahan Hitler ka dar hi nahi, dabi hui tareef bhi thi.

Aur ab kismat ka pher. Jo log Hitler aur Mussolini ke aage jhukte the, wo azadi aur democracy ka jhanda uthaye hain. Aur Nehru jaise log jail mein hain.

Bharat mein bhi aise log the, jo hamesha hukumat ke aas-paas mandrate hain aur wahi bolte hain jo upar walon ko achha lage. Kal tak wo Hitler aur Mussolini ki misaal dete the aur Soviet Union ko koste the. Aaj wahi sabse zor se fascism ke khilaaf hain. Nehru ka andaaza hai ki jung ka nateeja ulta hota, to ye log jeetne wale ko bhi haar pehnate.

Nehru barson se is jung ke aane ki baat kar rahe the. Wo chahte the ki Bharat isme poori taaqat se hissa le. Shart ek thi: azaad aur barabar desh ki tarah. Yahi Congress ka rukh tha, jo lagataar fascism aur samrajyawad, dono ke khilaaf rahi. Spain, Czechoslovakia aur China ke saath khadi rahi.

Aur wahi Congress lagbhag do saal se gair-qanooni hai. Uske chune hue vidhayak, speaker, purane mantri, mayor, sab jail mein hain.

Nehru is hisse ko ek kadwe vyang par chhodte hain: udhar democracy aur chaar azadiyon ke liye jung jaari hai.

## Part 4: Jail mein waqt

Yahan se chapter ka mizaaj badalta hai. Rajneeti peeche jaati hai, aur Nehru apne andar dekhne lagte hain.

Jail mein waqt ka swabhav badal jaata hai. "Aaj" jaise hota hi nahi. Bahar ki khabrein bhi sapne jaisi lagti hain. Qaidi ya to yaadon mein jeeta hai, ya kalpana mein.

Ateet mein ek khinchaav hai. Wo kisi tasveer ya murti ki tarah shaant aur atal hai. Pareshan man wahan panaah dhoondhta hai. Par Nehru chetavni dete hain ki ye zindagi nahi hai. Ateet ko aaj ke sawalon se na joda jaye, to aadmi usi ka qaidi ban jaata hai. Umeed aur josh dheere-dheere ris jaate hain. Aur jail mein, jahan kuch karne ki ijazat nahi, ye phislan aur aasaan hai.

Phir bhi ateet se bhaaga nahi ja sakta. Hum usi se bane hain. Nehru ka nuskha ye hai: ateet ko apne andar zinda cheez ki tarah samjho, use aaj se jodo, kal tak le jao, aur jahan wo jud na sake, wahan usse naata tod do.

Yahan wo ek purana sawal uthate hain. Kya insaan sach mein azaad hai, ya sab kuch pehle se tay hai? Unka imaandar jawab hai: pata nahi. Humara har kaam pichhle saare anubhavon se nikalta hai, apne bhi aur apni qaum ke bhi. Bachpan, parvarish, mahaul, dabi hui ichhayein, sab milkar us ek pal ko taiyar karte hain.

Wo Schopenhauer ko yaad karte hain, jinka kehna tha ki aadmi jo chahe wo kar sakta hai, par ye nahi chun sakta ki wo chahega kya. Aurobindo ka ek khayal bhi aata hai, ki vartamaan ek bilkul kora pal hai, ateet aur bhavishya ke beech ki dhaar. Nehru poochte hain: kya wo pal sach mein kora hai, ya ateet ka rang us par pehle se chadha hota hai?

Par "sab tay hai" maan lena unhe manzoor nahi. Unke hisaab se ye soch aadmi ko haath par haath rakhkar bitha deti hai. Phir wo khud hi maan lete hain ki ho sakta hai unki ye baghawat bhi pehle se tay ho.

Aise sawal wo roz nahi sochte. Ye jail ki lambi khamoshi mein aa jaate hain, ya kabhi kisi tez kaam ke beech. Nehru khud ko kaam ka aadmi maante hain. Unke liye sabse bhara-poora pal wo hai jab soch aur kaam ek sur mein hon. Aise pal bahut kam aate hain.

Jawani mein wo josh mein doobe rehte the. Ab josh thanda hai, soch ka bojh zyada hai, aur jahan yakeen tha wahan shak aa jaata hai. Shayad umar hai, shayad zamane ka mizaaj.

Phir bhi khatre ki pukaar unhe ab bhi sunayi deti hai. Maut se unhe lagaav nahi, aur shayad dar bhi nahi. Zindagi se pyaar hai, isliye wo uske kinaare tak jaakar jhaankna chahte hain, uske ghulam bane bina. Kehte hain, shayad mujhe pilot hona chahiye tha. Yahan wo Yeats ki ek kavita yaad karte hain, ek pilot ke baare mein jo zindagi aur maut ko ek taraazu mein tolta hai.

Isi mizaaj ne unhe itihaas tak pahunchaya. Wo maante hain ki itihaas mein unki dilchaspi der se jagi. Tareekhein aur tathya ratne mein unhe kuch nahi mila. Parlok ke sawalon mein aur bhi kam. Unhe vigyaan aur aaj ki samasyaein kheenchti thi.

Unka raasta ulta tha. Kaam ne unhe soch ki taraf bheja. Aaj ko samajhne ki koshish unhe ateet mein le gayi. Aur kyunki unki apni zindagi bade aitihasik modon se guzri thi, itihaas unke liye kitaab nahi, zinda cheez ban gaya. Kabhi lagta ki wo ateet ke hain, kabhi lagta ki poora ateet unka hai. Aur kabhi kisi tez ghatna ke beech achanak lagta ki wo use door se, guzri hui cheez ki tarah dekh rahe hain.

Isi khoj se baarah saal pehle "Glimpses of World History" nikli, beti ke naam chitthiyon ke roop mein. Beti tab kishori thi, isliye bhasha saral rakhi. Jail mein fursat thi, koi jaldi nahi thi, to wo alag-alag yugon mein ghoomte rahe aur purane logon ko apna saathi banate rahe. Aatmakatha bhi isi tarah ki khoj thi, bas daayra chhota aur niji tha.

In baarah saalon mein wo badle hain. Zyada chintansheel, zyada shaant. Dukh ab pehle jaisa hilata nahi, haalaanki dukh pehle se bade hain. Wo khud se poochte hain: ye sabr hai, ya man sakht ho gaya hai? Umar hai, ya jail ne zindagi ko dheere-dheere nichod liya hai? Baar-baar ke jhatkon se ehsaas kund ho jaate hain, aur lagne lagta hai ki duniya mein itna dukh hai ki thoda kam ya zyada se fark nahi padta.

Ek cheez wo pakde rehte hain, jo koi chheen nahi sakta: himmat aur garima se kaam karna, aur un usoolon par tike rehna jinhone zindagi ko matlab diya. Saath mein chutki lete hain ki ye rajnetaon ka tareeqa nahi hai.

Kisi ne unse kaha tha ki maut har insaan ka janmasiddh haq hai. Nehru ko ye jumla ajeeb bhi laga aur dilchasp bhi. Unke liye iska matlab bas itna hai ki insaan poori tarah bebas kabhi nahi hota.

## Part 5: Nehru kis cheez mein yakeen karte the

Chhe-saat saal pehle ek American publisher ne Nehru se ek nibandh maanga tha: aapki zindagi ka falsafa kya hai? Khayal achha laga, par jitna socha utni jhijhak badhi, aur nibandh kabhi likha nahi gaya. Chapter ka sabse lamba hissa usi adhoore nibandh jaisa hai.

**Pehle, uljhan.** Kuch saal pehle unki soch saaf thi. Phir Bharat, China aur Europe mein jo hua, usne sab dhundhla kar diya. Kaam wo ab bhi karte the, par us teer ki tarah nahi jo seedha nishane par jaata hai. Rajneeti se man uchatne laga. Aadarsh wahi the, chamak kam ho gayi thi. Burai ka jeetna to tha hi. Usse bura ye tha ki jo cheezein sahi lagti thi, wo bhi bigadkar bhaddi ho jaati thi.

Sawal uthe. Kya insaan ki fitrat itni kharab hai ki use sudharne mein sadiyan lagengi? Kya galat raaste se sahi manzil mil sakti hai? Aur phans ye hai: kuch na karna burai ke aage haar maanna hai, aur kuch karna aksar usi burai se samjhauta.

**Dharm.** Jawani mein unka nazariya vaigyanik aur aashawadi tha. Aaraam ki zindagi aur apne par bharose ne ise aur pakka kiya. Dharm jaisa unhone amal mein dekha, chahe Hindu, Islam, Bauddh ya Isai, unhe kheench nahi paya. Usme andhvishwas, kattar maanyata aur chamatkar par bharosa dikha.

Saath hi wo maante hain ki dharm insaan ki kisi gehri zaroorat ko poora karta hai. Usne behtareen insaan bhi diye hain aur tang-dil zaalim bhi. Usne zindagi ko mulya diye. Unme kuch aaj bekaar ya nuksaandeh hain, aur kuch ab bhi naitikta ki buniyaad.

Wo ye bhi maante hain ki vigyaan ke naqshe ke bahar ek bada anjaana ilaqa hai. Zindagi sirf utni nahi jitni dikhti aur sunayi deti hai. Vigyaan zindagi ka maqsad nahi batata. Par uska daayra badh raha hai, aur ho sakta hai kal wo dharmik anubhav ko bhi apne tareeqe se parkhe.

**Rahasyavad aur darshan.** Rahasyavad se unhe chidh hoti hai. Unhe wo dheela aur dhundhla lagta hai, jisme dimaag ko anushasan nahi, chhutti di jaati hai. Haalaanki bade rahasyavadi santon ko wo bewakoof kehkar taal nahi dete.

Darshan unhe zyada jachta hai, kyunki usme kadi soch lagti hai. Par uski buniyaad kuch maani hui baaton par hoti hai, jo sach ho bhi sakti hain aur nahi bhi. Purane log andar ki zindagi ko zyada ahmiyat dete the. Aaj ka aadmi bahar ki cheezon mein uljha hai, phir bhi sankat mein wo bhi darshan ki taraf mudta hai. Nehru khud wahan zyada der tikte nahi, aur bahar aakar raahat mehsoos karte hain.

**Aatma aur punarjanm.** Unki dilchaspi isi duniya aur isi zindagi mein hai. Aatma hai ya nahi, maut ke baad kuch hai ya nahi, unhe nahi pata, aur ye sawal unhe pareshan nahi karte.

Jis mahaul mein wo bade hue, wahan aatma, karm aur punarjanm maani hui baatein thi. Isliye in khayalon ke liye unke man mein narmi hai, aur inme kuch tark bhi dikhta hai. Par ye unke liye aastha nahi hain. Ye anjaane ilaqe ki atkalein hain. Sahi niklein ya galat, unki zindagi nahi badlegi.

Aatmaon ko bulane wali baithkon ko wo bekaar aur aksar bhole, dukhi logon ka shoshan maante hain.

**Ishwar.** Duniya ko dekhkar unhe rahasya aur gehrai ka ehsaas hota hai. Use samajhne ka raasta unke liye vigyaan ka hai, haalaanki wo maante hain ki poori tarah nishpaksh hona mumkin nahi.

Us rahasya ko wo "Ishwar" nahi kehte, kyunki is shabd ke saath bahut kuch juda hai jise wo nahi maante. Insaan jaisa, niji Ishwar unhe ajeeb lagta hai, aur unhe hairat hoti hai ki itne log aisa sochte hain. Advaita Vedanta unhe kheenchta hai, par uski niraakar, anant udaan thoda darati bhi hai.

Kudrat ki rang-birangi bharmaar unhe sukoon deti hai. Kehte hain ki purane Bharat ya Greece ke prakriti-pujak mahaul mein wo ghar jaisa mehsoos karte, bas devtaon ke bina.

**Gandhi.** Naitikta unhe kheenchti hai, bhale wo use tark se saabit na kar paayein. Gandhiji ki ek badi den wo ye maante hain ki unhone sahi saadhan par zor diya. Khayal purana tha. Naya ye tha ki ek naitik usool ko itne bade paimane par saarvajanik jeevan mein utaara gaya. Jis duniya ko sirf nateeje dikhte hain, wahan ye baat anokhi thi. Ye Bharat mein kitni kaamyaab hui, Nehru nahi keh sakte. Par iski gehri chhaap logon ke man par padi hai.

**Marx.** Marx aur Lenin ko padhkar unhe itihaas mein ek silsila aur matlab dikha. Soviet Union ki uplabdhiyon ne unhe bahut prabhavit kiya. Wahan ki kai baatein unhe khatki bhi, mauqaparasti aur taaqat ki rajneeti lagi. Phir bhi wo Soviet kranti ko insaani samaj ki ek badi chhalaang maante hain.

Wo khud ko vyaktigat azadi ka himayati kehte hain, jise zyada qataar-bandi pasand nahi. Par maante hain ki pecheeda samaj mein badi azadi ke liye chhoti azadiyon par kuch rok lagani padti hai.

Marxwad ka darshan unhe kaafi had tak manzoor hai, par usne unhe poora santusht nahi kiya. Man mein Vedanta jaisa ek dhundhla aadarshwad chala aata tha. Communist amal mein usoolon aur kaam ke beech ki doori unhe pasand nahi thi. Wo khud maante hain ki unke man mein ek ajeeb mishran tha, jise wo suljha nahi paye.

Marx ka samaj ka vishleshan unhe mote taur par sahi laga. Par baad mein bahut kuch aisa hua jo us dhaanche mein fit nahi baitha. Lenin ne use naye haalat ke hisaab se dhaala. Phir fascism aaya, aur technology ki tez raftaar ne tasveer phir badal di.

Isliye wo samajwad ki buniyaad maante hain, par Bharat ke vaampanthi guton ki baal ki khaal nikalne wali behson ke liye unme sabr nahi. Unka kehna hai ki zindagi itni pecheeda hai ki kisi ek bandhe siddhant mein nahi samati.

Ek chetavni bhi hai. Jo log brahmand ki paheli mein doob jaate hain, wo aksar samaj ki burayiyon ko insaani fitrat ya pichhle janm ka phal kehkar chhod dete hain. Jabki un burayiyon mein se zyadatar hatayi ja sakti hain.

**Vigyaan.** To unka langar kya hai? Tark aur prayog se parkha hua gyaan. Kala, kavita aur antargyaan ke liye wo jagah rakhte hain, kyunki har sach vigyaan ke tareeqe se pakad mein nahi aata. Par kasauti ye hai ki zinda falsafa aaj ke sawalon ka jawab de.

Saath mein ek vinamra baat: ho sakta hai hum bhi apne yug ke qaidi hon, jaise purane log apne yug ke the.

Vigyaan ne kami wali arthvyavastha ko bharpoori mein badalne ki taaqat di hai. Naye physics ne duniya ki tasveer badal di hai. Insaan ab khud ko kudrat se alag nahi dekhta.

Vaigyanik khud darshan ki dehleez par aa gaye hain, aur unme matbhed hai. Bertrand Russell jaise log duniya ki ekta ko bakwas kehte hain aur insaan ko anuon ka ittefaq. Doosre kehte hain ki naya physics kudrat ki ekta saabit kar raha hai. Nehru ko isme Advaita ki goonj sunayi deti hai: ek hi tatva, badalte roop, urja ka jod wahi, aur aisa brahmand jo apne aap viksit hota hai.

Vigyaan "kaise" poochta rahega, aur shayad kisi din "kyun" ke kareeb bhi le jaye. Ya shayad rahasya, rahasya hi rahe.

Aur ek dar. Agar vigyaan naitikta se kat gaya, to taaqat aur tabahi ke hathiyar bure aur swarthi logon ke haath mein simat jayenge, aur vigyaan apni hi uplabdhiyan mita dega. Nehru ko is jung ke peeche yahi andar ka takraav dikhta hai.

Hissa umeed par khatam hota hai. Insaan mein hazaar kamiyan hain, par wo sach, vishwas, desh aur izzat ke liye sab kuch qurbaan karta aaya hai. Brahmand mein dhool ke kan se bhi chhota, phir bhi kudrat ki taaqaton ko lalkarta hai. Usme kuch devta jaisa hai, aur kuch shaitan jaisa bhi.

Aage andhera hai, par raaste ka kuch hissa dikhta hai. Aur zindagi mein, saari takleefon ke saath, khushi aur khoobsurti bhi hai. Yahan Nehru ek purane Greek naatak ki panktiyon ka sahara lete hain.

## Part 6: Likhun ya na likhun

Ab wapas qile mein. Ikkeesvan mahina chal raha hai. Ek aur janmdin aane wala hai. Pichhle chaar janmdin jail mein beete hain, aur usse pehle ke kitne, ginti yaad nahi.

Dost maankar chalte the ki Nehru jail mein hain to kitaab aayegi. Ye lagbhag aadat ban chuki thi. Par is baar wo ruke rahe. Aisi kitaab kyun likhein jo chhapne tak baasi ho jaye? Kiske liye, aur kab ke liye? Ho sakta hai kabhi chhape hi nahi. Ho sakta hai Bharat khud maidan-e-jung ban jaye. Unhe lagta tha ki ye sirf ek aur badi jung nahi hai, isse ek nayi duniya niklegi, achhi ya buri.

Yahi pichhli qaid mein hua tha, October 1940 se December 1941 tak, Dehradun jail ki usi kothri mein jahan chhe saal pehle aatmakatha shuru hui thi. Das mahine likhne ka man nahi bana. Wo padhte rahe, mitti aur phoolon mein lage rahe. Phir kuch hafte tezi se likha, aatmakatha ka agla hissa. Kaam poora hone se pehle achanak rihai ho gayi, chaar saal ki saza khatam hone se kaafi pehle.

Aaj wo ise achha hi maante hain. Wo panne ab unhe pheeke lagte hain. Ghatnayein yaadon ka malba ban chuki hain.

Jo bacha hai, wo kuch aur hai. Kuch logon se mulaqatein. Bheed se rishta: Bharat ke log, jo itne alag hokar bhi hairatangez tareeqe se ek hain. Man ke safar. Dukh, aur usse ubarne ki khushi. Kaam ke pal ka nasha. Aur wo maante hain ki andar ki zindagi poori tarah kisi ko batayi nahi ja sakti.

Yahin wo baaghbani aur kamal wala kissa aata hai, jisse humne shuruaat ki thi. Aur uske saath ek aur khudai ki yaad. Teen saal pehle Dehradun jail ke aangan mein khodte hue lakdi ke do purane khambhe nikle. Wo tees-chaalees saal purane phaansi-ghar ke bache hue nishaan the. Qaidiyon ne milkar unhe ukhaad phenka, aur sab khush hue ki ek manhoos cheez gayi.

To ab kalam. Par likhein kya?

Aaj ke baare mein nahi, kyunki aaj ko wo tabhi mehsoos karte hain jab usme kuch kar rahe hon. Kal ke baare mein nahi, kyunki wo bhavishyavakta nahi hain, aur kal pehle bhi umeedon ko dhokha de chuka hai. Bacha ateet. Par wo itihaaskar ki tarah nahi likh sakte. Na wo taaleem hai, na wo mizaaj. Unke liye ateet tabhi zinda hai jab wo aaj ko chhuta hai.

Yahan Goethe ka khayal aata hai, ki itihaas likhna ateet ke bojh se raahat deta hai. Nehru ise psychoanalysis jaisa batate hain, bas mareez ek aadmi nahi, poori qaum hai. Aur Nietzsche ki chetavni bhi: hum sadiyon ki samajh ke hi nahi, unke pagalpan ke bhi vaaris hain. Bharat aur China jaisi purani sabhyataon par ye bojh sabse bhaari hai.

To meri viraasat kya hai? Nehru ka jawab do hisson mein hai. Pehla, wo sab jo insaaniyat ne hazaaron saal mein socha, saha aur paya. Ye sabka saajha hai. Doosra, ek khaas viraasat jo Bharat walon ke khoon aur haddiyon mein hai. Wo kisi ki jaagir nahi, par usne humein wo banaya jo hum hain.

Kitaab isi viraasat ke baare mein hogi. Wo pehle hi maan lete hain ki vishay bada hai aur wo sirf satah chhu payenge. Unhe umeed bas itni hai ki likhte-likhte unka apna man saaf hoga.

Nazar niji rahegi: ye khayal unke man mein kaise bana, aur usne unhe kaise badla. Wo kehte hain ki kisi desh ko hum uske logon se milkar hi samajhte hain. Unke man mein tasveeron ki ek gallery hai. Kuch chehre. Kuch purane saathi. Aur anginat bheed, jo unki taraf dekh rahi hai, aur jiski hazaaron aankhon ke peeche wo jhaankne ki koshish karte hain.

Wo bata dete hain ki agla chapter poori tarah niji hoga, kyunki usi se unka us waqt ka mizaaj samajh aayega. Par ye doosri aatmakatha nahi hai.

Chapter wahin khatam hota hai jahan shuru hua tha. Bahar jung, andar ek qaidi jo kuch kar nahi sakta. Wo jung ko bhukamp ya baadh ki tarah, bina niji bhaav ke dekhne ki koshish karte hain, aur maante hain ki ye ho nahi pata.

Aakhir mein unhe 8 August 1942 ki shaam ko kahi Gandhiji ki baat yaad aati hai: duniya ki aankhon mein khoon utra ho, tab bhi humein use shaant aur saaf nazar se dekhna hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, vigyaan wala dar. Nehru ne 1944 mein likha ki naitikta se kata hua vigyaan tabahi ke hathiyar galat haathon mein de dega. Agle hi saal, August 1945 mein, Hiroshima hua.

Doosri, Soviet Union ki tareef. Aaj ye hissa sabse purana lagta hai, kyunki us daur ki sachchai baad mein khulkar saamne aayi. Par dhyan dijiye ki Nehru tab bhi apna shak darj kar rahe the, aur khud ko kisi ek siddhant mein baandhne se mana kar rahe the.

Teesri, aur shayad sabse dilchasp. Yahan ek neta hai jo Ishwar, aatma aur maut ke baad ke sawal par saaf kehta hai: mujhe nahi pata. Aaj kitne neta ye teen shabd bol sakte hain?

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru kehte hain ki ateet tabhi zinda hai jab wo aaj ko chhuta hai. Bharat ke ateet ka kaun sa hissa aapki aaj ki zindagi ko sach mein chhuta hai?

## Outro

Agle episode mein kitaab ka sabse niji chapter. Nehru jail se chhootkar seedhe Germany ke ek sanatorium pahunchte hain, jahan Kamala beemar hain. Ek shaadi, jise unhone bahut der se samjha. Aur wapsi ki udaan, jisme unke paas ek tokri hai.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
