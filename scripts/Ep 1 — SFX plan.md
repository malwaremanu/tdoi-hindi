# Ep 1 — SFX plan (cold open, 00:00–01:08)

**Show:** The Discovery of India, Hindi mein · **Narrator:** Asha (voice: Fola)
**Scene:** Ahmadnagar Fort, 1944. A prisoner-gardener digs in hard, stony soil in the summer heat.
**Style:** Think of a BBC history documentary, not a radio drama. The sound should be sparse, dry and literal. Only the spade, the earth, the stone and the pen get spot sounds. The history section (Chand Bibi) is **not** illustrated.

Level convention: narration is mastered to **-16 LUFS integrated** (peaks ≤ -1 dBTP). "dB under voice" means the sound's short-term loudness relative to the voice's short-term loudness at that moment.

## 2. Continuous bed (B1): hot, dry outdoor courtyard

**What it is:** a still, hot exterior. Very faint warm air movement over stone walls, plus one or two insects far away. No birdsong chorus, no traffic, no voices. It should feel like heat, not like wind.

| Time | Bed level (under voice) | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00.0 | Starts at -26 dB | Fade in over **1.0 s**. Starts with the first spade stroke, so the scene arrives already in progress |
| 00:02 → 00:15.8 | -26 dB | Steady |
| 00:15.8 → 00:19.0 | -29 dB | Dip for the lotus reveal (400 ms down, 600 ms back) |
| 00:19 → 00:28 | -26 dB | Steady |
| 00:28 → 00:42.5 | **-28 dB** (-32 dB in quiet passages if the voice is soft) | Eases down for the history section so the listener "leaves" the courtyard |
| 00:42.5 → 00:52.6 | -26 dB | Back in the present |
| 00:52.6 → 00:53.2 | → -∞ | **Complete fade-out.** Nothing under the "खुदाई बंद / शुरू" beat |
| 00:53.2 → 01:08 | Off | The host intro is clean |

Use a mono or narrow-stereo bed (width ≤ 50%), high-pass at 120 Hz and gentle low-pass at 9 kHz, so it never competes with Fola's sibilance. Choose a loop of at least 50 s, or crossfade two takes, so no loop point can be heard.

## 3. Where silence matters more than sound

1. **00:11.1 → 00:12.0, after the spade hits stone.** Leave 0.8 s with only the bed. The listener's attention should hang on "what was that?"
2. **00:16 → 00:19, the lotus.** No sparkle, chime or "reveal" sound. The bed dips, and the image is the narration's job.
3. **00:28 → 00:42, Chand Bibi and Akbar's army.** This is the most tempting place for swords and battle sounds, so it gets **none**. Illustrating 16th-century fighting would turn the documentary into drama and break the 1944 frame.
4. **00:53 → 00:57, "जमीन की खुदाई बंद। इतिहास की खुदाई शुरू।"** This is the thesis of the series. The bed is gone and there is true digital silence around the voice. The 0.6 s pause after "बंद।" must stay exactly as recorded. Do not tighten it.
5. **00:57 → 01:05, the host intro.** Clean voice. The cold open is over and we are in the studio.

## 5. Mix notes

- **Ducking:** Do not sidechain-duck the bed. Its level is already set low, and audible pumping sounds like radio. Place the spot cues by hand in the gaps between words, which works better than ducking. If a cue has to overlap a word (S4, S7, S8), automate a further -4 dB for that word only.
- **Levels:** Nothing goes above -14 dB under voice (S3 is the ceiling). The bed sits at -26 to -29 dB. With all FX on, the integrated loudness of the open should rise by **less than 0.5 LU**. If it rises more, the FX are too loud.
- **EQ / space:** High-pass every spot cue at 80–100 Hz. Cut 2–4 kHz by 2–3 dB on S2, S3 and S4 so they don't mask Hindi consonants (ख, ट, ड). Use no added reverb. Everything is dry and close except S5 and S6, which should be naturally distant recordings, not distance faked with reverb.
- **Stereo:** Keep the voice dead centre. Spade cues go slightly left of centre (≤ 20%). The crow and gate go 30–40% right, so the courtyard feels wide without distracting.
- **Timing:** Cue times are on the narration file's timeline (00:00.0 = first sample of "एक"). If narration gets re-rendered, re-spot S3 (00:11.1), S6 (00:27.6), S1 at 00:42.5 and the bed fade at 00:52.6. These four cues carry the structure.
- **Avoid:**
  - Music stings, risers, drones, "documentary tension" pads
  - Whooshes, swooshes and reverse-cymbal transitions (including into the host intro)
  - Cartoon or exaggerated impacts, and boom or sub-drop on the stone hit
  - Swords, horses, battle crowds or war drums under the Chand Bibi lines
  - Sparkles or chimes on the lotus
  - Period-radio crackle or vinyl noise ("1944" filter effects)
  - Any loop with an audible seam, and any bed carrying birdsong or human voices
- **QC:** Listen once at very low volume on phone speakers. If any FX is noticed before the words, pull it down 3 dB. Then listen once on headphones and check that the 00:53–00:57 silence is truly silent, with no bed tail, hiss or click.

## 6. Kya sunayi dega

1. धूप में सूखी मिट्टी पर फावड़े की दो-तीन चोटें सुनाई देंगी, पीछे बहुत हल्की गरम हवा होगी, बस।
2. फावड़ा पत्थर से टकराएगा, एक भारी "ठक", और फिर एक पल के लिए सब रुक जाएगा।
3. कमल और चांद बीबी की बात पर कोई आवाज़ नहीं होगी, सिर्फ़ आशा की आवाज़ और दूर की गर्मी।
4. फावड़ा ज़मीन पर रखा जाएगा, कलम उठेगी, और फिर "खुदाई बंद, खुदाई शुरू" पूरी खामोशी में गूंजेगा।
5. आख़िर में साफ़ आवाज़ में नमस्कार होगा, और तारीख के साथ कलम कागज़ पर धीरे-धीरे चलती सुनाई देगी।
