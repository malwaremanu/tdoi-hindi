Nehru ka andaaza hai ki Malwa mein ek purana samvat chalta tha. Aur baad mein use Vikram ke naam se jod diya gaya. Par ye bhi pakka nahi.

Nehru ko sabse zyada hairat is baat par hai ki samajhdaar log bhi is kahani ko kheench-taan kar itihaas banane mein jut gaye.

Isi parampara mein Mahabharat bhi hai. Uski badi ladai shayad chaudahvi sadi isa poorv ke aas-paas maani jaati hai. Haalaanki Nehru khud kehte hain ki ye tareekh bhi dhundhli hai.

Isi mein ek shabd pakka hota hai. Bharatvarsh.

Aur Mahabharat ke beech mein Gita hai. Saat sau shlok. Ek sankat ki kavita. Jab insaan ke saamne do kartavya takra rahe hon.

Tilak, Aurobindo aur Gandhi, teeno ne iski apni vyakhya likhi. Gandhi ne isse ahimsa nikali. Doosron ne nyay ke liye ladna.

Gita ka sandesh Nehru ke hisaab se seedha hai. Haath par haath rakhkar mat baitho. Kaam karo. Par phal ki chinta mein mat ulajho.

## Part 7: Ek purane bazaar mein

Bahut darshan ho gaya. Ab chaliye bazaar.

Nehru ek shikayat karte hain. Itihaaskaron ne raajaon aur darshan par bahut likha. Par aam log kaise jeete the, kya khaate the, kya bechte the, is par bahut kam.

Unke paas do khazaane hain. Ek, Jataka kathaein, jo Buddha se bhi pehle ke zamane ki kahaniyan sunati hain. Doosra, Kautilya ka Arthashastra.

To aaiye, in kahaniyon ke sahare, kalpana mein ek purane shehar ki gali mein chalte hain.

Sabse pehle phalon ki dukaanein. Aam. Anjeer. Angoor. Kela. Khajoor.

Bagal mein phool wale. Aur haan, phoolon ki maala. Nehru muskurate hue kehte hain ki tab bhi maala logon ko utni hi pyaari thi, jitni aaj.

Thoda aage anaaj. Chawal, gehun, baajra. Ganne se bani cheeni. Doodh aur doodh se bani cheezein, jinki tab bhi badi qadar thi.

Aur ek kone mein sharaab ki dukaan bhi. Chawal se, phalon se, ganne se bani.

Kapde wali gali mein resham hai. Oon hai. Sooti kapda hai. Kaaleen aur kambal hain.

Kahin se chandan ki khushboo aa rahi hai. Phoolon aur chandan se itr aur tel bante the.

Aur log? Kaise-kaise log!

Vaid. Shalya-chikitsak. Adhyaapak. Sangeetkaar. Abhineta. Nachne wale. Kartab dikhane wale nat. Kathputli wale. Pheri wale.

Ab ek anokhi baat. Jataka mein aise poore gaon ka zikr hai, jahan sab ek hi kaam karte the.

Badhaiyon ka gaon. Kaha jaata hai, hazaar parivaron ka. Lohaaron ka gaon. Aur aise hi aur.

Ye gaon kisi shehar ke paas hote. Shehar unka maal khareedta. Aur poora gaon milkar bade-bade order poore karta.

Nehru sochte hain ki shayad isi alag rehne aur alag kaam karne se jaati vyavastha aur phaili.

Vyapaariyon ki shreniyan taaqatwar thi. Aur unka mukhiya, shreshthi, yaani aaj ka seth, bahut bada aadmi maana jaata tha.

Ab is gali se nikal kar door ka safar.

Registaan paar karte kaafile pashchim mein Bharuch ke bandargaah tak jaate. Wahan se jahaaz Faras ki khaadi hote hue Babylon tak.

Aur nadiyon par naavein. Banaras se, Patna se, Champa se, samundar tak. Aur phir Lanka tak.

Jataka mein likha hai ki ek-ek jahaaz par sainkdon vyapaari aur musaafir chadhte the.

Ab ek drishya jo mujhe is chapter mein bahut pyaara laga.

Uttar-pashchim mein, aaj ke Peshawar ke paas, ek mashhoor vishvavidyalaya tha. Takshashila.

Jataka ki kahaniyon mein baar-baar aisa aata hai ki bade gharon ke ladke, Brahmin aur ameeron ke bete, akele Takshashila padhne jaate the.

Na koi naukar saath. Na koi hathiyar.

Sochiye ek naujawan ko. Haath mein shayad ek potli. Saamne sainkdon kos ka raasta. Aur man mein padhne ki lagan.

Takshashila khaas taur par chikitsa ke liye mashhoor tha. Kehte hain ki jab bhi Buddha ki tabiyat bigadti, unke chaahne wale Takshashila se padhe ek mashhoor vaid ko bulaate.

Par Nehru andhere hisse bhi nahi chhupate. Gharon mein daas the. Aur kuch log achhoot maane jaate the, chandaal, jinka kaam laashein uthana tha.

Aur auraton ki haalat? Manu ke qanoon mein aurat hamesha kisi par nirbhar thi. Pita par, pati par, ya bete par.

Phir bhi kahaniyon mein auraton ka bada maan hai. Aur vidushi auraton ka zikr baar-baar aata hai. Manu khud kehte hain ki jahan aurat ka aadar hota hai, wahan devta baste hain.

Aur aakhir mein, ek Yunani ki gawahi. Sikandar ki muhim ka itihaas likhne wala Arrian. Usne likha ki gaane aur naachne ka itna shauk kisi aur qaum ko nahi tha, jitna Bharatiyon ko.

## Part 8: Buddha: mutthi bhar patte

Ab ek din. Ek ittefaq.

Nehru likh rahe hain. Aur unhe dhyaan aata hai ki aaj Vaishakh ki poornima hai. Bauddh naya saal. Saal 2488.

Bauddh granthon ke mutabik isi poornima ko Buddha janme. Isi poornima ko unhe gyaan mila. Aur isi poornima ko wo is duniya se gaye.

Aur aaj, us din, Nehru unke baare mein likh rahe hain.

Buddha akele nahi the. Unke hi zamane mein ek aur mahapurush the. Mahavir, jinse Jain dharm aaya.

Dono Kshatriya the. Dono ne Ved ka adhikaar nahi maana. Dono ne ahimsa par zor diya.

Parampara ke hisaab se Buddha assi saal ki umar mein, 544 isa poorv mein gaye. Aur wahin se Bauddh samvat shuru hota hai.

Nehru ka Buddha se rishta bahut purana hai. Bachpan mein unhe ek kitaab bahut pasand thi. Edwin Arnold ki "Light of Asia". Aur wo us naujawan Siddharth ki taraf kheenche chale gaye, jo bahut dard aur uljhan se guzarkar Buddha bana.

Bade hokar, jab wo apne soobe mein ghoomte, to Buddha se judi jagahon par jaate. Kabhi raasta badalkar bhi.

Nepal ki seema ke paas, jahan wo janme. Gaya, jahan Bodhi ped ke neeche unhe gyaan mila. Banaras ke paas wo Mrigdaav, jahan unhone pehla updesh diya.

Ab Buddha ne kaha kya?

Nehru kehte hain ki Buddha mein himmat thi. Unhone pujariyon, karmkaand aur andhvishwas, sab par seedha hamla kiya. Chamatkaaron ko bhi nahi maana.

Unki baatein, Nehru ke shabdon mein, band kamre ke baad pahaadon ki taaza hawa jaisi lagti hain.

Ek baat suniye. Buddha kehte the: meri baat sirf isliye mat maano ki tum mera aadar karte ho. Use aise parakho, jaise sone ko aag mein parakhte hain.

Ishwar hai ya nahi? Buddha na haan kehte the, na naa. Aatma hai ya nahi? Is par bhi chuppi.

Unka kehna tha ki jahan jaanna mumkin nahi, wahan faisla rok do.

Par ek cheez pakki thi. Har cheez ka ek kaaran hai. Aur har kaaran ka ek nateeja.

Buddha ne dukh ki baat ki. Dukh hai. Dukh ka kaaran hai. Dukh khatam ho sakta hai. Aur uska ek raasta hai.

Aur raasta? Beech ka. Na bhog mein doobo. Na shareer ko itna sukhao ki chalne ki taaqat hi na bache.

Aur ye raasta kisi ki kripa se nahi, apni mehnat se tay hota hai.

Ab wo kissa jo Nehru ko bahut pyaara hai.

Patjhad ka mausam hai. Buddha ek din mutthi mein kuch sookhe patte uthate hain. Aur apne pyaare shishya Anand se poochte hain: kya bas yahi patte hain? Ya aur bhi hain?

Anand kehte hain: chaaron taraf patte gir rahe hain. Itne ki gine nahi ja sakte.

Buddha kehte hain: bas aise hi. Maine tumhe mutthi bhar sach diye hain. Par inke alawa hazaaron aur sach hain. Ginti se bahar.

Jaati par Buddha ne seedha vaar nahi kiya. Par apne sangh mein unhone use maana bhi nahi.

Aur do baatein Buddha ki, jo yaad rakhiye. Kyunki aaj ki kahani ke aakhir mein ye wapas aayengi.

Ek: koi hazaar logon ko ladai mein hara de, par sabse bada vijeta wo hai jo khud ko jeet le.

Do: jeet nafrat ko janm deti hai. Kyunki haarne wala dukhi rehta hai.

Ab Nehru ek imaandar baat kehte hain. Bauddh dharm ki niraasha unhe nahi jachti. Zindagi se door bhaagna bhi nahi. Wo kehte hain ki andar se wo ek "pagan" hain. Jise zindagi aur kudrat ki bharmaar se pyaar hai.

Phir bhi, Buddha ki ek moorti unhe rok leti hai.

Kamal par baithe. Aankhein band. Shaant.

Pehli nazar mein wo bahut door lagte hain. Pahunch se bahar.

Par dobara dekhiye. Us shaanti ke peeche ek gehri aag hai. Aur Nehru ko lagta hai ki wo dheere se kehte hain: ladai se bhaago mat. Shaant aankhon se uska saamna karo.

## Part 9: Chanakya ka shehar

Chauthi sadi isa poorv. Sikandar uttar-pashchim se Bharat mein ghusta hai.

Nehru ke hisaab se, fauji nazar se, ye ek chhota sa chhapa tha. Aur Sikandar ke liye bahut kaamyaab bhi nahi.

Seema par ek chhote se sardar ne itni zabardast takkar di ki Sikandar ko sochna pada.

Agar seema ka ek chhota raja aise ladta hai, to andar ke bade raajya kaise ladenge?

Shayad isiliye uski fauj ne aage badhne se mana kar diya. Aur wo laut gaya.

Phir, 323 isa poorv mein, Babylon se ek khabar aati hai. Sikandar mar gaya.

Is khabar ka do logon ko intezaar tha. Ek naujawan, Chandragupta. Aur uske guru, Chanakya.

Dono logon ko bahar walon ke khilaaf uthate hain. Yunani fauj ko nikaal diya jaata hai. Takshashila par kabza hota hai.

Phir Chandragupta poore uttar Bharat ko paar karta hai, aur Pataliputra pahunchta hai.

Sikandar ki maut ke do saal ke andar wo Pataliputra ka maalik hai. Maurya samrajya ki neev pad chuki hai.

Ek baar aur koshish hoti hai. Sikandar ka senapati Seleucus Sindhu paar karke aata hai.

Aur haarta hai.

Use Afghanistan ka ek hissa, Kabul aur Herat tak, Chandragupta ko dena padta hai. Aur Chandragupta Seleucus ki beti se vivaah karta hai.

Dakshin ko chhodkar, Arab saagar se Bangal ki khaadi tak, aur uttar mein Kabul tak, ek hi raajya.

Nehru kehte hain ki likhe hue itihaas mein pehli baar Bharat mein itna bada, ek kendra se chalne wala raajya bana.

Ab us aadmi ko dekhiye jo is sab ke peeche tha. Chanakya. Jinka doosra naam Kautilya hai.

Unhe aksar Bharat ka Machiavelli kaha jaata hai. Nehru kehte hain ki ye tulna kuch had tak theek hai. Par Chanakya har maayne mein usse bade the.

Ek purana naatak hai, Mudrarakshasa. Usme unki tasveer milti hai.

Chaalak. Himmatwala. Ghamandi. Apmaan kabhi na bhoolne wala. Apna lakshya kabhi na bhoolne wala.

Aur saath hi, bilkul saadi zindagi. Shaan-o-shaukat mein koi dilchaspi nahi. Samrat unke liye maalik nahi, ek pyaara shishya tha.

Aur jab unka vachan poora ho gaya, to wo sab chhodkar dhyaan ki zindagi mein laut jaana chahte the.

Unki kitaab Arthashastra mein raajya chalaane ki har baat hai. Aur ek baat Nehru ko khaas lagti hai.

Rajyabhishek ke waqt raja ko praja ki seva ki kasam khaani padti thi. Ki agar main tum par zulm karun, to mujhe swarg na mile, jeevan na mile, santaan na mile.

Aur agar raja galat raste par chale, to log use hata kar doosra raja bitha sakte the.

Ab chaliye Pataliputra. Is samrajya ki rajdhani. Aaj ka Patna. Jahan Ganga aur Sone milti hain.

Yunani doot Megasthenes yahan aaya tha. Aur usne jo dekha, wo likh gaya.

Kalpana kijiye. Ganga ke kinaare ek lamba shehar. Lagbhag nau meel lamba. Chaudai lagbhag dedh-do meel.

Chaaron taraf ek deewar. Lakdi ki. Usme teer chalane ke liye chhed.

Deewar ke upar 570 burj. Aur shehar mein aane-jaane ke liye 64 darwaze.

Aur deewar ke aage ek chaudi, gehri khaai. Jo hifaazat bhi karti thi, aur shehar ka gandaa paani bhi le jaati thi.

Sirf deewar hi nahi, zyadatar ghar bhi lakdi ke the.

Kyun? Nehru ka andaaza hai ki bhukamp ki wajah se. Ye ilaqa bhukamp ke liye jaana jaata hai. Aur 1934 ke Bihar ke bade bhukamp ne ye baat phir yaad dila di.

Lakdi ke ghar the, to aag ka dar tha. Isliye har ghar mein seedhi, kaante, aur paani se bhare bartan rakhna zaroori tha.

Aur sabse hairat ki baat.

Pataliputra ki ek nagarpalika thi. Log usse chunte the.

Tees sadasya. Paanch-paanch ki chhe samitiyan. Ek udyog aur kaarigari dekhti. Ek janm aur maut ka hisaab rakhti. Ek yaatriyon aur teerthyaatriyon ki dekhbhaal karti.

Aur poori parishad milkar paisa, safai, paani, imaaratein aur baag sambhaalti.

Aur ek chhoti si baat, jo Megasthenes ne note ki. Bharatiyon ko saj-dhaj ka shauk tha. Itna ki kuch log apna kad badhaane ke liye khaas joote pehente the.

## Part 10: Ashoka: talwar ke baad

Chandragupta ke baad uska beta Bindusara. Uske darbar mein Misr ke Ptolemy aur pashchim Asia ke Antiochus ke doot aate the.

Aur phir Chandragupta ka pota.

Ashoka.

Ashoka ke man mein kya tha, ye hume kisi aur ki zabaani nahi, uske apne shabdon mein pata hai.

Usne apni baatein pattharon aur dhaatu par khudwaayin. Poore Bharat mein. Aur Nehru kehte hain ki ye shilalekh aaj bhi hamare saath hain.

Ab ek maidan ki kalpana kijiye. Ye kalpana hai, Nehru ka byora nahi.

Ladai khatam ho chuki hai. Dhuaan dheere-dheere baith raha hai. Kahin koi ghayal karah raha hai. Kahin qaidiyon ki qataar le jaayi ja rahi hai.

Is jagah ka naam hai Kalinga.

Nehru kitaab mein na ginti dete hain, na saal. Par itna batate hain ki Kalinga mein log maare gaye, aur log qaidi banaaye gaye.

Aur phir Ashoka ka ek shilalekh.

Usme Ashoka kehta hai ki ab wo aur khoon-khraba bardasht nahi karega. Kalinga mein jitne maare gaye aur jitne qaidi bane, uska sauva hissa bhi nahi. Hazaarva hissa bhi nahi.

Sochiye. Ek samrat. Jeet uski hui hai. Aur wo pattharon par khudwa raha hai ki ye jeet use manzoor nahi.

Ashoka ke liye asli jeet kya thi?

Logon ke dil jeetna. Kartavya aur dharma ke raaste se.

Ab yaad kijiye Buddha ki wo do baatein. Jeet nafrat ko janm deti hai. Aur sabse bada vijeta wo hai jo khud ko jeet le.

Shayad Kalinga ke maidan mein Ashoka ne ye dono baatein ek saath samjhi.

Par Ashoka koi shaant bhikshu nahi ban gaya, jo dhyaan mein khoya rahe.

Wo din-raat kaam karne wala raja tha.

Usne khudwaya ki main khaana kha raha hoon, ya mahal ke andar hoon, ya sone ke kamre mein, ya rath mein, ya baag mein, jahan bhi hoon, praja ka kaam mujhe turant bataya jaye. Har ghadi, har jagah, mujhe logon ke liye kaam karna hai.

Uske doot door-door gaye. Syria. Misr. Macedonia. Cyrene. Epirus. Madhya Asia. Burma. Siam.

Aur dakshin mein Lanka. Wahan usne kisi doot ko nahi, apne bete aur beti ko bheja. Mahendra aur Sanghamitra.

Aur sabse badi baat. Kahin koi zabardasti nahi. Har jagah man aur dil se baat.

Ashoka pakka Bauddh tha. Par doosre sab dharmon ka bhi aadar karta tha.

Bauddh dharm Kashmir se Lanka tak phaila. Phir Nepal. Aur baad mein Tibet, China, Mongolia.

Aur Bharat mein khaane ki thaali bhi badli. Shaakahaar badha. Sharaab kam hui. Aur pashu-bali par rok lagi.

Ab ek aakhri drishya. Pataliputra.

Ashoka ke mahal mein ek bada hall tha. Sainkdon khambhon wala.

Nehru ke likhne se kareeb tees saal pehle, puraatatva walon ne iska kuch hissa khoda. Dr Spooner ne apni report mein likha ki lakdi ke latthe do hazaar saal baad bhi waise hi chikne aur saabut the, jaise jis din rakhe gaye.

Itne ki jod tak nahi dikhte the.

Sochiye. Bharat ki garmi. Barsaat. Deemak. Aur do hazaar saal.

Lakdi ka kya ilaaj kiya gaya tha, Nehru kehte hain, ye aaj bhi raaz hai.

Ashoka ne iktalis saal tak jamkar raaj kiya. Aur 232 isa poorv mein wo chala gaya.

Nehru H.G. Wells ko yaad karte hain. Wells ne likha ki itihaas ke panne raajaon ke hazaaron naamon se bhare hain. Unke beech Ashoka ka naam akela taare ki tarah chamakta hai.

Volga se Japan tak use aaj bhi yaad kiya jaata hai. Aur Wells ke hisaab se aaj zinda logon mein se zyada log Ashoka ko yaad karte hain, Constantine ya Charlemagne ka naam sune logon se bhi zyada.

Ab socho is poori kahani ko.

Shuruaat ret se ladte ek shehar se hui thi. Jahan log baar-baar apni deewar oonchi karte rahe.

Aur ant ek aise raja par hua, jisne apni sabse badi jeet ke baad talwar neeche rakh di.

Shayad Bharat ki khoj yahi hai. Ek purani deewar. Jo baar-baar giri. Aur baar-baar kisi ne uske upar nayi eent rakhi.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, "Hindu" shabd. Nehru ka kehna tha ki Bharat ki sanskriti ko "Hindu sanskriti" kehna galat hai. Kyunki Bauddh, Jain, aur apna jeevan yahan ke rang mein dhaalne wale Isai aur Musalmaan bhi isi sanskriti ke hisse hain. Ye behas aaj bhi chalti hai. Aur shayad Nehru ki Sindhu nadi wali kahani is behas mein ek achha shuruaati sawal hai: naam kahan se aaya, aur hum use kitna chhota ya bada bana dete hain?

Doosri, Vikram Samvat wala kissa. Nehru ki shikayat thi ki hum parampara ko bina jaanche itihaas maan lete hain. Aaj kahaniyan pehle se kahin tez phailti hain. Ek phone se doosre phone tak. Nehru ki ye chetavni shayad aaj aur bhi kaam ki hai: koi baat sirf isliye sach nahi ho jaati ki wo purani hai, ya pyaari hai.

Teesri, Ashoka. Nehru ke hisaab se Ashoka ki asli jeet talwar se nahi, dil jeetne se hui. Aaj bhi jeet ka jashn manaane wale bahut hain. Jeet ke baad ruk kar haarne wale ka dard dekhne wale kam.

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru kehte the ki Bharat ki sabse badi taaqat sab ko apne andar sama lena hai. Aapko apne aas-paas aaj ye taaqat kahan dikhti hai? Aur kahan kam hoti lagti hai?

## Outro

Agle episode mein Maurya ke baad ka Bharat. Gupta raja, dakshin Bharat, purana Bharatiya natak, aur wo log jo Bharat se China aur Dakshin-purvi Asia ke dweepon tak pahunche. Aur phir ek mushkil sawal: itni chamak ke baad, thakaan kahan se aayi?

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
