Sochiye, dopahar hai. Hawa chal rahi hai, aur apne saath ret laa rahi hai.

Ek saal thodi si. Agle saal thodi aur.

Koi zyada dhyan nahi deta. Ret to roz ki baat hai.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Ek qaidi, Jawaharlal Nehru, wahan ek kitaab likh raha hai. Pichhli baar unhone khud se ek seedha sawal poocha tha: ye Bharat aakhir hai kya? Ab is sawal ka jawab dhoondhne wo peechhe lautte hain. Kitaabon se bhi peechhe. Seedhe zameen ke neeche.

## Part 1: Ittefaq se mile do shehar

Ab ek doosri jagah chaliye. Harappa.

Harappa — Mohenjo-daro jaisa hi ek aur dabba hua shehar. Par usse bahut door. Ek Sindh mein, doosra pashchim Punjab mein.

Aur yahan Nehru ek ajeeb baat batate hain. Ye dono shehar kisi bade naqshe ya yojana se nahi mile. Bas ittefaq se mile. Kisi ne kahin khoda, aur neeche se eentein nikal aayin.

Ab zara sochiye.

Ek bahut bada khet hai. Aap aankh band karke do jagah ungli rakhte hain. Ek is kone mein, ek us kone mein. Aur dono jagah khodne par sona nikalta hai.

To aap kya sochenge?

Yahi, ki beech mein bhi sona hoga.

Nehru bhi yahi sochte hain. Do door-door ki jagahon par, ittefaq se, ek hi sabhyata mili hai. To beech ki zameen mein aur bhi shehar soye pade honge. Aur bhi gaon. Purane insaan ke haathon ka aur bhi kaam.

Matlab ye sabhyata Bharat ke ek bade hisse par phaili thi. Uttar Bharat par to pakka.

Aur ye sirf andaaza nahi hai. Iske nishaan pashchim mein Kathiawar mein mil chuke hain. Kathiawar — Gujarat ka wo hissa jo samundar mein aage nikla hua hai. Aur doosri taraf, Punjab ke Ambala zile tak.

Is phailaav ko ek pal mehsoos kijiye. Ek taraf samundar ki namkeen hawa. Doosri taraf Punjab ke khule maidan. Aur dono ke beech, ek hi tarah ki eentein, ek hi tarah ke ghar.

Itna hi nahi. Kuch kaaran hain ye maanne ke, ki ye sabhyata Ganga ki ghati tak pahunchi thi.

Isliye log ise "Sindhu ghati ki sabhyata" kehte hain. Par Nehru kehte hain ki ye naam chhota pad jaata hai. Ye ek nadi ki ghati se kahin badi cheez thi.

Par ek pareshaani hai. Ek badi pareshaani.

Mohenjo-daro mein kuch likha hua bhi mila hai. Kuch khude hue chinh.

Par wo likhaai aaj tak poori padhi nahi ja saki.

Sochiye. Koi aapse baat karna chahta hai. Hazaaron saal ki doori se. Wo likh kar gaya hai, apni baat chhod kar gaya hai. Aur aap uska ek shabd nahi samajh paate.

To pehla jawab yahin ek sawal ban jaata hai. Agar is desh ki pehchaan is shehar se shuru hui, to us shehar ki apni awaaz kahan hai?

Shehar chup hai.

Par uski eentein bolti hain.

## Part 2: Rota bachcha nahi

Chaliye, us chup shehar ke ek ghar mein chalte hain. Kalpana kijiye.

Ek darwaaza. Andar ek chhota sa aangan. Ek kone mein nahaane ki jagah, pakki eenton se bani. Farsh mein halka sa dhalaan hai, taaki paani ruke nahi.

Paani kahan jaata hai?

Ek naali mein. Naali ghar se bahar nikalti hai, aur gali ki badi naali mein mil jaati hai.

Aur ye ek ghar ka aaram nahi tha. Ye poore shehar ka niyam tha. Galiyan saaf, dhalaan pakke, aur gandha paani bah kar shehar se bahar.

Nehru kehte hain: aise snaanghar aur aisa nikaas, humari jaankari ke hisaab se, duniya mein sabse pehle yahin bane.

Ghar bhi pakki eenton ke. Kuch do manzil ke. Kuch mein ek darwaan ki kothri bhi. Aur kuch makaan kiraye par diye jaate the.

Ab Nehru ek tasveer banate hain. Bharat ki kahani jab pehli baar humari aankhon ke saamne aati hai, to wo koi rota-bilakhta bachcha nahi hai. Wo kai maayno mein bada ho chuka hai.

Wo kisi dhundhle, door ke parlok ke sapnon mein khoya nahi hai. Wo zindagi ka dhang jaanta hai. Sundar cheezein bhi banata hai, aur kaam ki cheezein bhi. Aur kaam ki cheezein aisi, jinhe aaj hum aadhunik sabhyata ki nishani maante hain. Snaanghar. Naaliyan.

Ek aur hairat ki baat. Ye sabhyata zyadatar duniyavi thi. Dharm tha, par wo har cheez par chhaaya hua nahi tha.

Aur ye sab ek din mein nahi bana. Itna aage pahunchne mein hazaaron saal lage honge. Nehru ke hisaab se aage aane wale Bharat ke saare sanskritik daur isi ke baad aaye. Ye unki pehli kadi thi.

Ab ek angrez ka naam aata hai. Sir John Marshall — angrez puratatvavid, jinhone in shehron ki khudai ko sabse kareeb se dekha.

Marshall ka kehna tha ki ye koi nayi-nayi shuru hui sabhyata nahi thi. Ye pehle se purani thi. Is mitti mein jami hui, apne saanche mein dhali hui. Iske peeche bahut lambi mehnat thi.

Nateeja? Ab Bharat ko Iran, Mesopotamia aur Misr ke saath gina jaana chahiye. Un jagahon ke saath, jahan insaan ne sabse pehle basna aur sabhya hona seekha.

Marshall ek kadam aage bhi jaate hain. Punjab aur Sindh mein ek aisi sabhyata thi jo door-door tak ek jaisi thi. Mesopotamia aur Misr ki apni sabhyata ke bilkul kareeb. Aur kuch baaton mein unse bhi behtar.

Behtar kaise? Wahan daulat mandir, mahal aur raja ke maqbron par lagti thi; aam log mitti ki jhonpadi mein. Yahan ulta tha: sabse achhi imaaratein shehar walon ke aaram ke liye bani thi.

Aur ek cheez, jo us zamane mein sirf yahin milti hai. Rui. Kapde ke liye rui ka istemaal, Marshall kehte hain, us daur mein sirf Bharat tak tha. Pashchim ki duniya tak ye do-teen hazaar saal baad pahuncha.

Aaj sochiye. Aap subah jo kapda pehente hain, wo us ek cheez ka waaris hai, jo sabse pehle yahin bunna shuru hua.

Par ek sawal bachta hai. Itna aage badha hua shehar, kya akela tha? Ya uske raaste kahin door tak jaate the?

## Part 3: Samundar ki machhli, Sumer ka bazaar

Usi ghar ki rasoi mein jhaankiye. Phir se kalpana kijiye.

Chulha jal raha hai. Aur paas mein machhli rakhi hai. Samundar ki machhli.

Ab dhyan dijiye. Mohenjo-daro samundar ke kinaare nahi tha. To ye machhli aayi kahan se?

Arab saagar ke kinaare se. Aur ek-do baar nahi. Baqayda, niyam se. Is shehar ke roz ke khaane mein samundar ka hissa tha.

Yaani is shehar ke raaste door tak jaate the. Aur sirf samundar tak nahi.

Pashchim mein ek aur purani sabhyata thi. Sumer — Mesopotamia ki ek sabhyata, Tigris aur Euphrates nadiyon ke kinaare.

Sindhu ke logon ka Sumer se gehra lena-dena tha. Sindhu ke shehron ka bana maal Tigris aur Euphrates ke bazaaron tak pahunchta tha.

Kalpana kijiye ek mitti ke bartan ki. Sindhu ke kinaare bana. Shayad naav mein, shayad kisi kaafile ke saath, wo hafton chalta hai. Aur ek din kisi pardesi bazaar mein, ek pardesi haath use uthata hai.

Aur ek jagah to shayad Bharatiya vyapaariyon ki apni basti bhi thi. Akkad mein. Akkad — Mesopotamia ka ek purana shehar.

Lena-dena ulta bhi chalta tha. Sumer ki kala ke kuch tareeqe yahan aaye. Wahan ke shringaar ke saamaan ki nakal yahan bani. Aur ek belan jaisi mohar, jise ghuma kar chhaap banti thi, uski bhi nakal Sindhu ke kinaare hui.

Ab ek angrez vidvaan, Gordon Childe, in khandaron se do baatein nikaalte hain. Pehli: yahan ke karigar apne paas ke liye kaam nahi kar rahe the. Wo bazaar ke liye kaam kar rahe the. Doosri: bade-bade gharon se lagi godaam-jaisi kothriyan batati hain ki unke maalik vyapaari the. Unki ginti aur unka size — dono batate hain ki ye ek mazboot aur samruddh vyapaari samaj thi.

Aur Childe ek teesri baat jodte hain. Yahan ki galiyan soch samajh kar banayi gayi thi, aur naaliyan baqayda saaf hoti thi. Iska matlab, koi na koi nagar-nigam tha. Koi aisi satta, jo ye niyam lagu karati thi. Aur baadh ke baad, galiyon aur naaliyon ki wahi purani lines dobara khinchwayi jaati thi.

Par ye shehar sirf kaam ka shehar nahi tha. Marshall kehte hain ki yahan ki kala aur yahan ka dharm — dono par ek aisi chhaap thi, jo kisi doosre desh se milti nahi. Unke hisaab se yahan ka dharm, apne poore rang mein, aaj ke zinda Hindu dharm se alag pehchana hi nahi ja sakta.

Rasoi dikh gayi. Bazaar dikh gaya. Naaliyan dikh gayi.

Par ek sawal bacha hai. Is shehar ko aaj ke Bharat se jo dhaaga jodta hai — wo kahan tak khinchta hai?

## Part 4: Ret ke neeche se aati hui deewar

Cold open ki wo deewar yaad hai? Ret aati thi, aur log deewar oonchi karte the.

Nehru kehte hain ki Mohenjo-daro ke khandar khud us chup ladai ke gawaah hain. Zameen parat dar parat jamti gayi. Ghar ka farsh oopar uthta gaya. Aur logon ne purani neev par baar-baar nayi deewar chadha di.

Isi wajah se kuch ghar khudai karne walon ko do ya teen manzil ke lage.

Par wo manzilein nahi hain. Wo ek hi ghar hai, jise waqt ke saath oopar uthaya gaya.

Ab wo khudai ki baat, jo hum cold open mein chhod kar aaye the.

Khudai shuru hui, kuch saal chali, phir band ho gayi. Pehle tees ke dashak ki badi mandi ne roka. Kaaran bataya gaya paise ka — halanki shaan dikhane ke liye paisa kabhi kam nahi padta tha. Phir doosre vishwa-yuddh ne sab thanda kar diya. Aur jo khod kar nikala gaya tha, uski dekhbhal tak theek se nahi hui.

Nehru likhte hain: ye aisa nuksaan hai, jiska koi bahana nahi. Kuch saalon mein Mohenjo-daro ki khaas pehchaan mit jaayegi. Jo jaayega, wo dobara nahi banega. Peechhe bas tasveerein bachengi, aur kuch likhe hue byore. Aur unhe ye bhi nahi pata ki unke aakhri daure ke baad ke aath saalon mein wahan kya hua.

Ab Nehru ek lambi lakeer kheenchte hain.

Is shehar aur aaj ke Bharat ke beech bahut se khaali panne hain. Kai daur aise, jinke baare mein hum lagbhag kuch nahi jaante. Ek daur doosre se kaise juda, ye hamesha dikhta bhi nahi. Aur beech mein anginat badlaav aaye.

Par ek dhaaga hai jo kabhi nahi toota. Chhe-saat hazaar saal lamba dhaaga. Kyunki ye sabhyata shayad tabhi shuru hui thi.

Mohenjo-daro aur Harappa mein aisi cheezein milti hain jo aaj bhi zinda hain. Lok-reetiyan. Kaarigari. Yahan tak ki pehnaave ke kuch dhang. Aur inmein se bahut kuch pashchim Asia tak gaya, aur wahan apna asar chhod aaya.

To dhaaga lamba hai. Par abhi tak humein sirf eentein mili hain. Log nahi.

## Part 5: Wo kaun the, kahan gaye

Pehla sawal. Ye log kaun the, aur kahan se aaye?

Nehru ka jawab seedha hai: hum abhi nahi jaante.

Ek kitaab likhne wale ke liye ye kehna aasaan nahi. Par Nehru andaaze ko andaaze ki tarah hi rakhte hain. Use sach bana kar nahi bechte.

Unka andaaza ye hai. Ye sanskriti shayad isi mitti ki thi. Bahar se laayi hui nahi. Aur uski jadein, uski shaakhayein, shayad dakshin Bharat tak mil jaayein.

Kuch vidvaan in logon mein, aur dakshin Bharat ke Dravid logon aur unki sanskriti mein, ek gehri samanta dekhte hain.

Aur agar kabhi koi bahar se aaya bhi tha, to wo Mohenjo-daro se bhi hazaaron saal pehle ki baat hogi. Isliye Nehru kehte hain: inhe is desh ke mool niwasi hi maaniye.

Ab doosra sawal. Ye shehar khatam kaise hue?

Kuch vidvaan kehte hain ki ant achanak aaya. Koi aafat, jiska kaaran aaj tak samajh nahi aaya. Ye baat Gordon Childe ki hai.

Ho sakta hai wo aafat nadi khud ho. Sindhu apni bhayanak baadh ke liye mashhoor hai. Aisi baadh, jo gaon ke gaon aur shehar ke shehar baha le jaaye.

Ya ho sakta hai mausam badla ho. Zameen dheere-dheere sookhi ho. Aur registan, qadam-qadam, kheton ki taraf badha ho.

Sochiye wahi ret, jo shuruaat mein har saal thodi aur jamti thi.

To mausam ne shayad logon ki zindagi par gehra asar daala. Aur Sindh ka haal bhi isi taraf ishaara karta hai. Purane zamane mein Sindh hara-bhara aur upjaau tha. Madhyakaal se uska bada hissa registan hai.

Par Nehru yahan bhi jaldi faisla nahi sunaate. Ye sabhyata bahut badi thi. Ganga ki ghati tak, shayad usse bhi aage. Mausam ka badalna uske ek chhote hisse ko hi chhoo sakta tha. Aur sach ye hai, ki pakka kehne laayak jaankari humare paas hai hi nahi.

Phir ek ulti si baat. Jis ret ne kuch shehron ko dhaka, usi ne unhe bacha bhi liya. Aur jo shehar ret ke neeche nahi aaye, wo sadiyon mein mit gaye.

Shayad aage ki khudai baad ke zamanon se aur kadiyan jod de.

To pehla jawab yahin kamzor padta hai. Shehar ret mein so gaya.

Par kahani ret mein nahi soyi. Kyunki uttar-pashchim ke raaston par, kuch naye log chal pade the.

## Part 6: Hal chalane wale mehmaan

Mohenjo-daro ke baad jo sabhyata dikhti hai, wo usse judi bhi hai, aur alag bhi.

Dono baatein sach hain. Ek dhaaga hai, aur ek daraar bhi. Waqt ki bhi, aur rehan-sehan ki bhi.

Nayi sabhyata shuru mein zyada kheti wali thi. Qasbe the, thodi shehari zindagi bhi thi. Par zor khet par tha.

Ye zor shayad naye aane walon ne diya. Arya.

Arya uttar-pashchim se aaye. Ek saath nahi. Lehar par lehar. Ek jattha aaya, basa. Phir doosra aaya.

Aam taur par maana jaata hai ki ye aana Sindhu ke shehron ke lagbhag ek hazaar saal baad hua. Par Nehru ek doosri sambhavna bhi saamne rakhte hain. Shayad beech mein koi bada khaali waqt tha hi nahi. Shayad kabile aur log uttar-pashchim se baar-baar aate rahe, jaise baad ke zamanon mein bhi aate rahe. Aur yahin ghul-mil gaye.

Sochiye ek naya parivaar. Bailon ki ek jodi. Kandhe par ek hal. Aage ek chaudi, bhoori nadi, jiske paar wo pehli baar dekh rahe hain.

Ye wahi nadi hai. Sindhu.

Wo use paar karte hain. Us kinaare par ek purani duniya hai. Purane gaon, purane log, purani reetiyan. Aur ye naye log apne saath kya laaye hain? Sabse keemti cheez, ek hal.

Kyunki "Arya" shabd ka matlab bhi yahin chhupa hai. Ye ek aise mool shabd se bana hai, jiska matlab hai zameen jotna.

Arya zyadatar kisaan the. Aur kheti izzat ka kaam maana jaata tha.

Ab dhyan dijiye. Shuru mein in logon ka ek hi varg tha. Kaam ka koi bada bantwaara nahi tha. Jo aadmi hal chalata tha, wahi puja bhi karta tha. Wahi zaroorat padne par ladta bhi tha. Wahi vyapaar bhi karta tha. Pujaariyon ka koi alag, khaas haq wala varg nahi tha.

Ye kisaan yaad rakhiye. Episode ke aakhir se pehle hum us se phir milenge.

Phir milan hua. Ek taraf aane wale Arya. Doosri taraf Dravid, jo shayad Sindhu ke shehron ke hi waaris the.

Nehru ise is desh ka pehla bada sanskritik milan kehte hain. Do dhaaron ka ek nadi ban jaana.

Is milan se Bharat ke log bane. Aur Bharat ki buniyadi sanskriti bani. Jisme dono ke rang the, dono ki pehchaan thi.

Par ye pehla milan tha.

Aakhri nahi.

## Part 7: Teen sikke, teen peedhi

[SFX: hatheli par do-teen sikkon ki halki khanak]

Ek sikka sochiye. Purana, ghisa hua. Ise apni hatheli par rakhiye.

Is par ek raja ka naam hai. Naam ajeeb hai. Videshi. Kisi door ki zubaan ka.

Ab doosra sikka uthaiye. Ye uske bete ka hai.

To teesra sikka. Pote ka. Is par naam Sanskrit ka hai.

Aur ye raja ab yahan ki purani reeti se gaddi par baitha hai. Usi vidhi se, jo Kshatriya raajaon ke rajtilak ke liye thi.

Dada pardesi. Pota is mitti ka raja. Do-teen peedhi. Bas itna waqt lagta tha.

Ye sikke ki baat kalpana nahi hai. Nehru batate hain ki Isa ke janm ke aas-paas ke sikke yahi badlaav dikhate hain. Kalpana bas itni hai, ki humne unhe aapki hatheli par rakh diya.

Kaun the ye raja?

Aryon ke baad bhi log aate rahe. Yunani aaye. Shak aaye — uttar-pashchim se aaye hamlawar. Baad mein Hoon aaye. Aur bhi kai log, jinki poori fehrist lambi hai.

Wo aaye. Unhone kuch badla. Aur phir wo isi mitti ke ho gaye.

Ek angrez itihaaskar, Dodwell, ne Bharat ke liye samundar ki tasveer di. Ek aisa samundar, jo sab kuch apne andar sama leta hai, aur kabhi bharta nahi.

Aur ek baat sabko hairaan karti hai. Rajputon ke kai Kshatriya kul isi raaste se aaye. Kuch Shakon se — jinke hamle Isa se do sau saal pehle ke aas-paas shuru hue. Kuch baad mein aaye Safed Hoonon se.

Unhone yahan ka dharm apnaya. Yahan ke tor-tareeqe apnaye. Phir mahakavyon ke bade veeron se apna naata joda.

Nateeja? Kshatriya hona khoon se kam, aur haisiyat aur kaam se zyada tay hota tha. Jo raaj kare, jo lade, wo Kshatriya. Isliye bahar walon ke liye is varg mein aana aasaan tha.

Aur ye sokhna dono siron par chalta tha. Upar, jeetne wala koi bhi gut jaldi hi Kshatriya ban jaata. Neeche, samaaj ki seedhi ke sabse nichle paayedaan par, naye aane walon se nayi jaatiyan ban jaati thi.

Ab Nehru ko ek baat ajeeb lagti hai.

Ek desh, jo jaati mein bandha hai. Jo apne aap ko doosron se alag rakhta hai. Usme sab ko sama lene ki ye hairat-angez taaqat? Dono ek saath kaise?

Unka andaaza hai ki shayad isi taaqat ne Bharat ko zinda rakha. Baar-baar thakne ke baad, use baar-baar naya kar diya.

Musalmaan aaye, to un par bhi is desh ka gehra asar pada. Ek aur angrez itihaaskar hain, Vincent Smith. Unhone likha ki Turk bhi Shakon ki raah par chale. Wo bhi yahan ki sokhne wali taaqat ke aage jhuk gaye. Aur jaldi hi badal gaye.

Is badlaav ke liye Smith ek shabd chunte hain. "Hinduised."

Aur yahin, is ek shabd par, Nehru ki kalam ruk jaati hai.

## Part 8: Ek nadi, chaar naam

Sochiye. Ahmadnagar ki kothri. Mez par ek khuli kitaab. Nehru ek shabd ke neeche ungli rakhte hain.

"Hinduised."

Unhe ye shabd khatakta hai. Kam se kam is tarah istemaal karna.

Kyun? Kyunki aaj "Hindu" sunte hi ek dharm ka khayal aata hai. Ek sankara, khaas dharmik matlab. Agar is shabd ka matlab poori Bharatiya sanskriti ho, sabse chaude arth mein, tab to theek. Warna ye bhatkata hai.

To Nehru is shabd ka peechha karte hain. Peechhe. Aur peechhe.

Pehli hairat. Humare purane sahitya mein "Hindu" shabd hai hi nahi. Kahin nahi.

To kisi Bharatiya kitaab mein ye pehli baar kab aata hai? Nehru likhte hain ki unhe bataya gaya hai: aathvi sadi ki ek Tantrik pothi mein. Tantrik, yaani tantra ki purani parampara se judi.

Aur wahan bhi "Hindu" ka matlab koi dharm nahi. Ek kaum. Ek desh ke log.

Par shabd khud bahut purana hai. Wo Avesta mein milta hai. Avesta — Iran ka sabse purana dharm-granth. Aur purani Farsi mein bhi.

Pashchim aur Madhya Asia ke log hazaar saal se bhi zyada ye shabd bolte rahe. Kiske liye? Bharat ke liye. Ya theek kahein to, Sindhu nadi ke us paar rehne walon ke liye.

Ab wo nadi yaad kijiye. Sindhu.

Sindhu is nadi ka purana Bharatiya naam bhi hai, aur aaj ka bhi. Aur is ek shabd se chaar naam nikle.

Hindu.

Hindustan.

Indus.

Aur India.

Ek nadi. Chaar naam. Aur charon ke peechhe ek hi tasveer: nadi ke us paar ke log.

Sochiye. Isi nadi ke kinaare Mohenjo-daro ki deewarein uthi thi. Isi ko hal wale wo kisaan paar kar rahe the. Aur yahi nadi ek din poore desh ka naam ban jaati hai. Ek aisa naam, jo desh ne khud nahi rakha.

Phir ek Chini yaatri aate hain. I-tsing — ek Chini tirthyatri, jo is Tantrik pothi se kuch pehle Bharat aaye.

I-tsing apne safar ki kitaab mein likhte hain ki "uttar ke kabile", yaani Madhya Asia ke log, Bharat ko "Hindu" kehte hain. Unki zubaan mein, "Hsin-tu".

Par wo saath mein ye bhi jodte hain. Ye koi aam naam nahi hai. Is desh ke liye sabse theek naam hai Aryadesha. Yaani shreshth logon ka desh.

To "Hindu" naam aaya kahan se? Bahar walon ki zubaan se. Nadi ke us paar khade logon ki nazar se.

Aur ise ek dharm ke naam ki tarah bolna? Wo bahut baad ki baat hai. Bahut, bahut baad ki.

Nehru ek behtar naam sujhaate hain: Hindi. Iran se Misr tak, poora pashchim Asia Bharat ko "Hind" kehta aaya. Iran, Turkey, Iraq, Afghanistan — in sab jagah aaj bhi Hind hi chalta hai. Aur us naam ka dharm se koi rishta nahi.

Aaj sochiye. Jab hum kisi ko "Hindi" kehte hain, to hum ek zubaan ka naam le rahe hain. Par Nehru ke zamane mein ye shabd zubaan se pehle ek desh ka naam tha — bina kisi dharm ke.

Nehru isliye kehte hain ki shabd chahe jo chuniye. Indian kahiye, Hindi kahiye, Hindustani kahiye. Asal baat shabd nahi. Asal baat wo cheez hai, jise ye shabd pakadne ki koshish karte hain.

To us cheez ka apna, purana naam kya tha?

## Part 9: Jo thaam kar rakhe

Ek chhappar sochiye. Beech mein ek khamba. Khamba hata dijiye, to chhappar gir jaata hai.

Purane Bharat mein us cheez ka naam tha dharm. Aur Nehru batate hain ki ye shabd ek aise mool shabd se bana hai, jiska matlab hai thaam kar rakhna. Jod kar rakhna. Us khambe ki tarah.

Kisi cheez ka dharm uski sabse andar ki banawat hai. Uske andar ka niyam, jisse wo wahi hai jo hai. Isliye dharm sirf puja-paath nahi. Isme naitikta hai, sahi aur galat ki samajh, aur insaan ke saare farz aur zimmedaariyan.

Aur "Hindu dharm" khud dhundhla hai. Nehru likhte hain ki uski koi seema nikaali nahi ja sakti. Usme sabse oonchi soch bhi hai, aur sabse neechi bhi. Wo lachakdar hai, bahu-mukhi hai, har aadmi ke liye alag matlab rakhta hai. Bhav ek hi hai: jeene do.

Sab raaston ke liye ek purana, bada naam tha: Arya dharm. Isme wo saare panth aate the jo is desh mein paida hue. Jo Ved maante the, wo bhi. Jo nahi maante the, wo bhi. Bauddh bhi ise apna kehte the, aur Jain bhi. Buddha to apne raaste ko hamesha "Arya maarg" kehte the.

Ek chhota ghera bhi tha: Vaidik dharm. Isme wo log aate the jo Vedon ka adhikaar maante the.

Aur ek teesra naam: Sanatan dharm, yaani purana dharm. Ye kisi bhi purane Bharatiya panth ke liye bola ja sakta tha. Bauddh aur Jain ke liye bhi. Par Nehru likhte hain ki unke zamane tak kuch kattar log is naam ko lagbhag sirf apna maan baithe the.

Ab Nehru ka tark. Bauddh aur Jain dharm na Hindu dharm hain, na Vaidik dharm. Phir bhi wo isi desh mein paida hue. Ek Bauddh ya Jain sau feesadi Bharatiya soch ki paidaish hai. Par dharm se wo Hindu nahi.

Nateeja? Bharat ki sanskriti ko "Hindu sanskriti" kehna bhatkaane wala hai.

Jab tak purana dharm ek jeene ka dhang tha, wo is sanskriti ke barabar tha. Jab wo reeti-rivaazon mein kas gaya, to wo kuch zyada ban gaya. Aur kuch kam bhi.

Aage chalkar Islam aaya, aur is sanskriti par uska gehra asar pada. Phir bhi ye Bharatiya hi rahi. Aur Pashchim ki karkhanon wali sabhyata is par sau tarah se asar daal rahi thi. Uska nateeja kya hoga, Nehru nahi jaante.

Ek Isai ya Musalmaan bhi is jeevan-dhang ko apna sakta tha. Aksar apnaya bhi. Wo apne dharm mein pakka raha, par Bharatiya ban gaya. Dharm badle bina.

Nehru ko is poore itihaas mein jodne ki ek andar ki khinchaav dikhti hai. Har baar koi bahar se aaya, to ek chunauti aayi. Har baar jawab ek naya milan tha, aur har milan ke baad ek nayi bahaar. Neev wahi rahi.

Ek angrez lekhak ne isi ko duniya ke liye Bharat ka khaas tohfa kaha: anek se ek bana dena.

Teesra jawab ab dikhne laga hai. Ek aadat. Sab ko apne andar sama lene ki.

Par isi ghar ke andar, chupchaap, kuch aur bhi ban raha tha.

## Part 10: Andar ki deewar

Yaad hai wo kisaan, jo bailon ki jodi lekar Sindhu paar aaya tha? Jo hal chalata tha, puja bhi karta tha, ladta bhi tha, vyapaar bhi karta tha?

Ab uske saath kya hota hai?

Arya aaye, aur ek nayi samasya bhi laaye — nasal ki, aur raaj ki bhi. Doosri taraf Dravid the, jinke peechhe ek lambi sabhyata thi. Teesri taraf jangal-pahaad ke wo kabile, jo peeche reh gaye the.

Nehru likhte hain: dono taraf ke beech ek chaudi khaai thi, aur Arya khud ko bahut oopar maante the. Yahin se jaati paida hui.

Us zamane mein jeetne wale haarne walon ko mita dete the, ya ghulam banate the. Greece ki sabhyata ghulamon par khadi thi; Bharat mein aisa ghulam-khand nahi tha.

Bharat ne teesra raasta chuna. Sabko neeche rakh lo — par andar rakh lo.

Nehru kehte hain: jaati na poori taur par Aryon ki thi, na Dravidon ki. Wo alag-alag kaumon ko ek samaaj mein baithane ki koshish thi, jo aage chalkar ek bojh ban gayi — par hum use baad ke paimana se nahi naap sakte.

Shuruaat ek sakht lakeer se hoti hai: ek taraf Arya, doosri taraf wo, jo Arya nahi the.

Ye deewar doosron ko alag rakhne ke liye uthi thi. Par ek baar uth jaaye, to andar bhi kaatne lagti hai.

Kaam bantne lage: koi sirf puja, koi sirf ladai, koi sirf vyapaar. Jaise-jaise kaam khaas hote gaye, varg jaatiyon mein badalte gaye: Vaishya — kisaan, karigar, vyapaari. Kshatriya — raaj karne aur ladne wale. Brahmin — pujari aur sochne wale. Shudra — mazdoor aur be-hunar log.

Jo deewar Aryon aur baaki logon ke beech uthi thi, wo ab Aryon ke apne beech uth gayi.

Shabd bhi badal gaya: "Arya" ka nasl wala matlab mit gaya — ab matlab tha bhala, shreshth; aur "anarya" ka matlab hua neech.

Yunani darshanik Plato ne apni ek kitaab mein aadarsh samaaj ka wahi bantwaara socha tha; madhyakaal ke Catholic Europe mein bhi wahi.

Aryon ne samaaj hi nahi, insaan ki zindagi bhi chaar hisson mein baanti: vidyarthi — seekhna, sanyam; grihasth — ghar, kaam, duniya; phir ek buzurg, bina apne fayde ke samaaj ka kaam karne wala; aakhir mein sanyasi — duniya se lagbhag alag.

Isme ek samajhdaari thi: zindagi ko poora jeene aur use chhod dene, dono ko jagah mili.

Is desh mein, jaise China mein, vidya ka darja sabse oopar rahi. Raja aur yodha bhi vidwan ke aage jhukte the; daulat unse neeche.

Brahmin logon ne ek suvidha-prapt varg ke saare aib dikhaye — bahut se aise, jinke paas na vidya thi, na sadgun. Phir bhi izzat kam nahi hui: wo izzat pad ki nahi, gun ki thi.

Aaj bhi is paise ke zamane mein Gandhi, jo Brahman nahi hain, desh ke sabse bade neta hain. Isse Nehru ek kasauti nikaalte hain: dekhiye, ek desh kaisa neta chunta hai.

Purane Bharat ka ek bada vichaar tha: dharm, yaani farz — apne prati, aur doosron ke prati. Ye dharm Rita ka hissa tha, us naitik niyam ka jisse poora brahmaand chalta hai. Farz theek se karo, nateeja apne aap aayega.

Adhikaar ki baat kam hoti thi, farz ki zyada. Aaj ka zamana ulta hai: aaj har koi adhikaar maangta hai — insaan bhi, samooh bhi, desh bhi.

To is desh ki pehchaan kahan se shuru hui?

Shehar se nahi. Shehar ret mein so gaya. Naam se bhi nahi. Naam doosron ne diya, aur der se diya.

Ek aadat se. Sab ko apne andar sama lene ki aadat. Aur usi ke saath ek doosri aadat bhi: andar deewarein uthane ki.

Ret aati rahi. Aur deewar oonchi hoti rahi.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, "Hindu" shabd. Nehru ke liye ye pehle nadi ke us paar ke logon ka naam tha. Phir ek sanskriti ka. Aur bahut baad mein ek dharm ka. Aaj ye shabd kabhi sabse sankare arth mein bola jaata hai, kabhi sabse chaude mein. Dono baar ek hi sawal poochhne laayak hai: ye shabd jodne ke liye bola ja raha hai, ya baantne ke liye?

Doosri, Gandhi wali kasauti. Nehru ne poocha tha ki ek desh kis tarah ke neta ke peeche chalta hai. 1944 mein jawab tha: ek aadmi, jiske paas na pad tha, na paisa, na fauj. Aaj ye sawal humare saamne hai. Hum kisi neta ke peeche kyun chalte hain? Uski taaqat ke liye? Uske paise ke liye? Ya uske kirdaar ke liye? Jawab humare baare mein utna hi batata hai, jitna neta ke baare mein.

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapke aas-paas aaj kaun si deewar sabse oonchi hai? Aur wo aapko bacha rahi hai, ya qaid kar rahi hai?

## Outro

Mohenjo-daro ki likhaai aaj tak koi padh nahi paaya. Wo shehar chup hai.

Par jo log hal lekar aaye the, unke paas ek cheez thi jo Mohenjo-daro ke paas nahi bachi. Aise shabd, jo aaj bhi padhe ja sakte hain. Geet. Duniya ki sabse purani kitaabon mein se ek.

Aur us kitaab mein, pehli baar, koi poochta hai: ye sab aaya kahan se?

Agli baar, wahi pehle padhe ja sakne wale shabd.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
