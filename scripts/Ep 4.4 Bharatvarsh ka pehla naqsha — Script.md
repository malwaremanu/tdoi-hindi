[SFX: oonchi choti par tez hawa]

Sochiye. Himalaya ke un pahaadon mein ek choti hai. Uska naam Kailash hai. Us choti par devta baithte hain — aur neeche maidan ko dekhte hain.

Neeche nadiyan hain. Gaon hain. Khet hain. Dhuen ke patle-patle khambe. Aur log — chhote-chhote, apne kaam mein lage hue.

Wo dekhte hain ki ye log kaise jeete hain. Kaise ladte hain. Kaise jashn manate hain. Kaise darte hain.

Garm dhoop. Bheegi mitti. Bazaar ki chah-chah. Shaam ko chulhon se uthta hua dhuaan. Aur raat ko aag ke aas-paas baithi ek toli, jismein koi purani kahani suna raha hai.

Devta upar se ye sab dekhte hain. Aur unhe neeche utarne ka mann karta hai.

[SFX: bahut neeche, maidan mein ek dhol ki door wali thap]

Kyunki ye devta kisi door aasman ke raja nahi hain. Ye baith kar pooja lene wale nahi hain. Ye utarte hain. Ye aadmiyon ke beech khelte hain. Kabhi unke saath khate hain. Kabhi unse ladte hain. Kabhi unhe sazaa dete hain.

Pehle unhe shakl leni padegi. Naam chahiye honge. Ghar chahiye honge. Pati, patni, bete, bhai. Shaktiyan, aur kamzoriyan. Bhool, aur uska pachhtava.

Yahin se ek poori duniya khulti hai. Ek duniya, jismein ek ped kisi stri ke pairon ke chhune se phoolon se bhar jaata hai. Jismein pyaar aag mein jal jaata hai, par marta nahi. Jismein ek chhoti si bhool se poori ladai chhoot jaati hai.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Ret mein dabe shehar tak. Vedon ke sawalon tak. Aur pichhli baar pata chala ki is desh ne apna itihaas likha hi nahi. Par do kahaniyan bach gayin. Ramayan aur Mahabharat. Unme ek poore desh ka naqsha bana hua hai.

## Part 1: Devta jo utar kar khelte the

Nehru in kahaniyon ko padhte hain, aur saamne ek ajeeb dikkat aati hai.

Inhe sach maana jaaye, to kya hoga?

To bahut kuch bematlab lagne lagta hai. Ajeeb. Hansi ke laayak. Devta jo aadmiyon ke ghar khate hain, unse ladte hain, aur apni hi galtiyon se bhatakte hain.

Par jaise hi aap sach maanna chhod dete hain, sab badal jaata hai. Kahani ek nayi roshni mein aati hai. Kalpana ka ek bhara-poora bageecha, jiski har kyaari mein insaan ke liye koi seekh.

Yunan ki misaal saamne rakhiye. Aaj koi Olympas ke devtaon ko sach nahi maanta. Isliye hum bina kisi bojh ke unki kahaniyon ki tareef kar paate hain. Wo humari saanjhi virasat ban gaye.

Ab sochiye, agar unhe sach maanna zaroori hota. Tab wo kahaniyan ek bojh ban jaatin. Aur us bojh ke neeche unki khoobsurti dab jaati.

Nehru ke hisaab se Bharat ke puraan Yunan se bhi ameer hain. Zyada vishaal. Bahut sundar. Aur arth se bhare hue.

Phir wo us zamane mein jhaankte hain, jab ye kahaniyan bani. Zindagi bhari-poori thi, prakriti ke saath ek taal mein. Insaan hairat aur khushi se brahmaand ko dekhta tha. Aasmaan aur dharti paas-paas lagte the.

Purane Bharatiya, Yunan ki tarah, sundarta aur zindagi ke premi the. Isi bhare-poori zindagi aur rich kalpana se ye devta nikle. Tez. Khoobsurat. Aur bilkul insaan jaise.

Aur devta Kailash se neeche utar aate the. Himalaya ke apne thikaanon se. Insaanon ke saath khelne. Kabhi-kabhi unhe sazaa dene bhi.

Bilkul waise hi, jaise Yunan ke devta Olympus se utarte the.

Yunan ke devtaon ke ek bade vidvaan ne unke baare mein kuch likha tha, aur Nehru wahi baat Bharat par bhi lagaate hain. Ye devta kalakaron ke sapne the. Prateek the, kisi badi cheez ki taraf ek ishaara. Inhone kabhi aisa aqeeda nahi diya jo gyaan se takraaye. Na aisa koi hukm, jo insaan ko apni andar ki roshni ke khilaaf chalaaye.

Phir dheere-dheere ye devta peechhe hat gaye. Unki jagah kathin, gehre darshan ne le li.

Par logon ke mann mein unki tasveerein tairti rahin. Khushi mein saathi. Dukh mein dost.

Aur Nehru sochte reh jaate hain. Kaun the wo log, jinhone ye chamakte sapne gadhe? Kis khaan se unhone ye sona nikala?

Jawab kisi ek kitaab mein nahi milta. Ye kahaniyan kisi ek ne nahi likhin. Ye sadiyon tak zabaani chalti rahin — har ghar mein, har mela mein, har maa ki godh mein.

Aur Nehru ki pehli mulaqat in kahaniyon se hui ek bachche ke roop mein. Allahabad ke ek ghar mein.

## Part 2: Maa ki kahaniyan, saal ka mela

Allahabad, ek bada ghar. Shaam ho rahi hai. Ek chhota ladka baitha hai, aur uske paas uski maa — ya ghar ki koi aur badi aurat.

Kahani shuru hoti hai. Ramayan ki. Mahabharat ki.

Ye ladka Jawaharlal hai. Nehru likhte hain ki unke bachpan ki sabse purani yaadon mein yahi kahaniyan hain. Waise hi, jaise Europe ya America ka koi bachcha pariyon ki kahaniyan sunta hai. Unme saahas bhi tha, aur jaadu bhi.

Aur phir har saal ek mela. Khule maidan mein Ramayan ka khel. Hum ise Ramlila kehte hain.

[SFX: door se bheed ki gunj aur dhol, ek pal, phir dheere]

Bahut badi bheed. Juloos. Sab kuch kachcha-pakka sa. Par kisi ko parwah nahi thi. Kyunki har koi kahani zabaani jaanta tha. Aur ye tyohaar ka waqt tha.

Nehru in kahaniyon ko kabhi sach ghatna nahi maante the. Jaadu aur chamatkaar wale hisson ki to wo aalochna bhi karte the. Par unke liye ye kalpana mein utni hi sachchi thi, jitni Alif Laila. Ya Panchatantra — janwaron ki kahaniyon ka wo khazana, jisse pashchim Asia aur Europe ne bahut kuch liya.

Ladka bada hota hai. Sochiye uski kitaabon ki shelf: Akbar-Birbal ke kisse, Rajput veeron ki kahaniyan, Sherlock Holmes, Alice in Wonderland, Yunan ke devta, King Arthur ke yoddha, France ki Joan of Arc, aur Jhansi ki Rani. Sab ek ajeeb gadbad mein mil gaye.

Par in sab ke peechhe ek parda hamesha tana raha. Bachpan mein suni pauranik kahaniyan.

Ab ek baat dhyan dijiye. Ye kahaniyan zabaani chalti rahin, isliye badalti bhi rahin. Har gaon ne usme apni ek line jodi. Har peedhi ne apna ek patra joda. Isliye inka ek lekhak nahi hota. Inka ek poora desh hota hai.

Aur ye dono mahakavya kisi ek ne nahi likhe. Ye kai sau saalon mein bane, aur baad mein bhi inme jodte rahe. Kahani Aryon ke shuruaati dino ki hai, unki jeeton aur aapas ki ladaiyon ki. Par likhe ye baad mein gaye.

Nehru kehte hain ki duniya mein aisi koi kitaab unhe nahi pata, jisne aam logon ke mann par itna lagaataar asar daala ho. Sanskrit mein inhe gine-chune vidvaan padhte the. Baaki tak ye anuvaad se pahunche. Naye roopon se. Aur un hazaar raaston se, jinse parampara kisi desh ki rag-rag mein utarti hai.

Ek hi kahani, har star ke liye. Bade vidvaan ke liye bhi, aur gaon ke anpadh kisaan ke liye bhi.

Aur ye ittefaq nahi tha. Ek samaaj, jo jaatiyon mein bata tha, use isne ek saanjha ateet diya. Saanjhe veer. Jeene ka ek saanjha dhang. Ye jaan-boojh kar kiya gaya, taaki ek nazariya saari vividhta ke oopar tika rahe.

Aaj sochiye. Ramlila aaj bhi har saal hoti hai. Aur aaj bhi aage ki qataar mein baithe bachche agla dialogue abhinetaon se pehle bol dete hain.

Phir Nehru ek hisaab lagaate hain. Mere mann par itni taraf se asar pada, phir bhi ye kahaniyan neev mein bani rahin. To jo log padh-likh nahi sakte, unke mann par inka asar kitna gehra hoga?

To doosra suraag ye hai. Log apna ateet kitaab se nahi, kahani se jaante the. Par wo kahani unhe sikhaati kya thi?

## Part 3: Raakh se bacha prem

Teen chhoti kahaniyan suniye.

Pehli. Ek bageecha. Ashoka ka ek ped, jis par abhi phool nahi aaye. Ek sundar stri aati hai, aur apne pair se ped ko chhooti hai. Aur ped phoolon se bhar jaata hai.

[SFX: halki hawa, patton ki sarsaraahat, ek koyal door]

Aisi kalpanaon se Sanskrit ke kavi khelte the. Kyunki Nehru batate hain ki puraan sirf mahakavyon tak simit nahi. Wo Vedon ke zamane tak jaate hain. Aur kavi aur natakkar unhi ke ird-gird apni kahaniyan bunte the.

Doosri kahani. Kaamdev, prem ke devta. Saath mein unki patni Rati — jiske naam ka matlab hai aanand mein doob jaana. Aur unka dost Vasant, bahaar ka devta.

Ek din Kaamdev bada jokhim lete hain. Apna phoolon wala teer Shiv par chala dete hain. Shiv ki teesri aankh khulti hai. Usme se aag nikalti hai. Aur Kaamdev raakh ho jaate hain.

Par kahani yahan khatam nahi hoti. Kaamdev bach jaate hain. Bina shareer ke. Ananga ban kar — yaani jiska koi ang nahi.

Prem jala, par mara nahi. Bas dikhna band ho gaya.

Teesri kahani ek angrez lekhak ne purani Bharatiya kathaon se dobara kahi. Srishti ke kaarigar Twashtri ko stri banani thi. Par purush banate-banate saara thos maal khatam ho chuka tha.

To Twashtri ne gehra dhyan kiya. Aur phir chaand ki golai li. Bel ki lachak. Ghaas ki kanpan. Hiran ki nazar. Shahad ki mithaas. Aur baagh ki kroorta bhi. Sab ko milaya, aur stri bana di.

Teen kahaniyan. Ek bhi sach nahi. To ye sikhaati kya hain?

Nehru kehte hain, bahut kuch. Zyadatar puraan veerta ke hain. Sach par tike rehna. Diya hua vachan nibhaana, nateeja chahe jo ho. Maut tak, aur uske paar bhi, wafadaar rehna. Himmat. Achhe kaam. Sab ki bhalai ke liye apna tyaag.

Kabhi kahani poori kalpana hoti hai. Kabhi kisi sachchi ghatna ko badha-chadha kar kaha jaata hai, aur parampara use sambhaal leti hai. Sach aur kalpana aise bun jaate hain, ki unhe alag karna na-mumkin ho jaata hai.

Is mishran ko Nehru ek naam dete hain. Kalpit itihaas.

Ye nahi batata ki theek-theek kya hua. Par ek utni hi zaroori baat batata hai. Log kya maante the ki hua tha. Wo apne purvajon ko kis laayak samajhte the. Aur kaun se aadarsh unhe khinchte the.

Isliye ye kahaniyan unki zindagi ka hissa bani. Roz ki thakaan aur badsoorti se upar uthaati hui. Ek oonche raaste ki taraf ishaara karti hui, chahe wo raasta kitna bhi door ho.

Germany ke ek bade kavi bhi kuch aisa hi maante the. Rome ki ek veer stri ki purani kahani ko kuch log jhootha bata rahe the. Unhone unhe khari-khoti sunaayi. Jo cheez andar se jhoothi ho, wo sundar aur prerak ho hi nahi sakti. Aur agar Rome ke log itne mahaan the ki aisi kahani gadh sakein, to hum kam se kam use maanne laayak mahaan to hon.

To teesra suraag. Kahani sapne batati hai. Par kya sapne ek desh ka naqsha bhi bana sakte hain?

## Part 4: Do mahakavya, ek naqsha

Ab do kitaabein ek mez par rakhiye. Ye drishya kalpana hai, par dono kitaabein asli hain.

Ek patli, ek moti. Ramayan, aur Mahabharat.

Pehla sawal jo dimaag mein aata hai: ye likhi kab gayin?

Jawab milta hai: pata nahi. Nehru is par dukh nahi jatate, wo sirf sach kehte hain. Mahakavya ko tareekh dena mushkil hai, kyunki wo ek hi zamane ki cheez nahi hain.

Sochiye. Ye kahaniyan us daur ki hain jab Aryan abhi bas rahe the, apni jagah bana rahe the. Bahut door ka zamana. Uske baad kai lekhak aaye. Kai peedhiyan. Kisi ek ne poori kitaab nahi likhi. Sab ne usmein kuch joda. Aur jodne ka ye kaam rachaai ke baad bhi nahi ruka.

Phir bhi dono mein ek fark hai. Ramayan ek kasa hua kavya hai, ek hi dhaage mein bandha hua. Wo logon ki kitaab hai — gaon-gaon gai jaati hai, aur uske nayak ghar-ghar ke naam hain. Mahabharat ek vishaal bhandaar hai — parampara, katha, aur us zamane ke raaj aur samaaj ka poora shabdkosh. Ismein sab aa gaya, aur phir bhi sab kuch reh gaya.

Mahabharat itni badi hai ki wo kai kitaabon ke barabar hai. Ismein raja hai. Rishi hai. Vyapaari hai. Juaari hai. Aur ek chhoti si gaon ki aurat bhi.

Aur dhyan dijiye: ye kahaniyan likhi isliye nahi gayin ki itihaas bache. Ye likhi gayin aur suni gayin, isliye ki log unhe yaad rakhein. Ek kitaab jo yaad rakhne ke liye bani ho, uska hisaab kisi bahi-khaate se nahi milta.

Dono ek hi kaam kar rahi hain. Wo ek purane zamane ko apne andar sambhaale hue hain, aur usse aage ki peedhiyon ko saunp deti hain.

Dono mein ek baat ek si hai. Nehru ke hisaab se dono ka dhaancha Buddha se pehle ban chuka tha. Jodte log baad mein bhi rahe, par neev purani hai.

1944 mein bhi in kitaabon par kaam chal raha tha. Das saal se zyada se Bharatiya vidvaanon ki ek toli alag-alag pothiyan milaa rahi thi, taaki ek pramanik sanskaran nikle. Kuch hisse chhap chuke the. Baaki par kaam jaari tha.

Aur usi bhayanak, sab kuch nigal jaane wale yuddh ke beech, ek aur khabar aayi. Russi ke vidvaanon ne Mahabharat ka Russi anuvaad kar daala. Sochiye. Ek kitaab, jo kai sau saal se is mitti mein padi hai, ek din Russian bhasha mein bolne lagti hai.

Waise, pashchim ki nazar pehli baar in kahaniyon par nahi padi thi. Ramayan par to Nehru se koi assi saal pehle ek French itihaaskar ne likha tha. Pashchim mein sab kuch tang hai, unhone kaha. Yunan chhota hai, mera dam ghutta hai. Mujhe poorab ki taraf dekhne do. Wahan mera mahakavya hai, Hind Mahasagar jitna vishaal.

To do kitaabein mez par padi hain. Ek patli, ek moti. Par kisi ek ke bhi pehle panne par likha hua nahi hai: yahan ek desh ka naqsha hai.

## Part 5: Aryavarta se Bharatvarsha

Ab naqsha kholiye. Kalpana ka naqsha, ek bade kagaz par.

Sabse pehle uttar mein ek ghera. Uska naam hai Aryavarta. Aryon ki zameen. Ye ghera neeche Vindhya ke pahaadon tak jaata hai, madhya Bharat tak. Nehru ke hisaab se us waqt Aryan shayad un pahaadon ke paar nahi gaye the.

Ghera ka matlab hai seema. Aur seema ka matlab hai: bahar wale, aur andar wale. To ye naqsha ek saath do cheezein hai. Ek dhaancha, jo sab ko andar leta hai. Aur ek lakeer, jo batati hai ki andar kahan khatam hota hai.

Phir naqshe par ek lakeer dakshin ki taraf badhti hai. Ye Ramayan hai. Nehru ise dakshin ki taraf Aryon ke phailne ki kahani kehte hain.

Aur phir ek badi ladai. Mahabharat ka grihayuddh. Ise ek dhundhle andaaze se Isa se chaudah sau saal pehle ke aas-paas rakha jaata hai. Ladai is baat ki thi ki poore Bharat par, ya shayad uttar Bharat par, kiska raaj chalega.

[SFX: dhoop mein sookhi dharti par ghode ki taap, door se ek bheri]

Aur isi ladai ke saath naqshe par ek naya naam likha jaata hai. Bharatvarsh. Bharat ke naam par, jo is kaum ke pauranik purvaj maane jaate the.

Nehru kehte hain ki yahin se Bharat ko ek poore desh ki tarah dekhne ki soch shuru hoti hai. Mahabharat jaan-boojh kar is ekta par zor deta hai. Baar-baar, jaise koi ek hi baat samjha raha ho.

Is naqshe ki seemaayein dekhiye. Uttar-pashchim mein aaj ke Afghanistan ka ek bada hissa, jiska tab naam tha Gandhara. Isi naam se aaj ka shehar Kandahar bana. Gandhara is desh ka apna hissa maana jaata tha. Mahabharat ke mukhya raja ki rani ka naam hi tha Gandhari. Gandhara wali.

Aur naqshe ke beech mein ek raajdhani. Aaj ki Dilli ke aas-paas. Par aaj wali Dilli nahi. Wahan ke purane shehar, Hastinapur aur Indraprastha.

Ab is naqshe ko aankh se dekhiye. Uttar mein ek pahaadi seema. Beech mein ek raajdhani. Uttar-pashchim se aakar Gandhara. Aur dakshin ki taraf ek raasta, jisse Ramayan ki kahani utri. Ek desh — aur ek kahani. Dono ek hi kagaz par.

Aur ek baat. Ye naqsha kisi naqshakaar ne nahi banaya. Ise un logon ne banaya jinhone ye kahaniyan gai aur suni, peedhi dar peedhi, gaon dar gaon.

Aryavarta aur Bharatvarsh — do naam, ek zameen. Pehla naam ek kaum ka tha. Doosra naam ek desh ka. Aur aage ki sadiyon mein naye log aate rahe, nayi bhashayein aati rahin, naye dharm aate rahe. Par ye naam rah gaya — ek chhat ki tarah sab ke oopar.

Aaj sochiye. Kandahar ka naam hum khabron mein sunte hain. Aur us naam ke andar ek rani chhupi hai, jise har Bharatiya ghar jaanta hai.

Ab ek videshi aankh se dekhiye. Ek aisi mahila, jinhone Bharat ko apna ghar bana liya tha. Unhe Mahabharat mein do baatein chaunkaati hain. Itni uljhan ke beech bhi ek ekta. Aur baar-baar ek hi koshish: ek akhand Bharat ki tasveer bithaana.

Par is bade naqshe par sirf purane log nahi chal rahe the. Naye log aa rahe the. Apne rivaaz saath laa kar.

## Part 6: Paanch bhai, ek patni; badalti thaali

Ek purani shaadi ki kalpana kijiye. Ek naayika. Aur uske paanch pati. Paanch sage bhai.

Mahabharat ki ek mukhya naayika ke saath yahi hai. Hum use Draupadi ke naam se jaante hain.

Nehru yahan ek ulti baat pakadte hain. Aryon mein ek stri ke kai pati hone ka rivaaj tha hi nahi. To ye kahani mahakavya ke beech mein aayi kahan se?

Unka andaaza hai ki wo zamana tha jab bahar se naye log aa rahe the, apne rivaaz saath laa rahe the. Unme se kai Aryon se bilkul alag the. Nateeja? Ulte-seedhe vichaaron aur reetiyon ka ajeeb mel.

Dheere-dheere sab ghulne lage. Yahan ke purane log bhi, aur naye aane wale bhi. Vaidik dharm bhi badalta gaya.

Wo ek aisa roop le raha tha, jismein sab sama sakein. Yahi roop aage chal kar aaj ka Hindu dharm bana.

Ye mumkin kaise hua? Iski jad ek soch mein thi. Sach par kisi ek ka thheka nahi. Use dekhne ke kai raaste hain. Isliye ek-doosre ke ulte vishwas bhi saath jee sakte the.

Isiliye Mahabharat mein har tarah ki aastha milti hai. Vedon ke anek devta. Upanishadon ka ek hi brahm. Aur ek ishwar ki bhakti. Sab ek hi kitaab ke andar.

Aur ye kitaab sanyaas ki nahi hai. Ismein zindagi ubalti hai. Bharpoor, rang-birangi. Naitikta bahut hai, par ye sirf updesh ki kitaab nahi.

Iski ek seekh ek line mein kahi gayi hai. Jo tumhe khud bura lage, wo doosron ke saath mat karo.

Aur iska zor samaaj ki bhalai par hai. Ye Nehru ko khaas lagta hai. Kyunki aam taur par maana jaata hai ki Bharatiya mann bas apni poornata dhoondhta hai. Mahabharat kehta hai: jo samaaj ke bhale mein na ho, ya jis par baad mein sharm aaye, wo kabhi mat karo.

Kaamyaabi ka raasta bhi ye ginata hai. Sach, sanyam, tap, daan, ahimsa, aur dharm mein tike rehna. Jaati ya khaandaan nahi.

Daulat ke peechhe bhaagne walon par ek chutki bhi hai. Resham ka keeda apni hi daulat se mar jaata hai.

Aur ek line, jo kisi zinda, aage badhte samaaj ki pehchaan hai. Asantosh hi tarakki ka ankush hai.

Is daur mein jaati sakht nahi thi. Soch rachnatmak thi, kaafi had tak tark wali. Alag rehne ki bhaavna abhi kam thi.

Par phir bahar se dabaav badha. Purani vyavastha ki suraksha ko chunauti mili. Bharosa thoda ghata. Andar ki ekta ke liye sab ko ek saanche mein dhaalne ki maang uthi. Aur naye nishedh bane.

Ek misaal thaali se hai. Mahabharat mein zikr hai ki khaas mehmaan ke aage gaay ya bachhde ka maans parosa jaata tha. Pehle ye maanya tha. Baad mein ise varjit kar diya gaya.

Thaali badli, kyunki samaaj ka darr badla.

Aur ek baat aur. Ek taraf ahimsa par zor badhta ja raha hai. Doosri taraf theek usi kitaab ke beech ek aisa yuddh chal raha hai, jise dharm ki ladai kaha jaata hai. Dono saath rehte hain, aur kisi ko koi takraav nahi dikhta.

Isi Mahabharat ke beech, ek yoddha ke haath kaanpne lagte hain.

## Part 7: Sankat ki kavita

Ek maidan. Do senayein aamne-saamne. Beech mein ek rath. Aur rath par ek yoddha, jiska dhanush dheere-dheere neeche jhuk raha hai.

[SFX: rath ke pahiye ki charr-charr, door pataakon ki dhamak]

Saamne uske apne log hain. Ladna farz hai. Na ladna bhi farz lagta hai.

Ye Arjun hai. Aur yahin se Gita shuru hoti hai.

Gita Mahabharat ka hi ek hissa hai. Us vishaal natak ka ek drishya. Phir bhi apne aap mein poori hai. Saat sau shlokon ki ek chhoti si kavita.

Europe ke ek vidvaan ne kaha tha ki shayad yahi duniya ka akela sachcha darshanik geet hai.

Nehru ke hisaab se ye Buddha se pehle ke zamane mein rachi gayi. Aur tab se aaj tak iska asar kam nahi hua.

Iski ek wajah hai. Ye sankat ki kavita hai. Raajnitik sankat. Samaajik sankat. Aur sabse zyada, insaan ki aatma ka sankat. Jab mann shak mein jalta hai, jab do farz takraate hain, tab log Gita ki taraf mudte hain.

Har darshan ise apni nazar se padhta hai. Anginat teekaayein likhi gayi, aur aaj bhi likhi ja rahi hain.

Nehru ke apne zamane mein bhi. Tilak ne is par likha. Aurobindo ne likha. Gandhi ne likha. Teeno ki apni-apni vyakhya.

Aur yahan ek hairat hai. Gandhi apni ahimsa ki neev isi par rakhte hain. Aur doosre isi se nyay ki ladai mein hinsa ko sahi thehraate hain. Ek hi kitaab. Do ulte raaste.

To Gita kehti kya hai? Nehru ke padhne mein, ye karm ki pukaar hai. Zindagi ke farz poore karo. Haath par haath rakh kar baithna galat hai. Kuch karne se pehle hamesha poora nateeja maangna bhi galat hai.

Par har yug ke apne aadarsh hote hain. Gita iske liye ek shabd deti hai: yugadharma. Yaani apne waqt ka dharm.

Aur Nehru ka Bharat? Nirasha se bhara, bahut der se chup baitha hua. Isliye ye pukaar unhe khaas taur par chhooti hai. Ise aaj ki zubaan mein samaaj-seva bhi padha ja sakta hai. Desh ke liye kaam, insaaniyat ke liye kaam. Par Gita ke hisaab se uske peeche ek aatmik aadarsh hona chahiye.

Yaad hai wo baat, jo do episode pehle dabi hui thi? Poori taaqat se kaam karo, par phal se bandho mat. Wo line yahin se aati hai. Sahi karm ka sahi phal aayega hi, chahe turant na dikhe. Kyunki kaaran aur kaarya ka niyam kabhi nahi tootta.

Aur ye sandesh sabke liye hai. Brahman ke liye bhi, achhoot ke liye bhi. Gita kehti hai: saare raaste mujh tak aate hain.

Isme kuch aisa hai jo kabhi baasi nahi hota. Khoj aur dhyan. Takraav ke beech bhi santulan. Ye apne mahaul se bhaagti nahi, uske saath taal milaati hai. Dhai hazaar saal mein ye desh kitni baar bana, bigda, badla. Har baar Gita mein kuch zinda mila.

Par ek kitaab sab kuch nahi badalti. Aur usi daur mein do aise log paida hue, jo kitaab se nahi, apni zindagi se hamla kar rahe the.

## Part 8: Kaunsi jaat, kis kaam ki

Ek shehar ke bahar ki sadak sochiye. Dhoop. Dhool.

Kuch log chal rahe hain. Munde sir. Kaasaya vastra. Haath mein bhiksha-patra. Ped ke neeche baitha ek chhaatra dheere se kehta hai: ye log Ved ko nahi maante.

Bilkul. Ye uthal-puthal ka zamana tha. Mahakavyon ke daur se lekar shuruaati Bauddh daur tak. Raajneeti badal rahi thi. Log ghul-mil rahe the, aur kaam bant raha tha.

Vichaaron mein bhi halchal thi. Upanishad khud purohiton ke karmkaand ke khilaaf ek bagaavat the. Uske baad bhautikvaad aaya. Phir Jain aur Bauddh dharm. Gita ne sab ko jodne ki koshish ki. Par is sab ke peechhe ek badhta hua desh tha.

Jain aur Bauddh, dono Vaidik dharm se alag hue — haalaanki ek tarah se usi se upje bhi the. Dono isi zameen ki upj hain. Dono Vedon ka adhikaar nahi maante. Srishti ke kisi pehle kaaran ke baare mein ya to chup rehte hain, ya usse saaf inkaar karte hain. Dono ahimsa par zor dete hain. Dono ne brahmachaari bhikshuon ke sangh banaye.

Sochiye in toliyon ko. Gaon se gaon, shehar se shehar. Ek hi munder sir. Ek hi bhiksha-patra. Aur ek hi baat, jo wo har aangan mein dohraate the.

Jain dharm ki ek buniyadi seekh hai: sach is par nirbhar karta hai ki aap kahan khade hokar dekh rahe hain. Ye sakht naitik dharm hai, jo tyaag aur tapasya par zor deta hai.

Jain dharm ke sansthapak Mahavira aur Buddha ek hi zamane ke the. Aur dono Kshatriya the.

Yahin ek baat chaukane wali hai. Dono rajaon ke kul se the. Yaani jaati ki seedhi ke oonche mukhaam par baithay hue log. Jinhone sab kuch paaya hua tha.

Aur phir Buddha ne ek ajeeb kaam kiya. Unhone jaati par seedha hamla nahi kiya. Par apne sangh mein usse maana bhi nahi. Jo bhi aa gaya, wo andar aa gaya. Unki toliyon mein jaati ka koi hisaab nahi tha. Sab ek saath chalte the, ek saath baithte the. Isse jaati ki jadein kamzor hui.

Aur ek baat yaad rakhiye. Ye dono log jaati ki sabse oonchi seedhi se aate the. Vidya, hukumat, izzat — teenon unke paas thi. Phir bhi unhone us seedhi ko apna sahara nahi banaya.

Aur ek tark se pata chalta hai ki tab jaati kitni dheeli thi. Jo samaaj jaati mein jakda ho, wo samundar paar vyapaar nahi kar sakta. Na door-door apni bastiyan basa sakta hai. Par Buddha ke baad pandrah sau saal tak Bharat ka vyapaar badhta raha. Bharatiya bastiyan door-door phali-phooli.

To deewar abhi poori nahi uthi thi. Par Nehru batate hain ki uski neev mein ek aur cheez thi. Shuru mein jaati ka aadhaar rang tha.

## Part 9: Jaati se aage

Ab 1944. Nehru apne zamane ko dekhte hain, aur agle sawal par ruk jaate hain.

Sadiyon tak bade logon ne is deewar ko chetaaya. Baar-baar awaaz uthi. Par ajeeb baat ye hai ki har awaaz deewar ka ek aur hissa ban gayi.

Jain dharm ne jaati se takkar nahi li. Wo uske saath dhal gaya — aur aaj tak zinda hai. Bauddh dharm uske saath nahi dhala, aur wo is desh se laut kar nahi aaya. Isai aaye, Mussalman aaye. Dono ne jaati ke bhed se inkaar kiya. Aur dono ke andar bhi aage chal kar apni-apni jaatiyan ban gayin.

Nehru ise kismat kehte hain. Jaise ye is desh ki niyati hi ho. Jaati dheere-dheere badhi, aur usne zindagi ka har hissa pakad liya.

Aur ye kaam itna dheere hua ki kisi ek peedhi ne badalte hue bhi nahi dekha. Ye deewar roz thodi-thodi bani — jaise purane ghar ki deewar par lagta hua namm, jo poora ghar kha jaata hai.

Par isi deewar mein ek darwaza bhi tha. Neeche nayi-nayi jaatiyan banti rahin. Aur upar jo bhi jeetne wala aata, wo do-teen peedhi mein andar aa jaata. Rajputon ke kai kul Shak aur Hoon hamlavaron se nikle. Kshatriya hona khoon ka rishta nahi tha — hukumat ka.

Aur isse bhi pehle, Buddha se saat sau saal pehle, ek bahut purane vidvan Yagnavalkya ne ek baat keh di thi. Sadgun dharm se nahi aata. Na chamdi ke rang se. Use jeena padta hai.

Ab 1944. Madhyam varg mein jaati ke khilaaf kai andolan uthe. Unhone seedha hamla kiya. Kuch fark pada, par aam janta tak nahi.

Phir Gandhi aaye. Unhone purane Bharatiya dhang se, ghuma kar waar kiya. Chaar varnon ke mool siddhaant ko nahi chheda. Par uske upar ugi jhaadiyon ko kaata. Aur jaante the ki isse poori imaarat hil jaayegi. Janta ke liye ye imaarat ya to poori khadi rehti hai, ya poori girti hai.

Aur Nehru ko ek isse bhi badi taaqat dikhti hai: aaj ki zindagi khud. Unhe lagta hai ki ye purani zid ab aakhir mar kar rahegi.

Par wo ek chetaavni bhi dete hain. Jab hum yahan jaati se lad rahe hain, pashchim mein nayi, ghamandi jaatiyan khadi ho gayi hain. Nasl ki shreshthta wali. Kabhi raajneeti ke kapde pehne, kabhi arthshastra ke. Kabhi loktantra ki bhasha bolti hui.

To sawal wapas aata hai. Kis cheez ne jaatiyon aur dharmon mein bante is desh ko ek desh banaya?

Do kahaniyan.

Ramayan aur Mahabharat. Wo har jaati tak pahunchi. Har bhasha mein utrin. Har umar ke liye ek si rahin. Aur unhone Aryavarta ko Bharatvarsh ka pehla naqsha de diya.

Deewar khadi rahi. Par naqshe ne apna kaam kar diya. Hazaaron saal baad bhi, jab is desh ko kisi ek cheez par ek hona hota hai, to wo aksar inhi do kahaniyon par hota hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Nehru ke paas in dono kitaabon ke liye ek saaf niyam tha. Unhe tathya maano mat. Unhe suno. Unhe jiyo.

Aaj hum aksar ulta karte hain. Hum inhe itihaas maan lete hain, aur phir unke chhote-chhote hisson par ladte hain. Kab kis ka janm hua. Kis ne kya kaha. Aur tab kahani ka wo hissa, jo hamare liye asli tha — saanjhi mitti, saanjhe veer — kho jaata hai.

Aur ye sawal sirf purani kitaabon ka nahi hai. Aaj bhi har ghar mein koi ek aisi kahani zinda hoti hai, jo itihaas ke naam par aage badhai jaati hai.

Ramlila mein ye baat saaf dikhti hai. Wahan koi nahi poochhta ki ye hua tha ya nahi. Sab jaante hain ki ye kahani hai. Aur isiliye us raat gaon ke saare log ek cheez ban jaate hain.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aapke ghar mein kis kahani ko itihaas ki tarah sikhaya gaya? Aur kya aapne usse kabhi poochha: ye kahani hai, ya byora?

## Outro

Ab ek drishya. Bhor se pehle. Ganga ke maidan ka ek gaon. Ek naujawan zameen par baitha hai, aur ek kapde mein apni cheezein baandh raha hai. Thoda sattu. Ek dhoti. Kuch sikke.

Uske saath koi sevak nahi jaayega. Kamar par koi talwar nahi hogi. Wo paidal north-west jaayega — saikdon kos — ek aise shehar tak, jahan us zamane mein duniya padhne aati thi.

Agli baar hum usi ke saath usi raaste par chalenge.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
