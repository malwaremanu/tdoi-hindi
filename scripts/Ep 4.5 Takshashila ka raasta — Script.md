## Cold open

Ganga ke maidan ka ek gaon. Kachche ghar. Ek aangan, jismein chulha abhi-abhi jala hai.

Ek naujawan zameen par baitha hai. Saamne ek kapda bichha hai. Wo usmein cheezein rakh raha hai. Thoda sattu. Ek dhoti. Kuch sikke.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, jahan Jawaharlal Nehru qaid mein ye kitaab likh rahe hain. Is set mein hum Bharat ki jadon tak peechhe ja rahe hain. Pichhli baar devta dharti par utar kar kahaniyon mein bas gaye the, aur do mahakavyon ne jaatiyon mein bante desh ko pehla naqsha de diya tha. Usi desh ka ek raasta ab shuru hota hai.

## Part 1: Do khazane

Par ek mushkil hai. Dhai hazaar saal pehle ke kisi kisaan ne apni diary nahi chhodi. To Nehru uske baare mein jaanein kaise?

Sochiye, qile ki kothri mein unki mez. Darshan ki moti kitaabein ek taraf khiska di gayi hain. Nehru likhte hain ki is vishay par ab jakar dhyaan jaane laga hai. Kuch Bharatiya vidvaanon ne kitaabein likhi hain. Ek American ne bhi. Par kaam bahut baaki hai.

Teen jagah se roshni aati hai.

Pehli, Mahabharat. Nehru ke liye wo samaaj ki jaankari ka ek bhandaar hai — khaane-peene, kaam-dhandhe, reet-riwaj, sab kuch usmein bikhra pada hai. Bas use is nazar se, parakh kar padhna hoga.

Doosri, Kautilya ka Arthashastra. Raaj chalane par likhi ek kitaab, Isa se chaar sau saal pehle ki. Usmein Maurya samrajya ka poora dhaancha hai. Raajneeti, samaaj, paisa, fauj — aur roz ka hisaab.

Teesri, aur sabse purani, Jataka kahaniyan. Ye Buddha ke pichhle janmon ki kahaniyan maani jaati hain. Inhe aaj ka roop Buddha ke kuch baad mila. Par kahaniyan khud kahin purani hain. Wo Buddha se pehle ke Bharat ki baat karti hain.

Ek angrez vidvaan ne inhe duniya ka sabse purana aur sabse poora lok-katha sangrah kaha. Jaanwaron ki kai kahaniyan, jo baad mein pashchim Asia aur Europe tak pahunchi, unki jadein yahin milti hain.

Jataka us zamane ki hain, jab Arya aur Dravid, do badi dhaarayein, aakhri baar ghul-mil rahi thi. Aur unmein jo samaaj dikhta hai, wo bahurangi hai. Uljha hua. Kisi khaane mein fit nahi hota. Ek vidvaan ka saar ye hai, ki us daur ke liye jaati ke kisi pakke dhaanche ki baat hi nahi ki ja sakti.

Ye baat yaad rakhiye. Episode ke aakhir mein ye laut kar aayegi.

Jataka isliye bhi khaas hain. Pujaariyon ki apni parampara hai. Raajaon ki apni. Jataka teesri awaaz hain. Aam logon ki.

To chaliye, us awaaz ke saath chalte hain. Humara musafir abhi apne gaon ki seema par hai.

## Part 2: Gaon ki sabha, raja ka chhatha hissa

Gaon ke bahar ek bada sa ped hai. Uske neeche gaon ke buzurg baithe hain. Ye gaon ki sabha hai.

Musafir ek pal ruk kar sunta hai.

Sochiye, is saal baarish kam hui hai. Fasal kamzor hai. Aur ek buzurg sir hila kar kehta hai: zaroor raja mein hi koi dosh hai.

Sunne mein ajeeb lagta hai. Par Nehru likhte hain ki us zamane mein yahi maana jaata tha. Kuch bhi bigde, dosh raja ka. China mein bhi aisa hi maana jaata tha.

To raja banta kaun tha?

Shuru mein raja chuna jaata tha. Baad mein gaddi khaandaani ho gayi. Bada beta waaris. Auraton ko is haq se bahar rakha gaya, haalaanki kuch apvaad bhi milte hain.

Raja ke paas mantriyon ki ek parishad thi. Kahin-kahin ek tarah ki raajya-sabha ka bhi zikr hai. Phir bhi raja ki marzi hi chalti thi. Bas use purani maryadaon ke andar rehna padta tha. Darbaar mein ek bada purohit hota tha. Wo salaah deta tha, aur dharmik vidhiyan karwaata tha.

Aur agar raja zaalim nikle?

To log uske khilaaf uth khade hote the. Kahaniyon mein aise vidroh baar-baar aate hain. Kabhi-kabhi aise raja ko apne gunahon ke liye jaan se bhi haath dhona pada.

Jataka mein raajwanshon ki vanshavaliyan bhi milti hain, aur rajaon ke zamane ki suchiyan. Yaani ye kahaniyan sirf kisse nahi thi. Ye apne rajaon ka hisaab bhi rakhti thi.

Par gaon ke liye raja door tha. Sabha paas thi. Gaon ki sabhaon ko apne kaam khud chalane ki kaafi azaadi thi.

Tabhi raja ka ek aadmi aata hai. Bailgaadi lekar. Wo upaj mein raja ka hissa lene aaya hai.

Kitna hissa? Shayad lagbhag chhatha. Yaani har chhe bori anaaj mein se ek. Aur aksar anaaj ki shakl mein hi, sikkon mein nahi.

Ye zameen ka lagaan tha. Raja ki kamaai ka sabse bada zariya. Kyunki ye kheti ki sabhyata thi.

Ab poora dhaancha dekhiye. Iski sabse chhoti eent tha apna kaam khud chalane wala gaon. Das gaon milkar ek ikai. Sau gaon milkar ek badi ikai. Raajya ki raajneeti aur arthvyavastha, dono neeche se oopar, inhi gaon se bante the.

Manu, purane Bharat ke pehle vidhi-kaar, ke man mein raajya ek chhota sa raajya tha. Par ye soch badal rahi thi. Yahi soch aage badh kar ek vishaal samrajya tak jaane wali thi. Maurya samrajya.

Musafir potli theek karta hai, aur aage badhta hai. Uske gaon mein sab apna-apna tha. Par aage ka raasta ek shehar se guzarta hai.

Aur shehar mein sab kuch bikta hai.

## Part 3: Shehar ka bazaar

[SFX: bheed bhara bazaar, aawazon ka shor, kisi bail ke gale ki ghanti]

Ganga kinaare ka ek bada shehar. Sochiye, subah ka waqt hai. Musafir ne itni bheed pehle kabhi nahi dekhi.

Shehar ke bahar baag hain. Bade-bade bageeche, jahan log ghoomne aate hain. Phalon ke baag. Gaay-bhains ke baade. Doodh aur doodh se bani har cheez, tab bhi utni hi keemti thi, jitni aaj.

Darwaaze ke andar pehli gali phalon ki hai. Ek dukaan par aam. Agli par anjeer, angoor, kele aur khajoor. Bagal mein sabzi waale.

Aur phir phool waale. Phoolon ki itni qismein, ki unki list hi lambi hai. Ek aadmi baitha maala goonth raha hai. Phoolon ki maala tab bhi Bharat ko utni hi pyaari thi, jitni aaj.

Thoda aage ek alag gandh aati hai. Yahan maans bikta hai. Shikaar sirf shauq nahi tha. Khaane ka zariya tha. Murgi, machhli, aur sabse zyada pasand, hiran ka maans. Shehar mein machhli ke talaab the, aur qasaai-khaane bhi.

Par thaali ka asli bojh anaaj tha. Chawal, gehun, baajra, aur doosre anaaj. Ganne se cheeni nikaali jaati thi.

Kone mein ek sharaab ki dukaan bhi hai. Sharaab chawal se banti thi, phalon se bhi, aur ganne se bhi.

Ab sunaaron ki gali. Yahan khaanon se nikli dhaatuein pahunchti hain. Sona, chaandi, taamba, loha. Keemti patthar bhi: heera, laal, moonga, aur moti.

Ek vyapaari sikke gin raha hai. Sone ke, chaandi ke, taambe ke. Do vyapaari saath milkar dhandha karne ki baat kar rahe hain. Saajhedaari. Aur ek kone mein koi byaaj par karz le raha hai.

Phir kapde ki gali. Resham, oon, aur sooti kapda. Galeeche, kambal, dariyan. Peechhe ke aangan mein koi soot kaat raha hai. Koi bun raha hai. Koi rang raha hai.

Ek luhaar bhatti par hathiyaar bana raha hai. Raaj-mistri patthar, lakdi aur eent se makaan utha rahe hain.

Badhai ki dukaan par cheezon ka dher hai. Gaadi, rath, palang, sandook, aur bachchon ke khilaune. Bagal mein bent ka kaarigar chatai, tokri, pankha aur chhatri bana raha hai. Aur kumhaar? Wo to har gaon mein tha.

Ek dukaan se khushbu aati hai. Phoolon aur chandan se bane itra, tel aur shringaar ka saamaan. Paas mein dawaiyan ban rahi hain. Aur ek ajeeb baat: kabhi-kabhi murde ke shareer par lep lagaakar use surakshit bhi rakha jaata tha.

Ab chowk. Ek nat rassi par chal raha hai. Neeche ek kathputli wala apna parda taan raha hai. Ek kone mein nachne wale, gaane wale, abhinay karne wale. Mod par ek vaid kisi ki nabz dekh raha hai.

Sooraj sar par aa gaya hai, aur bazaar ka shor badh raha hai.

Ek dukaan par bhaav-taav chalta hai. Grahak munh banata hai, dukaandar hasta hai, aur phir dono kisi ek daam par ruk jaate hain.

Bheed mein dekhiye kaun-kaun hai. Gaon se aaya ek kisaan, jo apna anaaj bech kar namak aur kapda lena chahta hai. Ek naujawan, jo apne guru ke liye phool le ja raha hai. Ek maa, jo bete ke liye khilauna chun rahi hai. Aur ek boodha, jo dawa lene aaya hai.

Musafir ko lagta hai, ye shehar kisi ek badi cheez se nahi chalta. Ye hazaaron chhote-chhote kaamon se chalta hai.

Musafir ki potli halki hai. Wo yahan kuch nahi khareed sakta.

Par uske man mein ek sawal uthta hai. Ye gaadiyan, ye rath, ye khilaune, aakhir bante kahan hain?

## Part 4: Hazaar parivaron ka gaon

Shehar se thodi door ek gaon hai. Is gaon ke har ghar se ek hi awaaz aati hai. Aari ki. Basule ki. Hathaudi ki.

Ye badhaiyon ka gaon hai. Jataka kehti hain ki aise ek gaon mein hazaar parivaar the. Hazaar parivaar. Aur sab ek hi kaam mein.

Aise aur gaon bhi the. Luhaaron ka gaon. Aur doosre kaarigaron ke bhi. Ye aksar kisi shehar ke paas hote. Shehar unka maal khareedta, aur badle mein unhe zindagi ki baaki zarooratein deta.

Sochiye, shehar ke ek vyapaari ne bada order diya hai. Sau gaadiyan. Ek parivaar akela itna nahi bana sakta. Par poora gaon milkar kaam karta hai. Sahkaar se. Aur bade-bade order poore ho jaate hain.

Kaarigar akele nahi the. Unke sangh the. Aaj ki bhasha mein, guild. Ek German vidvaan ka mat tha ki aise sangh Bharat ki sanskriti ke bahut shuruaati daur se chale aa rahe the. Kuch paise ki wajah se: poonji ka behtar istemaal, lena-dena aasaan. Kuch apne varg ke haq bachane ke liye.

Jataka kehti hain ki athaarah sangh the. Par naam sirf chaar ke lete hain. Lakdi aur patthar ke kaarigar. Luhaar. Chamde ke kaarigar. Aur chitrakaar.

Mahabharat mein bhi inka zikr hai. Wahan seekh ye hai ki sangh ki raksha uski ekta mein hai.

Aur in vyapaari sanghon ka rutba itna tha, ki raja unke niyamon ke khilaaf koi kanoon nahi bana sakta tha. Raja ki fikr ki list mein pehle pujaari aate the. Aur theek unke baad, sanghon ke mukhiya.

Vyapaariyon ka mukhiya kehlata tha shreshthi. Bahut bada aadmi.

Aaj sochiye. Ye shabd ab bhi humare bazaaron mein ghoomta hai. Shreshthi se hi bana hai, seth.

Par isi gaon mein kuch aur bhi dikhta hai. Ek bade ghar mein ek daas jhaadu laga raha hai. Gharon mein daas rakhna kaafi aam tha. Par kheton mein jo log kaam kar rahe hain, wo daas nahi. Wo mazdoori par rakhe gaye hain.

Aur gaon ke bilkul kinaare, ek aadmi ek laash utha kar le ja raha hai. Koi uske paas nahi jaata. Wo chandaal hai. Murdon ko thikaane lagana uska kaam hai. Achhoot tab bhi the.

Musafir chalte-chalte sochta hai. Hazaar parivaar. Ek kaam. Ek gaon. Inke bachche bhi shayad yahi kaam karenge.

Nehru ka andaaza bhi yahi hai. Shayad isi alag rehne aur alag sangathan se jaati ki vyavastha bani aur phaili. Pehle Brahman aur raees alag hue. Phir unki dekha-dekhi kaarigaron aur vyapaariyon ke sangh bhi.

Yaad hai 4.1 ki andar ki deewar? Kaam bantte gaye, aur deewar uthti gayi. Yahan wo deewar eent-eent judti dikhti hai.

Par kya ye deewar tab tak sakht ho chuki thi? Iska ishaara ek jahaaz par milega.

## Part 5: Bharuch ka jahaaz

[SFX: nadi ka ghaat: paani ki chhapak, kheechi jaati rassiyan, naavikon ki pukaar]

Kuch din baad musafir ek nadi ke ghaat par pahunchta hai.

Ghaat par jahaaz khade hain. Badhaiyon ke haath ke bane. Bharat mein jahaaz nadiyon ke liye bhi bante the, aur khule samundar ke liye bhi.

Jataka batati hain ki Banaras se, Patna se, aur Champa se jahaaz chalte the. Champa, yaani aaj ka Bhagalpur. Ye jahaaz nadi ke raaste samundar tak jaate. Wahan se dakshin ke bandargahon tak, Lanka tak, aur Malaya tak.

Kitne bade the ye jahaaz? Jataka kehti hain ki ek jahaaz par saikdon vyapaari aur pardes basne wale log chadhte the.

Aur dakshin mein? Purani Tamil kavitayein Kaveripattinam ki baat karti hain. Kaveri nadi par basa ek bandargah, jahan duniya bhar ka vyapaar hota tha.

Mahakavyon mein bhi ek dilchasp baat hai. "Door se aane wale vyapaariyon" se jahaaz ka mehsool liya jaata tha.

Par musafir ka raasta paani ka nahi, zameen ka hai. Wo ek kaafile ke saath ho leta hai.

Uttar Bharat mein badi-badi sadkein thi. Raaste mein musafiron ke liye sarai. Aur kahin-kahin aspataal bhi.

Raat ko ek sarai mein aag ke paas kaafile ke vyapaari baithe hain. Ek vyapaari apni gathri kholta hai. Resham. Malmal. Haathi-daant ka saamaan. Itra. Gehne. Yahi to Bharat bahar bhejta tha.

Vyapaari kisse sunate hain. Kuch kaafile registan paar karke pashchim jaate hain. Bharuch tak, pashchimi samundar ke bandargah tak. Wahan se jahaaz Faras ki khaadi hote hue Babylon jaate hain. Kuch kaafile uttar jaate hain. Gandhara ki taraf, aur usse aage Madhya Asia tak.

Aur ek kissa sabse door ka hai. Misr mein, Memphis naam ke shehar mein, Bharatiya vyapaariyon ki ek basti thi. Lagbhag dhai hazaar saal pehle. Wahan Bharatiyon ke chehron wali mitti ki moortiyan mili hain. Shayad dakshin-poorab Asia ke dweepon se bhi vyapaar tha.

Kuch sadiyon baad, uttar Bharat ke ek Yunani raja, Milinda, Bauddh ban gaye. Unse judi ek kitaab mein ek jahaaz ke maalik ki misaal hai. Wo bandargah-bandargah mehsool kamaata hai, aur phir khule samundar mein nikal jaata hai. Bangal tak. China tak. Alexandria tak.

Aag ki roshni mein chehre alag-alag hain. Ek kehta hai ki usne khula samundar dekha hai. Doosra kehta hai, mujhe to sirf nadi mili. Teesra chup baitha hai, aur uski nazar apni gathri par hai.

Musafir ke liye ye kisse hain. In logon ke liye ye roz ka hisaab hai. Kitna maal. Kitna mehsool. Kaun sa bandargah. Kaunsa mausam.

Musafir aag ki taraf dekhta hai. Uski potli mein bas kuch sikke bache hain. Par ye log poori duniya naap rahe hain.

Subah kaafila uttar-pashchim mudta hai. Gandhara ki taraf. Aur wahan, pahaadon ke paas, musafir ka safar khatam hone wala hai.

## Part 6: Takshashila

Mahino baad. Aaj ke Peshawar ke paas ek shehar. Takshashila.

Musafir shehar ke darwaaze par khada hai. Dhool mein lipta. Kandhe par wahi potli.

Aur wo akela nahi hai. Uske aage ek aur ladka hai. Peechhe ek aur. Jataka aisi kahaniyon se bhari hain. Raeeson aur Brahmanon ke bete, bina naukar, bina hathiyaar, akele Takshashila padhne aate the.

Sirf Bharat ke door kono se nahi. Shayad Madhya Asia aur Afghanistan se bhi. Kyunki ye shehar raaste par tha. Sab ke liye paas.

Par itni door kyun aana? Ghar ke paas bhi to guru the.

Musafir Banaras se guzra tha. Banaras Buddha ke zamane mein bhi vidya ka purana kendra tha. Wahan guru the, unke shishya the. Alag-alag toliyan. Aur toliyon ke beech zordaar behas.

Par Banaras kabhi vishwavidyalaya jaisa nahi bana.

Aur Banaras ke paas ek hiran-van hai. Isse yaad rakhiye. Wahan Buddha apna pehla updesh denge.

Mahakavyon mein ek aur tarah ke vidyalaya baar-baar aate hain. Jangal ke vidyalaya. Kisi shehar se zyada door nahi. Kisi bade vidvaan ke chaaron or chhaatra jama hote. Kai vishay padhte. Fauji shiksha bhi. Jangal isliye, ki shehar ka shor door rahe. Zindagi anushaasan aur sanyam mein bite. Kuch saal baad chhaatra laut jaate, aur ghar-grihasthi basaate.

Takshashila in sab se alag tha. Ye vigyan ke liye mashhoor tha. Khaas taur par chikitsa ke liye. Aur kalaon ke liye bhi.

Yahan se padh kar nikalna izzat ki baat thi. Yahan ke padhe vaidon ka bada naam tha.

Musafir ko ek baat hairan karti hai. Darwaaze par koi nahi poochhta ki tum kahan se aaye ho. Gaon ka naam nahi poochha jaata. Jaati nahi poochhi jaati. Sirf ek baat poochhi jaati hai: tum kya seekhna chahte ho?

Kuch dinon mein usse samajh aata hai. Ye shehar kisi raja ka nahi hai. Ye raaste ka shehar hai. Yahan har cheez raaste se aayi hai. Anaaj. Kapda. Kitaabein. Aur log.

Kehte hain, jab bhi Buddha ki tabiyat bigadti, unke chahne wale ek mashhoor vaid ko bula laate. Wo vaid Takshashila ka padha hua tha.

Aur yahin padhe the Panini. Panini, Sanskrit ke sabse bade vyakaran-kaar.

Takshashila Buddha se pehle ka vishwavidyalaya tha. Brahman vidya ka kendra. Baad mein, Bauddh daur mein, ye Bauddh vidya ka bhi kendra bana. Poore Bharat se, aur seema ke paar se, Bauddh chhaatra yahan aane lage.

Aur aage chal kar ye Maurya samrajya ke uttar-pashchimi praant ki raajdhani banega. Ye baat bhi yaad rakhiye.

Musafir darwaaze ke andar qadam rakhta hai. Kal subah uski pehli kaksha hai.

## Part 7: Panini, shunya aur pachaas shunya

[SFX: kaksha mein dheemi sooti awaaz; kisi ke likhne ki halki kharakh]

Subah. Ek guru, aur unke saamne das-baarah chhaatra. Sochiye, guru kuch akshar likhte hain.

Ye likhaai bahut purani hai. Nehru likhte hain ki Bharat mein likhaai bilkul shuruaati zamane tak jaati hai. Patthar yug ke mitti ke bartanon par bhi Brahmi lipi mili hai. Brahmi, poore Bharat mein mili wo lipi, jisse Devanagari aur doosri lipiyan nikli.

Mohenjo-daro ki likhaai? Wo aaj bhi poori padhi nahi gayi. Wo hum 4.1 mein dekh chuke hain.

Aur aage chal kar Ashoka ke kuch shilaalekh Brahmi mein honge. Uttar-pashchim wale, Kharoshti mein. Kharoshti, isi ilaake ki ek doosri lipi.

Ab vyakaran ki baari. Panini ne apna mahaan vyakaran Isa se chhe-saat sau saal pehle likha. Wo apne se pehle ke vyakarnon ka zikr karte hain. Matlab, Sanskrit tab tak pakki ho chuki thi.

Leningrad ke ek Soviet professor ne Panini ki kitaab ko insaan ke dimaag ki sabse badi rachnaon mein gina. Aur aaj bhi Sanskrit vyakaran mein aakhri baat Panini ki hi maani jaati hai.

Ek chhoti si baat bahut kuch kehti hai. Panini Yunani lipi ka zikr karte hain. Yaani Yunaniyon se rishta Sikandar ke aane se bahut pehle ka tha.

Phir ganit ki kaksha. Purane Bharatiyon ki kuch khojein yug badalne wali thi. Shunya ka chinh. Dashamlav ki sthaan-maan paddhati. Ghataane ka chinh. Aur beejganit mein anjaan sankhya ke liye akshar.

Inki tareekh batana mushkil hai. Khoj aur uske istemaal ke beech aksar lamba waqt lagta tha. Par ankganit, beejganit aur rekhaganit ki neev bahut pehle pad chuki thi. Rig Ved ke zamane mein bhi ginti das par tiki thi.

Ab guru ek khel khelte hain. Sabse badi sankhya kaun bata sakta hai?

Yunani, Romi aur Arab hazaar tak, ya zyada se zyada das hazaar tak, naam rakhte the. Bharat mein das ke aage athaarah shunya tak ke alag-alag naam the. Aur kahaniyon mein bachche Buddha das ke aage pachaas shunya tak ke naam gina dete hain.

Doosri taraf, samay ki sabse chhoti ikai thi ek second ka lagbhag satrahvaan hissa.

Ye sab zyadatar darshan ke liye tha, roz ke kaam ke liye nahi. Par isse ek baat dikhti hai. Ye log bade paimane par sochte the. Inki kahaniyon mein yug karodon saal lambe the.

Isliye Darwin ke vichaar ne Europe ko hila diya. Wahan ka aam aadmi duniya ko kuch hazaar saal purani maanta tha. Bharat mein koi uthal-puthal nahi hui.

Aur ek kaksha aisi bhi thi jahan shareer padha jaata tha. Dawa ki apni kitaabein thin, aur aspataal bhi. Dhanwantari ko us vidya ka purana sthapak maana jaata hai. Aur aage chalkar do bade naam yahan padhaye jaate hain. Charak, jo dawa ke the. Aur Sushruta, jo shalya-chikitsa ke. Unki kitaabon mein rog, unki pehchaan, aur ilaaj ka poora hisaab hai. Shalya-kriya, prasav, aahaar, safai, bachchon ko doodh pilana. Aur shalya seekhne ke liye murde khol kar dekhna bhi shamil tha. Isi parampara se kuch sadiyan pehle jaanwaron ke aspataal bhi bane. Shayad ahimsa ke asar se.

Aur kaksha ke bahar bhi naap-jokh thi. Arthashastra mein us zamane ke baat aur maap darj hain. Yaad hai wo bazaar? Wahan baaton ki baqayda jaanch hoti thi.

Ek baat aur samajhiye. Haathi sikhaane ki kitaab, ghode paalne ki kitaab, har ek ko shastra kehte the. Aaj ye shabd dharm-granth jaisa lagta hai. Tab ganit se lekar naach tak, har vidya shastra thi. Dharm aur duniya ke gyaan ke beech koi pakki lakeer nahi thi.

Par is kaksha mein ek kami thi. Saare chhaatra ladke the.

## Part 8: Aurat, daas, aur ek galat Yunani

Shaam ko musafir guru ke ghar jaata hai. Sochiye, ghar ki ek aurat kisi shlok ka arth samjha rahi hai. Musafir chupchaap sunta hai. Wo use apne guru se kam nahi lagti.

Takshashila mein ladkiyon ke padhne ka koi zikr nahi milta. Kisi bhi purane vishwavidyalaya mein nahi. Par vidushi auraton ka zikr baar-baar aata hai. To kahin na kahin wo padhti thi. Aur baad ke zamanon mein kai badi vidushiyan hui.

Ab kanoon kya kehta tha?

Manu, wahi pehle vidhi-kaar, ke hisaab se aurat hamesha kisi par nirbhar thi. Bachpan mein pita par. Shaadi ke baad pati par. Phir bete par. Kanoon ki nazar mein wo lagbhag ek sampatti thi.

Par mahakavyon ki kahaniyan kuch aur batati hain. Ye kanoon sakhti se laagu nahi hota tha. Ghar mein aur samaaj mein aurat ka sammaan tha. Aur Manu khud ek jagah kehte hain: jahan aurat ka maan hota hai, wahan devta baste hain.

Nehru saaf kehte hain ki aaj ke paimane se ye haalat buri thi. Par purane Yunan se, Rome se, aur madhyakaal ke Europe ke Church ke kanoon se kahin behtar. Aur ye fark unneesvi sadi ki shuruaat tak bana raha.

Phir guru ke ghar ek vyapaari aata hai. Shayad wahi kaafile wala. Wo apne saajhedaar ke saath hisaab ki baat kar raha hai.

Iske bhi niyam the. Manu saajhedaari ki baat zyadatar pujaariyon ke liye karte hain. Yagnavalkya, ek aur purane vidhi-kaar, vyapaar aur kheti ko bhi jodte hain. Aur baad ke ek lekhak, Narada, saaf hisaab dete hain. Jitni poonji, utna munaafa. Utna hi nuksaan.

Ab ek Yunani ki galti.

Megasthenes, Bharat aaya ek Yunani doot. Usne likha ki Bharat mein gulaami hai hi nahi.

Wo galat tha. Gharon mein daas the. Humne Part 4 mein ek dekha bhi. Us zamane ki Bharatiya kitaabon mein daason ki haalat sudhaarne ki baatein bhi milti hain.

Par Megasthenes ki galti samajh mein aati hai. Yahan bade paimane ki gulaami nahi thi. Gulaamon ke jhund nahi the, jinse zanjeeron mein majdoori karwaayi jaaye. Jabki us zamane ke kai deshon mein ye aam tha.

Ek niyam bhi tha: kisi Arya ko kabhi gulaam nahi banaya jaayega. Aur Arya kaun? Tab tak iska matlab dhundhla sa ho chuka tha. Chaaron varn, Shudra bhi. Par achhoot nahi.

China ke Han raajvansh ke shuruaati daur mein bhi yahi tasveer thi. Daas gharon mein the, kheton mein nahi, aur gine-chune the. Yahi Bharat aur China ko Yunan aur Rome se bahut alag karta tha.

Musafir ko gaon ke kinaare wala wo aadmi yaad aata hai. Laash uthaane wala. Chaaron varn ek taraf. Aur wo, sab se bahar.

Ye lakeer aayi kahan se?

## Part 9: Guru aur shishya

Us zamane mein ek nayi baat poore uttar Bharat mein phail rahi thi. Ek naya dharm, jo shuru hua ek Kshatriya aandolan ke roop mein: raja aur purohit ki takkar. Zyadatar Brahman uske khilaaf the, par wo aam logon tak pahunch raha tha. Aur dhai sau saal baad ek samraat uske saamne sir jhukayega.

Par us samraat se pehle ek samrajya khada hona tha — aur wo isi shehar se shuru hua.

Sochiye, wahi Takshashila. Kakshaon ke beech do aadmi ghoom rahe hain: ek naujawan, aur ek buzurg Brahman. Dono Magadha se aaye the. Wahan ka raajya tha Nanda vansh, raajdhani Pataliputra. Dono nikaale gaye the, aur dono yahan is seema ke shehar mein panaah le kar baithe the.

Yahan unhone wo log dekhe jo Sikandar apne peechhe chhod gaya tha — Yunani sainik, is door-poorab ke shehar mein pahraa de rahe the. Aur naujawan ne Sikandar ko khud dekha tha. Uske andar ek chahat jaagi. Waisa hi banna hai.

Phir khabar aayi. 323 BC. Sikandar Babylon mein mar gaya.

Bas usi pal unhone wahi purana naara lagaya, jo har zamane mein naya hota hai — nationalism.

Yunani fauj ka thikana ujaad diya gaya, Taxila chhin liya gaya. Aur jab desh ki awaaz ne saath diya, to sahayak bhi milte gaye. Naujawan poore uttar Bharat ko paar karke Pataliputra pahuncha — Sikandar ki maut ke do saal ke andar wo shehar uske haath mein tha.

Us naujawan ka naam tha Chandragupta. Us buzurg ka naam Chanakya — jise Kautilya bhi kehte hain.

Ab Sikandar ka senapati aata hai — Seleucus. Usne Sindhu paar kar ke apna raaj dobara jamane ki koshish ki, par wo haar gaya. Haarke usne Afghanistan ka ek bada hissa, Kabul aur Herat tak, Chandragupta ko de diya — aur apni beti bhi byaah di.

Ab samrajya dekhiye. Arab saagar se Bengal ki khaadi tak, aur uttar mein Kabul tak. Sirf dakshin Bharat chhoot gaya. Itihaas mein pehli baar itna bada raajya ek jagah se chalta tha.

Aur Maurya sena ke paas ek cheez thi jo doosron ke paas nahi thi — trained jangi haathi, jinke aage Nehru aaj ke tanks rakhte hain. 302 BC mein Seleucus ne apne yuddh ke liye Bharat se paanch sau haathi liye.

Par samrajya ek aadmi se nahi chalta.

Chanakya ke baare mein ek purane Sanskrit natak se ek tasveer nikalti hai. Bahadur aur chaalbaaz. Ek chhoti si beizzati bhi kabhi nahi bhoolta. Par samrajya ki lagaam uske haath mein thi, aur wo samraat ko apna maalik nahi, pyaara shishya maanta tha.

Log usse Bharat ka Machiavelli kehte hain, par wo usse kahin bada aadmi tha. Jeet sirf talwar se nahi hoti, aur dushman ko mitane se behtar hai usse jeet lena. Usne apni aakhri jeet dushmanon ke beech matbhed se haasil ki — aur jeet ke usi pal Chandragupta ko udaar rehne ki salaah di.

Aur uski apni zindagi? Bilkul saral — jis din usne apna wada poora kiya, us din wo sant ki tarah baith jaana chahta tha.

Us samrajya ke rishte Yunani duniya se bhi the, aur inki neev thi dono taraf ka vyapaar. Central Asia ki nadi Oxus usi zanjeer ki ek kadi thi — usi se Bharatiya maal Caspian aur Black Sea hote hue Europe pahunchta tha. Arthashastra mein ek zikr bhi hai: raja ke astabal mein "Arabi ghode" khade the.

## Part 10: Gaata-naachta desh

[SFX: door se dhol aur manjeere, gaane ki awaaz, hansi; utsav ki raat]

Takshashila mein utsav ki raat hai. Sochiye. Mashaalein jal rahi hain. Dhol baj raha hai. Log gaa rahe hain, naach rahe hain. Galiyon mein bheed hai — chhaatra, kaarigar, vyapaari, aur door-door se aaye musafir. Kisi ne aisa utsav pehli baar dekha hoga. Kisi ke liye ye roz ka kaam hai.

Musafir ek kone mein baith kar apni potli kholta hai. Sattu kab ka khatam. Sikke lagbhag khatam. Bas wo dhoti bachi hai. Aur maa ka wo haath, jo sir par tha.

Wo itni door aaya hai. Kai sau kos. Usne nadiyan dekhi, bazaar dekhe, jahaaz dekhe, dukaanon par bhaav-taav dekha. Ab wo ek aise shehar mein baitha hai jahan duniya bhar se log padhne aate hain.

Wo log kaise the?

Nehru maante hain ki itne door aur itne alag zamane ki kalpana mushkil hai. Phir bhi bikhri jaankari se ek tasveer ubharti hai.

Hanste-khelte log. Khud par bharosa. Apni parampara par garv. Rahasya ki khoj mein dilchaspi. Prakriti aur zindagi se sawal hi sawal. Apne banaye mulyon ki kadar. Zindagi ko aasaani aur khushi se jeena. Aur maut ka saamna bina zyada fikr ke.

Aur ye sab un logon ka hai, jinhe itihaas aksar bhool jaata hai.

Arrian, Sikandar ke uttar Bharat abhiyan ka Yunani itihaaskar, is mizaaj ko dekh kar hairaan tha. Usne likha: gaane aur naachne ka itna shauqeen koi aur desh nahi.

Aur Arrian ne ye kisi ek raat ke baare mein nahi likha. Usne poore desh ke baare mein likha — usi desh ke baare mein, jiske raaste bazaar se, kaarigaron ke gaon se, ghaat se hokar Takshashila tak jaate the.

Is safar mein musafir ne kisi raja ka darbaar nahi dekha. Usne dekhi gaon ki sabha, dekhe dukaan ke daam, dekhi guild ki ekta, dekha ghaat ka mehsool. Aur inhi cheezon se ek desh chalta tha.

To raja aur rishi ko ek taraf rakh dein, to aam Bharatiya kaise jeeta tha?

Gaon ki apni sabha mein. Kaarigaron ki guild mein. Door tak jaate raaston par. Kaam aur vyapaar mein doobe, gaate-naachte log.

Aur inhi raaston par dhai hazaar saal baad ek samrajya khada ho gaya.

Musafir potli baandhta hai. Ek din wo ghar lautega. Ek vaid banke, ya ek vidvaan banke. Uske paas sikke kam hain, par ek raasta yaad hai — wahi raasta jo usse yahan laya tha. Bazaar se. Ghaat se. Gaon se hokar.

Aur dhol bajta rehta hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Is episode mein ek ajeeb jodi dikhi. Kanoon ki kitaab kehti thi: aurat hamesha kisi par nirbhar. Aur kahaniyan dikhati thi ek sammaanit aurat, aur vidushiyan. Megasthenes ne bhi kanoon nahi, zindagi dekhi, aur aadhi baat samjha.

Aaj bhi ye fark zinda hai. Kanoon mein barabari likhi hai. Ghar mein kya hota hai, wo alag kahani hai. Kabhi kanoon aage hota hai, kabhi kahani. Aur samaaj aksar dono ke beech kahin jeeta hai.

Aur ise ek line mein samjha ja sakta hai. Kanoon badalta hai. Aadat dheere badalti hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapko kisne zyada gadha — jo kanoon mein likha tha, ya jo ghar mein dekha?

## Outro

Takshashila ka musafir ab hum se vida leta hai. Uske haath mein wahi potli hai, par ab uske andar kuch aur hai.

Par Banaras ke paas wo hiran-van yaad hai? Wahan ek aadmi, jo kabhi rajkumar tha, apni pehli baat kehne wala hai.

Aur dhai hazaar saal baad, ek jail mein, ek aur aadmi theek usi ke janmdin par uske baare mein likhne baithta hai.

Agli baar, usi hiran-van se, mutthi bhar patte, aur ek talwar.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
