## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat par kitaab likh raha hai. Pichhli baar hum ek naujawan ke saath paidal Takshashila pahunche the, aur lautte-lautte ek samrajya khada ho gaya tha — Chandragupta ka, jise chalane wale the Chanakya. Us safar ke aakhir mein Banaras ke paas ek hiran-van ka zikr tha. Wahan ek aadmi apni pehli baat kehne wala tha. Wahi aadmi aaj humare saamne hai.

## Part 1: Ek kitaab mein poora raaj

Saal hai 321 BC. Naya samrajya Kabul se lekar Bengal ki khaadi tak phaila hua hai.

Ab sawal ye hai. Itna bada raaj chalaya kaise jaata tha?

Uska jawab ek kitaab mein hai. Arthashastra — sarkar chalane ki vidya, jise Chanakya ne likha. Ye koi chhoti pustak nahi hai. Ye ek samrajya ka poora naqsha hai.

Sabse oopar ek autocracy thi — yaani saari taqat ek aadmi ke haath mein. Par uske neeche, shehron aur gaon ki ikaiyon mein, bahut local azaadi thi, aur wahan ke chune hue buzurg apne kaam khud dekhte the. Ye azaadi itni keemti thi ki koi raja usme haath daalne se pehle sau baar sochta tha.

Par centre ki hukumat ka haath lamba tha. Nehru likhte hain, kuch tarah se ye Maurya raajya aaj ki dictatorship ki yaad dilata hai. Par phir wo sacchai jodte hain: us zamane ki sirf-kheti wali arthvyavastha mein aaj jaisa control mumkin hi nahi tha. Aur ye raajya isse bahut aage jaata tha — sirf ek police state naam ki cheez bhi nahi thi.

Bureaucracy ghani aur sakht thi, aur jasoosi ke zikr baar-baar aate hain. Kheti par niyam the, byaaj ki dar par niyam the. Aur nirikshan — khane par, bazaaron par, karkhanon par, qassai-khane par, maveshi paalne par, paani ke haq par, khel par, adalaton par. Tol-naap theek the. Khane mein milaawat karne wale ko sakht sazaa milti thi. Vyapaar par kar tha. Aur mandir ka paisa bhi zabt ho sakta tha.

Baaki cheezein? Safai, aspataal, aur bade kendron par hakeem. Vidhwaon, anaathon, beemar aur kamzor logon ki madad. Aur aakaal — us ke liye hukumat ke godaam ka aadha anaaj hamesha bacha kar rakha jaata tha.

Ab Arthashastra ke vishay dekhiye. Raja ke kartavya. Mantri. Mantri-parishad. Vibhaag. Kootniti. Jang aur shanti. Ek fauj, jismein chaar ang the — paidal, ghudsawar, rath, hathi. Par Chanakya ek baat jodte hain: anushasan ke bina badi fauj bojh ban jaati hai.

Aur phir wo kitaab rok-roj ki zindagi par aa jaati hai. Vyapaar. Adalat. Kanoon. Shaadi. Talaq. Auraton ke haq. Kar. Khanan. Kaarigar. Bazaar. Seechai. Naav aur jahaz. Janganana. Paasport. Jail.

Vidhwa ka doosra vivaah wahan maanya tha. Aur talaq bhi — kuch suraton mein.

Ek chhota-sa zikr China ka bhi hai. Chinapatta — yaani China ka resham. Aur kitaab China ke resham aur Hindustan ke resham mein farq batati hai. Itna hi: chauthi sadi BC tak China se vyapaar chalu tha.

Ab us kitaab ki sabse ajeeb baat. Raja ke liye ek shapath.

Taj pehnate waqt raja ko kehna padta tha: agar main tum par zulm karun, to mujhe swarg se haath dhona pade — jeevan se bhi, aur santaan se bhi.

Aur aage likha hai: raja ki khushi apni praja ki khushi mein hai. Jo raja ko achha lage par praja ko na lage, wo achha nahi.

Jo raja urja wala ho, uski praja bhi urja wali hogi. Aur lok-kalyan ka kaam raja ke aaram par nahi ruk sakta tha.

Aur agar raja galat kare? To logon ke paas haq tha ki wo use hata dein, aur doosra baitha dein.

Sarkar ka ek poora vibhaag seechai ke nahron ka tha. Ek doosra bandargah, ghaat, pul aur naav. Inhi naavon se maal Burma tak jaata tha. Aur ek navy bhi thi.

Nehru ka nateeja? Ye ek samrajya tha — par ek aisi kitaab ke saath, jismein aam aadmi ki fikr likhi hui thi.

Aur us samrajya ke saare shehron mein sabse bada shehar, aankhon se kaisa dikhta tha?

## Part 2: Lakdi ka shehar

Us shehar ke dwar par ek pardesi khada hai. Megasthenes — Sikandar ke senapati Seleucus ka bheja doot, jo Pataliputra aaya aur use apni aankhon se dekha. Use shehar ke bahar wali khai par khada kijiye.

Uski nazar deewar par jaati hai. Lakdi ki deewar. Usme jhiriyan hain — teer chalane ke liye. Deewar par burj hain, aur dwar. Saikdon burj, aur chausath dwar.

Shehar do nadiyon ke sangam par baitha hai — Ganga aur Sone. Aaj ka Patna. Kitna bada? Kareeb nau meel lamba, aur lagbhag do meel chauda.

Aur saamne wali khai sirf suraksha ke liye nahi hai. Usme shehar ki gandagi bhi girti hai.

Deewar hi nahi — ghar bhi lakdi ke hain. Iske peeche ek soch thi: bhookamp. Wo ilaaka bhookamp ke liye mashhoor tha. Aur 1934 mein aaye Bihar ke bade bhookamp ne aaj bhi ye baat yaad dila di.

Aur kyunki sab lakdi ka tha, aag se bachne ke niyam sakht the. Har ghar mein seedhi, aankde, aur paani se bhari matke.

Par shehar ki sabse badi baat deewar nahi hai.

Shehar mein ek nagar-sabha thi — logon ne chuni hui. Tees sadasya. Chhe samitiyaan, paanch-paanch log ek samiti mein.

Ek samiti karigari aur karkhane dekhti hai, ek janm-mrityu ki ginti, ek banayi hui cheezon ki nigrani, ek yaatriyon aur teerth-yatriyon ke intezaam ki. Baaki do bhi apna-apna kaam.

Aur poori sabha? Paisa, safai, paani, sarkari imaarat, aur baag.

Aaj sochiye. Shehar ki safai, paani aur bazaar ka kaam aaj bhi ek chuni hui sabha dekhti hai. Do hazaar saal pehle wahi kaam isi tarah bantta tha.

Samrajya ke door-door ke hisse sadakon se jude the, aur raaston mein musafiron ke liye aaraam-ghar the. Sabse badi sadak rajdhani se uttar-pashchim ki sarhad tak jaati thi. Pardesi vyapaariyon ka khaas dhyan rakha jaata tha.

Vyapaar kitna door tak? Misr mein mummy ko Hindustan ki muslin mein lapeta jaata tha, aur kapde par yahan ka indigo chadta tha. Kaanch bhi mila.

Yahan luxury badh rahi thi — saraayein, bhojan-ghar, jua-khana. Aur manoranjan? Naachne wale, gaane wale, nat — gaon-gaon tak jaate the. Arthashastra ke likhne wale ko ye pasand nahi tha, kyunki ek aam manch zindagi ko khaych deta hai. Par tyohaar par help karne se inkaar karne wale par jurmana lagta tha. Raja apni taraf se manch banwata tha — naatak, mukka-yuddh, jaanwar-chaudharon ke muqable. Tyohaar par raaton ko galiyaan roshan hoti thin.

Megasthenes ek chhoti baat bhi note karta hai. Bharat ke log sajne-dhajne aur sundarta se pyaar karte the. Wo joote tak batate hain, jo aadmi ki lambai badha de.

Aur isi samrajya ke ek aadmi ne ek din ek faisla liya. Usne use patthar par khudwakar duniya ko suna diya.

## Part 3: Bagi rajkumar

Par wo aadmi paida kaise hua? Usse pehle wo ek naujawan rajkumar tha.

Paanch sau saal Isa se pehle, uttar Bharat mein ek chhota raajya hai. Uske rajkumar ka naam hai Siddhartha.

Ghar mein sab kuch hai. Mahal. Baag. Aaram.

Par ye naujawan khush nahi hai. Uske andar kuch aur chal raha hai. Nehru likhte hain ki wo andar ki badi ladaiyan lada — aur un ladaiyon ka koi gawah nahi tha.

Phir ek din wo mahal chhod deta hai. Apne shareer ko satata hai. Bhookha rehta hai.

Aur ek ped ke neeche baithkar usse wo cheez mil jaati hai jise wo dhoondh raha tha. Us din ke baad wo Siddhartha nahi rehta. Buddha ban jaata hai.

Wo assee saal ki umar tak jeete hain. Aur book kehti hai ki unka dehaant 544 BC mein hua — usi saal se Buddhist era shuru hota hai. Itihaas-kar pehle 487 BC kehte the. Par ab wahi log purani tareekh ko zyada sahi maan rahe hain.

Buddha ek Kshatriya the — yaani usi varg se, jismein raja aur yoddha aate hain. Aur usi varg ka ye aadmi khada hokar pujariyon ke khilaaf bolta hai.

Wo lok-priya dharm par hamla karta hai. Andhvishwas par. Karmkand par. Aur us pujari-varg par, jiske saath in sab ka apna swarth juda hua tha.

Wo metaphysics chhodta hai. Chamatkar. Aasman se aaye sandesh. Devtaon aur bhoot-pret se lena-dena.

Aur uske peeche sirf daleel nahi thi; ek zinda shakhs tha — ek aisa aadmi, jiski yaad logon ke mann mein unke jaane ke baad bhi taaza rehti thi. Uska sandesh purana tha, aur us zamane ke logon ke liye ekdum naya. Usne padhe-likhe logon ki kalpana pakadi, aur aam logon ke dil tak utar gaya.

Uske sahare teen cheezein hain: tark, anubhav, aur naitikta. Uske dekhne ka dhang hai mann ka vishleshan — ek mann, jismein aatma naam ki koi cheez nahi.

Nehru kehte hain ki uski poori baat aisi hai, jaise band kamre ki basi hawa ke baad pahadon se aayi taazi hawa.

Buddha ki kahani ne unhe bachpan se khincha. Ek angrez ki likhi lambi kavita unki pasandeeda kitaab thi.

Bade hokar wo apne praant mein Buddha se judi jagahon tak, kabhi-kabhi raasta ghuma kar, jaate the.

Kahan paida hue — Nepal ki sarhad ke paas. Kahan baithkar unhe gyaan mila — Gaya, us Bodhi ped ke neeche. Kahan unhone pehla updesh diya — wahi hiran-van, jiska zikr pichhli baar tha. Aur kahan unhone aankhein hamesha ke liye band kar lin.

Par asli sawal yahi hai. Us zindagi mein unhone kaha kya?

Aur ek baat to aur bhi ajeeb thi. Buddha chahte the ki log unki baat maanein hi nahi. Pehle parkhein.

## Part 4: Sona aag mein

Kalpana kijiye ek sunaar ki dukaan.

Aag jal rahi hai. Ek aadmi sone ka tukda aag mein pakad kar rakhta hai. Wo use tapata hai, thanda karta hai, phir dekhta hai ki andar asli sona bacha kya.

Buddha apne raaste ke liye yahi tasveer dete hain. Unka kehna tha: meri baat ko sirf shraddha se mat maano. Pehle parkho, jaise sone ko aag mein parkha jaata hai.

Aur phir wo apni baat rakhte hain.

Hazaar aadmiyon ko yuddh mein harane se bada vijeta wo hai jo khud ko jeet le.

Aadmi jaati se neech ya Brahman nahi banta. Wo apne karm se banta hai.

Aur agar kisi ne paap kiya hai, to uske ghaav par namak kaun chhidkega?

Aur jeet? Jeet nafrat ko janm deti hai. Kyunki hara hua aadmi dukhi rehta hai.

Wo ek aur cheez kehte hain, jo us zamane mein sabse ajeeb thi. Wo apne shishyon ko har disha mein bhejte hain aur kehte hain: jao, aur logon ko batao ki neech aur oonch, gareeb aur ameer — sab ek hain. Aur is dharm mein saari jaatiyan aise milti hain, jaise nadiyaan samundar mein.

Aur ek line, jise aaj bhi log dohraate hain. Is duniya mein nafrat se nafrat kabhi khatam nahi hoti. Nafrat prem se khatam hoti hai.

Gusse ko dayaa se jeeto. Burai ko bhalai se.

Ye sab unhone kaha — bina kisi dharm ki chhaya, bina bhagwan ka zikr, bina doosri duniya ka laalach.

Wo kehte hain: sach apne mann mein dhoondo. Raah tark par chalti hai, anubhav par chalti hai. Aur agyaan saare dukh ki jad hai.

Aur Bhagwan? Kya wo hai, kya nahi?

Buddha iska jawab dete hi nahi. Na haan, na na. Jahan gyaan mumkin nahi, wahan hamara faisla latka rehna chahiye.

Ek shishya ne poocha: aap is absolute ki baat kyun nahi karte? Jawab saaf tha. Agar "absolute" ka matlab koi aisi cheez hai, jo har jaani hui cheez se bahar hai, to uska hona kisi jaane hue tark se saabit nahi ho sakta. Poori duniya, jaise hum use jaante hain, aapas ke rishton ka jaal hai. Is liye hum wahan tak chalte hain, jahan tak dekh sakte hain.

Aur aatma? Is par bhi wo chup rehte hain — na inkaar, na ikraar. Nehru kehte hain, ye us zamane mein hairat ki baat thi, kyunki us waqt Bharat ka mann aatma, poorv-janm aur parlok se bhara hua tha.

Iske badle wo ek aur cheez par zor dete hain: niyam. Kaaran aur nateeja. Ek aisa niyam, jo sab jagah chalta hai.

Aur mann? Mann shareer ka hissa hai. Aadmi man ki halaton ka guchha hai. Self ek dhaara hai — vichaaron ki dhaara.

Nadi behti rehti hai. Har pal wo wahi dikhti hai, par paani badalta rehta hai. Aag bhi wahi — lau dikhti wahi hai, par har pal wahi lau nahi hoti. Sab kuch behta rehta hai.

Nehru ek hairat ki baat likhte hain. Ye soch aaj ki physics ke naye khayalon ke itna kareeb kyun hai?

Aur ek line mein unhone sab rakha: hum jo kuch hain, wo humare sochne ka nateeja hai.

Par ek sawal bacha hua hai. Agar sach yahan hai, to dukh ka jawab kya hai?

## Part 5: Chaar samundar se zyada aansu

Buddha ka jawab ek ginti se shuru hota hai. Aansuon ki ginti se.

Wo apne shishyon se kehte hain: is lambi yatra mein tum bahut roye ho. Tumhara jo hissa tha, wahi chhinta raha. Aur jise tum pyaar karte the, wo tumhara nahi tha.

Aur us dukh mein tumne itne aansu bahaye hain, ki chaaron bade samundaron ka saara paani kam pad jaaye.

Chaaron samundar. Aur ek aadmi ke aansu.

Buddha yahi dukh le kar aate hain, aur uske chaar hisse karte hain.

Pehla: dukh hai. Ye sach hai.

Doosra: dukh ka ek kaaran hai — hamari galat ichha.

Teesra: ye dukh ruk bhi sakta hai.

Chautha: aur rukne ka ek raasta hai.

Is aakhri jagah ka naam hai nirvana.

Nirvana — log ise bujh jaana samajhte hain. Jaise ek deepak bujh jaye. Buddha ne ise mana kiya. Unka kehna tha: bujhti to galat ichha hai, par iska matlab mit jaana nahi. Wo ek tez kriya hai.

Aur dikkat ye hai ki hum ise kya kahein. Hamari bhasha iske liye bani hi nahi. Isliye hum ise sirf "aisa nahi, waisa nahi" keh sakte hain.

Ab raasta kya hai? Do kinaare, aur beech mein ek raasta.

Ek kinaara hai khane-peene aur bhog-vilas mein doob jaana. Doosra kinaara hai shareer ko tarasa kar thukra dena. Buddha ne khud doosra kinaara chuna tha. Wo bhookhe rehte, shareer satate. Aur wahi anubhav sikhata hai: jo apni shakti kho chuka hai, wo sahi raah par nahi chal sakta.

Isliye unka raasta beech ka raasta hai. Aath "sahi" ka raasta — sahi vishwas, sahi ichha, sahi bhasha, sahi karm, sahi kamai, sahi koshish, sahi dhyan, sahi thahraav.

Yahan koi devta kuch nahi deta, koi kripa nahi barasti. Sab kuch ek cheez par tika hai: apne aap ko sudhaarna.

Aur agar koi is raah par chalta hua khud ko jeet leta hai, to uske baad kuch nahi bachta. Buddha ki apni ek line hai: us aadmi ki jeet ko devta bhi haare mein nahi badal sakta, jo khud ko jeet chuka ho.

To jawab mil gaya? Dukh hai, kaaran hai, ant hai, raasta hai.

Par ek baat hai jo Buddha ne jaan-boojh kar nahi batayi. Aur jab unse poochha gaya, to unhone ek mutthi bhar patte utha liye.

## Part 6: Mutthi bhar patte

[SFX: sookhe patte — ek mutthi, halke se girte hue]

Sharad ka mausam hai. Patte jhad rahe hain.

Buddha ke haath mein sookhe patte hain. Wo apne sabse pyaare shishya se poochhte hain.

Ananda — Buddha ka sabse pyaara shishya. In patton ke alawa, aur patte hain?

Ananda kehte hain: bhagwan, patte har taraf gir rahe hain. Inhe gina nahi ja sakta.

Phir Buddha kehte hain: bilkul waise hi, maine tumhe mutthi bhar sach diye hain. Inke alawa hazaaron sach aur hain, jinhe gina nahi ja sakta.

Ek mutthi bhar. Baaki sab wahi sach the, jo wo ji sakte the, keh nahi sakte the.

Ab kahani se hatt kar, ek aadmi apni aankhon se ye sab dekh raha hai.

Wahan unhone bahut kuch dekha jo unhe achha nahi laga. Buddha ko devta bana diya gaya tha, aur uske bade-bade putle mandiron mein khade the. Us aadmi ki saaf-suthri baat ke oopar vivaad, karmkand, canon law aur jaadu ki moti parat chadh gayi thi. Bahut se bhikshu agyaani the, ghuroor rakhte the, aur apni chadar ke liye bhi jhukwan maangte the. Har desh ne apne riwaz dharm par thop diye the.

Par kuch vihaaron aur unse jude schools mein unhe ek shaant, adhyayan mein doobi hawa mili. Chehron par sukoon tha, ek gambhirta, ek halki doori — duniya ki fikron se.

Aur unke mann mein sawal utha: kya ye zindagi ke saath chalta hai? Ya ye usse bhaagne ka raasta hai?

Nehru ka jawab imaandaar hai. Buddha ka udasi-bhara rukh unke swabhav se match nahi karta tha. Zindagi se mooh modna unhe pasand nahi thi.

Wo andar se kisi hadd tak ek "pagan" hain — zindagi ki bharpoori unhe pasand hai, aur jhagde se wo bahut peeche nahi hat-te.

Kya Buddha ka raasta nakaaraatmak tha? Bahut log kehte hain.

Par Nehru kehte hain: Buddha ke baare mein sochte hue mujhe aisa kuch mehsoos nahi hota. Aur jis dharm ki jadein sirf nirasata mein hoti, wo karod logon ko itne mazbooti se bandh nahi sakta tha. Aur un logon mein apne zamane ke sabse pratibha-shaali log the.

Aur ye baat unhone ek murti ko dekhte hue mehsoos ki.

Buddha ki murti. Kamal par baithe. Shaant, ichha se upar. Itne door lagte hain, ki pahunchna namumkin lage.

Par phir dobara dekhiye. Un shaant chehre ke peeche kuch aur hai. Ek josh. Aankhein band hain, par unn aankhon se kuch jhaankta hai.

Jaise wo keh rahe hon: daud kar mat bhaago, shaant aankhon se saamna karo. Zindagi mein badhne ka mauka dekho.

Nehru ek angrez lekhak ka saar bhi dete hain. Unke hisaab se Buddha "shaant aur meethi garima ka poora namuna" hain — jismein har saans lene wale ke liye komalta hai.

Jis kaum ne aisa aadmi diya, uske andar gyaan aur andar ki shakti ke gehre bhandar honge.

Aur phir bhi — Buddha ke jaane ke baad na, usse pehle hi — iss desh mein ek doosri cheez poori tarah taiyaar ho chuki thi.

Talwar.

## Part 7: Seema ka ek sardar

[SFX: loha loha par — ek tez, chhoti tankar]

Uttar Bharat apne hathyaron ke liye mashhoor tha. Khaaskar apne faulad ke liye. Apni talwaron aur khanjaron ke liye.

Paanch sau saal Isa se pehle, ek badi Bharatiya fauj Fars ki sena ke saath Greece tak pahunchi. Ghudsawar bhi, aur paidal bhi.

Sadiyon baad, ek Farsi maha-kavita mein ek line hai. Jab Sikandar ne Fars par hamla kiya, to Farsi logon ne apni talwaron ke liye jaldi-jaldi Bharat se maang bheji.

Aur Arabi mein talwar ka ek purana shabd hai: muhannad. Matlab — "Hind se aaya hua". Ye shabd aaj bhi chalta hai.

Is shabd ko yaad rakhiye. Ek desh ka naam ek hathyar ke naam mein itna bas gaya, ki aaj tak log us hathyar ko usi naam se pukaarte hain.

Loha unke haath mein kuch aur bhi tha. Dilli ke paas aaj bhi lohe ka ek bahut bada khamba khada hai.

Aaj ke scientists iska jawab nahi dhoondh paaye ki wo kaise banaya gaya. Do hazaar saal ki hawa, barish aur garmi us par kuch nahi kar paayi. Zang wahan nahi lagti.

Us khambhe par ek likhaai hai, jo Gupta script mein hai. Par kuch vidvaanon ka kehna hai ki khamba khud us likhaai se bhi purana hai.

Wo faulad sirf talwar mein nahi tha. Loha pakane ka jo hunar us zamane mein Hindustan ke paas tha, wo kisi doosre desh ke paas nahi tha. Wo khamba itna bada hai, aur itna sunhara, ki lagta hai kal kisi ne wahan khada kiya ho.

Ab Sikandar. Yahan ek baat chaukanne wali hai.

Jise hum duniya ka bada vijeta maante hain, uska Bharat par hamla sena ke hisaab se koi bada kaam nahi tha.

Ye sarhad paar ka ek dhava tha. Aur uske liye bhi safal nahi.

Raste mein ek sarhad ka chhota-sa sardaar aisa lada ki aage badhne ka poora khayal dobara sochna pada.

Us sardaar ka naam itihaas ne sambhal kar nahi rakha. Par us din usne wo kar dikhaya, jo duniya ka vijeta aage nahi kar paaya.

Aur yahi sawal uski fauj ke dimaag mein aaya. Agar sarhad ka ek chhota raja itna lad sakta hai, to aage ke bade raajya kya karenge?

Isliye uski fauj ne aage jaane se inkaar kar diya. Wo laut gayi.

Par Sikandar ke jaane ke baad jo hua, wo iss episode ka doosra hissa hai. Ek samrajya khada hua — aur us samrajya mein ek aadmi paida hua, jisne sabse badi jeet apni hi talwar se haari.

Us samrajya ko khada karne ke liye talwar lagi thi. Use chalane ke liye ek kitaab. Aur use badalne ke liye ek edict.

## Part 8: Kalinga ke baad

[SFX: chhaini patthar par — dheemi, baar-baar]

Chandragupta ke bete Bindusara ke zamane mein bhi Pataliputra mein pardesi doot aate rahe — Misr ka Ptolemy, aur Antiochus.

Unka beta tha Ashoka — Chandragupta ka pote, jo pehle Taxila ke praant ka viceroy raha. Uski kahani ek jeet se shuru hoti hai, jise wo aadhi zindagi baad apni sabse badi haar maanega.

Usne Kalinga par hamla kiya — aaj ka Odisha, aur Andhra ka ek hissa. Wahan ke logon ne zameen bhar kar saamna kiya. Aur Ashoka ki fauj jeet gayi.

Aur jeet ke baad jo hua, wo usne khud patthar par khudwaya. Uske apne shabdon mein: tajposhi ke aathve saal mein Kalinga jita gaya. Dedh lakh log bandi banakar le jaaye gaye. Ek lakh wahan maare gaye. Aur unse kai guna zyada log mare gaye.

Us edict mein aage likha hai: Kalinga ko jeetne ke baad us samraat ke andar pachhtava shuru hua. Kyunki ek na jeete hue desh ko jeetne ka matlab hai — qatl, maut, aur bandi. Aur ye baat uske liye gehri soch aur afsos ki thi.

Aur phir ek wada. Ab wo koi hatya nahi sahega, aur koi bandi nahi banwayega — Kalinga mein mare gaye logon ke sauven ya hazaarven hisse jitna bhi nahi.

To asli jeet kya hai? Edict khud jawab deta hai: asli vijay dilon ki hoti hai, aur wo dharm se hoti hai — kartavya ke niyam se. Aur Ashoka jodte hain: aisi asli jeetein unhone pehle hi jeet li hain. Apne raajya mein bhi, door ke raajyon mein bhi.

Yahin cold open ke do shabd lautte hain.

Pehla: hazaar aadmiyon ko yuddh mein harane se bada vijeta wo hai jo khud ko jeet le.

Doosra: jeet nafrat ko janm deti hai.

Ashoka ne ye samjha nahi tha. Usne ye talwar se seekha tha.

Aur usi edict mein ek line hai, jo sirf samraat ki nahi, ek insaan ki lagti hai. Agar koi usse bura kare, to wo bhi sah lena chahiye. Jungli logon tak ko wo dayaa se dekhta hai — kyunki wo chahta hai ki saare jeev surakshit rahein, khush rahein, aur apne mann par kaabu rakhein.

Par ye samraat sant ban kar nahi baitha. Wo pehle se zyada kaam kar raha tha. Uske apne shabdon mein: jab main kha raha hoon, jab antahpur mein hoon, jab apne kamre mein, apni gaadi mein, apne baag mein hoon — sarkari report likhne wale mujhe logon ke kaam ki khabar dete rehna. Har samay, har jagah, mujhe janta ke kaam karne hain.

Pashchim mein wo wahan tak gaye, jahan Sikandar ki apni duniya thi. Misr tak. Macedonia tak. Poorab mein Burma aur Siam tak.

Aur dakshin mein Lanka? Wahan koi doot nahi gaya — apne bete aur beti ko bheja, Mahendra aur Sanghamitra.

Kahin bhi kisi par zor nahi dala gaya. Baat ki gayi mann se, aur dil se. Aur Bauddh hokar bhi usne baaki dharmon ka poora aadar rakha. Uske ek edict ki line hai: saare panth izzat ke haqdaar hain — kisi na kisi wajah se. Aisa karne se aadmi apna panth bhi ooncha karta hai, aur doosron ke panth ki seva bhi.

Isi liye Buddha dharm Kashmir se Ceylon tak phailta hai. Nepal tak. Aur baad mein Tibet, China aur Mongolia tak.

Bharat ke andar ek badlaav aata hai: maans kam hua, sharaab kam hui, pashu-bali band ho gayi — jabki pehle Brahman aur Kshatriya dono maans khaate the.

Vyapaar phir se phailta hai — Khotan mein ek Bharatiya basti banti hai, aur Taxila mein bahar se vidyarthi badhte hain.

To is episode ka sawal. Asli jeet kya hai — talwar ki, ya apne aap ki?

Ashoka ke paas dono the. Ek taraf wahi talwar, jisne duniya ka pehla bada samrajya khada kiya. Doosri taraf wo mutthi bhar sach, jo Buddha sookhe patte le kar aaya tha.

Usne talwar se sab jeet kar ye seekha ki jeet hi apne andar nafrat bo deti hai.

Jo jeet dilon par hoti hai, wahi tikti hai. Baaki sab ek din patthar ke khandhar ban jaati hai.

Par Ashoka ne ek aur cheez chhodi, jo patthar ki nahi thi. Wo lakdi ki thi.

## Part 9: Do hazaar saal ki lakdi

[SFX: sookhi lakdi par ungli — halki, chikni khurak]

Pehle Ashoka ka doosra chehra. Wo ek bada banane wala aadmi tha.

Kuch vidvaanon ka kehna hai ki usne badi imaartein banane ke liye bahar se karigar bulaye. Uske kuch khambhe Fars ke shahron jaise hain. Par usi kaam mein Hindustani kaarigari ki chhaap saaf dikhti hai.

Kareeb tees saal pehle, khudai karne wale ek angrez afsar ne uske Pataliputra wale mahal ka ek bahu-khamba hall nikaala.

Usne report mein likha: ye shah-bal aise chikne aur poore the, jaise aaj hi rakhe gaye hon — do hazaar saal se zyada purane. Jodon ki lakeerein tak alag nahi dikhti. Itni bariki ki aaj bhi usse behtar banaya jaana mumkin nahi.

Sochiye wo drishya. Mitti ke neeche do hazaar saal se ek chhat padi thi. Jab use khola gaya, to lakdi par dhool ki ek patli parat thi — aur bas. Do hazaar saal, aur itni si nishani.

Aise shah-bal doosre ilaakon mein bhi mile — bahut achhi halat mein.

Aur yahi sabse hairani ki baat hai. Hindustan mein hawa lakdi ko kha jaati hai, keede us par toot padte hain. Zaroor koi khaas treatment hua hoga. Wo kya tha, aaj tak raaz hai.

Patna aur Gaya ke beech Nalanda hai, jo aage mashhoor hui. Ashoka ke zamane mein uska zikr nahi.

Ashoka ne 232 BC mein dehaant diya — ek-chalis saal ka raaj.

Ek angrez itihaas-kar ne likha: itihaas ke panno par hazaaron rajaon ke naam bhare hain. In sab ke beech Ashoka ka naam akela chamakta hai — ek sitara. Volga se Japan tak uska naam aaj bhi izzat se liya jaata hai. Uski yaad utne log rakhte hain, jitne Constantine ya Charlemagne ka naam sun nahi paaye.

Ab lautiye us jagah, jahan se ye set shuru hua.

Ye set shuru hua tha Mohenjo-daro ki ret se — aur un deewaron se jo har peedhi thodi-thodi oonchi hoti gayi. Ret barasti rahi, aur deewar khud ko bachati rahi.

Set khatam hota hai us lakdi par, jo do hazaar saal se nahi gali. Jise aaj bhi koi chhoo sake, to us par ungli phisalti hai — jaise kal hi usko rakha gaya ho.

To Nehru ne jo khoj ki, usme mila kya?

Ek jaati? Nahi. Jaati tooti, aur badalti rahi.

Ek dharm? Nahi. Dharm hazaaron rukhon mein phaila.

Mila ek sabhyata, jo aane wale har naye log ko apne andar sama leti hai. Aur phir apne hi andar deewarein bana leti hai. Ek mann, jo har cheez par sawal poochta rehta hai. Ek janta, jo kaam karti hai, gaati hai, nachti hai.

Aur ek samraat, jisne talwar se sab jeet kar seekha, ki jeet apne aap ki hoti hai.

Ret sookhi rahi. Deewar oonchi hoti rahi. Aur lakdi nahi gali.

Ek naujawan paidal chalkar Takshashila gaya tha. Ek qaidi kothri mein baith kar likh raha hai. Aur ek khamba — jispar Sanskrit likha hua hai — abhi dikhne wala hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Aaj humare desh ke jhande par ek pahiya hai. Aur sarkari muhar par ek stambh ke oopar baithe chaar sher. Ye Ashoka ke hi kaam ki nishaniyan hain.

Ek samraat, jisne patthar par khudwaya tha: asli vijay dilon ki hoti hai.

Aur hum? Hum apni jeet ka hisaab lagane baithein, to hum kya ginnte hain? Kitne hare, kitne maare?

Ya hum ye ginnte hain ki humne apne aap par kya jeeta?

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aaj aapki zindagi mein jo sabse badi jeet hui hai — usme aapne kisi ko hara, ya khud ko?

## Outro

To khoj mein mila kya? Ek sabhyata jo sab ko apna leti hai, ek mann jo sawal poochta hai, aur ek samraat jisne khud ko jeeta.

Maurya samrajya dheere-dheere bikhar gaya. Par uske baad ek cheez hui, jo Nehru ko bahut ajeeb lagti hai.

Uttar-pashchim se phir Yunani aaye. Par is baar ek Yunani doot ne, Sanskrit mein, ek khamba khada kiya.

Kyun?

Yahin se shuru hota hai Chapter 5 — "Through the Ages". Agli baar hum wahi khamba dekhenge, aur usse poochenge: itne hamlavar aaye, itni takkarein huin — phir bhi Bharat, Bharat kaise bana raha?

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
