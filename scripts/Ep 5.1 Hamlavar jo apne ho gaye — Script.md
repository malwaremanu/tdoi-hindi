## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhla set Maurya samrajya ke bikharne par khatam hua tha — aur wahan, uttar-pashchim se aaye ek Yunani doot ne, Sanskrit mein, ek khamba khada kiya. Wahi khamba aaj hamare saamne hai. Aaj se Chapter 5 shuru hota hai.

## Part 1: Sookhi shaakha, naya beej

Maurya samrajya dheere-dheere phika pad gaya. Uski jagah Sunga aaye — ek aisa raajvansh, jo Mauryon ke muqable bahut chhote ilaake ka maalik tha. Na wahi seema, na wahi shaan.

Ek bada raaj gaya, aur ek chhota raaj baitha. Aur ye khaalipan kuch saalon ka nahi tha. Ye sadiyon ka tha.

Isi khaali waqt mein do cheezein dikhti hain.

Ek taraf, dakshin mein badi riyasatein utth rahi thin.

Doosri taraf, uttar-pashchim mein Kabul se Punjab tak ek nayi taaqat phail rahi thi. Bactria ke log. Inhe Indo-Greek kehte hain — ya aasan bhasha mein, is seema par base hue Yunani.

Ye log kabhi is desh ke bahar the. Ab wo is desh ke andar the. Aur aage badh rahe the.

Ek naam samne aata hai. Menander — un Yunanion ka raja, jo apni sena lekar Pataliputra tak pahunch gaya. Pataliputra, Mauryon ki rajdhani. Us par khatra khada ho gaya tha.

Par Menander ko haraya gaya. Hata diya gaya.

Aur phir wahi hua, jo is chapter mein baar-baar hota hai. Haar kar lautne wale par is desh ka mahaul aise chha gaya, ki wo khud badal gaya.

Menander Bauddh ho gaya. Aur Bauddh parampara mein wo ek bade naam ban gaye — Milinda. Logon ne unhe lagbhag ek sant maan liya.

Ek raja, jisne is desh ko jeetna chaha. Aur aage chalkar wahi is desh ka ho gaya.

Ye sirf dharm ka badlaav nahi tha. Do kalaayein mili — Yunani shakl, Bharatiya bhaav — aur ek nayi kala utthi. Gandhara ki kala. Afghanistan aur us Sarhad wale ilaake ki kala.

Aur us kala mein ek baat dikhti hai, jo aage baar-baar dikhegi. Murti ke chehre Yunani hain, par unki aankhon mein jo shaant bhaav hai, wo Bharatiya hai. Do khoon, ek chehra.

Ab ek line ke liye Besnagar yaad kijiye. Wahi sadi. Wahi seema. Wahi Yunani. Wo khamba issi safar ki pehli tasveer hai.

Aur ye akeli misaal nahi thi. Kyunki aur log aane wale the. Aur unke saath bhi wahi hota, jo Menander ke saath hua.

## Part 2: Do raah

Central Asia mein pehle se ek log baithe the. Shakas — jinhe Scythian bhi kehte hain. Unka ilaaka itna unka ho gaya, ki us ilaake ka naam hi pad gaya Shakasthan. Aaj ka Seistan.

Phir poorab se, aur door se, Yueh Chih aaye. Unhone Shakon ko wahan se ukhaad diya, aur dhakel diya uttar Bharat ki taraf.

To Shak yahan aaye — baahar nikale hue, naye ghar ki talaash mein. Unmein kai Bauddh ho gaye, kai Hindu. Do raah.

Aur Yueh Chih ke andar ek khaandaan tha: Kushan. Unhone apni sarvochta sthapit ki, apna raaj uttar Bharat tak badhaya, aur Shakon ko aur dakshin dhakela — Kathiawad aur Deccan tak. Uske baad ek bada, lamba chalne wala samrajya khada kiya: poora uttar Bharat, aur Madhya Asia ka ek bada hissa.

Inmein se kuch Hindu hue. Par zyadatar Bauddh hue. Aur unke sabse mashhoor raja Kanishka — Bauddh kathaon ke hero, jinke bade kaam aur bade nirmaan yaad kiye jaate hain.

Dilchasp baat: Bauddh raja hone ke bawajood, unke raaj ka dharm ek mila-jula maamla tha — jisme Zoroastrian dharm ka bhi hissa tha.

Ye seema-par base raaj, jiska kendra aaj ke Peshawar ke paas tha, aur jiske paas wahi purana Taxila — wo kai deshon ke logon ka milne ka adda ban gaya. Yahan Bharatiya mile Scythian se, Yueh Chih se, Irani se, Bactrian Yunani se, Turk se, aur Chinese se. Aur inke milan se murti aur chitrakala ka ek joshila daur uttha.

Yahin, isi aangan mein, China se Bharat ka pehla likha hua sampark hua. Ek Chinese doot aaya — 64 AC mein. Aur uske saath do chhote tohfe aaye: aadu aur naashpati. Us raaste ki poori kahani aage, Ep 5.6 mein.

Gobi ke kinaare, Turfan aur Kucha mein, isi mel se ek nayi cheez bani — Bharatiya, Chinese aur Irani, teenon ka mel. Aur ye mel shaant nahi tha: yahan se ek poora aaya hua daur shuru hua, jo pahaadon ke us paar tak gaya.

Aur ye ek baar ki baat nahi thi. Ye is desh ka tareeka tha: jo aaya, wo yahin ka ho jaata tha.

Par ye raaj yahan ka nahi tha. Aur ye baat kisi ne bhooli nahi thi. Uske khilaaf ek dheema swar chalta raha. Abhi wo bas gunjta tha. Par gaayab nahi hua tha.

## Part 3: Chautha Chandra

Kushan kaal mein ek badi phoot padi. Buddh dharm do hisson mein bant gaya — Mahayan aur Hinayan. Do naam, ek dharm, aur dono mein zabaani takraar.

Par is desh ka apna dhang tha. Vivaad yahan talwaron se nahi lade jaate the. Unhe badi sabhaon mein rakha jaata tha. Door-door se pratinidhi aate the. Sunte the. Bolte the. Phir faisla hota.

Kashmir us samrajya ke lagbhag beech mein tha. Isliye wahi us behes aur us sanskritik kaam-kaaj ka kendra ban gaya.

Behes ek jagah shuru hoti thi, par uski goonj door tak jaati thi. Aur faisla sun lena, usi ko maan lena — ye is desh ka purana bauddhik dhang tha.

Ek naam is behes se sabse oopar nikalta hai. Nagarjuna — pehli sadi ke mahan Bauddh vidvaan. Unki buddhi tez thi, aur himmat badi. Nehru likhte hain ki Bharat mein Mahayan ki jeet ka bada hissa unhi ka hai. Unke apne darshan ki kahani agle episode — 5.5 — mein hai.

Nateeja? Do raaste do deshon mein baant gaye. Mahayan China tak pahuncha. Aur Lanka aur Burma Hinayan par tik gaye. Ye baant sirf dharm ki nahi thi. Isse do alag duniyaan bani, aur unka asar aage sadiyon tak raha.

Par ek doosri baat bhi chalti rahi. Ye raaj videshi tha — chahe wo kitna bhi yahan ka ho gaya ho. Aur uske khilaaf wahi dheema swar gunjta raha. Ek desh-prem ka swar.

Chauthi sadi ke shuru mein naye kabile pahaadon se utar kar aane lage. Aur ab log chup nahi baithe. Wo swar ek aandolan ban gaya.

Uska neta ek raja bana. Aur us raja ka naam bhi wahi tha. Chandragupta.

Usne naye hamla-varon ko bhaga diya, aur ek bada, phaila hua samrajya khada kiya. Yahin se Gupta samrajya ka daur shuru hota hai. 320 AC.

## Part 4: Shell

Gupta kaal — ek ke baad ek bade raja. Jang mein kaamyaab, aur aman ki kalaon mein bhi.

Par ye raaj sirf taaqat se nahi bana tha. Dar se bhi bana tha. Baar-baar ke hamlon ne logon ke andar ek gehra bhaav paida kiya. Bahar wala khatra hai.

Sabse zyada asar purane Brahman aur Kshatriya vargon par pada. Unhe do cheezein bachani thi. Apni mitti, aur apni sanskriti.

Ab dhyan dijiye. Jo log pehle aa chuke the, ghul chuke the, unhe apnaya jaata tha. Par jo ab aata tha, usse ladha jaata tha. Aur isi soch se ek koshish shuru hui: poore desh ko ek jaisa banana, purane Brahmanik aadarshon par.

Par purana bharosa ja raha tha. Aur jab bharosa jaata hai, to aadarsh sakht ho jaate hain. Wo sakhti is desh ke swabhaav ki nahi thi. Par wo aa gayi.

Bharat apne shell mein simatne laga — bahar se nahi, andar se. Zameen par bhi, aur mann mein bhi. Shell ka matlab kya hai? Ek ghar, jiska darwaza andar se band ho jaaye.

Aur jo ghar apna darwaza khud band karta hai, wo bahar ki duniya ko bhoolne lagta hai. Nehru ka ye shabd, shell, apni jagah rakhiye. Wo Part 11 mein laut kar aayega.

Par wo shell kaisa tha? Nehru saaf likhte hain: wo itna gehra tha, aur itna chaura, ki usme sab samaa jaaye. Kyunki is dharm aur darshan ka swabhaav hi udarta tha. Anek mat aur anek jaatiyon ke log isme jagah paate the. Samaane ka kaam chalta rehta tha.

Sirf ek cheez badli. Bahar wale ke khilaaf ye soch ab pehle se zyada tez ho gayi. Bachav ki zid ban gayi.

Aur jab rashtra-prem taaqat pakadta hai, to wo aksar samrajyvaad jaisa dikhne lagta hai. Gupton ka daur bahut tez tha, bahut roshan, kala se bhara hua. Par wahi daur samrajyvaad ki taraf bahut jaldi mud gaya. Unke ek raja ka naam Samudragupta tha. Unhe Bharat ka Napoleon kaha gaya hai.

Ek baat samajhiye. Iss daur mein dharm sirf dharm nahi raha. Purane Brahmanik aadarsh — jinhe aage chalkar Hinduism kaha gaya — rashtra ka nishaan ban gaye. Ek rashtriya dharm, jo poore desh ke gehere instinct ko chhoota tha.

Aur Buddhism? Buddhism bhi isi mitti ki santaan thi, aur uske paas bhi ek rashtriya pichhe ki baat thi. Bharat uske liye wahi paavan bhoomi thi, jahan Buddha jiye, padhaya aur mare. Par Buddhism asal mein international tha, ek vishwa-dharm — aur jitna phaila, utna vishwamani hota gaya.

Yahi wo soch thi, jo aage chalkar is daur ki sabse badi taaqat bani — aur isi se iski sabse badi kamzori bhi aayi.

Isliye yahan baar-baar ek hi cheez hui. Purana Brahmanik vishwas rashtriya jaagran ka nishaan banta gaya. Wo andar udar tha, sabko sameta. Aur bahar sakht, kyunki wo ab khud ko bachane ki soch mein tha.

## Part 5: Safed Hoon

Gupton ne uttar Bharat par lagbhag dedh sau saal raaj kiya — ek taaqatwar, samriddh riyasat. Phir unke baad aane wale aaye. Wo bhi dedh sau saal. Par ab wo bachav par the. Samrajya sikudta gaya. Chhota hota gaya.

Jo shell Gupta kaal mein bana tha, wo ab ek dhaal ban gaya tha — aur ek dhaal sirf rok sakti hai.

Kyunki pahaadon ke us paar se phir koi aa raha tha. Safed Hoon.

Madhya Asia se aaye, aur jahan pahunche, wahan lutiya daali — bilkul waise hi jaise, usi zamane mein, Europe mein Attila luta raha tha. Kahar ki koi sarhad nahi hoti.

Khet jale. Bazaar lute. Shehar khaali hue. Aur yahi wo haal tha, jisne aakhir logon ko ek kar diya.

[SFX: door se ghode ki tez taap, ek jhund]

Unke kaale kaam jab poore desh tak pahunche, to logon mein kuch jaaga. Wo swar, jo sadiyon se dheema chalta aa raha tha, ab ek jut ban gaya.

Yashovarman ke neeche riyasaton ka ek sangh bana. Unn par ek saath hamla hua. Hoonon ki taaqat toot gayi. Aur unka sardaar, Mihiragula, bandi ban liya gaya.

Ab is kahani ka sabse ajeeb mod aata hai.

Gupta vansh ka ek raja zinda tha. Baladitya. Usne Mihiragula ke saath waisa vyavhaar kiya, jaisa is desh ki reet kehti thi. Udaarta. Usse jaane diya gaya.

Aur Mihiragula ne badle mein kya kiya? Wo chupchaap laut kar aaya. Aur apne hi meherbaan par ek chhupa hua hamla kiya.

Ye chhota waqia hai. Par isme is desh ke bharose ka poora itihaas hai. Ek aadmi ki udaarta. Aur usi aadmi ka dhokha.

Baladitya ko jaane dena koi kamzori nahi thi. Wo is desh ka likha hua niyam tha: jo sharan mein aaye, use nahi maara jaata. Aur yahi baat unki maut bani. Yahan se ek baat saaf hoti hai, jo aage baar-baar lautegi: is desh ka tareeka do tarah ka tha — jo bhaga sakte the, unse ladna; aur jo bhaga nahi sakte the, unhe apna banana.

Phir bhi ek baat yaad rakhiye. Hoonon ka raaj yahan bahut lamba nahi chala. Aadhi sadi, ya usse bhi kam.

Aur unmein se bahut se yahin reh gaye. Chhote-chhote sardar ban kar. Kabhi-kabhi upadrav karte. Aur dheere-dheere is desh ke samundar mein ghul gaye — jaise pehle bhi kai ghul chuke the.

## Part 6: Kanauj ka raja

Saatvin sadi ki shuruaat mein wahi bache hue sardar phir sarkash ho gaye.

Is baar unhe Kanauj ke raja ne kuchla. Harshavardhana. Usne uttar aur madhya Bharat ko mila kar ek lamba samrajya khada kiya.

Wo dharm mein Bauddh the. Par unka Bauddh dharm Mahayan tha — wahi dhaara, jo kai maayno mein Hindu parampara ke kareeb thi. Yahan do dharmon ko lada nahi gaya. Dono ko jagah di gayi. Dono ko badhaya gaya.

Do dharm, ek raaj. Ye bhi is desh ka purana dhang tha.

Unke zamane mein ek mehmaan door se aaya. Ek Chinese yaatri. Hsuan Tsang. 629 AC. Uska aana aage ek lambi sadak kholta hai, jis par hum is set mein baad mein chalenge.

Harsha khud kavi the. Natak likhte the. Unke aas-paas kalaakaar aur kavi ikattha the. Ujjayini phir se ek bade sanskritik kendra ki tarah jagmaga uthi.

Aur 648 AC mein Harsha chale gaye.

Theek usi waqt, Arab ke registaan se ek nayi taaqat utth rahi thi: Islam. Wo aage Africa aur Asia mein tezi se phailne wali thi.

Zara sochiye, kya waqt tha. Ek taraf ek raja kala aur sahitya ko ikattha kar raha tha. Doosri taraf ek naya dharm apna raasta bana raha tha. Ek hi zamane ki do kahaniyan: ek andar ki, ek bahar ki.

Harsha ka daur ye bhi dikhata hai ki dhalta hua waqt khaali nahi hota. Jab tak koi raja kala ko jagah deta hai, us daur ki roshni khatam nahi hoti. Aur Hsuan Tsang ka aana ek aur baat batata hai: us waqt is desh ka darwaza bahar walon ke liye khula tha. Padhne wale, poochne wale, yaatra karne wale — sab aate the.

Unke jaane ke baad wo bada raaj zyada din nahi khada raha. Uttar phir chhote raajon mein bantne laga. Par us kahani tak hum baad mein pahunchenge. Ab hum uttar chhod kar dakshin chalte hain. Wahan sab kuch alag hai.

## Part 7: Samundar ki taaqat

Dakshin mein Maurya samrajya ka ant waisa nahi aaya, jaisa uttar mein. Wo wahan kabhi poora phaila hi nahi tha. Aur jab wo samrajya sikud kar khatam ho gaya, tab bhi dakshin mein ek hazaar saal se zyada tak badi riyasatein phalti-phoolti rahi.

Ek hazaar saal. Ye chhota waqt nahi hai.

Pehle Andhras — dakshin ki wo badi taaqat, jisne Shakon ko haraya. Baad mein wo Kushanon ke samkaalin ban gaye. Phir pashchim mein Chalukya samrajya aaya. Uske baad Rashtrakuta. Unse aur dakshin mein Pallav — dakshin ki wo riyasat, jisne samundar paar ja kar bastiyan basayi. Aur phir Chola samrajya, jiska phailaav poore peninsula par tha. Lanka tak. Aur dakshin Burma tak. Unka aakhri bada raja Rajendra. Unki mrityu 1044 AC mein hui.

[SFX: samundar ki lehrein; door se ek jahaaz ki rassi kheenchne ki aawaaz]

Par ye taaqat kheti se nahi, samundar se aayi thi. Samundar in riyasaton ki sadak thi.

Dakshin apne badhiya utpaadon ke liye jaana jaata tha. Aur apne samundari vyapaar ke liye. Ye sea-power the. Inke jahaaz door-door deshon mein maal le jaate the.

Yahan Yunanion ki bastiyan bhi thi. Yahan Roman sikke bhi mile hain. Aur Chalukya rajaon ne Persia ke Sassanid rajaon se doot bheje. Doot dono taraf se aate-jaate rahe.

Ab uttar ki taraf ek sawal dekhiye.

Uttar mein hamla ke baad hamla hota raha. Rajvansh badle. Bazaar jalte rahe. Dakshin par in hamlon ka seedha asar nahi pada.

Par "seedha nahi" ka matlab "koi asar nahi" nahi hota.

Jab uttar mein aag lagti thi, to log bhaagte the. Kaun bhaagta tha? Karigar. Mistri. Chitrakar. Wo dakshin mein ja bas gaye.

Nateeja? Dakshin purani kala aur purani parampara ka ghar ban gaya. Aur uttar, naye logon aur nayi hawaon ke saath, badalta raha. Aage aane wali sadiyon mein ye kaam aur tez hua, aur dakshin kattar Hindu parampara ka bada gadh ban gaya.

Par ek baat dhyaan mein rakhiye. Hoon. Shak. Kushan. Yunani. Andhra. Chalukya. Chola. Aisi lambi fehrist padh kar ek galat tasveer ban jaati hai. Jaise is desh ka itihaas sirf jang aur toot-phoot ka itihaas ho.

Wo tasveer galat hai. Aur ise theek karne ke liye Nehru humein rok kar ek baat kehte hain.

## Part 8: Do sau saal ki sarkar

Wo kehte hain: ye daur ek hazaar saal se zyada lamba hai. Aur is lambi avadhi mein is desh ne lambe-lambe saal aman aur intezaam ke saath guzare.

Ek ginti dekhiye. Maurya. Kushan. Gupta. Aur dakshin mein Andhra, Chalukya, Rashtrakuta.

Inmein se har ek do-so-so-tees sau saal chala. Aur ye tulna yaad rakhiye — inmein se har ek, is desh mein Angrezon ke raaj se zyada lamba chala.

Aur inmein se lagbhag saare raajvansh isi mitti ke the. Jo bahar se aaye, jaise Kushan, wo bhi is desh ke dhang mein dhal gaye. Wo yahan ke raja ban kar raaj karte the. Unki jadein isi mitti mein thi.

Seema par chhoti-moti jhankiyan hoti rahi. Bagal ki riyasaton mein kabhi takraar hoti rahi. Par aam haal aman ka hi tha.

Aur ye aman khaali nahi tha. Uske andar kala, sahitya aur vidya phalti rahi.

Aur raja log kis baat par garv karte the? Kala par. Sahitya par. Vidya par.

Ye kaam ek seema tak mehdood nahi rehta tha, kyunki poore Bharat ka sanskritik aur sahityik aadhaar ek hi tha. Isi liye koi bhi dharmik ya daarshnik vivaad turant phail jaata tha — poorab se pashchim tak, uttar se dakshin tak. Sab jagah usi par behes hoti.

Aur jang ke waqt? Jang ke waqt bhi aam admi ka kaam band nahi hota tha. Sena nikal jaati thi, aur gaon ka kaam chalta rehta tha.

Yahan tak ki likhit samjhautey bhi mile hain. Ladne wale raja, aur gaon ki khud-hukumat wali sabhaon ke sardar. Dono ke beech likha jaata tha: fasal ko nuksaan nahi pahunchayenge. Aur galti se pahunch gaya, to bharpaayi denge.

Zara sochiye. Ek likha hua vaada, jisme jang ke pakshe kisaan ki fasal ki fikar karte hain.

Par is niyam ki ek seema thi. Ye desh ke andar ki ladaiyon ke liye tha. Bahar se aane wali senaon ke liye nahi. Aur jahan asli satta ki ladai thi, wahan bhi nahi.

## Part 9: Jang ke niyam

In jangon ke niyam kahan se aaye? Is desh ki purani soch se.

Jo jang dharm ke liye hai, wo dharm se ladni chahiye. Isme koi galat tareeka nahi chalega. Kitna amal hua, wo alag sawal hai. Par niyam likhe hue the, aur bahut saaf.

Zeher wale teer mana the. Chhupi hui hathiyaar mana thi.

Jo so raha hai, use nahi maro. Jo bhaag kar sharan mein aaya hai, use nahi maro. Jo haath jod kar khada hai, use nahi maro.

Aur ye sun kar aaj hairat hoti hai: achhi imaaratein girayi nahi jaayengi. Ek jang, jisme sundarta ko bachaya jaata tha.

Par ye soch apne waqt se aage nahi chal rahi thi. Chanakya ke waqt mein isme daraar aa chuki thi. Chanakya — Maurya rajaon ke mahamantri, Arthashastra ke rachayak. Wo saaf likhte hain: agar shatru ko harane ke liye zyada tabahi wale, chhal-pooran tareeke zaroori hain, to unhe apnaya ja sakta hai.

Aur yahi nahi. Arthashastra mein wo aise yantra bhi batate hain, jo ek saath sau aadmiyon ko maar saken. Kuch aise vismakaari padarthon ka bhi zikr hai. Aur trench jang ka bhi.

Ye asli mein kya the, ye aaj batana mushkil hai. Shayad ye purani kathaon ke jaadui karname the. Ye maanne ka koi aadhaar nahi ki iska matlab barood tha.

To ye daur jang ka tha ya aman ka?

Nehru yahan ek badi baat likhte hain. Is desh ke itihaas mein bure daur aaye. Aag. Talwar. Akaal. Andar ka intezaam toot gaya. Par poore itihaas par nazar daaliye, to ek baat dikhti hai: is desh ne Europe se kahin zyada lambe daur aman aur intezaam mein guzare. Ye Turk aur Afghan hamlon ke baad ki sadiyon par bhi laagu hoti hai — aur us waqt tak bhi, jab Moghul samrajya toot raha tha.

Aaj sochiye. Ek soch aaj bhi chalti hai: ki Angrezon ne pehli baar is desh ko aman aur intezaam diya. Nehru ise sabse ajeeb bhram kehte hain — Pax Britannica, yani Angrezon ka aman.

Sach kya hai? Jab Angrezon ka raaj shuru hua, tab ye desh apne sabse neeche nuqte par tha. Andar sab bikhar chuka tha. Aur yahi ek wajah thi, ki wo raaj khada ho paaya.

## Part 10: Soch, aur mila-jula khoon

Ab ek kavi ki baat suniye. Ye lines aaj bhi dohrai jaati hain.

Kavi likhte hain: purab jhuk gaya. Chup-chaap, gehri udaasini se. Usne senaon ko guzarte dekha, aur phir apni soch mein doob gaya.

Nehru kehte hain, isme aadha sach hai. Ye desh soch ka aashiq raha hai — aksar aisi baaton ka, jo vyavhaarik logon ko bekaar lagti hain. Usne soch ko, aur sochne walon ko, talwar se aur paise se oopar rakha. Aur apne bure dinon mein bhi usne soch ka saath nahi chhoda.

Par ye sach nahi hai. Usne har baar roka — kabhi kaamyaabi se, kabhi nakaam hokar. Aur jab wo us waqt haar gaya, to usne yaad rakha, aur agli baar ki taiyari ki. Uska tareeka wahi tha, jo hum Baladitya ke paas dekh chuke hain: ladna, ya apna banana.

Usne Sikandar ki senaon ko roka. Uske marne ke baad Yunani sainikon ko ukhaad phenka. Aage Indo-Greek aur Indo-Scythian logon ko apna liya, aur phir apni sarvochta khadi ki. Hoonon se peedhiyon tak ladta raha, aur unhe bhaga diya; jo reh gaye, ghul gaye. Arab aaye, to wo Indus ke paas ruk gaye. Turk aur Afghan dheere-dheere aage badhe; Dilli ke takht par jamne mein unhe kai sadiyan lagin.

Aur is ladai ke saath doosra kaam chalta raha: samaane ka. Aakhir mein hamla-var utne hi Bharatiya ho gaye, jitne baaki sab. Akbar isi purani soch ka sabse bada namuuna bane. Wo naye the, par unhone khud ko Bharat se joda, aur Bharat ne unhe apna liya. Jab tak unke baad aane wale isi soch par rahe, unka raaj khada raha. Jab unhone is soch se mooh moda, tab wo kamzor pade, aur unka samrajya gir gaya. Phir naye aandolan utthe — soch mein tang, par ek nayi rashtriyata ka swar. Wo todne mein kaafi the, banane mein nahi. Unhe laga, ateet ko wapas laaya ja sakta hai — par ateet vartman ki jagah nahi leta.

Ab Nehru likhte hain, hum ek aise log rahe hain jo apne ateet par garv karte hain, aur us virasat ko bachane ke liye deewarein aur baadhayein uthate rehte hain. Jaati bhi sakht ho chuki hai.

Aur phir bhi — isi desh ka khoon duniya ka sabse mila-jula khoon hai.

Arya. Dravid. Turani. Semitic. Mongol. Paanch. Arya baar-baar ki lehron mein aaye, aur Dravidon se unka milan hua. Uske baad hazaaron saal tak ek ke baad ek giroh aata raha. Shak, jinhe Scythian bhi kehte hain. Yunani. Kushan. Aur unke peeche Turk. Kuch bade jhund mein aaye, kuch chhote, kuch akela.

Ek angrez itihaaskar ne iska vaakiya likha hai. Bahut jangli, ladaku kabile baar-baar uttar ke maidanon par aate hain. Rajaon ko haraate hain. Shehar jalate hain. Naye raaj banate hain. Aur phir gaayab ho jaate hain — ek badi lehar mein ghul kar. Peechhe sirf do cheezein chhodte hain: thoda sa mila-jula khoon, aur kuch reetiyon ke chithe, jo dheere-dheere apne mahaul se milte jaate hain.

To ye mahaul kya tha? Nehru kehte hain, kuch hissa mausam aur mitti ka hai. Is desh ki hawa ka, bhugol ka. Par usse bada kuch aur hai: ek tez khinchaav, ek ichha — zindagi ke matlab ka ek vichaar, jo is desh ke andar uski sabse shuruaati umar mein hi chhap gaya tha. Itna gehra ki wo tik gaya. Aur jo bhi is desh ke sampark mein aaya, wo usi taaqat se andar aa gaya — chahe wo kitna bhi alag ho.

Wo kehte hain: kisi sabhyata ke neeche ek ichha dhoondhna khokhla lagta hai. Ek insaan ki zindagi sau jagah se ras aati hai; ek desh ki zindagi usse bhi zyada jagah se. Satah par ideein tairti rehti hain, aur bahut si ek doosre ki dushman. Koi bhi giroh utha lijiye, apni baat saabit kar lijiye; doosra giroh utha lijiye, wahi baat tor dijiye.

Aur yahan Nehru khud apni baat par sawal utthate hain. Wahi sawal agle part ka darwaza hai.

## Part 11: Hamlavar jo apne ho gaye

To kya wo ichha sirf ek khayaal thi? Ek baat dikhti hai. Hum badalte rehte hain; har daur pichhle se alag hai.

Par Bharat aur China ki sabhyataon mein ek ajeeb si jaan bachi hai. Bahut badlaav aaye, bahut sankat aaye, par dono apni buniyadi pehchaan bachaye rakh paaye. Ye tabhi mumkin tha, jab wo zindagi aur prakriti ke saath taal mein hon. Jo cheez unhe unki purani jadein pakde rakhti hai — achhi ho, buri ho, ya dono ka mel — wo taaqat ki cheez hai. Ho sakta hai uski upyogita bahut pehle khatam ho gayi ho, aur wo tab se bojh ban gayi ho. Ya ab uske andar ka bhala dabe hone se sirf khaal bachi ho — ek jeevashm ka khaali shell.

Aur yahan Nehru ek doosri baat jodte hain. Tarakki aur suraksha — ye do cheezein ek saath fit nahi hoti. Tarakki badlaav chahti hai; suraksha ek na badalne wala thikana. Purane daur golden past mein vishwas karte the — ek sona, jo beet chuka hai. Yahan jo sabhyata khadi hui, wo suraksha aur sthirta par khadi thi; usne samooh ko suraksha di, kamzor ko bachaya, aur taaqatwar ko roka. Aadmi ko sochne ki poori azaadi thi, par riwaaz par poora chalna padta tha.

Is conformity ke saath ek lachak bhi thi. Koi niyam aisa nahi tha, jo riwaaz se na badal sake. Naye samooh apne naye riwaaz, naye vishwas le kar aate the, aur phir bhi bade samooh ka hissa maane jaate the. Isi lachak ne bahar se aane walon ko sokhna aasan banaya. Aur iske peeche kuch buniyadi naitik niyam the, zindagi ko dekhne ka ek darshanik tareeka, aur doosron ke liye sahishnuta.

Par asli chunauti kisi sena se nahi aayi. Wo ek nayi soch se aayi — aage badhne ki soch, jo purane thahre hue saanche mein fit hi nahi hoti thi. Purane Bharat mein badlaav ki zaroorat maani jaati thi — aur isi zaroorat se paida hua synthesis ka josh. Ye jod sirf bahar walon ka nahi tha; ye aadmi ke bahar aur andar ke jeevan ka jod tha, aadmi aur prakriti ka jod. Isi saanjhi pichhe se Bharat bana, aur isi ne anekta ke beech ek jut ka nishaan diya.

Ab is episode ke do sire milaiye. Ek khamba, Besnagar mein — ek Yunani ka khamba, jis par Sanskrit likha hai. Aur aaj is desh ke wo log, jo sabse zyada Bharatiya maane jaate hain, jo Bharat ki parampara par sabse zyada garv karte hain: Rajput, Jat, Kathi.

Rajput — veer, aur parampara ke sabse pakke rakhwale. Unmein se kai Indo-Scythian ke vanshaj bataye jaate hain, kuch Hoonon ke bhi. Jat — Kathiawad ka wo mazboot kisan, jo apni zameen mein dagi nahi sehta. Unka bhi Scythian mool bataya jaata hai. Aur Kathi — wahi lamba, khoobsurat kisan. Mool jo bhi raha ho, ye sab poore-poora Bharatiya ban gaye, is sanskriti mein saajhi bhagedaar hain, aur is desh ke ateet ko apna ateet kehte hain.

Aur jo bahar se aaya, aur apne mein hi reh gaya, usne yahan koi lamba asar nahi chhoda. Wo mit gaya — aur mitne mein usne apna nuksaan kiya, aur kabhi-kabhi is desh ka bhi.

[SFX: Besnagar wali sookhi hawa, bahut halki, laut kar aati hai]

To Besnagar ka wo khamba ab ek jawab ban jaata hai. Jisne use khada kiya, wo bahar se aaya tha. Usne yahan ki bhasha chuni, yahan ki baat likhwayi, yahan ke devta ko apnaya — aur yahin ka ho gaya.

To jawab yahi hai.

Bharat Bharat bana raha, kyunki usne har aane wale ko apna bana liya.

Aur darwaza us waqt band hone laga, jab "apna" ka matlab "sirf apna" hone laga.

Wahi waqt Gupta kaal ki chamakti hui khaal ke andar chhupa hua tha. Chamak aur darwaza — dono saath aaye.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Do baatein.

Pehli: "shuddh khoon" ek nayi soch hai, purani nahi. Is episode mein Nehru khud paanch naslein ginte hain — Arya, Dravid, Turani, Semitic, Mongol — aur phir sabse zyada Bharatiya maane jaane wale logon ka mool Scythian aur Hoon nikalta hai. Jo aaj shuddhta ki baat karta hai, wo is desh ki sabse purani kahani ke khilaf bol raha hai.

Doosri: desh deewaron se nahi, soch se bandha rehta hai. Besnagar ka khamba usi soch ka nishaan hai — jo aaya, wo andar aa gaya. Par wahi soch, jab bachav ki zid ban gayi, tab darwaza band hone laga. Wo darwaza kisi ek sadi mein nahi band hua. Wo dheere-dheere band hua — aur uski aawaz aaj bhi sunai deti hai.
