## Ab tak

Ab tak: pichhli baar hum ruke the us khamba par, jise ek Yunani ne Besnagar mein Sanskrit mein khada kiya tha — aur us sawal par, ki ye desh har aane wale ko apna kaise bana leta raha. Us episode ke ant mein Allahabad ka ek kamra aaya tha. Ek mehmaan. Do bhai. Ek purani dhun. Aaj wahi dhun bajegi.

## Part 1: Ek mool, do raahein

Aaiye, bahut peechhe chalte hain. Itne peechhe ki wahan "Iran" aur "Bharat" jaise naam bhi nahi the.

Nehru likhte hain ki is desh ke jitne bhi sampark rehte aaye hain, unmein sabse purana Iran hai. Aur sabse zinda bhi. Sabse lambe waqt tak chalne wala.

Ye naata kisi jang se shuru nahi hota. Ye naata us din se hai, jab ek hi mool se do shaakhayein alag hui.

Sochiye ek purana ghar — ek hi chhat, ek hi aangan. Phir do bhai bant jaate hain. Ek purab ki taraf aa jaata hai, doosra wahin pashchim mein reh jaata hai.

Bhai wahi. Khoon wahi. Aur sirf khoon nahi.

Nehru likhte hain, dono ke purane dharmon ki jadein ek saath thi. Vaidik dharm mein aur Zoroastrian dharm mein bahut kuch saajha tha. Zoroastrian dharm — Iran ka purana dharm, jiski pothi Avesta hai.

Aur bhasha? Vaidik Sanskrit aur purani Pahlavi, jo Avesta ki zubaan hai, ek doosre se itni milti-julti hain, ki unhe alag-alag maanna mushkil hai.

Aage chalkar dono zubaanon ne alag raaste liye. Ek taraf classical Sanskrit, doosri taraf Farsi. Par unke bahut se mool shabd aaj bhi saajhe hain — jaise saare Aryan bhai-bhashaon mein hote hain.

Aur sirf zubaan aur dharm nahi. Kala bhi.

Farsi kala Iran ki mitti se juda hua tha — wahin ke rang, wahin ki roshni, wahin ke pahaad. Nehru kehte hain, Iran ki kala ki parampara itni sadiyon tak chalne ka raaz shayad yahi hai. Aur Bharatiya kala? Wo uttar ke barf wale pahaadon se, ghane jangalon se, aur badi nadiyon se utthi.

Do mitti. Do aasmaan. Aur ek hi mool.

Aur ek baat yaad rakhiye. Aage hum Iran se na kisi jang ke maidan par milenge, na kisi sandhi ke kaagaz par. Ye rishta darbaaron mein bana, kavita mein bana, aur imaaraton mein bana. Isi liye wo itne saal chala.

To ye do bhai mile kab pehli baar?

## Part 2: Parshavas se Parsi tak

Wo mile. Aur sirf mile hi nahi — dono ne ek doosre ko likhkar yaad bhi rakha.

Pahle ek purani nishani. Nehru likhte hain, Sindhu ghati ki sabhyata ka Iran aur Mesopotamia se bhi naata tha — mohron par, naqshon par. Dono jagah ke design aise milte-julte hain, ki wo ittefaq nahi lagte.

Aur ye dosti kitni purani thi? Avesta — wahi pothi jiska zikr abhi aaya — usme Bharat ka naam likha hai, aur uttar Bharat ka ek halka-sa naqsha bhi.

Yaani us daur se pehle bhi, jab Iran mein koi bada raaj hi nahi utha tha, dono taraf ek doosre ki khabar thi. Nehru use "Cyrus aur Darius se pehle ka daur" kehte hain.

Aur idhar? Rig Veda mein Iran ka zikr hai. Iran ke logon ko Rig Veda "Parshavas" kehti hai. Baad mein unhone "Parasikas" naam liya. Aur usi shabd se aaj ka "Parsi" bana.

Aur Irani? Unhone humein "Parthavas" kaha.

Ek naam, do zubaanon mein, aur neeche ek hi jadein. Nehru likhte hain, Iran aur uttar Bharat sabse purane zamaane se ek doosre mein dilchaspi rakhte the, aur ye aana-jaana baad ki sadiyon mein bhi nahi ruka.

Phir aate hain Cyrus — Iran ka wo raja, jise "sab rajaon ka raja" kaha gaya. Wo Bharat ki seema tak aaya: shayad Kabul tak, Baluchistan tak bhi.

Aur phir Darius. Chhathi sadi Isa poorv mein Iran ka raaj poore uttar-pashchim Bharat tak phaila hua tha. Sind uske andar tha. Aur shayad pashchim Punjab ka bhi kuch hissa.

Nehru likhte hain ki is daur ko kabhi "Bharat ke itihaas ka Zoroastrian daur" bhi kaha jaata hai. Uska asar door tak gaya. Aur us daur mein sooraj ki puja ko badhaya gaya.

Ek baat yaad rakhiye. Ye raaj bhoolne wala nahi tha. Uska sabse amir hissa yahin tha.

## Part 3: Darius ka sabse amir ilaaka

Dekhiye Darius ki jagah. Uske poore samrajya mein Bharat ka ilaaka sabse amir tha. Aur sabse zyada aabadi wala bhi.

Ek baat yaad rakhiye. Aaj ka Sindh ek sookha, ret bhara ilaaka hai. Us waqt Sindh aisa nahi tha. Hara bhara. Bada. Zinda.

Aur Darius ke darbar mein ek ginti rakhi jaati thi. Kahan se kitna maal aata hai.

Wo ginti ek Greek ne likhkar rakhi. Herodotus — wo Greek lekhak, jise aaj itihaas ka baap kaha jaata hai.

Herodotus likhte hain ki duniya ke jin logon ko hum jaante hain, unmein Bharatiya log sabse zyada hain, aur wahi sabse zyada khiraaj dete hain — teen sau saath talent, sone ki dhool ke roop mein. Us zamane ke hisaab se ye bahut bhaari rashi thi.

Aur khiraaj ke saath ek aur cheez aati thi. Herodotus likhte hain, Irani sen mein ek Bharatiya toli bhi thi. Pyaade. Ghudsawaar. Aur rath. Baad ke zamon mein haathi bhi.

Ab sirf khiraaj nahi. Vyapar bhi.

Saatvi sadi se pehle bhi ye raasta chalta tha. Nehru likhte hain ki Bharat aur Babylon ke beech vyapar bahut purana tha — Babylon, Mesopotamia ka wo bada shehar. Aur wo vyapar zyadatar Iran ki khadi ke raaste hota tha, jise aaj Persian Gulf kehte hain.

Chhathi sadi ke baad raasta aur khul gaya — Cyrus aur Darius ke hamlon ke saath. Ab Iran aur Bharat seedhe aamne-saamne the.

Sochiye. Ek taraf Bharat Iran ke samrajya ka sabse amir hissa tha — jahan se sabse zyada anaj aur sabse zyada sone ki dhool aati thi. Aur doosri taraf ek zubaan chal rahi thi, jo aage is desh ke ghar mein bas jaane wali thi.

Aur ek sawal wahin khada hota hai: ek ilaaka jo poore samrajya ka khazana bharta hai, aur uske ladke kisi doosre ke rath par. Ye kis tarah ka rishta tha?

## Part 4: Persepolis se Safed Bharat tak

Alexander ke baad Iran kai sadiyon tak Greekon ke haath mein raha. Par ek baat dekhiye. Raaj badla. Rishta nahi ruka.

Nehru likhte hain ki us daur mein bhi Iran aur Bharat ka aana-jaana chalta raha. Aur is rishte ki ek nishani patthar mein milti hai.

Persepolis. Persepolis — Iran ka wo shahi mehal shehar, jise Darius aur uske baad ke Irani rajaon ne banwaya tha. Nehru likhte hain ki Ashoka ki imaaraton par Persepolis ki banawat ka asar dikhta hai.

Zara sochiye: ek samraat jisne talwar chhodi, aur ek Irani mehal, patthar ke oonche khambon wala. Rishta talwar se nahi, karigari se chalta tha — ye tulna humari hai.

Aur kala? Usme to teen rang ghul gaye.

Uttar-pashchim ki seema par ek nayi kala utthi: ek taraf Greekon ka khiyal, doosra taraf Bharat ka bhaav, aur uspar Iran ka rang. Log use Yunani-Bauddh kala kehte hain.

Gupta kaal mein bhi, chauthi-paanchvi sadi ke us kala-wale daur mein, Iran se baat hoti rahi.

Aur ek ilaaka tha, jahan ye do log roz milte the. Kabul. Kandahar. Seistan. Ye seema wale ilaake aksar Bharat ka hissa bhi hote the.

Us ilaake ka naam hi badal gaya tha — baad ke Parthiyon ke zamon mein use "Safed Bharat" kaha jaata tha. Nehru ek French vidvaan ko yaad karte hain, jinka kehna tha ki Musalman vijay tak wo ilaaka Iran se zyada Bharat hi raha.

Sochiye — ek mitti, do naam. Irani log use apna kehte the, aur uspar is desh ka raaj chalta tha.

Vyapar bhi do raaste chalta tha. Uttar mein kaafile zameen ke raaste aate the. Dakshin mein samundar tha, aur samundar hi uski sadak.

Aur ek din ek dakshini raaj ne Iran ke rajaon ke paas apne doot bheje.

Par ye lambi dosti ek din ek chhat ke neeche aa gayi.

## Part 5: Farsi ghar mein, Iran imaarat mein

Iran ka asar is desh mein kabhi-kabhi nahi aata tha. Wo lagatar chalta raha.

Aur iske peeche ek baat thi: Iran apni jadein itni mazboot thi ki wo apne hamla-varon ko bhi apna leta tha.

Saatvi sadi mein Arabon ne Iran jeeta. Par kuch saalon mein wahi Arab Iran ke ho gaye — apne sadeh registan ki reet chhod kar unhone Iran ki padi hui sanskriti apna li.

Ab is desh ki taraf dekhiye.

Afghan aur Moghul rajaon ke daur mein Farsi is desh ke darbar ki zubaan thi. Raja ki. Uske vidvaanon ki. Uske kavi-mandli ki.

Ye kab tak chala? Angrezon ke aane tak. Bilkul Angrezon ke raaj ki shuruaat tak.

To iske chinh aaj kahan hain? Dekhiye apni zubaan.

Aaj ki koi bhi aadhunik Bharatiya bhasha uthaiye — usme Farsi ke shabd milenge. Hindustani to poora hi mila-jula bana, par dakshin ki Dravid bhashayein bhi is asar se bachi nahi.

Aur sirf lafz nahi. Is desh ne Farsi mein bade kavi diye. Nehru likhte hain, aaj bhi yahan Farsi ke bahut achhe vidvaan hain. Hindu bhi, aur Musalman bhi.

To Farsi ka darja kya tha? Nehru ek tulna dete hain: Europe mein French jaisa — ek aisi zubaan, jise poore Asia mein padhe-likhe log samajhte the.

Uska asar bhi utna hi chaura tha. Irani kala aur Irani reet Constantinople se lekar Gobi ke registaan ke kinare tak phaili thi. Constantinople — wo shehar, jo aaj Istanbul hai.

[SFX: patthar par chhaini ki halki thap-thap, dheemi]

Aur phir wo waqt aaya jab ye rishta patthar mein dikha.

Pandrahvi sadi ka daur tha. Europe mein Renaissance ka zamana. Aur hamare uttar-pashchim mein, Samarkand aur Bokhara ke shehron mein ek aur Renaissance phool raha tha — Timurid Renaissance — jispar Iran ka gehra asar tha.

Samarkand aur Bokhara — wo shehar, jahan se Babar nikla.

Babar ek Timurid shahzada tha. Wo isi mahaul se nikal kar aaya, aur Delhi ke takht par baitha. Solhvi sadi ki shuruaat mein.

Us waqt Iran par Safavi rajaon ka raaj tha. Us daur ko Farsi kala ka sunehra daur kaha jaata hai.

Aur Babar ke bete ko Iran ki zaroorat padi. Humayun. Museebat ke waqt wo Irani raja ke paas sharan mein gaya, aur usi ki madad se wapas aakar usne is desh ka takht sanbhala.

Iske baad Moghul rajaon ne Iran se rishta aur mazboot rakha.

Irani vidvaanon aur kalaakaaron ki ek toli is seema ko paar karke aane lagi. Unka lakshya ek tha: Badshah ka darbar.

Inn raaston ka nateeja ek nayi banawat mein dikha — us imaarat ke dhang mein Bharatiya aadarsh the, aur Farsi inspiration.

Delhi aur Agra khoobsurat imaaraton se bhar gaye. Aur unmein sabse mashhoor kaun? Taj Mahal.

Ek French vidvaan ne uske baare mein likha: Taj Mahal Iran ki rooh hai, jo Bharat ke jism mein utar aayi.

Sochiye. Duniya us imaarat ko is desh ki imaarat kehti hai. Aur uske andar Iran ki rooh baithi hai.

To ye rishta tootaa kaise?

## Part 6: Nadir Shah, aur band darwaze

Nehru ek badi baat likhte hain: mool se lekar poori history tak, shayad hi duniya ke koi do log itne kareeb rahe hon, jitne is desh aur Iran ke log.

Itna lamba rishta. Itna apna rishta. Aur uski aakhri yaad kya bani?

Nadir Shah ka hamla — do sau saal pehle, ek chhota sa waqt, par bhayanak. Nehru likhte hain, wo is lambi, izzat wali dosti ki aakhri yaad hai.

Uske baad wo aaye. Angrez.

[SFX: door ki ghode ki taap, dheemi; uske baad bhaari darwaze ke band hone ki ek thak]

Aur unhone sirf raaj nahi kiya — unhone darwaze band kar diye. Wo saare raaste, jo is desh ko uske Asia wale padosiyon se jodte the, band kar diye.

Naye raaste khole gaye — samundar ke raaste, jo humein Europe ke kareeb le gaye, aur Angrez ke kareeb.

Par zameen ke wo raaste band ho gaye.

Iran. Central Asia. China. Inse koi baat nahi hoti rahi — bahut lambe waqt tak. Jab tak hawa ke jahaaz aaye, tab ja kar wo purana saath phir juda.

Nehru ise Angrezon ke raaj ka sabse ajeeb aur sabse dukhad nateeja kehte hain. Ye desh Asia se kat gaya. Poori Asia se.

Aaj sochiye. Aaj wahi darwaze khule hain, aur hawa ke raaste bhi. Par ek sawal bacha hua hai: darwaze khul jaane se rishta laut kar aata hai? Ya rishta lautane ke liye ek dhun chahiye?

Par ek cheez bachi rahi. Ek rishta jo kabhi nahi toota.

Nehru likhte hain, terah sau saal pehle, jab Islam Iran mein aaya, to purane Zoroastrian dharm ke kuch sau ya kuch hazaar log is desh ki taraf nikal aaye. Unhe yahan jagah mili. Wo paschim ki tati par bas gaye — apna dharm manate rahe, apni reet chalate rahe — kisi ne unhe roka nahi.

Nehru ek baat par rukte hain. Ye Parsi — jaisa unhe kaha jaata hai — chupchap is desh mein ghul-mil gaye, ise apna ghar bana liya, aur phir bhi apni purani reet thaam kar ek alag si chhoti basti ki tarah khade hain. Bahar se shaadi nahi hoti thi — par yahan ye ajeeb bhi nahi tha, kyunki har jaati apne hi behron mein byah karti thi. Vyapar mein wo aage badhe, aur aaj unmein se bahut log is desh ke udyog ke sardar hain. Iran se unka koi rishta nahi raha. Wo poori tarah Hindustani hain — aur phir bhi apne purane desh ki yaad thaam kar rakhte hain.

Aur Iran? Wahan log phir apne us daur ki taraf dekh rahe hain jo Islam se pehle ka tha. Ye dharm ki baat nahi — ye apni virasat par garv ki baat hai.

Nehru likhte hain, Europe ke raaj ka daur ek bure sapne ki tarah guzar gaya, aur purane dost phir ek doosre ko yaad karne lage. Wo ye bhi likhte hain: aane wale waqt mein Bharat Iran ke aur kareeb aayega, jaise China ke.

To pehle padosi ka jawab yahan mil jaata hai. Iran. Sabse purana. Sabse zinda.

Par ek padosi aur bhi tha — ek aisa padosi, jiski chamak itni tez thi ki log usse Europe ki sabhyata ka ghar kehte hain.

## Part 7: Chamak jo doob gayi

Greece ki taraf chaliye.

Yunani logon ke baare mein ek kahawat duniya mein chali hui hai: ki poore Europe ki sabhyata ka chashma Greece hai. Aur ek lambi bahes likhi jaati hai — Purab aur Pashchim ka farq, Orient aur Occident.

Iske liye ek badi daleel di jaati hai: ki is duniya mein kuch bhi hile-dhule jo asal mein Greek na ho.

Par Nehru in baaton par sawal rakhte hain. Pehla: ye Purab-Pashchim ka farq unhe samajh hi nahi aata — unke hisaab se ye dhundhla hai, unscientific hai. Aur doosra: Europe ke vidvaan, jo Greek aur Latin mein itne gehre the, unhe is desh aur China ke baare mein bahut kam pata tha.

Nehru batate hain ki Europe ki padhai lambe waqt tak sirf teen zubaanon mein band thi — Greek, Hebrew, aur Latin. Us padhai se duniya ki jo tasveer bani, wo bhoomdhyasagar ki thi, jispar Plato aur Aristotle chhaye hue the — aur ye tasveer vigyan ke raaste mein bhi khadi thi.

Aur ek adhyaapak ne ek tez baat likhi: Greek sabhyata jis purabi pichhwade par khadi hui, usse wo kabhi poora alag hua hi nahi — alag wo sirf padhe-likhon ke dimaag mein hua.

Upar se Europe ka kaarkhaana aaya. Kaarkhane aur daulat ke saath ye farq aur gehra ho gaya — aur ek ajeeb si dalil banti gayi: aaj ka Europe aur America purane Greece ki santaan hain.

Nehru ise manne ko taiyar nahi hain. In sabhyataon mein koi jadd ka rishta nahi hai. Purani sabhyatayein — Greek, Bharatiya, Cheeni, Irani — sab zindagi ka ek dhang, ek santulan dhoondh rahi thi. Aur aaj ka Europe? Uska sabse bada sapna hai — aaram. Ye sapna purane Greece ki kisi bhi kitaab mein nahi milta.

Phir Nehru ek doosri baat likhte hain: har desh ka apna ek sandesh hai. Greece apne aap mein ek poori cheez hai — saaf, aur apne dhang mein behtareen. Waise hi Bharat, waise hi China, waise hi Iran.

Greece ki chamak badi thi, par uski zindagi chhoti thi. Wo aaj sirf apni kaamyaabi mein zinda hai — apne asar mein, aur us chhote, chamkile din ki yaad mein. Nehru ka andaaza: shayad wo apne vartmaan mein itna kho gayi ki wo ateet ban gayi.

To is desh ko kya cheez in lambi sadiyon tak zinda rakhti hai?

Nehru likhte hain, koi gupt gyaan nahi, koi chhipa hua raaz nahi. Bas ek narm insaani dil, ek mila-jula sahishnu culture, aur zindagi ki ek gehri samajh.

Aur ek baat, jo Nehru saaf kehte hain: Bharat aaj ke Europe se nahi, purane Greece ke zyada kareeb hai. Log kehte hain, Bharat dharmik hai, soch mein kho jaane wala. Nehru kehte hain: haan — par isse bahut zyada bhi hai.

Par humare paas us kala ka sirf chhota hissa bacha hai. Phir bhi, jo bach gaya, wo bolta hai.

Elephanta ki gufaon mein ek Trimurti hai. Nehru likhte hain, ye Trimurti shayad khud is desh ki bahu-mukhi murat hai — zordaar, jiski aankhon mein ek taaqat hai. Aur Ajanta ki deewaron par tasveerein hain — zindagi se pyar, sundarta se pyar, aur phir bhi jaise kisi gehrai ki halki si chhap.

## Part 8: Do desh, ek dhang

Ye do desh ek doosre ko naye nahi the. Ye sampark sabse purane likhe hue daur se chalta aa raha tha aur aage chalkar Greek ilaake se bhi juda.

Ab dekhiye mitti hi. Greece aur Bharat — mitti alag thi.

Nehru likhte hain: Greece mein asli nadiyan nahi hain. Jungle nahi hain. Bade ped nahi hain. Wahan samundar hai — aur wo samundar Greek logon par is desh ke logon se zyada chhaya. Is desh ki zindagi zameen ki thi — bade maidan, oonche pahaad, badi nadiyan, gehre jungle.

Pahaad dono jagah the: Greece ne apne devta Olympus par rakhe, humne apne devta aur rishi Himalaya par. Aur dono jagah dev-katha aur itihaas aise ghul gaye ki alag karna mushkil ho gaya.

Zara sochiye, Greek log kaisa the — na aish-parast, na sanyasi. Jo bhi karte the, poore ho kar karte the, aur isi liye wo hamein aaj ke logon se zyada zinda lagte hain. Is desh ki purani kitaabon se bhi wahi tasveer uthti hai.

Haan, yahan tyaag ka ek pehlu tha — par wo thode se logon tak. Aage Jain aur Buddh dharm ke asar mein wo bada hua, par zindagi ka poora rang nahi badla.

To dono jagah zindagi ko waise hi apnaya gaya, jaisi wo thi — poori, bharpoor. Aur dono jagah ek baat maani gayi: ki ek andar ki zindagi hai, jo sabse oopar hai.

Isi bharose se dono jagah jigyaasa paida hui, soch paida hui. Par ye soch kis taraf gayi?

Wo log bahar ki cheezon ko chhoo kar nahi seekhte the. Wo apne mann mein baith kar, jin vichaaron ko wo pehle se sahi maante the, unhi par tark khada karte the. Yaani tark tha, par tajurba nahi.

Aur ye sirf Greece aur Bharat ki baat nahi thi — ye us poore zamane ka dhang tha, jab vigyan ka tareeka aaya hi nahi tha.

Aur ye soch sirf chand padhe-likhon mein nahi thi; aam log bhi isse door nahi the.

To aam log kahan sochte the?

Yahan Nehru ek khoobsurat tasveer dete hain. Wo zindagi mili-juli thi — jaise aaj bhi is desh mein hai, gaon mein khaas. Bazaar. Mandir ya masjid ka aangan. Kuan ka kinara. Panchayat ki jagah. Yahin din ki khabar baanti jaati thi, yahin logon ki raay banti thi. Aur ye sab karne ke liye waqt bhi tha — logon ke paas fursat thi.

Aur saal? Saal melon se banta tha. Dono jagah mausam badalne par mela hota tha — mela insaan ko mausam se jod kar rakhta tha.

Nehru likhte hain, ye mela aaj bhi hamare paas hain. Basant ka. Faslon ke katne ka. Deepavali — patjhad ke ant ka roshni ka utsav. Aur garmi se pehle Holi. In melon mein aaj bhi gaana-naach hai — lok geet, lok naach, aur rasa-lila.

To soch ke maamle mein Bharat aur Greece door nahi the — wo bahut paas the.

## Part 9: Do ghar, do reet

Nehru likhte hain, purane Bharat mein aurat par parda nahi tha. Sirf ek jagah thoda tha — rajaon aur bade sardaron ke ghar. Warna nahi.

Aur Greece? Wahan halaat ulte the. Wahan aurat aur mard ka mel-jol is desh se zyada bandha hua tha.

Idhar kitaabon mein gyani auraton ka zikr milta hai. Aur sirf zikr nahi. Wo sabhaon ki bahes mein hissa leti thi.

Shaadi? Greece mein shaadi ek sauda thi. Ek samjhauta. Aur yahan? Yahan shaadi ek sanskar thi.

Aur ek ajeeb baat: Yunani auratein is desh mein khinchti thi. Purane natakon mein rajdarbar ki jo sewikaayein likhi hui hain, wo aksar Yunani hoti thi.

Nehru ek nishani dete hain. Gujarat ke ek bade bandar par — Barygaza, jise aaj Broach kehte hain — Greece se jo cheezein aati thi, unmein gaane wale ladke aur sundar ladkiyan bhi thi.

Aur ek cheez, jiske bina wo darbar nahi chalta tha. Sharaab.

Megasthenes — wo Greek, jisne Chandragupta ke darbar ko apni aankhon se dekha. Wo likhte hain ki raja ka khaana auratein tayyar karti thi. Aur wahi unhe sharaab bhi deti thi. Nehru jodte hain, wo sharaab saare Bharatiya peete the.

Aur kuch sharaab Greek ilaakon se aati thi. Ek purane Tamil kavi ne likha hai. Yavana log apne achhe jahaazon mein wo thandi, khushbu wali sharaab le kar aate the. Yavana yaani Greek.

Pataliputra ke ek raja ne ek Greek raja ko sandesh bheja. Usne kaha, mujhe meethi sharaab bhejiye. Sookhe anjeer bhejiye. Aur ek aisa pandit bhejiye, jo ghoom-ghoom kar gyaan bechta hai.

Us Greek raja ka naam Antiochus tha. Uska jawab? Anjeer aur sharaab hum bhej denge. Par Greece mein kanoon aise pandit ko bechne ki ijazat nahi deta.

Do duniya, do reet, aur ek darbar.

Ek baat aur, jise hum ek hi jumle mein chhod denge. Nehru kehte hain, Greek sahitya mein mard-mard ka prem bura nahi maana jaata tha; Iran ke sahitya mein bhi waisi reet milti hai; aur Sanskrit sahitya mein iska koi nishaan nahi. Ye unki apni padhai hai — ispar hum "Aaj ke hisaab se" mein baat karenge.

Ye do padosi khaali padosi nahi the — wo apne ghar ke logon ka aana-jaana apne saath laate the.

Par Greece ke paas ek cheez thi, jo is desh ke paas nahi thi. Aur wahi cheez uski sabse badi daulat thi.

## Part 10: Taaza hawa

Nehru likhte hain, Greek sabhyata mein ek cheez baaki sabse alag thi: tajurba karne wala vigyan — experimental science.

Aur ye Greece mein nahi hui, balki Alexandria mein — Egypt ka wo shehar, jo Greekon ne basaya tha. Wahan ye vigyan poore zor se chala.

Kab? Isa se poorv 330 se 130 tak. Ye do sau saal vigyan aur naye yantron ki khoj ke itihaas mein alag khade hain.

Us waqt duniya mein aisa kuch kahin nahi tha — is desh mein nahi, kisi bhi jagah nahi. Agli baar aisa daur satrahvin sadi mein aaya.

Aur Rome? Rome ke paas bada samrajya tha, apne daur ka aman, aur Greek sabhyata se seedhe rishte. Par usne vigyan mein kuch nahi joda.

Jab Europe mein purani sabhyata toot gayi, tab vigyan ka diya kisne jalaya rakha? Arabon ne. Poora madhya kaal.

To ye vigyan aaya kahan se? Nehru kehte hain, ye waqt ki zaroorat se aaya — ek badhta hua samaaj, samundari safar. Jahan zaroorat thi, wahan vigyan khada hua.

Aur yahi baat is desh mein hui. Ganit aage badha. Algebra aaya. Shunya ka chinh aaya. Ankdon ko jagah ke hisaab se likhne ka tareeka aaya — wo bhi waqt ki zaroorat se, badhte vyapaar se.

Par Nehru imaandar hain: ye pata karna mushkil hai ki Greek logon mein vigyan ki bhaavna kitni thi. Shayad wo bhi apne purane dhang hi chalte the.

Unka dhang kya tha? Insaan ke andar, aur qudrat ke saath, ek sur aur ek santulan dhoondhna. Aur wahi dhang Greece aur is desh ka saajha tha.

Ujjayini ka khagol mandir — jahan taare dekhe jaate the — Alexandria se juda hua tha. Ek Greek pothi kehti hai ki is desh ke kuch vidvaan Socrates ke paas gaye the aur unhone unse sawal poochhe. Aur Pythagoras — jiske naam par aaj school mein triangle wala niyam chalta hai — uspar is desh ke darshan ka asar maana jaata hai.

Phir aayi ek aur sadi, aur ek doosra vidvaan.

Alberuni — Central Asia se aaya ek vidvaan, jo gyarhvin sadi mein is desh mein aaya. Baghdad mein usne Greek darshan padha tha, aur yahan usne Sanskrit seekhi — sirf isliye ki wo is desh ka darshan padh sake. Usne dono soch ki tulna apni kitaab mein likhi.

Usne ek baat aur likhi. Ki yahan ki Sanskrit kitaabon mein Greek aur Roman khagol ki baatein likhi hui hain. Do duniya, ek almari mein.

Nehru kehte hain, dono sabhyatayein ek doosre par asar daalti rahi — par dono itni mazboot thi ki apni-apni lakeer par chalti rahi. Aur aaj ek ulta jhonka uttha hai: ab log us purani reet se peeche hat rahe hain jo har cheez Greece aur Rome ka kar deti thi, aur Asia ka hissa bahut bada nikla hai.

Par is episode ka sabse ajeeb tohfa ab bacha hua hai.

## Part 11: But — ek lafz jo Buddha se aaya

Wahi tohfa, jise is desh ne apna liya, aur phir sadiyon tak sajaya.

Nehru likhte hain, murat ki puja is desh mein Greece se aayi. Ye ek dilchasp khiyal hai.

Dekhiye. Vaidik dharm mein murat ki puja mana thi. Devtaon ke mandir tak nahi the. Kuch purane dharmon mein murat ki puja ki halki nishaniyan thi, par wo aam nahi thi.

Aur Buddh dharm? Wo bhi murat ka sakht khilaf tha. Buddha ki moorat banane par saaf rok thi.

Par Afghanistan aur seema wale ilaakon mein Greek kala ka asar tez tha. Aur dheere-dheere usne apna raasta bana liya.

Shuru mein Buddha ki moorat nahi bani. Pehle wo mooratein bani, jaisi Greek devta Apollo ki thi — Bodhisattva ki mooratein. Bodhisattva, yaani Buddha ke pichhle janm, unse pehle ke roop.

Uske baad Buddha ki khud ki mooratein bani.

Aur isse Hindu dharm ke kuch roopon mein murat ki puja ko badhawa mila.

Aur ek lafz yaad rakhiye. Farsi mein, aur Hindustani mein bhi, murat ko "but" kehte hain. Wo lafz "Buddha" se aaya hai.

Sochiye. Ek Greek kala ne is desh ke devtaon ko chehra diya. Aur us chehre ka naam Buddha se aaya. Do padosi, ek lafz ke andar.

Aur aakhir mein Nehru ek badi baat likhte hain.

Insaan ke mann mein ek bhi bhookh hoti hai — wo dhoondhna chahta hai ki sab kuch aakhir mein ek hai. Zindagi mein, qudrat mein, aur us poore brahmaand mein.

Nehru likhte hain, aaj ke kuch tez vichaarak kehte hain ki aisa koi ek nahi hai — koi niyam nahi, koi ekta nahi. Ho sakta hai wo theek hon.

Par Nehru kehte hain, ye bhram bhi bekaar nahi gaya. Greece mein, is desh mein, aur bahut jagah — is ekta ki talash se kuch nikla. Ek sur. Ek santulan. Ek gehri zindagi.

Ab cold open wala kamra yaad kijiye. Allahabad. 1944. Us mehmaan ki baat.

Do bhai. Ek purab, ek pashchim. Aur unke paas bachi ek cheez — bansuri ki kuch purani dhun.

To jawab yahi hai.

Is desh ke sabse purane padosi Iran the. Sabse purane, aur sabse zinda.

Unse rishta kisi jang ne nahi tootaa. Samundar ke naye raaste ne tootaa — wo raaste jo is desh ko Asia se kaat kar Europe se jod gaye. Aur wo darwaze humne band nahi kiye the.

Aur doosre padosi ka jawab bhi yahin hai. Greece ka rishta toota hi nahi, kyunki wo kabhi lamba hua hi nahi. Chamak badi thi, par din chhota. Wo doob gaya.

Par jaane se pehle, usne is desh ko apne but de diye.

[SFX: bansuri ki wahi dhun, ek baar, poori; phir ghul jaati hai]

Dono padosi apne nishaan chhod gaye. Ek zubaan ke roop mein. Ek imaarat ke roop mein. Ek chehre ke roop mein.

Aur ek dhun ke roop mein, jo aaj bhi bajayi ja sakti hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli. Nehru ne 1944 mein likha tha ki is desh aur Iran ke rishte phir judenge. Aaj wo darwaze khule hue hain. Par ek sawal bacha hua hai. Padosi hone ke liye darwaze kaafi hote hain? Ya padosi hone ke liye ek dhun chahiye?

Doosri. Us ek jumle ke baare mein, jo humne Greek sahitya par kaha tha. Nehru ne 1944 mein ek poore padosi ke sahitya ke baare mein do jumlon mein faisla likh diya. Aaj hum aisa nahi karte. Aaj hum apne padosi ki kitaabein khud padhte hain, uski zubaan seekhte hain, usse khud poochte hain. Ek kitaab ka, ek sadi ka, ek aadmi ka faisla kisi poore desh ko naapne ke liye kaafi nahi hota.

Teesri. Wahi kahani, ek baar phir. Do bhai, do ghar, ek dhun. Aapke ghar mein aaj kaun si purani cheez bajti hai, jo aapke padosi ke ghar mein bhi bajti hai? Ek geet? Ya koi aisa shabd, jo dono jagah ek hi matlab deta hai?

## Outro

Ek padosi doob gaya. Par jaane se pehle, usne is desh ko apne but de diye — apne chehre, apni mooratein.

Aur usi padosi ne humein ek bada sawal bhi de diya.

Sadiyon baad, jab Europe ne is desh ka purana natak khoja, to usne turant poochha: kya ye natak Yunani natak se aaya tha? Ye sawal Europe ne sau saal bahas kiya. Aur 1789 mein ek Angrez ne is desh ke ek natak ka anuvaad chhapa — aur Europe hil gaya.

Agli baar: Shakuntala ka safar.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
