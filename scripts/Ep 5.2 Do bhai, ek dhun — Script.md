## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar hum Maurya kaal ke baad ke Bharat par ruke the. Us episode ke aakhir mein, Allahabad mein, ek mehmaan ne do bhaiyon ki kahani sunayi thi. Aaj wahi kahani khulti hai.

## Part 1: Ek mool, do raahein

Aaiye, bahut peechhe chalte hain. Itne peechhe ki wahan "Iran" aur "Bharat" jaise naam bhi nahi the.

Nehru likhte hain ki is desh ke jitne bhi sampark rehte aaye hain, unmein sabse purana Iran hai. Aur sabse zinda bhi. Sabse lambe waqt tak chalne wala.

Ye naata kisi jang se shuru nahi hota. Ye naata us din se hai, jab ek hi mool se do shaakhayein alag hui.

Sochiye ek purana ghar. Ek hi chhat, ek hi aangan. Phir do bhai bant jaate hain. Ek purab ki taraf aa jaata hai. Doosra wahin pashchim mein reh jaata hai.

Bhai wahi. Khoon wahi. Aur sirf khoon nahi.

Nehru likhte hain, dono ke purane dharmon ki jadein ek saath thi. Vaidik dharm mein aur Zoroastrian dharm mein bahut kuch saajha tha. Zoroastrian dharm — Iran ka purana dharm, jiski pothi Avesta hai.

Aur bhasha? Vaidik Sanskrit aur purani Pahlavi, jo Avesta ki zubaan hai, ek doosre se bahut milti-julti hain. Itni, ki unhe alag-alag maanna mushkil hai.

Aage chalkar dono zubaanon ne alag raaste liye. Ek taraf classical Sanskrit, doosri taraf Farsi. Par unke bahut se mool shabd aaj bhi saajhe hain. Jaise saare Aryan bhai-bhashaon mein hote hain.

Aur sirf zubaan aur dharm nahi. Kala bhi.

Farsi kala Iran ki mitti se juda hua tha. Wahin ke rang, wahin ki roshni, wahin ke pahaad. Isi liye wo itne saalon tak zinda raha. Aur Bharatiya kala? Wo uttar ke barf wale pahaadon se, ghane jangalon se, aur badi nadiyon se utthi.

Do mitti. Do aasmaan. Aur ek hi mool.

To ye do bhai mile kab pehli baar?

## Part 2: Parshavas se Parsi tak

Wo mile. Aur sirf mile hi nahi, dono ne ek doosre ko likhkar yaad bhi rakha.

Pahle ek purani nishani. Sindhu ghati ki sabhyata ka Iran aur Mesopotamia se bhi naata tha. Uske nishaan milte hain. Mohron par. Naqshon par. Dono jagah ke design aise milte-julte hain, ki wo ittefaq nahi lagte.

Aur ye dosti kitni purani thi? Nehru kehte hain, Iran aur Bharat ke aane-jaane ke nishaan us daur se bhi milte hain, jo Cyrus aur Darius ke daur se pehle ke hain.

Yaani us daur se pehle, jab Iran mein koi bada raaj hi nahi utha tha. Aur ye dilchaspi doosri taraf se bhi thi. Nehru likhte hain, Iran aur uttar Bharat dono sabse purane zamaane se ek doosre mein dilchaspi rakhte the. Ye aana-jaana baad ki sadiyon mein bhi nahi ruka.

Ab ek kitab dekhiye. Avesta mein Bharat ka naam likha hai. Aur sirf naam nahi. Us kitab mein uttar Bharat ka ek halka-sa naqsha bhi hai.

Aur idhar? Rig Veda mein Iran ka zikr hai. Iran ke logon ko Rig Veda "Parshavas" kehti hai. Baad mein unhone "Parasikas" naam liya. Aur usi shabd se aaj ka "Parsi" bana.

Aur Irani? Unhone humein "Parthavas" kaha.

Ek naam, do zubaanon mein, aur neeche ek hi jadein.

Phir aate hain Cyrus. Cyrus — Iran ka wo raja, jise "sab rajaon ka raja" kaha gaya. Wo Bharat ki seema tak aaya. Shayad Kabul tak. Uske saamne poori Asia thi, aur wo uttar-pashchim ki taraf badhta chala gaya.

Aur phir Darius. Chhathi sadi Isa poorv mein Iran ka raaj poore uttar-pashchim Bharat tak phaila hua tha. Sind uske andar tha. Aur shayad pashchim Punjab ka bhi kuch hissa.

Nehru likhte hain ki is daur ko kabhi "Bharat ke itihaas ka Zoroastrian daur" bhi kaha jaata hai. Uska asar door tak gaya. Aur us daur mein sooraj ki puja ko badhaya gaya.

Ek baat yaad rakhiye. Ye raaj bhoolne wala nahi tha. Uska sabse amir hissa yahin tha.

## Part 3: Darius ka sabse amir ilaaka

Dekhiye Darius ki jagah. Uske poore samrajya mein Bharat ka ilaaka sabse amir tha. Aur sabse zyada aabadi wala bhi.

Ek baat yaad rakhiye. Aaj ka Sindh ek sookha, ret bhara ilaaka hai. Us waqt Sindh aisa nahi tha. Hara bhara. Bada. Zinda.

Aur Darius ke darbar mein ek ginti rakhi jaati thi. Kahan se kitna maal aata hai.

Wo ginti ek Greek ne likhkar rakhi. Herodotus — wo Greek lekhak, jise itihaas ka baap kaha jaata hai.

Herodotus likhte hain ki duniya ke jin logon ko hum jaante hain, unmein Bharatiya log sabse zyada hain. Aur wahi sabse zyada khiraaj dete hain. Teen sau saath talent, sone ki dhool ke roop mein. Us zamane ke hisaab se ye bahut bhaari rashi thi.

Aur khiraaj ke saath ek aur cheez aati thi. Herodotus likhte hain, Irani sen mein ek Bharatiya toli bhi thi. Pyaade. Ghudsawaar. Aur rath. Baad ke zamon mein haathi bhi.

Ab sirf khiraaj nahi. Vyapar bhi.

Saatvi sadi se pehle bhi ye raasta chalta tha. Nehru likhte hain ki Bharat aur Babylon ke beech vyapar bahut purana tha. Babylon — Mesopotamia ka wo bada shehar. Aur wo vyapar zyadatar Iran ki khadi ke raaste hota tha. Us khadi ko aaj Persian Gulf kehte hain.

Chhathi sadi ke baad raasta aur khul gaya. Cyrus aur Darius ke hamlon ke saath. Ab Iran aur Bharat seedhe aamne-saamne the.

Sochiye. Ek taraf Bharat Iran ke samrajya ka sabse amir hissa tha. Aur doosri taraf ek zubaan chal rahi thi, jo aage is desh ke ghar mein bas jaane wali thi.

## Part 4: Persepolis se Safed Bharat tak

Alexander ke baad Iran kai sadiyon tak Greekon ke haath mein raha. Par ek baat dekhiye. Raaj badla. Rishta nahi ruka.

Nehru likhte hain ki us daur mein bhi Iran aur Bharat ka aana-jaana chalta raha. Aur is rishte ki ek nishani patthar mein milti hai.

Persepolis. Persepolis — Iran ka wo shahi mehal shehar, jise Darius aur uske baad ke Irani rajaon ne banwaya tha. Nehru likhte hain ki Ashoka ki imaaraton par Persepolis ki banawat ka asar dikhta hai.

Sochiye, kaisi baat hai. Ek samraat, jisne talwar chhodi. Aur ek Irani mehal, patthar ke oonche khambon wala. Rishta talwar se nahi, karigari se chalta tha.

Aur kala? Usme to teen rang ghul gaye.

Uttar-pashchim ki seema par ek nayi kala utthi. Usme ek taraf Greekon ka khiyal tha, doosra taraf Bharat ka bhaav. Aur uspar Iran ka rang. Log use Yunani-Bauddh kala kehte hain.

Aage Gupta kaal mein bhi ye baat chalti rahi. Chauthi-paanchvi sadi ka wo daur, jo kala ke liye yaad kiya jaata hai. Us daur mein bhi Iran se baat hoti rahi.

Aur ek ilaaka tha, jahan ye do log roz milte the. Kabul. Kandahar. Seistan. Ye seema wale ilaake aksar Bharat ka hissa bhi hote the.

Us ilaake ka naam hi badal gaya tha. Baad ke Parthiyon ke zamon mein use "Safed Bharat" kaha jaata tha.

Sochiye. Ek mitti, do naam. Irani log use apna kehte the, aur uspar is desh ka raaj chalta tha.

Vyapar bhi do raaste chalta tha. Uttar mein kaafile zameen ke raaste aate the. Dakshin mein samundar tha, aur samundar hi uski sadak.

Aur ek din ek dakshini raaj ne Iran ke rajaon ke paas apne doot bheje.

Par ye lambi dosti ek din ek chhat ke neeche aa gayi.

## Part 5: Farsi ghar mein, Iran imaarat mein

Iran ka asar is desh mein kabhi-kabhi nahi aata tha. Wo lagatar chalta raha.

Aur iske peeche ek baat thi. Iran apni jadein itni mazboot thi, ki wo apne hamla-varon ko bhi apna leta tha.

Saatvi sadi mein Arabon ne Iran jeeta. Par kuch saalon mein wahi Arab Iran ke ho gaye. Apne sadeh registan ke sahaj reet chhod kar unhone Iran ki padi hui sanskriti apna li.

Ab is desh ki taraf dekhiye.

Afghan aur Moghul rajaon ke daur mein Farsi is desh ke darbar ki zubaan thi. Raja ki. Uske vidvaanon ki. Uske kavi-mandli ki.

Ye kab tak chala? Angrezon ke aane tak. Bilkul Angrezon ke raaj ki shuruaat tak.

To iske chinh aaj kahan hain? Dekhiye apni zubaan.

Aaj ki koi bhi aadhunik Bharatiya bhasha uthaiye. Usme Farsi ke shabd milenge. Sanskrit se nikli bhashaon ke liye ye swabhaavik tha. Hindustani to poora hi mila-jula bana. Par dakshin ki Dravid bhashayein bhi is asar se bachi nahi.

Aur sirf lafz nahi. Is desh ne Farsi mein bade kavi diye. Nehru likhte hain, aaj bhi yahan Farsi ke bahut achhe vidvaan hain. Hindu bhi, aur Musalman bhi.

To Farsi ka darja kya tha? Nehru ek tulna dete hain. Europe mein French jaisa. Ek aisi zubaan, jise poore Asia mein padhe-likhe log samajhte the.

Uska asar bhi utna hi chaura tha. Irani kala aur Irani reet Constantinople se lekar Gobi ke registaan ke kinare tak phaili thi. Constantinople — wo shehar, jo aaj Istanbul hai.

[SFX: patthar par chhaini ki halki thap-thap, dheemi]

Aur phir wo waqt aaya jab ye rishta patthar mein dikha.

Pandrahvi sadi ka daur tha. Europe mein Renaissance ka zamana. Aur hamare uttar-pashchim mein, Samarkand aur Bokhara ke shehron mein ek aur Renaissance phool raha tha. Use Timurid Renaissance kehte hain. Aur uspar Iran ka gehra asar tha.

Samarkand aur Bokhara — wo shehar, jahan se Babar nikla.

Babar ek Timurid shahzada tha. Wo isi mahaul se nikal kar aaya, aur Delhi ke takht par baitha. Solhvi sadi ki shuruaat mein.

Us waqt Iran par Safavi rajaon ka raaj tha. Us daur ko Farsi kala ka sunehra daur kaha jaata hai.

Aur Babar ke bete ko Iran ki zaroorat padi. Humayun. Museebat ke waqt wo Irani raja ke paas sharan mein gaya. Usi ki madad se wo wapas aaya. Aur wapas aakar usne is desh ka takht sanbhala.

Iske baad Moghul rajaon ne Iran se rishta aur mazboot rakha.

Ek toli Irani vidvaanon aur kalaakaaron ki is seema ko paar karke aane lagi. Unka lakshya ek hi tha. Badshah ka darbar.

Inn raaston ka nateeja ek nayi banawat mein dikha. Is desh mein ek nayi imaarat ka dhang uttha. Usme Bharatiya aadarsh the, aur Farsi inspiration.

Delhi aur Agra khoobsurat imaaraton se bhar gaye. Aur unmein sabse mashhoor kaun? Taj Mahal.

Uske baare mein ek French vidvaan ne ek line likhi. Usne kaha, Taj Mahal Iran ki rooh hai, jo Bharat ke jism mein utar aayi.

Sochiye. Duniya us imaarat ko is desh ki imaarat kehti hai. Aur uske andar Iran ki rooh baithi hai.

To ye rishta tootaa kaise?

## Part 6: Nadir Shah, aur band darwaze

Nehru yahan ek badi baat likhte hain. Wo kehte hain, mool se lekar poori history tak, shayad hi duniya ke koi do log itne kareeb rahe hon, jitne is desh aur Iran ke log.

Itna lamba rishta. Itna apna rishta. Aur uski aakhri yaad kya bani?

Nadir Shah ka hamla. Do sau saal pehle. Ek chhota sa waqt, par bhayanak. Nehru likhte hain, wo is lambi, izzat wali dosti ki aakhri yaad hai.

Uske baad wo aaye. Angrez.

[SFX: door ki ghode ki taap, dheemi; uske baad bhaari darwaze ke band hone ki ek thak]

Aur unhone sirf raaj nahi kiya. Unhone darwaze band kar diye. Wo saare raaste, jo is desh ko uske Asia wale padosiyon se jodte the, band kar diye.

Naye raaste khole gaye. Samundar ke raaste. Wo raaste humein Europe ke kareeb le gaye, aur Angrez ke kareeb.

Par zameen ke wo raaste band ho gaye.

Iran. Central Asia. China. Inse koi baat nahi hoti rahi. Bahut lambe waqt tak. Jab tak hawa ke jahaaz aaye. Tab ja kar wo purana saath phir juda.

Nehru is baat ko is desh par Angrezon ke raaj ka sabse ajeeb aur sabse dukhad nateeja kehte hain. Ye desh Asia se kat gaya. Poori Asia se.

Zara sochiye. Ek desh, apne saare padosiyon se kat gaya.

Aaj sochiye. Aaj wahi darwaze khule hain. Hawa ke raaste khule hain, aur naye raaste bhi. Par ek sawal bacha hua hai. Darwaze khul jaane se rishta laut kar aata hai? Ya rishta lautane ke liye ek dhun chahiye?

Isi waqt Iran mein ek aur cheez ho rahi thi. Wahan log apne us purane daur ki taraf laut kar dekh rahe the, jo Islam se pehle ka tha. Ye dharm ki baat nahi thi. Ye apni virasat par garv ki baat thi.

Aur aaj? Asia ke desh phir ek doosre ki taraf dekhne lage hain.

Nehru likhte hain, Europe ke raaj ka daur ek bure sapne ki tarah guzar gaya. Aur purane dost phir ek doosre ko yaad karne lage.

Aur Nehru ek bharosa bhi likhte hain. Aane wale waqt mein Bharat Iran ke aur kareeb aayega. Jaise wo China ke kareeb aa raha hai.

To pehle padosi ka jawab yahan mil jaata hai. Iran. Sabse purana. Sabse zinda.

Par ek padosi aur bhi tha. Ek aisa padosi, jiski chamak itni tez thi, ki log usse Europe ki sabhyata ka ghar kehte hain.

## Part 7: Chamak jo doob gayi

Greece ki taraf chaliye.

Nehru yahan ek baat par rukte hain. Ek badi baat.

Yunani logon ke baare mein ek kahawat duniya mein chali hui hai. Ki poore Europe ki sabhyata ka chashma Greece hai. Aur ek lambi bahes likhi jaati hai — Purab aur Pashchim ka farq. Orient aur Occident.

Is baat ke liye ek badi daleel di jaati hai. Ki is duniya mein kuch bhi hile-dhule, jo asal mein Greek na ho.

Zara sochiye. Kitna bada daava. Aisa daava, jaise Greece ke bina kuch hua hi na ho.

Par Nehru in dono baaton par ek sawal rakhte hain. Pehla sawal ye, ki ye Purab-Pashchim ka farq unhe samajh hi nahi aata. Unke hisaab se ye dhundhla hai. Ye unscientific hai. Iske peeche tathya bahut kam hai.

Aur doosra sawal? Europe ke bade-bade vidvaan, jo Greek aur Latin mein itne gehre the, unhe is desh aur China ke baare mein bahut kam pata tha.

Aur ek adhyaapak ne ek tez baat likhi. Ki Greek sabhyata jis purabi pichhwade par khadi hui, usse wo kabhi poora alag hua hi nahi. Wo alag sirf padhe-likhe logon ke dimaag mein hua.

Phir Nehru ek tathya dete hain. Greece ki chamak badi thi, par uski zindagi chhoti thi. Wo aaj sirf apni kaamyaabi mein zinda hai. Apne asar mein. Aur us chhote, chamkile din ki yaad mein.

Us daur ke baad wo gayab ho gayi. Kyun? Nehru ka andaaza hai. Shayad wo apne vartmaan mein itna kho gayi, ki wo ateet ban gayi.

Ek chamak. Jo doob gayi.

To is desh ko kya cheez in lambi sadiyon tak zinda rakhti hai?

Nehru likhte hain, koi gupt gyaan nahi. Koi chhipa hua raaz nahi. Bas ek narm insaani dil. Ek mila-jula, sahishnu culture. Aur zindagi ke ajeeb raazon ki ek gehri samajh.

Us zindagi ki taaqat har daur mein bahar aati hai. Uske sahitya mein. Uski kala mein.

Par humare paas us kala ka sirf chhota hissa bacha hai. Bahut kuch abhi bhi chhipa padi hui hai. Bahut kuch qudrat ne mitaya, aur bahut kuch insaan ki barbadi ne.

Phir bhi, jo bach gaya, wo bolta hai.

Elephanta ki gufaon mein ek Trimurti hai. Nehru likhte hain, ye Trimurti shayad khud is desh ki bahu-mukhi murat hai. Zordaar. Jiski aankhon mein ek taaqat hai, ek gehri samajh, jo neeche humein dekhti hai.

Aur Ajanta ki deewaron par tasveerein hain. Usme jeevan se pyar hai, sundarta se pyar. Aur phir bhi, jaise kisi chhipi hui gehrai ki halki si chhap.

Dono ek hi baat kehti hain. Par saath mein ek doosri baat bhi. Ye dono desh zindagi ko ek hi tarah dekhte the.

## Part 8: Do desh, ek dhang

Ye do desh ek doosre ko naye nahi the. Nehru likhte hain, ye sampark sabse purane likhe hue daur se chalta aa raha tha. Aage chalkar Greek ilaake se bhi rishta juda.

Ab dekhiye zindagi ka dhang.

Bharat aur Greece, dono jagah zindagi ko waise hi apnaya gaya, jaisi wo thi. Poora jeeya gaya.

Aur dono jagah ek baat maani gayi. Ki ek andar ki zindagi hai, jo sabse oopar hai.

Isi bharose se dono jagah jigyaasa paida hui. Soch paida hui. Par ye soch kis taraf gayi?

Ye baat dhyan se suniye.

Wo log bahar ki cheezon ko chhoo kar nahi seekhte the. Wo apne mann mein baith kar, kuch aise vichaaron par tark khada karte the, jinhe wo pehle se sahi maan chuke the. Yaani tark tha, par tajurba nahi.

Aur ye sirf Greece aur Bharat ki baat nahi thi. Ye us poore zamane ka dhang tha. Wo daur tha, jab vigyan ka tareeka aaya hi nahi tha.

Aur ye soch sirf kothri mein nahi thi. Haan, uski gehrai chand padhe-likhe logon mein thi. Par aam log bhi isse door nahi the.

To aam log kahan sochte the?

Yahan Nehru ek khoobsurat tasveer dete hain. Wo likhte hain ki wo zindagi mili-juli thi. Jaise aaj bhi is desh mein hai. Gaon mein khaas.

Bazaar. Mandir ya masjid ka aangan. Kuan ka kinara. Gaon ki panchayat ki jagah. Yahin din ki khabar baanti jaati thi. Yahin aam zarooratein rakhi jaati thi. Aur yahin logon ki raay banti thi.

Aur ye sab karne ke liye waqt bhi tha. Logon ke paas fursat thi.

Aur saal? Saal melon se banta tha. Dono jagah, mausam badalne par mela hota tha. Mela insaan ko mausam se jod kar rakhta tha.

Nehru likhte hain, ye mela aaj bhi hamare paas hain. Basant ka. Faslon ke katne ka. Deepavali — patjhad ke ant ka roshni ka utsav. Aur garmi se pehle Holi.

In melon mein gaana-naach aaj bhi hai. Lok geet. Lok naach. Rasa-lila, jise Krishna aur gopiyon ka naach kehte hain. Aur purane veeron ki kathaon ke mela bhi.

To soch ke maamle mein Bharat aur Greece door nahi the. Wo paas the. Bahut paas.

Par ghar ke andar? Wahan ek cheez thi, jo dono jagah alag thi.

## Part 9: Do ghar, do reet

Nehru likhte hain, purane Bharat mein aurat par parda nahi tha. Sirf ek jagah thoda tha — rajaon aur bade sardaron ke ghar. Warna nahi.

Aur Greece? Wahan halaat ulte the. Wahan aurat aur mard ka mel-jol is desh se zyada bandha hua tha.

Idhar kitaabon mein gyani auraton ka zikr milta hai. Aur sirf zikr nahi. Wo sabhaon ki bahes mein hissa leti thi.

Shaadi? Greece mein shaadi ek sauda thi. Ek samjhauta. Aur yahan? Yahan shaadi ek sanskar thi.

Aur ek ajeeb baat. Yunani auratein is desh mein khinchti thi. Purane natakon mein rajdarbar ki jo sewikaayein likhi hui hain, wo aksar Yunani hoti thi.

Nehru ek nishani dete hain. Gujarat ke ek bade bandar se, jo us waqt Barygaza ke naam se jaana jaata tha, Greece se jo cheezein aati thi, unmein gaane wale ladke aur sundar ladkiyan bhi thi. Barygaza — wo bandar, jise aaj Broach kehte hain.

Aur ek cheez, jiske bina wo darbar nahi chalta tha. Sharaab.

Megasthenes — wo Greek, jisne Chandragupta ke darbar ko apni aankhon se dekha. Wo likhte hain ki raja ka khaana auratein tayyar karti thi. Aur wahi unhe sharaab bhi deti thi. Nehru jodte hain, wo sharaab saare Bharatiya peete the.

Aur kuch sharaab Greek ilaakon se aati thi. Ek purane Tamil kavi ne likha hai. Yavana log apne achhe jahaazon mein wo thandi, khushbu wali sharaab le kar aate the. Yavana yaani Greek.

Aur ek mazedaar khat bhi hai.

Pataliputra ke ek raja ne ek Greek raja ko sandesh bheja. Usne kaha, mujhe meethi sharaab bhejiye. Sookhe anjeer bhejiye. Aur ek aisa pandit bhejiye, jo ghoom-ghoom kar gyaan bechta hai.

Us Greek raja ka naam Antiochus tha. Uska jawab? Anjeer aur sharaab hum bhej denge. Par Greece mein kanoon aise pandit ko bechne ki ijazat nahi deta.

Sochiye. Ek raja ko sharaab chahiye, anjeer chahiye, aur ek accha gyaani chahiye. Do duniya, do reet, aur ek darbar.

Ab yahan ek baat saaf dikhti hai. Ye do padosi khaali padosi nahi the. Wo apne ghar ke logon ka aana-jaana apne saath laate the.

Par Greece ke paas ek cheez thi, jo is desh ke paas nahi thi. Aur wahi cheez uski sabse badi daulat thi.

## Part 10: Taaza hawa

Nehru likhte hain, Greek sabhyata mein ek cheez baaki sabse alag thi. Tajurba karne wala vigyan. Experimental science. Vigyan ki shuruaat.

Aur ye Greece mein nahi hui. Ye wahan hui, jise Alexandria kehte hain.

Alexandria. Egypt ka wo shehar, jo Greekon ne basaya tha. Wahan ye vigyan poore zor se chala.

Kab? Isa se poorv 330 se 130 tak. Ye do sau saal vigyan aur naye yantron ki khoj ke itihaas mein alag khade hain.

Us waqt duniya mein aisa kuch nahi tha. Is desh mein nahi. Aur kisi bhi jagah nahi. Agli baar aisa daur satrahvin sadi mein aaya.

Aur Rome? Rome ke paas ek bada samrajya tha, aur apne daur ka aman. Uske Greek sabhyata se seedhe rishte the. Par usne vigyan mein aisa kuch nahi joda.

Jab Europe mein purani sabhyata toot gayi, tab vigyan ka diya kisne jalaya rakha? Arabon ne. Poora madhya kaal.

To ye vigyan aaya kahan se? Nehru kehte hain, ye waqt ki zaroorat se aaya. Ek badhta hua samaaj. Samundari safar. Jahan zaroorat thi, wahan vigyan khada hua.

Aur yahi baat is desh mein hui. Ganit aage badha. Algebra aaya. Shunya ka chinh aaya. Ankdon ko jagah ke hisaab se likhne ka tareeka aaya.

Wo bhi waqt ki zaroorat se. Badhte vyapaar se. Bade hote samaaj se.

Par Nehru yahan imaandar hain. Wo likhte hain, ye pata karna mushkil hai ki Greek logon mein vigyan ki bhaavna kitni thi. Shayad wo bhi purane dhang hi chalte the.

Unka dhang kya tha? Insaan ke andar, aur qudrat ke saath, ek sur aur ek santulan dhoondhna.

Aur wahi dhang Greece aur is desh ka saajha tha.

Ek cheez wahan aage thi. Aur doosri cheez idhar aage thi. Do padosi, do tohfe.

Par is episode ka sabse ajeeb tohfa ab bacha hua hai.

## Part 11: But — ek lafz jo Buddha se aaya

Wahi tohfa, jise is desh ne apna liya, aur phir sadiyon tak sajaya.

Nehru kehte hain, murat ki puja is desh mein Greece se aayi. Ye ek dilchasp khiyal hai.

Dekhiye. Vaidik dharm mein murat ki puja mana thi. Devtaon ke mandir tak nahi the. Kuch purane dharmon mein murat ki puja ki halki nishaniyan thi, par wo aam nahi thi.

Aur Buddh dharm? Wo bhi murat ka sakht khilaf tha. Buddha ki moorat banane par saaf rok thi.

Par Afghanistan aur seema wale ilaakon mein Greek kala ka asar tez tha. Aur dheere-dheere usne apna raasta bana liya.

Shuru mein Buddha ki moorat nahi bani. Pehle aisi mooratein bani, jaisi Greek devta Apollo ki thi. Wo Bodhisattva ki mooratein thi. Bodhisattva — Buddha ke pichhle janm, unse pehle ke roop.

Uske baad Buddha ki khud ki mooratein bani.

Aur isse Hindu dharm ke kuch roopon mein murat ki puja ko badhawa mila.

Aur ek lafz yaad rakhiye. Farsi mein, aur Hindustani mein bhi, murat ko "but" kehte hain. Wo lafz "Buddha" se aaya hai.

Sochiye. Ek Greek kala ne is desh ke devtaon ko chehra diya. Aur us chehre ka naam Buddha se aaya. Do padosi, ek lafz ke andar.

Aur sirf kala nahi. Soch bhi aayi-jaati rahi.

Ujjayini ka bada khagol mandir — yaani wo jagah, jahan taare dekhe jaate the — Alexandria se juda hua tha.

Ek Greek pothi mein ek purani baat likhi hai. Ki is desh ke kuch vidvaan Socrates ke paas gaye the, aur unhone unse sawal poochhe. Socrates — Greece ka wo darshanik, jise aaj poori duniya jaanti hai.

Aur Pythagoras par is desh ke darshan ka gehra asar maana jaata hai. Pythagoras — wo Greek vidvaan, jiske naam par aaj school mein triangle wala niyam chalta hai.

Phir aayi ek aur sadi. Aur ek doosra vidvaan.

Alberuni — Central Asia se aaya ek vidvaan, jo gyarhvin sadi mein is desh mein aaya. Baghdad mein usne Greek darshan padha tha. Yahan aakar usne Sanskrit seekhi. Sirf isliye, ki wo is desh ka darshan padh sake. Aur usne dono soch ki tulna apni kitaab mein likhi.

Usne ek baat aur likhi. Ki yahan ki Sanskrit kitaabon mein Greek aur Roman khagol ki baatein likhi hui hain. Do duniya, ek almari mein.

Aur aakhir mein Nehru ek badi baat likhte hain.

Insaan ke mann mein ek bhookh hoti hai. Ek hi bhookh. Wo dhoondhna chahta hai ki sab kuch aakhir mein ek hai. Zindagi mein. Qudrat mein. Aur us poore brahmaand mein — ek.

Nehru likhte hain, aaj ke kuch tez vichaarak kehte hain ki aisa koi ek nahi hai. Koi niyam nahi. Koi ekta nahi. Ho sakta hai wo theek hon.

Par Nehru kehte hain, ye bhram bhi bekaar nahi gaya. Greece mein, is desh mein, aur bahut jagah — is ekta ki talash se kuch nikla. Ek sur. Ek santulan. Ek gehri zindagi.

Ab cold open wala kamra yaad kijiye. Allahabad. 1944. Us mehmaan ki baat.

Do bhai. Ek purab, ek pashchim. Aur unke paas bachi ek cheez — bansuri ki kuch purani dhun.

To jawab yahi hai.

Is desh ke sabse purane padosi Iran the. Sabse purane, aur sabse zinda.

Unse rishta kisi jang ne nahi tootaa. Darwaze band hone se tootaa. Aur wo darwaze humne band nahi kiye the.

Aur doosre padosi ka jawab bhi yahin hai. Greece ka rishta toota hi nahi, kyunki wo kabhi lamba hua hi nahi. Chamak badi thi, par din chhota. Wo doob gaya.

Par jaane se pehle, usne is desh ko apne but de diye.

[SFX: bansuri ki wahi dhun, ek baar, poori; phir ghul jaati hai]

Dono padosi apne nishaan chhod gaye. Ek zubaan ke roop mein. Ek imaarat ke roop mein. Ek chehre ke roop mein.

Aur ek dhun ke roop mein, jo aaj bhi bajayi ja sakti hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, Nehru ne 1944 mein likha ki is desh aur Iran ke rishte phir judenge. Aaj wo darwaze khule hue hain. Naye raaste bhi hain. Par ek sawal bacha hua hai. Padosi hone ke liye darwaze kaafi hote hain? Ya padosi hone ke liye ek dhun chahiye?

Doosri, wo do bhaiyon ki kahani. Ek purab gaya, doosra pashchim. Dono khandaanon ne ek doosre ko bhula diya. Bas bansuri ki kuch dhun dono gharon mein bachi rahi.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aapke ghar mein aaj kaun si purani cheez bajti hai, jo aapke padosi ke ghar mein bhi bajti hai? Ek geet? Ek pakwan? Ek mela? Ya koi aisa shabd, jo dono jagah ek hi matlab deta hai?

Sadiyon baad, jab Europe ne is desh ka purana natak khoja, to usne turant poochha. Kya ye natak Yunani natak se aaya tha? Ek sawal, do padosiyon ke beech.
