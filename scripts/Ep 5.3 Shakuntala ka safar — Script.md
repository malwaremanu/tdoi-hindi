## Cold open

[SFX: chhapai; kagaz par syaahi]

Saal 1789 hai. Hindostan ke ek shehar mein ek chhapa-khana hai. Wahan ek kitaab chhap rahi hai.

Kitaab ke pehle panne par ek naam hai. Shakuntala.

Aur uske neeche ek doosra naam. Kalidasa.

Kuch mahine baad wahi kitaab ek jahaz par hai. Jahaz Hindostan se Europe ki taraf ja raha hai.

[SFX: samundar; jahaz ki rassi]

Kalpana kijiye. Germany. Ek chhota kamra. Mez par wahi kitaab khuli hui hai. Ek aadmi baith kar padh raha hai. Bahar sard hawa hai.

Us aadmi ka naam Goethe hai — German ka wo bada kavi, jisne Faust likha.

Wo padhta jaata hai. Panne palte jaate hain. Aur phir wo ruk jaata hai.

Kyunki usse ek baat samajh aa gayi hai.

Europe ke padhe-likhe logon mein ek hulchul mach gayi. Is kitaab ke chhape kai baar nikle. Aur jo log German, French, Danish ya Italian padhte the, unke paas bhi ye kahani pahunchi.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: pichhli baar hum do padosiyon ke paas ruke the — Iran aur Greece. Greece ke saath ek sawal khula reh gaya tha: is desh ka natak bhi unhi se aaya? Europe ne is sawal par poori ek sadi bahas ki. Aaj uska jawab milega — aur ek kitaab ke safar ke saath.

## Part 1: Europe ka sawal

Europe ko jab pata chala ki is desh mein bahut purana natak hai, to uska pehla khayal yahi aaya.

Ye natak Yunanion se aaya hoga.

Is khayal mein dum tha. Do wajah thi.

Pehli wajah. Tab tak duniya ke padhe-likhe logon ko pata hi nahi tha ki Greece ke alawa kisi purane desh mein natak hota tha. To ek naya, purana natak? Wo kisi bhi fehrist mein nahi tha.

Doosri wajah zyada wazandar thi. Sikandar ke hamle ke baad is desh ki seema par Yunani raaj ban gaye the. Wo raaj sadiyon tak chalte rahe.

Unke shehron mein tamasha hota tha. Naach. Gaana. Khel. Is tarah ki cheezein wahan se yahan aati thi.

Aur doosri taraf, ye desh apna natak bana raha tha.

To kya is desh ne unse natak seekha?

Is sawal par Europe ke vidvaanon ne poori ek sadi behes ki — unneesvi sadi bhar. Kitaabein likhi gayin, dalilein chhapin.

Aaj lagbhag sab maante hain ki ye behes khatam ho chuki hai.

Is desh ka natak apne dam par bana. Uske mool apne the. Uske niyam apne the. Uska vikaas apna tha.

To uski jad kahan thi?

Jad bahut peeche hai. Rig Veda ke un geeton mein, jahan do log baat karte hain. Ek poochta hai, doosra jawab deta hai. Ye natak nahi hai. Par natak ka beej isi mein hai.

Phir Ramayana aur Mahabharata ka zikr aata hai. Wahan "Nataka" ka naam aata hai. Nataka, yaani natak.

Phir Krishna ki kathaon ke geet, sangeet aur naach. Yahin se ise aakaar milne lagta hai.

Aur is beech ek naam aata hai. Panini — wahi bade vyakaran-kar, jinke baare mein hum pehle sun chuke hain. Panini natak ke kuch roopon ka zikr karte hain.

Sochiye. Ek vyakaran ki kitaab mein natak ke roop. Iska matlab, unke zamane mein natak ek jaani-pehchani cheez thi.

Par geet aur naach se natak nahi banta. Natak ko jagah chahiye. Ek manch chahiye.

Aur us manch ka sabse purana nishaan kahan mila?

Zameen ke neeche. Ek pahadi mein.

## Part 2: Pahadi ke neeche ek manch

[SFX: kudaal; patthar par chheni]

Chota Nagpur ke Ramgarh pahad mein kuch log khodai kar rahe hain. Ye khodai aaj ki nahi hai. Par tasveer aaj ki bana lijiye.

Mitti hat rahi hai. Aur neeche se ek aisi jagah nikalti hai, jo ghar nahi lagti.

Andar chhoti kholiyan, aas-paas baithne ki jagah, aur beech mein ek chaura farsh.

Ye ek natya-shala hai. Natak dekhne ki jagah. Do hazaar saal se bhi purani.

Ab is kahani ka sabse ajeeb hissa. Is desh mein ek bahut purani kitaab hai. Natya-Shastra — natak ki kala par ek poora shastra.

Usme likha hai ki natya-shala kaisi honi chahiye. Kamre kahan, baithne wale kahan, mancha kahan.

Aur jo manch pahadi se nikla, wo usi kitaab ki likhi hui shakal se milta hai.

Nehru yahan ek baat par ruk jaate hain. Aisi kitaab tab likhi jaati hai, jab natak poori tarah ban chuka ho. Jab bade paimane par, aam logon ke saamne, natak hone lage hon.

Yaani kitaab ke peeche sadiyon ka kaam hai. Kitaab ke peeche kai kitaabein hain, jo aaj nahi hain.

Sanskrit natak teen sau saal Isa se poorv tak poora ban chuka tha. Kuch vidvaan isi tareekh ko paanchvi sadi BC tak le jaate hain.

Manch mil gaya. Kitaab mil gayi. Aur ek French vidvaan ne is natak ke baare mein ek baat likhi, jo aaj bhi yaad rakhi jaati hai.

Usne kaha: natak kisi bhi sabhyata ki sabse oonchi abhivyakti hota hai. Chahe wo zindagi ko dikhaye ya samjhaye, use zindagi ko nichod kar ek nishaan mein badalna padta hai. Aur is desh ki apni khoobi poori tarah iske natak mein hi nikli.

Phir bhi ek sawal khada reh jaata hai. Itna bada manch tha, itni purani kitaab thi — to us manch par sabse bada naam kiska tha?

## Part 3: Ek kavi jise zindagi ne laad kiya

[SFX: baarish; badalon ki goonj]

Kalpana kijiye. Chauthi sadi ka ant. Ujjayini — aaj ka Ujjain. Shaam dhal rahi hai.

Ek darbaar hai. Raja Chandragupta doosra. Unke naam ke saath ek doosra naam bhi juda hua hai: Vikramaditya. Aur unka raajvansh wahi hai, jiske chamak ke daur ke baare mein hum pehle sun chuke hain. Gupta.

Us darbaar mein nau log baithe hain. Parampara kehti hai ki wo us waqt ke nau sabse bade hunar-mand log the — nau ratan.

Unmein se ek hain Kalidasa.

Unhe Sanskrit sahitya ka sabse bada kavi aur natakkaar maana jaata hai. Unki tareekh pakki nahi hai, par samajh yahi hai ki wo isi shehar mein, isi zamane mein the.

Aur Kalidasa ke baare mein ek baat unhe baaki bade kaviyon se alag kar deti hai.

Bahut se bade kavi zindagi se ladte rahe. Kalidasa ko zindagi ne apne laadle bete ki tarah rakha. Unhone zindagi ki sundarta aur narmi zyada dekhi. Uske khurdure kinare kam.

Unki likhaai se yahi jhalakta hai. Zindagi se pyaar. Aur prakriti ki sundarta ke liye ek betab khinchaav.

Isi jhalak ka ek natak hai Shakuntala. Wahi kitaab, jo samundar paar kar rahi thi. Wo unhi ki thi.

Europe ne is desh ke natak ko pehli baar isi ek kitaab se jaana.

Par Kalidasa ne sirf natak nahi likhe. Unke lambe kavya bhi bahut bade hain. Unmein se ek hai Meghaduta — baadal ka doot.

Ek premik hai. Wo bandi hai, apni priya se alag. Bahut door. Aur baarish ka mausam aa gaya hai. Baadal aakaash mein umad rahe hain.

Uska dil kya karta hai?

Wo baadal se baat karta hai. Wo usse kehta hai: tu meri baat us tak pahuncha de. Tu itna door ja sakta hai. Main nahi ja sakta.

Ek bandha hua aadmi. Ek azad baadal. Aur do bichhda hua pyaar.

Ek vidvaan ne is kavita par likha: yahan bahar ki prakriti insaan ki bhaavna se bun jaati hai, aur insaan ka dil prakriti ke dhaanche mein baith jaata hai. Kalidasa ne wahi samajh liya tha, jo Europe bahut baad mein ja kar samjha — ki ye duniya sirf insaan ke liye nahi bani.

Ye ek kavi ki chamak thi. Par Kalidasa akela nahi the. Unke peechhe aur unke aage, is desh mein aur bhi log natak likh rahe the.

## Part 4: Char natak, char zamane

Pehle un natakkaaron ko dekhiye, jo humse pehle is manch par aaye.

Jin natakon ke naam aaj humein mile hain, unmein aksar pehle ke natakkaaron ka zikr aata hai. Naam ke saath unke natakon ka zikr aata hai — aur wo natak aaj nahi hain.

Ek naam aisa hi hai. Bhasa. Unki bahut tareef milti hai, baad ke kai natakkaar unka naam lete hain. Par unka ek natak nahi bacha tha.

Aur phir, pichhli sadi ke shuru mein, unke terah natak mil gaye. Ek saath.

Isse bhi aage ki ek kahani hai. Is desh ke sabse purane natak jo ab tak mile hain, wo ek Bauddh vidvaan ke hain. Ashvaghosha. Ye natak tukdon mein mile hain. Taad ke patton par likhi pothiyan.

Aur ye pothiyan kahan mili? Is desh mein nahi. Gobi registan ke kinaare, Turfan naam ki ek jagah par. Ashvaghosha ne Buddha ki zindagi par ek kavya bhi likha — ek aisi rachna, jo is desh, China aur Tibet mein bahut lokpriya thi. Uska Chini anuvaad ek Bharatiya vidvaan ne kiya tha.

Ab do naam aur.

Kalidasa se bhi purana ek naam ho sakta hai: Shudraka. Unka natak hai Mrichhkatika — mitti ki gaadi. Angrezi mein Clay Cart.

Nehru ise narm aur thoda banawati kehte hain. Par usme ek such hai, jo aapko chhoo jaata hai, aur us zamane ki sabhyata ki ek jhalak de deta hai.

Ab ek doosra raasta.

Kareeb chaar sau saal Isa ke baad. Wahi zamana, wahi raja — Chandragupta doosra. Is saal ek aur natak likha jaata hai. Vishaka-datta ka Mudra-Rakshasa — mohar ki anguthi.

Is natak mein prem ki koi kahani nahi hai. Devta nahi hain. Ye sirf raajneeti hai.

Iska nayak wahi Chanakya hai — Maurya rajaon ke mahamantri, Arthashastra wale. Unke zamane ki baat hai, Chandragupta Maurya ke zamane ki.

Nehru kehte hain, ye natak kai maayno mein aaj bhi taaza lagta hai. Satta, chal, mohar, aur ek chatur aadmi.

Teen sau saal baad, saatvi sadi mein, ek aur natakkaar aata hai. Harsha — saatvi sadi ke shuru ka wo raja, jisne is desh mein ek naya samrajya khada kiya tha. Wo khud natak likhte the. Unke teen natak aaj bhi humare paas hain.

Aur saat sau saal ke aas-paas ek aur chamakta sitara. Bhavbhuti. Unki khoobi zyada tar zubaan mein hai. Isliye unka anuvaad karna aasan nahi. Par is desh mein wo bahut lokpriya rahe. Kalidasa ke baad, sirf unka naam aata hai.

Oxford mein Sanskrit ke ek professor ne dono ki tulna karne ke baad likha tha: aisi surili, aur aisi shaandar zubaan, ki kalpana karna hi mushkil hai.

Saikdon saal. Saikdon naam. Aur unmein ek cheez hai hi nahi.

## Part 5: Jahan bura ant likhna mana tha

Wo cheez hai tragedy.

Greece ke natak ka poora aadhaar tragedy hai. Tragedy — wo natak, jiska ant bura hota hai, aur jiska sawal bada hota hai.

Insaan dukh kyun bhugta hai? Duniya mein buraai kyun hai? Bhagwan kahan hai? Aur insaan kya hai? Ek din ka bachcha, jo ek aisi taaqat se ladta hai jo use kuchal deti hai.

Greece mein insaan dukh se seekhta hai. Par wo ye bhi seekhta hai ki aakhri raaz khula nahi hota.

Sanskrit ke natak mein aisa kuch hai hi nahi.

Nehru saaf likhte hain: wahan tragedy thi hi nahi. Bura ant likhna mana tha.

Iske peeche ek soch thi. Jo dharm log maante the, wahi natakkaar maante the.

Punrjanm. Karam. Karam ka phal.

Is duniya mein kuch bhi bina kaaran nahi hota. Jo aaj ho raha hai, wo pichhle janm ki kisi ghatna ka nateeja hai.

Nateeja? Andhi taqdeer naam ki koi cheez nahi hai. Buraai bina kaaran nahi hoti. Insaan ko kisi andhi taaqat se nahi ladna hai.

Sochne wale log in saadhe jawabon se santusht nahi the. Wo aakhri kaaran dhoondhte rahe. Par zindagi inhi vishwas par chalti thi, aur natakkaar in se ladte nahi the. Us zamane ke natak us desh ki soch se poore mel mein the. Usme bagaavat ki chhaap kahin nahi milti.

Natak likhne ke niyam sakht the. Todna aasan nahi tha.

Par phir bhi, us natak mein taqdeer ke saamne sir hila dena nahi tha. Uska nayak hamesha himmat wala hota hai, jo har khatre ka saamna karta hai.

Mudra-Rakshasa mein Chanakya apne munh se kehte hain: bewaqoof log taqdeer par bharosa karte hain. Wo sitaaron se madad maangte hain, apne aap se nahi.

Ek kamzori bhi hai. Nayak hamesha nayak. Khalnayak hamesha bura. Beech ke rang kam.

Phir bhi us natak mein aise drishya hain, jo aaj bhi kheenchte hain — aur uske peeche wali zindagi ek sapne ke chitre jaisi lagti hai. Sach bhi, aur sapna bhi.

Laga aisa hai, jaise us zamane ka Bharat zyada shaant tha, zyada tika hua. Jaise usne apni jadein khoj li ho, apne sawalon ke jawab pa liye hon. Wahan Greece ki tragedy ka toofan nahi hai. Bas ek shaant dhaara, jise kabhi tez hawa chhoo kar nikal jaati hai.

Par jo hai, wo bahut insaani hai. Usme ek sundar santulan hai.

Ek French vidvaan ne is poore natak ke baare mein ek line likhi: Nataka is Bharatiya buddhi ka sabse khush-naseeb aavishkaar hai.

Aur ek-do hazaar saal baad, 1924 mein, ek angrezi anuvaad mein, ye natak New York mein manch par khela gaya. Shudraka ki wo Mitti ki Gaadi.

Wahan ke ek bade samikshak ne likha: is natak mein sach kisi gupt shastra mein nahi — ek narmi mein hai, jo gehri hai aur sachchi hai. Ye poori tarah banawati hai, phir bhi ye dil ko chhoo jaati hai. Kyunki ye asli hai. Sachcha nahi.

## Part 6: Do manch

[SFX: dholak; door se bheed ki chaun]

Ab is natak ki zubaan dekhiye.

Ye sirf Sanskrit nahi hai. Ye mili-juli zubaan hai. Sanskrit, aur ek ya zyada Prakrit.

Prakrit kya hai? Sanskrit ke aam bol-chaal wale roop. Wahi zubaan, jo log ghar mein aur gali mein bolte the.

Aur ek hi natak mein ye bantwaara tha. Padhe-likhe log Sanskrit bolte the. Aam log, aur aksar auratein, Prakrit bolti thi. Kavita aur geet Sanskrit mein the.

Nehru ek samajh dete hain. Ye mila-jula tareeka shayad is natak ko aam logon ke kareeb laata tha — padhi-likhi zubaan aur lok-kala ki maang ke beech ek samjhauta.

Par sach ye bhi hai. Ye natak asli mein darbaar ka natak tha. Padhe-likhe, shaandar mehfil wale logon ka.

Use ek French vidvaan ne France ke purane tragedy natak se milaaya. Wo bhi aam bheed se kat gaya tha. Usne asli zindagi se mooh mod liya, aur apni hi ek banawati duniya bana li.

Par yahin ek doosri cheez bhi thi. Aur yahi is episode ka dil hai.

Hamesha ek aam manch bhi raha hai.

Ek lok-natya. Uske vishay wahi purane the. Ramayana. Mahabharata. Devtaon ki kathayein. Wo kahaniyan log pehle se jaante the.

Us lok-natya mein kahani se zyada dikhaawa tha. Saj-dhaj. Rang. Tamasha.

Aur uski zubaan kya thi? Us ilaake ki zubaan. Jahan wo khela jaata tha, wahan ke logon ki zubaan.

Yahi uski taaqat thi. Aur yahi uski seema bhi.

Sanskrit ka manch poore desh ka manch tha. Kyunki wo un logon ki zubaan thi, jo poore desh mein kahin bhi padhe-likhe the. Isliye uska vogue poore desh mein tha.

Aur lok-natya? Wo apne ilaake mein hi reh gaya.

Ek aur baat. Ye natak padhne ke liye nahi, khelne ke liye likhe gaye the.

Kitaabon mein manch ke poore nirdesh hain — kaun khada hoga, kahan jaayega. Baithne walon ke liye alag niyam.

Aur Greek natak ke ulat, yahan natak mein auratein bhi hissa leti thi.

To do manch saaf dikh gaye.

Ek darbaar ka manch. Ek gaon ka manch.

Dono ke paas ek hi kahaniyan thi. Phir bhi dono do alag duniyaon mein rehte the. Ek mein darbaar, shastra, aur kavi. Doosri mein gaon, tyohaar, aur bheed. Ek zubaan poore desh ki thi. Doosri sirf apne ilaake ki.

Dono saath-saath chale. Sadiyon tak.

## Part 7: Zubaan ka faasla

Sanskrit natak girne laga.

Kyun?

Ek jawab aasani se diya jaata hai — aur wo jawab kamzor hai. Kaha jaata hai ki Afghan aur Mughal daur mein darbaari sarparasti band ho gayi, aur natak ko ek kala maan kar us par rok lag gayi.

Par is baat mein dum nahi hai.

Natak ka girna un rajnaitik badlaavon se bahut pehle shuru ho chuka tha. Aur wo badlaav sadiyon tak sirf uttar Bharat tak seemit the. Agar us natak mein jaan baaki hoti, to wo dakshin mein apna kaam chalata rehta.

Sach ye hai ki un rajaon ka record ulta tha. Indo-Afghan, Turk, Mughal — inmein se zyadatar ne is desh ki kala ko roka nahi, badhaya.

Bharatiya sangeet un darbaaron mein poora apnaya gaya — aur uske kuch sabse bade ustaad Musalmaan rahe. Kavita bhi badhi. Is desh ke un logon mein bhi kavi hue, jo Hindi mein likhte the.

Aur ek raja tha. Bijapur ka Ibrahim Adil Shah. Usne Bharatiya sangeet par ek poora nibandh Hindi mein likha.

Hindi kavita aur sangeet Hindu devtaon se bhara hua tha, phir bhi apnaya gaya — wahi rupak, wahi upmaayein. Sirf moorat banane par ek rok thi. Baaki kisi kala par lagbhag koi rok nahi thi.

To asli wajah kya thi?

Do wajah. Ek: us waqt is desh mein bahut cheezein gir rahi thi, rachna ka josh kam ho raha tha. Aur do: natak ki zubaan aur zindagi ki zubaan ke beech ka faasla badhta ja raha tha.

Natak Sanskrit mein likha ja raha tha. Aur gali-zindagi ki zubaan? Wo badal chuki thi. Hazaar saal ke aas-paas hamari aaj ki zubaaen banne lagi — aur likhne lagi.

Manch par wo zubaan thi, jo darbaar mein chalti thi. Aur darwaze ke bahar ek doosri zubaan bol rahi thi.

Nateeja? Darbaari manch khaali ho gaya.

Par yahan ek hairat-angez baat aati hai.

Sanskrit natak mara nahi.

Wo sadiyon tak likha jaata raha. Aur chhapai ke baad bhi. 1892 mein Shakespeare ke ek natak ka Sanskrit anuvaad aaya — Midsummer Night's Dream. Aaj bhi purane natakon ki pothiyan milti rehti hain.

Ek vidvaan ne unneesvi sadi ke ant mein inki fehrist banayi. Us fehrist mein sau se zyada natakkaar the, aur kareeb chaar sau natak. Aaj wahi fehrist kai guna badi ho chuki hai.

To ye zubaan muh se gayi thi. Kalam se nahi.

Zubaan natak mein nahi rehti. Zubaan ghar mein rehti hai. Aur usi zubaan ne ek nayi cheez ko janm diya.

Ek poora naya shastra. Aur us shastra ka janm wahi aadmi tha, jise hum cold open mein chhod kar aaye the.

## Part 8: Jones ka doosra kaam

Us aadmi ka naam William Jones hai.

Cold open mein usne Shakuntala ka anuvaad chhapa. Par wo uska doosra kaam tha. Pehla kaam paanch saal pehle hua tha.

1784 mein usne Sanskrit ki banawat par ek baat likhi.

Uska matlab: ye zubaan Greek se zyada poorn hai. Latin se zyada samriddh. Aur dono se zyada nafees.

Aur phir usne wo baat kahi, jo aage chalkar ek poora shastra bana degi.

Usne kaha: Sanskrit, Greek aur Latin mein itni samanta hai — kriyapad ki jaadon mein, vyakaran ke roopon mein — ki ye ittefaq nahi ho sakta.

Itni samanta sirf tab hoti hai, jab ye teenon kisi ek purani zubaan se nikle hon — wahi zubaan, jo ab shayad kahin nahi hai.

Ek angrez, is desh mein baith kar, ye keh raha tha ki tumhari zubaan aur meri zubaan ka purakha ek hi jagah tha. Unke desh mein, unki kitaabon mein, is baat ki gunjaish hi nahi thi.

Jones ke peeche bahut log chale — Angrez, French, German. Sab ne Sanskrit padha, aur sab ne milkar ek naye shastra ki neev rakhi — comparative philology, yaani zubaaon ki tulna ka shastra.

German vidvaan is daud mein sabse aage nikal gaye. Unneesvi sadi ki sabse badi Sanskrit-khoj unhi ki maani jaati hai.

Germany ke lagbhag har university mein Sanskrit ka ek vibhaag tha. Ek ya do professor, uske in-charge.

Kalpana kijiye. Ek German shehar. Ek university ka kamra. Chaaron taraf kitaabein. Mez par ek taad-patr ki pothi. Ek professor padhta hai, likhta hai, shabd ginata hai.

Aur is desh mein?

Yahan Sanskrit ki vidya kam nahi thi, par purane dhang ki — bina alochana ke. Bahar ki purani zubaaen unhe aati hi nahi thi, sirf Farsi aur Arabi.

Phir ek nayi tarah ki vidya yahan shuru hui. Europe ki chhap se.

Kai Bharatiya log Europe gaye. Aksar Germany. Naye tareeke seekhne ke liye. Alochana ke. Tulna ke. Khoj ke.

Nehru kehte hain, in logon ko ek fayda tha. Aur ek nuksaan bhi.

Fayda kya? Ye log us likhaai ke andar ghus sakte the. Wo mahaul dekh sakte the, jisme wo bani — bahar ke vidvaan ke liye ye mumkin nahi tha.

Nuksaan kya? Apne hi vishwas. Purani maanyatayein. Parampara. Ye aksar aisa chashma bana deti hain, jisse nirpakhi se dekhna mushkil ho jaata hai.

Par ek sawal bachta hai. Sanskrit padhna seekhne ke baad bhi, uski aatma tak pahunchna kyun mushkil rehta hai?

## Part 9: Zubaan vyakaran se badi cheez hai

Sanskrit phoolon ki tarah khili hui zubaan hai. Har taraf badh. Har taraf saj.

Par wo ek vyakaran ke dhaanche ke andar bandhi hui hai — wahi vyakaran, jo Panini ne do hazaar chhe sau saal pehle khada kiya tha.

Wo phaili, ameer hui, aur sajti rahi — par apni jadein kabhi nahi chhodi.

Phir ek daur aaya, jab is zubaan ka sahitya girne laga — aur uske saath saralata chhin gayi.

Uske badle mein kya aaya? Jatil roop. Lambe upmaayein. Alankaar.

Aur wo vyakaran ka niyam, jo shabdon ko jodne deta hai — wo haath mein dikhaave ki cheez ban gaya. Ek hi vakya kai panktiyon tak khinchta chala jaata tha. Ab kala nahi thi. Ab karatab thi.

Kyunki zubaan, vyakaran se badi cheez hoti hai.

Wo ek kaum ke hunaar ka kavya-vasiat hai — uski soch aur khayalon ka jeevit roop.

Shabd badalte rehte hain. Purane khayal naye ban jaate hain, par aksar apna purana libaas pehne rehte hain.

Isi wajah se purane shabd ka matlab pakadna mushkil hai, aur uski aatma pakadna usse bhi mushkil. Uske liye ek kavi jaisa nazariya chahiye.

Sanskrit par ek doosri mushkil bhi hai. Uske shabd sirf sundar nahi hote — unke saath ek poori duniya judi hui hoti hai, jo kisi ajnabi zubaan mein utaar nahi ja sakti.

Sirf shabd nahi. Uska vyakaran bhi kavya se bhara hua hai, uska darshan bhi. Is desh mein ek shabd-kosh tha, jise kavita ke roop mein likha gaya.

Nehru saaf kehte hain: Sanskrit bade vidvaanon ke liye bhi aasan nahi hai. Jinhone ise padha, unke liye bhi us purani duniya mein lauta jaana mushkil hai.

Thoda-thoda ho jaata hai, kyunki hum us purani parampara ke waaris hain.

Vidvaan aksar kavi nahi hote. Jise ye zubaan kholni chahiye, use vidvaan bhi hona chahiye, aur kavi bhi. Aise log kam hote hain.

Isliye jo anuvaad milta hai, wo har lafz ke paas hota hai, aur asli baat se door. Ek vidvaan ne kaha tha: aise anuvaad itne lafzi hote hain, ki beimaan ho jaate hain.

Aur phir bhi. Tulna ka shastra badha, Sanskrit par khoj hoti rahi — par wo saara kaam us jagah tak nahi pahunchta, jahan kavi ki nazar chahiye. Itni mehnat, phir bhi banjar.

Aaj bhi bahar ki kisi zubaan mein aisa anuvaad nahi hai, jo Sanskrit ki asli ke barabar ho. Ismein Bharatiya kam rahe, videshi bhi. Dono, apne-apne kaaranon se.

Aur Nehru ise ek bada dukh kehte hain. Duniya ek aisi cheez se mehroom reh gayi, jisme sundarta, kalpana aur gehri soch hai. Wo poore insaan ki virasat honi chahiye thi, sirf is desh ki nahi.

Europe ne apne classics ke liye ye kaam kiya tha. Bible ka angrezi anuvaad ek mahaan kitaab to banaya hi — angrezi zubaan ko taaqat bhi di. Greek aur Latin par peedhiyon ne pyaar se kaam kiya, aur usse aam log bhi un sabhyataon ka hissa ban gaye.

Sanskrit ke classics ke saath ye kaam abhi hona baaki hai. Kab hoga, ya hoga bhi ya nahi — koi nahi jaanta.

Hamare vidvaanon ki ginti badhti ja rahi hai, hamare kavi bhi hain — par dono ke beech ek khaai hai, jo badhti ja rahi hai.

## Part 10: Wo zubaan jo kisi ke muh par nahi thi

Ab ek sawal, jo is episode ka doosra aadha kholta hai.

Sanskrit kitne samay se ek murda zubaan hai — yaani aisi zubaan jise log bolte nahi? Ye koi nahi jaanta.

Ek baat hum jaante hain. Kalidasa ke zamane mein bhi Sanskrit logon ki zubaan nahi thi.

Wo poore desh ke padhe-likhe logon ki zubaan thi. Isi liye wo sadiyon tak chali, aur seema ke bahar bhi pahunchi — dakshin-poorv Asia ke un ilaakon tak, jahan is desh ke log bas gaye the. Isi liye aaj bhi Thailand mein kuch rasmon mein Sanskrit boli jaati hai.

Phir ek badlaav aaya. Terahvin sadi ke shuru mein, jab Afghan raja Dilli ki gaddi par baithe, to Farsi uttar Bharat ke darbaar ki zubaan ban gayi, aur bahut se padhe-likhe log Sanskrit ko chhod kar usi ki taraf chale gaye. Aam zubaaen bhi likhne lagin.

Phir bhi Sanskrit chalti rahi. Uska star gira. Par wo gayab nahi hui.

1937 mein Trivandrum mein ek sabha hui. Uske saar-par ek vidvaan ne ek ajeeb sujhaav diya: Sanskrit ka ek saral roop banaya jaaye — ek basic Sanskrit — aur use poore desh ki aam zubaan banaya jaaye.

Wo akela nahi tha. Usne Max Müller ki baat dohrayi: is desh mein itni na tooti nirantarta hai, ki saikdon rajnaitik hilaave, dharm-sudhaar aur videshi hamle ke baad bhi Sanskrit ko itne bade desh ki ek hi zubaan kaha ja sakta hai.

Aur ek baat, jo hairat-angez hai. Aaj bhi is desh mein Sanskrit ko Latin se zyada log samajhte hain — wahi Latin, jiska darza Europe mein Dante ke zamane mein tha.

Nehru imaandari dikhate hain. Unhe nahi pata ki Dante ke zamane ke Europe mein kitne log Latin samajhte the. Unhe ye bhi nahi pata ki aaj is desh mein kitne Sanskrit samajhte hain.

Par wo ek baat kahenge: jo bhi sankhya hai, wo chhoti nahi hai. Aur khaas taur par dakshin mein.

Aur phir wo ek baat batate hain, jo sunne wale ke liye sabse kaam ki hai: boli jaane wali Sanskrit samajhna mushkil nahi hai.

Agar aapko aaj ki koi Indo-Aryan zubaan aati hai — Hindi, Bangla, Marathi, Gujarati — to aap ise kaafi hadd tak samajh lenge.

Aur ek ankda, jo hairat deta hai. Aaj ki Urdu poori tarah ek Indo-Aryan zubaan hai. Nehru kehte hain, uske kareeb assi feesadi shabd Sanskrit se aaye hain.

Dakshin ki zubaaen isi khaandaan ki nahi hain, par unhone Sanskrit se itne shabd le liye, ki unki aadhi bol-chaal iske kareeb pahunch jaati hai.

Aaj bhi is zubaan mein kitaabein aur natak likhe jaate hain, patrikaayein chhapti hain — Nehru imaandar hain, unka star ooncha nahi hai. Kabhi-kabhi sabhaayein bhi Sanskrit mein sambodhit ki jaati hain. Haan, chune hue logon ki.

Par yahan Nehru ek keemat batate hain: ye cheez is desh ko muft nahi mili.

Sanskrit ke is chalte rehne ne hamari aaj ki zubaaon ko normal tareeke se badhne nahi diya.

Padhe-likhe log unhe neechi zubaaen maante the. Asli kaam, asli kavita aur gyaan Sanskrit mein likha jaata tha. Ya baad mein aksar Farsi mein.

Phir bhi wo badi zubaaen sadiyon mein dheere-dheere bani — apne likhe roop, apna sahitya.

Aaj sochiye. Thailand ko jab naye technical, scientific aur sarkari shabd chahiye the, to usne wahan se liye. Sanskrit se.

## Part 11: Do manch, ek zubaan

Ab is episode ke do siron ko milaiye. Cold open ki wo kitaab. Aur ye zubaan.

Shakuntala samundar paar gayi. Usne Europe ke ek bade kavi ko hilaa diya. Uske chhape kai baar nikle. Uske anuvaad German, French, Danish aur Italian mein bane.

Kyun?

Kyunki wo ek aisi zubaan mein likhi thi, jo poore desh ke padhe-likhe logon ki zubaan thi. Ek aisi zubaan, jise door ke log bhi padh sakte the.

Aur wahi natak ghar mein kyun ruk gaya?

Kyunki usi zubaan mein koi apni maa se baat nahi karta tha.

[SFX: chhapai ki halki thap-thap, laut kar]

To jawab yahi hai.

Natak duniya tak isliye pahuncha, ki wo un logon ki zubaan mein tha, jo poore desh mein padhe-likhe the.

Aur ghar mein wo isliye ruk gaya, kyunki usi zubaan ko koi ghar ki zubaan nahi bolta tha.

Ek vidvaan ne likha tha: Ujjayini mein Shakuntala ke janm par jo taaliyan bajhi thi, wo sadiyon baad duniya ke ek sire se doosre sire tak pahunchi — jab ek angrez ne use Europe ke saamne rakha.

Do manch the. Ek darbaar ka. Ek gaon ka.

Darbaar ka manch samundar paar chala gaya — aur wahan wo ek kitaab ban gaya, jise Europe ne padha. Gaon ka manch yahin raha. Chhota. Apne ilaake mein. Apni boli mein.

Par uski boli hi sabse aage nikli. Wahi zubaan, jo darbaar mein thi, aaj gali mein hai. Aur jo boli gaon ke manch par boli jaati thi, usi se aaj ki zubaaen bani.

Do raaste the. Ek bahar gaya — kitaab ban kar, anuvaad ban kar, ek doosre desh ke mez tak. Doosra andar gaya — gali mein, gaon mein, hamari zubaan mein.

Ismein ek ajeeb si baat hai.

Ek zubaan zinda reh sakti hai, bina uske bolne walon ke. Uske shabd kaafi hote hain.

Natak ruk gaya. Zubaan nahi ruki. Wo aaj bhi hamare muh par hai. Jis zubaan mein hum aap se ye sun rahe hain, wo usi ki beti hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Pehli baat. Ek zubaan, jise koi ghar par nahi bolta, apni kala ko kitna aage le ja sakti hai? Wo kala khoobsurat banti hai — aur naazuk. Wo kitaabon mein rehti hai, manchon par nahi. Aur jo zubaan log ghar mein bolte hain, wo "chhoti" zubaan kehlati hai. Phir ek din wahi log apni hi zubaan ko neecha maanne lagte hain.

Doosri baat. Do manch ki baat aaj badli nahi hai. Aaj bhi ek manch thoda ooncha hai, aur ek manch zameen par. Farq sirf itna hai, ki ab zameen wale manch ki zubaan mein desh ki sabse badi bheed baithi hai.

Aur ek baat, jo is episode mein baar-baar aayi. Jo cheez poore desh mein samajh li jaati hai, wo zinda rehti hai — chahe koi use bolta na ho. Sanskrit humein wahi sikhati hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aapki zubaan kis manch ki zubaan hai — darbaar ki, ya gaon ki? Aur jo zubaan aap ghar mein bolte hain, kya wo aapke shehar ke manch par hai?

## Outro

Sanskrit kisi ke ghar ki zubaan nahi thi. Uska natak duniya ke ek sire se doosre sire tak pahuncha — aur ghar mein ruk gaya.

To logon ki zubaan mein kaun bola?

Magadh mein ek aadmi uthega, jo apni pehli baat logon ki boli mein kahega — aur us boli se ek dharm paida hoga, jo is desh ke ghar-ghar tak pahunchega.

Agli baar: Vihara se Bihar.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
