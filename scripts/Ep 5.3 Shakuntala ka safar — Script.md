Kitaab ke pehle panne par ek naam hai. Shakuntala.

Aur uske neeche ek doosra naam. Kalidasa.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar hum Iran aur Greece ke paas ruke the. Greece ko chhodte waqt ek sawal khula reh gaya tha: kya is desh ka natak bhi unhi se aaya? Aaj uska jawab milega — aur ek kitaab ke safar ke saath.

## Part 1: Europe ka sawal

Europe ko jab pata chala ki is desh mein bahut purana natak hai, to uska pehla khayal yahi aaya.

Ye natak Yunanion se aaya hoga.

Is khayal mein dum tha. Do wajah thi.

Pehli wajah. Tab tak duniya ke padhe-likhe logon ko pata hi nahi tha ki Greece ke alawa kisi purane desh mein natak hota tha. To ek naya, purana natak? Wo to kisi bhi fehrist mein nahi tha.

Doosri wajah zyada wazandar hai. Sikandar ke hamle ke baad is desh ki seema par Yunani raaj ban gaye the. Wo raaj sadiyon tak chalte rahe.

Unke shehron mein tamasha hota tha. Naach. Gaana. Khel. Is tarah ki cheezein wahan se yahan aati thi.

Aur doosri taraf, ye desh apna natak bana raha tha.

To kya is desh ne unse natak seekha?

Is sawal par Europe ke vidvaanon ne poori ek sadi behes ki. Unneesvi sadi bhar. Kitaabein likhi gayin. Dalilein chhapin.

Aaj lagbhag sab maante hain ki ye behes khatam ho chuki hai.

Is desh ka natak apne dam par bana. Uske mool apne the. Uske niyam apne the. Uska vikaas apna tha. Ye kisi ne kisi se chura kar nahi banaya.

To uski jad kahan thi?

Jad bahut peeche hai. Rig Veda ke un geeton mein, jahan do log baat karte hain. Ek poochta hai, doosra jawab deta hai. Ye natak nahi hai. Par natak ka beej isi mein hai.

Phir Ramayana aur Mahabharata ka zikr aata hai. Wahan "Nataka" ka naam aata hai. Nataka, yaani natak.

Phir Krishna ki kathaon ke geet, sangeet aur naach. Yahin se ise aakaar milne lagta hai.

Aur is beech ek vyakaran-kar ka naam aata hai. Panini — wahi bade vidvaan, jinke baare mein hum pehle sun chuke hain. Panini natak ke kuch roopon ka zikr karte hain.

Sochiye. Ek vyakaran ki kitaab mein natak ke roop. Iska matlab, unke zamane mein natak ek jaani-pehchani cheez thi.

Par geet aur naach se natak nahi banta. Usko jagah chahiye. Ek manch chahiye.

Aur us manch ka sabse purana nishaan kahan mila?

Zameen ke neeche. Ek pahadi mein.

## Part 2: Pahadi ke neeche ek manch

[SFX: kudaal mitti par; patthar par chheni ki halki khit-khit]

Chota Nagpur ke Ramgarh pahad mein kuch log khodai kar rahe hain. Ye khodai aaj ki nahi hai. Par tasveer aaj ki bana lijiye.

Mitti hat rahi hai. Aur neeche se ek aisi jagah nikalti hai, jo ghar nahi lagti.

Andar chhoti kholiyan, aas-paas baithne ki jagah, aur beech mein ek chaura farsh.

Ye ek natya-shala hai. Natak dekhne ki jagah. Do hazaar saal se bhi purani.

Ab is kahani ka sabse ajeeb hissa. Is desh mein ek bahut purani kitaab hai. Natya-Shastra — natak ki kala par ek poora shastra.

Usme likha hai ki natya-shala kaisi honi chahiye — kamre kahan, baithne wale kahan, mancha kahan.

Aur jo manch pahadi se nikla, wo usi kitaab ki likhi hui shakal se milta hai.

Nehru yahan ek baat par ruk jaate hain. Aisi kitaab tab likhi jaati hai, jab natak poori tarah ban chuka ho. Jab bade paimane par, aam logon ke saamne, natak hone lage hon.

Yaani kitaab ke peeche sadiyon ka kaam hai. Kitaab ke peeche kai kitaabein hain, jo aaj nahi hain.

Sanskrit natak teen sau saal Isa se poorv tak poora ban chuka tha.

Poora ban chuka hone ka matlab? Natak pehle se the. Aur natakkaar pehle se the.

To un natakkaaron ka kya hua?

Kai naam aise hain, jinka zikr baad ke natakkaar karte hain. Par unmein se ek kitaab bhi nahi mili. Wo kho gaye.

Ek naam aisa hi hai. Bhasa. Unki bahut tareef milti hai. Par unka ek natak nahi bacha tha.

Aur phir, pichhli sadi ke shuru mein, unke terah natak mil gaye. Ek saath.

Isse bhi aage ki ek kahani hai. Is desh ke sabse purane natak jo ab tak mile hain, wo ek Bauddh vidvaan ke hain. Ashvaghosha.

Ye natak tukdon mein mile hain. Taad ke patton par likhi pothiyan. Ashvaghosha ne Buddha ki zindagi par ek kavya bhi likha — ek aisi rachna, jo is desh, China aur Tibet mein bahut lokpriya thi. Uska Chini anuvaad ek Bharatiya vidvaan ne kiya tha.

Aur ye pothiyan kahan mili? Is desh mein nahi. Gobi registan ke kinaare, Turfan naam ki ek jagah par.

To manch mila. Kitaab mili. Natakkaar milen.

Ab ek naam bachta hai, jo aaj bhi is desh ki har zubaan par gaaya jaata hai. Aur wahi naam cold open wali kitaab ke pehle panne par likha tha.

## Part 3: Ek kavi jise zindagi ne laad kiya

[SFX: door se baarish; aakaash mein baadalon ki gehri goonj]

Kalpana kijiye. Chauthi sadi ka ant. Ujjayini — aaj ka Ujjain. Shaam dhal rahi hai.

Ek darbaar hai. Raja Chandragupta doosra. Unke naam ke saath ek doosra naam bhi juda hua hai: Vikramaditya. Aur unka raajvansh wahi hai, jiske chamak ke daur ke baare mein hum pichhli baar sun chuke hain. Gupta.

Us darbaar mein nau log baithe hain. Parampara kehti hai ki wo us waqt ke nau sabse bade hunar-mand log the. Nau ratan.

Unmein se ek hain Kalidasa.

Nehru likhte hain ki Kalidasa ko Sanskrit sahitya ka sabse bada kavi aur natakkaar maana jaata hai. Unki tareekh pakki nahi hai, par samajh yahi hai ki wo isi shehar mein, isi zamane mein the.

Aur Kalidasa ke baare mein ek baat aisi hai, jo unhe baaki bade kaviyon se alag kar deti hai.

Bahut se bade kavi zindagi se ladte rahe. Kalidasa ko zindagi ne apne laadle bete ki tarah rakha. Unhone zindagi ki sundarta aur narmi zyada dekhi. Uske khurdure kinare kam.

Unki likhaai se yahi jhalakta hai. Zindagi se pyaar. Aur prakriti ki sundarta ke liye ek betab khinchaav.

Isi jhalak ka ek natak hai Shakuntala. Wahi kitaab, jo samundar paar kar rahi thi. Wo unhi ki thi.

Europe ne is desh ke natak ko pehli baar isi ek kitaab se jaana.

Par Kalidasa ne sirf natak nahi likhe. Unke lambe kavya bhi bahut bade hain. Unmein se ek hai Meghaduta — baadal ka doot.

Ek premik hai. Wo kisi ki nazar-band hai. Apni priya se alag hai. Aur baarish ka mausam aa gaya hai. Baadal aakaash mein umad rahe hain.

Uska dil kya karta hai?

Wo baadal se baat karta hai. Wo usse kehta hai: tu meri baat us tak pahuncha de. Tu itna door ja sakta hai. Main nahi ja sakta.

Ek bandha hua aadmi. Ek azad baadal. Aur do ka bichhda hua pyaar.

Ye ek kavi ki chamak thi. Par Kalidasa akela nahi the. Unke peechhe aur unke aage, is desh mein aur bhi log natak likh rahe the. Aur unmein se kuch aise hain, jo kisi bhi zamane ke natak se kam nahi lagte.

## Part 4: Char natak, char zamane

Pehla naam Kalidasa se bhi purana ho sakta hai. Shudraka.

Unka natak hai Mrichhkatika — mitti ki gaadi. Angrezi mein Clay Cart.

Nehru ise naram aur thoda banawati kehte hain. Par usme ek such hai, jo aapko chhoo jaati hai. Aur wo us zamane ke mann ki, aur us zamane ki sabhyata ki, ek jhalak de deta hai.

Ab ek doosra raasta.

Kareeb 400 AC. Wahi zamana, wahi raja. Chandragupta doosra. Is saal ek aur natak likha jaata hai. Vishaka-datta ka Mudra-Rakshasa — mohar ki anguthi.

Is natak mein prem ki koi kahani nahi hai. Devta nahi hain. Ye sirf raajneeti hai.

Iska nayak wahi Chanakya hai — Maurya rajaon ke mahamantri, Arthashastra wale. Unke zamane ki baat hai, Chandragupta Maurya ke zamane ki.

Nehru kehte hain, ye natak kai maayno mein aaj bhi taaza lagta hai. Satta, chal, mohar, aur ek chatur aadmi.

Teen sau saal baad, saatvi sadi mein, ek aur natakkaar aata hai. Harsha — wahi Kanauj ke raja, jinhone humare saamne ek samrajya khada kiya tha.

Wo khud natak likhte the. Unke teen natak aaj bhi humare paas hain.

Aur saat sau saal ke aas-paas ek aur chamakte sitara. Bhavbhuti. Unki khoobi zyada tar zubaan mein hai. Isliye unka anuvaad karna aasan nahi. Par is desh mein wo bahut lokpriya rahe. Kalidasa ke baad, sirf unka naam aata hai.

Oxford mein Sanskrit ke ek professor ne dono ki tulna karne ke baad likha tha. Itni madhur, aur itni shaandar zubaan, ki kalpana hi mushkil hai.

Aur ab ek aakhri baat, jo is episode ke cold open ko aage badhati hai.

Shudraka ki wo Mitti ki Gaadi, angrezi anuvaad mein, 1924 mein New York mein manch par kheli gayi.

Zara sochiye. Do hazaar saal. Do samundar. Ek doosre mahadeep ka ek bada shehar. Aur wahan wahi kahani khadi hui. Wahan ke ek bade samikshak ne likha ki wo natak use hilaa gaya.

Itne natak. Itne naam. Itne zamane.

Par in sab mein ek cheez nahi hai. Ek aisi cheez, jiske bina aaj ki duniya natak ki kalpana bhi nahi kar sakti.

## Part 5: Jahan bura ant likhna mana tha

Wo cheez hai tragedy.

Greece ke natak ka poora aadhaar tragedy hai. Tragedy — wo natak, jiska ant bura hota hai, aur jiska sawal bada hota hai.

Insaan dukh kyun bhugta hai? Duniya mein buraai kyun hai? Bhagwan kahan hai? Aur insaan kya hai? Ek din ka bachcha, jo ek aisi taaqat se ladta hai jo use kuchal deti hai.

Greece mein insaan dukh se seekhta hai. Par wo ye bhi seekhta hai ki aakhri raaz khula nahi hota.

Sanskrit ke natak mein aisa kuch hai hi nahi.

Nehru saaf likhte hain: wahan tragedy thi hi nahi. Bura ant likhna mana tha.

Iske peeche ek soch thi. Jo dharm log maante the, wahi natakkaar maante the.

Punrjanm. Karam. Karam ka phal.

Is duniya mein kuch bhi bina kaaran nahi hota. Jo aaj ho raha hai, wo pichhle janm ki kisi ghatna ka nateeja hai.

Nateeja? Andhi taqdeer naam ki koi cheez nahi hai. Buraai bina kaaran nahi hoti. Insaan ko kisi andhi taaqat se nahi ladna hai.

Isliye natak ka nayak darpok nahi hota. Wo himmat wala hota hai. Wo har khatre ka saamna karta hai.

Mudra-Rakshasa mein Chanakya apne munh se kehte hain: bewaqoof log taqdeer par bharosa karte hain. Wo sitaaron se madad maangte hain, apne aap se nahi.

Ek kamzori bhi hai. Nayak hamesha nayak. Khlanayak hamesha bura. Beech ke rang kam.

Phir bhi us natak mein aisi ghatnayein hain, jo aaj bhi kheenchti hain.

Us natak ke peeche wali zindagi ek sapne ke chitre jaisi lagti hai. Sach bhi, aur sapna bhi.

Lagna aisa hai, jaise us zamane ka Bharat zyada shaant tha. Jaise usne apni jadein khoj li ho. Bas ek shaant dhaara, jise kabhi tez hawa chhoo kar nikal jaati hai. Greece ki tragedy ka toofan wahan nahi hai.

Par jo hai, wo bahut insaani hai. Usme ek sundar santulan hai.

Ab ek sawal bachta hai. Is natak mein aam aadmi kahan hai? Aur auraton ki zubaan kaun si hai?

Yahin par is natak ka sabse bada raaz khulta hai. Aur hamare aaj ke sawal ka aadha jawab bhi.

## Part 6: Do manch

[SFX: gaon ke manch par ek dholak; door se bheed ki halki chaun]

Ab is natak ki zubaan dekhiye.

Ye sirf Sanskrit nahi hai. Ye mili-juli zubaan hai. Sanskrit, aur ek ya zyada Prakrit.

Prakrit kya hai? Sanskrit ke aam bol-chaal wale roop. Wahi zubaan, jo log ghar mein aur gali mein bolte the.

Aur ek hi natak mein ye bantwaara tha. Padhe-likhe log Sanskrit bolte the. Aam log, aur aksar auratein, Prakrit bolti thi. Kavita aur geet Sanskrit mein the.

Nehru ek samajh dete hain. Ye mila-jula tareeka shayad is natak ko aam logon ke kareeb laata tha — padhi-likhi zubaan aur lok-kala ki maang ke beech ek samjhauta.

Par sach ye bhi hai. Ye natak asli mein darbaar ka natak tha. Padhe-likhe, shaandar mehfil wale logon ka.

Nehru ise France ke purane tragedy natak se milaate hain. Wo bhi aam bheed se kat gaya tha. Usne asli zindagi se mooh mod liya, aur apni hi ek banawati duniya bana li.

Par yahin ek doosri cheez bhi thi. Aur yahi is episode ka dil hai.

Hamesha ek aam manch bhi raha hai.

Ek lok-natya. Uske vishay wahi purane the. Ramayana. Mahabharata. Devtaon ki kathayein. Wo kahaniyan log pehle se jaante the.

Us lok-natya mein kahani se zyada dikhaawa tha. Saj-dhaj. Rang. Tamasha.

Aur uski zubaan kya thi? Us ilaake ki zubaan. Jahan wo khela jaata tha, wahan ke logon ki zubaan.

Yahi uski taaqat thi. Aur yahi uski seema bhi.

Sanskrit ka manch poore desh ka manch tha. Kyunki wo un logon ki zubaan thi, jo poore desh mein kahin bhi padhe-likhe the. Isliye uska vogue poore desh mein tha.

Aur lok-natya? Wo apne ilaake mein hi reh gaya. Ek hi zubaan, ek hi jagah.

Ek aur baat. Ye natak sirf padhne ke liye nahi likhe gaye the. Ye khelne ke liye the.

Kitaabon mein manch ke poore nirdesh hain — kaun khada hoga, kahan jayega. Baithne walon ke liye alag niyam.

Aur Greek natak ke ulat, yahan natak mein auratein bhi hissa leti thi.

To do manch saaf dikh gaye.

Ek darbaar ka manch. Ek gaon ka manch.

Dono saath-saath chale. Sadiyon tak.

Aur phir ek din ek manch khaali ho gaya. Aur wo zubaan nahi thi, jo badli thi.

## Part 7: Zubaan ka faasla

Sanskrit natak girne laga.

Kyun?

Nehru do wajah dete hain, par ek sabse saaf dikhti hai.

Pehli. Us waqt is desh mein bahut cheezein gir rahi thi. Rachna ka josh kam ho raha tha. Aur yahi girna Afghan aur Turk rajaon ke Dilli ki gaddi par baithne se bahut pehle shuru ho gaya tha.

Baad mein Sanskrit ko Farsi se bhi ladna pada.

Par is natak ki asli maut ki wajah ye nahi thi.

Natak ki zubaan aur zindagi ki zubaan ke beech ka faasla badhta ja raha tha.

Natak Sanskrit mein likha ja raha tha. Aur gali-zindagi ki zubaan? Wo badal chuki thi. Hazaar saal ke aas-paas hamaari aaj ki zubaaen banne lagi.

Manch par wo natak tha, jiski zubaan darbaar mein chalti thi. Aur darwaze ke bahar ek doosri zubaan bol rahi thi.

Nateeja? Darbaari manch khaali ho gaya.

Par yahan ek hairat-angez baat aati hai.

Sanskrit natak mara nahi.

Wo sadiyon tak likha jaata raha. Aur chhapai ke baad bhi. 1892 mein Shakespeare ke ek natak ka Sanskrit anuvaad aaya. Aaj bhi purane natakon ki pothiyan milti rehti hain.

Ek vidvaan ne pichhli sadi ke ant mein inki fehrist banayi. Kareeb 400 natak, 189 natakkaar.

Aur aaj wahi fehrist aur badi ho gayi hai. Ab 650 natak.

To ye zubaan muh se gayi thi. Kalam se nahi.

Jo raja aage aaye — Afghan, Turk, Moghul — unmein se zyadatar ne is desh ki kala ko roka nahi. Badhaya.

Bharatiya sangeet un darbaaron mein poora apnaya gaya — aur uske kuch sabse bade ustaad Musalmaan rahe. Kavita bhi badhi. Is desh ke un logon mein bhi kavi hue, jo Hindi mein likhte the.

Aur ek raja tha. Bijapur ka Ibrahim Adil Shah. Usne Bharatiya sangeet par ek poora nibandh Hindi mein likha.

Hindi kavita aur sangeet Hindu devtaon se bhara hua tha, phir bhi apnaya gaya — wahi rupak, wahi upmaayein. Sirf moorat banane par ek rok thi. Baaki kisi kala par lagbhag koi rok nahi thi.

Natak ka manch khaali ho gaya. Par zubaan khatam nahi hui.

Zubaan natak mein nahi rehti. Zubaan ghar mein rehti hai. Aur usi zubaan ne ek nayi cheez ko janm diya.

Ek poora naya shastra. Aur us shastra ka janm wahi aadmi tha, jise hum cold open mein chhod kar aaye the.

## Part 8: Jones ka doosra kaam

Us aadmi ka naam William Jones hai.

Cold open mein usne Shakuntala ka anuvaad chhapa. Par wo uska doosra kaam tha — pehla kaam paanch saal pehle hua tha.

1784 mein usne Sanskrit ki banawat par ek baat likhi.

Uska matlab: ye zubaan Greek se zyada poorn hai. Latin se zyada samriddh. Aur dono se zyada nafees.

Aur phir usne wo baat kahi, jo aage chalkar ek poora shastra bana degi.

Usne kaha: Sanskrit, Greek aur Latin mein itni samanta hai — kriyapad ki jaadon mein, vyakaran ke roopon mein — ki ye ittefaq nahi ho sakti.

Itni samanta sirf tab hoti hai, jab ye teenon kisi ek purani zubaan se nikalte hon — wahi zubaan, jo ab shayad kahin nahi hai.

Ek angrez, is desh mein baith kar, ye keh raha tha ki tumhari zubaan aur meri zubaan ka purakha ek hi jagah tha. Unke desh mein, unki kitaabon mein, is baat ki gunjaish hi nahi thi.

Jones ke peeche bahut log chale. Angrez. French. German. Sab ne Sanskrit padha. Aur in sab ne milkar ek naye shastra ki neev rakhi — comparative philology, yaani zubaaon ki tulna ka shastra.

German vidvaan is daud mein sabse aage nikal gaye. Unneesvi sadi ki sabse badi Sanskrit-khoj unhi ki maani jaati hai.

Germany ke lagbhag har university mein Sanskrit ka ek vibhaag tha. Ek ya do professor, uske in-charge.

Kalpana kijiye. Ek German shehar. Ek university ka kamra. Chaaron taraf kitaabein. Mez par ek taad-patr ki pothi. Ek professor padhta hai, likhta hai, shabd ginata hai.

Aur is desh mein?

Yahan Sanskrit ki vidya kam nahi thi, par purane dhang ki — bina alochana ke. Bahar ki purani zubaaen unhe aati hi nahi thi, sirf Farsi aur Arabi.

Phir ek nayi tarah ki vidya yahan shuru hui. Europe ki chhap se.

Kai Bharatiya log Europe gaye. Aksar Germany. Naye tareeke seekhne ke liye. Alochana ke. Tulna ke. Khoj ke.

Nehru kehte hain, in logon ko ek fayda tha. Aur ek nuksaan bhi.

Fayda kya? Ye log us likhaai ke andar ghus sakte the. Wo mahaul dekh sakte the, jisme wo bani — bahar ke vidvaan ke liye ye mumkin nahi tha.

Nuksaan kya? Apne hi vishwas. Purani maanyatayein. Parampara. Ye aksar aisa chashma bana deti hain, jisse nirpakhi se dekhna mushkil ho jaata hai.

Par ek sawal. Ye kaam itna mushkil kyun tha? Sanskrit padhna seekhne ke baad bhi, uski aatma tak pahunchna kyun mushkil rehta hai?

## Part 9: Zubaan vyakaran se badi cheez hai

Pehle us zubaan ko dekhiye.

Sanskrit phoolon ki tarah khili hui zubaan hai. Har taraf badh. Har taraf saj. Par ek vyakaran ke dhaanche ke andar bandhi hui — wahi vyakaran, jo Panini ne do hazaar chhah sau saal pehle khada kiya tha.

Wo phaili. Wo ameer hui. Wo aur saja. Par apni jadein kabhi nahi chhodi.

Phir ek daur aaya, jab is zubaan ka sahitya girne laga. Aur uske saath ek cheez chhin gayi. Saralata.

Uske badle mein kya aaya? Jatil roop. Lambe upmaayein. Alankaar.

Aur ek vyakaran ka niyam, jo shabdon ko jodne deta hai, wo haath mein dikhaave ki cheez ban gaya.

Zubaan, vyakaran se badi cheez hoti hai.

Wo ek kaum ke hunaar ka kavya-vasiat hai — uski soch aur khayalon ka jeevit roop.

Shabd badalte rehte hain. Purane khayal naye ban jaate hain, par aksar apna purana libaas pehne rehte hain.

Isi wajah se purane shabd ka matlab pakadna mushkil hai, aur uski aatma pakadna aur bhi. Uske liye ek romani, kavi jaisa nazariya chahiye.

Sanskrit par ek doosri mushkil bhi hai. Uske shabd sirf sundar nahi — unke saath ek poori duniya judi hui hai. Aisi zubaan mein unhe utaara nahi ja sakta, jo soch se hi ajnabi ho.

Sirf shabd nahi. Uska vyakaran bhi kavya se bhara hua hai. Uska darshan bhi. Is desh mein ek shabd-kosh tha, jise kavita ke roop mein likha gaya.

Purane logon ne dhwani ko bahut bada aadar diya. Isliye unki likhaai — kavita ho ya gadya — usme sur aur taal thi. Sahi ucharan ke liye poore niyam likhe gaye.

Ye zaroori bhi tha. Padhai muh se hoti thi. Poori pothiyan yaad ki jaati thi, aur ek peedhi doosri ko yuun hi deti thi.

Shabd ke sur ko itna mahatva milega, to log matlab ko sur se baandhne lage. Kabhi sundar cheezein bani. Kabhi khichdi, banawati.

Veda ke path aaj bhi unhi purane niyamon ke saath hote hain.

Nehru saaf kehte hain: Sanskrit bade vidvaanon ke liye bhi aasan nahi hai. Jinhone ise padha, unke liye bhi us purani duniya mein laut jaana mushkil hai.

Thoda-thoda ho jaata hai. Kyunki hum us purani parampara ke waaris hain.

Vidvaan aksar kavi nahi hote. Jise ye zubaan kholni chahiye, wo vidvaan bhi ho, aur kavi bhi. Aise log kam hote hain.

Isliye jo anuvaad milta hai, wo har lafz ke paas hota hai, aur asli baat se door. Ek vidvaan ne kaha tha, aise anuvaad itne lafzi hote hain ki beimaan ho jaate hain.

Aaj bhi bahar ki kisi zubaan mein aisa anuvaad nahi hai, jo Sanskrit ki asli ke barabar ho. Ismein Bharatiya kam rahe, videshi bhi.

Aur Nehru ise ek bada dukh kehte hain. Duniya ek aisi cheez se mehroom reh gayi, jisme sundarta hai, kalpana hai, gehri soch hai. Wo poore insaan ki virasat honi chahiye thi, sirf is desh ki nahi.

Par ek sawal bachta hai. Agar wo zubaan itni mushkil hai, aur itne saalon se khatam hai, to aaj is desh mein use kaun jaanta hai?

## Part 10: Wo zubaan jo muh par hai

Nehru yahan poori imaandari dikhate hain.

Wo likhte hain: mujhe nahi pata, Dante ke zamane ke Europe mein kitne log Latin samajhte the. Aur mujhe ye bhi nahi pata ki aaj is desh mein kitne Sanskrit samajhte hain.

Par ek baat wo kahenge. Jo bhi sankhya hai, wo chhoti nahi hai. Aur khaas taur par dakshin mein.

Phir wo ek baat batate hain, jo sunne wale ke liye sabse kaam ki hai. Boli jaane wali Sanskrit samajhna itna mushkil nahi hai.

Agar aapko aaj ki koi Indo-Aryan zubaan aati hai — Hindi, Bangla, Marathi, Gujarati — to aap ise kaafi hadd tak samajh sakte hain.

Aur ek ankda, jo hairat deta hai. Aaj ki Urdu poori tarah ek Indo-Aryan zubaan hai. Nehru kehte hain, uske kareeb assi feesadi shabd Sanskrit se aaye hain.

To Urdu ko Sanskrit se alag samajhna? Wo baat hi galat hai.

Ek mushkil aur hai. Kabhi batana mushkil ho jaata hai ki koi shabd Farsi se aaya ya Sanskrit se. Dono ki jadein bahut jagah ek jaisi hain.

Aur dakshin? Wahan ki zubaaen isi khaandaan ki nahi hain. Par unhone Sanskrit se itne shabd lekar apna liye, ki unki aadhi bol-chaal iske kareeb pahunch jaati hai.

Nateeja? Ye zubaan muh se gayi. Par kaan se nahi gayi. Aur ghar se nahi gayi.

Aaj bhi is zubaan mein kitaabein likhi jaati hain, natak bhi. Aaj bhi Sanskrit ki patrikaayein chhapti hain. Nehru imaandar hain: unka star ooncha nahi hai.

Par phir wo ek hairat ki baat likhte hain. Ye cheez itni lambi sadiyon tak kaise chali? Aaj bhi kuch sabhaayein Sanskrit mein sambodhit ki jaati hain — haan, chune hue logon ki.

Aur yahan Nehru ek keemat batate hain. Ye cheez isse muft nahi mili.

Sanskrit ke is chalte rehne ne hamaari aaj ki zubaaon ko normal tarah se badhne nahi diya.

Padhe-likhe log unhe neechi zubaaen maante the. Asli kaam, asli kavita aur gyaan Sanskrit mein likha jaata tha. Ya baad mein aksar Farsi mein.

Phir bhi ye badi zubaaen sadiyon mein dheere-dheere bani. Unke apne likhe roop bane. Unka apna sahitya khada hua.

Aaj sochiye. Ye zubaan sirf itihaas nahi hai. Thailand ko jab naye technical, scientific aur sarkari shabd chahiye the, to usne wahan se liye. Sanskrit se.

Aur ek aakhri baat. Yahan asli sawal itni zubaaon ka nahi hai. Asli sawal Hindi-Urdu ka hai.

Ek hi zubaan. Do likhe hue roop. Do lipiyan. Boli mein lagbhag koi farq nahi. Likhne mein, khaas kar sahitya ke dhang mein, faasla badhta jaata hai.

Koshishein hoti rahi hain ki ye faasla kam ho, ek saajha roop bane. Log usse Hindustani kehte hain. Aur Nehru kehte hain, wahi ab poore desh ki zubaan ban rahi hai.

## Part 11: Do manch, ek zubaan

Phir ek chhoti ginti dekhiye. Ye zubaan kitni door tak gayi.

Pashto. Wo zubaan jo Afghanistan se lekar hamari uttar-pashchim seema tak boli jaati hai. Wo bhi isi khaandaan se hai.

Aur yahin se wo log nikle, jo aage chalkar Sanskrit ke bade vidvaan aur vyakaran-kar bane.

Singhalese. Lanka ki zubaan. Wo bhi isi khaandaan ki hai. Lanka ke logon ko Bauddh dharm isi desh se mila.

Aur Sanskrit sirf is desh ki zubaan nahi hai. Wo Europe ki purani aur aaj ki zubaaon ki behen hai. Slavonic zubaaon mein bhi uske kai roop aur jadein milti hain.

Europe mein iske sabse kareeb kaun si zubaan hai? Lithuanian.

Ek chhoti si zubaan, Europe ke ek kone mein. Aur wo is desh ki zubaan ki sabse kareeb.

Ab is episode ke do siron ko milaiye. Cold open ki wo kitaab. Aur ye zubaan.

Shakuntala samundar paar gayi. Usne Europe ke ek bade kavi ko hilaa diya. Uske chhape kai baar nikle. Uske anuvaad German, French, Danish aur Italian mein bane.

Kyun?

Kyunki wo ek aisi zubaan mein likhi thi, jo poore desh ke padhe-likhe logon ki zubaan thi. Ek aisi zubaan, jise door ke log bhi padh sakte the.

Aur wahi natak yahan khatam kyun hua?

Kyunki usi zubaan mein koi apni maa se baat nahi karta tha.

[SFX: cold open wali chhapai ki thap-thap, bahut halki, laut kar aati hai]

To jawab yahi hai.

Natak duniya tak isliye pahuncha, ki wo un logon ki zubaan mein tha, jo poore desh mein padhe-likhe the.

Aur ghar mein wo isliye mara, kyunki wahi zubaan kisi ke ghar ki zubaan nahi thi.

Do manch the. Ek darbaar ka. Ek gaon ka.

Darbaar ka manch samundar paar chala gaya. Gaon ka manch yahin raha. Bilkul chhota. Bilkul apne ilaake mein.

Par uske shabd sabse aage nikal gaye. Wahi zubaan, jo darbaar mein thi, aaj gali mein hai.

Aur ismein ek ajeeb si baat hai.

Ek zubaan zinda reh sakti hai, bina uske bolne walon ke. Uske shabd kaafi hote hain.

Natak mar gaya. Zubaan nahi mari. Wo aaj bhi hamare muh par hai. Jis zubaan mein hum aap se ye sun rahe hain, wo usi ki beti hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, do manch ki baat aaj bhi sach hai. Aaj bhi ek manch un logon ka hai jinki zubaan poore desh mein chalti hai, aur ek un logon ka jo apni jagah ki zubaan mein gaate-bolte hain. Jo cheez poore desh tak jaati hai, wo badi zubaan mein jaati hai. Jo sirf apne muhalle mein rehti hai, wo wahin khatam ho jaati hai. Sawal yahi hai: aapki zubaan kis manch ki zubaan hai?

Doosri, ye zubaan sau-sau saal se kisi ke ghar ki zubaan nahi hai. Par wo hamare saare kaam ki zubaaon ke andar zinda hai. Ek zubaan ko zinda rakhne ke liye uske bolne wale zaroori nahi hote. Uske shabd kaafi hote hain. Isliye purani cheezein marti nahi. Wo roop badal kar rehti hain.

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapki zindagi mein kaun si cheez bahut purani hai? Aur wo aapke ghar ki zubaan mein hai, ya kisi darbaar ki?

## Outro

Yahan ek aakhri baat dhyan dene wali hai.

Is desh mein ek aadmi bhi hua, jiske paas ye dono zubaaen thi. Wo padha-likha tha. Sanskrit jaanta tha. Par usne logon se unhi ki zubaan mein baat ki.

Natak darbaar mein reh gaya. Us aadmi ne aam manch chuna.

Aur uski baat itni phaili, ki wo desh ke bahar tak pahunchi. Par phir wahi dharm apne ghar se gayab ho gaya. Bina kisi jung ke.

Agli baar: Bina jung ki vidaai.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
