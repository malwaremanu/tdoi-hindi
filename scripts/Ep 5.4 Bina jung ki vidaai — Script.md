## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Hum chapter 5 ke beech mein hain, Gupton ke chamakte daur ke thoda aage. Pichhli baar humare saamne do zubaanein thi: paathshaala ki Sanskrit, aur aam aadmi ki bolchaal. Us episode ne ek sawal chhoda tha: agar ek dharm ko aam logon tak pahunchana ho, to zubaan kaun si chuni jaayegi?

## Part 1: Buddh ki zubaan

Buddha ka jawab seedha tha. Unhone aam logon ki zubaan chuni.

Us ilaake mein log Prakrit bolte the — Sanskrit se nikli hui ek bolchaal ki zubaan, padhe-likhon ki nahi. Buddha Sanskrit jaante the. Par unhone wahi zubaan chuni, jo log roz bolte the.

Kyunki dharm samajhna hai, to pehle sunaai dena hoga.

Is ek chunav se aage ek poori zubaan bani. Pali. Pali — wo zubaan, jismein Buddh ke shuruaati dharm-granth likhe gaye.

Yaad kijiye, Buddha ne khud kuch likha nahi. Unki baatein, unke sawal-jawab, unki charchayein, sab unke jaane ke bahut baad Pali mein likhi gayi.

Aur wo likhaai aaj bhi zinda hai. Kahan? Ceylon mein. Burma mein. Siam mein. Yaani aaj ka Sri Lanka, aur Burma aur Thailand.

In teen jagahon par aaj bhi Buddh dharm ka wo purana roop chalta hai, jise Hinayan kehte hain.

Ab waqt palat ta hai. Bharat mein Sanskrit ki wapasi hoti hai. Aur ab Bauddh vidvaan apni bahas, apna darshan, apne granth Sanskrit mein likhne lagte hain.

Usi zamane mein ek Bauddh lekhak hote hain — Ashvaghosha. Wo naatak likhte hain. Aur dhyan dijiye: humare paas jo sabse purane naatak bache hain, wo isi Bauddh lekhak ke hain. Wo naatak Buddh dharm ke prachaar ke liye likhe gaye the.

Ek dharm, jo apne prachaar ke liye natak likhwa raha hai. Ye apne aap mein ek badi baat hai.

Aur ye Sanskrit wali Bauddh kitaabein yahin nahi rukti. Wo China jaati hain. Japan. Tibet. Madhya Asia.

In sab jagahon par Buddh dharm ka wo doosra roop phailta hai, jise Mahayan kehte hain.

To ek dharm. Do zubaanein. Aur aage chalkar do ghar.

## Part 2: Us daur ka toofan

Ye sab kisi sooni jagah mein nahi hua.

Buddha ka daur soch ka toofan tha. Bharat mein sawal poochhne ka, bahas karne ka, dhoondhne ka aisa mahaul tha, jo us se pehle kabhi nahi tha.

Aur ye sirf Bharat mein nahi tha.

Usi waqt China mein do guru the — Lao-tze aur Confucius. Iran mein Zoroaster. Yunani ke saahil par Pythagoras.

Poore purane zamane mein, ek saath, itne alag-alag deshon mein, log ek hi cheez kar rahe the. Poochh rahe the: ye duniya hai kya? Hum hain kya? Jeena kaise chahiye?

Aur Bharat mein isi toofan ne kya banaya?

Ek taraf Buddha ka raasta. Doosri taraf Mahavir ka. Ek taraf wo log, jo maante the ki sab kuch mitti aur jism se bana hai — unki kitaabein nahi bachi, par unka naam materialism hai. Ek taraf Gita.

Sab ek hi zameen se. Ek hi mitti se. Ek hi mahaul se.

Nehru kehte hain, ye alag-alag paratein thi — ek parat doosri ko kheenchti thi, aur kabhi ek doosre ke oopar chadh bhi jaati thi.

Buddh dharm ke saath-saath aur bhi darshan ban rahe the. Aur Buddh dharm ke andar bhi phoot pad rahi thi. Ek ke baad ek khanda.

Aur phir toofan thahar jaata hai.

Soch dheemi pad jaati hai. Sawal saanche ban jaate hain. Aur un saanchon mein takrar hoti hai — kaun sahi, kaun galat.

Nehru yahan ek shabd use karte hain: scholasticism. Yaani wahi soch, jo apne hi niyamon mein kas kar reh gayi, aur nayi cheez nahi dhoondhti.

Buddh dharm bhi isi raaste par chal padta hai.

Par ye takrar kisi vichaar ki takrar nahi thi. Asli takrar roz ki zindagi se thi.

## Part 3: Phoot kyun padi

Sunkar ye baat ajeeb lagegi: Buddh dharm ke khilaaf aitraaz uske darshan par nahi tha. Koi bhi philosophy maan sakte the, koi bhi tark chal sakta tha. Bas ek shart: wo tark vichaar hi rahe, zindagi ka dhang na badle.

Kyunki purane dharm ka niyam yahi tha: soch mein khula, vyavhaar mein sakht. Jo uske riwaaz ko na maane, uske liye jagah nahi.

Isliye takrar hui. Takrar siddhant se nahi, roz ke rehan-sehan se hui.

Aur Buddha ke jaane ke baad ye daraar chaudi hoti gayi.

Ab dharm do ghar ban jaata hai. Jo naya, phailne wala roop tha — wo Mahayan. Aur purana, saaf-suthra, apne niyamon par tika hua roop — Hinayan.

Ab dhyan dijiye kya badla.

Mahayan mein Buddha khud badal jaate hain. Wo ek bhagwan ban jaate hain. Unse aise maango, jaise kisi devta se. Unki murti banti hai — aur wo uttar-pashchim se aati hai, us Yunani ilaake se, jahan Gandhara ki kala bani thi.

Aur usi waqt ek doosri cheez bhi ho rahi thi. Brahman dharm ki wapasi. Sanskrit ki wapasi.

Dono ke beech ki ladai kadwi thi. Nehru likhte hain, ye takrar aage ke poore itihaas mein chalti rehti hai. Aur aaj bhi wahi hai. Hinayan wale desh China aur Japan ke Buddh dharm ko thoda neeche maante hain. Aur wo bhi unhe.

Hinayan purane darshan ki safai par tika raha. Usne apne granth ek band dareeche ki tarah sambhal liye.

Aur Mahayan? Wo har taraf phail gaya, lagbhag sab kuch bardaasht karta tha, aur jahan gaya wahin ka rang le liya. China mein ek roop. Japan mein doosra. Tibet mein teesra. Aur Bharat mein? Bharat mein wo aam logon ke dharm ki taraf chal padta hai.

Ek aakhri baat isi daur ki. Buddha ne aatma ke baare mein kuch nahi kaha tha. Sawal ko latka kar rakh diya tha. Unke baad ke sabse bade Bauddh acharyon ne wo latakta sawal kaat diya. Aatma hai hi nahi.

Aur isi soch par ek aisa aadmi uthta hai, jiske tark ke aage aaj bhi darshan chup ho jaate hain.

Nagarjuna.

## Part 4: Nagarjuna ka tark

[SFX: ek gehri, lambi ghanti ki goonj; uske neeche door ki bheed, jaise kisi bahas-sabha ki bhoorbhooraahat]

Nagarjuna — Kanishka ke zamane ka ek Bauddh acharya, jise Mahayan ka asli shilpkaar maana jaata hai. Wo Isa ke janm ke aas-paas hue.

Nagarjuna ka daur bahas ka daur tha. Sabha hoti, door-door se acharya aate, aur tark se faisla hota. Is sabha mein Nagarjuna ka tark sabse tez tha.

Nehru likhte hain: Nagarjuna ka tark bahadur tha. Par usse bhi badi baat ye thi, ki wo un nateejon se nahi darte the, jo logon ko besharm lagte the. Ganda. Ghalat. Anokha.

Wo apne tark ko ek baar pakadte the, aur usse chhodte nahi the. Aage. Aur aage.

Aur phir wahi hua, jo aise tark ke saath hota hai. Wo itna aage pahuncha, ki usne unhi cheezon ko mita diya, jin par Nagarjuna khud vishwas karte the.

Soch khud ko nahi jaanta. Soch apne se bahar nahi ja sakti. Isliye wo doosri cheez ko bhi nahi jaan sakti.

Bhagwan duniya se alag nahi. Duniya bhagwan se alag nahi. Dono ek hi tarah dikhte hain.

Aur phir wo aur aage chalte hain.

Sach aur galat mein koi farq nahi bachta. Samajhne aur na samajhne mein bhi nahi. Kyunki jo cheez hi nahi hai, use kaun samjhega?

Kuch bhi asli nahi. Duniya bas dikhti hai. Wo ek dhang hai, ek tarteeb hai, jise hum maan lete hain, par samjha nahi sakte.

Is poore dikhte hue kaam ke peeche Nagarjuna kisi aisi cheez ki taraf ishara karte hain, jise hum soch hi nahi sakte.

Us cheez ka naam hai Shunyata.

Aur yahin sabse ajeeb baat hai. Shunyata ka matlab hai shoonya. Khaali. Wahi shabd, jo ganit mein zero ke nishaan ke liye use hota hai.

Par ye khaali pan nahi hai. Hamari zubaan mein iske liye koi doosra shabd hi nahi hai. Iska matlab: wo cheez, jo sab se door hai, aur sab ke andar hai.

Ek mashhoor Bauddh vidvaan ne is par ek line likhi, jo yaad rakhne layak hai. Shunyata ke kaaran hi sab kuch mumkin hai. Uske bina, duniya mein kuch bhi mumkin nahi.

Ab Nehru ek mazedaar kahani likhte hain. Russia ke ek vidvaan ne dekha ki is Bauddh soch aur aaj ke science mein kuch shakal milti hai — khaas taur par entropy wale siddhant se, yaani brahmaand ki aakhri haalat se.

Soviet Russia mein Buriatia ke ilaake mein logon se kaha gaya: aaj ka science maanta hai ki sab kuch mitti se hai, jism se hai. Koi aatma nahi.

Wahan ke Mahayan Bauddh bhikshu chup nahi baithe. Unhone ek pamflet chhapa: ye humare liye nayi baat nahi. Isse pehle humare hi ek school ne yahi theory banayi thi.

Sochiye. Ek hazaar saal purana darshan. Ek nayi sarkar. Aur jawab chuppi nahi tha — jawab tha: ye hum likh chuke hain.

Par ek sawal bachta hai. Buddha ne khud aise tarkon se manaa kiya tha. Phir unke dharm ka sabse bada naam aise tark par kaise khada ho gaya?

## Part 5: Seedi jo khaali mein khatam hoti hai

Nehru yahan apni hi baat par ruk jaate hain. Dekhiye, metaphysics kahan tak le gayi. Aur dekhiye, Buddha ne aise tarkon se manaa karne mein kitni samajhdari dikhayi thi.

Par insaan ka mann qaid hona nahi maanta. Use wo phal chahiye, jo wo jaanta hai ki haath se door hai. Wo phir bhi haath badhata hai.

Ye poora tark khara darshan nahi tha. Iska tareeka mann ke andar jhaankna tha.

Sochne ka dhang hi nahi. Mann ke haalat bhi. Wahi cheez, jise aaj ka manovigyan subconscious kehta hai, un granthon mein pehle se likhi hui hai. Nehru ko ek aisa hi paragraph dikhaya gaya, jo aaj ki Oedipus complex wali soch jaisa lagta hai — tarika alag, jaankari wahi.

Ab is poore safar ko ek seedi ki tarah chadhiye. Buddh dharm mein chaar darshan bane — do Hinayan ke, do Mahayan ke. Aur ek vidvaan ne is seedi ke chaar kadam gine hain.

Pehla kadam. Gyaan seedha hota hai. Tumhe cheez dikhti hai, bas.

Doosra kadam. Beech mein vichaar aa jaate hain. Ab tumhe cheez nahi, vichaar dikhte hain. Mann aur cheez ke beech ek parda aa gaya.

Ye do kadam Hinayan ke hain.

Teesra kadam. Mahayan aage badhta hai. Parde ke peeche jo asli cheez thi, use mita diya jaata hai. Ab bacha kya? Mann ke andar vichaaron ki ek lambi kataar. Yahin relativity aur subconscious aate hain.

Aur chothha kadam. Ye Nagarjuna ka hai. Madhyamika — madhya ka raasta. Yahan mann khud bhi ghul jaata hai. Bachte hain sirf dhaage — vichaar aur anubhav ke alag-alag tukde, jinke baare mein kuch bhi pakka nahi kaha ja sakta.

To hum pahunch gaye khaali aasmaan par. Zyada se zyada hum itna keh sakte hain: ye ek chetak hai, ek hoshiyari. Uska naam hai vijyana.

Par yahan ek ulat phat jati hai. Poora tark kehta hai: yahan kuch nahi hai. Par usi tark wale ye bhi kehte hain: is duniya mein, jismein hum rehte hain, naitikta ka pakka mol hai.

Us zindagi par, aur is dikhti duniya par, hum tark laga sakte hain. Gyaan. Anubhav. Sirf us anant par nahi — wo kahin us paar hai.

To ek dharm ke do chehre saamne aa gaye. Ek taraf khaali aasmaan tak pahunchne wali soch. Doosri taraf ek imaarat, jo gaon-gaon mein utthi.

Wahi imaarat ab dekhiye.

## Part 6: Vihara se Bihar

Buddh dharm ne sabse pehle jad pakdi Magadha mein. Magadha — Bihar ka purana ilaaka, wahi zameen jahan purana Brahman dharm kamzor tha.

Wahan se wo pashchim aur uttar ki taraf badha. Shuru mein ye Kshatriyon ka aandolan tha, par iska appeal aam logon ka tha. Aur bahut se Brahman bhi isme shamil ho gaye.

Nehru ka andaaza ye hai: inhi der se aaye Brahmanon ke kaaran Buddh dharm metaphysics ki taraf jhuka, aur shayad Mahayan ka chauda roop bana.

Ab dekhiye, usne purane dharm se kya cheen liya.

Bohot saare riwaaz gaye — aur sabse badi cheez, pashu-bali.

Ahimsa. Ahimsa ki baat Ved aur Upanishad mein pehle se thi. Par Buddh dharm ne use ubhaara, aur Jain dharm ne usse bhi zyada. Zindagi ka aadar badha, jaanwaron par daya.

Aur in sab ke peeche ek hi koshish thi: achha jeevan, uncha jeevan.

Par ek doosra chehra bhi tha.

Buddha ne khud ye manaa kiya tha, ki sakht tapasya ka koi dharmik mol nahi hota. Par unki shiksha ka kul asar zindagi ki taraf udaasi ka tha.

Ye khaas taur par Hinayan ki soch thi. Aur Jain dharm ki soch usse bhi zyada. Doosri duniya ki taraf khinchaav. Is duniya ke bojh se azaadi. Sanyam. Aur shakahari bhojan ki taraf jhukav.

Dhyan dijiye. Ye sab baatein Buddha se pehle bhi Bharat mein thi. Farq sirf zor ka tha.

Purane Aryan aadarsh ka zor kuch aur tha. Poori, gol zindagi. Vidyaarthi ka daur — sanyam. Grihasth ka — poore jeevan ka hissa. Phir dheere-dheere peeche hatna, samaj ka kaam. Aur aakhri daur, budhape ka. Sanyas.

Pehle sanyasi log jangal mein chhote giroh banakar rehte the, aur unke aas-paas shishya aa jaate the.

Buddh dharm ke aane par bade-bade math aur vihara har jagah uthte hain. Ek ke baad ek. Aur logon ka ek lagatar bahav unki taraf shuru ho jaata hai.

Ye bahav kitna bada tha? Iska sabse bada saboot humari zubaan mein aaj bhi hai.

Bihar.

Vihara, yaani math. Bihar ka naam hi math se bana hai. Agar ek poore rajya ka naam math ho gaya, to sochiye, us ilaake mein kitne math the.

Aur ye math sirf rehne ki jagah nahi the. Ye paathshaala the — kai jagah school, kai jagah university. Poore Madhya Asia mein bhi bade Bauddh vihara the. Balkh mein ek mashhoor vihara tha, jahan ek hazaar bhikshu rehte the — Nav-Vihara, naya vihara. Farsi mein wo Naubahar ban gaya.

[SFX: ek door ki mandir ki ghanti, dheemi, ek baar]

Aaj sochiye. Ek imaarat ka naam, jo ek poore rajya ka naam ban gaya.

Par Nehru ek sawal poochhte hain. Ye udaasi China, Japan aur Burma mein itni kyun nahi hui?

Unka andaaza: har desh ka mahaul dharm ko apne saanche mein dhaal leta hai. China ke paas Confucius aur Lao-tze ki badi parampara thi, aur China aur Japan ne Mahayan chuna, jo kam udaasi wala tha.

Bharat par Jain dharm ka zor bhi tha — in sab mein sabse zyada jeevan se mooh modne wala darshan.

Aur isi ke saath ek purani cheez aur gahri hoti gayi: jis dharm ne kaha tha ki insaan kirdaar se tay hota hai, usi ke daur mein jaati ka kamra aur sakht hota gaya.

Wahan se ek kahani milti hai, jo hairaan karne wali hai.

## Part 7: Satyakama ka jawab

Sabse ajeeb baat ye hai. Buddh dharm ka jaati par jo asar hua, wo uski poori soch ke ulta tha.

Buddh dharm ne jaati ko theek nahi maana, par uski buniyadi wajah sweekar kar li.

Aur us zamane ki jaati aaj ki jaati nahi thi. Buddha ke waqt jaati dheeli thi. Qaabiliyat, kirdaar aur kaam ki zyada ahmiyat thi. Janm ki kam.

Buddha khud bahut jagah "Brahman" shabd ka matlab use karte hain — ek samajhdaar, imaandaar, anushasan mein dhala hua insaan. Naam se nahi. Kirdaar se.

Aur is par ek kahani hai, jo bahut purani hai. Chhandogya Upanishad ki.

Kalpana kijiye.

Ek ladka hai. Satyakama. Uska mann hai ki wo Gautam rishi ke paas padhne jaaye. Ye Gautam Buddha nahi hain. Koi aur rishi hain.

Ghar chhodne se pehle ladka apni maa se poochhta hai. Maa, hum kis khandaan ke hain? Mera gotra kya hai?

Maa ka naam Jabala hai. Wo jhoot nahi bolti.

Wo kehti hai: mere bachche, mujhe nahi pata. Jawan waqt mein main ek ghar se doosre ghar naukri karti phirti thi. Wahin tujhe paaya. Mujhe tera khandaan nahi pata. Main Jabala hoon. Tu Satyakama hai. Ja, aur keh dena: main Satyakama hoon, Jabala ka beta.

Ladka jaata hai. Rishi ke saamne khada hota hai. Aur jo maa ne kaha, wahi shabd-ba-shabd dohra deta hai.

Ab rishi ka jawab suniye.

Rishi kehte hain: sach bolne se hi asli Brahman banta hai. Jo insaan apni maa ke shabdon ko is tarah dohra de, wo Brahman ho sakta hai. Ja, lakdiyaan le aao. Main tujhe apna shishya banata hoon. Tu sach se nahi hata.

[SFX: lakdiyon ka ek gaththar zameen par rakhne ki halki aawaaz; paas mein chhoti aag ki chatpatahat]

Is ek jawab mein jaati itihaas nahi thi. Jaati ek gun tha.

To us zamane mein jaati kaisi thi?

Shayad sirf Brahman hi ek sakht jaati thi. Kshatriy apni parampara par garv karte the, par us varg ka darwaza khula tha — jo raja ban gaya, wo andar aaya.

Baaki zyadatar log Vaishya the. Kisan. Aur kisaani izzat ka kaam thi. Aur bhi kaam wali jaatiyan thi.

Aur jo jaati se bahar maane jaate the? Wo bahut kam the — kuch jangal ke log, aur kuch wo, jinka kaam murdon ko thikane lagana tha.

Ye sab Buddh dharm se pehle ki baat hai. Ab dekhiye iske baad kya hua. Aur yahin is episode ki sabse ulti baat hai.

## Part 8: Hal chalane wala neeche

Jain dharm aur Buddh dharm ka ahimsa ka zor ek bilkul hi ajeeb jagah pahunchta hai.

Kheti.

Sochiye. Hal chalane se zameen palat jaati hai. Zameen palatne se mitti ke andar ki chhoti chhoti jaan, keede, un sab ka ant hota hai.

To jo dharm kehta hai jaan ko nahi maarna, uske liye kheti ka kaam kaisa dikhega? Neech. Ganda.

Aur phir jo hua, wo is desh ki sabse purani izzat par chot thi.

Kisaani Indo-Aryon ka garv thi. Wo kaam, jo is desh ki reedh ki haddi tha, wo kuch hisson mein neeche girne lagta hai. Aur jo log asli mein hal chalate the, unki haisiyat samaj ki seedhi par neeche sarak jaati hai.

Ab zara rukiye. Aur ye baat samajhne ki koshish kijiye.

Buddh dharm kya tha? Pujaari ke dhandhe ke khilaaf bagavat. Riwaaz ke khilaaf bagavat. Kisi ko neecha dikhane ke khilaaf bagavat. Kisi ka bada hone ka mauka chheen lene ke khilaaf bagavat.

Aur wahi dharm, apne aap ko pata bhi nahi chala, karodon kisano ko neeche gira deta hai.

Nehru yahan bahut saavdhan hain. Wo kehte hain: iske liye Buddh dharm ko dosh dena galat hoga. Jahan Buddh dharm phala, wahan aisa kuch nahi hua. Wo apni aankhon se dekh sakte the.

Wajah kahin aur thi. Jaati ki vyavastha ke andar kuch aisa tha, jo isi taraf kheenchta tha. Jain dharm ne usse aur dhakka diya, kyunki uski ahimsa usse bhi sakht thi. Aur Buddh dharm ne, bina chaah kar, uska saath diya.

Ye is episode ka sabse bhaari hissa hai. Ek dharm, jo sab ke liye khula tha, apne hi desh mein sabse neeche khade insaan ko aur neeche dhakel deta hai.

Aur isi ke saath waqt bhi badal raha tha. Peechhe se ek sawal aa raha hai. Wahi sawal, jo Malraux ne Paris mein poochha tha.

Ek dharm, jisne yahan lakhon dilon par raaj kiya. Wo is desh se gayab kaise ho gaya? Bina talwar? Bina yuddh?

## Part 9: Bina yuddh ki vidaai

Pehle ek baat saaf kar lein. Bharat mein Buddh dharm ko kisi ne mitaya nahi. Poore desh mein koi khoon wala safaya nahi hua.

Kabhi-kabhi takrar hui — kisi Hindu raja aur Sangh ke beech. Sangh, yaani bhikshuon ka wo bada sangathan, jo bahut taaqatwar ho gaya tha. Par wo takrarein rajneeti ki thi, aur dharm ki buniyad nahi hilti thi.

Aur doosri baat: Hindu dharm kabhi poori tarah Buddh dharm se hata nahi. Jab Buddh dharm apne oonche mukam par tha, tab bhi Hindu dharm sab jagah maujood tha.

To tasveer "pehle Buddh, phir Hindu" wali nahi hai. Tasveer isse ajeeb hai.

Buddh dharm yahan apni maut mara. Wo dheere-dheere phika padta gaya, aur badal kar kuch aur ban gaya.

Nehru yahan ek angrez vidvaan ki baat likhte hain. Bharat mein ek ajeeb hunar hai — jo cheez bahar se udhaar le, use badal dena, aur apne andar ghula lena.

Aur wo aage kehte hain: agar ye baat bahar se li hui cheez ke liye sach hai, to us cheez ke liye kitni zyada sach hogi, jo is desh ke apne mann se nikli thi.

Aur Buddh dharm poori tarah is desh ki paidaish tha. Uska darshan pehle ki Bharatiya soch ki line par tha — Vedant ki, Upanishad ki. Aur Upanishad khud? Unhone pujaari ke dhandhe ka mazaak udaya tha. Riwaaz ka mazaak. Aur jaati ki ahmiyat ko chhota kar diya tha.

To Brahman dharm aur Buddh dharm ek doosre par asar daalte rahe. Unki ladaai thi, par usi ladaai se dono kareeb aa gaye. Darshan mein bhi, aur aam logon ke vishwas mein bhi.

Mahayan ne to Brahman dharm ke roop aur reetiyon se samjhauta kar liya. Uska usool: jitna samjhauta ho, karo — bas naitikta ki jad na chhoote.

Aur phir do lakeerein ek doosre ko kaat gayi.

Brahman dharm ne Buddha ko ek avatar bana diya. Bhagwan. Aur Buddh dharm ne bhi yahi kiya.

Dhyan dijiye ye kya hai. Do badi soch, ek doosre ko nakaar rahi thi. Aur ab wo ek doosre ka bhagwan ek hi bana rahi hain.

Haan, par is daur mein ek badi cheez kho bhi gayi.

Mahayan tez phaila, par chaurai mein jo mila, wo gehraai mein kho diya. Math ameer ho gaye, vested interest ke kendra ban gaye. Anushasan dheela pad gaya. Aur aam puja mein jaadu aur andhavishwas ghus gaye.

Uske pehle hazaar saal ke baad Buddh dharm andar se bigadta gaya.

Ek angrez lekhak ne uski is haalat ka chitra khincha, aur wo bhayanak hai. Wo likhti hain: in bimaar khayaalon ke asar se us dharm ke sansthapak ki saaf, seedhi shiksha dikhna hi band ho gayi. Ek theory par doosri chadhti gayi. Aur aakhir mein poora aasmaan mann ki banayi hui cheezon se bhar gaya. Wo asli, saada niyam us chamakte hue dher ke neeche dab gaya.

Aur Nehru ek chubhne wali line jodte hain: ye baat Brahman dharm aur uski saari shaakhaon par bhi utni hi lagti hai. Wahan bhi wahi bimari thi.

To ab do gawah bachte hain. Ek gawah ne ise phalte hue dekha. Doosre ne girte hue.

## Part 10: Do gawah

Buddh dharm ek aise waqt mein shuru hua, jab poore desh mein samaaj aur soch ko naya karne ki baat ho rahi thi.

Usne logon mein nayi jaan dali. Logon ki taaqat ke naye chashme khole. Naye logon ko mauka diya. Naye neta banaye.

Aur phir Ashoka ka saaya aaya. Wahi Ashoka, jinka naam hum Ep 4.5 mein le chuke hain. Unke raajkiya aashray se Buddh dharm bahut tezi se phaila, aur Bharat ka sabse bada dharm ban gaya.

Usi ke saath wo bahar bhi gaya. Bahut sadiyon tak apne vidvaan aur bhikshu bahar bheje, aur bahar se apne paas bulaye.

Ab pehla gawah. Paanchvi sadi. Ek Chini yaatri Bharat aate hain. Fa-hien.

Fa-hien Buddha ke jaane ke kareeb hazaar saal baad aate hain. Aur wo dekhte hain: Buddh dharm apne hi ghar mein phal-phool raha hai. Poori tarah.

Do sau saal baad doosra gawah aata hai. Wo zyada mashhoor hai. Hsuan Tsang. Uska naam aage ek poori kahani banega. Par abhi sirf itna.

Hsuan Tsang aate hain, aur wo dekhte hain: kami. Giraavat ke nishaan. Kuch jagahon par dharm ab bhi mazboot hai. Par pehle wala roop gaya.

In do gawahon ke beech ek aur cheez ghati.

Chauthi aur paanchvi sadi mein Brahman dharm ki wapasi hui. Gupton ke raaj mein ek bada sanskritik punarjagran aaya. Yaad kijiye, Gupt wahi the, jinke baare mein hum 5.1 mein baat kar chuke hain.

Ye punarjagran Buddh dharm ke khilaaf nahi tha, par usne Brahman dharm ki taaqat badha di. Aur wo Buddh dharm ki udaasi ke khilaaf ek jawab bhi tha.

Uske baad Gupton ke waaris ne Hoonon se lambi ladai ladi. Hoonon ko aakhir mein bhagaya, par desh kamzor pad gaya. Andar sarne ka daur shuru ho gaya.

Kuch chamakte daur phir aaye. Kuch bade aadmi phir aaye. Par dono dharm andar se bigadte gaye. Dono mein nichi reetiyan ghar kar gayin. Aur phir wo waqt aaya, jab dono mein farq karna mushkil ho gaya.

Ye baat dhyan se suniye. Agar Brahman dharm ne Buddh dharm ko sokh liya, to us sokhne ne Brahman dharm ko bhi bahut badal diya. Dono badle.

## Part 11: Malraux ke sawal ka jawab

Ab aakhri kadi. Aathvi sadi. Ek aadmi uthta hai. Shankaracharya. Bharat ke sabse bade darshanikon mein se ek. Wo Hindu sanyasiyon aur bhikshuon ke liye math shuru karte hain. Ek nahi. Kai. Ek doosre se door-door.

Aur ye kya tha?

Ye theek wahi cheez thi, jo Bauddh sangh pehle se kar raha tha. Isse pehle Brahman dharm mein sanyasiyon ke aise bade sangathan nahi the. Bas chhote-chhote giroh.

Ek dharm doosre dharm ki imaarat apne naam par dobara khada kar leta hai. Usi purane naqshe par.

To wahi imaarat, jise hum cold open se dhoondh rahe the, apne safar ke aakhir par apne hi virodhi ke haath chali gayi.

[SFX: wahi door ki ghanti, ab bahut halki, jaise door hoti hui]

Aur Buddh dharm ka baaki kya bacha?

Poorab Bengal mein kuch bigad chuke roop. Sindh mein, uttar-pashchim mein, kuch aur. Iske alawa Buddh dharm is desh se dheere-dheere gayab ho gaya. Ek badi, phaile hue dharm ki itni chup-si vidaai.

To ab Paris ke us kamre mein wapas chaliye.

Malraux ne poochha tha: kis taaqat ne ek bade dharm ko bina yuddh ke andar ghula diya?

Jawab ye hai, aur ye jawab thoda ulta hai.

Buddh dharm ko kisi ne bahar nahi kiya. Kyunki wo bahar wala tha hi nahi. Wo usi ghar ka tha.

Uska darshan isi ghar ki soch, Vedant aur Upanishad, ki line par khada tha. Isliye wo wapas usi line mein mil gaya. Uska sansthapak, jis dharm se lada tha, usi dharm mein ek bhagwan ban gaya — ek avatar. Aur uska sangh, usi ghar ke haathon, math ban gaya.

Yuddh isliye nahi hua, kyunki seema thi hi nahi. Dono taraf ek hi soch thi. Ek hi zameen. Ek hi mitti. Ek cheez apne hi ghar mein ghul gayi, aur ghar waise hi khada raha.

Aur Malraux ka doosra sawal? Wo kaun si taaqat thi, aur kya wo aaj bhi hai? Nehru us sawal ka jawab apne paas nahi rakhte. Wo likhte hain: mujhe iska santosh-janak jawab nahi mila. Wo sawal humare saamne rakh dete hain.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli: Buddha ka avatar banna. Yahi is poore episode ka asli mechanism hai. Is desh mein nayi soch ko bahar nahi kiya jaata. Uske liye andar ek jagah bana di jaati hai. Buddha ko bhagwan banana ek bahut badi khaatir thi. Par uski ek chhoti si keemat bhi thi. Jise bhagwan bana diya jaaye, uske sawal uske gun ban jaate hain.

Doosri: Malraux ka doosra sawal. Kya is desh mein wo taaqat aaj bhi hai? Nehru ne jawab nahi diya. Wo sawal humare liye chhod gaye. Aur ye sawal itihaas ka nahi, aaj ka hai.

Teesri: ek imaarat ka naam. Bihar. Ek poore rajya ka naam aaj bhi math se bana hai. Imaaraton ke naam zubaan mein zinda rehte hain, chahe unke andar kuch bhi na bacha ho.

Aur aapke liye ek sawal. Aaj jab koi naya vichaar is desh mein aata hai, to hum use apna banate hain, ya use sokh jaate hain? Aur ye do baatein ek hi hai kya? Sochiye, jab aap kisi bahar ki cheez ko bilkul apna maan lete hain, to wo badalti hai, ya aap badalte hain?

## Outro

Buddh dharm is desh se vida ho gaya. Par ek aadmi peechhe reh gaya, jiske baare mein aaj bhi ek ilzaam lagta hai.

Ki wo Bauddh tha. Sirf bhes badal kar.

Agli baar: ek naujawan, dakshin ke garam ilaake se, poore desh ko paidal naapne nikalta hai.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
