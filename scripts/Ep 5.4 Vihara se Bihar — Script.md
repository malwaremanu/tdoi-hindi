## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peeche lautta hai. Hum chapter 5 ke beech mein hain, Gupton ke chamakte daur se thoda aage. Pichhli baar humne dekha ki Sanskrit is desh mein kabhi aam logon ki zubaan nahi thi — wo padhe-likhon ki zubaan thi. Par usne peeche ek poora khaandaan chhod diya. Aur us episode ne ek sawal khula chhoda tha: agar kisi dharm ko aam logon tak pahunchana ho, to wo kaun si zubaan chunega?

## Part 1: Jo zubaan ghar-ghar mein bachi

Pehle wo zubaan, jo Sanskrit apne peeche chhod gaya.

Aaj is desh mein koi apni maa se Sanskrit mein baat nahi karta. Par jab aap apni maa se baat karte hain, to us baat ke bahut se shabd usi purani zubaan se aate hain.

Bharat ki zubaaen do bade khaandaan mein baanti jaati hain. Ek khaandaan Sanskrit ka hai: Hindi-Urdu, Bangla, Marathi — aur unke peeche Oriya, Assamese, Punjabi, Sindhi, Kashmiri.

Doosra khaandaan bilkul alag hai: dakshin ki zubaaen — Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam. Inki jadein Sanskrit se nahi hain. Phir bhi inke shabdon ki aadhi bol-chaal us purani zubaan ke kareeb pahunch gayi hai.

Do zubaaen ghar se door bhi gayi.

Pashto — wo zubaan jo Afghanistan se lekar hamari uttar-pashchim seema tak boli jaati hai. Wo bhi isi khaandaan se hai. Aur yahin se wo log nikle, jo aage Sanskrit ke bade vidvaan aur vyakaran-kar bane.

Lanka ki zubaan Singhalese bhi isi khaandaan ki hai. Aur Lanka ke logon ko Bauddh dharm isi desh se mila.

Sanskrit sirf is desh ki zubaan nahi thi. Wo Europe ki purani aur aaj ki zubaaon ki behen hai. Slavonic zubaaon mein bhi uske roop aur jadein milti hain. Europe mein uski sabse kareeb zubaan kaun si hai? Lithuanian — Europe ke ek kone ki chhoti si zubaan.

Ab ek baat, jo is ghar ke logon ne sabse zyada sambhal kar rakhi thi: aawaz.

Purane logon ke liye shabd ki dhwani hi aadhi baat thi. Padhna-likhna kam, sunna zyada. Isliye sahitya mein — kavita ho ya gadya — sur tha, lay thi. Aur shabdon ko theek se bolne ke liye saaf niyam bane. Ye zaroori bhi tha: padhai zubani hoti thi.

Wo niyam aaj bhi chalte hain. Aaj bhi Vedon ka paath usi purane niyam se hota hai — wahi uchchaaran, wahi lay.

Ek zubaan, jo muh se gayi. Par kaan se nahi gayi.

To ye zubaan kis kaam ki thi? Aur jo naya dharm aane wala tha, usne is khaandaan mein se kaun si zubaan chuni?

## Part 2: Buddh ki zubaan

Buddha ka jawab seedha tha. Unhone aam logon ki zubaan chuni.

Us ilaake mein log Prakrit bolte the — Sanskrit se nikli hui bolchaal ki zubaan, padhe-likhon ki nahi. Buddha Sanskrit jaante the. Par unhone wahi zubaan chuni, jo log roz bolte the.

Kyun? Kyunki dharm pehle sunaai maangta hai. Jo baat kaan tak na pahunche, wo dil tak kaise pahunchegi?

Aur yahin se ek badi baat shuru hoti hai. Jo dharm logon ki zubaan mein bolta hai, wo logon ka ho jaata hai. Jo dharm sirf padhe-likhon ki zubaan mein bolta hai, wo padhe-likhon ka reh jaata hai. Buddha ne ye baat samajh li thi. Isliye unhone wahi zubaan li, jo khet mein, gali mein, dukaan par boli jaati thi.

Is ek chunav se aage ek poori zubaan bani. Pali. Pali — wo zubaan, jismein Buddh ke shuruaati dharm-granth likhe gaye.

Yaad kijiye: Buddha ne khud kuch likha nahi. Unki baatein, unke sawal-jawab, unki lambi charchayein — sab unke jaane ke bahut baad Pali mein likhi gayi.

Aur wo likhaai aaj bhi zinda hai. Kahan? Lanka mein. Burma mein. Siam mein — yaani aaj ka Sri Lanka, Myanmar aur Thailand. In teen jagahon par aaj bhi Buddh dharm ka wo purana roop chalta hai, jise Hinayan kehte hain.

Ab waqt palat ta hai. Do-teen sau saal baad Bharat mein Sanskrit ki wapasi hoti hai. Aur ab Bauddh vidvaan apni bahas, apna darshan, apne granth Sanskrit mein likhne lagte hain.

Usi daur mein ek Bauddh lekhak hote hain — Ashvaghosha. Wo kavita likhte hain, aur naatak bhi.

Dhyan dijiye: humare paas jo sabse purane naatak bache hain, wo isi Bauddh lekhak ke hain. Aur wo naatak Buddh dharm ke prachaar ke liye likhe gaye the.

Ek dharm, jo apne prachaar ke liye naatak likhwa raha hai. Aur is chunav se ek baat saaf hui: is naye dharm ka darwaza sabke liye khula tha, par us darwaze ki zubaan badalni padi thi. Wahi uski taqkat bani.

Aur ye Sanskrit wali Bauddh kitaabein yahin nahi rukti. Wo China jaati hain. Japan. Tibet. Madhya Asia. Wahan Buddh dharm ka doosra roop phailta hai, jise Mahayan kehte hain.

Ye chunav chhota nahi tha. Ek hi dharm ke do darwaze khul gaye — ek aam logon ke liye, ek padhe-likhon ke liye. Aur dono ke peeche, aage chalkar do alag ghar khade hone wale the.

## Part 3: Us daur ka toofan

Ye sab kisi sooni jagah mein nahi hua.

Jis daur ne Buddha ko paida kiya, wo soch ka toofan tha. Bharat mein sawal poochhne ka, bahas karne ka, dhoondhne ka aisa mahaul tha, jo us se pehle kabhi nahi tha.

Aur ye sirf Bharat mein nahi tha.

Usi waqt China mein do guru the — Lao-tze aur Confucius. Iran mein Zoroaster. Yunani ke saahil par Pythagoras.

Poori purani duniya mein, ek saath, itne alag-alag deshon mein, log ek hi cheez kar rahe the. Wo poochh rahe the: ye duniya hai kya? Hum hain kya? Jeena kaise chahiye?

Aur Bharat mein isi toofan ne kya banaya?

Ek taraf Buddha ka raasta. Doosri taraf Mahavir ka. Ek taraf wo log, jo maante the ki sab kuch mitti aur jism se bana hai — unki kitaabein nahi bachi, par unke vichaar ka naam materialism hai. Ek taraf Gita. Ek taraf Jain dharm.

Sab ek hi zameen se. Ek hi mitti se. Ek hi mahaul se.

Nehru kehte hain, ye alag-alag paratein thi. Ek parat doosri ko kheenchti thi. Kabhi ek doosre ke oopar chadh bhi jaati thi. Aur in sab se aage chalkar wahi darshan bane, jo sadiyon tak chale.

Ek hi mitti se, ek saath, itne alag-alag raaste. Isi mahaul mein Mahavir ne apna raasta liya. Isi mahaul mein wo log khade hue, jo maante the ki aatma ka koi wajood hi nahi.

Nehru is daur ko ek toofan kehte hain. Aur toofan ka swabhav hai — guzar jaana.

Aur is toofan ka nishaan aaj bhi hai. Thodi der ke liye hi sahi, us daur mein is desh ne sawal poochhne ko hi ek aadarsh bana liya tha. Yahan mann ko khula chhodne ki himmat pai gayi.

Par phir toofan thahar jaata hai.

Soch dheemi pad jaati hai. Sawal saanche ban jaate hain. Aur un saanchon mein takrar shuru hoti hai — kaun sahi, kaun galat.

Aur phir ek cheez hoti hai, jo Buddha ne pehle hi dekh li thi.

## Part 4: Phoot kyun padi

Daraarein pehle school ke andar padi, phir dharm ke andar.

Buddh dharm ke saath-saath aur bhi school khade ho rahe the. Aur Buddh dharm ke andar khud phoot pad rahi thi. Ek ke baad ek khanda. Ek ke baad ek mata.

Pehle soch zinda thi. Ab soch ko saancha mil gaya. Bahas chhoti, takrar badi. Nehru ise ek shabd se bulate hain: scholasticism — yaani wahi soch, jo apne hi niyamon mein kas kar reh gayi, aur nayi cheez dhoondhna chhod deti hai.

Aur yahin Buddha ki chetavni yaad aati hai.

Unhone apne logon ko baar-baar mana kiya tha — metaphysics par panditon wali bahas mat karo. Jispar baat nahi ho sakti, uspar chup rehna hi behtar hai.

Unke liye sach zindagi mein tha, duniya se bahar ke sawalon ke jawab mein nahi. Unhone zindagi ke naitik hisse par zor diya. Unhe lagta tha, mahin sochan isi naitikta ko kha jaati hai.

Sochiye, ek sabha mein do acharya baithe hain. Ek kehta hai: cheez hai. Doosra kehta hai: cheez nahi hai. Dono ke paas tark hai, dono ke paas shabd hain. Aur jis aadmi ne ye sab shuru kiya tha, usne aise sawalon par chup rehna kaha tha.

Aur ye chetavni aaj bhi kaam ki hai. Jab bahas lambi ho jaati hai, to asli sawal peechhe chhoot jaata hai. Log shabdon par ladne lagte hain, aur jis baat ke liye wo lade the, wo bhool jaate hain.

Shuruaati Buddh dharm mein is baat ki chamak bachi thi. Wo anubhav par khada tha. Jis cheez ka anubhav nahi hota — jaise 'shuddh sat' ka khayal, ya ek rachnahar bhagwan ka khayal — use sawal se hata diya gaya. Par anubhav to tha. Aur wo badalta rehta tha. To uske dar-miyan wale roop maan liye gaye, aur aage ki khoj mann ke andar hoti rahi.

To ye baat dhyan se suniye. Buddha baghi the. Par wo is desh ke purane dharm se kat kar alag nahi hue the.

Aur yahin sawal uthta hai. To naya kya tha? Aur jo bagavat unhone ki, wo asli mein kis cheez ke khilaf thi?

## Part 5: Wo naya kya tha?

Is sawal par Nehru ek vidvaan ki baat likhte hain.

Us vidvaan ka saaf kehna hai: Gautam ka paida hona, bade hona, jeena aur marna — sab isi desh ke purane dharm ke andar hua. Wahan se wo bahar kabhi nikle hi nahi.

Unke darshan mein aisa kuch bhi nahi hai, jo us zamane ke kisi purane mat mein pehle se na ho. Aur unki naitikta ka bahut sa hissa pehle ki ya baad ki kitaabon se mil jaata hai.

To unki apni cheez kya thi?

Wo ye thi. Jo pehle se kaha ja chuka tha, use unhone uthaya. Usme jaan daali. Bada kiya. Saaf kiya. Usme tarteeb di. Aur jo baat doosron ne kahi thi, use aakhir tak nibhaya.

Kisi ne kaha tha: insaan ke saath insaaf hona chahiye. Unhone us baat ko apne nateeje tak pahunchaya. Jo nyaay aur baraabari ke usool kuch bade sochne walon ne pehle maan liye the, unhone unhe ant tak chalaya.

Unki asli baat unka tark nahi thi. Unki baat unki lagan thi — aur aam logon ki bhalai ka khayal.

Par ye bhi sach hai: unhone ek cheez zaroor boyi. Wo the riwaaz ke khilaf bagavat — us dhang ke khilaf, jis tarah us zamane ka dharm chalta tha.

Aur dhyan dijiye: aitraaz unke darshan par nahi tha.

Purane dharm mein soch bilkul khuli thi. Koi bhi philosophy maan sakte the. Koi bhi tark chal sakta tha. Bas ek shart thi — wo tark vichaar hi rahe, zindagi ka dhang na badle.

Kyunki soch mein wo khula tha, aur vyavhaar mein sakht. Jo uske riwaaz ko na maane, uske liye jagah nahi thi.

Isliye takrar siddhant se nahi, roz ke rehan-sehan se hui. Aur Buddha ke jaane ke baad ye daraar chaudi hoti gayi.

Yahin ek baat saaf ho jaati hai. Ye dharm bahar se nahi aaya tha. Ye isi ghar ke andar paida hua tha. Isliye jab ye lada, to bhi apne hi logon se lada. Aur ye baat aage ke liye yaad rakhne wali hai. Jo cheez ghar ke andar paida hoti hai, wo ghar se ladti hai — aur uski haar bhi ghar se hi hoti hai.

## Part 6: Seedi jo khaali mein khatam hoti hai

Dharm ke do ghar ban jaate hain. Naya, phailne wala roop — Mahayan. Purana, apne granthon par tika hua — Hinayan.

Mahayan mein Buddha khud bhagwan ban jaate hain, aur unki murti banti hai — wo uttar-pashchim se aati hai, us Yunani ilaake se, jahan Gandhara ki kala khadi thi.

Dono ke beech ki ladai kadwi thi, aur wo takrar aage ke poore itihaas mein chali. Aaj bhi Hinayan wale desh China-Japan ke Bauddhon ko thoda neeche maante hain. Hinayan ne apne granth ek band dareeche ki tarah sambhal kar rakhe. Mahayan har taraf phaila, lagbhag sab kuch bardaasht karta raha.

Buddha ne aatma ka sawal latka rakha tha. Unke baad ke sabse bade acharyon ne wo sawal kaat diya — aatma hai hi nahi.

[SFX: ek gehri, lambi ghanti ki goonj; uske neeche door ki bheed, jaise kisi bahas-sabha ki bhoorbhooraahat]

Isi soch par ek aadmi uthta hai.

Nagarjuna — Kanishka ke waqt ke ek Bauddh acharya, jise Mahayan ka asli shilpkaar maana jaata hai, aur jiske tark ke aage aaj bhi darshan chup ho jaate hain.

Unka daur bahas ka daur tha: acharya door-door se aate, faisla tark se hota. Is sabha mein Nagarjuna ka tark sabse tez tha. Nehru likhte hain, wo un nateejon se nahi darte the, jo logon ko besharm lagte the.

Wo apne tark ko pakadte the, aur chhodte nahi the. Aage. Aur aage. Soch khud ko nahi jaanta. Bhagwan duniya se alag nahi.

Wo aage chale. Sach aur galat mein farq nahi bacha. Kuch bhi asli nahi — duniya bas dikhti hai, ek tarteeb, jise hum maan lete hain.

Us ke peeche wo kisi aisi cheez ki taraf ishara karte hain, jise hum soch nahi sakte — Shunyata.

Shunyata ka matlab hai shoonya — khaali. Wahi shabd, jo ganit mein zero ke nishaan ke liye use hota hai. Par ye khaali pan nahi: iska matlab wo cheez, jo sab se door hai, aur sab ke andar.

Ek mashhoor Bauddh vidvaan ki line yaad rakhne layak hai: Shunyata ke kaaran hi sab kuch mumkin hai. Uske bina kuch bhi mumkin nahi.

Ab ek mazedaar kahani. Russia ke ek vidvaan ne dekha ki is Bauddh soch aur aaj ke science mein kuch shakal milti hai. Soviet Russia ke ek ilaake mein logon se kaha gaya: aaj ka science maanta hai ki koi aatma nahi. Bhikshu chup nahi baithe — unhone ek pamflet chhapa.

Par Nehru ruk jaate hain. Dekhiye, metaphysics kahan tak le gayi — aur Buddha ki chetavni kitni sahi thi.

Par mann qaid hona nahi maanta. Use wo phal chahiye, jo haath se door hai. Wo phir bhi haath badhata hai.

Ye poora tark khara darshan nahi tha; iska tareeka mann ke andar jhaankna tha. Wahi cheez, jise aaj ka manovigyan subconscious kehta hai, un granthon mein pehle se maujood hai — ek paragraph, jo aaj ki Oedipus wali soch jaisa lagta hai.

Ab is safar ko ek seedi ki tarah chadhiye. Buddh dharm mein chaar darshan bane — do Hinayan ke, do Mahayan ke. Sab Upanishadon se nikle, par Vedon ki satta nahi maante. Ek vidvaan ne iske chaar kadam gine hain.

Pehla kadam. Gyaan seedha hota hai. Cheez dikhti hai, bas.

Doosra kadam. Beech mein vichaar aa jaate hain. Mann aur cheez ke beech parda aa gaya.

Ye do kadam Hinayan ke hain.

Teesra kadam. Mahayan parde ke peeche ki asli cheez mita deta hai. Ab bacha kya? Mann ke andar vichaaron ki ek lambi kataar.

Aur chothha kadam — Nagarjuna ka, Madhyamika, madhya ka raasta. Yahan mann khud bhi ghul jaata hai. Bachte hain sirf vichaar aur anubhav ke tukde, jinke baare mein kuch pakka nahi.

Hum pahunche khaali aasmaan par. Zyada se zyada itna kaha ja sakta hai: ye ek chetna hai — vijyana.

Par yahan ek ulat phat jati hai. Poora tark kehta hai, yahan kuch nahi. Par wahi tark ye bhi kehta hai: is dikhti duniya mein naitikta ka pakka mol hai — tark yahan kaam karta hai, us anant par nahi, jo us paar hai.

## Part 7: Duniya ka pehla prachaarak

Ab lautiye us imaarat par. Pehle ek sawal: ye dharm kis zameen par jama?

Buddh dharm ne sabse pehle jad pakdi Magadha mein. Magadha — wahi purana ilaaka, jahan Brahman dharm kamzor tha. Wahan se wo pashchim aur uttar ki taraf badha.

Shuru mein ye Kshatriyon ka aandolan tha, par iska appeal aam logon ka tha. Aur bahut se Brahman bhi isme shamil ho gaye.

Nehru ka andaaza ye hai: inhi der se aaye Brahmanon ke kaaran Buddh dharm darshan ki taraf jhuka, aur shayad Mahayan ka chauda roop bana.

To is naye dharm ne purane dharm se kya cheen liya?

Bohot saare riwaaz gaye. Aur sabse badi cheez — pashu-bali. Yajna, jismein jaanwar maare jaate the, wo dheere-dheere simat gaya.

Ahimsa ki baat Ved aur Upanishad mein pehle se thi. Par Buddh dharm ne use ubhaara, aur Jain dharm ne usse bhi zyada. Zindagi ka aadar badha. Jaanwaron par daya badhi.

Aur in sab ke peeche ek hi koshish thi: achha jeevan, uncha jeevan.

Par asar sirf riwaaz par nahi hua. Ek badi baat ye thi — ye dharm apne aap tak seemit nahi raha.

Purana Aryan dharm is zameen ka dharm tha. Uske darwaze bahar ki taraf nahi khulte the. Koi prachaar nahi. Koi mission nahi. Kai sau saal aisa hi chala. Jo bahar se aata, wo andar ghul jaata — aur nayi jaati ban jaata.

Aur uske dil mein se ek naya jharna phoota. Wo jharna purani seemaon ko tod kar bahar baha.

Buddh dharm ki pukar apne desh ki thi. Par wo sirf desh ki nahi thi. Wo ek universal pukar thi — achhe jeevan ki pukar, jismein na jaati ki deewar thi, na varg ki, na mulk ki.

Aur is baat ko sabse pehle bade paimane par kaam mein kisne lagaya? Ashoka. Wahi Ashoka, jinka naam is set mein pehle aa chuka hai. Unhone doosre deshon mein apne doot bheje. Unhone prachaar karwaya.

Prachaar ka matlab sirf doot bhejna nahi hota. Iska matlab hota hai — apni baat doosre ki zubaan mein kehna. Aur us waqt tak koi dharm aisa nahi karta tha. Ek dharm pehli baar apni seema se bahar gaya, aur ye baat apne aap mein nayi thi.

Isi se is desh ki aankh bahar ki taraf khuli. Aage chalkar log samundar ke raaste dhoondhne lagte hain, aur bahar apne raajya khade karte hain. Us raaste ki apni kahani hai; wo aage aayegi.

Filhaal itna: ek dharm, jo apne ghar mein band tha, nikal kar duniya ka pehla prachaarak dharm ban gaya.

## Part 8: Bhikshu ka ghar

Par is dharm ka ek doosra chehra bhi tha.

Buddha ne khud mana kiya tha ki sakht tapasya ka koi dharmik mol nahi hota. Par unki shiksha ka kul asar zindagi ki taraf udaasi ka tha.

Ye khaas taur par Hinayan ki soch thi. Aur Jain dharm ki soch usse bhi zyada. Doosri duniya ki taraf khinchaav. Is duniya ke bojh se azaadi. Sanyam. Aur shakahari bhojan ki taraf jhukav.

Dhyan dijiye: ye baatein Buddha se pehle bhi is desh mein thi. Farq sirf zor ka tha.

Purane Aryan aadarsh ka zor kuch aur tha. Poori, gol zindagi. Pehla daur — vidyaarthi ka, sanyam ka. Doosra — grihasth ka, poore jeevan ka hissa. Phir dheere-dheere peeche hatna, samaj ka kaam. Aur aakhri daur, budhape ka — sanyas.

Pehle jo log duniya chhodte the, wo jangal mein chhote giroh banakar rehte the. Unke aas-paas kuch shishya aa jaate the. Bas.

Buddh dharm ke aane par in girohon ki jagah bade math aa gaye. Vihara. Aur bhikshuniyon ke bhi math. Aur logon ka ek lagatar bahav unki taraf shuru ho gaya.

Ye bahav kitna bada tha? Iska sabse bada saboot hamari zubaan mein aaj bhi hai.

Bihar.

Vihara, yaani math. Bihar ka naam hi math se bana hai. Sochiye — agar ek poore pradesh ka naam math ho gaya, to us ilaake mein kitne math the. Pure shahar ke barabar log, ek hi chhat ke neeche.

Sochiye, ek imaarat mein ek poore gaon ke barabar log rehte the. Unke khane ki vyavastha thi, padhne ki, aur sochne ki. Aisa ghar akela khada nahi rehta — wo apne aas-paas gaon khada kar deta hai.

Aur ye math sirf rehne ki jagah nahi the. Ye paathshaala the. Kai jagah school, kai jagah uncha paathshala.

Sirf Bharat mein nahi. Poore Madhya Asia mein bade Bauddh vihara the. Balkh mein ek mashhoor vihara tha, jahan ek saath hazaar bhikshu rehte the — Nav-Vihara, naya vihara. Farsi mein wo Naubahar ban gaya.

Par Nehru ek sawal poochhte hain. Ye udaasi China, Japan aur Burma mein itni kyun nahi hui?

Unka andaaza: har desh ka mahaul dharm ko apne saanche mein dhaal leta hai. China ke paas Confucius aur Lao-tze ki badi parampara thi. Aur China-Japan ne Mahayan chuna, jo kam udaasi wala tha. Aur Bharat par Jain dharm ka zor bhi tha — in sab mein sabse zyada jeevan se mooh modne wala darshan.

## Part 9: Satyakama ka jawab

Ab ek ajeeb baat. Buddh dharm ka jaati par jo asar hua, wo uski poori soch ke ulta tha.

Buddh dharm ne jaati ko theek nahi maana, par uski buniyadi wajah sweekar kar li.

Aur us zamane ki jaati aaj ki jaati nahi thi. Buddha ke waqt jaati dheeli thi. Qaabiliyat, kirdaar aur kaam ki zyada ahmiyat thi. Janm ki kam.

Buddha khud bahut jagah "Brahman" shabd ka matlab use karte hain — ek samajhdaar, imaandaar, anushasan mein dhala hua insaan. Naam se nahi. Kirdaar se.

Aur is par ek kahani hai, jo bahut purani hai. Chhandogya Upanishad ki.

Kalpana kijiye.

Ek ladka hai. Satyakama. Uska mann hai ki wo Gautam rishi ke paas padhne jaaye. Ye Gautam Buddha nahi hain — koi aur rishi hain.

Ghar chhodne se pehle ladka apni maa se poochhta hai: maa, hum kis khandaan ke hain? Mera gotra kya hai?

Gotra ka matlab hota hai khandaan — kis khaandaan ka beta hai tu. Us zamane mein ye sawal aasan nahi tha. Is ek sawal ke jawab se tay hota tha ki ladka padh sakta hai ya nahi.

Maa ka naam Jabala hai. Wo jhoot nahi bolti.

Wo kehti hai: mere bachche, mujhe nahi pata. Jawan waqt mein main ek ghar se doosre ghar naukri karti phirti thi. Wahin tujhe paaya. Mujhe tera khandaan nahi pata. Main Jabala hoon. Tu Satyakama hai. Ja, aur keh dena: main Satyakama hoon, Jabala ka beta.

[SFX: sookhi lakdiyon ka ek gaththar zameen par rakhne ki halki aawaaz]

Ladka jaata hai. Rishi ke saamne khada hota hai. Jo maa ne kaha, wahi shabd-ba-shabd dohra deta hai.

Ab rishi ka jawab suniye.

Rishi kehte hain: sach bolne se hi asli Brahman banta hai. Jo insaan apni maa ke shabdon ko is tarah dohra de, wo Brahman ho sakta hai. Ja, lakdiyaan le aao. Main tujhe apna shishya banata hoon. Tu sach se nahi hata.

Is ek jawab mein jaati itihaas nahi thi. Jaati ek gun tha.

To us zamane mein jaati kaisi thi?

Shayad sirf Brahman hi ek sakht jaati thi. Kshatriy apni parampara par garv karte the, par us varg ka darwaza khula tha — jo raja ban gaya, wo andar aaya.

Baaki zyadatar log Vaishya the — kisan. Aur kisaani izzat ka kaam thi. Aur bhi kaam wali jaatiyan thi.

Aur jo jaati se bahar maane jaate the? Wo bahut kam the — kuch jangal ke log, aur kuch wo, jinka kaam murdon ko thikane lagana tha.

Ye sab Buddh dharm se pehle ki baat hai. Ab dekhiye iske baad kya hua. Aur yahin is episode ki sabse ulti baat hai.

## Part 10: Hal chalane wala neeche

Jain dharm aur Buddh dharm ka ahimsa ka zor ek bilkul hi ajeeb jagah pahunchta hai.

Kheti.

Sochiye. Hal chalane se zameen palat jaati hai. Zameen palatne se uske andar ki chhoti chhoti jaan — keede, aur unke ande, sab ka ant hota hai.

To jo dharm kehta hai jaan ko nahi maarna, uske liye kheti ka kaam kaisa dikhega? Neech.

Aur ye baat us insaan par utarti hai, jo subah andhere mein uth kar hal chalane jaata tha. Uske haath mailay hote the. Ab wo haath dharm ki nazar mein mailay se zyada kuch ban gaye.

Aur phir jo hua, wo is desh ki sabse purani izzat par chot thi.

Kisaani Indo-Aryon ka garv thi. Wo kaam, jo is desh ki reedh ki haddi tha, wo kuch hisson mein neeche girne lagta hai. Aur jo log asli mein hal chalate the, unki haisiyat samaj ki seedhi par neeche sarak jaati hai.

Ab zara rukiye. Aur ye baat samajhne ki koshish kijiye.

Buddh dharm kya tha? Pujaari ke dhandhe ke khilaf bagavat. Riwaaz ke khilaf bagavat. Kisi ko neecha dikhane ke khilaf bagavat. Kisi ka bada hone ka mauka chheen lene ke khilaf bagavat.

Uske haath ki mitti savdhaan rehne ki nishani thi. Aur ab usi mitti ko paap kaha jaane laga.

Aur wahi dharm, apne aap ko pata bhi nahi chala, karodon kisaanon ko neeche gira deta hai.

Nehru yahan bahut saavdhan hain. Wo kehte hain: iske liye Buddh dharm ko dosh dena galat hoga. Jahan Buddh dharm phala, wahan aisa kuch nahi hua.

Pehle uske kaam mein koi sawal nahi tha. Ab sawal khada ho gaya: jo jaan ko maarta hai, wo shuddh kaise hoga? Aur is sawal ke jawab mein us insaan ki izzat giri — uski nahi, uske kaam ki.

Wajah kahin aur thi. Jaati ki vyavastha ke andar kuch aisa tha, jo isi taraf kheenchta tha. Jain dharm ne usse aur dhakka diya, kyunki uski ahimsa usse bhi sakht thi. Aur Buddh dharm ne, bina chaah kar, uska saath diya.

To ek dharm, jo sab ke liye khula tha, apne hi desh mein sabse neeche khade insaan ko aur neeche dhakel deta hai.

Aur ab wo sawal aa jaata hai, jise hum pehle se jaante hain. Wo dharm, jisne yahan lakhon dilon par raaj kiya — wo is desh se gaya kaise?

## Part 11: Vihara se Bihar

Lautiye us ghanti par. Wahi subah. Wahi darwaze. Wahi kataar.

Sochiye, wahi gali, wahi patthar, wahi ghanti. Ek hazaar saal pehle is gali mein sau log chale the. Aaj wahan koi nahi chalta.

[SFX: wahi door ki ghanti, ab bahut halki, jaise door hoti hui]

To is dharm ne apne desh ke saath kya kiya?

Usne ghar diye. Hazaaron vihara. Un gharon se paathshaala paida hui. Paathshaala se daulat. Daulat se taaqat. Aur taaqat se ek poore pradesh ka naam — Bihar.

Sochiye, wo ghar un logon ke liye bana tha, jo bhaag gaye the. Aur wahi ghar dheere-dheere ek shahar jaisa ban gaya. Usme rasoi thi, bazaar tha, paathshaala thi, aur hazaar logon ka roz ka hisaab.

Par us dharm ne apni sabse unchi baat bhi saath laayi thi. Kisi jaan ko na maarna. Aur wahi baat, jab aakhri hadd tak le jaayi gayi, to jis kaam ko is desh ne sabse bada maana tha — kheti — wo neeche gir gaya. Aur jo log wahi kaam karte the, wo usse bhi neeche.

Yahi is episode ka jawab hai.

Jo dharm sabse zyada logon ki zubaan mein bola, wo apne desh mein sabse badi imaarat khada kar gaya. Usne yahan ke riwaaz badle. Pashu-bali rok di. Ahimsa ko uncha kiya. Aur duniya bhar tak apni baat pahunchai.

Aur wahi dharm apne hi ghar ka chehra badal deta hai. Dheere-dheere wo yahan se nikal bhi jaata hai.

Ek darwaza khula tha. Wo band nahi hua. Log usse bhool gaye.

To jawab: usne ghar diye, daulat di, aur ek pradesh ko naam diya. Par jo cheez usne sabse oonchi rakhi, usi ne kheti ko neeche kar diya, aur un logon ko aur neeche, jinka kaam kheti thi.

Ek imaarat. Hazaaron imaaraatein. Aur ek naam, jo un sab se lambi umar paata hai.

Naam kya hota hai? Naam imaarat ka sabse halka hissa hota hai — aur sabse lamba chalne wala. Wahi naam, jo aaj bhi Bihar hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Bihar. Ek pradesh ka naam ek imaarat se aaya hai. Aaj us imaarat ke zyadatar khandhar hain. Par naam zinda hai. Naam imaarat se lambi umar paata hai.

Aur ek baat, jo shayad aaj humare kaam ki hai.

Log samajhdaar hote jaate hain, aur samajhdaari ke saath kayi achhi baatein pakad lete hain. Ahimsa. Jaan ki izzat. Kisi ko neecha na dikhana.

Par koi bhi achhi baat, jab aakhri hadd tak le jaayi jaaye, to wo ulti pad sakti hai. Hal chalane wala neeche gir sakta hai — apne hi jaisi soch ke kaaran.

Aur us dharm ke saath ek aur cheez hui, jo har bade aandolan ke saath hoti hai. Bagavat se sanstha bani. Sanstha ke apne ghar bane. Ghar paathshaala bane, paathshaala daulat bani.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aaj aapki zindagi mein koi aisi achhi baat hai, jise aap itni sakhti se maante hain, ki usse kisi kaam karne wale ka darja neeche na gir jaaye?

## Outro

Aath-nau saal pehle, Paris mein, ek French lekhak ne Nehru se namaskar se bhi pehle ek sawal poochha tha.

Aapke desh mein ek bada dharm paida hua. Wo yahan phala. Yahin lakhon logon ka dharm bana. Aur phir wo dheere-dheere yahan se gayab ho gaya.

Bina jang. Bina talwar. Koi khoon wala safaya nahi hua.

To kis cheez ne ye kaam kiya?

Jawab agle episode mein — aur jawab ki taraf jaane wala raasta chhe darwazon se hokar jaata hai.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
