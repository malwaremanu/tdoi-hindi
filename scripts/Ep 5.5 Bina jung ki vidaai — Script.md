Bahar barsaat ho rahi hai. Andar ek mez, do kursiyan, aur kheeki par boondon ki thaap.

Ek aadmi Nehru ke saamne baithta hai. Andre Malraux — ek French lekhak. Par sirf lekhak nahi. Usne is sadi ki ladaiyan apni aankhon se dekhi hain, aur unhe sirf dekhkar nahi chhoda. Kitaab bhi likhi, morche par bhi khada raha.

Dono milte hain. Ek haath aage badhta hai.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: hum ek imaarat ke peeche chal rahe the — vihara, wo ghar jo duniya chhodne walon ke liye bana. Wo paathshaala bana, daulat ka gadh bana, aur ek poore soobe ka naam ban gaya: Bihar. Pichhli baar ek sawal khula tha: jis dharm ne logon ki zubaan chuni, wo apne hi ghar se chup-chaap gayab kaise ho gaya?

## Part 1: Ek sawal, do ghar

Paris ka wo sawal hawa mein nahi chhoda gaya.

Nehru likhte hain: Malraux us sawal se bhare hue the, aur jaise hi hum mile, wo sawal unse phoot pada.

Ek French buddhijeewi ka aisa sawal hona ajeeb nahi tha. Wo aisa aadmi tha, jisne likha bhi, aur jiya bhi.

Par ek baat dhyan dene wali hai.

Europe ya America mein bahut kam log aise sawal par apna waqt lagate. Unke saamne aaj ki apni samasyayein hoti hain, aur wo unhi mein ghule rehte hain.

Malraux bhi un samasyayon se bhare hue the. Phir bhi wo roshni dhoondhte rahe — soch mein, bolne mein, likhne mein. Aur sabse zyada, kaam mein. Zindagi aur maut ke khel mein.

To sawal unke liye kitaabon wala sawal nahi tha. Wo ek aise aadmi ka sawal tha, jo usse roz ji raha tha.

Aur ye sawal Nehru ka apna sawal bhi tha. Nehru likhte hain: ye wahi sawal tha, jo mera mann khud baar-baar banata rehta tha.

Ab dono taraf se sawal ek hi tha. To jawab kyun nahi mila?

Nehru imaandari se likhte hain. Wo kehte hain: is sawal ka santosh-janak jawab mere paas nahi tha. Apne liye bhi nahi, Malraux ke liye bhi nahi.

Jawab hain. Wajahein hain. Kaafi hain. Par wo sawal ki jad ko chhoote hi nahi.

Aur yahan ek baat dhyan dijiye. Malraux ne sawal poochha, aur baat aage badh gayi. Nehru ne usse chhoda nahi. Wo use apni kitaab mein le gaye — Ahmadnagar ki kothri mein — aur poore desh se guzar kar uska jawab dhoondhne nikle.

To aaj hum wahi karenge, jo Nehru ne kiya. Hum jawab se pehle sawal ke aas-paas ki zameen naapenge. Aur pehle do gawah bulayenge.

## Part 2: Do gawah

Pehle ek baat saaf kar lein. Bharat mein Buddh dharm ko kisi ne mitaya nahi.

Poore desh mein koi khoon wala safaya nahi hua. Kabhi-kabhi takrar hui — kisi Hindu raja aur Sangh ke beech. Sangh, yaani bhikshuon ka wo bada sangathan, jo bahut taaqatwar ho gaya tha. Par wo takrarein rajneeti ki thi. Unse dharm ki buniyad nahi hilti thi.

Aur doosri baat: Hindu dharm kabhi poori tarah Buddh dharm se hata nahi. Jab Buddh dharm apne oonche mukam par tha, tab bhi Hindu dharm sab jagah maujood tha.

To tasveer "pehle Buddh, phir Hindu" wali nahi hai. Tasveer isse ajeeb hai.

Uska ant bahar se nahi aaya. Wo dheere-dheere phika pada. Wo badla — aur badal kar kuch aur ban gaya.

Nehru yahan ek angrez vidvaan ki baat likhte hain. Uska kehna tha: is desh mein ek ajeeb hunar hai. Jo cheez bahar se udhaar le, use badal dena, aur apne andar ghula lena.

Aur wo aage kehte hain: agar ye baat bahar se li hui cheez ke liye sach hai, to us cheez ke liye kitni zyada sach hogi, jo is desh ke apne mann se nikli thi.

Kyunki Buddh dharm poori tarah is desh ki paidaish tha. Uska darshan pehle se yahan chali aa rahi soch ki line par tha — Upanishadon ki line par. Aur Upanishad khud? Unhone pujaari ke dhandhe ka mazaak udaya tha. Riwaaz ka mazaak. Aur jaati ki ahmiyat ko chhota kar diya tha.

Ab do gawah. Dono door se aaye. Dono ne ek hi dharm dekha — par ek ne use phoolte hue dekha, doosre ne murjhaate hue.

[SFX: door kisi vihara ki ek gehri ghanti, ek baar; phir bahut dheeme, nange pair patthar par]

Pehla gawah paanchvi sadi mein aata hai. Fa-hien. Ek Chini bhikshu, jo Buddha ke jaane ke kareeb hazaar saal baad yahan pahuncha. Usne jo dekha, wo saaf tha: Buddh dharm apne hi ghar mein phal-phool raha hai. Poori tarah.

Do sau saal baad doosra gawah aata hai. Wo zyada mashhoor hai. Hsuan Tsang. Usne yahan kya dekha? Kami. Giraavat ke nishaan. Kuch jagahon par dharm ab bhi mazboot tha — par pehle wala roop chala gaya tha.

In dono gawahon ke beech, ek cheez ghati.

## Part 3: Brahman dharm ka punarjanm

Buddh dharm ek aise waqt mein shuru hua, jab poore desh mein samaaj aur soch ko naya karne ki baat ho rahi thi.

Usne logon mein nayi jaan dali. Logon ki taaqat ke naye chashme khole. Naye logon ko mauka diya, naye neta banaye.

Aur phir Ashoka ka saaya aaya — wahi Ashoka, jinka naam hum Ep 4.5 mein le chuke hain. Unke raajkiya aashray se Buddh dharm bahut tezi se phaila, aur Bharat ka sabse bada dharm ban gaya.

Usi ke saath wo bahar bhi gaya. Sadiyon tak apne vidvaan aur bhikshu bahar bheje, aur bahar se apne paas bulaye. Kai vidvaan dheere-dheere Bharat se China jaakar bas gaye.

Ab wo cheez aayi, jo do gawahon ke beech ghati.

Chauthi aur paanchvi sadi mein Brahman dharm ki wapasi hui. Gupton ke raaj mein ek bada sanskritik punarjagran aaya. Yaad kijiye, Gupt wahi the, jinke baare mein hum 5.1 mein baat kar chuke hain. Ye punarjagran Buddh dharm ke khilaaf nahi tha — par usne Brahman dharm ki taaqat badha di. Aur wo Buddh dharm ki us udaasi ke khilaaf ek jawab bhi tha, jo bahut logon ne zindagi ki taraf mehsoos ki thi.

Uske baad Gupton ke waaris ne Hoonon se lambi ladai ladi. Hoonon ko aakhir mein bhaga diya — par desh kamzor pad gaya. Andar sarne ka daur shuru ho gaya.

Izafat hoti gayi. Dono dharm andar se bigadte gaye. Dono mein nichi reetiyan ghar kar gayin. Aakhirkar dono mein farq karna mushkil ho gaya.

Yahan ek baat dhyan se suniye.

Agar Brahman dharm ne Buddh dharm ko sokh liya, to us sokhne ne Brahman dharm ko bhi badal diya. Dono badle.

Aur aathvi sadi mein ek darshanik uthte hain. Wo Hindu sanyasiyon ke liye math shuru karte hain — ek nahi, kai, aur ek doosre se door-door. Us waqt tak Brahman dharm mein aise bade sanyasi-sangathan nahi the. Sirf chhote-chhote giroh the.

Ye theek wahi cheez thi, jo Bauddh sangh pehle se kar raha tha. Usi purane naqshe par. Usi dhang se.

Ek dharm doosre dharm ki imaarat apne naam par dobara khada kar leta hai.

Buddh dharm ka baaki kya bacha? Poorab Bengal mein kuch bigad chuke roop. Sindh mein, uttar-pashchim mein, kuch aur. Iske alawa wo dheere-dheere is desh se gayab ho gaya.

Ek badi, phaile hue dharm ki itni chup-si vidaai.

Par is vidai ke peeche ek sawal chhup gaya tha, aur wahi sawal is episode ka asli saar hai. Kyun nahi lada is desh ne? Kyun kisi ne ise bahar karne ki koshish hi nahi ki?

Us jawab ke liye pehle humein woh samajhna hoga, jise is desh mein sochne ka tareeka kaha jaata tha.

## Part 4: Darshan sabke paas hota hai

To ab poochhiye: darshan kya cheez hai?

Ye bade sawalon ka guchha hai. Duniya kya hai. Main kaun hoon. Sahi kya hai, galat kya hai. Jeena kaise chahiye.

Ek soch doosri soch ko kheenchti hai. Par soch kisi seedhi line par nahi chalti. Purani soch aur nayi soch saath-saath chalti hain — ek doosre se ladti hui, ek doosre se na milti hui.

Ek aadmi ka dimaag hi contradictions ka guchha hota hai. Uski ek baat doosri se nahi milti. Aur ek poora desh? Usme atit ke kai daur ek saath khade hote hain.

Nehru kehte hain, ye sirf peeche reh gaye deshon ki baat nahi hai. Jahan aadmi nayi se nayi cheez istemaal karta hai, wahan bhi aise vishwas zinda rehte hain, jinhe tarak nahi maanta. Ek scientist apne laboratory se baahar nikalte hi science ka tareeka bhool jaata hai.

Aur ye baat un samasyayon par bhi sach hai, jo humari roz ki zindagi ko chhooti hain.

Par phir bhi. Har aadmi ke paas zindagi ka koi na koi darshan hota hai. Kabhi sochkar banaya hua, aur kabhi maa-baap ya muhalle se liya hua.

Aur bahut baar hum ek doosra raasta lete hain. Sochne ke jhamele se bachne ke liye hum kisi vishwas mein baith jaate hain. Koi dharm. Koi kattar vichaar. Koi desh ki kismat wali baat. Ya koi dhundhla sa, achha lagne wala insaaniyat ka bhaav.

Aksar ye sab ek saath chalte hain — bina kisi jodne wali cheez ke. Aur phir hum ek nahi, kai aadmi ban jaate hain. Har ek apne alag kamre mein. Kabhi ek baahar aata hai, kabhi doosra.

Nehru ise split personality kehte hain.

Aur purane zamane ke baare mein wo ek baat likhte hain, jo thodi tasalli wali hai. Shayad tab insaan ke andar zyada jut tha, zyada harmony — haan, aaj se neeche star par. Par ek gaon, tay kiye hue riwaaz, aur bahar ki aandhi se bacha hua zindagi ka ek chhota kamra — us chhote ghere mein jutaav banana aasaan tha.

Aaj wo ghera toot gaya hai. Aaj ek aadmi ka ghera hi itna bada ho gaya hai ki ek jagah uthi tez hawa doosri jagah toofan ban jaati hai, aur teesri jagah uski ulti hawa.

Isliye aaj ek aadmi ka akela jutaav tik nahi sakta. Uske liye poori duniya ka jutaav chahiye.

Aur Bharat mein? Nehru likhte hain, yahan duniya mein kahin bhi se zyada, purane samaaj ka dhaanche wala vichaar, aur uske peeche ki soch, aaj tak kisi hadd tak bani hui hai.

## Part 5: Ek hi mann, kai soch

Ab asli sawal. Ye purani soch itni der tak tiki kyun?

Nehru pehle ek tarazoo rakhte hain. Koi cheez itne saal tikti hai, to usme koi gun zaroor hota hai. Wo gun, jo samaaj ko jode rakhe, aur use zindagi ki badalti haalat ke saath dhalne de.

Aur wo soch aakhir mein bojh kyun ban gayi, gir kar raasta rokne wali cheez kyun ban gayi? Nehru ka jawab seedha hai: kyunki usi ke andar jo bura tha, wo us gun se bada ho gaya. Soch zindagi se alag ho gayi.

Ab is par ek angrez vidvaan ka saar aata hai, jo is poore khel ki chaabi hai.

Uska kehna tha: Bharat mein dharm koi sakht dogma nahi hai. Wo insaan ke chalne ka ek niyam hai — ek working hypothesis — jise har aadmi ki haalat aur uske star ke hisaab se badla ja sakta hai.

Dogma aap deewar par chipka kar rakh sakte hain, zindagi se alag. Par chalne ka niyam zindagi se alag nahi reh sakta. Ya wo kaam karega, ya raaste mein khada ho jaayega. Jis din wo zindagi ki lakeer se hat jaata hai, us din wo bekaar ho jaata hai.

Ab darshan ki taraf.

Nehru ek baat note karte hain. Darshan ki baatein zindagi ke badalte maal par nahi hoti. Wo uske peeche ki sthir sachai par hoti hain — agar aisi koi sachai hai. Isliye unme ek sthirta aati hai, jo bahar ke badlaav se nahi hilti.

Par doosri taraf: wo bhi usi mahaul mein paida hote hain. Jis zameen par ugte hain, uski mitti unme lagti hai.

Aur agar kisi darshan ki hawa phail jaaye, to wo poori kaum ki zindagi wali soch badal deta hai.

Bharat mein yahi hua.

Nehru likhte hain, yahan darshan oopar ke thode se chune hue logon tak hi rehta tha. Par kahin aur se zyada, yahan wo nicha tak pahuncha. Usne is desh ka dekhne ka tareeka ghaala, aur ek khaas soch ka swabhaav banaya.

Isme Bauddh darshan ka bada hissa tha. Madhyakaal mein Islam ka asar bhi pada — seedha bhi, aur un naye panthon ke raaste bhi, jo Hindu aur Islami dhang ke beech pul banana chahte the.

Par sabse bada asar kis cheez ka tha?

Nehru ka jawab saaf hai: chhe darshanon ka. Inhe yahan darshana kaha jaata hai.

## Part 6: Chhe darwaze

Aur ab hum us ghar ke saamne khade hain.

Nehru likhte hain ki in chhe darshanon ki shuruaat bahut purani hai — Buddha se bhi pehle ki. Wo dheere-dheere bane. Brahman wale darshan ek taraf, Bauddh wale doosri taraf. Kabhi ek doosre ki ninda karte, kabhi ek doosre se udhaar lete.

Aur Isai sadi shuru hone se pehle, bahut se aise darshanon mein se chhe ghaale ja chuke the. Har ek apna raasta, apni dalil. Phir bhi wo kate hue nahi the — wo ek bade plan ke hisse the.

Chhe naam. Aur hum inhe ek-ek darwaze ki tarah kholenge.

Pehle teen. Nyaya. Vaisheshika. Samkhya.

Phir teen. Yoga. Mimamsa. Vedanta.

Aaj paanch khulenge. Chhetta — sabse bada — agle episode ke liye khada rahega.

To ye chhe darwaze ek doosre se kitne alag hain? Bahut. Koi anek devtaon ko maanta hai. Koi ek Ishwar ko. Koi sirf ek hi tattva ko. Aur ek aisa hai, jo Ishwar ko bilkul nahi maanta, aur sirf ek evolution par khada hai. Kuch idealist hain, kuch realist.

Phir bhi Nehru kehte hain, inke beech bahut si baatein ek jaisi hain. Ek vidvaan ne inhe ek saanjhi rashtriya soch ki poonji kaha tha — ek khaata, jisse har sochne wala apne kaam ka maal utha leta tha.

Sabse pehli saanjhi baat: ye sab maante hain ki brahmaand ek niyam se chalta hai. Ki usme ek bada lay hai, ek tarteeb.

Nehru kehte hain, aisa maanna zaroori hi tha — warna brahmaand ke baare mein koi baat hi nahi ban sakti.

Doosri baat: kaaran aur asar chalta hai — par aadmi ke paas apni kismat kuch khud banane ki azaadi hai.

Teesri: janm baar-baar hota hai. Aur niswarth prem aur bina matlab ke kiye kaam ki badi jagah hai.

Chauthi: tarak par sab bharosa karte hain, aur tarak ka istemaal karke bahas bhi karte hain. Par ye bhi maana jaata hai ki anubhav aur andar ki samajh — intuition — aksar tarak se bhi oopar hoti hai.

Aur ek aakhri baat, jo sabse ajeeb hai. Ye saare darshan shastra ko maante hain. Orthodox hain. Phir bhi apne sawal wo insaani tareekon se suljhaate hain. Nehru ek vidvaan ka saar likhte hain: shastra ka kaam sirf nateejon ko pavitra banana reh jaata hai — kyunki nateeje tarak se nikalte hain, shastra se nahi.

Pehla darwaza ab khulega. Aur pehla darwaza sabse sakht hai.

Kyunki wahan sirf tarak hai.

## Part 7: Pehla darwaza — Nyaya

Nyaya ka matlab hi hai tarak. Sahi sochne ka vidhi. Sahi reasoning ka vigyaan.

Nehru kehte hain, ye tareeka analytic hai. Wo cheezon ko todta hai, chhaanta hai, kadam-kadam aage badhta hai.

Aur Nehru yahan ek tulna karte hain. Nyaya ka logic bahut hadd tak Yunani darshanik Aristotle ke tarak jaisa hai. Bahut milta hai. Par kuch gehra fark bhi hai.

Ab dhyan dijiye, ye darwaza kaisa tha.

Nyaya ke jo usool the, unhe baaki saare darshanon ne maan liya. Koi usse bahas nahi karta. Iska matlab ye hai ki poore desh ki sochne ki tarkeeb mein is ek darshan ka dhaancha baith gaya.

Aur ye sirf kitaabon mein nahi rehta tha. Purane aur madhyakaal, dono dauron mein Nyaya Bharat ke pathshalaon mein padhaya jaata tha. Aaj bhi padhaya jaata hai. Nayi padhai ne usse hata diya — par jahan Sanskrit purane tareeke se padhai jaati hai, wahan Nyaya aaj bhi syllabus ka hissa hai.

Uska darja kya tha? Ye darshan padhne ki tayyari thi. Aur har padhe-likhe insaan ki zaroori dimaagi kasrat.

Nehru kehte hain: purani Bharatiya padhai mein Nyaya ki jagah kam se kam utni badi thi, jitni European padhai mein Aristotle ke tarak ki.

Ab iska tareeka aaj ke vigyaan jaisa nahi tha. Aaj ka vigyaan baahar jaa kar cheezon ko naapta hai, chhaanta hai. Nyaya ka bhi tareeka chhaanne wala tha, par wahi andhvishwas par nahi chalta tha. Wo gyaan ki har cheez ko parakhta tha, aur kadam-kadam tarak se saabit karta tha.

To kya usme vishwas bilkul nahi tha?

Tha. Aur wahi is darwaze ki neev hai — ya uski hadd.

Nyaya pehle kuch baatein maan leta hai. Aisi baatein, jinhe tarak se saabit nahi kiya ja sakta. Aur unhe maan lene ke baad, poora mahal unhi neevon par khada hota hai.

Kaun si baatein? Ki zindagi aur prakriti mein ek lay aur ek jut hai. Ki ek Ishwar hai. Ki har aatma alag hai. Ki ye brahmaand anuon se bana hai.

Aur insaan? Wo na sirf aatma hai, na sirf shareer. Dono ke jude hone se banta hai. Aur sach kya hai? Aatmaon aur prakriti ka mila-jula jaal.

Pehla darwaza khul gaya. Isme tarak tha — aur ek chhupi hui neev bhi.

Agla darwaza isi neev se shuru hota hai. Aur wahin ek aisa darshan khada hai, jo sab kuch alag-alag dekhta hai.

## Part 8: Doosra darwaza — alag-alag, aur anu

Doosra darwaza. Vaisheshika.

Vaisheshika bahut hadd tak Nyaya jaisa hi hai. Par usme ek zor alag hai.

Wo kehta hai: har cheez alag hai. Har aatma apni jagah, har vastu apni jagah. Sab kuch mila hua nahi hai — sab alag-alag khada hai.

Aur isi alag-alag se wo brahmaand ka anu-siddhant khada karta hai. Sab kuch chhote-chhote anuon se bana hai.

Aur dekhiye, yahan bhi Nyaya wali neev hi kaam kar rahi hai. Bas zor alag hai: wahan lay aur jut par, yahan alag-alag honay par.

Ab dekhiye, is darshan mein ek cheez sabse oopar rakhi jaati hai. Dharm. Yaani naitik niyam.

Nehru kehte hain: poora brahmaand isi dharm se chalta hai, aur poora darshan isi ke aas-paas ghoomta hai.

Sochiye is soch ka aakaar. Yahan har aatma apni alag, har vastu apni alag. Aur unhe jodne wali cheez koi Ishwar nahi — dharm hai. Ek niyam. Par wo niyam kisi raja ka nahi. Wo brahmaand ka niyam hai.

Aur Ishwar? Yahan Ishwar ko saaf-saaf maana nahi gaya. Uska darwaza khula hi chhod diya gaya.

Nehru ek baat jodte hain. Is darshan ka, aur Nyaya ka, shuruaati Bauddh darshan se gahara rishta hai. Aur dono ka tareeka, poore maayne mein, realistic hai — yaani wo dikhne wali duniya ko asli maante hain, sirf mann ka aks nahi.

Do darwaze khul gaye. Dono ne tarak ko apni neev banaya. Dono ne duniya ko bahar se dekha.

Teesra darwaza isse hatkar hoga. Wahan ek purana naam khada hai. Aur wo aadmi tarak ko apne kinaare tak le jaayega — itna aage, ki khud uska apna vishwas wahan bach nahi paayega.

## Part 9: Teesra darwaza — Samkhya

Teesra darshan. Samkhya.

Nehru likhte hain ki ise Kapila naam ke ek purane darshanik ne ghaala. Kapila ka samay saatvi sadi Isai-poorv ke aas-paas maana jaata hai. Unhone bahut si purani dhaaraon ko samet kar ise ek aakaar diya.

Ek vidvaan ne is darshan par ek badi baat likhi. Uska kehna tha: duniya ke itihaas mein pehli baar, insaan ke dimaag ki poori azaadi aur apne bal par poora bharosa — ye yahan dikhta hai.

Samkhya ki shakal Buddh dharm ke uthte waqt pakki hui.

Iska tareeka kya tha? Ye sirf dimaag se nikla ek siddhant tha, baahar ki cheezon ko naap kar nahi bana tha. Aur jo cheezein iski pahunch se bahar thin, unhe naapna mumkin hi nahi tha.

Par isme ek baat kamaal ki hai. Jaise Buddh dharm, wahi Samkhya bhi sirf tarak ki line par chala. Usne Buddh dharm ko uske hi maidanon mein lalkara — bina kisi adhikari ke sahare.

Aur isi wajah se Ishwar ko hataana pada.

Samkhya mein na ek Ishwar hai, na koi niraakaar Ishwar. Na ek-Ishwar-wad, na ek-tattva-wad. Uska raasta atheist tha, aur isne us dharm ka neev hila diya jo oopar wale kisi bal par khada tha.

Yahan duniya ko kisi Ishwar ne banaya nahi. Yahan ek lagatar evolution chalti hai. Wo chetna aur prakriti ke takraav se banti hai. Par wo prakriti bhi, apne aap mein, ek urja hai. Aur ye evolution kabhi rukti nahi. Ye chalti rehti hai.

Isi wajah se Samkhya ko dvaita kaha jaata hai. Dvaita, yaani do. Kyunki wo do mool kaaranon par khada hai.

Ek: Prakriti. Wo prakriti jo kabhi na ruke, jo badalti rahe. Doosra: purusha — wo chetna jo badalti nahi. Purush anant hain. Aur purusha khud kuch karta nahi. Bas uska asar prakriti par padta hai, aur prakriti banti chali jaati hai.

Yahan ek baat jodni zaroori hai. Samkhya maanta hai ki kaaran aur asar ek hi cheez ke do roop hain. Asar kaaran ke andar chhupa hua pehle se maujood hai — jaise beej mein ped. Kaam-kaaj ke liye hum unhe alag maante hain, par buniyad mein wo ek hi hain. Aur isi se brahmaand ke sabse neeche se sabse oopar tak, ek lagatar jut khada hota hai.

Ab tak humne teen darwaze khole. Aur teenon dimaag ka khel the.

Chautha darwaza isse hatkar hai. Wahan dimaag nahi, shareer khada hai.

## Part 10: Chautha darwaza — Patanjali ka Yoga, aur ek qaidi ki asana

Chautha darshan. Yoga.

Yoga ka naam aaj ghar-ghar mein hai. Par Nehru kehte hain, ise bahut kam log samajhte hain.

Yog ka purana vidhi kaafi tha; use Patanjali naam ke ek vidvaan ne shakal di. Patanjali sirf Yoga ke darshanik nahi the — unhone Panini ke Sanskrit vyakaran par ek badi teeka bhi likhi, Mahabhashya.

Aur is darshan mein ek baat sab se alag hai. Yoga koi socha hua siddhant nahi hai — ye khud jaanchne ka tareeka hai, ek experiment. Isliye koi bhi ise apna sakta hai, aur Ishwar iska zaroori hissa nahi hai.

Nehru kehte hain, ise Europe aur America mein bahut kam samjha gaya hai. Wahan log ise ajeeb riwaazon se jodte hain.

Yoga ka usool ye hai: dimaag ki theek tayyari se kuch oonchi haalaton tak pahuncha ja sakta hai.

Nehru imaandari se ek sawal chhodte hain. Yoga ke aakhri padav ek aisi samajh tak le jaate hain, jahan shabd nahi pahunchte — mujhe nahi pata, wo oopar ki haalat hai ya khud ko hi dhoka dena. Par ek baat saaf hai: bina rehnumai ki gayi Yoga se kabhi-kabhi dimaag par bura asar pada hai.

Par usse pehle ek kaam hai. Shareer ka kaam.

Shareer mazboot ho. Halka ho. Sundar ho. Sakht ho. Aur lachak wala ho.

Iske liye shareer ke kuch khaas aakaar hain, aur saans lene ke tareeke — saans ko lamba, gehri, aur thoda kaabu mein.

Par Nehru ek shabd pakadte hain. "Kasrat" galat shabd hai, kyunki isme koi zor hi nahi. Koi daud, koi kood, koi jhatka nahi. Ye asana hai.

Aur theek se kiye jaayein, to ye shareer ko aaram dete hain, thakate nahi.

Nehru tulna karte hain: baaki tareeke — daud, jhatke, uchhal-kood — aadmi ko haanfta hua chhod dete hain. Par asana wala purana tareeka is desh ka apna lagta hai: usme ek thehraav hai, shareer chal raha hai par mann shant.

[SFX: ek kothri mein gehri, lambi saans; phir chup]

Aur is darwaze ke peechhe wo aadmi khada hai — Ahmadnagar ki kothri mein.

Kai saalon se unhone kuch chune hue, aasan asana roz kiye — jab bhi mauka mila. Aur unhe koi shak nahi: unhe inka bada faayda hua. Kyunki unki zindagi aise mahaul mein guzri, jo shareer aur mann, dono ke liye achha nahi tha.

Unka poora riyaaz — thode asana aur kuch saans ki kasratein — bas itna hi tha. Wo shareer ke shuruaati padav tak hi pahunche. Aur mann? Wo aaj bhi ek aisa sadasya hai, jo sunta nahi. Bahut baar hadd se bahaar chala jaata hai.

Yoga sirf shareer ka kaam nahi hai. Uske saath khana-peena sahi rakhna, aur ek naitik tayyari — ahimsa, satya, sanyam. Aur ahimsa ka matlab sirf haath rok lena nahi: dil mein kisi ke liye bura bhaav na rakhna.

Ye sab indriyon par kaabu ke liye hai — phir dhyan, ekagrata, aur wo samajh jo tarak se nahi aati.

Yahin Nehru ek aadmi ko saamne laate hain. Vivekananda — aadhunik Bharat ke sabse bade Yoga aur Vedanta ke prachaarak.

Vivekananda baar-baar ek hi baat par zor dete the: Yoga andhvishwas nahi hai, wo ek experiment hai jo tarak par khada hai. Koi bhi Yoga aapse tarak chhodne ko nahi kehta — sab kehte hain, apne tarak ko pakde raho.

Aur wo tarak kitni door jaata tha? Nehru unka ek vakya likhte hain. Tarak se atheist ban jaana behtar hai, kisi ke kehne par do sau crore devtaon ko maan lene se.

Aakhir mein Nehru ek saavdhani likhte hain. Yoga ke ilaake aam aadmi ki pahunch se bahar hain. Aur ye sirf Bharat mein nahi hui — Isai sadhuon aur Parsi Sufiyon ke anubhav bhi ek doosre se hairaan karne wale milte hain.

Aaj sochiye. Aaj yog ki koi kami nahi — studio, video, app. Par usme se kitna asana hai, aur kitna wo cheez jo Vivekananda maangte the — tarak ko pakde rakhna?

Paanchwa darwaza baaki hai. Aur uske baad, Paris ke us kamre ka jawab.

## Part 11: Paanchwa darwaza — Mimamsa, aur Paris ka jawab

Paanchwa darwaza. Mimamsa.

Ye reet ka darshan hai. Vidhi. Puja. Yagya. Aur iska jhukav anek devtaon wali soch ki taraf hai.

Nehru likhte hain: aaj ka aam lok Hindu dharm, aur Hindu Law — dono par is darshan ka bada asar hai. Kyunki isi ne jeene ka dharm khada kiya. Sahi jeene ka poora nizam. Kaun sa kaam kis din ho, kaunsa vrat, kaunsa daan.

Aur yahan Nehru ek ajeeb baat par rukte hain.

Hindu anek-devta-wad ek ajeeb kism ka anek-devta-wad hai.

Devta, yaani "chamakte hue". Unke apne khaas bal hain. Phir bhi wo insaan se neechi paidaish maane jaate hain.

Aur ye soch Hindu aur Bauddh, dono mein hai. Dono maante hain: insaan ka janm hi sabse oonchi seedhi hai, jahan tak koi cheez pahunchi hai. Khud ko jaanne ka raasta yahin se jaata hai. Devta bhi aage nahi badh sakte — unhe bhi insaan ka janm lena padta hai.

Bauddh to ye bhi kehte hain: sirf insaan hi Buddh ki us sabse oonchi haalat tak pahunch sakta hai.

Sochiye, kaisa anek-devta-wad hai ye. Jisme devta bhi insaan banne ki raah dekhte hain.

Paanchwa darwaza khul gaya. Panch darwaze khul gaye.

Aur ab hum wapas us kamre mein ja sakte hain, jahan barsaat ho rahi thi.

[SFX: wahi Paris ki barsaat, bahut halki, door se laut kar aati hai; phir chup]

Ab Malraux ke sawal ka jawab.

Unhone poochha tha: kis taaqat ne ek bade, phaile hue dharm ko bina yuddh ke andar ghula diya?

Jawab ye hai, aur ye jawab thoda ulta hai.

Buddh dharm ko kisi ne bahar nahi kiya. Kyunki wo bahar wala tha hi nahi. Wo usi ghar ka tha.

Uska darshan isi ghar ki soch ki line par khada tha — Upanishadon ki line par. Aur unhi Upanishadon ne pujaari ke dhandhe ka mazaak udaya tha. Riwaaz ka mazaak udaya tha. Aur jaati ki ahmiyat chhoti kar di thi.

Isliye jab dono ghar aapas mein lade, to ladaai ne unhe door nahi kiya. Ladaai ne unhe paas-paas kar diya — darshan mein bhi, aur aam logon ke vishwas mein bhi.

Mahayan ne to samjhauta hi kar liya. Jitna samjhauta ho sake, karo — bas ek cheez na chhoote: naitikta ki jad.

Aur phir wo do lakeerein ek doosre ko kaat gayin, jinhe kaatna na-mumkin lagta tha.

Brahman dharm ne Buddha ko ek avatar bana diya. Bhagwan. Aur Buddh dharm ne bhi yahi kiya.

Dhyan dijiye ye kya hai. Do badi soch, ek doosre ko nakaar rahi thi. Aur ab wo ek doosre ka bhagwan ek hi bana rahi hain.

Aur sangh? Bhikshuon ka wo bada sangathan, jise hum Part 3 mein chhod aaye? Wo bhi gaya nahi. Kai sadiyon baad usi purane naqshe par math khade hue.

Yuddh isliye nahi hua, kyunki seema thi hi nahi.

Dono taraf ek hi soch thi. Ek hi zameen. Ek hi mitti. Ek cheez apne hi ghar mein ghul gayi, aur ghar waise hi khada raha.

Is desh ki soch ek virodh ko maar kar doosre ko nahi jeetti. Wo donon ko ek hi ghar mein rakhti hai.

Aur Malraux ka doosra sawal? Wo kaun si taaqat thi — aur kya wo is desh mein aaj bhi hai? Nehru us sawal ka jawab apne paas nahi rakhte. Wo likhte hain: mujhe uska santosh-janak jawab nahi mila. Aur wo sawal humare saamne rakh dete hain.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli: Buddha ka avatar banna. Yahi is poore episode ka asli mechanism hai. Is desh mein nayi soch ko bahar nahi kiya jaata. Uske liye andar ek jagah bana di jaati hai. Buddha ko bhagwan banana ek bahut badi khaatir thi — par uski ek chhoti si keemat bhi thi. Jise bhagwan bana diya jaaye, uske sawal dheere-dheere uske gun ban jaate hain. Uske shabd wahi rehte hain, par unki dhaar mand pad jaati hai.

Doosri: Malraux ka doosra sawal. Kya is desh mein wo taaqat aaj bhi hai? Nehru ne uska jawab nahi diya. Wo sawal humare liye chhod gaya. Aur ye sawal itihaas ka nahi, aaj ka hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Jab hum kisi naye vichaar ko apna banate hain, to hum uski baatein sunte hain — ya uske sawal chup-chaap daba dete hain? Aur ye do cheezein, kya ek hi hain?

## Outro

Paanch darwaze khul gaye.

Chhetta bacha hai. Sabse bada. Sabse chhupa hua. Wo darwaza, jisne baaki sabko apne andar nigal liya.

Aur us darwaze ke peeche ek aadmi khada hai. Wo is desh ke chaar kone tak paidal jaayega — sirf isliye ki ye ek desh lage.

Agli baar wo darwaza khulega.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
