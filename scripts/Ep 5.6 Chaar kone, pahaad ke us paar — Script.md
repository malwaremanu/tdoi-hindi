## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo is desh ko samajhne ke liye apni hi kitaab likh raha hai. Pichhli baar paanch darwaze khule the — tarak ka, anuon ka, do kaaranon ka, shareer ka, aur reet ka. Aur jawab mila tha: yahan Buddh dharm ko hataane ke liye jang isliye nahi hui, kyunki yahan seema thi hi nahi. Chhetta darwaza bacha tha. Sabse bada. Aaj wahi khulega.

## Part 1: Chhetta darwaza — Vedanta

Chhetta darwaza. Vedanta.

Ye Upanishadon se nikla — Upanishad, wo purani kitaabein, jo is desh ki sabse purani soch ka nichod hain. Saikdon saal mein is darshan ne kai shaklein li. Par ek baat kabhi nahi badli: ye hamesha ek-tattva par khada raha. Ek hi tattva, aur bas.

Samkhya ke purusha aur prakriti, jo pichhli baar do alag kaaran lage the, yahan alag cheezein nahi rehte. Wo ek hi sachai ke do roop ban jaate hain — the absolute.

Aur is darshan ka dil? Sirf ek. Ek hi aakhri sachai hai — Atman. Atman, wo poora, bada, absolute soul. Atman subject hai. Baaki sab kuch object.

Ab ek sawal. Wo ek, jo sab mein hai, anek kaise dikhne lagta hai? Nehru kehte hain, iska jawab tarak nahi de sakta — humara dimaag iss finite duniya se bana hai, aur wo infinite ko soch hi nahi sakta, bina usse chhota kiye.

Yahan Upanishad ki ek chhoti line hai. Poora wo hai, poora ye hai. Poore se poora nikalta hai. Poore mein se poora hataaiye — to bhi poora bacha rehta hai.

Nehru kehte hain, Shankara ne gyaan ka ek baareek nizam banaya. Maanyataon se shuru kiya, kadam-kadam par tarak se aage badhe, aur poora Advaita khada kiya. Advaita, yaani do nahi — ek.

Us nizam ki ek tasveer kamaal ki hai. Sochiye ek ghada. Ghade ke andar ki jagah — wahi humari alag aatma. Baahar ki poori jagah — wahi Atman. Dono alag lagte hain, kaam-kaaj ke liye alag maane bhi jaate hain. Par ye alag hona sirf dikhta hai, sach mein nahi. Aur azaadi kya hai? Ye samajhna, ki wo dono ek hain.

To phir ye duniya? Nehru kehte hain, ye us sachai ki parchhaayi hai. Ek saaya, jo is duniya par pada hai. Iska naam Maya hai.

Aur yahan ek galat-fahmi hai, jo aaj tak chalti aayi hai. Log Maya ko "illusion" kehte hain — yaani aisa jhooth, jo hai hi nahi. Nehru kehte hain, ye tarjuma galat hai. Maya ka matlab gayab hona nahi. Maya "hai" aur "nahi hai" ke beech baithi hai. Ek aadhi sachai. Nehru ek shabd sujhaate hain: relativity.

To phir achha aur bura? Nehru yahan saaf ho jaate hain. Aakhri sachai kuch bhi ho, is duniya mein achhe aur bure ka fark kaam karta hai — wahin, jahan hum alag-alag insaan ban kar jeete hain. Dharam ka roop relative ban jaata hai.

Chhe darwaze khul gaye. Ab is aakhri darwaze ke peeche khade aadmi ko dekhiye.

## Part 2: Malabar ka pandit

To wo aadmi kaun tha?

Uska naam Shankara. Shankaracharya.

Ek pandit. Ek darshanik. Dakshin ke ek chhote se ilaake se. Brahmin parivar se.

Us zamane mein aisa aadmi gaon mein baith kar shastra padh sakta tha. Poore desh ka safar uski majboori nahi thi. Phir bhi wo chala.

Aur uske darshan mein ek baat hai, jo aaj bhi chaubhaati hai. Nehru batate hain, Shankara ne samaaj ke caste wale dhaanche ko maan liya tha — usse kaum ka jama hua anubhav kaha. Par unka ek saaf vichaar tha: kisi bhi caste ka koi bhi aadmi sabse oonchi vidya tak pahunch sakta hai.

Ek taraf dhaancha maan lena. Doosri taraf darwaza khol dena. Dono saath.

Aur unke darshan par ek chhaap hai. Duniya se mooh modna. Uske kaam-kaaj se hat jaana. Baar-baar ek hi baat: apna tyaag.

Ab yahan ek ajeeb baat hai. Jis aadmi ne duniya se mooh modne ka darshan khada kiya, wo khud kisi ghaar mein bhaag kar nahi baitha.

Nehru likhte hain: wo ek hi aadmi mein kai log the. Darshanik bhi, vidvaan bhi. Agyani bhi, aur mystic bhi. Kavi, sant, sudhaarak — aur ek kabil manager.

Wo Malabar ka tha — dakshin ke us chhor ka, jahan nariyal ke ped hain. Wahan se uthta hai, aur zindagi bhar poore desh mein chalta rehta hai. Logon se milta hai. Behes karta hai. Unhe apni aag ka ek hissa de deta hai.

Nehru kehte hain, wo apne mission ke baare mein poori tarah hosh mein tha. Wo is poore desh ko — Kanyakumari se Himalaya tak — apne kaam ka maidan maanta tha. Ek aisi cheez, jo sanskriti se bandhi hui hai, jisme ek hi jaan hai, chahe bahar ke roop kai hon.

Us waqt is desh ke mann mein alag-alag ulajhi dhaaraayein chal rahi thin. Shankara ne unhe jodne ki koshish ki. Alag-alag se ek nazariya banane ki koshish ki.

Aur ye sab usne battis saal mein kiya. Battis saal.

Nehru kehte hain, itni chhoti zindagi mein unhone kai lambi zindagiyon ka kaam kiya.

Ab wo chaar ghar, jinke liye wo itna chala.

## Part 3: Chaar kone, chaar math

Nehru likhte hain, Shankara ne is desh ke chaar kone par chaar math banaye. Math, yaani monastery. Wahan sanyasi rehte the, aur vidya padhi jaati thi.

Ek dakshin mein. Sringeri, Mysore ke paas.

Ek poorab mein. Puri, samundar ke kinaare.

Ek pashchim mein. Dvaraka, Kathiawad mein.

Aur ek uttar mein. Badrinath, Himalaya ke beech.

Zara naqsha kholiye, aur chaaron jodiye. Dakshin. Poorab. Pashchim. Uttar. Ek aadmi ne poori zameen ka naqsha apne gharon se bhar diya.

Aur ye ghar aise hi nahi chune gaye. Ye chaar jagah pehle se hi teerth the — poore desh se log wahan jaate the, aaj bhi jaate hain. Shankara ne wahan ghar banaya, to wahan aur bheed aane lagi.

[SFX: door se ek mandir ki ghanti, ek baar; phir hawa]

Ab ye safar khud kuch keh raha hai. Nehru kehte hain, us zamane mein raaste kachche the, sawari dheemi — ek jagah se doosri jagah pahunchna mahino ka kaam tha.

Phir bhi wo chala. Baar-baar chala. Har jagah usse aise log milte the jo usi tarah sochte the, aur wo unse Sanskrit mein baat karta tha — Sanskrit, poore Bharat ke padhe-likhe logon ki sajhi bhasha.

Yahi baat is desh ka jut dikha deti hai.

Log safeer-chaar hote rahe, phir bhi aate-jaate rahe. Nayi kitaabein safar karti rahi, aur har nayi soch par poore desh mein garam behes hoti thi.

Aur ye sirf padhe-likhe logon ki baat nahi thi. Aam log bhi teerthon ki taraf chalte rahe — teerth itne purane, ki unka zikr maha-kathaon tak jaata hai.

Nateeja? Ye chalta-phirta rehna ek hi zameen aur ek hi sanskriti ka bhaav badhata raha.

Teerthon mein har caste, har varg ke log milte the. Aur teerth ka dharmik matlab jo bhi raha ho, log usse ghoomne aur mauj ke din ki tarah bhi lete the.

Ek teerth par jaaiye, to wahan poora Bharat ek jagah dikhta hai — alag kapde, alag rasm, alag bhasha — aur phir bhi sab jaante hain, ki hum ek hain. Uttar aur dakshin ki bhasha ka fark bhi samvaad ke aade nahi aaya.

Nehru ka andaaza hai, Shankara is jut ke bhaav mein kuch jodna chahte the. Unhone do jagah kaam kiya — oopar ki duniya mein, jahan unhone poore desh ki soch ko ek karne ki koshish ki; aur aam logon ki duniya mein, jahan unhone kai dogme tod diye, aur apne darshan ka darwaza har us aadmi ke liye khol diya.

Aur purane log apne teerth chunne mein kitne achhe the. Wo lagbhag hamesha khoobsurat jagahon par hain. Amarnath ki wo barfeele gufa, Kashmir ke pahaadon mein. Rameshwaram ka mandir, dakshin ke sabse chhor par. Hardwar, jahan Ganga pahaadon se nikal kar maidan mein utrti hai. Aur dakshin mein itni jagah.

Ye teerth sirf pooja ki jagah nahi the. Dakshin ke purane mandiron mein kamaal ki kala hai — to teerthon se purane art ki ek jhalak bhi milti hai.

Phir wo Kedarnath gaya. Himalaya ki un unchaiyon mein, jahan saal bhar barf rehti hai. Aur battis saal ki umar mein wahan mara.

[SFX: Kedarnath wali tez hawa, bahut halki, laut kar aati hai]

Aur ek baat. Ye bhi kaha jaata hai, ki Shankara ne Buddhism ko yahan badi dharm ki tarah khatm karne mein haath diya. Par Buddhism us se pehle hi sikud chuka tha. Aur kuch log Shankara ko ek chhupa hua Buddhist kehte the. Nehru kehte hain, isme sach bhi hai.

Ab tak hum poore andar rahe. Chaar kone, chaar ghar, aur ek aadmi jo unhe jodne chala. Par sawal wahi hai jo humne shuru mein poochha tha.

## Part 4: Pahaad ke us paar

Is desh aur China ke beech bahut purane raaste the — zameen ke bhi, samundar ke bhi. Nehru kehte hain, Ashoka se pehle ka kuch pakka pata nahi hai. Par silk to China se aata tha. Aur Nepal, Assam aur Bengal ke kinaron par aise chehre milte hain, jo batate hain ki log bahut purane waqt mein bhi aate-jaate the.

Pakka saboot ek aadmi ka hai. Ashoka. Uske bheje hue logon ne raasta khola.

Uske baad ek hazaar saal ka wahi silsila chalta raha — bhikshu, vidvaan, yaatri. Pahaad ke upar se, Gobi ke registaan se hokar, Central Asia ke maidanon aur pahaadon se hokar.

Nehru likhte hain, wo safar lamba tha, mushkil tha, khatron se bhara tha. Bahut log raaste mein hi mar gaye. Ek hisaab hai, ki sau mein se nabbe yaatri laut hi nahi paate the. Jo pahunch bhi jaate, unmein se bahut wahan hi bas jaate.

Ek doosra raasta bhi tha. Samundar wala — Indo-China, Java, Sumatra, Malaya, Nicobar ke hokar. Wo chhota tha, par bahut kam khatarnak nahi. Kabhi koi zameen se jaata, aur samundar se lautta.

Aur dekhiye, ye raasta sirf khatra nahi tha. Ye ek chhalti hui sadak bhi thi. Bauddh sanskriti poore Central Asia tak, aur Indonesia ke hisson tak phail gayi thi. Us raaste par vihara the, pathshaale thin, saraayen thin. Yaani ek yaatri ko poore raaste par chhat milti thi, khana milta tha, aur koi aisa milta tha, jo uski bhasha jaanta ho.

Nateeja? Ek aadmi ne raasta khola, aur sau saal mein wo raasta ek pul ban gaya. Pahaad ke us paar ek seedha pul.

## Part 5: Pehla vidvaan

China jaane wala pehla Bharatiya vidvaan, jiske baare mein humein pata hai, wo tha Kashyapa Matanga. Kashyapa Matanga, ek Bauddh vidvaan, jo sabse pehle China pahuncha. Saal tha 67 isvi.

Wo jita kar nahi aaya tha. Wo bulaya gaya tha — China ke us waqt ke samraat, Ming Ti, ke kehne par. Aur usne talwar nahi uthai. Wo baith gaya Lo Yang mein, Lo nadi ke kinaare.

Uske saath ek aur vidvaan gaya tha. Aur uske baad ek lambi kataar shuru hoti hai. Ek ke baad ek vidvaan. Kumarajiva. Jinagupta. Aur Bodhidharma.

Har ek apne saath bhikshuon ka ek jhund le kar aata tha. Ye akeli yaatrein nahi thi. Ye chhote-chhote karvaan the.

Aur ab ek ankda. Chhathi sadi tak, sirf Lo Yang pradesh mein, teen hazaar se zyada Bharatiya Bauddh bhikshu the. Aur das hazaar Bharatiya parivaar.

Sochiye. Ye koi do-teen yaatri nahi thi. Ye pahaad ke us paar ek poori basti thi. Bharatiya ghar. Bharatiya rasoil. Bharatiya bhikshu.

Aur ye log khaali haath nahi aaye. Wo Sanskrit ki pothiyan laaye, aur wahan baith kar unhe Chini bhasha mein badla. Kuch ne to seedhe Chini mein kitaabein likhi. Kavita bhi. Unka hissa Chini sahitya mein aaj bhi zinda hai.

Ek naam goonjta hai. Kumarajiva. China jaane wale vidvaanon mein sabse bada anuvaadak. Usne saintalees kitaabein likhi, aur wo aaj tak bachi hain. Uske Chini likhne ki shaili ko bahut uncha maana jaata hai. Aur usne ek badi cheez ka anuvaad bhi kiya — is desh ke bade vidvaan Nagarjuna ki jeevani. Nagarjuna, jinke baare mein hum pichhle episode mein sun chuke hain.

Aur Jinagupta. Chhathi sadi ka wo vidvaan, jiska shiksharthi ek T'ang samraat khud ban gaya. Usne saintis Sanskrit kitaabon ka anuvaad kiya.

To pahaad ke us paar: pothiyan, Chini bhasha mein dhalti hui. Ye pehla raasta tha. Aur ye raasta agle hazaar saal chalta raha.

## Part 6: Guru ki aakhri salah

Par ye raasta ek-tarfa nahi tha. Isi raaste par log is desh mein bhi aaye. Unmein sabse mashhoor yaatri the Fa Hien, Hsuan Tsang aur I-tsing.

Fa Hien paanchvi sadi mein aaya. Wo China mein Kumarajiva ka shishya tha. Par Fa Hien ki yaatra se pehle ek chhoti si baat hui.

Wo apne guru se vida lene gaya. Aur Kumarajiva ne use ek aisi salah di, jo iss poori kahani ki chaabi hai. Usne kaha: sirf dharmik gyaan jama karne mein apna poora waqt na lagana. Is desh ke logon ka jeena, unki aadatein, unka dhang — inhe dhyan se dekhna. Taaki China is desh ko samajh sake, poora samajh sake.

Rukiye, is salah par.

Ek guru apne shishya ko doosre desh bhej raha hai. Aur wo usse kehta hai: kitaabein padhna kaafi nahi. Logon ki zindagi dekho.

Sochiye, ye salah kitni aasaan lagti hai. Aur kitni mushkil. Ek kitaab padhne ke liye do saal kaafi hain. Ek desh ke logon ko samajhne ke liye poora jeevan chahiye.

Nateeja? Ye pahaad wala raasta sirf pothiyon ka raasta nahi tha. Ye dekhne ka raasta bhi tha. Log kaise khaate hain. Kahan rehte hain. Kya maante hain. Aur ye baat aage aane wale har yaatri ke saath chali.

Fa Hien ne Pataliputra ki vishwavidyalaya mein padha — Pataliputra, aaj ka Patna. Usne yahan ke rasoil ka, yahan ke kapdon ka, yahan ke tyohaaron ka hisaab rakha. Wahi hisaab aage aane wale har yaatri ke kaam aaya.

Ab isi raaste par ek aur naam aata hai. Hsuan Tsang — wahi, jise humne cold open mein dekha.

Wo saatvi sadi mein aaya. Us zamane mein China mein T'ang raajvansh apne poore javan par tha, aur uttar Bharat mein Harshavardhana ka samrajya tha. Wo Gobi registaan se hokar aaya, Central Asia ke shehron se hokar — Turfan, Samarkand, Balkh — aur phir Himalaya laangh kar is desh mein utra.

Wo apne kaarname likhta hai. Raaste mein kaise khatre the. Central Asia ke Bauddh raja, aur unke vihara. Aur wahan ke Turk, jo pakke Bauddh the.

Yahan wo poore desh mein ghoomta hai. Sab jagah sammaan milta hai. Wo jagahon ka hisaab rakhta hai, logon ka. Kai khoobsurat kahanis likhta hai — aur kuch ajeeb si bhi, jo usne suni thi.

Par uski asli jagah Nalanda hai. Nalanda, Pataliputra se door nahi. Wo vishwavidyalaya, jahan door-door se vidyaarthi aate the. Nehru likhte hain, wahan das hazaar vidyaarthi aur bhikshu ek saath rehte the. Hsuan Tsang ne wahan padhai ki, degree li, aur aakhir mein us vishwavidyalaya ka up-aacharya ban gaya.

## Part 7: Paanch hisse, ek pyaas

Ab uski kitaab. Uska naam hai Si-Yu-Ki. Si-Yu-Ki, yaani "pashchim ke desh ka vrittant". Pashchim, yaani Bharat. Kyunki Bharat usse pashchim mein tha.

Uski kitaab aaj bhi padhi jaati hai. Aur ek khaas wajah se.

Wo ek bahut sabhya desh se aaya tha. China ki rajdhani Si-an-fu us waqt kala aur padhai ka bada kendra thi. Yaani wo kisi pichhde gaon ka yaatri nahi tha. Isliye jab wo yahan ki tareef karta hai, to sunne layak baat hai.

Wo likhta hai: yahan padhai jaldi shuru hoti thi, ek-ek padav par aage badhti thi, aur aakhir mein vidyaarthi vishwavidyalaya tak pahunchta tha. Wahan paanch cheezein padhai jaati thi. Vyakaran. Kaarigari. Chikitsa. Tarak. Aur darshan.

Par usse sabse zyada chubhi yahan ka padhne ka bhookh. Yahan ke saare bhikshu-purohit khud adhyaapak the. Isliye shuruaati padhai gaon-gaon tak pahunchti thi — jo padha hua tha, wo padha bhi sakta tha.

Aur sarkaar ke baare mein wo likhta hai: yahan ka raaj koshish karta hai, ki logon par bojh kam pade. Koi zabardasti ka kaam nahi. Kar halke hain. Aur vyapaari aate-jaate rehte hain, apna kaam karte hue.

Wo logon ke baare mein likhta hai, ki wo seedhe aur imaandar hain. Paison ke maamle mein unmein chalaki nahi, wada nibhate hain, aachran mein narmi hai. Aur apradhi ya bagaawat karne wale kam hote hain.

Sochiye. Ye aadmi apne desh ko neecha nahi dikhata. Wo sirf ye likhta hai, ki ye log padhne ke kitne bhookhe hain.

Wapas jaate waqt wo Central Asia ke raaste se hi lautta, aur saath thi pothiyon ka ek bada dher. Uske hisaab se us waqt Bauddh dharm bahut door tak phaila hua tha — Khorasan, Iraq, aur Syria ki sarhadon tak.

Par yahi wo waqt tha, jab wahan Bauddh dharm kamzor pad chuka tha, aur Arabia mein Islam shuru ho chuka tha.

Nateeja? Jo dharm yahan se pahaad paar gaya, wo wahin ruk nahi gaya — uske peeche ek naya dharm bhi chal pada.

Iran ke baare mein Hsuan Tsang likhta hai: ye log padhai ke peechhe nahi bhagte, apna poora mann kala mein lagate hain. Jo wo banate hain, padosi desh usse keemti maante hain.

Aur ek chhota rajya, Gobi ke kinaare. Turfan. Nehru likhte hain, yahan kai sabhyatayein aakar milee. Wahan ki bhasha Iran ke raaste se aayi thi, aur Europe ki Celtic bhashaon jaisi lagti thi. Dharm India se aaya. Jeene ka dhang Chini, aur kaam ke bartan Iran ke. Buddha aur devi-devtaon ki murtiyon par Bharatiya kapde the, aur sire par Greek saj.

Ek French vidvaan ne ise ek khoobsurat vakya diya: yahan Hindu lachak, Greek eloquence, aur Chinese noor — teeno ek jagah mil gaye.

Sochiye. Ek registaan ke kinaare ek chhota sa rajya, jismein chaar duniya ek saath baithi hui. Yahi tha pahaad wale raaste ka asli tohfa.

## Part 8: I-tsing ki thaali

Hsuan Tsang ki mrityu ke thode hi baad, ek aur mashhoor Chini yaatri is desh mein aaya. I-tsing.

Usne 671 isvi mein shuru kiya. Aur use is desh ke ek bandargah tak pahunchne mein lagbhag do saal lage. Wo bandargah tha Tamralipti, Hooghly nadi ke muhane par.

Kyun do saal? Kyunki is baar wo samundar ke raaste se aaya. Raaste mein wo Sumatra ke ek ilaake mein kai mahine ruka — Sanskrit seekhne ke liye.

Aur ye baat apne aap mein badi hai. Us waqt Central Asia mein halchal thi, rajniti badal rahi thi — pahaad wale raaste ke mehmaannawaz vihara shayad ab bache hi nahi the. Aur samundar wala raasta aasaan ho gaya tha, kyunki Indonesia mein Bharatiya bastiyan badh gayi thin, aur vyapaar lagatar chalta tha.

Us zamane mein Iran se China tak lagatar jahaaz chalte the. I-tsing khud ek Parsi jahaaz par chadha, Kwangtung se. Aur wo pehle Sumatra gaya.

Wo bhi Nalanda mein padha. Lamba padha. Aur jab lautta, to saath le gaya kai sau Sanskrit kitaabein.

Uska mann puja-vidhi mein zyada tha — puja ke baareek niyam, sangh ke niyam. Par usme ek bahut insaani baat bhi chhod gaya. Usne likha, yahan ke log kya khaate hain, kya pehente hain. Uttar Bharat mein gehun roz ka khaana tha, jaise aaj hai. Dakshin aur poorab mein chawal. Gosht kabhi-kabhi khaya jaata tha, par kam. Ghee, tel, doodh, malai har jagah milte the, aur meethi cheezein aur phal bharpoor.

Aur usne ek baat note ki, jo aaj bhi suni ja sakti hai. Yahan ke log sabse zyada dhyan saaf aur gande ke farq par dete hain. Uske kehne mein, Bharat aur baaki deshon ke beech yahi sabse bada farq hai.

Ye chhoti baatein lagti hain. Par inmein ek bada sawal hai. Ek desh doosre desh ko samajh kaise paata hai? Khaane se. Baithne se. Chhoone se.

Ab ek ajeeb baat. I-tsing Bharat ko "pashchim" kehta hai. Aur likhta hai, is desh ka asli naam Aryadesha hai. Arya, yaani shreshth. Desha, yaani desh. Shreshth logon ka desh — kyunki yahan shreshth kirdaar ke log ek ke baad ek aate rehte hain.

Aur ek naam. Uttar ke kabile — Hu, Mongol, Turk — is desh ko "Hindu" kehte hain. Unki zubaan mein, Hsin-tu. Par ye koi aam naam nahi hai, aur is desh ke log khud isse jaante hi nahi.

Nateeja? "Hindu" naam bahar se aaya, aur yahan ke logon ne use apna nahi banaya. Unke hisaab se is desh ka theek naam Aryadesha tha.

Aur ek baat, jo uski tareef ko rok deti hai. Usne ek jagah likha: shreshth desh to Bharat hai, par asli sabhyata aur shishtachar China mein milta hai, aur kisi doosre desh mein nahi.

Sochiye. Yahi wo aadmi hai, jo Bharat ki tareef karte hue bhi apne desh ko oopar rakhta hai. Ye bhag-daud aaj bhi band nahi hui.

## Part 9: Nalanda ke khandhar

Hsuan Tsang ke aane se ek doosri cheez bhi hui. Do deshon ke rajaon ke beech baat shuru hui. Kanauj ka Harshavardhana aur T'ang samraat — dono ne ek doosre ke paas doot bheje.

Aur Hsuan Tsang khud is desh se jude rahe. Wahan se chitthiyan aati rahi, pothiyan aati rahi.

Do chitthiyan aaj bhi China mein sambhal kar rakhi hain. Wo Sanskrit mein likhi gayi thin. Ek 654 isvi mein likhi gayi — ek Bharatiya Bauddh vidvaan ne, Hsuan Tsang ko.

Us chitthi mein pehle purane doston ki khabar hai. Kisi ke kaam ki baat. Aur phir wo likhta hai: hum tumhe bhoolte nahi, isliye do safed kapde bhej rahe hain. Raasta lamba hai, to tohfe ki chhotai ka bura na maanna. Aur jo sutra-shastra tumhe chahiye hon, unki fehrist bhej do. Hum unhe likh kar tumhe bhej denge.

Hsuan Tsang ka jawab bhi rakha hua hai. Usme wo likhta hai: mujhe pata chala, ki mera bada guru Shilabhadra ab nahi raha. Ye khabar sun kar mera dil toot gaya. Jo kitaabein main laaya tha, unmein se main tees volumes ka anuvaad kar chuka hoon. Par ek baat bataani hai: Sindhu nadi paar karte waqt meri pothiyon ka ek bojh kho gaya tha. Ab main fehrist bhej raha hoon. Mauka mile, to wo bhi bhej dena.

Sochiye. Do desh. Do zubaan. Ek dariya, ek pahaad, ek registaan — aur beech mein safed kapde, aur kitaabon ki fehrist.

Aur Nalanda? Hsuan Tsang ne uske baare mein bahut likha, aur doosre logon ne bhi. Sadiyon baad Nehru khud wahan ke khandhar dekhne gaye. Wo hairan reh gaye. Itni badi thi wo jagah, itne bade plan par bani thi. Abhi usi ka ek chhota hissa khoda gaya hai. Baaki par aaj bhi log rehte hain. Par jo hissa khoda gaya, usme bade-bade aangan nikle hain, aur unke chaaron taraf patthar ki imaaratein.

## Part 10: Jo pahaad paar nahi gayi

Ab ek cheez, jo is poore lena-dene mein sabse ajeeb hai.

China mein ek kaam bahut pehle ho gaya, jo yahan nahi hua: chhapai. China mein sabse purane chhape hue kitabon mein aathvi sadi ki wo kitaabein bhi hain, jo Sanskrit mein hain. Lakdi ke takhton par chhapi hui.

Dasvi sadi mein China mein sarkari chhapai ka ek vibhaag khada ho gaya. Aur chhapai bahut tez badhi.

Aur yahan Nehru ek hairat ki baat likhte hain. Saikdon saal tak dono deshon ke vidvaan ek doosre se pothiyan bhejte rahe — kitaabein, pratiyan, anuvaad. Phir bhi, us poore zamane mein Bharat mein kitaabon ko chhapne ka ek bhi saakshya nahi milta.

Ek bhi.

Sochiye. Hum ek hazaar saal pothiyan bhejte rahe. Par jab wo cheez aayi, jisne ek kitaab ki sau-sau pratiyan bana di, to wo humare darwaze par aa kar ruk gayi — pahaad se aage nahi aayi.

Par ek baat hui, jo iss se bhi badi hai — aur ye baat bahut kam log jaante hain. Jo yahan ki purani kitaabein thi, wo is desh mein kho gayi. Par wahi kitaabein Chini aur Tibbati anuvaad mein bach gayi. Aaj bhi China aur Tibet mein wo mil jaati hain, aur haal ke saalon mein khojdahst logon ne unhe wahan se bahar nikaala hai. Sirf Bauddh kitaabein nahi — jyotish, ganit, chikitsa, darshan bhi. Aur ek achhi baat. Bharat, China aur Tibet ke vidvaanon ne milkar ek shabd-kosh banaya — Sanskrit, Tibbati aur Chini, teen zubaanon mein.

To is hazaar saal ka hisaab kya hua? Nehru likhte hain, dono ne ek doosre se kuch seekha. Sirf soch mein nahi, zindagi ke kaam mein bhi. Par shayad China ne Bharat se zyada liya, aur Bharat ne China se kam. Nehru kehte hain, yahi afsos ki baat hai. Unhe lagta hai, Bharat ko Chini logon ki wo saaf, seedhi samajh leni chahiye thi. Wo apne hawa mein udte vichaaron ko us se thaam leti.

China ne bahut liya. Par wo itna mazboot aur aatm-vishwasi tha, ki usne sab kuch apne dhang se liya, apne kapde mein piroya. Buddha ka darshan bhi wahan Confucius aur Lao-tze ke rang mein rang gaya. Aur Buddha ke darshan ki udaasi unki zindagi ki khushi ko chhoo bhi nahi paayi.

Unke paas ek purani kahawat hai. Sarkaar haath mein aa jaaye, to wo tumhe maaregi. Aur Bauddh haath mein aa jaayein, to wo tumhe bhookha maar denge.

Sochiye, ye kahawat unhone apne hi bhikshuon ke liye likhi.

Nateeja? Dene wala aage nahi badhta. Lene wala badhta hai. Aur sabse mushkil hai, doosre ke dhang se seekhna, apna dhang chhode bina.

## Part 11: Chaar kone, pahaad ke us paar

Sadiyon tak ye raasta chalta raha. Mughal daur mein bhi kabhi-kabhi baat hoti rahi. Delhi ke ek Sultan, Mohammed bin Tughlaq, ne ek Arab yaatri ko — Ibn Batuta ko — Chini darbaar mein doot bana kar bheja.

Aur chaudahvin sadi mein Bengal se China ke liye doot gaye. Unmein se ek, 1414 mein, apne saath tohfe le gaya. Un tohfon mein ek jinda zarafa bhi tha.

Ek jinda zarafa.

Nehru likhte hain, ye zarafa Bharat tak kaise pahuncha, ye rahasy hai. Shayad wo Africa se tohfe mein aaya, aur aage Ming samraat ke paas ek adbhut cheez ki tarah bhej diya gaya. China mein zarafa ko bahut pasand kiya gaya — Confucius ke maanne walon ke liye wo shubh ka nishaan tha. Uska ek lamba vrittant likha gaya, aur resham par uska ek chitra bhi bana. Chitra ke saath likha tha: mantri aur praja, sab dekhne ke liye jama hue, aur unke aanand ka koi ant nahi.

Sochiye. Ek jaanwar ek darwaze se nikalta hai, aur doosre darwaze par ek rashtra ka utsav ban jaata hai.

Par badi baat ye hai, ki ye raasta hamesha khula nahi raha. Sadiyon tak dono desh ek doosre se kat gaye.

Phir dono deshon ko wapas ek saath laaya kisi aur ne — ek vyapaari company, British East India Company. Bharat ko ye bojh lambe saalon tak jhelna pada. China ka saath chhota raha, par usme bhi afim aayi, aur jang.

Nehru kehte hain, samay ka pahiya poora ghoom gaya, aur aaj dono desh phir ek doosre ki taraf dekh rahe hain. Ek nayi tarah ke teerthyatri — jo pahaadon par se ud kar jaate hain — apna salaam aur sauhard le kar aate-jaate hain. Ek Chini vidvaan ne is rishte ko ek naam diya hai: China ka Indianization.

Ab is episode ke do siron ko milaiye. Ek taraf, ek pandit, jo chaar kone tak paidal chalta hai. Doosri taraf, ek bhikshu, jo pyale mein gholi hui mitti pi kar pahaad laanghta hai.

Aur beech mein wahi soch chalti rehti hai. Andar wahi soch chaar kone tak paidal gayi. Bahar wahi soch pothiyon aur bhikshuon ke saath pahaad paar gayi — kisi talwar ke bal par nahi, paidal.

To jawab yahi hai. Wo isliye pahunchi, kyunki uske paas ek lamba raasta tha. Pothiyan thin. Bhikshu the. Aur us raaste par chhat milti thi, khana milta tha, aur koi aisa milta tha jo uski bhasha jaanta ho.

Aur wahan se kya laayi? Kitaben laayi. Jo purani pothiyan yahan kho gayi thi, wo Chini aur Tibbati anuvaad mein bachi hui mili. Aur chehre laayi — wo yaatri, jo yahan aaye, yahan padhe, aur yahan ke logon, yahan ke khaane, yahan ke raaj ke baare mein likh gaye. Unki kitaabein aaj is desh ke us daur ka sabse saaf aaina hain.

Aur ek baat, jo humein aaj bhi chubhni chahiye. Nehru likhte hain, is hazaar saal ke mel-jol mein humne bahut diya, aur bahut kam liya.

Raasta dono taraf khula tha. Isi liye jab tak khula raha, dono taraf kuch na kuch naya paida hota raha.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, raaste wali. Ek sabhyata tab tak zinda rehti hai, jab tak uske raaste dono taraf khule hon. Pahaad wale raaste par hamare paas dene ke liye bahut kuch tha — soch, darshan, pothiyan. Lene ke liye bahut kam. Aur wo chhapai, jo ek kitaab ki sau pratiyan bana deti hai, humare darwaze par aa kar ruk gayi. Dena achhi baat hai, par sirf dena kaafi nahi. Jo le leta hai, wo aage nikal jaata hai.

Doosri, naam wali. I-tsing ne likha tha, ki is desh ke log apne naam ko jaante hi nahi the — "Hindu" naam bahar se aaya. Do naam, do raaste.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aapke aas-paas jo bhi aata hai — koi cheez, koi hunar, koi aadat — usme se aap kitna lete hain, aur kitna sirf rakhte hain? Aur kaun se raaste dono taraf khule hain, aur kaun se humne khud band kar diye?
