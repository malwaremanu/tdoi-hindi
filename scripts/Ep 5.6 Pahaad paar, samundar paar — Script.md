## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Ek qaidi, Jawaharlal Nehru, wahan is desh ki khoj mein ek kitaab likh raha hai. Pichhli baar humne dekha tha ki is desh ki soch ne apne andar hi safar kiya: chhah darshan, aur Shankara ke chaar math. Us episode ke aakhir mein ek Chini bhikshu pahaad laangh kar yahan pahuncha tha. Wo yahan kya dhoondhne aaya tha? Aaj wahi uske saath chalenge.

## Part 1: Pahaad ke us paar

China jaane wala pehla Bharatiya vidvaan, jiske baare mein humein pata hai, wo tha Kashyapa Matanga. Kashyapa Matanga, ek Bauddh vidvaan, jo sabse pehle China pahuncha.

Wo jita kar nahi aaya tha. Wo bulaya gaya tha. Aur usne talwar nahi uthai. Wo baith gaya Lo Yang mein, Lo nadi ke kinaare.

Uske baad ek lambi kataar shuru hoti hai. Ek ke baad ek vidvaan. Kumarajiva. Jinagupta. Aur Bodhidharma.

Har ek apne saath bhikshuon ka ek jhund le kar aata tha. Ye akeli yaatrein nahi thi. Ye chhote-chhote karvaan the.

Aur ab ek ankda. Chhathi sadi tak, sirf Lo Yang pradesh mein, teen hazaar se zyada Bharatiya Bauddh bhikshu the. Aur das hazaar Bharatiya parivaar.

Sochiye. Ye koi do-teen yaatri nahi thi. Ye pahaad ke us paar ek poori basti thi. Bharatiya ghar. Bharatiya rasoil. Bharatiya bhikshu.

Aur ye log khaali haath nahi aaye. Wo Sanskrit ki pothiyan laaye. Aur wahan baith kar unhe Chini bhasha mein badla.

Kuch ne to seedhe Chini mein kitaabein likh di. Kavita bhi. Unka hissa Chini sahitya mein aaj bhi zinda hai.

Ek naam goonjta hai. Kumarajiva, China jaane wale vidvaanon mein sabse bada anuvaadak. Usne saintalees kitaabein likhi, aur wo aaj tak bachi hain.

Aur Jinagupta, chhathi sadi ka wo vidvaan, jiska shiksharthi ek T'ang samraat khud ban gaya. Usne saintees Sanskrit kitaabon ka anuvaad kiya.

To pahaad ke us paar: pothiyan, Chini bhasha mein dhalti hui. Ye pehla raasta tha. Ye raasta agla hazaar saal chalta raha.

Par ye raasta ek-tarfa nahi tha. Isi raaste par log is desh mein bhi aaye.

## Part 2: Guru ki aakhri salah

Aana-jaana dono taraf tha. China se bhi log aaye. Unmein sabse mashhoor yaatri the Fa Hien, Hsuan Tsang aur I-tsing.

Fa Hien paanchvi sadi mein aaya. Fa Hien, ek Chini bhikshu, jo yahan ki mitti par padhne aaya.

Par Fa Hien ki yaatra se pehle ek chhoti si baat hui. Wo apne guru se vida lene gaya. Uska guru tha Kumarajiva.

Aur Kumarajiva ne use ek aisi salah di, jo iss poori kahani ki chaabi hai.

Usne kaha: sirf dharmik gyaan jama karne mein apna poora waqt na lagana. Is desh ke logon ka jeena, unki aadatein, unka dhang, inhe dhyan se dekhna. Taaki China inhe samajh sake. Poora desh samajh sake.

Rukiye, is salah par.

Ek guru apne shishya ko doosre desh bhej raha hai. Aur wo usse kehta hai: kitaabein padhna kaafi nahi. Logon ki zindagi dekho.

Sochiye, ye salah kitni aasaan lagti hai. Aur kitni mushkil. Ek kitaab padhne ke liye do saal kaafi hain. Ek desh ke logon ko samajhne ke liye poora jeevan chahiye.

Nateeja? Ye pahaad wala raasta sirf pothiyon ka raasta nahi tha. Ye dekhne ka raasta bhi tha. Log kaise khaate hain. Kahan rehte hain. Kya maante hain. Aur ye baat aage aane wale har yaatri ke saath chali.

Fa Hien ne Pataliputra ki vishwavidyalaya mein padha. Pataliputra, aaj ka Patna, jahan Mauryon ki rajdhani thi. Usne wahan padhai ki, aur wahan se wapas apne desh gaya.

Usne yahan ke rasoil ka, yahan ke kapdon ka, yahan ke tyohaaron ka hisaab rakha.

Wahi hisaab aage aane wale har yaatri ke kaam aaya.

## Part 3: Paanch hisse, ek pyaas

Ab isi raaste par ek aur naam aata hai. Hsuan Tsang.

Wo bhi kitaab likhne wala tha. Uski kitaab ka naam hai Si-Yu-Ki. Si-Yu-Ki, yaani "pashchim ke desh ka vrittant". Pashchim, yaani Bharat. Kyunki Bharat usse pashchim mein tha.

Uska likha aaj bhi padha jaata hai. Aur ek khaas wajah se.

Wo ek bahut sabhya aur nafees desh se aaya tha. China ki rajdhani Si-an-fu us waqt kala aur padhai ka ek bada kendra thi. Yaani wo kisi pichhde gaon ka yaatri nahi tha.

Isliye jab wo yahan ki padhai ki tareef karta hai, to sunne layak baat hai.

Wo likhta hai: yahan padhai jaldi shuru hoti thi, ek-ek padav par aage badhti thi, aur aakhir mein vidyaarthi vishwavidyalaya tak pahunchta tha. Wahan paanch cheezein padhai jaati thi. Vyakaran. Chikitsa. Aur darshan.

Par usse sabse zyada chubhi yahan ka padhne ka bhookh.

Yahan ke saare bhikshu aur purohit adhyaapak bhi the. Isliye shuruaati padhai gaon-gaon tak pahunchti thi. Jo bhi padha hua tha, wo padha bhi sakta tha.

Aur sarkar ke baare mein wo likhta hai: yahan ka raaj koshish karta hai ki logon par bojh kam pade. Koi zabardasti ka kaam nahi. Kar halke hain. Aur vyapaari aate-jaate rehte hain, apna kaam karte hue.

Bas, itna hi bacha hai us hisse se. Isse theek pehle jo paragraph tha, jisme wo yahan ke logon ke baare mein kehta hai, wo humare paas nahi hai. (GAP, neeche Sources dekhiye.)

Par ek baat saaf hai. Jo aadmi do deshon ko tulna kar raha hai, wo apne desh ko oopar nahi rakhta. Wo sirf ye likhta hai ki ye log padhne ke kitne bhookhe hain.

## Part 4: Wapas lauta bojh

Hsuan Tsang wapas usi raaste se gaya, Central Asia se hokar. Aur uske saath pothiyon ka ek bada dher tha.

Uske hisaab se us waqt Bauddh dharm bahut door tak phaila hua tha. Khorasan, Iran ka ek purana ilaaka. Iraq. Aur Syria ki sarhadon tak.

Par yahi wo waqt tha jab wahan Bauddh dharm pehle se kamzor pad chuka tha. Aur Arabia mein Islam shuru ho chuka tha. Wo jaldi hi in saari zameenon par phailne wala tha.

Nateeja? Jo dharm yahan se pahaad paar gaya, wo wahin ruk nahi gaya. Wo aage chala gaya, aur uske peeche ek naya dharm bhi chal pada.

Iran ke baare mein Hsuan Tsang likhta hai: ye log padhai ke peechhe nahi bhagte, apna poora mann kala mein lagate hain. Nehru jodte hain: Iran ne hamesha zindagi ko sundar banane par kaam kiya, aur uska asar Asia mein door tak gaya.

Ab ek chhota raaj, Turfan, Gobi registan ke kinaare.

Yahan kai sabhyatayein aakar milee. China, Bharat, Iran, aur door ka Yunani asar bhi.

Yahan ki bhasha India se aayi thi, Iran ke raaste se, aur wo Europe ki Celtic bhashaon jaisi lagti thi. Yahan ka dharm India se aaya. Jeene ka dhang Chini tha, aur kaam ke bartan Iran ke. Jahan Buddha aur devi-devtaon ki murtiyan bani, un par Bharatiya kapde the, aur sire par Greek saj.

Ek French vidvaan ne ise ek khoobsurat vakya diya: yahan Hindu lachak, Greek eloquence aur Chinese noor, teeno ek jagah mil gaye.

Sochiye: ek registan ke kinaare ek chhota sa rajya, jismein chaar duniya ek saath baithi hui. Yahi tha pahaad wale raaste ka asli tohfa. Isliye iska shrey kisi ek desh ko nahi ja sakta.

Laut kar aane par uske desh aur samraat ne usse khushi se apnaya. Wo baith gaya apni kitaab likhne, aur un pothiyon ka anuvaad karne.

Ek jagah ka naam yaad rakhiye. Nalanda.

Hsuan Tsang ne Nalanda ke baare mein bahut likha. Sadiyon baad Nehru khud wahan ke khandhar dekhne gaye, aur hairan reh gaye.

Itni badi thi wo jagah, aur abhi usi ka ek chhota hissa khoda gaya hai. Baaki par aaj bhi log rehte hain. Jo hissa khoda gaya, usme bade aangan nikle hain, aur unke chaaron taraf patthar ki imaaratein.

Aur ek baat. Chhathi sadi mein China mein ek upanyaas likha gaya, Monkey. Usme Hsuan Tsang ke isi safar ki rangeen kahaaniyan hain. Aur us kitaab ka ant Bharat ko ek shraddhanjali se hota hai.

## Part 5: I-tsing ki thaali

I-tsing saatvi sadi ka Chini bhikshu tha. Wo bhi Nalanda mein padha. Aur lamba padha.

Nalanda se wo apne desh gaya, aur saath le gaya kai sau Sanskrit kitaabein.

Uska mann puja-vidhi mein zyada tha. Par usme ek bahut insaani baat bhi chhod gaya. Usne likha, yahan ke log kya khaate hain, kya pehente hain.

Uttar Bharat mein gehun roz ka khaana tha, jaise aaj hai. Dakshin aur poorab mein chawal. Gosht khaya jaata tha, par kam. Ghee, tel, doodh, malai, ye sab har jagah milte the. Aur meethi cheezein aur phal bharpoor.

Aur usne ek baat note ki, jo aaj bhi suni ja sakti hai. Yahan ke log sabse zyada dhyan saaf aur gande ke farq par dete hain, aur uske kehne mein, Bharat aur baaki deshon ke beech yahi sabse bada farq hai.

Ye chhoti-chhoti baatein lagti hain. Par inmein ek bada sawal chhupa hai. Ek desh doosre desh ko samajh kaise paata hai? Khaane se. Baithne se. Chhoone se.

Ab ek ajeeb baat. I-tsing Bharat ko "pashchim" kehta hai. Aur likhta hai, is desh ka asli naam Aryadesha hai. Arya, yaani shreshth. Desha, yaani desh. Shreshth logon ka desh, kyunki yahan shreshth kirdaar ke log ek ke baad ek aate rehte hain.

Uska ek doosra naam bhi hai: Madhyadesha, yaani beech ka desh, kyunki ye saikdon deshon ka kendra hai.

Aur ab wo baat, jo hum Episode 4.1 mein sun chuke hain.

I-tsing likhte hain: uttar ke kabile, Hu, Mongol, Turk, is desh ko "Hindu" kehte hain. Unki zubaan mein, "Hsin-tu". Par ye koi aam naam nahi hai. Is desh ke log khud is naam ko jaante hi nahi.

Nateeja? "Hindu" naam bahar se aaya, aur yahan ke logon ne use apna nahi banaya. Unke hisaab se is desh ka theek naam Aryadesha tha.

I-tsing ne ek jagah likha: shreshth desh to Bharat hai, par asli sabhyata aur shishtachar China mein milta hai, aur kisi doosre desh mein nahi.

Yahi wo aadmi hai, jo Bharat ki tareef karte hue bhi apne desh ko oopar rakhta hai. Ye bhag-daud band nahi hui.

Aur ek cheez, jo is raaste par ulti taraf gayi. Sanskrit ka aadar China mein bhi tha.

Ek waqt aisa aaya ki kuch Chini vidvaanon ne Sanskrit ki dhwaniyan apni bhasha mein utaarne ki koshish ki. T'ang kaal mein ek Chini bhikshu, Shon Wen, ne aisi hi ek varnamala banane ki koshish ki.

Sochiye. Ek desh ki zubaan doosre desh mein padhaayi jaane lagi. Ye pothiyon ka asar hai. Ye kisi talwar ka asar nahi.

## Part 6: Jo cheez pahaad paar nahi gayi

China mein ek kaam bahut pehle ho gaya, jo yahan nahi hua: chhapai.

China mein sabse purane chhape hue kitabon mein aathvi sadi ki wo kitaabein bhi hain jo Sanskrit mein hain. Lakdi ke takhton par chhapi hui.

Dasvi sadi mein China mein sarkari chhapai ka ek vibhaag khada hua. Aur chhapai bahut tez badhi.

Aur yahan Nehru ek hairat ki baat likhte hain: saikdon saal tak dono deshon ke vidvaan ek doosre se pothiyan bhejte rahe. Kitaabein. Pratiyan. Anuvaad.

Phir bhi, us poore zamane mein Bharat mein kitaabein chhapne ka ek bhi saakshya nahi milta.

Ek bhi.

Sochiye. Hum ek hazaar saal pothiyan bhejte rahe. Par jab wo cheez aayi, jisne ek kitaab ki sau-sau pratiyan bana di, to wo humare darwaze par aa kar ruk gayi.

Wo chhapai pahaad se aage nahi aayi.

Nateeja? Jis desh ke paas pothiyan thi, uske paas unki pratiyan nahi thi. Aur jis desh ne chhapai li, wo ek kitaab sau jagah pahuncha gaya.

Aaj sochiye. Aaj bhi ek achhi cheez ek seema par aa kar ruk jaati hai, kabhi kisi pabandi se, kabhi kisi aadat se, kabhi sirf is wajah se ki koi use lene nahi gaya.

Aur wo chhapai ek din Europe bhi pahunchi, Mongol ya Yuan raajvansh ke zamane mein. Sabse pehle Germany mein. Pandrahavi sadi mein poore Europe mein.

Yahi wo cheez thi, jisne Europe ko badal diya.

Ab Nehru poore lena-dene ka hisaab lagate hain.

Wo likhte hain: hazaar saal se zyada ke is mel-jol mein dono deshon ne ek doosre se kuch seekha. Sirf soch mein nahi, zindagi ke kaam mein bhi.

Par shayad China ne Bharat se zyada seekha, aur Bharat ne China se kam. Nehru kehte hain, yahi afsos ki baat hai.

Unhe lagta hai, Bharat ko Chini logon ki wo saaf, seedhi samajh leni chahiye thi. Wo apne hawa mein udte vichaaron ko us se thaam leti.

China ne Bharat se bahut kuch liya. Par wo itna mazboot aur aatm-vishwasi tha, ki usne sab kuch apne dhang se liya, apne kapaas mein piroya. Buddha ka darshan bhi wahan Confucius aur Lao-tze ke rang mein rang gaya. Aur Buddha ke darshan ki udaasi unki zindagi ki khushi ko chhoo bhi nahi paayi.

Unke paas ek purani kahawat hai. Sarkaar haath mein aa jaaye, to wo tumhe maaaregi. Aur Bauddh haath mein aa jaayein, to wo tumhe bhookha maar denge.

Sochiye, ye kahawat unhone apne hi bhikshuon ke liye likhi. Yaani ek aisi cheez ko poora jagah, jis par wo hanste bhi the.

Nateeja? Dene wala aage nahi badhta. Lene wala badhta hai. Aur sabse mushkil hai, doosre ke dhang se seekhna, apna dhang chhode bina.

## Part 7: Samundar ki taraf

Samundar ke us paar ek poori duniya thi. Aur us duniya ke baare mein Nehru ek shikayat likhte hain.

Wo Tagore ki baat yaad karte hain. Tagore, Bangla ke bade kavi, jo apne desh ko jaanna chahte the.

Tagore ka kehna tha: is desh ko jaanna ho, to us zamane mein jao, jab isne doosre kinaron ke logon ko apna bana liya. Us daur mein iski udaarta door tak roshan thi.

Aur aaj? Ye desh ek tang deewar ke andar sookar gaya hai.

Nehru poochte hain: hum mein kitne log jaante hain ki humara ateet bahar kya tha?

Aur ek baat wo aise likhte hain, jo aaj bhi chaubhaati hai. Aaj bhi zyadatar pashchimi logon ko lagta hai ki purana itihaas Mediterranean ke aas-paas ka hai, aur aaj ka itihaas Europe ke us chhote mahadeep ka.

Ek angrez lekhak ne Bharat ke videshi kaam par kuch likha tha, par Nehru kehte hain, shayad wo un khojon se anjaan the jo haal hi mein South-East Asia mein hui hain.

Aur phir Nehru apni hi ek yaad sunaate hain.

Pandrah saal pehle, unhone South-East Asia ke itihaas ka ek vistrit hissa padha. Wo hairan hue. Unke saamne naye naqshe khul gaye. Is desh ke ateet ki nayi tasveerein. Unhe apni saari soch dobara sajaani padi.

Champa. Cambodia aur Angkor. Srivijaya. Majapahit. Ye sab samundar ke us paar ke rajya the.

Ye naam achanak soone aakaash se nikal kar zinda ho gaye.

Aur ek aadmi ke baare mein ek angrez vidvaan ne likha hai. Uska naam Sailendra. Sailendra, ek samundari vijeta, jiske naam se Persia se China tak ek goonj uthi thi.
