## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo is desh ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar do raaste the — ek pahaad paar, jispar bhikshu aur pothiyan gayi; doosra samundar ki taraf, jispar jahaaz. Pahaad wale raaste ki baat poori hui. Aaj samundar wale raaste par utrenge.

## Part 1: Sadiyon tak, samundar paar

Ek baat Nehru baar-baar dohraate hain: is desh ko jaanna hai, to do safar karne padte hain. Ek waqt mein. Aur ek jagah mein.

Waqt mein peeche jaana padta hai. Itna peeche, ki aaj ki tang galiyan, aaj ka shor, aaj ki sharmindagi peeche chhoot jaaye. Aur jagah mein? Jagah mein samundar ke us paar jaana padta hai.

Bangla ke bade kavi Tagore ka kehna tha: apne desh ko jaanna ho, to us zamane mein jao jab isne apni aatma pehchaani thi — jab uski udaarta itni door roshan thi, ki doosre kinaron ke log use apna maan baithe the. Aur aaj? Aaj ye desh apne hi andar sookar gaya hai.

Nehru ispar ek kadwa jod likhte hain. Is desh ke logon ne bahar jo banaya, wo itihaas aaj tak likha nahi gaya. Aur pashchim ki itihaas-kitaben? Wo Bharat ka zikr karti hain — par sirf uske hamlavaron ka. Jaise yahan ke log koi neend mein sapne dekhne wale log the, jinko samundar aur pahaad, dono ne duniya se kaat diya tha.

Ek angrez vidvaan is tasveer par rukte hain. Unka kehna hai: aisi soch aadhi sach bhi nahi hai. Hamlavaron ka hisaab dekh kar log ye bhool jaate hain ki is desh ne vichaar se bhi desh jeete. Ye samundari bastiyan, ye jotein — door tak phaili hui thin.

Aur yahi wo baat hai, jispar Nehru khud rukte hain. Pandrah saal pehle unhone South-East Asia ke itihaas ka ek vistrit hissa padha. Wo hairan reh gaye. Unke saamne naye naqshe khulne lage.

Champa. Cambodia aur Angkor. Srivijaya. Majapahit.

Champa — poorab tat ka ek purana rajya. Srivijaya — Sailendra samrajya ka doosra naam. Baaki do naam abhi rahne dijiye.

Ye naam achanak soone aakaash se nikal kar zinda ho gaye. Kagaz par pade shabd ek baar mein uth khade hue.

Aur ek aadmi ke baare mein ek vidvaan ne likha hai. Uska naam Sailendra hai — wo samundari vijeta, jiski goonj Persia se China tak pahunchti thi. Us vidvaan ke shabdon mein: is aadmi ka muqabla duniya ke sabse bade sipah-salaaron se kiya ja sakta hai. Do dashkon mein usne ek samundari samrajya khada kiya, jo paanch sau saal khada raha. Aur usi samrajya ne Java aur Cambodia mein Bharatiya kala ko phoolne ka mauka diya.

Par phir wo ek baat jodte hain, jo dil dukhaati hai. Aaj hamare encyclopedia aur itihaas ki kitaabon mein aap us samrajya ka naam dhoondhte reh jaayenge.

Is poore ilaake ko ek naam se bhi bulaya jaata hai: Greater India. Is par kaam ab bhi chalta hai — khaali jagah bahut hain, vidvaan aapas mein ladte bhi hain. Par srot ki kami nahi. Chini itihaas. Arab yaatriyon ke varnan. Purane shilalekh aur tamrapatra. Java aur Bali ka sahitya. Greek aur Latin lekhak bhi. Aur in sab se badh kar — patthar mein khade hue wo bade khandhar. Angkor. Borobudur.

To sawal ye nahi ki ye cheezein hain ya nahi. Sawal ye hai ki ye kitni door tak gayi, aur kaun gaye the. Uske liye ek naam chahiye. Aur wahi naam is episode ka title hai.

## Part 2: Sone ki dharti

Pehli sadi se shuru kariye. Isai sadi.

Us waqt se lehar par lehar Bharatiya log samundar ke us paar nikalne lage. Poorab aur dakshin-poorab ki taraf.

Pehle Ceylon. Phir Burma. Malaya. Java. Sumatra. Borneo. Siam. Cambodia. Indo-China.

Kuch log aur bhi door pahunche. Formosa. Philippines. Celebes. (Aur ek-do hadh se bhi aage — Madagascar tak. Aaj bhi wahan ki zubaan mein Sanskrit ke shabd milte hain.)

Ye safar ek saal ka kaam nahi tha. Saikdon saal lagte. Aur shayad har jagah seedhe is desh se nahi pahuncha — kahin pahunch kar, wahan se aage. Char bade daur the. Aur un dauron ke beech bhi log chupchaap chalte rahe.

Aur yahan ek baat hairan karne wali hai. Ye safar sirf bahaduron ka, ya sirf vyapaariyon ka nahi tha. Ye sansthit tha. Sarkari. Door-door, lagbhag ek saath, kai jagah bastiyan shuru hui. Aur jaane kahan? Aisi jagah jahan se aage ka raasta khulta ho. Bandargah par. Vyapaar ke raaste par.

Aur in bastiyon ke naam? Wahi purane naam. Cambodia ka naam usi Kamboja se aaya — Kamboja, Gandhara ya Kabul ki ghati ka ek purana shehar.

Nateeja? Ye log naqshe par utarte the, aur naqshe par apne purane naam likh dete the.

Ab un deshon ke naam dekhiye. Purani Sanskrit kitaabein unhe do naam deti hain. Svarnabhumi. Sone ki dharti. Aur Svarnadvipa. Sone ka dweep.

Sone ki. Isliye ki jaane wale ke haath mein kaam tha: vyapaar. Par naam mein ek lalach bhi tha. Sone ki dharti — is naam ko sun kar langar uthane ka mann kar jaaye.

Ye talwar se nahi hua. Ye dheere-dheere hua. Pehle kuch log bas gaye. Phir aur aaye. Wahan jo log pehle se the, unse unka mel ho gaya. Ek mili-juli sanskriti khadi hui. Aur uske baad, shayad, rajniti aayi. Kuch Kshatriya rajkumar, achhe khandaanon ke chhote-sa ladke, adventure aur raj ki talash mein.

Naamon ki samanta se lagta hai ki bahut log Malva kabile se the — Malva, madhya Bharat ka ek bada, phaila hua kabila. Isi se Malay nasal aayi, jisne poore Indonesia mein bada role nibhaya.

Jo pehle gaye, wo aksar Kalinga se gaye. Kalinga — poorab tat ka ilaaka, jise aaj Orissa kehte hain. Par asli sansthit prayas dakshin ke Pallav raajvansh ka tha — Pallav, dakshin ka ek Hindu raajvansh. Aur Sailendra khud? Maana jaata hai ki wo Orissa se aaya tha. Us waqt Orissa Bauddh dharm ka gadh tha, par uska raajvansh Brahmanical.

Chupchaap, saikdon saal, ek raasta. Aur us raaste ka naam sunte hi log chal padte the.

To sawal bada hai. Aise khatarnak samundari safar kaun karta hai? Koi yun hi langar nahi uthata.

## Part 3: Vyapaar ki pyaas

Aise safar yun hi shuru nahi hote. Inke peechhe sadiyon tak ek-ek aadmi, ek-ek chhota giroh hota hai. Aur us giroh ka kaam hota hai vyapaar.

Yaani pehle aaye vyapaari. Rajkumar baad mein aaye.

Isi baat ka ek aur saakshya hai. Sabse purani Sanskrit kitaabon mein in poorabi deshon ka zikr hai. Kabhi wo naam pahchane nahi jaate. Par kabhi bilkul saaf hote hain.

Java ka naam usi se aaya — Yavadvipa, yaani bajre ka dweep. Aaj bhi India mein "java" ka matlab hai jau ya bajra.

Baaki naam? Wo aksar dhaatuon, khanijon, ya kisi kheton aur udyog ke maal se bane hain. Naam khud btaate hain ki wahan jaane ka kaam kya tha.

Ek vidvaan ne likha hai: agar sahitya logon ke mann ka sahi aaina hai, to Isa ke aas-paas ke saikdon saalon mein Bharat mein vyapaar ek bada junoon tha.

Nateeja? Ek badhti hui arthavyavastha thi. Aur door ke bazaaron ki nirantar khoj.

Vyapaar teesri aur doosri sadi BC mein badha. Aur phir, theek usi daur mein, uske peechhe-peechhe ek doosre tarah ke log aaye. Dharm-pracharak. Kyunki ye wahi daur tha — Ashoka ke turant baad ka.

Purani Sanskrit kathaon mein aise safar bhare pade hain. Toofan. Jahaaz ka tootna. Bazaar ke liye poora-jahaaz bhar kar nikalna.

Aur ye sirf kahaniyan nahi thin. Greek aur Arab lekhak bhi gawaahi dete hain: pehli sadi Isai tak Bharat aur poorab ke beech niyamit samundari aana-jaana chal raha tha.

Kyun? Kyunki naqsha dekhiye. Malay peninsula aur Indonesia ke dweep theek us raaste par the jo China se Bharat, Persia, Arabia aur Mediterranean tak jaata tha. Aur in deshon mein kya tha? Khanij. Dhaatu. Masale. Lakdi. Malaya to tin ki khanon ke liye mashhoor tha — jaise aaj hai.

Shuru ke safar ka raasta kya tha? Poorab tat se — Kalinga se, Bengal se, Burma se — aur phir Malay peninsula se neeche. Baad mein poorab aur dakshin Bharat se seedhe samundari raaste khule.

Aur isi raaste par ek ulta safar bhi hota tha. China se aane wale bhikshu bhi isi raaste se aate the. Paanchvi sadi mein Fa Hsien yahan aaye — Fa Hsien, wo Chini bhikshu jinke safar ki baat hum pichhle episode mein kar chuke hain. Wo Java ke paas se hokar nikle. Aur wahan unki ek shikayat milti hai: us waqt wahan bahut "pakhndi" the. Yaani wo log jo Brahmanical dharm ke the, Bauddh nahi.

Sochiye. Ek Chini bhikshu, Java ke samundar ke kinaare. Aur wahan usse milte hain wo log jo is desh ke purane devta poojhte hain.

Raasta sirf maal ka nahi tha. Raasta soch ka tha.

## Part 4: Do mastool wale jahaaz

Aur us raaste par chalne ke liye ek cheez lazmi thi. Jahaaz.

Jahaaz banane ka kaam purane Bharat mein ek badhta hua, chamakta hua udyog tha. Us zamane ke jahaazon ke kuch vivran bhi humare paas hain. Aur bandargah? Bahut se — naam se naam tak gine hue.

Do saakshya dekhiye.

Pehla. Andhra — dakshin Bharat ka ek purana ilaaka. Uske sikke, doosri-teesri sadi ke, apne par ek do mastool wale jahaaz ka nishaan rakhte hain. Ek chhota sikka. Par uspar poora jahaaz, do mastoolon ke saath.

Doosra. Ajanta ki deewarein. Wahan samundar ka drishya bana hai — Lanka par vijay, aur jahaaz, jinpar hatti le jaayi ja rahi hain.

Nateeja? Ye log jahaaz banate the, langar uthate the, aur maal taiyaar karte the. Iske baad ek cheez baaki thi: samundar par apna raasta apna rakhna.

Aur yahan kahani kadi ho jaati hai. In bastiyon se jo bade rajya uthe, wo bas shehar nahi the. Wo samundari taaqatein thin. Unka poora dhyan vyapaar par tha — aur uske saath hi samundari raaste par kabza.

In taaqaton ki aapas mein takraar hoti thi. Samundar par. Aur ek baar kisi ne dakshin Bharat ki Chola taaqat ko lalkaara. Chola — dakshin ka samundari raajvansh, jiske jahaaz door-door tak jaate the.

Jawab mein Chola ne apne jahaaz bheje, aur Sailendra samrajya ko kuch samay ke liye jhuka diya.

Par dhyan dijiye: ye jang dharm ki nahi thi. Aage chal kar jab ek Bauddh rajya aur ek Brahmanical rajya mein bada takraav hua — wo takraav bhi asli mein vyapaar aur samundari raaste ke kabze ki jang thi. Raajnitik. Aarthik.

Dharm ka rishta alag tha. Shuru mein ye bastiyan Brahmanical thin. Bauddh dharm baad mein phaila. Dono wahan saath-saath rahe — dost ki tarah. Aur wahan ki aam puja mein dono cheezein mil gayi.

Aur ek baat. Kala bhi dharm ke saath gayi. Bharatiya kala ka mool dharm aur darshan mein hai. Jahan dharm gaya, wahan kala ka wo swaroop bhi gaya. Isliye ye dono ek hi jahaaz par savaar the — vishwas, aur uski tasveer.

Ab ek patthar ki taraf chaliye. Ek deewar par khudi hui lines. Unmein ek line aisi hai, jo poore vyapaar se bhi zyada btaati hai.

## Part 5: Pandrah sau log

Dakshin Bharat mein ek shilalekh mila hai. Saal tha 1088. Us par ek sangh ka zikr hai. Naam tha — Pandrah Sau ka Sangh.

Ek mandir ki deewar sochiye. Patthar par chhini chal rahi hai. Ek aadmi baith kar likhwa raha hai. Wo vyapaari hai, ya us sangh ka koi afsar. Aur jo line wo khudwa raha hai, wo apni hi tareef mein hai — par tareef aise, ki aaj bhi padhne wale ka sir ooncha ho jaaye.

Usmein likha hai: ye log "janm se ghoomne wale" hain.

Aur aage tafseel hai: wo bahut purane zamane se, door-door ke deshon mein ghoomte aaye hain. Zameen ke raaste se, paani ke raaste se. Chhah mahadeepon tak. Aur maal ka kya? Ghode. Hatti. Ratn. Itra. Dawa. Kabhi poora-thok, kabhi khuda.

Sangh ke naam mein hi ginti hai — pandrah sau.

Ye shilalekh Tamil mein hai — dakshin Bharat ki zubaan mein. Yaani jo sangh bahar ja raha tha, wo ghar ki zubaan mein likhwa raha tha.

Aur is shilalekh ka swar suniye. Tareef ka swar hai. Par tareef ka aadhaar kya hai? Paisa nahi. Safar. Wo ye nahi likhte ki hamare paas kitna maal hai. Wo ye likhte hain ki hum kaun hain — wo log, jo kabhi ek jagah nahi ruke.

Ek sangh. Pandrah sau log. Chhah mahadeep.

Sochiye. Ek sangh, jiske jahaaz door-door tak jaate the — aur jiska naam hi ye tha, ki ye ghoomne wale hain. Ye log apne ghar mein nahi baithe the. Unka ghar raaste mein tha.

Nehru kehte hain: yahi wo peechha tha, jispar baad mein ek poora ilaaka khada hua.

## Part 6: Do samrajya ke beech

Ye saari bastiyan do bade deshon ke beech baithi hui thin. Do badi sabhyataon ke beech. Ek taraf Bharat. Doosri taraf China.

Kuch bastiyan Asia ki mitti par thin — seedhe Chini samrajya ki sarhad ko chhu rahi thin. Baaki us seedhe vyapaar raaste par, jo China se Bharat jaata tha. Nateeja? Ye log dono ke asar mein the, aur ek mili-juli Indo-Chini sanskriti khadi hui.

Aur kamaal ki baat: dono sabhyataon mein takraav nahi hua.

Kaise? Kuch cheezein China se aayi. Kuch Bharat se. Ek aam niyam bana: raaj aur sarkaar chalane ke tareeqe, aur zindagi ka falsafa — wo China se. Aur dharm aur kala — wo Bharat se. Mainland ke desh — Burma, Siam, Indo-China — China ki taraf zyada jhuke. Dweep aur Malay peninsula par Bharat ki chhaap gehri thi.

Par ek baat hairan karne wali hai. Cambodia mein, aur Angkor ke un bade khandaron mein — jo kala ki chhaap ab tak mili hai, wo sirf Bharat ki hai.

Kyun? Kyunki Bharatiya kala ek dhang ki nahi thi. Wo lachili thi. Ek vidvaan ne likha hai: is kala mein ek ajeeb si jaan aur lachak hai. Jahan gayi, wahan ki mitti, wahan ki nasal, wahan ka dharm — sab dekh kar apna roop badal leti thi. Yunani kala mein bhi wahi gun tha. Dono chalne wale hunar the.

Ab us samrajya ki baat karte hain, jo in sab mein sabse bada tha. Sailendra samrajya. Doosre naam se — Sri Vijaya.

Aathvi sadi tak ye poore Malaysia par sabse badi taaqat tha. Samundar par bhi, zameen par bhi.

Iska janam kahan hua? Pehle maana jaata tha Sumatra. Baad ki khojon se lagta hai ki wo Malay peninsula se shuru hua. Uske failaav ko dekhiye: Malaya, Ceylon, Sumatra, Java ka ek hissa, Borneo, Celebes, Philippines, Formosa ka ek tukda. Aur Cambodia aur Champa par uski dabdaba ki chhaya. Ye ek Bauddh samrajya tha.

Par Sri Vijaya se bahut pehle bhi yahan rajya the. Malaya mein. Cambodia mein. Java mein.

Malay peninsula ke uttar mein, Siam ki sarhad ke paas, bade khandar milte hain. Ek angrez vidvaan inhe dekh kar likhte hain: ye batate hain ki yahan ek waqt taaqatwar rajya the, aur daulat aur aish ka star ooncha tha.

Aur Cambodia? Wahan Kamboja ek bada shehar ban gaya. Nauvin sadi mein ek bada raja, Jayavarman, chhote rajyaon ko jodta hai — aur Angkor raajdhani ban jaati hai. Jayavarman — wo raja jisne Cambodia ko ek samrajya banaya.

Uske baad lagbhag chaar sau saal: bade raja, aur bade banane wale. Angkor shehar Asia mein mashhoor ho jaata hai. Uske baare mein kaha jaata tha — Angkor the Magnificent. Ek raajdhani, jahan shayad das lakh log rahte the, aur jo Caesar ke Rome se bada aur zyada shaan-daar thi.

Aur phir, achanak, ye sab khatam ho jaata hai. Itna achanak ki kuch imaaratein adhoori chhoot gayi. Bahar se hamle. Andar ki gadbad. Par sabse badi chot thi Mekong nadi ka bhar aana — Mekong, wo badi nadi jo aaj bhi wahan se guzarti hai. Nadi ki mitti ne raasta bhar diya, aur shehar ke aas-paas sab daldal ban gaya. Log us shehar ko chhod gaye.

[SFX: door se paani ki dheemi laharein, phir sookha patthar]

Aaj sochiye: ek nadi apna raasta badal de, aur ek poora shehar — ek poori sabhyata — ka rehna band ho jaaye.

Par ye Cambodia ki kahani hai. Ab Java ki. Aur wahan do samrajya aamne-saamne honge.

## Part 7: Teen sau saal, do samrajya

Nauvin sadi mein Java alag ho gaya. Par Sailendra phir bhi Indonesia ki sabse badi taaqat bane rahe — gyarahvin sadi tak.

Aur phir dakshin Bharat ki Chola taaqat se takkar hui. Chola jeete. Unhone Indonesia ke bade hisson par pachaas saal se zyada raaj kiya.

Par jab wo laute, to Sailendra phir uthe. Aur lagbhag teen sau saal aur chale.

Sirf ek baat badal chuki thi. Ab wo poorab ke samundar ke maalik nahi the.

Terahvin sadi mein unke samrajya ka bikharav shuru hua. Java badhta gaya. Siam bhi. Aur chaudahvin sadi ke doosre hisse mein Java ne Sri Vijaya ko poori tarah hara diya.

Wahi Javan rajya ab saamne aata hai.

Uska apna lamba itihaas tha. Wo Brahmanical rajya tha — Bauddh dharm phailne ke baad bhi usne purana vishwas nahi chhoda. Usne Sri Vijaya ki raajnitik aur aarthik dabdaba rok kar rakhi — us waqt bhi, jab Java ka aadha hissa uske kabze mein tha.

Ye log samundar ke the. Vyapaari. Aur patthar mein badi imaaratein banane ke deewane.

Shuru mein iska naam tha Singhasari. Phir 1292 mein ek naya shehar basa — Majapahit. Aur usi shehar se wo samrajya uthta hai, jo Sri Vijaya ki jagah le leta hai.

Majapahit ne Kublai Khan ke bheje hue Chini dooton ka apman kiya. Kublai Khan — China ka wo samraat, jiske naam se Asia kaamp uthti thi. Uska daam bharna pada: China ne ek sena bheji. Par usi takkar se Javan logon ne ek cheez seekh li. Barood. Aur usi barood se unhone Sailendra ko hara diya.

Majapahit ka raaj bahut kendra-baddh tha. Uska kar-vyavastha achhi thi. Aur vyapaar aur upniveshon par khaas dhyan tha. Sarkaar ke alag-alag vibhaag the — vyapaar ka, upniveshon ka, jan-swasthya ka, yuddh ka. Nyay ke liye ek bada nyayalaya, kai nyayadhishon ke saath.

Nehru likhte hain, ye dekh kar hairat hoti hai ki wo samrajya kitna achhe se sanchalit tha. Uska mukhya kaam tha, Bharat se China tak ka vyapaar. Uski ek mashhoor shasak rani thi — Rani Suhita.

Par ek baat kadi thi. Sri Vijaya aur Majapahit ke beech ki jang bahut nirmam thi. Majapahit ki poori jeet hui. Aur usi jeet ne aage ki ladai ke beej bo diye.

Wo beej kab phoote? Uske liye chand saal kaafi the.

## Part 8: 1511

1511. Ye saal yaad rakhiye, kyunki isi par ye kahani rukti hai.

Pehle dekhiye ki usse pehle kya hua.

Sailendra ki tabahi ke khandaron se ek nayi taaqat uthti hai. Uske saath kuch aur log bhi jude — khaas taur par Arab, aur naye-naye Musalmaan. Us nayi taaqat ne Sumatra aur Malacca mein apna raaj khada kiya.

Malacca — Malay peninsula ka wo bada bazaar-shehar, jahan sadiyon se jahaaz aate the. Uske ghat par roz naye jahaaz lagte the. Aur har jahaaz ke saath, ek nayi zubaan.

Aur ab ek badi baat. Poorab ke samundar par jo kabza saikdon saal se dakshin Bharat ya Bharatiya bastiyon ke paas tha — wo ab Arabon ke haath chala gaya.

Malacca uth kar bada vyapaar-kendra ban gaya, aur siyasat ka bhi kendra. Islam Malay peninsula aur wahan ke dweepon par phaila.

Isi nayi taaqat ne Majapahit ko hamesha ke liye khatam kiya — pandrahavi sadi ke aakhir mein.

Par us samrajya ko mitaane walon ke paas bhi zyada din nahi the.

Kyunki chand hi saal baad, 1511 mein, Portuguese log aaye. Unka sardar tha Albuquerque. Albuquerque — Portuguese sardar, jisne Malacca par kabza kar liya.

[SFX: door se top ki ek lagi awaaz, phir samundar ki laharein]

Aur us ek din ke saath ek lambi kahani khatam hui. Terah sau saal purani. Europe apni nayi samundari taaqat ke dum par Far East tak pahunch gaya tha.

Wo raasta, jo itne saal se is desh ke haath mein tha, ab is desh ke haath mein nahi tha. Raasta band nahi hua. Wo bas kisi aur ka ho gaya. Jahaaz chalte rahe. Par ab un jahaazon par is desh ke vyapaari nahi the.

Ab hum peeche lautte hain. Us cheez ki taraf, jo us raaste par gayi — aur jo aaj bhi wahan khadi hai.

## Part 9: Naam pehle

Kisi bhi raaste par sabse pehle maal jaata hai. Par yahan ek cheez maal se bhi pehle gayi. Naam.

Yahan ke jo bhi raja uthe, unke naam poore Bharatiya the. Sanskrit ke. Iska matlab ye nahi ki wo log poore Bharatiya the. Iska matlab ye hai ki wo Indianized ho gaye the — yaani unki raaj-reet, unki bhasha, unke pad, sab is desh ke dhang ke ho gaye the.

Doosra saakshya: rajdarbar. Rajya ke samaroh Bharatiya the, aur bhasha Sanskrit thi. Sarkar ke har afsar ke paas koi purana Sanskrit ka pad tha. Aur hairat ki baat: ye pad aur ye naam aaj tak chal rahe hain. Thailand mein bhi. Aur Malaya ke Musalmaan riyason mein bhi.

Teesra saakshya: sahitya aur kala. Indonesia ke purane sahitya Bharatiya puran-kathaon se bhare hain. Java aur Bali ke mashhoor naach Bharat se aaye. Bali — Indonesia ka wo chhota dweep — ne apni purani Bharatiya sanskriti aaj tak sambhali. Yahan tak ki Hindu dharm bhi wahan zinda raha. Aur likhne ki kala? Wo Philippines tak gayi.

Cambodia ko dekhiye. Uska lipi dakshin Bharat se aaya. Sanskrit ke shabd wahan gaye, halke badlaav ke saath. Aur kanoon? Cambodia ka kanoon is desh ke purane kanoon-granthakar Manu ke kanoonon par khada hai. Bauddh asar se kuch badlaav ke saath, wahi kanoon aaj ke Cambodian kanoon mein dhal gaya.

Aur naam? Champa. Angkor. Sri Vijaya. Majapahit — in sab jagah Sanskrit padhai ke bade kendra the.

Ek French vidvaan ne is poore ilaake par ek line likhi hai. Unka kehna hai: ye ek "Greater India" tha — rajnitik roop se utna hi dheela, jitna Alexander ke baad ka Greek jahan. Par mann se utna hi ek, utna hi ek-sura.

Nateeja? Yahan sirf mala aur dhupaal nahi gayi. Rajdarbar gaya. Bhasha gayi. Lipi gayi. Kanoon gaya.

Par sabse khaas cheez jo wahan gayi, wo patthar thi.

## Part 10: Patthar ki zubaan

Patthar se pehle ek jagah dekhiye.

Java mein imaaraton ka ek dher hai. Borobudur. Uska poora varnan ek vakya mein: wahan Buddha ki saari jeevan-katha patthar mein tarashi gayi hai. Aur doosri jagahon par deewaron par ubhare hue chitra — Vishnu ki katha, Ram ki, Krishna ki.

Nateeja? Ye kala wahan ja kar mari nahi. Wo wahan ki mitti mein utar gayi.

Ek angrez lekhak ne Angkor par likha hai: ye aaj duniya ka sabse bada ajooba hai. Patthar mein insaan ki pratibha ka ek shikhar. Aur phir unhone ek baat jodi: ye ek aisi sabhyata ke nishaan hain, jo chhah sau saal tak apne pankh faila kar chami — aur phir itni poori tarah mit gayi, ki uska naam bhi insaan ke hothon se mit gaya.

Ye tareef bahar se aayi hai. Iska kaam kisne kiya? In khandaron ki khudai European vidvaanon ne ki — khaas taur par French aur Dutch vidvaanon ne. Nehru kehte hain, itni badi imaaratein aur shehar abhi bhi zameen ke neeche pade honge. Aur Malaya ke kuch purane khandar? Wo khudai ke kaam mein, ya sadak ke liye patthar nikaalne mein, nasht kar diye gaye. Jang un khandaron par ek aur chot thi.

Aur ek chitthi. Kuch saal pehle ek Thai chhatra, jo Tagore ke Santiniketan aaya tha — Santiniketan, Tagore ka apna school — wapas jaate waqt likhta hai: main apne aap ko bahut bhagyashali maanta hoon ki main is purane desh, Aryavarta, aakar "dadi maa" Bharat ke charnon mein apna namaskar rakh saka. Jiski mamta mein meri maa-bhoomi pali, aur jise sikhlaya gaya ki culture aur dharm mein jo mahaan aur sundar hai, usse pyaar karna.

Nehru ispar ek saaf baat jodte hain. Aaj wahan tang raashtravad bahut hai. Europe ke raaj ka darr hai, aur nafrat bhi. Bharat ke liye kabhi-kabhi ek tuchchta bhi — kyunki Bharat khud gulaam hai. Par inse hatta kar, purani yaad zinda hai. Aur wo yaad ye hai: ek zamana tha jab ye desh in deshon ke liye maa tha.

Aur ye jaan-pehchaan hamari nahi hai. Hellenism bhi isi tarah phaila tha — Greece se Mediterranean ke deshon mein, aur pashchim Asia mein. Jaise wo asar wahan gaya, waise hi Bharat ka asar poore poorab mein.

[SFX: mandir ke patthar par ek chhini, aur door kuch ghantiyaan]

Aaj sochiye: log badalna aur mann badalna do alag kaam hain. Ek desh doosre desh par raaj kar sakta hai, aur uske mann mein kuch bhi na de. Aur ek desh, bina talwar, poori duniya ka dhang badal sakta hai.

## Part 11: Wahi muskaan

Ab wapas us chehre par. Angkor Vat.

Uske chaaron taraf ek vishaal ilaaka hai — bade-bade khandar. Naqli jheel aur talab. Nahrein. Un par pul. Aur ek bada dwar, jispar wahi chehra, bahut bada, tarasha hua. Ek pyaara, muskurata, par rahasyamayi Cambodian chehra — jaise kisi devta ka chehra.

Wo muskaan. Angkor ki muskaan. Bar-baar wahi muskaan.

Aur uske paas hi ek aur mandir, Bayon. Ek vidvaan ne ise duniya ka sabse kalpana-bhara mandir kaha hai. Uski soch "is duniya ki" nahi lagti. Jaise koi mandir kisi doosre grah ke shehar ka ho.

Nateeja? Pehla jhatka Bharat se aaya. Par use aage badhaya Khmer logon ki apni pratibha ne. Ye dono mile, aur jo bana, wo na poora Bharat ka tha, na poora Cambodia ka. Wo ek teesri cheez thi. Us bade mandir ko banwaya ek raja — Jayavarman VII, ek theek-thaak Bharatiya naam.

Ek vidvaan ise aage le jaate hain. Unka kehna hai: jab Bharat ka haath yahan se hat gaya, tab bhi uski prerana bhooli nahi gayi. Khmer pratibha azaad hui, aur usne naye, bade vichaar gade — aise vichaar, jo shudh Bharatiya mahaul mein ban hi nahi sakte the.

Par wo ek baat yahan bhi jodte hain. Khmer sanskriti ki jadein Bharatiya prerana mein hain. Bina us prerana ke, Khmer log zyada se zyada wahi bana paate, jo Central America ke Maya logon ne banaya — bas chamak-dhaar, aur kuch nahi. Par ye prerana aisi upjaau mitti par giri.

Aur ab is katha ka sabse zaroori jumla.

Nehru isi par rukte hain. Unka kehna hai: wahi pehli aag Bharat mein dheere-dheere bujh gayi. Kyunki mann aur mitti, dono thak gaye. Naye vichaar aane band ho gaye.

Phir unka vyakyaa. Jab tak Bharat ne apna mann khula rakha — bahar apni daulat di, aur bahar se wo liya jiski use zaroorat thi — tab tak wo taaza, mazboot aur zinda raha. Par jitna zyada usne khud ko bachane ke liye apne khol mein ghusna shuru kiya, utni hi uski wo aag dheemi padi. Uska jeevan ek sookhe, bematlab chakkar mein badal gaya — sab kuch mare hue ateet ke aas-paas ghoomta raha. Aur jin bachchon ne sundar cheez banane ki kala kho di, wo usse pehchaan bhi nahi paate.

Ek French vidvaan ne poorab mein is phailav ko ek line mein baandha: Persia se Chini samundar tak, Siberia ki barf se Java aur Borneo ke dweepon tak — is desh ne apne vishwas, apni kathayen aur apni sabhyata phailayi. Insaan ki chauthai nasal par aisi chhaap chhodi, jo aaj tak miti nahi.

To aaj ke sawal ka jawab?

Bharat samundar ke us paar apna naam le gaya. Apni zubaan. Apna kanoon. Apne devta. Aur apna patthar. Aur ye kaam sadiyon tak chalta raha — terah sau saal, jab tak samundar wala raasta is desh ke haath mein tha. 1511 mein wahi raasta kisi aur ka ho gaya.

Jo bahar gayi cheez apni jagah khadi hai. Angkor mein. Borobudur mein. Par uski jarh? Wo wapas is desh mein sookhne lagi thi.

Aur wahi muskaan — Angkor ki muskaan — bahar roshan rahi, jab andar diya dheema pad raha tha.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Ek cheez samundar ko aasani se paar karti hai. Talwar nahi. Vichaar. Ek naam. Ek lipi. Ek mandir. 1511 ke baad is desh ke jahaaz us raaste par nahi gaye. Par us raaste par jaane wali cheezein aaj bhi khadi hain — Cambodia ke kanoon mein, Bali ke mandir mein, Java ke naach mein, Thailand ke padon mein.

Aur ek doosri baat bhi hai, jo thodi kadi hai. Jo log bahar gaye, unhone wahan khud ko maalik nahi bataya. Wo apne desh ka naam, apni zubaan aur apne devta le gaye — aur wahan ke logon ne unhe apna bana liya. Ek sabhyata ko lamba chalane wali cheez yahi hai. Naqsha badalna aasan hai. Mann badalna nahi.

To aaj ye poochhiye: hum aaj samundar ke us paar kya bhej rahe hain? Aur jo bhej rahe hain, use wahan ke log apna bana paate hain?

Sochiye.

## Outro

Jo cheez bahar gayi, wo patthar mein khadi hai. Par is desh ne apne haathon se kya banaya? Deewarein, jinpar auratein hain. Dhaatu, jo talwar se zyada mazboot thi. Dawa. Aur ek chhota sa nishaan — shunya.

Agli baar hum ghar mein lautenge. Dekhne ke liye ki jab uske jahaaz door-door tak jaate the, tab usne apne haathon se kya banaya.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
