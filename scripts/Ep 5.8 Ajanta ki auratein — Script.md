## Ab tak

Ab tak: hum samundar ke us paar the. Wahan is desh ke haathon se bane patthar khade the — Angkor ka muskurata chehra, jo baar-baar dohraya gaya. Us safar ke ant mein ek sawal bacha tha: jo cheez bahar gayi, wo patthar mein khadi hai. Par is desh ne apne haathon se kya banaya? Aaj hum ghar lautte hain.

## Part 1: Aadha itihaas

Ajanta se nikalte hain. Aur Nehru ki pehli baat hairan karne wali hai.

Is desh ki sabse acchi kala shayad is desh mein hi nahi hai.

Ek angrez puratatvavid ne likha tha: Bharat mein kala ko sirf Bharat mein dekhna, uski aadhi kahani jaanna hai. Poori kahani ke liye aapko Buddha dharm ke peeche chalna padega. Central Asia tak. China tak. Japan tak. Tibet, Burma, Siam, Cambodia, Java tak. Har desh mein us kala ko ek nayi nasal ka hunar mila, naya mahaul mila, aur uske asar se uska roop badalta gaya.

Aur is taraf? Is taraf bahut kuch mit gaya. Khaas taur par uttar Bharat ke purane mandir aur murtiyan — sau-sau saal ke hamle, toot-phoot, aur bhoolne ki aadat.

Ab ek chhoti si baat samajhni zaroori hai. Yahan ki kala dharm aur darshan se itni bandhi hui hai ki usse alag karke dekhna mushkil hai. Greece mein sundarta ke liye sundarta pyaari thi — usme khushi bhi milti thi, satya bhi. Is desh mein bhi sundarta pyaari thi, par usme hamesha kuch gehri baat daalne ki koshish thi: ek antardrishti, ek jhalak us satya ki, jo unhe dikhta tha.

Aur yahi wajah hai ki Europe ke purane girjaghar banane wale kaarigar is kala ke zyada kareeb lagte hain. Unki soch mein bhi dharm tha, unki nigah bhi is duniya se aage thi. Renaissance ke baad ke Europe ke kalakar is kala se door hain.

Isi wajah se yahan ki kala do tarah ke logon ko uljhaati hai. Jahan kala sabse oonchi hai, wahan aadmi ruk jaata hai, chahe use samajh na aaye. Par jahan kala chhoti hai, wahan ye na-samajhna ek deewar ban jaati hai — pehle ek halki bechaini, phir chidh, aur phir aisa lagta hai ki kalakar ko kaam hi nahi aata tha.

Nehru yahan ek imaandaar baat likhte hain. Main kala ka jaankaar nahi hoon. Main aise dekhta hoon jaise koi aam aadmi dekhta hai. Kuch cheezein mujhe khushi deti hain, kuch mujhe andar tak chhu jaati hain, kuch bas theek lagti hain, aur kuch se main bina rukey guzar jaata hoon.

Aur phir wo ek murti ka zikr karte hain. Ceylon, Anuradhapura ka ek Buddha. Wo murti unhe gehri tarah chhui, aur uski ek tasveer barson tak unke saath rahi. Par dakshin Bharat ke kuch mashhoor mandir — jinpar tarashai ki koi kami nahi — unhe bechaini se bhar dete hain.

To sawal ye hai: agar kala samajh mein na aaye, to hum uspar faisla kaise dein?

## Part 2: Yunani aankh se dekha

Is sawal ka ek jawab Europe ne do sau saal tak diya. Aur wo jawab galat tha.

Europe ke log Greek kala ki parampara mein pale-badhe the. Jab unhone is desh ki kala dekhi, to unhe ek cheez jaani-pehchani lagi — Peshawar ke aas-paas ki wo kala, jise Gandhara kehte hain, jismein Yunani chehre aur Bauddh katha ek saath hain. Unhone usi ko asli maan liya, aur baaki saari kala ko uski giri hui nakal.

Dheere-dheere nazar badli. Naya nazariya pehle mahadeep se aaya, England se nahi — aur wo baat thi: Gandhara khud kisi badi cheez ka peela pratibimb hai, jad nahi.

Nehru ek baat yahan jodte hain, jo apne desh ke liye bhi teekhi hai. Is kala ki qadar England se zyada mahadeep mein hui — aur ye baat Sahitya mein bhi sach hai. Wo sochte hain ki iska kuch rishta us rajniti se bhi tha, jo is desh aur England ke beech chal rahi thi. Par wo ye bhi likhte hain: ye khayi rajniti se gehri hai, aur wo dono taraf se gehri hoti gayi.

Is beech ek angrez lekhak ne is desh par ek kitaab likhi, jo India ke liye ghin se bhari thi. Usne likha: Bharat ka khayal, apne saare ajoobon ke baawajood, use ghinona lagta raha. Aur Hindu kala par, uske shabdon mein, ek chipchipi, ghinoni chhaap thi.

Nehru ek aur baat likhte hain. Un angrez buddhijeeviyon ki nazar mein is desh ka aadmi "paap ka ek khaas roop" lagta tha — aur uske har kaam par wahi daag.

Nehru ka jawab shaant hai. Unhe aisa lagta hai, to lagne ka haq unke paas hai. Main khud is desh ki bahut cheezon se ghin karta hoon — par poore desh se nahi. Kyunki main khud se nafrat nahi kar sakta.

Aur phir wo ek sawal poochte hain, jo baith jaata hai. Jinhe humne nuksaan pahunchaya hai, kya hum unse nafrat karte hain?

Do angrez vidvaan is kala ki izzat karne walon mein the. Unmein se ek ki baat gehri hai. Ek qaum ki kala us qaum ki soch aur kirdaar ka khaas izhaar hoti hai, aur use tabhi samjha ja sakta hai jab uske peeche ke aadarsh samjhe jaayein. Jo hukumat us soch ko samajhti nahi, wo soch ke khilaaf nafrat ke beej bo deti hai.

Usi ki doosri baat is episode ka mool hai. Ye kala kisi chuni hui pandit-mandali ke liye nahi bani thi. Iska kaam tha: dharm aur darshan ke bade vichaar aam logon tak pahunchana. Aur wo kaamyaab rahi.

Saakshya? Uska apna. Is desh ka kisaan pashchimi matlab mein padha-likha nahi hota — phir bhi apne varg ke sabse sanskrit logon mein gina jaata hai.

Aur kalakar ke liye ek reet thi: khud ko prakriti ke har mausam mein utaar dena. Insaan aur brahmaand ka mel. Yahi poore Asia ki kala ka mool-raag hai — isliye itni vividhta ke baad bhi Asia ki kala mein ek ekta hai.

Ab us deewar ki taraf lautte hain.

## Part 3: Ajanta ki auratein

[SFX: gaar ke andar gehri sannata; door paani tapakne ki dheemi boond; phir ek halka saans]

Ajanta ek doosri duniya hai. Sapne jaisi — par bilkul asli.

Yahan ka koi drishya kisi ek pal ka nahi lagta. Poori katha ek saath utar aayi hai — jaise kisi ne bade se parde par ek hi baar mein sab kuch likh diya ho.

In gaaron ko khodne aur in deewaron ko rangne ka waqt tha Gupta kaal: chauthi se chhathi sadi. Isi daur ke Bagh aur Badami bhi hain. Yahi wo waqt hai jise is desh ka sone ka daur kaha jaata hai — wahi Gupta, jinke baare mein hum is set ke shuru mein sun chuke hain.

Aur ek baat dhyan mein rakhiye. Is desh mein purani painting bahut kam bachi hai — sirf Ajanta ke wo pyaare frescoes. Shayad baaki sab mit gaya. Yahan ki asli chhaap murti aur imarat mein thi.

Aur in frescoes ka ek ajeeb nateeja hua, jo Nehru khud likhte hain. Unhe dekh kar aaj ke kalaakar itne mohan mein aa gaye ki wo jeevan se mooh mode kar Ajanta ki nakal karne lage. Nehru kehte hain: iska nateeja achha nahi raha.

Saatvi-aathvi sadi mein us se bhi badi cheez hui. Ellora. Wahan gaarein thos patthar mein khodi gayin, aur beech mein Kailasa mandir khada hua — ek poora mandir, ek hi chattan se, oopar se neeche, tarasha hua. Nehru kehte hain: ye sochna mushkil hai ki insaan ne aisa kuch pehle kalpana kaise kiya, aur phir apne haathon se usse aakar kaise diya. Aur Ellora ki ek hi pahadi mein Bauddh, Hindu aur Jain, teeno ki gaarein ek doosre ke paas khodi gayi hain. Sochiye kitne haath lage honge, aur kitne saal.

Aur in deewaron par ek poora shehar hai — bazaar, mahal, gaadiyan, jahaaz, jhoola, baithak. Ye kala kisi aise aadmi ki kala nahi thi, jo duniya se mooh mod kar baith gaya ho. Ye un logon ki kala thi, jo duniya ko dekhna pasand karte the.

Elephanta ki gaarein bhi isi daur ki hain, aur dakshin mein Mamallapuram ki imaaron ka poora jhund. Elephanta mein gehri Trimurti hai — ek hi chattan mein ek hi murti ke teen roop.

Aur wahan ek tooti hui murti hai. Shiv, naachte hue. Ek kala-samalochak ne likha: tootne ke baad bhi wo shaan ki kalpana hai, titaan-shakti ka roop. Us chattan mein naach ki laya hai, phir bhi us sir par wahi shaant bhaav hai — jo Buddha ke chehre par hota hai.

British Museum mein us jaisi ek aur murti hai. Ek aadhunik shilpi ne uspar likha: yahan prem aur maut ka bhayanak saar hai, aur hamari European kahaniyan iske saamne bekaar lagti hain.

Par in sab mein sabse pyaara chehra kahan hai? Wo is desh mein nahi hai.

## Part 4: Ek sir, teen jagah

Java. Borobudur.

Wahan Buddha ki poori jeevan-katha patthar mein tarashi gayi hai. Aur doosri jagahon par deewaron par ubhare hue chitr — Vishnu ki katha, Ram ki, Krishna ki. Kala wahan ja kar mari nahi. Wo wahan ki mitti mein utar gayi.

Aur usi Borobudur se ek sir aaj Copenhagen ke ek museum mein rakha hua hai. Bodhisattva ka sir — Bodhisattva, yaani wo jo Buddha banne ki raah par hai.

Us sir par ek gehri baat hai. Wo sundar hai, saaf-suthre saundarya ke matlab mein. Par uske andar kuch aur bhi hai — wo chehra, jaise ek aaine mein, uski shudh aatma dikhata hai. Us chehre mein samundar ki gehrai ki shaanti hai, ek nirmal aasman ki shaanti, aur ek aisa sukh jo insaani shabdon se bahar hai.

Aur Java ki kala ki apni ek baat hai. Usme bhi wahi gehri shaanti hai. Par Elephanta aur Mamallapuram ki kadi tapa nahi. Java ki kala mein insaani santosh aur khushi zyada hai — ye us shaanti ka chehra hai, jo in logon ko apne pyaare dweep par mili, sadiyon ke sangharsh ke baad. Ek hi mool-raag, aur phir bhi do deshon mein do bilkul alag chehra — yahi is kala ki sabse badi hairani hai.

Ab ek shak, jise Nehru bhi rakhte hain. Is kala ka jo shuruaati naturalism hai — asli jeevan ki seedhi nakal — wo shayad China ke asar se aaya. China ka asar is kala ke kai mod par dikhta hai, khaas taur par is naturalism mein. Aur ye baat un sab ko hairan karti hai, jo sochte hain ki yahan ki kala sirf soch aur pratik ki kala thi.

Aur ulta bhi hua. Bharatiya aadarshvaad China aur Japan gaya, aur unke bade daur mein unhe andar se badal diya.

Aur ek baat: murti aur imarat is desh ka maidan thi, jaise painting China aur Japan ka. Sangeet mein bhi is desh ka apna ek dhang tha, jisne poorab se pashchim tak — Persia, Afghanistan, Arabia, Turkestan — Asia ke sangeet par chhapa chhoda. Nehru ko lagta hai ki ye sangeet aage chal kar un sab deshon mein saraaha jayega.

Par ye sab ek baat ko chhupa nahi sakta. Is desh mein murti-puja ka virodh tha. To phir ye murtiyan aayi kahan se?

## Part 5: Patthar mein bhagwan

Vedon ka saaf virodh tha. Murat ki pooja nahi.

Isi wajah se Buddha ke dharm mein bhi bahut der tak Buddha ka roop patthar mein nahi tarasha gaya. Log unki baat maante the, unke pichhe chalte the, par unka chehra nahi banate the.

Is desh ki kala ka kram yahi raha. Pehle updesh. Phir kitaabein. Aur roop sabse aakhir mein.

Aur ye der sirf saalon ki nahi thi. Us se pehle ki Bauddh kala mein Buddha ka chehra banaya hi nahi jaata tha — sirf unke chinh dikhte the: unka pag, unki chhatri, unka ped. Aaj ye humein ajeeb lagta hai — ek poora dharm, aur uske founder ka chehra do sau saal tak nahi bana.

Phir wo waqt aaya jab roop bana. Mathura ke museum mein ek bada patthar ka Bodhisattva hai — taaqat se bhara hua, bal se bhara hua. Patli kamar, chaudi chhaati, aur ek thahra hua chehra. Wo Kushan kaal ka hai, yaani Isa ke janm ke aas-paas ka. Mathura ke us patthar se ek darwaza khula, aur uske baad is desh mein murti ka doar kabhi ruka nahi.

Aur ab wo baat yaad kijiye jo humne is set ke shuru mein suni thi — Iran aur Greece wale episode mein. Purane Vedic dharm mein murti nahi thi. Mandir nahi the. Murat ki pooja is desh mein der se aayi, aur wo raasta Greece se aaya.

To jab wo roop aakhir bana, to usmein do cheezein mili. Ek, is desh ki gehri soch — jo kuch kehna chahti thi. Doosra, bahar se aayi wo reet, jismein bhagwan ko aakar dena padta hai.

To ek sawal khada hota hai: Mathura ke us patthar se Ajanta ki deewar tak ka raasta kisne banaya?

Nateeja? Haath kaam par lag gaye. Aur wo haath sirf mandir aur murti hi nahi bana rahe the.

## Part 6: Bazaar se duniya tak

[SFX: door samundar ki dheemi lehren; ek jahaaz ki rassi par khinchav]

Pehle hazaar saal mein is desh ka vyapaar door-door tak phaila hua tha. Bharatiya vyapaariyon ke haath mein bahut se videshi bazaar the.

Poorab ke samundar par unka raaj tha, aur unka haath Mediterranean tak pahunchta tha. Kaali mirch aur doosre masale is desh se — ya is desh hote hue — pashchim jaate the. Kuch jahaaz Bharatiya the, kuch Chini. Rome ka ek hamlavar apne sang teen hazaar pound kaali mirch le gaya tha. Aur Rome ke lekhak ye likh kar dukhi hote the ki sona Rome se nikal kar is desh ki taraf ja raha hai — badle mein aish-aaraam ki cheezein aati hain.

Ye vyapaar asli mein dene-lene ka vyapaar tha. Jo cheez yahan mili, wo bahar gayi. Is desh ki mitti upjaau thi, aur kuch cheezein aisi thi jo aur kisi ke paas nahi thi. Jo cheez uske paas nahi thi, wo wo doosron se le leta tha. Samundar khula tha — aur khula samundar ek tarfa nahi hota.

Aur kapda? Kapda is desh mein sabse purane zamane se banta tha — jab doosre desh abhi seekh rahe the. Yahan ek poora kapde ka udhyog khada ho gaya, aur Bharatiya kapda door-door ke deshon tak gaya. Resham bhi yahan bana, par China ka resham behtar tha; wo chauthi sadi BC se yahan aane laga.

Ek cheez ne is vyapaar ko hawa di: rang. Kapde par aise rang chadhane ke tareeqe yahan khoje gaye jo chhutte nahi the. Unmein ek nila rang tha, jiska naam hi is desh se aaya — Greece ke raaste duniya mein phaila, aur aaj bhi duniya use indigo kehti hai. Aise rang banane ke liye phool, patte, jad aur keede — sab kaam aate the, aur unhe pakka karne ka apna hi tareeqa tha.

Sochiye iska matlab kya tha. Jo kaarigar rang banata tha, wo asal mein vyapaar ke darwaze khol raha tha. Kyunki door ke bazaar mein kapda tabhi bikta tha jab uska rang saalon tak na jaaye. Ye ek chhota sa hunar tha, par isi ne is desh ke kapde ko duniya ke bazaar tak pahuncha diya.

To kapda aur rang bik rahe the. Aur jo cheezein dikhti nahi, par bina unke na kapda banta, na talwar?

## Part 7: Dhaatu aur dawa

[SFX: ek dhaatu par chhoti chot; phir bhatti ki dheemi aanch]

Un hazaar saalon mein is desh ka rasayan-shastra bahut aage tha — shayad us waqt ke kisi bhi desh se aage. Rasayan us zamane mein do cheezon se juda hua tha: dhaatu ke kaam se, aur us ajeeb vidya se jo sona banane ki koshish karti thi.

Us daur mein ek naam mashhoor hai — ek rasayan-vid aur dhaatu-shastri. Uska naam Nagarjuna tha.

Aur yahi naam us zamane ke ek bade darshanik ka bhi tha. Kuch logon ne dono ko ek hi aadmi maan liya. Nehru kehte hain: ye shak wali baat hai. Par naam ka milna hi bata deta hai ki us daur mein dhaatu aur soch kitne kareeb rakhe jaate the.

Faulad ki baat kijiye. Faulad ko sakht karne ka tareeqa yahan bahut pehle maloom tha. Bharatiya loha aur faulad bahar mashhoor tha — khaas taur par jang ke liye. Yaad hai Dilli ke paas wo loha, jo itne saalon baad bhi zang nahi khaya? Us jaisi dhaatu yahan se bahar jaati thi, aur wahan talwar banti thi.

Aur dhaatu sirf talwar ke liye nahi thi. Bahut si dhaatuen yahan jaani jaati thi, aur dhaatuon ke yog banakar dawaen banaayi jaati thi. Bhaap se cheezein alag karne ka tareeqa, aur jalane ka tareeqa — dono maloom the.

Aur dawa? Kaafi aage thi. Wo zyadatar purani kitaabon par khadi thi, par tajurba bhi hote rahe, aur aage ke waqt tak hote rahe. Shareer ki banaawat aur uske andar ka kaam padha gaya. Aur khoon ka daur — wo baat yahan bahut pehle kahi gayi thi, us doctor se sau-sau saal pehle, jiske naam se aaj Europe us khoj ko yaad karta hai.

Iska matlab ye bhi tha ki is desh ke logon ke paas dhaatuon ki aisi samajh thi, jo bahar ke logon ko nahi thi. Wahi samajh auzar banane mein kaam aayi, wahi jang mein, wahi dawa mein. Isi wajah se yahan ke lohaar aur sunaar bahar ke kaarigaron se alag kaam kar sakte the: unke paas dhaatuon ke milaap ka gyaan tha, aur aag ko kaabu karne ka tareeqa tha. Rasayan, dhaatu aur dawa — teeno ek hi kaam ke teen chehre the.

Itne purane rasayan ka poora hisaab lagane ke liye ek badi kitaab bhi likhi gayi hai — kyunki wo gyaan itne jagah bikhra hua tha ki usse ek jagah jama karna khud ek zindagi ka kaam tha.

## Part 8: Aakash ki ginti

[SFX: raat ka aangan, door jhingur; phir ek halki ghanti]

Sabse purana vigyan kaunsa hai? Khagol-shastra — taaron aur grahon ka hisaab. Yahan wo vishwavidyalaya ke paathyakram ka ek niyamit vishay tha.

Par uske saath ek doosri cheez chipki hui thi: jyotish. Taaron ka hisaab aur taaron se bhavishya — dono ek hi thaali mein rakhe jaate the.

Aur us hisaab ne ek cheez banayi: ek calendar. Bahut sahi calendar. Wo calendar aaj bhi logon ke kaam mein hai. Wo sooraj ka calendar hai, par mahine chaand ke; isliye beech-beech mein jod-ghata karna padta hai.

Ye kaam zyadatar Brahmins ka tha. Wo tyohaar tay karte the. Aur grahan ka samay pehle se bata dete the — sooraj ya chaand ka grahan bhi ek tyohaar ki tarah mana jaata tha.

Nehru yahan ek teekhi baat likhte hain. Ye jaankari un logon ke paas thi. Aur unhone is jaankari ka istemaal janta mein aise vishwaas aur riti-riwaz badhane ke liye kiya, jinke baare mein wo khud jaante the ki wo andhvishwas hai. Isse unki apni izzat badhi.

Aur samundar par jaane walon ke liye? Unke liye aasman ka hisaab kaam ki cheez tha. Taron se raasta milta tha. Us daur ke Arab khagol-shastra se bhi rishta tha, jo zyadatar Alexandria par khada tha. Aur is desh ke log apne taaron ke gyaan par ghuroor bhi karte the.

Aur ye gyaan sirf punditon ke paas nahi tha. Jahaaz yahin bante the, saahil par; aur unhe chalane wale log aasman ko padhna jaante the. Boai ka waqt, tyohaar ka din, samundar ka raasta — teeno ek hi hisaab se nikalte the.

Aur ek baat dhyan mein rakhiye: taaron ka hisaab bina ginti ke nahi hota. Aur ginti mein bhi yahan kuch hua, jo aage chalkar poori duniya ka hisaab badal dega.

Aur machine? Kitni aage thi, ye pakka nahi. Par jahaaz banane ka kaam phalta-phoolta udhyog tha, aur machine ka zikr baar-baar aata hai — khaas taur par jang ke liye. Isi wajah se kuch zyada josh wale log tarah-tarah ki jaadui machineyan soch baithe hain.

Itna pakka hai: auzar banane aur chalane mein, rasayan mein, aur dhaatu ke gyaan mein ye desh kisi se peeche nahi tha. Isi liye ye sadiyon tak videshi bazaar par qaabu rakh paaya.

Par in sab ke peeche ek cheez thi, jise koi nahi ginta tha. Haath.

## Part 9: Ghulaam nahi the

Wo baat thi: is desh par ghulaami ka bojh nahi tha.

Sochiye, ye kitni badi baat hai. Greece aur us zamane ki doosri badi sabhyataon ki tarakki ko ghulaami ne roka hua tha — bahut saara kaam unke haath se nahi, ghulaamon ke haath se hota tha. Is desh par wo bojh nahi tha.

Iska matlab ye hua ki yahan kaam karne wale haath ghulaam nahi the, aur kaam ke andar hunar paida hone ki jagah thi. Kapde ka udhyog, dhaatu ka kaam, imarat, rang, dawa — in sab mein log peedhiyon tak ek hi kaam sikhate the, usme maharat paida karte the, aur us maharat se wo cheezein banate the jo bahar bikti thi.

Iska naqsha aise samajhiye: kapda bunne walon ka ek ghar, rang banane walon ka doosra, dhaatu garhne walon ka teesra, patthar tarashne walon ka chautha. Chaaron ke andar apna hunar peedhiyon tak chalta tha, aur chaaron ek doosre ke kaam se bandhe hue the — bina ek doosre ke koi kaam poora nahi hota tha.

Aur yahan Nehru ek baat likhte hain, jo unki apni hai — kitaab ka tathya nahi. Jaati-pratha, apne saare doshon ke saath, ghulaami se kayi guna behtar thi. Ye un logon ke liye bhi sach tha jo sabse neeche the. Uska kaaran ye tha ki ek jaati ke andar barabari thi, aur kuch azaadi thi.

Ab ye baat dhyan se rakhiye — hum uspar aakhir mein lautenge.

Hunar tha, vyapaar tha, haath the. To ginti mein is desh ko aage jaana hi tha. Aur usne ginti mein aage ja kar kya kiya?

## Part 10: Das nishaan

[SFX: lakdi ke takhte par chhote nishaane banane ki halki khurak]

Pehle ek baat, jise Europe ne saikdon saal tak nahi maana.

Europe ko apni ginti Arabon se mili. Isi liye aaj bhi unhe "Arabic numerals" kaha jaata hai. Par Arabon ne khud wo ginti is desh se li thi. Naam galat jagah baith gaya.

Aur sabse ajeeb baat? Ye log sochne mein itne aage the ki ginti mein unhe aage jaana hi tha. Nehru yehi likhte hain: jo log itne sochak the, unse ummeed ki jaati thi ki wo ganit mein sabse aage honge.

Ek French vidvaan ne, Napoleon ke zamane mein, is par likha. Unke shabdon ka saar ye hai: ye is desh ne humein diya — das nishaan, jismein har nishaan ki apni ek keemat hai, aur ek keemat uski jagah ki bhi. Itna aasaan ki hum uski asli ahmiyat bhool jaate hain. Aur wo jodte hain: ye khayal purane Greece ke do sabse bade ganit-shastriyon se bhi chuk gaya.

Ab wo cheez dekhiye jise ye das nishaan hatakar laaye. Us waqt tak log ginti ke liye lakdi ka takhta istemaal karte the, aur ank likhne ke liye Roman jaise nishaane. In dono ne tarakki ko saikdon saal roka hua tha. Phir ye das nishaan aaye — aur insaan ka dimaag un bandhanon se azaad ho gaya. Aaj ye nishaane hamein itne aam lagte hain ki hum unhe kuch bhi nahi samajhte. Par unke andar ek poore naye daur ke beej the.

Aur ye das nishaan itne aasan the ki ek baar dekhne ke baad bachcha bhi ginti kar leta tha. Isi asaani ne unhe duniya bhar tak pahuncha diya.

To ginti, geometry aur algebra — teeno ke janm is desh mein bahut purane zamane se jude hain. Shuruaat shayad vedik vedi banane se hui. Purani kitaabon mein ek niyam milta hai: ek varg ko ek aayat mein badalna, jiski ek bhuja tay ho. Aur aise jaamitri ke aakar aaj bhi Hindu riti-riwaz mein kaam aate hain.

Ek sach bhi hai. Geometry mein Greece aur Alexandria aage nikal gaye. Par ginti aur algebra mein is desh ne batti banaye rakhi — aur wahi batti aage chal kar poori duniya ke kaam aayi.

Aur us batti ka sabse bada kaam ek chhote se nishaan par khada tha. Aisa nishaan, jise dekh kar aap ko lagta hai ki wahan kuch hai hi nahi.

## Part 11: Khaali jagah ka nishaan

[SFX: tel ke diye ki lau halke se kaanpti hai; phir sannata]

Wo nishaan pehle ek bindu tha. Ek chhota sa nukta, jaise kisi ne zameen par ungli se bana diya ho. Phir wo bada hua — ek chhote gol ki tarah. Aur uska naam pada: shunya. Yaani kuch nahi.

Ab ise kisne khoja? Iska jawab Nehru saaf dete hain: kisi ka naam nahi milta. Is khoj ke karne wale ka pata nahi.

Jo sabse purana shunya ka nishaan ab tak mila hai, wo ek dharm-granth mein hai — lagbhag Isa se do sau saal pehle. Aur jagah ke hisaab se ank likhne ka tareeqa, yaani ek nishaan ki keemat uski jagah se badalna, wo shayad Isa ke janm ke aas-paas shuru hua.

Isi wajah se ek ganit-shastri ne likha: is nishaan ki ahmiyat ko kabhi badha-chadha kar nahi kaha ja sakta. Khaali hawa jaisi cheez ko ek naam dena, ek nishaan dena, aur usme kaam karne ki taaqat bhar dena — ye is desh ke logon ki khaas baat hai.

Ek doosre ganit-shastri ne ise aise dekha: paanch hazaar saal tak ginti ke auzaar mein ek bhi sudhaar nahi hua; phir us anjaan aadmi ka kaam duniya bhar ki ghatna ban gaya.

Aur ek sawal bade-bade ganit-shastriyon ko chubhta raha. Greece ke sabse bade ganit-shastri is nishaan tak kyun nahi pahunche?

Ek vidvaan ka jawab: Greece kaam-kaaj ke vigyan ko halka maanta tha. Wahan bachchon ko padhane ka kaam bhi ghulaamon par chhod diya jaata tha. To jo desh duniya ko geometry de kar itna aage gaya, wo algebra ka shuruaati roop kyun nahi bana paaya?

Iska jawab ek British vidvaan dete hain, aur ye jawab is episode ka sabse kaam ka jawab hai. Wo kehte hain: agar aap tarakki ko kuch gine-chune pratibhashali logon ki roshni samajhkar dhoondenge, to sawal kabhi hal nahi hoga. Asli cheez wo samaaj hai, jo sabse bade dimaag ko bhi apne ghere mein rakhta hai.

Yaani jo yahan hua, wo kisi ek bhramit pratibha ki pal-bhar ki chamak nahi thi. Wo us waqt ke samaaj ka paida kiya hua tha. Ek zaroorat thi.

Aur wo zaroorat dikhti bhi hai. Us daur ki Sanskrit ganit-kitaabein dekhiye. Wo bhari hui hain vyapaar aur rishton ki mushkil samasyason se. Kar, rin, sud, saajhedari, adla-badli, aur sone ki shudhta ka hisaab. Samaaj bada aur ulajha ho gaya tha, aur hazaaron log sarkari kaam aur door ke vyapaar chala rahe the. Aasaan tareeqe ke bina ye sab chal hi nahi sakta tha.

Ab us gaar mein lautte hain.

Wo diya wahi ruka hua hai — us khaali jagah par, jahan koi rang nahi. Us khaali jagah ne ginti badal di.

To jawab ye hai. Jis desh ki deewarein, dhaatu aur dawa duniya bhar mein bikte the, usne apna sabse bada tohfa banaya — wo cheez jo dekhne mein kuch bhi nahi thi. Ek chhota sa nishaan, jise khaali jagah dikhane ke liye likha jaata hai. Us nishaan ne ginti ke darwaze khol diye. Aur yahi is katha ka sabse bada sabak hai: is desh ne jo sabse bada diya, wo patthar mein nahi, khaali jagah mein likha tha.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli. Aaj humne suna ki is desh par ghulaami ka bojh nahi tha, aur jaati-pratha ghulaami se behtar thi. Wo Nehru ka apna vichaar tha, kitaab ka tathya nahi — aur unhone wo 1944 mein likha. Us baat ko aaj ke samay par rakhiye. Ek vyavastha jo apne ghar ke andar sab ko jagah deti hai, par ghar ka darwaza bahar se band kar deti hai — wo apne logon ki raksha kar sakti hai, par unhe aage nahi badha sakti. Aur jo hunar darwazon ke peeche band reh jaata hai, wo dheere-dheere kho jaata hai.

Doosri. Aaj bhi wahi sawal khada hai, jo us daur mein khada tha: padhaai kis tak pahunchti hai? Us nishaan ko khojne ke liye ek poora samaaj chahiye tha — vyapaari, kaarigar, munshi, kar wusool karne wale, sab ginti kar rahe the. Jab padhaai sirf kuch gharon tak simat jaaye, to khoj bhi simat jaati hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aapke aas-paas koi aisa hunar hai, jo kisi ke paas hai — par use seekhne ka darwaza band hai? Aur agar wo darwaza aap khol sakte hain, to aap kiske liye kholenge?

## Outro

To ek nishaan bacha tha: shunya.

Usne ginti ke darwaze khol diye — bhinn aaye, rin aaya, anant khada hua. Par ye nishaan yahin nahi ruka.

Wo ghar se nikal gaya. Baghdad ke ek khalifa ne kitaben mangwayi, aur wahan us nishaan ko ek naya naam mila: Hindsa. Us nishaan ko yahan se nikal kar wahan pahunchne mein sadiyan lag gayi — aur usi raaste par aage Europe ka darwaza khula.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
