## Cold open

[SFX: chhat par ek purana pankha, dheere-dheere ghoomta hua; neeche kisi machine ki halki kharkhadahat]

Madras. Ek daftar. Dopahar.

Ek lambi mez par ek khula register. Aur register ke saamne ek aadmi baitha hai.

Umar kam. Kapde sadhe hue. Aankhon mein wo thakan, jo neend se nahi jaati.

Uska kaam hai ginti likhna.

Jahaaz ka maal aaya? Tole likho. Maal gaya? Tole likho. Roz ka kharcha? Likho. Register ke panno par ank chalte jaate hain — ek ke peechhe ek, jaise koi lamba silsila, jiska koi ant nahi.

[SFX: kalam kagaz par ruk-ruk kar sar-sarahati hai]

Shaam ko register band ho jaata hai. Aur tab ye aadmi apni jeb se ek chhoti si diary nikalta hai.

Daftar ke numbers register mein rehte hain. Diary mein wo numbers likhta hai jo uske apne hain.

Kuch aise, jo usne khud se poochhe hain. Kuch aise, jinka jawab kisi kitaab mein nahi milta. Wo unhe raat bhar likhta rehta hai. Aur agli subah kuch panno ko lifafe mein daal kar, ek pate par bhej deta hai.

Pata England ka.

Ab sochiye. Ek aadmi, jise theek se padhne ka mauka hi nahi mila. Din bhar doosron ka hisaab. Aur raat mein apne sawal — wo sawal, jinpar us waqt duniya ke sabse bade ganit-shastri kaam kar rahe the.

Yahan, is daftar ke ek kone mein, wahi cheez baithi thi jo is desh ne hazaar saal pehle khoji thi. Ginti ka woh nishaan, jo dekhne mein kuch bhi nahi lagta.

Ek taraf wo nishaan duniya bhar ka hisaab badal chuka tha. Doosri taraf, uske paida hone ki jagah par, ek clerk apni diary mein akele likh raha tha.

[SFX: lifafa ek lakdi ke dabbe mein girta hai; tanpura dheere se uthta hai]

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 5.9: Shunya, aur phir dopahar — Chapter 5 ke is set ka aakhri episode.

Aaj ka sawal ek hi hai, aur wahi is poore set ka sawal bhi hai. Itni chamak ke baad — mandir, ganit, samundar paar phaili hui bastiyan — is desh par thakaan kahan se aayi?

Aur ek chhoti si cheez aaj humein raasta degi. Ek khaali safed kagaz par ek chhota sa gol bana dijiye. Itna chhota, ki wo kuch bhi nahi lagta. Us gol ko is desh ne khoja tha. Aur usi gol ne duniya ka hisaab badal diya.

To chaliye.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Jawaharlal Nehru wahan qaid mein is desh ka ateet likh rahe hain. Is set mein humne Gupta kaal ki kala dekhi, samundar paar ke patthar dekhe, aur ek chhota sa nishaan — shunya. Pichhli baar wo nishaan ghar se nikal gaya tha. Aaj dekhte hain: wo kahan pahuncha, aur peeche ghar mein kya hua.

## Part 1: Shunya, woh khaali jagah

[SFX: ek sookhi lakdi ke takhte par halki thap, jaise koi ek nukta rakhe; phir sannata]

Sabse pehle wahi khaali jagah.

Dhyan dijiye: ye koi khel nahi tha. Ye roz ka hisaab tha — khet ka, jahaaz ka, dukaan ka.

Ek ank ki keemat do tarah se hoti hai. Uska apna matlab, aur uski jagah. Bees likhiye — do, aur shunya. Do sau likhiye — do, aur do shunya. Wahi do ank, par jagah badalte hi keemat badal gayi.

Is khayal par Europe ke bade ganit-shastri sadiyon tak nahi pahunche. Yahan pahunche. Aur jab is khayal ne darwaza khola, to andar jo aaya, wo dekhiye.

Bhinn-ank aa gaye. Aadha, chauthai, teen-chauthai. Aur unka guna, unka bhaag. Ek vyapaari ke liye iska matlab saaf tha: aadhi gathri, aadha munafa, aadha hissa.

Phir aaya teen cheezon ka niyam. Do cheezein pata hon, to teesri khud nikal aati hai. Aaj bhi har dukaan par, har hisaab-kitaab mein, yahi niyam chal raha hai — bas kisi ko uska naam yaad nahi.

Phir warg aur wargmool. Aur wargmool ko apna nishaan mila. Ghan aur ghanmool. Ghataane ka chinh. Sine ki tables, taaron ki ginti ke liye. Aur pi ki keemat — teen point ek chaar ek chhe.

Aur algebra mein akshar aa gaye. Jo cheez hamein nahi pata, uske liye ek akshar. Samikaran khade hone lage.

Aur phir shunya ka apna ganit likha gaya. Aaiye, ek kagaz par likhiye.

A mein se a ghataiye — shunya. A mein shunya jodiye — wahi a. A ko shunya se gunaiye — shunya.

Aur ab a ko shunya se bhaagiye.

Jawab? Anant.

Ise sochiye. Kuch nahi se bhaagna. Jawab kuch nahi nahi tha. Jawab tha — sab kuch.

Aur ek din ganit ko ek naya nishaan chahiye pada. Kisi ne poochha: chaar ka wargmool kya hai? Jawab do bhi ho sakta hai, aur ghata do bhi. To usne ek nishaan banaya — ghataane ka.

Ye sab ek hi cheez se aaya. Ek chhota sa gol, khaali jagah ke liye rakha gaya.

To sawal ye hai: ye log kaun the? Jinhone ye darwaze khole? Unke naam kahan hain?

## Part 2: Naam ki kataar

Kataar lambi hai. Aur purani.

Sabse pehle aati hain wo kitaabein, jinka rishta ginti se kam, naap se zyada tha. Teen naam — Baudhayana, Apastamba, Katyayana.

In teen kitaabon mein warg, aayat aur tribhuj ke sawal hain. Yaani wo ganit, jo vedi aur mandap banane mein kaam aata tha. Nehru ek jagah likhte hain ki aise naap ke niyam aaj bhi Hindu samaroh-mandap banane mein kaam aate hain.

Phir algebra ki sabse purani bachi hui kitaab aati hai.

Uske likhne wale ek khagol-shastri the — taaron aur grahon ke vidvaan. Naam: Aryabhata.

Aryabhata ka janm 476 Isvi mein hua. Aur ye kitaab unhone likhi, sirf teis saal ki umar mein.

Kuch log unhe algebra ka aavishkarak kehte hain. Nehru saaf likhte hain: nahi. Wo bhi apne se pehle walon ke kaam par khade the. Ganit kisi ek aadmi ki bijli nahi hoti — ye baat is episode ke ant mein laut kar aayegi.

Uske baad ek aur naam. Dedh sau saal baad, Brahmagupta. Ye bhi khagol-shastri the — aur inhone wo niyam likhe, jo shunya ke aage-peechhe ghoomte hain.

Phir ek lambi kataar hai. Par aakhri sabse bada naam — aur sabse pyaara naam — Bhaskara II ka hai.

Unka janm 1114 Isvi mein hua. Unhone teen kitaabein likhin: khagol-shastra, algebra, aur ank-ganit.

Unki ank-ganit ki kitaab ka naam hai — Lilavati.

Ek ganit ki kitaab, aur naam ek aurat ka? Ye ajeeb hai. Par kitaab kholiye: andar baar-baar ek jawan ladki se baat hoti hai. Usse kaha jaata hai, "Hey Lilavati." Aur phir uske saamne ek sawal rakh diya jaata hai.

Mana jaata hai — bina kisi pakke saakshya ke — ki Lilavati Bhaskara ki beti thi.

Aur kitaab ki bhasha? Saaf. Saral. Itni saral, ki aaj bhi Sanskrit ke pathshalaen ise padhati hain — sirf ganit ke liye nahi, bhasha ke liye bhi. Ek pita, aur uski beti ka naam, ek ganit ki kitaab ke panno par.

Par ek baat dhyan se dekhiye.

Saat sau saal ki ye kataar thi. Paanchvin sadi se baaraahvin sadi tak. Uske baad bhi kitaabein aati rahin — Naraayan ne likha, Ganesh ne likha. Par wo sirf wahi dohrai thi, jo pehle ho chuka tha.

Baaraahvin sadi ke baad, is desh mein ganit par naya kaam lagbhag band ho gaya.

Ruka kyun? Ye sawal hum aakhri hisse ke liye bacha kar rakhenge. Par pehle ek doosri jagah chalte hain — kyunki jab ye kataar chal rahi thi, tab is ghar ki sabse badi cheez ghar chhod chuki thi. Aaiye, saat sau saal peechhe.

## Part 3: Hindsa

[SFX: door ek dheemi ghanti; uske neeche kagaz ki halki sar-sarahat]

Aathvin sadi. Baghdad.

Us waqt Baghdad duniya ke sabse bade gyaan-kendron mein se ek tha. Wahan Khalifa Al-Mansur ka raaj tha, aur uske zamane mein — saat sau pachaas se lekar saat sau chauhattar tak — is desh se kuch vidvaan Baghdad pahunche.

Unke haath mein kitaabein thin. Ganit ki. Khagol-shastra ki.

Isse pehle bhi ye ginti wahan pahunch chuki thi — par ye pehli baar tha ki wo ek poore tareeke se gayi. Kitaabon ke saath. Naam ke saath. Logon ke saath.

Aryabhata ki kitaab, aur doosre vidvaanon ki kitaabein, Arabic mein anuvaad ki gayin. Aur Baghdad mein us waqt Greek aur Jewish vidvaan bhi jama the — apne saath Greek darshan, geometry aur vigyaan laaye hue. Ab wahan do dhaaraen ek jagah miltin hain.

Nateeja? Baghdad ki chhaap poore Musalmaan sansaar par phaili — Central Asia se Spain tak. Aur us chhaap ke saath ye ginti bhi.

Wahan ise ek naya naam mila. Arabon ne inhe kaha: "Hind ke nishaan."

Aur ginti ke liye jo Arabic shabd bacha, wo tha — Hindsa. Uska matlab hi hai: Hind se aaya hua.

Par rukiye. Baghdad mein ye ginti ek din mein nahi pahunchi. Uske pehle ki ek chhoti si gawahi maujood hai — aur wo is desh se nahi, ek doosre hi desh ke ek math se aati hai.

Isvi ke chhe sau baasath — yaani is Baghdad wale din se lagbhag sau saal pehle. Furaat nadi ke kinare ek math hai. Wahan ek Shaami saadhu rehta hai — aur wo apni shikayat likh raha hai.

Uski shikayat ye hai: kuch Greek vidvaan Shaam ke logon ko chhota samajhte hain.

Aur apni baat saabit karne ke liye wo ek doosre hi desh ka udaharan deta hai. Uska kehna hai: agar wo log, jo sirf is wajah se apne aap ko sabse aage maante hain ki wo Greek bolte hain, hinduon ke hisaab-ganit ko jaante hote, to unhe pata chalta ki ilm sirf unke paas nahi hai.

Uska hisaab, likhta hai wo, bayaan se bahar hai. Aur wo kaam nau nishaano se hota hai.

Sochiye, kitni door pahunch chuki thi wo cheez. Ek purani gaar mein, ek saadhu ke lifafe mein, kisi ke Greek ghamand ke khilaaf ek dalil ban chuki thi.

To ye ginti Baghdad se aage badhi — duniya ki taraf. Par aage jaane se pehle, Europe ke darwaze par do thapede lagte hain.

## Part 4: Europe ke darwaze

Baghdad ke Arab sansaar se ye naya ganit Europe pahuncha — shayad sabse pehle Spain ke un vaishwavidyalayon ke raaste, jahan us waqt Arab raaj tha. Aur jahan wo gaya, wahan wo Europe ke ganit ki neev ban gaya.

Par Europe ne ise turant gale nahi lagaya.

Ek shikayat thi: ye nishaan begane dharm ke nishaan hain. Aur jo begana ho, wo shak ke saath dekha jaata hai. Isliye inhe apne hisaab mein utarne mein saikdon saal lage.

Iska matlab ye nahi tha ki log ginti nahi karte the. Karte the — par apne purane tareeke se. Aur ek tareeka, jo poori zindagi chalta aaya ho, use chhodne mein waqt lagta hai. Nishaan khud nahi chalte. Log chalte hain — aur log dheere badalte hain.

Sabse purana saakshya jahan milta hai, wo ek sikka hai. Sicily ka sikka. Saal: gyarah sau chautees.

Ek sikka sochiye. Uska kaam bada nahi hota — wo sirf ye batata hai ki kis ke raaj mein dhala gaya, aur kitne ka hai. Par is sikka par kuch aur bhi tha. Wohi nishaan. Shunya samet.

Aur Britain mein? Wahan pehla istemaal milta hai chaudah sau nabbe mein. Yaani is sikke se saadhe teen sau saal baad.

Is itni der ka matlab kya tha? Ye nahi ki nishaan kamzor the. Raasta pehle khula, phir nishaan aaye — aur wohi kram har jagah chala. Shunya pehle Baghdad pahuncha, Baghdad se Spain, Spain se Europe, aur Europe se poori duniya.

Par jis waqt ye ginti Europe ke darwaze khatkhata rahi thi, usi waqt yahan ek kamre mein ek aadmi baitha tha, aur usi ginti se khel raha tha — akele.

Aaiye, us kamre mein lautte hain.

## Part 5: Madras ka ek clerk

[SFX: kagaz ke panno ki halki sar-sarahat; door ek pankha]

Diary wale aadmi ka naam tha Srinivasa Ramanujan.

Uska janm dakshin Bharat ke ek gareeb Brahman parivaar mein hua. Theek se padhne ka mauka usse kabhi nahi mila. Aur jab jeene ka sawal aaya, to naukri mili — Madras Port Trust mein, ek clerk ki naukri. Bandargah ke daftar mein, ginti likhne wali naukri.

Par uske andar ek cheez chal rahi thi, jo rukne ka naam nahi leti thi. Wo daftar ka kaam khatam karta, aur khaali waqt mein numbers aur samikaranon ke saath khelne baithta.

Aur ek din ek ittefaq hua. Ek ganit-shastri ki nazar uske kaam par padi. Unhone uske kaam ke kuch panno ko England bhej diya — Cambridge. Wahan ke log hairan reh gaye.

Cambridge se scholarship aayi. Ramanujan ne apni clerk ki naukri chhodi, aur England chala gaya. Wahan bahut thode waqt mein usne bahut gehri, aur bilkul nayi tarah ka kaam kiya.

England ki Royal Society ne use apna Fellow bana liya.

Aur do saal baad wo chala gaya. Shayad TB se. Wo taintees saal ka tha.

Us waqt ke ek bade vaigyanik ne, kahin, usse is sadi ka sabse bada ganit-shastri kaha.

Nehru yahan ruk kar likhte hain. Ramanujan ki chhoti si zindagi aur maut, is desh ke haalaat ka ek nishaan hai.

Karodon log. Inmein kitne ko thodi si bhi padhai milti hai? Kitne bhookh ke kinare par jeete hain? Aur jinhe thodi padhai mil bhi gayi, unmein kitne aise hain jinke saamne kisi daftar mein clerk ki naukri hai — aur wo bhi itni kam tankhwah par, jo England ke unemployment dole se bhi kam ho?

Aur phir wo ek sawal rakhte hain. Agar is jeevan ne apne darwaze khol diye hote — khana, sehat, padhai, aur badh jaane ka mauka — to in karodon mein se kitne bade vaigyanik honge? Kitne shikshak, kitne lekhak, kitne kalaakar?

Sochiye. Ek desh jise shunya mila tha. Aur uske sabse chamakte bete ko apni pratibha dikhane ke liye duniya ke doosre kinare jaana pada.

Par ye kahani aaj ki nahi hai. Ye us thakaan ki kahani hai jo usse hazaar saal pehle shuru ho chuki thi. Ab hum mudte hain un sadiyon ki taraf.

## Part 6: Ek hazaar saal, kai utaar-chadhaav

Ye hazaar saal ek seedhi lakeer nahi the. Inmein utaar-chadhaav bhare hue the — aur isi wajah se aaj ka aakhri sawal itna mushkil hai.

Chaliye, shuru se chalte hain.

Isvi ki pehli sadi se lekar sauvi sadi tak: baar-baar ladaiyan. Andar ki gadbad. Bahar se aane wale log, sarhadon par dhakka dete hue. Ye koi shaant daur nahi tha.

Par in sab ke beech, ek cheez kabhi nahi ruki.

Zindagi. Josh. Wo sabhyata, jo har disha mein phailti hai — jaise paani, jo rukta nahi.

Sahitya, natak, darshan, kala, vigyaan, ganit — sab saath-saath khilte hain. Vyapaar badhta hai. Kshitij failta hai. Iran se, China se, Greek duniya se, Central Asia se rishte bante hain.

Aur ye phailav sirf kitaabon ka nahi tha. Ek nagar mein natak likha ja raha hai. Doosre mein darshan ki nayi tika tay ho rahi hai. Teesre mein ek mandir ka naqsha khinch raha hai. Aur kahin ek kavi apni pankti gun-guna raha hai. Ek hi waqt mein itni cheezein.

Aur ek pyaas, jo kabhi nahi bujhti: samundar ke us paar jaane ki. Usi pyaas se wo bastiyan banti hain, jinke patthar aaj bhi khade hain.

Aur is hazaar saal ke theek beech, chauthi se chhathi sadi ke beech, ek daur aata hai, jise aaj bhi is desh ka sunehri kaal kaha jaata hai.

Gupta kaal.

Us daur ki kitaabein padhiye. Unmein lafzon ke beech se ek cheez saaf sunai deti hai: apne aap par bharosa. Ek sukoon. Aur ek garv — garv is baat ka, ki us waqt zinda hona khud ek saubhagya hai.

Nehru ise ek naam dete hain: sabhyata ka high noon. Dhoop sar par. Roshni sabse tez. Aur us roshni ka poora zor is baat mein tha ki ek saath itne log soch rahe the, likh rahe the, banate ja rahe the.

Ye baat yaad rakhiye. Kyunki aaj ka poora aakhri hissa isi dhoop ka hissa hai.

## Part 7: Safed Hoon, phir bhi

Aur is dhoop ke bhadakne ke beech, kamzori ke pehle nishaan dikhne shuru ho gaye — sunehri kaal ke band hone se pehle hi.

Uttar-pashchim se Safed Hoon aaye.

Ek jhund. Unhe peeche dhakel diya gaya. Wo phir aaye. Phir dhakel diye gaye. Phir aaye.

Ye koi ek dhava nahi tha. Ye aandhi thi, jo laut-laut kar aati hai.

Dheere-dheere wo Uttar Bharat mein andar ghus gaye. Aadhi sadi tak unhone poore uttar par raaj kiya.

Phir ek bada prayaas hua. Gupt vansh ke aakhri bade raja ne, Central India ke ek shasak Yashovarman ke saath haath milaya — aur Hoonon ko bahar khada kar diya.

Jang jeet li gayi. Par jo bacha, wo aaram nahi tha.

Ye lambi ladai is desh ko raajnitik roop se thaka chuki thi, aur sainya roop se kamzor.

Aur ek gehri baat. Ye Hoon log is desh mein bas gaye. Wo ghul gaye — jaise is mitti ne har bahar wale ko ghola tha. Par is baar ek cheez ulti bhi hui: unka asar reh gaya. Purane Indo-Aryan aadarsh kamzor pad gaye. Unke baare mein purane varnan kehte hain ki unmein aisi nirmamta dikhti thi, jo is desh ki jang aur shasan ki tameezon ke khilaaf thi.

Ek doosri baat bhi. Is desh ne Hoonon ko bhi ghol liya. Par gholne mein is baar zyada waqt laga — aur usi der mein andar kuch badal gaya.

Chati sadi mein ek punarutthan hua — raajnitik bhi, sanskritik bhi. Us daur ka bada naam Harsha hai. Aur Ujjayini, jo Gupta kaal ki chamakti raajdhani thi, ek baar phir kala aur saanskritik kaam ka kendra ban gayi.

Harsha ka zikr humne pehle bhi kiya tha — uske darbaar tak ek Chinese yaatri pahuncha tha. Yaani us daur mein bhi is desh ke darwaze poori tarah band nahi hue the. Log aate the. Padhte the. Aur lautt kar apne desh mein is desh ka halka chhod jaate the.

Chamak lauti. Par ye chamak lambi nahi chali.

Aur uske baad wo cheez aayi, jiska koi tareekh nahi hai.

## Part 8: Dopahar shuru hoti hai

Kuch roshni to phir bhi baaki thi. Nauvin sadi mein ek raja ne Kanauj se poore uttar aur madhya Bharat ko ek raajya mein baandha, aur usi waqt sahitya mein ek naya punarutthan hua. Gyarahvin sadi ke shuru mein ek aur raja uthe — aur Ujjayini ek baar phir ek bade raajya ki rajdhani ban gayi. Wo vidvaan bhi tha, kalakaar bhi, khud kavi bhi. Uska naam itna bada ho gaya, ki aaj tak kahawaton mein chalta hai.

Par in saari chamakti jagahon ke beech, ek andar ki kamzori is desh ko pakad leti hai.

Uska koi tareekh nahi hai. Wo ek din ki ghatna nahi thi. Wo dheemi thi, raingti hui thi — aur wo uttar mein pehle aayi, dakshin mein baad mein.

Yahan ek ulti baat dhyan dijiye. Uttar mein raja the, darbaar the, raajdhaniyan thin. Phir bhi dakshin dheere-dheere zyada zaroori hone laga — raajnitik roop se bhi, sanskritik roop se bhi.

Kyun? Shayad isliye ki dakshin us lagataar ladai se bach gaya, jo uttar hamesha ladta raha. Aur shayad isliye ki bahut se lekhak, kalakaar aur bade shilpi, uttar ke bechain haalaat se bhaag kar dakshin chale gaye. Wahan taaqatwar raajya the, chamakte darbaar the, aur wo mauka tha, jo kahin aur nahi milta tha.

Uttar chhote-chhote raajyon mein bat gaya tha. Par jaan wahan bhi baaki thi.

Benares, hamesha ki tarah, dharm aur darshan ka dil tha. Ek baat yaad rakhiye: agar kisi ne naya vichaar diya, nayi vyakhya nikali, to use Benares aana padta tha — apne aap ko saabit karne ke liye. Kashmir bhi lambi der tak ek bada Sanskrit kendra raha.

Aur vidyapeeth khade the.

Sabse bada naam Nalanda. Us daur mein Nalanda ka matlab hi tha — tahzeeb ki sanad. Wahan padhna aasan nahi tha. Daakhla unhi ko milta tha, jinhone pehle ek sthaayi dar tak padhai kar li ho. Wo aakhri dar tha — sabse upar ki kaksha. Aur wahan China se, Japan se, Tibet se chhaatra aate the. Kaha jaata hai, Korea aur Mongolia se bhi.

Suniye, wahan kya-kya padhaya jaata tha. Dharm aur darshan — dono. Par uske saath kala ki ek poori kaksha. Imarat ka ek mehka. Dawa ki pathshala. Kheti ka vibhaag. Gaushaale, aur gaay-bail. Aur kaha jaata hai, wahan ki zindagi behas aur charcha se bhari thi.

Bahar tak jo sanskriti gayi, uska zyadatar kaam Nalanda ke vidvaanon ka tha.

Aur uske saath do aur naam aate hain. Vikramshila, aaj ke Bhagalpur ke paas. Aur Vallabhi, Kathiawar mein. Dakshin mein Amravati.

Itni kakshaen. Itne chhaatra. Itne aadmi, jo ek saath soch rahe the. To kami kahan thi?

## Part 9: Kitaben, aur aakhri naam

Aaiye, is tasveer ko thoda peechhe khade hokar dekhiye.

Ek hazaar saal apne ant ki taraf badhte hain. Aur door se dekhiye, to ek cheez saaf dikhti hai.

Ye ek sabhyata ki dopahar thi.

Subah ki roshni kab ki bujh chuki thi. High noon beet gaya. Ab dhoop dhal rahi thi.

Dakshin mein abhi bhi jaan thi, aur wo jaan kuch sadiyan aur chali. Samundar ke us paar, apni bastiyon mein, zindagi agle sahasrabdi ke beech tak josh se bhari rahi.

Par ghar ke andar dil dheema padta lag raha tha.

Pehle darshan. Aathvin sadi mein Shankara ke baad koi bada naam nahi aata. Aate hain tika-kaar, tark-kaar — ek lambi kataar. Par khoj ka tevar chala gaya. Dimaag ka adventure chala gaya. Uski jagah ek sookhi, sakht tark aur ek sooni behas aa gayi.

Aur dono dharm bigadte hain — Brahman dharm bhi, Bauddh dharm bhi. Puja ke gire hue roop upar aane lagte hain. Khaas taur par tantr-mantr ke kuch roop, aur Yoga ka bigda hua gyaan.

Phir sahitya. Aathvin sadi ke Bhavabhuti aakhri bade naam hain. Uske baad kitaabein likhi jaati rehti hain — bahut likhi jaati hain. Par unki bhasha jaise-jaise ulajhti jaati hai. Na soch ki taazgi, na lafzon ki.

Aur ganit mein? Bhaskara II aakhri bada naam hain. Wahi, jinki Lilavati se hum abhi mile hain.

Aur kala mein? Ek vidvaan ise thoda aur aage le jaate hain. Unke hisaab se Bharat ki kala ka sabse bada daur saatvin-aathvin sadi se chaudahvin sadi tak chala — theek waise hi, jaise Europe mein Gothic kala ka daur tha. Aur sohli sadi mein us purani kala ki banane ki shakti saaf kam hone lagi. Nehru kehte hain, mujhe nahi pata ye kitna sahi hai — par mujhe lagta hai ki kala mein bhi dakshin ne uttar se zyada der tak purani parampara sambhali.

Aur bahar jaane ka aakhri bada safar bhi dakshin se hua. Nauvin sadi mein. Uske baad Chola vansh gyarahvin sadi tak samundar par taaqatwar raha. Aur gyarahvin sadi mein usne Sri Vijaya ko takkar de kar hara diya.

Kitaben likhi ja rahi thin. Kakshaen bhare hue the. Patthar tarashe ja rahe the. To phir kami kahan thi?

## Part 10: Jarh kahan thi

[SFX: dhoop mein ek lamba, sust saans; door kuch pakshi]

Teen jawab diye jaate hain.

Ek: shayad ye sirf umar thi — wahi umar, jo sabhyataon par waise hi hamla karti hai, jaise insaan par. Doosra: shayad ye koi samundari lehar thi, jiska aage-peechhe ka dole chal tha, aur us waqt wo utar rahi thi. Teesra: shayad bahar ke hamle.

Ek Bharatiya darshanik likhte hain ki is desh ke darshan ne apna josh kho diya, kyunki pehle raajnitik azaadi gayi.

Aur ek French vidvaan likhte hain, ki Sanskrit sanskriti Bharat ki azaadi ke saath khatam ho gayi. Nayi bhashaen aayin, naya sahitya aaya. Usne aaryon ka ilaaka bhar diya, aur Sanskrit ek kone mein ja kar baith gayi — aur wahan usne ek paandit-wala lehjaa apna liya.

Nehru kehte hain: ye sab sach hai. Raajnitik azaadi ka jaana sanskritik patan ko laya — ismein koi shak nahi.

Par phir wo ek sawal ulta karte hain. Aur yahi is episode ki jarh hai.

Raajnitik azaadi gayi kyun?

Ek chhota desh kisi badi taaqat se dab sakta hai. Par Bharat jaisa bada desh — itni sabhyata wala desh — bahar ke kisi hamle se waise hi nahi jhukta. Wo tab tak nahi jhukta, jab tak andar se kuch sad na gaya ho.

Aur wo sadan, is hazaar saal ke ant mein, saaf dikhti hai.

Par rukiye. Yahi baat har sabhyata ke saath hoti hai — aur isi wajah se ye sawal itna mushkil hai.

Har sabhyata ki zindagi mein patan ke daur aate hain. Is desh mein bhi pehle aaye the. Aur is desh ne unhe jhela bhi tha. Wo phir utha tha. Kabhi thodi der apne khol mein ghus jaata tha, aur phir taaza josh ke saath bahar aata tha. Andar ek jaan hoti thi, jo naye sampark se khud ko naya kar leti thi — kuch aur, par ateet se judi hui.

To kya wo lachak, jo is desh ko baar-baar bachaati aayi thi, ab chali gayi thi? Kya uske pakke vishwas aur uske samaaj ki badhti hui sakhti ne uske mann ko bhi sakht kar diya tha?

Sochiye is line ko: agar zindagi badhna aur badalna band kar de, to soch ka badhna bhi band ho jaata hai.

Ek vidvaan ne likha tha: unki aankhen purane shabd padhti jaati thin, par unka dimaag un shabdon mein naye vichaar dekhta tha. Bharat badla — aur use pata bhi nahi chala.

Par jab soch apni taazgi kho deti hai, jab wo purane lafzon ko bas dohraane lagti hai, jab wo har nayi cheez se darti hai — to zindagi thahar jaati hai. Bandh jaati hai. Ek jail, jo usne khud banayi ho.

Isi tarah Rome doobta hai. Rome par uttar ke hamlavar chadhe — par usse bahut pehle, wo andar se phat chuka tha. Uska vyapaar sikud gaya. Karigari mari. Shehar chhote aur gareeb hote gaye, aur shaasan ke saare upaay haalat sudhaarne ki jagah usse bigadte gaye.

Bharat ka patan aisa dhamaakedaar nahi tha. Yahan koi ek din sab khatam nahi hua. Yahan ek cheez hui, jo shayad zyada darawni hai: dheere-dheere patan.

Aur is dheemi thakaan ki jarh kya thi?

Samundar ke us paar, apni bastiyon mein, ye log utne sakht nahi the. Wahan unhe badhne ka mauka mila, aur wahan wo chaar-paanch sau saal josh se bhare rahe.

Par yahan, is mitti par, ek hi cheez upar uthti hai — apne aap ko sabse alag rakhne ki soch. Aur wahi soch, is desh ki banane ki shakti ko khaane lagti hai.

## Part 11: Dopahar

To chaliye, aakhir mein is soch ko kholte hain.

Jab zindagi alag-alag khaneon mein kaat di jaati hai, aur har aadmi ke liye uska kaam pehle se tay ho jaata hai — to kya hota hai?

Kshatriya ka kaam, ladna. Aur baaki sab ka kaam — nahi ladna. Isi wajah se vyapaar bhi chhota kaam ban jaata hai: Brahman aur Kshatriya use neecha maante hain.

Aur padhai? Padhai upar walon ke paas reh jaati hai. Neeche walon ko sikhaaya jaata hai ki wo aage jhukein — unke liye darwaze band ho jaate hain.

Yaad hai Benares wali baat? Naya vichaar lekar wahan aana padta tha? Wo darwaza ab sabke liye nahi tha.

Is desh ke shehron ka vyapaar vikasit tha, aur karigari badhi hui thi. Par raajya ki banawat, kaafi hadd tak, jaageerdaari thi. Aur shayad ladai ke tareeke mein bhi ye desh peeche reh gaya.

Is haalat mein bada badlaav mumkin hi nahi tha — jab tak banawat badle, aur pratibha ke naye srot khulein.

Aur yahin jaati-pratha khadi thi.

Uske bahut gun the. Usne ek samaaj ko sadiyon tak bandhe rakha. Usne kachchi mitti par ek imarat khadi ki, jo hazaar saal tik gayi. Par usi imarat mein ek cheez bhi thi: apne vinash ke beej.

Kyunk jo imarat sthirta deti hai, wahi badhne se rokti bhi hai. Jis ghar mein har aadmi ka kaam pehle se tay hai, wahan naya kaam karne ka raasta nahi bachta. Hunar khada hota hai — par hamesha apne hi khane ke andar. Kaam virasat mein milne lagta hai, soch purani khaayi mein chalti jaati hai, aur naye tareeke apnane ki aadat chali jaati hai.

Aur jab tak wo banawat badhne ke raaste deti rahi, tab tak wo aage badhti rahi. Par jis din wo apni hadd par pahunchi, us din wo rukhi — aur phir peeche hatne lagi.

Nateeja? Patan har taraf hua. Soch mein. Darshan mein. Raajneeti mein. Ladai ke tareekon mein. Bahar ki duniya se rishte mein. Aur sabse upar — is desh ka vichaar khud chhota hone laga. Poora Bharat ek vichaar tha; ab wahi vichaar ghar, gaon aur giroh mein batne laga. Aur arthvyavastha sikudi.

Par Nehru ek aakhri baat jodte hain: phir bhi us purani banawat mein jaan baaki thi. Ek lachak, aur badalne ki salahiyat. Isi se wo bachi rahi, naye sampark se faayda uthaya, kuch tarakki bhi ki — par wo tarakki baandhi hui thi. Ateet ki zanjeerein uske pairon mein thin.

To ab wo sawal, jiske liye ye poora set khada tha: itni chamak ke baad, thakaan kahan se aayi?

[SFX: pankha dheere-dheere ghoomta hua; door ek daftar ki halki kharkhadahat]

Thakaan bahar se nahi aayi.

Darwaze band hone ki awaaz bahar se aayi thi. Par darwaza andar se band hua. Jab tak is desh ne diya aur liya, tab tak wo taaza raha, mazboot raha, zinda raha. Aur jis din usne khud ko bachane ke liye apne khol mein ghusna shuru kiya — jis din "apna" ka matlab "sirf apna" hone laga — us din wo aag dheemi padne lagi.

Aur wahi vyavastha, jisne is samaaj ko sadiyon tak saambhal kar rakha, wahi nayi pratibha ko upar aane ka raasta band kar baiti.

Pichhli baar ek British vidvaan ki line aayi thi: har sabhyata apne andar apna ant liye chalti hai — agar wo sirf chuni hui pratibha ko padhaye, aur jan-samuh ko nahi.

Isi liye ek aadmi, jo shunya khoj sakta tha, apna sabse tez dimaag lekar ek daftar ke kone mein baith gaya — aur diary bharta raha. Aur bahar, samundar ke us paar, duniya usi ginti se apna poora hisaab bana rahi thi.

Wahi diary. Wahi panna.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Do baatein.

Pehli: is episode mein ek hi cheez baar-baar laut rahi thi — kaun padh sakta hai, aur kaun nahi. Nehru ne zindagi ke darwaze kholne ka sawal 1944 mein poochha tha. Padhai ab bahut zyada logon tak pahunchi hai. Par sawal wahi khada hai: kitne bachchon ke liye darwaza sach mein khula hai, aur phaisla kaun karta hai ki kaun andar aayega?

Doosri: shunya. Aaj hum jo ank likhte hain, jo hisaab karte hain — unki jarh yahin hai. Aur jab wo cheez ghar se nikal gayi, to wo khuli hi chali gayi; usse kisi ne band nahi kiya.

Aur aaj wahi sawal laut kar aata hai: kitne log is desh mein apne kamre mein baithkar kuch aisa likh rahe hain, jo humein pata hi nahi? Unke liye humara darwaza khula hai ya nahi?

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aap apni zindagi mein ek aise darwaze ke paas khade hain — jo bandh hai, par aap hi usse khol sakte hain? To aap use kholenge kis ke liye?
