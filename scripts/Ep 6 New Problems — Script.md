## Cold open

Fatehpur Sikri. Solahvi sadi ka aakhri hissa. Badshah Akbar ke darbar mein Portugal ke kuch Jesuit paadri khade hain. Wo ek tohfa pesh karte hain: ek moti kitaab. Bible.

Par ye haath se likhi hui kitaab nahi hai. Ye chhapi hui hai. Har panna ek jaisa, har akshar ek saancha. Europe mein aisi kitaabein ab hazaaron ki ginti mein ban rahi hain.

Akbar apne zamane ke sabse jigyasu raajaon mein gine jaate hain. Unhe har cheez jaanni hai: dharm, jung, mashinein. Unke darbar mein Hindu, Musalmaan, Jain, Parsi aur Isai, sab behas karte hain.

Aur phir bhi, Akbar us kitaab ko palat-te hain aur rakh dete hain. Ye sawal nahi poochte ki ise chhapa kaise gaya. Koi chhapakhana nahi mangvaate. Koi kaarigar Europe nahi bhejte.

Teen sau saal baad, ek jail mein baithe Nehru isi pal par ruk jaate hain. Unke liye ye ek chhota sa pal hai, jisme ek bada sawal chhupa hai: Europe aage kaise nikal gaya, aur Bharat peeche kyun reh gaya?

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 6: Nayi samasyaein.

## Part 1: Registan se uthi lehar

Kahani Arab se shuru hoti hai. Ek registani desh, jiska itihaas mein tab tak koi bada naam nahi tha. Phir Paigambar aaye, bhaichaare ka sandesh aaya, aur kuch hi dashakon mein Arab log Asia, Africa aur Europe ke ek hisse tak phail gaye.

Nehru yaad dilate hain ki Arab sabhyata khaali zameen se nahi uthi thi. Islam se pehle bhi Yemen mein ek purani sanskriti thi, aur Arab vyapaari samandar paar China tak jaate the. Islam ne bas unme ek nayi aag aur naya yakeen bhar diya.

Damascus mein Arab-Syrian rang aaya. Phir Baghdad mein Iran ka asar pada, aur ek Arab-Irani sabhyata bani. Arabon se josh aur sawal poochne ki aadat aayi. Irani logon se kala aur shauq.

Ab ek hairat ki baat. Arab saal 712 mein Sindh tak aaye, aur wahin ruk gaye. Nehru maante hain ki shayad Bharat tab bhi itna mazboot tha ki aage badhna mushkil tha.

Par rishte bane. Bharat ki ganit aur jyotish ki kitaabein Baghdad pahunchi aur Arabi mein anuvaad hui. Ek dilchasp kissa hai. Khalifa Harun-al-Rashid beemar pade to Bharat se Manak naam ke ek vaidya bulaye gaye. Manak Baghdad mein hi bas gaye aur ek bade aspataal ke mukhiya bane.

Nehru yahan ek kami ko pakadte hain. Arabon ne Bharat se bahut seekha. Bharat ne Arabon se lagbhag kuch nahi. Hum apne khol mein band rahe. Baghdad ka wo khula, tark wala mahaul shayad Bharat ke thake hue man ko jaga deta.

Aur Islam? Wo Bharat mein pehle dharm ki tarah aaya, talwar ki tarah nahi. Vyapaari aaye, maszidein bani, kisi ne virodh nahi kiya. Raajnaitik taaqat banne se kai sadiyan pehle Islam yahan ek dharm ke roop mein bas chuka tha.

Phir Mongol aaye. Chengiz Khan, jinhe Nehru shayad itihaas ka sabse bada senapati maante hain. Ek mazedaar baat: Chengiz Musalmaan nahi the. Wo aasmaan ki pooja karte the, aur Nehru andaaza lagate hain ki un par Bauddh dharm ke kisi bigde roop ka asar tha. Yaani itihaas ka sabse bada vijeta shayad ek tarah ka Bauddh tha.

Mongol Sindhu nadi par ruk gaye. Par unhone Baghdad ka wo sunehra daur khatam kar diya. Baad mein Samarkand aur Herat mein kala lauti, par sawal poochne wali Arab aadat nahi lauti.

## Part 2: Ghazni se Delhi tak

Arabon ke Sindh aane ke baad lagbhag teen sau saal tak Bharat par koi hamla nahi hua. Phir, saal 1000 ke aas-paas, Afghanistan ke Ghazni se Mahmud aaya. Wo Turk tha.

Uske hamle kai baar hue, aur har baar khoon-kharaba hua aur khazana loot kar le jaaya gaya. Us zamane ke vidwan Alberuni ne in hamlon ka dard likha hai, aur ye bhi ki logon ke man mein kitni gehri kadwahat baith gayi.

Par Nehru tasveer ko sahi naap mein rakhte hain. Mahmud sirf uttar Bharat ke ek hisse se guzra. Madhya, poorv aur dakshin Bharat poori tarah bache rahe. Dakshin mein tab Chola samrajya tha, jiske jahaaz Java aur Sumatra tak jaate the. Kashmir ne Mahmud ko hara diya. Somnath se lautte hue Rajasthan ke registan mein bhi use karaari maar padi, aur wo phir nahi aaya.

Nehru ki nazar mein Mahmud dharm ka aadmi kam, lutera zyada tha. Ek saboot dekhiye. Usne Bharat mein ek fauj banayi, jiska senapati Tilak naam ka ek Hindu tha. Aur ye fauj usne Central Asia mein apne hi hum-mazhab Musalmaanon ke khilaaf ladayi.

Asli nuksaan ye tha ki teen sau saal tak shaanti se aaya Islam ab pehli baar talwar ke saath juda dikha. Logon ko naye dharm se dikkat nahi thi. Bharat to pehle se Syrian Isaiyon, Yahudiyon aur Parsiyon ka bhi ghar tha. Dikkat thi us zabardasti se, jo zindagi ka dhang ulat de.

Mahmud ki maut 1030 mein hui. Phir 160 saal se zyada chain raha. Uske baad Afghan Shahab-ud-Din Ghuri aaya. Delhi ke raja Prithviraj Chauhan ne use buri tarah hara diya. Agle saal Ghuri nayi fauj ke saath lauta, aur 1192 mein Delhi ki gaddi par baith gaya.

Prithviraj aaj bhi geeton ke nayak hain. Unhone Kannauj ke raja Jaichand ki beti ko uske hi mahal se, swayamvar ke beech, utha liya tha. Prem jeet gaya, par do bade raajyon mein dushmani ho gayi, aur dono taraf ke sabse bahadur log aapas mein kat mare. Nehru ki baat ka saar: ek prem kahani ki keemat Delhi ne chukayi. Phir bhi geet Prithviraj ke gaaye jaate hain, aur Jaichand ko gaddaar jaisa maana jaata hai.

## Part 3: Afghan jo Hindustani ban gaye

Delhi jeetne ka matlab poora Bharat jeetna nahi tha. Dakshin tak Afghan raaj phailne mein dedh sau saal aur lage.

Aur yahan Nehru ek fark samjhate hain, jo is chapter ki jaan hai. Angrez Bharat mein hamesha baahri rahe. Unka dil aur unki tijori London mein thi. Afghan aise nahi the. Unhone Delhi ko apni rajdhani banaya, Ghazni ko nahi. Unhone yahan shaadiyan ki. Alauddin Khilji ne ek Hindu rani se byaah kiya. Firoz Shah Tughlaq ki maa Hindu thi. Moroccan yaatri Ibn Battuta ko Delhi duniya ke sabse bade shehron mein lagi.

Phir 1398 mein Timur aaya. Kuch hi mahine ruka. Par jahan se guzra, wahan kati hui khopdiyon ke dher chhod gaya. Delhi murdon ka shehar ban gayi, aur saltanat toot gayi.

Dakshin mein do bade raajya pehle hi khade ho chuke the: Gulbarga ka Bahmani raajya, aur Hindu Vijayanagar. Bahmani raajya baad mein paanch hisson mein bata. Unme ek tha Ahmadnagar, wahi qila jahan Nehru ye kitaab likh rahe hain. Iske sansthapak Ahmad Nizam Shah ke dada Bhairu naam ke ek Brahmin munshi the. Yaani Chand Bibi, Ahmadnagar ki veerangana, ki ragon mein mila-jula khoon tha. Dakshin ke saare Musalmaan raajya poori tarah Hindustani the.

Vijayanagar us waqt khoob phal-phool raha tha. Europe se aaye yaatriyon ne uski shaan ki tareef likhi.

Aur uttar mein? Ek aur hamlavar pahadon se utra. Panipat ke maidan mein, jahan Bharat ki kismat kai baar tay hui hai, usne 1526 mein Delhi jeet li. Naam tha Babar, Timur ka vansh. Yahin se Mughal daur shuru hota hai.

## Part 4: Mila-jula rang

Nehru ek galatfehmi saaf karte hain. "Musalmaan hamla" ya "Musalmaan daur" kehna galat hai. Waise hi jaise Angrezon ke aane ko "Isai hamla" kehna galat hoga. Islam to sadiyon pehle aa chuka tha. Hamle Turkon ke the, Afghanon ke the, Mughalon ke the.

Is milan ka asar do tarah ka hua, aur dono ek-doosre ke ulte the. Ek taraf log dakshin ki taraf bhaage. Jo reh gaye, wo apne khol mein sikud gaye, aur jaati ke niyam aur sakht kar diye. Doosri taraf, dheere-dheere, bina soche, ek mel shuru hua. Nayi imaaratein bani. Khana aur kapde badle. Sangeet naye raaston par chala. Farsi darbar ki bhasha bani, aur uske shabd aam boli mein ghul gaye.

Yahan Nehru achanak aaj mein kood jaate hain, aur parde ki baat chhedte hain. Unhe koi shak nahi ki purdah Bharat ki giravat ke bade kaaranon mein hai. Ye aurat ko to nuksaan pahunchata hi hai, aadmi ko bhi, aur us bachche ko bhi jo parde ke peeche pal-ta hai. Congress ke andolan ne hazaaron auraton ko ghar se bahar nikala, aur Gandhiji is pratha ke kattar virodhi rahe. Nehru yahan Gandhiji ke kuch shabd quote karte hain.

Wapas madhyakaal mein. Ye mel zyadatar upar hua, darbar aur shehron mein. Phir bhi kuch jagah khaas chamki. Jaunpur ki chhoti saltanat mein Hindi ko badhava mila. Kashmir mein Sultan Zain-ul-Abidin ne Sanskrit ki padhai ko sahara diya.

Isi daur mein do awaazein uthi, jo Hindu aur Musalmaan ke beech ki deewar par sawal karti thi. Banaras ke julaahe Kabir, jinke dohe aaj bhi har ghar mein bole jaate hain. Aur Punjab mein Guru Nanak, jinse Sikh panth shuru hua. Dono ne kaha ki Ishwar ek hai, aur jaati ke bhed bekaar hain.

Aur phir Amir Khusrau. Ek Turk, jiska parivar peedhiyon se Uttar Pradesh mein basa tha. Chaudahvi sadi ke aadmi. Farsi ke bade shaayar, Sanskrit ke jaankaar, aur bade sangeetkaar. Kehte hain sitaar unhi ki den hai. Unhone ginaaya ki Bharat kin cheezon mein sabse aage hai: dharm, darshan, ganit, sangeet. Aur aam. Haan, aam bhi us list mein hai.

Par Khusrau ki asli shohrat unke lok-geeton se hai. Unhone kitaabi bhasha nahi chuni, gaon ki boli chuni. Barsaat, dulhan ki vidaai, judaai ke geet. Saawan mein jab aam aur peepal par jhoole padte hain, to aaj bhi ye geet gaaye jaate hain. Nehru hairaan hain: chhe sau saal purane geet, ek shabd badle bina, aaj bhi logon ki zubaan par.

## Part 5: Gaon, jaati aur parivar

Ab Nehru ruk kar ek bada sawal uthate hain. Puraana Bharatiya samaj tika kis par tha?

Unka jawab: teen khambhon par. Apne aap chalne wala gaon. Jaati. Aur sanyukt parivar. Teeno mein ek baat saajhi thi. Samooh pehle, vyakti baad mein.

Gaon ki baat ek dasvi sadi ki kitaab se samajh aati hai: Shukracharya ka Nitisara. Isme gaon ki panchayat zameen baant-ti hai, lagaan ikattha karti hai, aur sarkar ka hissa khud pahunchati hai. Kuch purane shilalekh batate hain ki sadasya chune jaate the, committee mein auratein bhi baith sakti thi, aur hisaab na dene par sadasya hataya ja sakta tha. Ek niyam to aaj bhi kaam ka hai: sadasyon ke kareebi rishtedaar sarkari pad nahi le sakte the.

Bina shaahi parwane ke koi sipahi gaon mein ghus nahi sakta tha. Nitisara kehta hai ki praja kisi afsar ki shikayat kare to raja praja ka saath de, kyunki kursi ka nasha sabko chadhta hai. Aur pad dete waqt kaam aur charitra dekho, jaati aur khaandan nahi.

Ab jaati. Shuru mein ye kaam ke hisaab se bane samooh the, Europe ke guild jaise. Har samooh apne riwaaz ke saath jee sakta tha. Aur jo kisi samooh mein fit na ho, wo sanyaasi ban jaata. Ek ulatpher bhi hai. Samaj samooh ko maanta tha, par dharm vyakti ko. Moksh har jaati ke liye khula tha. Isliye jaati ke khilaaf awaaz uthane wale zyadatar dharm-sudhaarak the.

Zameen ka "maalik" koi nahi tha. Kisaan ko jotne ka haq tha. Fasal ka bada hissa kisaan ka, lagbhag chhatha hissa raja ka, aur baaki pujari, lohar, badhai, kumhar, naai, safai wale, sabka hissa. Gaon ka har samooh fasal ka hissedaar tha.

Par Nehru andhere ko bhi saaf dekhte hain. Shuddhta ki zid ne chhua-chhoot ko janm diya. Saath khana-peena band hua. Jo log zaroori par "gande" maane gaye kaam karte the, wo achhoot bana diye gaye. Ye cheez us had tak gayi, jaisi duniya mein kahin nahi dekhi gayi.

Teeno khambhon ka maqsad tha suraksha aur sthirta. Tarakki maqsad nahi thi, isliye tarakki ruk gayi. Haan, har samooh ke andar loktantra tha. Nehru ko hairat hoti thi ki anpadh kisaan bhi committee mein baithne ko kitna utsuk hota tha.

Par ye band samaj dheere-dheere aur sakht hota gaya. Smriti granthon mein kartavyon ki lambi soochi hai, adhikaaron ki ek bhi nahi. Aur Nehru ka nateeja ye hai ki jaati ko pasand ya naapasand karne ka sawal ab bacha hi nahi. Arthvyavastha use neeche se khod rahi hai. Sawal ye hai ki is badlaav ko sahi disha kaun dega.

## Part 6: Babar aur Akbar

Babar Bharat mein khush nahi the. Unhe Ferghana ki barf yaad aati thi, wahan ke phal aur phool, aur dosto ke saath lambi baatein. Phir bhi unhone maana ki Hindustan ek shaandaar desh hai. Chaar saal ke andar unki maut ho gayi. Nehru unhe Renaissance ka ek asli rajkumar kehte hain: bahadur, kala aur kitaabon ka shauqeen.

Par unke pote Akbar aur bhi badi shakhsiyat the. Bahadur, phir bhi narm-dil. Sapne dekhne wale, aur kaam karne wale bhi. Unke darbar ke Portuguese paadriyon ne unki aankhon ki chamak ko dhoop mein chamakte samandar jaisa likha.

Akbar ne 1556 se lagbhag pachaas saal raaj kiya. Unka sapna tha ek Bharat, sirf naqshe par nahi, dil se ek. Unhone Rajput rajkumari se shaadi ki. Unke bete Jahangir aadhe Rajput the. Shah Jahan ki maa bhi Rajput thi. Is tarah Turk-Mongol vansh khoon se zyada Hindustani ho gaya.

Sirf ek aadmi jhuka nahi: Mewar ke Rana Pratap. Unhone jungle mein bhatakna chuna, par kisi baahri ko salaam nahi kiya.

Akbar ke aas-paas kamaal ke log the: Faizi aur Abul Fazl, Birbal, Raja Man Singh, Abdul Rahim Khankhana. Darbar har dharm aur har naye khayal ka adda ban gaya. Kattar maulvi naraaz hue. Akbar ne ek naya, sabko jodne wala dharm bhi chalane ki koshish ki. Nateeja ye ki Hindu aur Musalmaan dono mein wo barabar lokpriya rahe.

## Part 7: Wo chhapakhana jo nahi aaya

Ab wapas cold open wale sawal par.

Akbar ko mashinon mein dilchaspi thi. Jesuit paadri likhte hain ki wo sab kuch ek saath seekhna chahte the, jaise bhookha aadmi ek hi kaur mein poora khana nigal le. Phir bhi teen jagah wo chook gaye.

Pehli, top-khana. Mughal fauj Ottoman Turki ke visheshagyon par tiki thi. Top-khane ke mukhiya ko "Rumi Khan" kaha jaata tha. Kisi ne apne log seekhne baahar kyun nahi bheje?

Doosri, chhapai. Bible haath mein aayi, par sawal nahi utha.

Teesri, ghadi. Portuguese aur baad mein Angrez spring wali ghadiyan laaye. Amiron ke ghar mein shaan ki cheez ban gayi. Kisi ne ghadi khol kar nahi dekha ki ye bani kaise hai. Aur ye us desh mein hua, jahan duniya ke behtareen kaarigar the.

Nehru kehte hain ye beemari sirf Bharat ki nahi thi. Arabon ke paas kabhi Damascus aur Baghdad mein mashhoor ghadiyan thi, wo hunar gayab ho gaya. Ottoman Turkon ke darwaze par Europe mein kitaabein chhap rahi thi, phir bhi unhone sadiyon tak chhapakhana nahi apnaya. Darr ye tha ki Quran chhapa to panne pairon tale aa sakte hain. Misr mein pehla chhapakhana Napoleon laya.

Udhar Europe mein Renaissance ne purani zanjeerein todi, aur amal se seekhne ki soch aayi. Nehru yahan Bacon aur Sir Thomas Browne ko quote karte hain. Akbar ki solahvi sadi mein Europe ne gati ka vigyaan paaya, aur unneesvi sadi tak ek nayi duniya bana di. Asia insaan ki mehnat par chalta raha.

Kyun? Nehru ka jawab saaf hai. Sabhyata baahri hamle se kam, andar ki thakaan se zyada girti hai. Bharat ki giravat Turk aur Afghan hamlon se pehle hi shuru ho chuki thi. Ye do purani sabhyataon ka milan tha, aur dono thaki hui thi. Aur Akbar apni saltanat jodne mein itne vyast the ki samaj ki jad tak unki nazar nahi gayi. Unke baad wo khulapan bhi tham gaya.

## Part 8: Saajhi sanskriti

Akbar ki imaarat itni mazboot thi ki kamzor waariso ke bawajood sau saal tiki. Har badshah ke baad bhaiyon mein gaddi ki jung hoti thi. Phir bhi Agra aur Delhi mein aisi imaaratein bani jinme Bharatiya kala aur nayi saadgi mili. Aur Agra mein, pyaar se, Taj Mahal khada hua.

Islam ke aane ne Hindu samaj ki burayiyan bhi ujaagar ki: jaati ka patthar ho jaana, chhua-chhoot. Bhaichaare ki baat un logon ko kheench gayi jinhe barabari kabhi nahi mili thi. Zyadatar dharm-parivartan neechi jaatiyon mein hue, khaas kar Bengal mein.

Kashmir ka kissa sochne layak hai. Wahan lagbhag 95 pratishat log Musalmaan ban chuke the, par purane riwaaz nibhate the. Unneesvi sadi ke beech mein wahan ke Hindu raja ko pata chala ki bahut log wapas Hindu banna chahte hain. Unhone Banaras ke panditon se poocha. Panditon ne mana kar diya. Baat wahin khatam.

Gaon mein Hindu aur Musalmaan sach mein saath jeete the. Ek hi kaam, ek hi kapde, ek hi boli, saajhe tyohaar aur lok-geet. Hindu Musalmaanon ko bas ek aur jaati samajhte the. Upar ke amiron mein bhi mel tha, par auraton ke beech nahi, kyunki parda unhe alag rakhta tha.

Sahitya mein mel saaf dikhta hai. Hinduon ne Farsi mein kitaabein likhi. Musalmaan vidwanon ne Sanskrit ka Farsi mein anuvaad kiya aur Hindi mein kavita likhi. Malik Muhammad Jayasi ne Padmavat likha. Aur Abdul Rahim Khankhana, Akbar ke senapati, Hindi ke behtareen kavi. Unhone Rana Pratap ki tareef mein likha, jo unki hi fauj se lad rahe the. Dushman ki izzat, yahi Akbar ki seekh thi.

## Part 9: Ghadi ulti chalaane ki koshish

Shah Jahan ka zamana. France mein Louis chaudahve ka. Udhar Versailles ban raha tha, idhar Taj Mahal, Delhi ki Jama Maszid, Diwan-e-Aam aur Diwan-e-Khas. Mayur Singhasan wala darbar Versailles se bhi zyada shaandaar tha. Par dono ki neev gareeb logon par thi. Gujarat aur Dakkhan mein bhayanak akaal pada.

Isi waqt Aurangzeb ne apne pita Shah Jahan ko qaid kar ke gaddi li. Nehru kehte hain ki sirf Akbar jaisa koi is waqt ko samajh paata. Aurangzeb ne ulta kiya. Wo kattar the, kala aur sahitya se door. Hinduon par phir se jaziya laga diya, kai mandir tudwaye. Rajputon ko naraaz kiya, jo saltanat ke khambhe the. Punjab mein Sikh, jo ek shaant panth the, zulm se ek ladaaku bhaichaara ban gaye. Aur pashchim mein Marathon ko chheda, theek tab jab unme ek kamaal ka neta uth raha tha.

Arthvyavastha bhi chatak rahi thi. 1669 se Delhi ke paas Jat kisaan baar-baar uthe. Satnami bhi uthe: sunaar, badhai, safai wale, mochi. Nehru ke liye ye naya tha. Ab tak bagawat raja aur nawab karte the. Ab aam log kar rahe the.

Aur phir Shivaji. 1627 mein janme. Pahadi logon ke guerrilla neta. Unke ghudsawaar Surat tak pahunche, jahan Angrezon ki kothi thi, aur door-door tak chauth vasool ki. Nehru unhe ubharti Hindu rashtriyata ka prateek maante hain. Unhone Marathon ko ek jhande ke neeche joda. 1680 mein unki maut hui, par Maratha taaqat badhti rahi.

## Part 10: Marathe, Angrez, aur ek kamzor Bharat

Atharahvi sadi mein Bharat ke liye chaar daavedaar the. Do desi: Marathe, aur dakshin mein Haidar Ali aur Tipu Sultan. Do videshi: Angrez aur Francisi. Lagta tha Marathe hi Mughalon ki jagah lenge. 1737 mein wo Delhi ke darwaze tak aa gaye.

1739 mein Iran se Nadir Shah aaya. Delhi loot-kar Mayur Singhasan tak le gaya. Mughal badshah ab sirf parchhaai reh gaye. Phir bhi riwaaz ki taaqat dekhiye: East India Company 1835 tak unke naam ke sikke chalati rahi. Aur Afghanistan Bharat se hamesha ke liye alag ho gaya.

1761 mein Panipat mein Ahmad Shah Durrani ne Marathon ko kuchal diya. Wo sambhle, Peshwa ke neeche Scindia, Holkar aur Gaekwad ka ek sangh bana. Par waqt nikal chuka tha.

Kyunki Bengal mein 1757 mein Plassey ho chuka tha. Clive ne lagbhag bina lade, saazish aur jaal-saazi se jeet haasil ki. Nehru kehte hain ki is shuruaat ka kadwa swaad kabhi gaya nahi. Aur kuch hi saal baad, 1770 mein, akaal ne Bengal-Bihar ki ek tihaai aabaadi khatam kar di.

Nehru ko Angrezon ki jeet kuch had tak kismat lagti hai. Wo kai baar haare: Haidar Ali aur Tipu se, Marathon se, Sikhon se, Gorkhon se. Par jeet sirf kismat nahi thi. Company local jhagdon mein ek paksh ki madad karti, aur bhaari keemat leti. Uski fauj zyadatar Hindustani sipahiyon ki thi. Jab samajh aaya ki Angrez sirf apna khel khel rahe hain, tab tak der ho chuki thi.

Desh-bhakti ki bhavna tab thi hi nahi. Wafaadari apne sardar se thi. Marathon mein kuch rashtriya bhaav tha, par unhone Rajputon ko naraaz kar diya, aur Maratha sardar khud aapas mein ladte rahe. Angrezon ke paas behtar sangathan tha, behtar jaankaari thi, aur samandar par kabza tha. Haar kar bhi wo phir khade ho jaate, aur Bengal ki daulat se agli jung ka kharcha nikalta.

Is daur ne Madhya Bharat aur Rajasthan ko ujaad diya. Nehru Edward Thompson ko quote karte hain, jinke hisaab se sabse buri haalat wahin thi jahan Angrezon ka dabdaba tha. Angrez yaad dilate hain ki unhone Bharat ko araajakta se bachaya. Nehru kehte hain, us araajakta mein Company ka bhi haath tha. Aur shayad unke bina bhi Bharat khud sambhal jaata, jaise paanch hazaar saal mein kai baar sambhla tha.

## Part 11: Do anokhe aadmi

Is andhere mein Nehru do aise logon ko dhoondhte hain, jinme jigyasa zinda thi.

Pehle, Maharaja Ranjit Singh. Jat Sikh, jinhone Punjab mein ek raajya banaya, jo Kashmir aur Frontier tak phaila. Ek Francisi yaatri Jacquemont ne unhe pehla Hindustani kaha jo sawal poochta hai, aur kaha ki unke sawal khatam hi nahi hote. Aur wo khoon-kharabe se nafrat karte the. Unhone har jurm ke liye maut ki saza khatam kar di, us waqt jab England mein chhoti chori par bhi phaansi hoti thi.

Doosre, Jaipur ke Sawai Jai Singh. Mughal saltanat ke bikharne ka daur. Wo chaalaak diplomat the, bahadur yoddha bhi. Par Nehru ko unki raajneeti mein dilchaspi nahi. Jai Singh ganitagya the, khagolshastri the, shehar ke naqshe banane wale the.

Unhone Jaipur, Delhi, Ujjain, Banaras aur Mathura mein vedhshalayein banwaayi. Apne aadmi Portugal bheje, aur wahan se Europe ki tables mangvaayi. Jai Singh ne unhe apni tables se milaya, aur galtiyan pakdi. Unki raay mein wajah ye thi ki un logon ke yantra chhote the. Unhone Euclid aur logarithm ki kitaabein Sanskrit mein anuvaad karwaayi. Phir Europe ke shehron ke naqshe dekhkar Jaipur basaya, jo aaj bhi town planning ki misaal hai.

Nehru ke liye Jai Singh akele sitaare nahi the. Unke saath vaigyanikon ki ek poori toli thi. Matlab Bharat mein vigyaan ki chingari bujhi nahi thi. Bas use hawa nahi mili. Aur shaanti aane ke baad bhi, naye hukmaraanon ne use hawa nahi di.

## Part 12: Do Englands

Atharahvi sadi tak Bharat ki arthvyavastha kaisi thi? Nehru V. Anstey ke hawale se kehte hain ki Bharat ka utpaadan aur vyapaar duniya ke kisi bhi desh ke barabar tha. Uski hundi Iran, Kabul aur Tashkent tak maani jaati thi. Napoleon ki jungon mein ek Angrez admiral ka jahaaz Bharat mein bana tha. Jab England mein mashin ka daur aaya, tab bhi Bharatiya kapda takkar deta raha, aur use rokne ke liye bhaari tax lagaye gaye.

Par vyapaariyon ke paas raajneetik taaqat nahi thi, aur unhone kabhi use chheenne ki sochi bhi nahi. Samaj thak chuka tha. Nehru kehte hain ki jo sabhyata badalna mana kar de, wo girti hai.

Udhar England mein Parliament raja se upar thi, aur naya madhyam varg phailne ko betaab tha. America ne 1776 mein azaadi ka elaan kiya. France mein Bastille gira.

Aur England? In kraantiyon se darkar wo aur sakht ho gaya. 1760 mein jab George teesre gaddi par baithe, tab lagbhag 160 jurmon ki saza maut thi. 1820 tak lagbhag sau aur jud gaye. Sipahiyon ko sau-sau kode lagte the. Nehru kehte hain ki insaaniyat ke maamle mein us waqt Bharat aage tha, aur saaksharta bhi zyada thi. Fark bas ye tha ki Europe ke andar nayi taaqatein kaam kar rahi thi, aur Bharat thehra hua tha.

Ab ek list suniye. 1600 mein East India Company ko charter mila. Tab Shakespeare likh rahe the. 1608 mein Milton paida hue. 1660 mein Royal Society bani. Phir flying shuttle, spinning jenny, steam engine, power loom.

To Nehru poochte hain: kaun sa England Bharat aaya? Shakespeare, Milton aur vigyaan wala, azaadi ke liye ladne wala England? Ya kode, phaansi aur purani zameendaari wala England? Har desh mein ye do chehre hote hain. Nehru ka jawab kadwa hai. Bharat mein galat wala England aage raha, aur usne yahan ke galat Bharat ko hi badhava diya.

Aakhir mein ek tulna. America ki azaadi aur Bharat ki ghulami lagbhag ek saath shuru hui. Dedh sau saal mein America kahan pahuncha, aur Bharat kahan? Nehru maante hain ki America ke paas khaali slate tha, aur humare paas yaadon ka bojh. Phir bhi wo halke tanz se poochte hain: agar Angrez humein "self-government sikhaane" ka ye bojh na uthate, to kya Bharat zyada azaad, zyada ameer, aur vigyaan mein zyada aage na hota?

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, chhapakhana. Akbar ne Bible dekhkar sawal nahi poocha. Aaj har nayi technology ke saath yahi sawal khada hai: kya hum sirf kharidar rahenge, ya banane wale bhi banenge?

Doosri, Nitisara ka niyam ki sadasyon ke rishtedaar sarkari pad nahi le sakte. Hazaar saal purana niyam, aur bhai-bhatijavaad par behas aaj bhi waise hi chal rahi hai.

Teesri, Amir Khusrau. Nehru ne 1944 mein likha tha ki unke geet chhe sau saal baad bhi gaaye jaate hain. Aaj saat sau saal ho gaye, aur qawwali ki mehfilon mein Khusrau ab bhi gaaye jaate hain.

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru kehte hain ki har desh mein do chehre hote hain. Aaj ke Bharat ke do chehre aapko kaun se dikhte hain, aur duniya ke saamne kaun sa zyada jaata hai?

## Outro

Agle episode mein kahani Angrezi raaj ke andar pahunchti hai. Company se Crown tak, 1857 ki bagawat, gaon ki dastkaari ka tootna, aur ek naye Bharat ki pehli awaazein.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
