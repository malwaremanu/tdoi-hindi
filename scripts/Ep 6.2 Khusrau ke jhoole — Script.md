Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 6.2: Khusrau ke jhoole — Chapter 6 ke chhah episodes wale set ka doosra episode.

1944 hai. Ahmadnagar ka qila. Nehru wahan is kitaab par kaam kar rahe hain. Us waqt Dilli par Angrezon ka raaj hai. Unse pehle Mughal the. Unse pehle Afghan.

Aaj hum badalte sinhasanon ke beech neeche jaayenge — aam zindagi ke paas. Raaste mein ek purani kitaab ki fehrist phatengi. Ek Afghan pahaadon se utar kar Dilli ko apna ghar bana lega. Ek raja apni jaan aur apna takht ek shaadi ke liye khota hoga. Aur aakhir mein hum usi aam ki daali ke neeche, usi gaane par lautenge.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo is desh ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar Islam yahan do baar aaya — pehle mehmaan ban kar, vaidyon, kitaabon aur masjidon ke saath; phir Mahmud ki talwaron ke saath. Uske baad ek sau saath saal khaamoshi. Aur phir ek Afghan pahaadon se neeche utra.

## Part 1: Mitti ke badshah

Ek purani kitaab kholiye. Aaj bhi school ki itihaas-kitaabon mein ek hi fehrist milti hai.

Pehla khaana: purana zamana. Hindu kaal.
Doosra khaana: madhya kaal. Musalman kaal.
Teesra khaana: aaj ka zamana. Angrezon ka kaal.

Teen khaane. Aur poora itihaas in teenon mein thoos diya gaya.

Nehru is fehrist par rukte hain. Aur wo saaf likhte hain: ye bantwara na samajhdari hai, na sach. Ye dhoka deta hai, aur itihaas ko galat kone se dikhata hai.

Kyun galat hai?

Kyunki ye bantwara upar ki tabdeeliyon ko ginta hai. Neeche ki zindagi ko chhod deta hai.

Dekhiye pehla khaana — "purana kaal". Jaise wo ek hi thaharav tha. Sach ye hai ki wo bahut bada tha. Usme utaar-chadhaav the, tootna tha, phir se uthna tha, phir girna tha. Ek hi shabd mein usko baandh dena uske saath anyay hai.

Aur "Musalman kaal"? Haan, us daur mein ek badlaav aaya. Ek ahem badlaav. Par wo badlaav zyadatar oopar hi raha. Usne is desh ki zindagi ki asli lakeer ko nahi toda.

Kyun nahi toda? Kyunki jo log uttar-pashchim se aaye, wo is mitti mein ghul gaye. Jaise unse pehle kitne hi log ghul chuke the.

Unke raajvansh yahin ke raajvansh ban gaye. Unki jadein is mitti mein chali gayin. Shaadiyon se khoon mila. Ek jaan-boojh kar koshish hui ki logon ki reetiyon mein haath na daala jaaye. Aur ye log is desh ko apna ghar maante the. Baaki poori duniya ko pardes.

Haan, ye theek hai ki is soch mein apni hi bhool thi. Hamlavar ko oopar baithne ka mauka mile bina raaj nahi chalta. Us bhool ke baare mein is episode ke ant mein baat hogi.

Par ek baat saaf thi. Angrez aane tak, is desh ki zindagi apne hi pairon par khadi rahi. Apne hi kanoon, apni hi reet, apne hi khet.

To agar upar ka takht badalte rahe, aur neeche ka dhancha wahi raha — to is lambe daur ki asli kahani kahan chali?

Uske liye ek saal dekhiye. Wo saal jab Dilli ki gaddi ek pal ke liye khaali hui thi.

## Part 2: Ghazni girta hai, Dilli banti hai

1030 mein Mahmud mar gaya.

Aur phir ek ajeeb si shanti chha gayi. Ek sau saath saal. Poore ek sau saath saal tak is desh par koi naya hamla nahi hua. Koi Afghan nahi aaya, koi Turk Punjab se aage nahi badha.

Ek sau saath saal — aap sochiye kitna lamba waqt hai. Us zamane mein Europe mein kya kya badla? Par yahan darwaza band raha.

Phir darwaza khula. Ek Afghan sardar, Shahab-ud-Din Ghuri — wo pehle Ghazni gaya aur Ghazni ko le liya. Ghaznavi raajvansh ka ant ho gaya.

Uske baad wo Lahore aaya. Aur phir Dilli.

Lahore theek. Dilli ne inkaar kar diya.

Dilli ke us waqt ke raja the Prithvi Raj Chauhan. Ghuri ki fauj ko usne bilkul haraa diya. Ghuri bhaga. Afghanistan laut gaya.

Par wo agle saal nayi fauj le kar wapas aaya.

Is baar ghodi uski jeeti. Aur 1192 mein wo Dilli ki gaddi par baith gaya.

Ab zara us shaadi ki taraf dekhiye, jisne ye darwaza khola.

Prithvi Raj us daur ke sabse mashhoor premi hain. Unke geet aaj bhi gaaye jaate hain, unki kahaniyan aaj bhi suni jaati hain. Kyunki laaparwahi se pyaar karne wale logon ki shohrat kabhi nahi ghatti.

Kanauj ka raja Jaichandra. Uski ek beti thi, jo Prithvi Raj se pyaar karti thi.

Uske liye poori rajputana se rajkunwar aaye the.

Aur Prithvi Raj ne usi darbar se usse utha liya. Saare ikatthe sardaron ke saamne. Apne ghode par bitha kar apne shehar le aaya.

Dhadiyon ne ise pyaar ka geet banaya. Raajneeti ne ise jhagda bana diya. Jaichandra ka gussa aisa uchhla ki wo apni saari izzat daanv par laga diya.

Ab wahi hua jo aise jhagdon mein hota hai. Dilli aur madhya Bharat ki saari bahaduri ek doosre par chali. Sabse bahadur log dono taraf mare gaye. Mulk andar hi se khaaya gaya.

Aur usi daur mein Ghuri doosri baar aaya.

Prithvi Raj apni kurbaani to chuka chuke the. Us ek jhagde ne unki jaan li, aur Dilli ka takht bhi.

Unki kahani aaj tak gaayi jaati hai. Unki maut ka gham geeton mein hai. Aur Jaichandra? Jaichandra ko aaj tak log giaddaar kehte hain.

Ye sach hai: Dilli ka girna poore Bharat ka girna nahi tha.

Us waqt dakshin mein Chola aise hi mazboot the. Bahut si riyasatein azad thin. Afghan raaj ko dakshin ke bade hisse tak pahunchne mein aur dedh sau saal lag gaye.

Par Dilli sirf ek shehar nahi thi. Wo naye zamane ka nishaan thi. Jahan tak nazar jaati thi, log wahi dekh rahe the.

Aur jo log aaye the, wo bahar se aaye the. To sabse pehla sawal yahi tha — ye kaun the?

## Part 3: Pashto, aur ek seema ka rishta

Yahan ek baat samajhni zaroori hai, warna poora daur galat padha jaayega.

Mahmud ka hamla videshi tha. Wo Turk tha, aur Punjab kuch saal ke liye is desh se alag ho gaya.

Par barahvin sadi ke ant mein jo aaye, wo alag the.

Ye Afghan the. Aur Afghan log — samjhiye — asal mein is desh ki ek nasal the. Ek Indo-Aryan nasal, jo Bharatiya logon se bandhi hui thi.

Aur ye bhi samajh lijiye: Afghanistan lambe samay tak is desh ka hissa tha, aur aage bhi uske hisse ka banna tha. Wahan ki zubaan, Pashto, mool roop se Sanskrit se nikli hai.

Aaj bhi Afghanistan mein itne purane nishaan milte hain, jitne shayad hi kahin aur. Aur wo zyadatar Bauddh daur ke hain.

Isi liye sahi naam ye hai: Indo-Afghan. Bahar wale nahi, seema wale.

Sakht log the. Apne aqeeday mein katthar. Sipahi, na ki baith kar sochne wale.

Shuruaat mein unhone fatih ki tarah bartav kiya — kraur aur kadwe, ek aise ilaake mein jo unhe apna maalik nahi maanta tha.

Phir wo dheele pade.

Is desh unka ghar ban gaya. Dilli unki rajdhani ban gayi — Ghazni nahi, jahan Mahmud baitha tha.

Aur ye sama lena bahut tez hua. Unmein se kai logon ne yahan ki auraton se shaadi ki.

Alauddin Khilji ne khud ek Hindu rani se shaadi ki. Uske bete ne bhi.

Aage kuch badshah khoon se Turk the — jaise Qutb-ud-Din Aibak, Sultana Razia, Iltutmish. Par darbari ameer aur fauj zyadatar Afghan hi rahi.

Aur kitna bada shehar tha, iska andaaza ek aise aadmi ne diya jo duniya ghoom chuka tha.

Ibn Batuta — Morocco se aaya ek Arab yaatri, jisne Cairo se Constantinople tak, aur wahin se China tak, sab dekha tha. Unhone chaudahvin sadi ki Dilli ka haal likha. Thodi badhaa-chadhaa ke saath.

Unke shabdon mein: dilli duniya ke sabse bade sheharon mein se ek thi.

Ab ek door ka mod. Upar afghani raaj bana, aur dakshin mein ek doosri kahani chal rahi thi.

Wahan Chola ka raaj dhal raha tha, aur uski jagah ek nayi samundari taaqat uthi. Pandya rajya. Chhota rajya, par badi manzil. Uski rajdhani Madura, aur uska bandargah Kayal.

Kayal us waqt duniya ke bade bandargahon mein se ek tha. Arab se aaye jahaaz, China se aaye jahaaz.

Marco Polo do baar wahan ruka — 1288 mein aur 1293 mein. Usne is shehar ko bada aur aala likha. Aur wahan ke malmal ko — uske shabdon mein — makdi ke jaale jaisa.

Aur ek baat usne likhi, jo aage kaam ki hai. Dakshin Bharat mein ghode samundar ke raaste aate the, Arabia aur Iran se — kyunki yahan ka mausam ghode paalne ka nahi tha. Aur ghode ke bina fauj nahi chalti.

Aur ek sardar ke paas ek ghoda aisa tha ki raja ki nazar us par padi.

Dilli ke ek Sultan ne Kathiawar ke ek Hara sardar ka ghoda dekha. Itna pasand aaya ki usne maang liya.

Us sardar ka jawab suniye. Usne kaha: ek rajput se teen cheezein kabhi nahi maangni chahiye — uska ghoda, uski pyari, aur uski talwar.

Ye keh kar wo apni ghodi mor kar chala gaya.

Baad mein jhagda bhi hua. Par is ek jawab mein poori sadiyaan chhupi hain. Ek waada, jo har galat maang ke saamne khada raha.

## Part 4: Timur ke baad, ek nayi Dilli

Un pahaadon ke us paar, jahan se hamlavar aate the, ek doosri aandhi khadi thi.

Mongol. Chengiz Khan ke ghudsawar. Wo poore Asia ko raakh kar rahe the — Bokhara, Samarkand, Herat, Balkh, Kiev.

Bharat? Bharat nahi. Wo Sindhu nadi par ruk gaye. Aur mud gaye.

Chengiz ke baare mein ek ajeeb baat suniye. Wo Musalman nahi tha — chahe uska naam aaj Islam se juda hua hai. Wo aasman ke devta par aastha rakhta tha. Us aqeeday ko Shamaism kehte hain.

Aur us shabd mein ek kanta hai: Arabi mein Bauddhon ke liye shabd tha samani, aur wo samani Sanskrit ke shravana se nikla tha.

Ab apne aap sochiye: is duniya ka sabse bada fatih, jiske saamne Asia aur Europe ki bahaduri bhusa thi — wo shayad ek Bauddh tha.

Aaj bhi Asia chaar vijetaon ko yaad karta hai. Sikandar, Sultan Mahmud, Chengiz Khan, aur Timur.

Aur ab wahi Timur samne aata hai. 1369. Wo khud ko Chengiz ka vanshaj batata tha, apni maa ki taraf se. Samarkand uski rajdhani bani.

Bharat mein wo kuch mahine raha. Aaya, Dilli pahuncha, laut gaya.

Par jo raasta usne chuna, uska har kos gawah hai: jahan-jahan se guzra, wahan khope khade kar gaya. Dilli murdon ka shehar ban chuki thi.

Aur usne poora Bharat nahi dekha — sirf Punjab ke kuch hisse aur Dilli.

Dilli ko us gehere sote se jagne mein saalon lage. Jab wo jagi, wo ek bade samrajya ki rajdhani nahi rahi.

Timur ne us samrajya ki kamar tod di thi. Aur usi tootne se dakshin mein naye rajya nikle.

Unmein do sabse bade the. Ek Gulbarga, jise Bahmani rajya kehte hain. Doosra Vijayanagar, ek Hindu rajya.

Gulbarga aage paanch riyasaton mein bant gaya. Unmein ek: Ahmadnagar.

Aur Ahmadnagar ke baare mein ek baat suniye — wo is kitab ki ajeeb lakeeron mein se ek hai.

Ahmadnagar ke sansthapak Ahmad Nizam Shah ne 1490 mein wo shehar basaya. Unke pita the Nizam-ul-Mulk Bhairi — Bahmani rajaon ke ek wazir, jo Bhairu naam ke ek Brahmin bahi-khata likhne wale ke beta the.

Wahi Ahmadnagar — jiske qile mein Nehru 1944 mein baith kar ye kitaab likh rahe the. Us shehar ke basane wale haath desi the. Aur uski mashhoor veerangana, Chand Bibi ka khoon bhi mila-jula tha.

Dakshin ki baaki saari Moslem riyasatein bhi isi tarah yahin ki thin.

Aur us daur mein dakshin ka sabse bada shehar? Vijayanagar.

Uttar se sharnarthi aaye the. Kuch sadiyon mein ye aankhon ka ajooba ban gaya.

Ek yaatri ne likha: is jaisa shehar kisi aankh ne nahi dekha — bazaar arcade wale, patthar ki nahrein, aur baag.

1420 mein aaye ek Italian ne ise naap kar likh diya — saath kos.

Sau saal baad ek Portuguese ne likha: ye shehar Rome jitna bada hai. Raja ke kamre haathi-dant se bhare the.

Wo raja, Krishna Deva Raya — videshi logon ko izzat deta tha, unse har mamle ka poochta tha.

Aur ek din wo bhi aaya, jo mitti ke badshahon ka silsila badal dega.

Uttar ke pahaadon se ek aur aadmi utra — Panipat ke maidan par, jahan is desh ki qismat itni baar tay hui. 1526 mein usne Dilli ka takht jeeta.

Naam: Babar. Central Asia ka ek Timurid shahzada. Timur ke khoon se — usi Samarkand ke aas-paas paida aur bada hua tha, jahan uske baap-dada ke waqt kala aur imaaratein pal rahi thi.

Aur Babar ki safalta ka ek karan tha: uske paas nayi topkhana thi, jo us waqt yahan prachalan mein nahi thi.

Us din se yahan jang ka hunar peechhe rehne laga. Sirf Bharat nahi — poora Asia wahi khada raha. Mughal samrajya do sau saal taqatwar raha, par 17vi sadi ke baad wo European faujon se barabari nahi kar paata.

To phir European fauj yahan aayi kyun nahi? Kyunki aane ke liye samundar ka raasta chahiye tha.

Aur samundar ka raasta is desh ke haath mein tha. Tab.

## Part 5: Samundar se kaun nikla

Is desh ke jahaaz ek zamane mein doosre deshon ke bandargahon par lagte the.

Pichhle chapter mein hum ye raasta chal chuke hain. Angkor. Borobudur. Srivijaya. Malacca. Terah sau saal tak is desh ke naam, lipi, mandir aur kanoon samundar ke us paar jaate rahe.

Wo raasta kisi ek talwar ne nahi band kiya. Wo dheere-dheere khaali hota gaya.

Terahvin sadi mein dakshin ka Chola raaj dhal gaya. Chola samundar ke log the — unke jahaaz door tak jaate the. Unke jaane ke baad samundar par is desh ka jhanda nahi raha.

Uski jagah ek chhota rajya tha, Pandya. Samundar se ghulmil, apne bandargah par bas. Par taaqat utni hi thi, jitna uska size.

Aur ye nahi ki sab kuch ek dum khatam ho gaya.

Ek doosri taaqat samne aayi. Arab. Unhone samundari raaste sambhal liye. Aur unke peeche Portuguese aane wale the.

Ek baat dhyan mein rakhiye: samundar par apna jhanda girana koi chhoti baat nahi thi. Jis raaste par jahaaz chalte the, us raaste par maal chalta tha, dharm chalta tha, aur khabar chalti thi. Jo raasta kisi aur ka hua, us par chalne wale bhi kisi aur ke ho gaye.

To samajh lijiye: ye raasta kisi din band nahi hua. Wo kisi aur ka hota gaya.

Aur jaise hi wo kisi aur ka hua, is desh ka darwaza khula chhoot gaya.

Par khula darwaza ka matlab ye nahi ki usi din koi andar aa gaya. Uske andar aane mein abhi do-teen sadiyan baaki thin.

Yaad rakhiye is chhoti si baat ko. Aaj hum is darwaze par lautenge.

## Part 6: Do ulti cheezein

Ab wapas upar. Dilli ki gaddi par Afghan baithe the.

Kyunki jo bahar se aaye the, unke paas ab sirf ek rasta tha — is desh ko apna maanna.

Afghan rajaon ke raajvansh maatra nahi rahe. Wo poore tarah se Indianized ho gaye. Unki jadein yahin ki mitti mein thin. Unke liye ye desh ghar tha, aur baaki poori duniya pardes.

Bahut se Rajput rajkunwar unhe apna upar wala maan chuke the. Kai nai maane. Unke beech lohaa bhi chala.

Aur phir bhi — Dilli ke sultanon mein ek Sultan Feroze Shah the. Unki maa Hindu thi. Ek aur the Ghyas-ud-Din Tughlak. Unki maa bhi.

Aise vivah roz nahi hote the. Par hote the.

Aur dakshin mein ek bahut badi shaadi hui — Gulbarga ke Moslem sultan ne Vijayanagar ki Hindu rajkumari se. Poori dhoom-dhaam se, shehar ke saamne.

Ab ek sawal. Ye log is desh ke ho gaye. Par bahar ki duniya inhe kaise dekhti thi? Aur yahan ke logon ko?

Us sawal ka jawab ek aise aadmi ne diya jo yahan aaya bhi nahi tha.

Idrisi — 11vi sadi ke ek Arab bhoogol-shastri, jinhone ye baat Afghan aane se pehle likhi.

Unka kehna: is desh ke log swabhav se nyay ki taraf jhukte hain, aur apne kaam mein usse kabhi nahi chhodte. Unki imaandari, unki sacchai, aur wade ka paalan door-door mashhoor hai. Log har taraf se unke desh aate hain.

Ye tareef kisi Bharatiya ne nahi ki. Ye us duniya ne ki, jo baad mein talwar lekar aayi.

Aur upar ki sarkaar? Ek kaam-chalau prashasan khada ho gaya. Sadakein sudhrin — zyadatar fauji zaroorat ke liye. Sarkar pehle se zyada markaz mein aa gayi, par logon ki reetiyon mein dakhal nahi diya gaya.

Afghan hukmaranon mein sabse tez dimaag Sher Shah ka tha. Usi ke banaye revenue system ko Akbar ke zamane mein aage badhaya gaya. Aur Akbar ke mashhoor revenue mantri, Raja Todar Mal, ko sabse pehle Sher Shah ne hi naukri par rakha tha.

Yani ye log is desh ki taakat istemaal karna seekh rahe the.

Ab is daur ka sabse ajeeb hissa suniye. Nehru kehte hain ki is jeet ka asar do hisson mein nikla, aur dono hisse ek doosre ka munh todte the.

Pehla hissa: bhaag-daud. Jahan Afghan raaj tha, wahan se log dakshin ki taraf chale gaye.

Aur jo bache? Wo sakht ho gaye. Wo apne khavche mein baith gaye. Bahar ke tareeqon se bachne ke liye unhone apni deewarein aur oonchi kar li. Jaati ko aur kasa.

Doosra hissa iske bilkul ulta tha.

Dheere-dheere, lagbhag bina jaane, log naye tareeqon ki taraf jhukne lage. Ek milan chupchaap kaam karne laga.

Bhavan banane ke naye tareeqe aaye. Khaana badla. Kapde badle.

Sangeet mein to ye milan sabse zyada dikha. Purane Bharatiya classical dhang ko pakad kar, sangeet kai nayi dishaon mein badha.

Farsi darbar ki zubaan ban gayi. Aur uske shabd aam bolchaal mein ghus gaye. Saath hi, yahan ki lok-bhashayein bhi phalti rahin.

Yani darwaza andar se band hua. Aur rasoi ki khidki khul gayi.

Aaj sochiye. Apne roz ke shabd giniye. Apni rasoi dekhiye. Apne kapde dekhiye. Jo aaj hum "apna" kehte hain, uska kitna hissa in dono haath-khiladiyon ki den hai?

## Part 7: Galiyon se ek nayi awaaz

Ab is milan ki jagah dekhiye.

Nehru ek kadwi baat likhte hain. Ye saare badlaav oopar hue. Rajkunwaron mein. Upar ki classes mein.

Aam logon ki bheed par — khaas taur par gaon ki bheed par — inka asar nahi hua.

Ye badlaav darbar se shuru hote the. Shehron mein phailte the. Gaon tak pahunchte hi nahi the.

Aur usi se uttar Bharat mein ek mili-juli sanskriti ban rahi thi. Uska kendra Dilli tha, aur wo ilaake jo aaj United Provinces hain.

Dhyan dijiye: ye wahi ilaake the jo purani Aryan sanskriti ke kendra the. Kuch aisa hua ki us purani sanskriti ka bada hissa dakshin ki taraf chala gaya. Aur dakshin Hindu reetivad ka gadh ban gaya.

Ab do jagah dekhiye, jahan ye mili-juli soch zinda thi.

Pehli: Jaunpur. Dilli sultanate kamzor pad gayi thi, to yahan ek chhota Moslem rajya khada ho gaya. Poori 15vi sadi mein Jaunpur kala, sanskriti aur dharmik sahanubhooti ka kendra raha.

Wahan Hindi jaise ugti hui lok-bhasha ko badhava diya gaya. Aur ek koshish to yahan tak hui ki Hindu aur Musalman aqeedon ke beech ek milan banaya jaaye.

Doosri jagah bahut door, uttar mein. Kashmir. Wahan ek azaad Moslem raja hua, Zainulabdin. Unki shohrat bhi isi baat se hui — sahanubhooti, aur Sanskrit vidya aur purani sanskriti ko badhava.

To ye milan darbaron mein chal raha tha. Shehron mein chal raha tha. Kashmir tak.

Aur usi daur mein ek nayi awaaz uthi. Wo darbar se nahi, galiyon se uthi.

Dakshin mein Ramanand — pandrahvi sadi ke ek Hindu sant. Unke sabse mashhoor shishya: Kabir. Banaras ke ek Musalman julaha.

Kabir ke geet aaj bhi gaaye jaate hain. Aur sirf gaaye nahi jaate — wo aaj bhi galiyon mein hain.

Uttar mein Guru Nanak. Sikh panth ke sansthapak maane jaate hain.

Ab dhyan dijiye is baat par. In sab ka asar apne-apne panth se bahut door gaya.

Bharat ka Islam waisa nahi raha jaisa baaki duniya mein tha.

Islam ka sakht ek-ishwarvaad Hindu soch par chhaya. Aur Hindu ka ishwar-sab-mein bhaav yahan ke Musalman par.

Aur ek cheez yahin badhi: Sufi panth.

Ye kaun the? Sufi wo log the jo dharm ko dhamke se nahi, pyaar se samajhte the. Geet se. Sangeet se. Chup baith kar dhyaan se.

Aur in sab ka sabse gehra saboot kisi darbar mein nahi mila.

Wo ek gaane mein mila.

## Part 8: Khusrau ke jhoole

Nehru likhte hain ki is poore samay ka sabse bada saboot ye tha.

Aane wale logon ne is desh ki aam bhasha apna li. Chahe Farsi darbar ki zubaan bani rahi.

Aise kai Musalman lekhak hain, jinhone Hindi mein kitaabein likhin. Inmein sabse mashhoor: Amir Khusrau.

Amir Khusrau — ek Turk, jinka parivaar do-teen peedhiyon se Uttar Pradesh mein bas chuka tha. Wo chaudahvin sadi mein jeete the, kai Afghan sultanon ke zamane mein.

Farsi mein wo pehle darje ke shayar the. Sanskrit bhi jaante the.

Bade sangeetkar the. Unhone Bharatiya sangeet mein kai nayi cheezein jodeen. Kehte hain sitar unhi ne banaya.

Wo bahut vishayon par likhte the. Aur Bharat ki tareef mein bhi likhte the. Us fehrist ko suniye: dharm, darshan, tark, bhasha aur vyakaran, sangeet, ganit, vigyan — aur aam!

Haan, aam. Us fehrist ka aakhri naam ek phal hai.

Par unki asli shohrat unke geeton se aayi.

Yahin unka bada faisla tha. Unhone sahityik bhasha nahi chuni — wo bhasha jo sirf thode padhe-likhe logon ki thi. Wo gaon ke paas gaya.

Bhasha lene ke liye. Aur reet-rivaaz lene ke liye. Jeene ke dhang lene ke liye.

Usne mausam ke geet likhe. Purane Bharatiya dhang mein har mausam ki apni dhun hoti thi, aur apne shabd.

Usne zindagi ke har mod par likha: dulhan ka aana. Pyaar se judaai. Aur barsaat — jab sookhi zameen se zindagi phir se ubharti hai.

Aur wo gaane abhi bhi gaaye jaate hain. Aaj bhi. Uttar aur madhya Bharat ke kisi bhi gaon ya shehar mein.

Khaas taur par jab barsaat shuru hoti hai. Tab har gaon mein aam ya peepal ki daaliyon se bade jhoole bandhte hain. Aur gaon ki ladkiyan aur ladke ikatthe hokar us mauke ko manate hain.

[SFX: jhoole ki rassi, aur kitni ladkiyon ki gaati hui awaazein, dheemi]

Khusrau ne paheliyan aur bujho-khel bhi likhe. Jo bachchon aur bado, dono ko pasand hain.

Unke apne jeevan mein hi ye gaane mashhoor ho gaye the. Aur wo shohrat badhti hi gayi.

Nehru ek baat likhte hain, jo sadiyon ka hisaab hai. Wo kehte hain ki unhe nahi pata ki duniya mein kahin aur aisi misaal hai ya nahi. Chhah sau saal purane gaane, jo aaj bhi itne pasand kiye jaate hain. Aaj bhi itne gaaye jaate hain. Aur ek shabd bhi badle bina.

[SFX: halki si kalam kaagaz par chalti hai; door se koi dikh nahi, bas akhbaar ki si sarsaraahat]

Is episode ki shuruaat us jhoole par hui thi. Aur ab pata chala ki jo milan upar darbar mein ho raha tha, uska sabse gehra nishaan neeche gaon mein pada. Ek gaana, chhah sau saal se ek hi.

Ab sawal ye nahi ki upar kya badla.

Sawal ye hai ki neeche gaon mein aisi kaun si cheez thi, jo itni pukhta thi? Jise raja badalne se bhi kuch nahi hua?

Iska jawab Nehru ek dhanche ki tasveer se dete hain. Aur wo dhancha bahut bada hai.

## Part 9: Jaati akeli khadi nahi hai

Ab Nehru ek bahut badi baat par aate hain.

Pehle wo sawal aise chhedte hain jaise koi purani kitaab kholta ho. Jo Bharat ke baare mein thoda bhi jaanta hai, usne jaati ka naam suna hai. Aur bahar wala use kya samajhta hai?

Ek darja-bandi ka patthar. Upar ki classes hamesha upar, neeche ki hamesha neeche. Ek chalaki, jisne oopar walon ko oopar aur neeche walon ko zameen par rakh diya.

Is baat mein sach hai. Shuruaat mein ye shayad ek tarqeeb thi — jeetne walon ko harte hue logon se alag aur oopar rakhne ki.

Par ye poori sach nahi hai. Aur asli sawal yahin rukta hai: phir ye cheez itni mazboot kaise rahi?

Dekhiye kis-kis se ye takrai aur bachi. Bauddh dharm ke bade vaar se. Afghan raaj ke sadiyon se. Mughal raj se. Islam ke phailav se. Aur un gintiheen sudhaarakon se.

Sab ke baad bhi khadi rahi.

Aaj hi pehli baar uski jad par chot padi hai. Aur ye chot kisi naye aqeeday se nahi aayi.

Nehru saaf kehte hain: ye badlaav Hindu samaj ke andar se uthe sudhaar ki chahat se nahi aaya. Bahar ke vichaaron se bhi nahi — chahe unka asar raha hai. Ye badlaav aarthik zindagi ki jadon se aaya hai. Zindagi ki shartein badli hain, soch badal rahi hai. Ab ye dhancha lamba nahi khinch sakta.

Uski jagah kya aayega? Nehru imaandari se kehte hain: ye wo nahi bata sakte.

Kyunki daanv par jaati se zyada kuch hai.

Asli jhagda do sochon mein hai. Ek kehti hai: samooh hi asli cheez hai. Doosri kehti hai: vyakti sabse oopar hai.

Aur ye jhagda sirf Bharat ka nahi hai. Ye Pashchim ka bhi hai, poori duniya ka bhi. Unnisvi sadi ki Europe wali sabhyata vyaktivaad ki sabse oonchi lakeer thi. Wo aage badhi, beesvi sadi mein aayi, aur ab purani pad chuki hai — sankat aur jung ke bojh se toot rahi hai.

Aaj phir samooh ki baat badh rahi hai. Aur asli masla yahi hai: vyakti ke haq aur samooh ke haq, dono ko ek saath kaise rakha jaaye? Alag desh alag jawab dhoondhenge, par ek buniyadi jawab dheere-dheere sab par lagu hoga.

Ab Nehru ek baat kehte hain, jo jaati se badh kar hai.

Jaati akeli khadi nahi hoti. Wo ek bahut bade dhanche ka hissa hai. Uska ang hai.

Ho sakta hai jaati ke kuch saaf aib hata diye jaayein, aur system bach jaaye.

Par Nehru kehte hain, aisa hona mushkil hai. Kyunki jo taaqatein kaam kar rahi hain, wo oopar ki deewar par nahi, dhanche ki jad par chot kar rahi hain.

Aur dhanche ko thaamne wale kai sahare to pehle hi ja chuke hain.

Nehru ek saaf baat kehte hain: ab sawal ye nahi ki jaati pasand hai ya nahi. Badlaav humari pasand ke baawajood ho raha hai. Par use dhaalna aur disha dena humare haath mein hai.

Aur yahan wo ek chetavni jodte hain.

Ek bade dhanche ko todne ka matlab sirf uska tootna nahi hota. Samaaj bikhar sakta hai. Aapas ka jod toot sakta hai. Bahut dukh ho sakta hai — jab tak uski jagah koi doosra dhancha na aa jaaye, jo is zamane ka ho aur is desh ke mizaaj ka ho.

Phir wo saaf kehte hain: sirf tod kar phenk dena kaafi nahi. Aage ki koi tasveer saamne rakhein — chahe dhundhli hi ho. Warna ek khaali jagah banegi, aur wo aise bharegi jiska baad mein pachhtana pade.

Kisi bhi scheme mein teen cheezein dekhni hain: kaam kis insaan ke saath karna hai, uska sochne ka background kya hai, aur mahaul kya hai.

Ye sab chhod kar hawai plan banana, ya kis aur desh ki nakal karna, bewakoofi hai.

Isi liye pehle us purane dhanche ko samajhna hoga, jisne humare logon ko itna gehra banaya hai.

To wo dhancha tha kya? Nehru teen cheezein saamne rakhte hain.

## Part 10: Neeche ki zindagi ka naqsha

Teen khambhe.

Pehla: gaon. Gaon ka apna faisla, apni zameen, apna hisaab.

Doosra: jaati.

Teesra: jukta parivaar.

Aur teenon mein ek hi baat chalti hai — samooh. Vyakti ki jagah doosri hai.

Nehru yahan imaandar hain. Wo kehte hain, alag-alag dekhne par in teenon mein koi aisi nayi baat nahi hai. Aise roop doosre deshon mein bhi milte hain, khaas taur par madhyakaal mein.

Ganrajya jaise roop aur bhi jagah the. Ek tarah ka samuhik swatva bhi tha. Russia ka purana mir kisi tarah is gaon se milta tha.

Jaati ka kaam tha function aur seva — Europe ke madhyakaal ke trade guild jaise.

Aur China ka parivaar-tantra Hindu jukte parivaar se bahut milta tha.

Nehru ye bhi maante hain ki unhe in sab ka itna pata nahi ki wo ye tulna door tak khinch sakein. Aur unke hisaab se isse koi farq bhi nahi padta.

Kyunki ek-ek cheez alag rakh kar dekhne se kahani poori nahi hoti.

Jo cheez poori ki poori thi — wo nayi thi. Aur jaisa-jaisa ye dhancha bada hua, wo aur bhi apni tarah ka hota gaya.

[SFX: barsaat ki halki awaaz laut kar aati hai; door se jhoole ki rassi]

Ab us aam ki daali ke neeche chaliye. Barsaat ki pehli shaam. Ladkiyan jhoole par baithi hain. Aur wahi gaana jo chhah sau saal pehle likha gaya tha.

Ab jawab saaf hai.

Badlaav sirf upar hua. Sultan aata tha, jaata tha. Uske dharm badle, uski zubaan badli, uske kapde badle.

Neeche ki zindagi unke haath mein thi hi nahi. Wo teen khambhon par khadi thi — gaon ki panchayat, jaati, aur jukta parivaar.

Teenon raja ke haath se bahar the. Aur teenon ka kaam tha cheezein chalau rakhna, badalna nahi.

Isi liye upar ki har aandhi ne unhe hilaya nahi. Sultan badla. Dharm badla. Dilli ki zubaan badli. Aur neeche, gaon ka faisla wahi raha, jaati ka dhancha wahi raha, ghar ka hisaab wahi raha.

Aur ek gaana chhah sau saal se ek hi raha.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli: Nehru ka tark tha ki jaati ke aib hata kar dhancha bachaya nahi ja sakta. Kyunki jo taaqatein kaam kar rahi hain, wo oopar ki deewar par nahi, jad par chot kar rahi hain. Ye kasauti aaj bhi kaam ki hai. Kisi bhi badlaav se poochhiye: ye sirf oopar saja hua hai, ya jad tak gaya hai?

Doosri: pichhli baar humne dekha ki mili-juli sanskriti ka sabse gehra nishaan gaon tak nahi pahuncha. Par uski khushboo hamari rasoi mein hai, hamare shabdon mein hai. Mila-jula hona koi kamzori nahi thi. Wo is desh ki sabse purani aadat thi.

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapki zindagi mein wo kaun si cheez hai jo teen peedhiyon se nahi badli — aur wo aapko thaam rahi hai, ya rok rahi hai?

## Outro

Upar sab badla — takht, zubaan, libaas, sangeet. Par ek cheez neeche tak pahunchi, aur wo badhi: ek aisi rasam jo aadhi aabadi ko ghar ke andar band kar deti hai. Us rasam ka naam humne aaj tak nahi liya. Agli baar lenge.

Aur uske peeche, teen khambhe — jinhone sadiyon tak is aam zindagi ko thaam kar rakha. Kya the wo teen? Aaj humne sirf naam liye.

Agli baar hum gaon ke darwaze par khade honge.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
