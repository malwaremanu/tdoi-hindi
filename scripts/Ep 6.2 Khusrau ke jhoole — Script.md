Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 6.2: Khusrau ke jhoole.

1944 hai. Ahmadnagar ka qila. Nehru wahan is kitaab par kaam kar rahe hain. Us waqt Dilli par Angrezon ka raaj hai. Unse pehle Mughal the. Unse pehle Afghan.

Aaj hum badalte sinhasanon ke beech neeche jaayenge — aam zindagi ke paas. Raaste mein ek 10vi sadi ki kitaab khulegi. Ek gaon ki panchayat ki baithak mein baithna hoga. Aur aakhir mein usi aam ki daali ke neeche, usi gaane par lautna hoga.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Pichhli baar humne dekha ki Islam yahan pehle dharm ban kar aaya — vyapaariyon, vaidon aur masjidon ke saath. Aur kai sau saal baad Mahmud ki talwaron ke saath. Uske baad Afghan aaye, aur unhone Dilli ki gaddi li. Par us badlaav ka asar neeche, aam zindagi par kya hua?

## Part 1: Do badshah, ek mitti

Dilli ki ek purani fehrist sochiye. Us par sultanon ke naam likhe hain. Aur kuch naam ke saath ek chhoti si baat bhi — maa ka naam.

Pehla naam: Feroze Shah. Dilli ke ek jaane-maane sultan — aur unki maa Hindu thi.

Doosra naam: Ghyas-ud-Din Tughlak. Ek aur sultan, aur unki kahani bhi wahi.

Ye ittefaq nahi tha, par aam bhi nahi. Afghan, Turk aur Hindu rajkunwaron ke beech shaadiyan hoti thi. Kam hoti thi, par hoti thi.

Aur dakshin mein ek shaadi ko dekhiye. Gulbarga — dakshin ka ek purana Moslem rajya. Uske sultan ne Vijayanagar ki ek Hindu rajkumari se shaadi ki. Poori dhoom-dhaam se, saare shehar ke saamne.

Afghan badshah bahar se aaye the. Par unki sultanate yahin ki ho gayi. Unki jadein isi mitti mein chali gayin.

Wo is desh ko apna ghar maante the. Aur baaki poori duniya ko pardes.

Kai Rajput rajkunwaron ne unhe apna oopar wala maan liya. Kai nahi maane. Un dono ke beech lohaa bhi laga. Kabhi bhaari lohaa. Par jo maan gaye, unke saath ek hi chhat ke neeche rehna shuru hua.

Nateeja? Ek aisa khandaan, jo bahar se aaya, do peedhiyon mein andar ka ho gaya.

Ab ek sawal bacha. Ye log is desh ke ho gaye. Par bahar ki duniya is desh ke logon ko kaise dekhti thi?

## Part 2: Idrisi ki gawahi

Is sawal ka jawab ek aise aadmi ne diya jo yahan aaya bhi nahi tha.

Idrisi — 11vi sadi ke ek Moslem bhoogol-shastri, jinhone ye baat Afghan aane se pehle likhi.

Unka kehna hai: is desh ke log swabhav se nyay ki taraf jhukte hain, aur apne kaam mein usse kabhi nahi chhodte. Unki imaandari aur wade nibhane ki shohrat itni hai ki log har taraf se unke desh aate hain.

Ye tareef kisi Bharatiya ne nahi ki. Ye us duniya ne ki jo baad mein talwar lekar aayi.

Aur upar ki sarkar? Ek kaam-chalau prashasan khada ho gaya. Sadakein sudhrin — zyadatar fauji zaroorat ke liye. Sarkar pehle se zyada markaz mein aa gayi. Par ek baat ka khayal rakha gaya: logon ki reetiyon mein dakhal nahi dena.

Afghan hukmaranon mein sabse tez dimaag Sher Shah ka tha. Sher Shah — wo Afghan raja jiske naam par aage ka revenue system khada hua.

Usi system ko Akbar ke zamane mein aage badhaya gaya. Akbar ke mashhoor revenue mantri, Raja Todar Mal, ko sabse pehle Sher Shah ne hi naukri par rakha tha.

Aur ek baat dhyan se. Afghan raja log is desh ki taakat ko istemaal karna seekh rahe the. Hindu talent unke prashasan aur fauj mein badhta gaya.

To ek taraf raja badal raha tha, doosri taraf raja ka kaam karne wale log. Ab dekhna ye hai ki is badlaav ka asar neeche kis shakal mein utra.

## Part 3: Do ulti cheezein

Nehru ek ajeeb baat likhte hain. Afghan fizawi jeet ka asar do hisson mein nikla. Aur dono hisse ek doosre ka munh todte the.

Pehla hissa: bhaag-daud. Jahan Afghan raaj tha, wahan se log dakshin ki taraf chale gaye.

Jo bache, wo sakht ho gaye. Wo apne khavche mein baith gaye. Bahar ke tareeqon se bachne ke liye unhone jaati-vyavastha ko aur kasa.

Yani darwaza andar se bandh ho gaya.

Aur doosra hissa? Dheere-dheere, lagbhag bina jaane, log unhi bahar ke tareeqon ki taraf jhukne lage. Soch mein bhi, jeene ke dhang mein bhi.

Ek milan chupchaap kaam karne laga. Bhavan banane ke naye tareeqe aaye. Khaana badla. Kapde badle. Zindagi ke kai aur hisse badle.

Sangeet mein to ye milan sabse zyada dikha. Purane Bharatiya classical dhang ko pakad kar, sangeet kai nayi dishaon mein badha.

Farsi darbar ki zubaan ban gayi. Aur Farsi ke shabd aam bolchaal mein ghus gaye. Saath hi, yahan ki lok-bhashayein bhi phalti rahin.

Yani darwaza band hua. Aur rasoiki khidki khul gayi.

Aaj sochiye. Apne roz ke shabd giniye. Khaana dekhiye. Kapde dekhiye. Jo aaj hum "apna" kehte hain, uska kitna hissa in dono haath-khiladiyon ki den hai?

Par isi milan ke beech ek cheez aisi thi jise Nehru sabse bada zakhm maante hain. Aur wahan wo beech mein aaj ki baat karne lagte hain.

## Part 4: Parda

Nehru khud maante hain ki unhone yahan ek chhalang lagayi hai. Wo beech mein aaj ke zamane par aa gaye. Par wo ye zaroori samajhte hain.

Unka kehna hai: is desh ki pichhle kuch sau saalon ki giraavat ke kaarnon mein parda ek bada kaaran hai. Jab tak ye reet khatam nahi hoti, is desh ki samajik zindagi aage nahi badh sakti.

Nuksaan sirf aurat par nahi hai. Nehru kehte hain ki nuksaan mard par bhi hai. Us bachche par bhi, jo apna zyadatar waqt parde mein ruke hue auraton ke beech bitata hai. Aur poore samaaj par bhi.

Ek acchi khabar bhi hai. Hindu samaj mein ye reet tezi se mit rahi thi. Musalman samaj mein thodi dheere.

Aur ise todne mein sabse bada haath kiska tha? Congress ke andolanon ka. Unhone hazaaron middle-class auraton ko ghar se nikaal kar public kaam mein khada kiya.

Gandhi ji iske sabse sakht khilafi the. Unhone parda ko ek "ghinauna aur darindagi wala riwaj" kaha.

Unka kehna tha ki mardon ne auraton ke saath jo zulm kiya, wo unhone ek purane riwaj ko pakad kar kiya. Wo riwaj shuruaat mein chahe jitna kaam ka raha ho, ab poori tarah bekaar ho chuka hai. Aur is desh ka bahut nuksaan kar raha hai.

Gandhi ji ki maang seedhi thi. Aurat ko wahi azaadi mile, aur khud ko banane ka wahi mauka, jo mard ko mila hai. Aur unke hisaab se dono ke beech koi deewar nahi honi chahiye. Dono ka baartav swabhavik aur khud-ba-khud hona chahiye.

Nehru likhte hain ki Gandhi ji ne aurat ki barabari aur azaadi ke liye bade josh se likha, aur unke gharelu gulami ki sakht ninda ki.

Ab wapas. Afghan Dilli mein baith chuke the. Ek naya milan chal raha tha. Par sawal ye hai: wo milan kahan ho raha tha?

## Part 5: Sab kuch upar hi hua

Yahin Nehru ek kadwa jawab dete hain.

Ye saare badlaav upar hue. Rajkunwaron mein. Upar ki classes mein. Aam logon ki bheed par — khaas taur par gaon ki bheed par — inka asar nahi hua.

Ye badlaav darbar se shuru hote the. Shehron mein phailte the. Gaon tak pahunchte hi nahi the.

Isi se ek lambi process shuru hui: uttar Bharat mein ek mili-juli sanskriti ban rahi thi. Uska centre Dilli tha, aur wo ilaake jo aaj United Provinces hain.

Dhyan dijiye — ye wahi ilaake the jo purani Aryan sanskriti ka centre the, aur rehte aaye the. Par us samay purani Aryan sanskriti ka bahut hissa dakshin ki taraf chala gaya. Aur dakshin Hindu reetivad ka gadh ban gaya.

Ab do jagah dekhiye jahan ye mili-juli soch zinda thi.

Pehli: Jaunpur. Dilli sultanate kamzor pad gayi thi, to yahan ek chhota Moslem rajya khada ho gaya. Poori 15vi sadi mein Jaunpur kala, sanskriti aur dharmik sahanubhooti ka centre raha.

Wahan Hindi jaise ugti hui lok-bhasha ko badhava diya gaya. Aur ek koshish to yahan tak hui ki Hindu aur Musalman aqeedon ke beech ek milan banaya jaaye.

Doosri: bahut door, uttar mein Kashmir. Wahan ek azaad Moslem raja hua, Zainul-abdin. Unki shohrat bhi isi baat se hui — sahanubhooti, aur Sanskrit vidya aur purani sanskriti ko badhava.

To ye milan darbaron mein, shehron mein, Kashmir tak chal raha tha.

Uska sabse gehra saboot kisi darbar mein nahi mila. Wo ek gaane mein mila.

## Part 6: Khusrau ke jhoole

Nehru likhte hain ki is sab ka sabse bada saboot ye tha ki aane wale logon ne is desh ki aam bhasha apna li — chahe Farsi darbar ki zubaan bani rahi.

Aise kai Musalman lekhak hain jinhone Hindi mein kitaabein likhin. Unmein sabse mashhoor Amir Khusrau.

Amir Khusrau — ek Turk, jinka parivaar do-teen peedhiyon se Uttar Pradesh mein bas chuka tha. Wo 14vi sadi mein jeete the, kai Afghan sultanon ke zamane mein.

Farsi mein wo pehle darje ke shayar the. Sanskrit bhi jaante the. Bade sangeetkar the, aur unhone Bharatiya sangeet mein kai nayi cheezein jodeen. Kehte hain sitar unhi ne banaya.

Wo kai vishayon par likhte the. Aur Bharat ki tareef mein bhi likhte the. Us fehrist ko suniye jo unke hisaab se is desh mein sabse badhkar thi: dharm, darshan, tark, bhasha aur vyakaran, sangeet, ganit, vigyan — aur aam!

Haan, aam. Us fehrist ka aakhri naam ek phal hai.

Par unki asli shohrat unke geeton se aayi, jo aam bol-chaal ki Hindi mein the.

Yahin unka bada faisla tha. Unhone sahityik bhasha nahi chuni, jo sirf thode padhe-likhe logon ki zubaan thi. Wo gaon ke paas gaya — bhasha lene ke liye, aur uske reet-rivaaz aur jeene ke dhang lene ke liye bhi.

Usne mausam ke geet likhe. Purane Bharatiya dhang mein har mausam ki apni dhun hoti thi, aur apne shabd. Usne zindagi ke har mod par likha: dulhan ka aana, pyaar se judaai, aur barsaat — jab sookhi zameen se zindagi phir se ubharti hai.

Aur wo gaane abhi bhi gaaye jaate hain. Aaj bhi. Uttar aur madhya Bharat ke kisi bhi gaon ya shehar mein.

Khaas taur par jab barsaat shuru hoti hai. Tab har gaon mein aam ya peepal ki daaliyon se bade jhoole bandhte hain. Aur gaon ki ladkiyan aur ladke ikatthe hokar us mauke ko manate hain.

Khusrau ne paheliyan aur bujho-khel bhi likhe, jo bachchon aur bado, dono ko pasand hain. Unke apne jeevan mein hi ye gaane mashhoor ho gaye the, aur wo shohrat badhti hi gayi.

[SFX: door se jhoole ki dhun, aur kitni ladkiyon ki gaati hui awaazein, dheemi]

Is episode ki shuruaat us jhoole par hui thi. Aur ab pata chala ki jo milan upar darbar mein ho raha tha, uska sabse gehra nishaan neeche gaon mein pada. Ek gaana, chhah sau saal se ek hi.

To neeche gaon mein aisi kaun si cheez thi jo itni pukhta thi? Iska jawab Nehru ek dhanche ki tasveer se dete hain.

Unke hisaab se us dhanche ke teen khambhe the.

## Part 7: Teen khambhe

[SFX: halki si kalam kaagaz par chalti hai; door se koi dikh nahi, bas akhbaar ki si sarsaraahat]

1944: Ahmadnagar ki kothri. Bahar duniya mein jung chal rahi hai.

Nehru us kothri mein ek ajeeb baat likhte hain. Unke hisaab se jo jhagda ab chal raha hai, wo sirf Bharat ka jhagda nahi hai. Wo Pashchim ka bhi hai. Poori duniya ka bhi, bas wahan uske roop alag hain.

Unka kehna hai ki 19vi sadi ki Europe wali sabhyata vyaktivaad ki sabse oonchi lakeer thi. Ab wo purani pad chuki hai, aur sankat aur jung ke bojh se toot rahi hai.

Aaj vyakti kam, aur samooh zyada gina jaane laga hai. Sawal: vyakti ke haq aur samooh ke haq, dono ko ek saath kaise rakha jaaye?

Aur yahin se Nehru jaati par aate hain. Unka tark: jaati akeli khadi nahi hoti. Wo ek bahut bade samajik dhanche ka hissa hai.

Ho sakta hai jaati ke kuch saaf aib hata diye jaayein, aur system bach jaye. Par Nehru kehte hain, aisa hona mushkil hai. Kyunki jo taaqatein kaam kar rahi hain, wo oopar ki deewar par nahi, dhanche ki jad par chot kar rahi hain.

Aur dhanche ko thaamne wale kai sahare pehle hi ja chuke hain. Jaati dheere-dheere akeli pad gayi hai.

Nehru ek saaf baat kehte hain: ab sawal ye nahi ki jaati pasand hai ya nahi. Badlaav humari pasand ke baawajood ho raha hai. Par use dhaalna aur disha dena humare haath mein hai.

Aur sirf tod kar phenk dena kaafi nahi. Nehru kehte hain, aage ki koi tasveer saamne rakhein — chahe dhundhli hi ho. Warna ek khaali jagah banegi, aur wo aise bharegi jiska baad mein pachhtana pade.

Kisi bhi scheme mein teen cheezein dekhni hain: kaam kis insaan ke saath karna hai, uska sochne ka background kya hai, aur mahaul kya hai. Ye sab chhod kar hawai plan banana, ya nakal karna, bewakoofi hai.

Isi liye pehle us purane dhanche ko samajhna hoga, jisne humare logon ko itna gehra banaya hai.

Wo dhancha teen khambhon par khada tha. Pehla: gaon ki apni sarkar. Doosra: jaati. Teesra: jukta parivaar.

Teenon mein ek hi baat chalti hai — samooh. Vyakti ki jagah doosri hai.

Nehru yahan imaandar hain. Wo kehte hain, alag-alag dekhne par in teenon mein khaas nayi baat nahi hai. Aise roop doosre deshon mein bhi milte hain, khaas taur par madhyakaal mein. Ganrajya jaise roop aur bhi jagah the. Ek tarah ka samuhik swatva bhi tha. Russia ka purana mir kisi tarah is gaon se milta tha.

Jaati ka kaam tha function aur seva — Europe ke madhyakaal ke trade guild jaise. Aur China ka parivaar-tantra Hindu jukte parivaar se bahut milta tha.

Unhe in sab ka itna pata nahi. Par jo cheez poori ki poori thi — wo nayi thi. Aur jaisa-jaisa ye dhancha bada hua, wo aur bhi apni tarah ka hota gaya.

To teen khambhe. Sabse pehle pehla. Gaon.

## Part 8: Gaon ki apni sarkar

Gaon mein ek chhoti si sarkar chalti thi. Uska naam tha panchayat. Aur uske paas apni kitab thi.

Nitisara — rajniti ka shastra, jise Shukracharya ne likha. Shukracharya — ek purane aacharya, jinke naam se ye 10vi sadi ki kitaab judi hai.

Ye kitaab un dinon ki tasveer deti hai jab Turk aur Afghan hamla shuru bhi nahi hua tha. Usme likha tha: markazi sarkar ka dhancha, raja ki council, alag-alag mahkame — aur shehar aur gaon ki zindagi.

Gaon ki panchayat chuni hui council hoti thi. Uske paas ammal ki taaqat bhi thi, aur nyay ki bhi. Raja ke adhikari us panchayat ke sadasya ki badi izzat karte the.

Zameen panchayat tay karti thi. Kis ko kitni milegi, ye wahin tay hota tha.

Aur tax? Panchayat hi fasal se hissa leti thi. Aur us hisse mein se sarkar ka hissa sarkar tak khud pahuncha deti thi. Yani gaon apni tax khud wasool karta tha, aur apne aadmi ke haath sarkar ko deta tha.

Ek gaon ke oopar ek badi panchayat bhi thi, jo zaroorat padne par dekhti aur dakhal deti thi.

Aur sadasya kaise chunte the? Ye sekdon saal purane pathar par khude hue lekh humein batate hain.

Har saal committee chuni jaati thi. Aur — ye dhyan se suniye — auraten bhi usme baith sakti thi.

Agar koi sadasya galat nikle, to use hata diya jaata tha. Aur ek aisi shart thi jo bahut aage ki soch lagti hai: agar wo sarkari paise ka hisaab dene mein chook gaya, to wo bahar.

Ek aur shart thi, jiska pura maksad hi ek hi tha — bhai-bhatijavaad rokna. Kisi sadasya ke kareebi rishtedaar ko sarkari naukri nahi mil sakti thi.

Aur panchayat apni azaadi par nakhra karti thi. Kisi bhi sainik ko raja ki likhit anumati ke bina gaon mein ghusne ki ijazat nahi thi.

Yani gaon ke darwaze par sarkar ki talwar ruk jaati thi.

## Part 9: Raja kis taraf khada hota tha

Ek sochiye. Gaon ka aadmi kisi sarkari aadmi se pareshaan ho, aur shikayat le kar jaaye. Raja kis taraf khada hota tha?

Nitisara kehta hai: raja apne adhikariyon ka saath nahi de. Wo apni praja ka saath de.

Agar bahut log shikayat karein, to us adhikari ko hata diya jaata tha. Aur uske liye Nehru kitaab se ek line uthate hain: kaun hai jo pad ka nasha peene ke baad chadhta nahi.

Aur raja ko kya karna tha? Praja ki bahumati ki raay ke hisaab se chalna tha.

Isi kitaab mein ek tasveer hai jo yaad rakhiye. Kehte hain: jan-mat raja se bhi zyada taaqatwar hota hai. Bilkul waisa hi jaise kai reshmon se bani ek rassi ek sher ko bhi kheech le jaati hai.

Ek resha kamzor. Kai reshe — rassi.

Aur naukriyan kis aadhar par deni thi? Ek line suniye: kaam, kirdaar aur kaabiliyat dekhi jaaye. Jaati nahi. Khandaan nahi.

Aur uske baad ek aur line, jo jaati ke zamane ke hisaab se bahut badi baat hai: na rang se, na purvajon se, wo aatma paida hoti hai jo ek Brahmin ka gaurav hai.

Yani dhanche ke baare mein likhne wali kitaabein khud keh rahi thi: asli cheez kaam hai.

Aur bade shehron mein? Wahan bahut kaarigar aur vyapaari the. Unki apni sansthayein thi — kaarigari ke sangh, vyapaar ke sangh, aur paisa lagane wale sangh. Har ek apne andar ka kaam khud dekhta tha.

Nehru maante hain ki ye jaankari tukdon mein mili hai. Par ye bhi saaf dikhta hai ki shehron aur gaon mein sangathanit swaraaj ka poora tanta tha.

Markazi sarkar andar nahi ghusi, jab tak uska tax ka hissa aata raha. Reet ka kanoon majboot tha. Siyasi ya fauji taaqat reet se bane haqon par kam hi haath daalti thi.

Zameen ka purana dhancha mil-jul kar chalne wala gaon tha. Har vyakti aur parivaar ke kuch haq the, aur kuch zimmedariyan bhi. Dono reet ke kanoon se tay hote the.

## Part 10: Jaati: ek bada dhancha

Jaati ko Nehru ek group system kehte hain. Yani kaam aur seva par bana hua samooh.

Ye sabko andar lene ke liye banaya gaya tha. Isme koi ek aam aqeeda zaroori nahi tha. Har group ko poori chhoot thi.

Isi liye iske andar ek shaadi, kai shaadiyan, aur bina shaadi ka jeewan — teenon chalte the. Jaise doosre riwaj aur tareeqe chalte the. Zindagi ko har star par chalte rehna tha.

Aur ek ajeeb si baat. Is dhanche mein kisi chhote samooh ko bade samooh ke saamne jhukna nahi tha. Wo apna alag, khud-mukhtar group bana sakta tha. Sirf ek kasauti thi: kya wo itna bada hai ki apna kaam khud chala sake? Do groups ke beech nasal, dharm, rang ya sanskriti ka koi bhi farq ho sakta tha.

Aur vyakti? Vyakti sirf apne group ka sadasya gina jaata tha.

Wo jo chahe kar sakta tha — bas group ka kaam na bigde. Usko bigaadne ka haq uske paas nahi tha.

Par ek rasta khula tha. Agar wo kaafi mazboot ho, aur apne saath kaafi log jod le, to naya group bana sakta tha.

Aur agar wo kisi bhi group mein fit na ho paaye? To wo duniya ke samajik kaam se bahar ho gaya. Phir wo sanyasi ban sakta tha — jaati chhod deta, har group chhod deta, kaam-kaaj ki duniya chhod deta. Aur jahan chahe ghoomta, jo chahe karta.

Yahan ek ulti si baat bhi hai. Samaj ka jhukav vyakti ko samooh ke neeche dabaata tha. Par dharma ki soch aur aatma ki khoj hamesha vyakti ko oopar rakhti thi.

Mukti aur param satya ka gyaan sabke liye tha — har jaati ka, oonchi ya neechi. Ye mukti group ki cheez ho hi nahi sakti thi. Aur uski khoj mein bhi koi sakht aqeeda zaroori nahi tha. Kehte the, har darwaza usi taraf le jaata hai.

Isliye Bharat mein vyaktivaad ki dhaara hamesha rahi. Aur dono dhaaraon ke beech takraav bhi dikhta rehta hai.

Nehru ek baat par dhyan dete hain. Jo log jaati ko chunauti dete the, wo aksar dharm ke sudhaarak hote the. Unka tark yahi tha: jaati ke bantware aatma ki unnati ke raste mein aa jaate hain.

Buddhism isi group-jaati ke aadarsh se ek toot tha — vyaktivaad ki taraf, aur sabko ek maanne ki taraf.

Par us vyaktivaad ke saath ek kamzori jud gayi. Wo aam samajik kaam se kat kar peechhe hat jaane laga, aur usne jaati ke saamne ek doosra kaam ka dhancha khada nahi kiya. Isliye jaati chalti rahi — us zamane mein bhi, aur uske baad bhi.

## Part 11: Chaar jaatiyan, ek hissa

To is dhanche mein kaun kaun tha?

Sabse pehle ek baat. Jo log jaati se bilkul bahar maane jaate the, unhe ek taraf rakh dijiye. Baaki chaar the.

Brahmin: pujaari, shikshak, sochne-samajhne wale log.

Kshatriya: raja aur yodha.

Vaishya: vyapaari, karobar aur paisa lagane wale.

Aur Shudra: kheti aur doosre kaam karne wale.

Nehru kehte hain ki shayad ek hi jaati poori tarah band thi — Brahmin. Kshatriya apni ginti badhate rehte the: kai bahar se aaye, kai yahan ke hi, jo taaqat mein badhe. Vaishya zyadatar vyapaar aur paisa lagane ka kaam karte the, aur kai doosre peshe bhi. Shudra zyadatar kheti aur ghar ke kaam mein.

Aur ek process chalta rehta tha: naye peshe banate, to nayi jaatiyan bhi banti. Aur purani jaatiyan seedhi par oopar chadhne ki koshish karti rahti thi. Ye aaj tak chalta hai — Nehru bhi ye likhte hain. Kabhi koi neechi jaati achanak wahi janeu pehen leti hai, jo sirf oopar ki jaatiyon ke liye tha.

Par isse zyada farq nahi padta tha. Har jaati apne ghere mein apna kaam karti rehti. Ye sirf izzat ka sawal tha.

Kabhi-kabhi neechi class ka koi aadmi apni kaabiliyat se sarkar mein badi jagah par pahunch jaata tha. Par ye bahut hi kam hota tha.

Aur kyunki us samaj mein muqabla aur jyada kamai kaam nahi thi, jaati ki lakeerein utna farq nahi daalti thi.

Oopar Brahmin tha — gyaan par garv, logon ki izzat. Par daulat uske paas kam hi hoti thi. Vaishya ameer the. Par poore samaaj mein unki haisiyat bahut oonchi nahi thi.

Aur ek badi baat. Is poore dhanche mein zyadatar log kisan the.

Yahan zamindari nahi thi, aur kisan ki apni milkiyat bhi nahi. Kanun ke hisaab se zameen kiski thi, ye kehna mushkil tha. Aaj ki tarah "maalik" ka koi khayal hi nahi tha.

Kisan ko sirf ek haq tha: wo us zameen par kheti kar sakta tha. Asli sawal fasal ka batwara tha.

Fasal ka bada hissa kisan ke paas jaata tha. Raja ya sarkar ka hissa, aam taur par chhatha. Aur ek hissa gaon ke har us group ka, jo logon ki koi seva karta tha.

Sochiye us fehrist ko. Pujaari aur shikshak. Vyapaari. Lohar. Badhai. Chamar. Kumhar. Raaj. Nai. Bhangi.

Sarkar se lekar safai karne wale tak — gaon ki fasal mein har ek ka hissa tha. Poora gaon ek hisse-daar company ki tarah tha.

Ab ek sawal bacha. Jo log is fehrist se bahar rakhe gaye, unka kya?

## Part 12: List se bahar wale

Wo kuch log the jinke paas hissa nahi tha. Unhe hum ab "depressed classes" kehte hain.

Naam naya hai. Ye un jaatiyon ke liye bola jaata hai jo seedhi ke sabse neeche paayedaan par hain. Unhe alag karne ki koi saaf lakeer nahi hai.

Jo saaf hai, wo untouchables hain. Uttar Bharat mein bahut thode log untouchable maane jaate the — sirf wo, jo gandagi ka kaam karte the.

Fa-Hsien — ek Chini tirthyatri, jo Nehru ke zamane se lagbhag pandrah sau saal pehle Bharat aaya tha — likhte hain ki us waqt wo log untouchable the jo insaan ka mal hataate the. Dakshin mein unki ginti bahut badi thi.

Ye shuruaat kaise hui, ye tay karna mushkil hai. Andaaza ye hai ki jo peshe gande maane jaate the, unhe karne wale untouchable kar diye gaye. Baad mein be-zameen khet mazdoor bhi isme jod diye gaye.

Ek shakal dekhiye. Aap apni jaati ke logon ke saath khaate the. Doosri jaati ke saath nahi. Ye rivaj duniya mein kahin aur itni khatarnak shakal mein nahi milta.

Isse bhi badi rok shaadi par thi. Kabhi-kabhi mili-juli shaadiyan hoti bhi thi. Par Nehru likhte hain, ye bahut ajeeb hai ki har jaati apne aap mein kitni bandh rahi.

Nasal banaye rakhna ek bhram hai. Phir bhi itni sadiyon mein jaati ne kuch khaas shakalein bacha li — khaas taur par oonchi jaatiyon mein.

Aur ek aakhri baat. Koi group "jaati se bahar" nahi hai. Untouchables bhi nahi. Wo bhi apni jaati banate the, aur apne faisle apni panchayat se karte the. Par sach ye bhi hai: unmein se bahut logon ko gaon ki aam zindagi se bahar rakh kar takleef di gayi.

Aur ye poora dhancha America jaisi vyaktivaad wali sabhyata ka bilkul ulta tha. Wahan har aadmi apna fayda dekhta hai. Jo tez hai, wo aage. Jo kamzor hai, wo deewar se takra jaata hai.

## Part 13: Teen khambhe, ek hisaab

Aaj Hindustan mein jukta parivaar tezi se toot raha hai. Vyaktivaad badh raha hai. Isse aarthik zindagi mein badi tabdeeliyan aa rahi hain — aur baartav ki nayi pareshaaniyan bhi.

Ab poora hisaab kijiye. Teenon khambhe samooh par khade the, vyakti par nahi. Maksad tha samaajik suraksha, sthirta, aur samooh ka chalta rehna. Taraqqi maksad nahi thi. Isliye taraqqi ko nuksaan uthana pada.

Har group ke andar ek saanjhi zindagi thi, barabari ka ehsaas tha, aur faisle aapas mein milkar hote the. Aaj bhi jaati ki panchayatein usi tarah faisle karti hain.

Nehru apna ek tajurba jodte hain. Unhe hairaani hui thi ki ek gaon ka aadmi — kabhi padha-likha bhi nahi — chuni hui committee mein baithne ke liye kitna utaavla rehta tha. Jab uski zindagi ka koi sawal aata, wo jhukta nahi tha. Ek dukh ki baat bhi thi: chhote-chhote group aapas mein ladte the.

Par faayde bhi the. Isi liye ye dhancha itni lamba chala. Iske peechhe Bharat ka darshan tha: insaan ko jodna, aur kamane-ganthne se zyada achchhai, sundarta aur sach par zor dena. Ek koshish ye bhi thi ki izzat, taaqat aur daulat ek jagah jama na ho jaayein.

Aur ek cheez hamesha dohrai gayi: farz. Adhikaar nahi. Smriti — purane dharm-granth — jaati ke dharm, kaam aur farz ki fehrist dete hain. Par unme se kisi ek mein adhikaaron ki fehrist nahi hai.

Yahi is dhanche ki taaqat thi. Aur yahi uski bedi.

Ye bandh system tha. Andar thodi jagah thi — badlaav ki, apnapan ki, thodi azaadi ki. Par wo dhire-dhire sakht hota gaya.

Wo nayi pratibha ko apne mein lene ki taaqat kho baithta. Purane fayde jinse kai log jude the, unhone koi bada badlaav hone hi nahi diya. Shiksha doosri classon tak pahunch hi nahi payi. Jo andhvishwas oopar ki class jaanti thi ki andhvishwas hain, unhe bachaya gaya — aur naye bhi jod diye gaye.

Nateeja? Sirf arth-vyavastha nahi — soch khud band, paramparik, sakht aur bina taraqqi ki ho gayi.

[SFX: barsaat ki halki awaaz laut kar aati hai; door se jhoole ki rassi]

Ab wapas us aam ki daali ke neeche chaliye. Barsaat ki pehli shaam. Ladkiyan jhoole par baithi hain. Aur wahi gaana jo chhah sau saal pehle likha gaya tha.

Ab jawab saaf hai.

Raja badalte rahe — kyunki raja upar ki cheez thi. Sultan aata tha, jaata tha.

Par aam zindagi raja ke haath mein thi hi nahi. Wo gaon ki panchayat par khadi thi, jo apni zameen baantti thi aur apna tax jama karti thi. Wo jaati ke dhanche par khadi thi. Aur wo jukte parivaar par khadi thi.

Teen khambhe. Upar ki har aandhi unhe hilaa na saki. Unhone neeche ki zindagi ko suraksha di — aur badle mein taraqqi maang li.

Isliye upar duniya badalti rahi. Aur neeche, ek gaana chhah sau saal se ek hi raha.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli: Nehru kehte hain ki mili-juli sanskriti ka sabse gehra nishaan gaon tak nahi pahuncha. Ab ek kaam kijiye. Aaj apne roz ke shabd giniye. Apni rasoi dekhiye. Jitna hum "apna" maante hain, usme kitna hissa un logon ka hai jinke naam hum jaante bhi nahi?

Doosri: Nehru ka tark tha ki jaati ke aib hata kar dhancha bachaya nahi ja sakta. Kyunki jo taaqatein kaam kar rahi hain, wo oopar ki deewar par nahi, jad par chot kar rahi hain. Ye kasauti aaj bhi kaam ki hai. Kisi bhi badlaav se poochhiye: ye sirf oopar saja hua hai, ya jad tak gaya hai?

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapki zindagi mein wo kaun si cheez hai jo teen peedhiyon se nahi badli? Aur wo aapko thaam rahi hai, ya rok rahi hai?

## Outro

Gaon ki panchayat, jaati, jukta parivaar — teenon ne neeche ki zindagi itni thaam li ki upar ka koi toofan unhe hilaa na saka.

Par upar bhi kuch naya aane wala tha: ek khandaan, jo bahar se aayega aur dheere-dheere yahin ka ho jaayega. Uske sabse bade badshah ka darbar poore Asia mein mashhoor hoga.

Aur ek din us darbar mein kaale chogon wale do Portuguese ek moti, chhapi hui kitaab rakhenge. Badshah use kholenge. Phir band kar denge.

Aur us kitaab ke baare mein ek sawal koi nahi poochhega. Wo chhapi kaise?

Agli baar: Fatehpur Sikri, ek chhapi hui kitaab, aur wo sawal jo kisi ne nahi poochha.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
