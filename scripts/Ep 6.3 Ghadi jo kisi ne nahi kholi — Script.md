## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Ek qaidi, jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Is set mein Islam pehle dharm bankar aaya, aur baad mein talwar bankar. Pichhli baar humne dekha ki raja badalte rahe, par neeche aam zindagi waisi hi thi. Aaj hum ek aise badshah ke paas jaate hain, jo sach mein badalna chahta tha.

## Part 1: Panipat ka ajnabi

Panipat ka maidan. Wahi maidan, jahan Bharat ki kismat kai baar tay hui hai. Saal 1526.

Ek fauj ne Delhi ki gaddi jeet li. Uska sardar Babar tha.

Babar — Central Asia se aaya ek Turco-Mongol, Timur ke vansh ka, jisne Panipat mein Delhi jeeti.

Garmi ki dopahar hai. Ek shamiyana. Babar baithe hain, aur likh rahe hain.

Delhi mil gayi hai. Agra mein nayi rajdhani ban rahi hai, aur uske liye unhone Constantinople se ek mashhoor vaastukar bulwaya hai — udhar Suleiman the Magnificent ka daur tha, aur wahan imaaratein uthti thi.

Par Babar khush nahi hain.

Yahan ki mitti unhe ajnabi lagti hai. Wo apna watan yaad karte hain. Ferghana — Central Asia ki wo ghati, jahan unhone bachpan dekha. Wahan ki barf. Wahan ke phool aur phal. Wahan ki baatein, aur doston ki mehfil.

Yahan unhe wo sab nahi milta.

Par phir bhi wo kalam utaarte hain, aur ek baat likhte hain: Hindustan ek kamaal ka accha desh hai.

Sochiye. Ek aadmi, jise yahan kuch bhi apna nahi lagta — aur wo phir bhi is desh ki tareef likh raha hai.

Aur Babar ke paas ek cheez hai, jo unhe peechhe ke hamlavaron se alag karti hai.

Wo Central Asia ke us mahaul mein bade hue the, jise aaj Timurid Renaissance kehte hain. Wahan Iran ka asar tha, aur wahan kala, imaaratein aur rehan-sehan phir se phal rahi thi. Usi mahaul ne Babar ko kala ka shauqeen banaya. Kitaabon ka. Aur acche khaane-peene ka.

Nehru unhe apne zamane ka ek asli Renaissance rajkumar kehte hain. Bahadur. Jaane-parast. Zindagi se pyaar karne wala.

Chaar saal. Bas chaar saal. Panipat ke baad itni hi der yahan kamaayi. Saara waqt ladte hue, aur Agra basate hue nikal gaya.

Ab ek doosri tasveer dekhiye. Usi zamane ka dakshin.

Wahan ek shehar hai — Vijayanagar. Europe se aaye yaatri uske baare mein likh kar jaate hain: itni kala, itni nafasat, itna aish, jaise duniya mein kahin aur na ho.

Par jahan Babar aaye, wahan uttar ki haalat alag thi. Wahan andar ki thakaan dikh rahi thi.

Kaise pata chali?

Soch kar amal karna ruk gaya tha. Yakeen jam gaye the. Samaj ka dhaancha sakht ho gaya tha. Aur jab soch nahi hilti, to zindagi bhi nahi hilti.

Isi thaki hui mitti par aage ek nayi cheez giri. Par wo kiski thi? Us sawal ka jawab Babar ke pote ke paas hai.

## Part 2: Do thaki hui duniyaon ka milan

Pehle ek baat samajh lijiye, jo Nehru har hamle par kehte hain.

Hamla bura hota hai. Lekin uske andar ek cheez chhupi hoti hai. Wo band aadmi ka khol tod deta hai. Aadmi ko dekhna padta hai ki bahar ki duniya us se badi hai, aur us se ajeeb bhi.

Afghan aaye, aur unka asar is desh par pada. Mughal aaye, aur unka asar aur gehra tha. Kyunki Mughal Afghanon se zyada sanskaari the, aur jeene ke dhang mein zyada aage.

Wo apne saath Iran ki nafasat laaye. Darbar chalane ka ek poora, sakht tareeqa — kya pehnna hai, kaise baithna hai, kis se kaise baat karni hai. Amiron ki zindagi ka dhang hi badal gaya.

Dakshin mein Bahmani raajya ka Iran se seedha rishta tha, Calicut ke raaste.

Ye sab ek nayi hawa thi. Par Nehru yahan ek kadwi baat jodte hain.

Ye dono sabhyatayein nayi nahi thi. Dono purani thi. Dono apni jawaani jee chuki thi. Dono sakht saanche mein dhal chuki thi.

Bharatiya sanskriti bahut purani thi, aur thak chuki thi. Arab-Irani sanskriti apni oonchaai paar kar chuki thi. Aur ek cheez jo kabhi Arabon ki pehchaan thi — nayi cheez jaane ki bechaini — wo ab kahin nahi thi.

To ye do thake hue logon ka milan tha.

Ab Babar ke pote ki baat kijiye.

Akbar. Bahadur aur bejhijhak. Kamaal ke senapati. Par saath mein narm-dil, aur daya se bhare hue. Ek sapne dekhne wale, aur ek kaam karne wale — dono ek hi aadmi mein. Aisa neta, jiske peeche log jaan de dete the.

Unke darbar ke Portuguese paadriyon ne unki aankhon ka haal likha hai: dhoop mein chamakte samundar jaisi.

Aur unke andar ek purana sapna phir se aakaar lene lagta hai. Ek Bharat. Sirf naqshe par ek, nahi. Ek aisa Bharat, jo andar se ek hi log ho jaaye.

Ye baat yaad rakhiye. Yahi sapna aage chalkar Akbar ki taaqat bhi hoga, aur unki hadd bhi.

## Part 3: Pachaas saal, ek daanv

Ab waqt aage badhaiye. Saal 1556 se Akbar ka raaj shuru hota hai, aur lagbhag pachaas saal chalta hai.

Unka asli daanv talwar nahi tha.

Is desh mein aise Rajput sardar the, jo kisi ke aage nahi jhukte the. Akbar ne unhe apne saath kiya. Shaadi ke rishte se bhi. Unhone ek Rajput rajkumari se byaah kiya.

Iska nateeja kya hua?

Unke bete Jehangir aadhe Mughal the, aadhe Rajput. Unke pote Shah Jahan ki maa bhi Rajput thi.

Sochiye. Teen peedhi. Aur ek Turco-Mongol khandan khoon se zyada Hindustani ho gaya.

Unhe Rajputon mein apnapan lagta tha, kyunki unme wo hi baatein thi jo Akbar ke andar thi. Bejhijhak bahaduri. Izzat ka dhyan. Aur zubaan ki pakki baat.

Is daanv ne unki saltanat ko bahut mazboot kiya. Aur iska asar sirf raajniti aur fauj par nahi pada. Kala par pada, jeene ke dhang par bhi. Mughal amir dheere-dheere Hindustani hote gaye, aur Rajput log Iran ki sanskriti se rangte gaye.

Sirf ek aadmi nahi jhuka. Mewar ke Rana Pratap — Rajputana ka wo sardar, jo kisi baahar wale ke aage nahi jhukta. Unhone jangal mein bhaagte rehna chuna, par ek baar bhi us aadmi ko salaam nahi kiya, jise wo baahar se aaya hamlavar maante the.

Akbar ke aas-paas kamaal ke log jama hue. Faizi aur Abul Fazl — do bhai. Aur Birbal. Unka darbar har dharm ka, aur har naye khayal ka, ek adda ban gaya. Koi naya tarika, koi nayi cheez — wo wahan pahunchti thi, aur wahan uski baat hoti thi.

Unki sahansheelta itni chaudi thi ki kattar maulvi naraaz ho gaye. Akbar ne to ek naya dharm hi chalane ki koshish ki, jo sabko jod de.

Nateeja ye nikla ki wo Hinduon mein bhi utne hi lokpriya the, jitne Musalmaanon mein. Mughal vansh Bharat ka apna vansh ban gaya.

Par isi jigyasu darbar mein ek cheez thi — jise kisi ne khola hi nahi.

## Part 4: Darbar mein ek ghadi

Fatehpur Sikri. Wahi daalan. Wahi chhapi hui kitaab.

Akbar kitaab ek taraf rakh dete hain, aur agle kaam par lag jaate hain. Koi nahi poochta ki ye panne chhape kaise.

Ye kitni ajeeb baat hai.

Kyunki Akbar ke baare mein paadri likhte hain ki wo sab kuch jaanna chahte the. Dharam bhi, jung bhi, aur mashin bhi. Unhe jangi hatyaar aur kaarigari ke tareeqe samajh aate the. Jangi haathi unhe bahut pyaare the.

Unki jigyasa ki misaal paadri aisi dete hain: wo sab kuch ek saath seekhna chahte the, jaise ek bhookha aadmi apna poora khana ek hi kaur mein nigal jaaye.

To phir sawal aur bada ho jaata hai. Teen jagah dekhiye.

Pehli. Top-khana. Mughal fauj ki topon ka bharosa videshi visheshagyon par tha — zyadatar Ottoman Turkon par. Top-khane ke mukhiya ka khitaab hi aisa tha: Rumi Khan. Rum, yaani poorab ka Rome — Constantinople. Ye videshi log yahan ke aadmiyon ko train karte the.

Par koi apne aadmi ko baahar seekhne kyun nahi bhejta? Koi research kyun nahi karwaata?

Doosri. Chhapai.

Ek chhapi hui Bible unke haath mein aayi thi. Aur paadri ek-do aur chhapi kitaabein bhi laaye the. Ek samrajya ke liye ye kitni badi cheez thi — farmaan, hisaab, hukumnama, sab ek saancha, ek naap mein, hazaaron copies mein.

Phir bhi koi ye nahi poochta ki ye bana kaise. Chhapakhana mangvane ki baat koi nahi karta.

Teesri. Ghadi.

Spring wali ghadiyan Portuguese laaye, aur baad mein Angrez. Mughal amiron ke ghar mein ye shaan ki cheez ban gayi. Aam log dhoop-ghadi, ret-ghadi aur paani-ghadi se kaam chalaate rahe.

Par ek bhi aadmi nahi utha, jisne ghadi khol kar dekha ho ki andar kya hai. Ye chal rahi hai kaise? Ye kamani kahan se aayi?

Aur ye us desh mein hua, jahan duniya ke behtareen kaarigar the. Jahan lohar aur badhai apne haath se wo cheezein banate the, jinhe Europe ke log sirf sapne mein soch sakte the.

Ek kaarigar ki jigyasa aur ek samrajya ki jigyasa — dono ek hi darbar mein. Par dono kabhi mile nahi.

Aaj sochiye. Unn dino us darbar mein har dharm ki behas hoti thi. Har naye khayal ki. Par kaarigari ki ek behas nahi hoti thi.

To sawal ye hai: ye bimari sirf Bharat ki thi?

## Part 5: Ghadiyan jo gum ho gayi

Nahi. Nehru saaf kehte hain — ye sirf Bharat ki bimari nahi thi.

Poora pashchim aur madhya Asia isse zyada grasit tha. Shayad China bhi.

Ye log amaan nahi the. Bharat aur China, dono, pehle vigyaan ke kai hisson mein aage badhe the. Jahaazbani. Door-door ka samundari vyapaar. Ye cheezein machine mein sudhaar ki ek lagatar khinchaav deti thi.

Par sadiyon tak na yahan koi bada machine ka aavishkar hua, na wahan. Us waqt ki duniya, hazaar ya do hazaar saal pehle wali duniya se bahut alag nahi thi.

Aur Arabon ki kahani to aur bhi dukh bhari hai.

Khud Arabon ne sabse pehle amal wala vigyaan shuru kiya tha. Kaha jaata hai ki sabse shuruaati ghadiyan Arabon ne hi banayi thi — saatvin sadi mein. Damascus mein ek mashhoor ghadi thi. Aur Harun-al-Rashid ke Baghdad mein bhi.

Phir Baghdad gira, Arab taaqat kam hui. Aur dekhiye kya hua. Ghadi banane ka hunar wahan se gaayab ho gaya.

Aur jahan wo hunar kho gaya, wahan Europe ke kuch deshon mein ghadiyan aisi cheez hi nahi rahi — wahan ye aam hoti gayi.

Chhapai ki kahani suniye.

Yahan chhape hone se bahut pehle, Spain ke Moorish Arab, lakdi ke thappon se chhapai karte the. Unka raajya ise apne farmaan aur hukumnama guna karne ke liye istemaal karta tha.

Par wo thappa aage nahi badha. Aur baad mein wo bhi mit gaya.

Udhar Ottoman Turk. Wo Europe aur pashchim Asia mein sabse badi Musalmaan taaqat the. Unke darwaze par, unke bilkul paas, Europe mein kitaabein hazaaron ki tadaad mein chhap rahi thi.

Unhe iska pata tha. Par unhone sadiyon tak chhapakhana apnaya hi nahi.

Wajah? Ek, koi khinchaav hi nahi tha. Doosri, dharm ki bezatti ka darr. Kya pata chhape hue panne kisi ne utha liye, pairon ke neeche aa gaye, ya koode ke dher mein chale gaye.

Chhapakhana Misr mein baad mein koi baahar se le kar aaya. Aur wahan se wo dheere-dheere phaila.

To udhar Europe mein kya ho raha tha?

## Part 6: Ek nayi aadat

Europe mein Renaissance ho chuka tha. Usne Europe ke mann ki kai purani zanjeerein tod di thi, aur kai aise buthaan gira diye the, jinhe log seene se laga kar baithaaye the.

Aur ek nayi aadat jag uthi. Cheezon ko gaur se dekhne ki. Poochne ki — ye sach hai kya? Ye kaam kaise karta hai? Ye baat kis buniyad par khadi hai?

Ye aadat purane adhikaar ko bhi chunauti deti thi. Aur un khokhli, dhundhli baaton ko bhi, jo sunne mein bahut gehri lagti hain.

Us sadi ke ek angrez darshanik ne ek baat likhi, jiska saar ye hai: insaan ki taaqat aur insaan ka gyaan, dono ek hi raaste par chalte hain. Aur behtar ye hai ki vigyaan ki neev un buniyaadon par uthai jaaye, jo amal se judi hain.

Saikde saal baad, ek doosre angrez lekhak ne bhi isi mahaul mein kuch kaha. Uska asar aage chalkar bahut hua — par uska asli quote hamari copy mein khaali hai. Usne bhi cheezon ko beech mein rakhe bina, seedhe amal se dekhne ki baat ki.

Ab dhyaan dijiye. Akbar ki sadi kaun si thi? Solahvin.

Aur Europe mein solahvin sadi ne ek cheez ko janm diya — gati ka vigyaan. Ye koi chhoti baat nahi thi. Ye insaani zindagi mein ek inqilaab tha.

Us ek khoj ke saath Europe chal pada. Shuru mein dheere. Phir badhti raftaar se. Aur unneesvin sadi tak usne ek nayi duniya khadi kar di.

Udhar Asia? Asia statik tha. So raha tha. Purane dhang se apna kaam chala raha tha — insaan ki mehnat aur paseene ke sahaare.

Aur sabse dilchasp baat ye hai. Us waqt ek Bharatiya yaatri ko Europe kai maamlon mein pichhda aur ganwaar lagta. Bharat ka vyapaar, kaarigari, darbar — sab chamak raha tha.

Par Europe ke andar wo bechaini jaag rahi thi, jo yahan kisi ke andar nahi thi.

To phir sawal ye hai: ye thakaan aakhir aayi kahan se?

## Part 7: Andar se toota

Nehru ka jawab bahut saaf hai.

Koi bhi sabhyata bahar ke hamle se kam girti hai, aur andar ki thakaan se zyada.

Kaise? Teen tareeqe hain.

Ek. Wo apna kaam poora kar chuki hoti hai. Uske paas ek badalti hui duniya ko dene ke liye kuch bacha hi nahi hota.

Do. Jo log us sabhyata ko uthaye hue hain, unki chaandi aa jaati hai. Wo uska bojh sambhal nahi paate.

Teen. Us samaj ka rehan-sehan hi ek deewar ban jaata hai. Uske aage badhna hi nahi ho sakta, jab tak wo deewar gire na, ya koi aisi nayi cheez na aaye jo sab kuch hilaa de.

Aur ye girta hua dhaancha Turk aur Afghan hamlon se bahut pehle hi dikh raha tha.

To kya in hamlon se kuchh naya hua?

Kuch hua. Kala nayi hui. Imaaratein nayi hui. Chitrakari, sangeet, jeene ke dhang — sab par asar pada.

Par ye asar zameen tak nahi pahuncha. Ye upar hi reh gaya. Samaj wahi ka wahi raha. Aur kuch maamlon mein to wo pehle se bhi sakht ho gaya.

Afghan koi nayi cheez apne saath nahi laaye the. Wo khud ek purane dhaanche ke log the — tabeli aur feudali.

Bharat mein Europe jaisa feudalism nahi tha. Par Rajput kul, jo is desh ki suraksha ki reedh ki haddi the, kisi tarah usi purane dhaanche mein bandhe the.

Mughal bhi aadhe feudali the. Fark ye tha ki unke beech mein ek mazboot raja khada tha. Aur us raja ne Rajputana ke aadhe-adhure feudalism ko kuchal diya.

Aur Akbar? Kya Akbar badlaav laa sakte the?

Nehru kehte hain, ho sakta tha.

Agar unki bechain jigyasa samaj ki taraf mud jaati, aur agar wo ye jaan-na chahte ki duniya ke doosre hisson mein kya ho raha hai, to wo badlaav ki neev rakh sakte the.

Par wo apni saltanat jodne mein itne lage hue the.

Aur unke saamne ek badi gaath thi: Islam, jo naye logon ko jodna chahta hai — aur is desh ke logon ka apna dharm, apni reet. Dono ko ek saath kaise chalayein? Ek rashtra kaise banayein?

Isliye unhone dharm ko tark ki roshni mein samajhne ki koshish ki. Aur kuch samay ke liye maamla badla hua bhi lagta tha.

Par ye seedha raasta nahi chala. Aur duniya mein kahin bhi aise seedhe raaste kam hi chalte hain.

## Part 8: Sau saal ki imaarat

Nateeja ye hua ki Akbar ne jo imaarat khadi ki, wo unke baad sau saal tak khadi rahi. Kamzor vaaris hone ke bawajood.

Kyunki Mughalon ke andar ek purani bimari thi. Har badshah ke baad, bhai aur rajkumar aapas mein gaddi ke liye ladte the. Har jung saltanat ki jaan kha jaati thi.

Phir bhi darbar ki raunak kam nahi hui. Grand Mughal ki shohrat Asia se Europe tak phaili.

Aur imaaratein banti rahi. Agra aur Delhi mein aisi imaaratein utthin, jinme purani Bharatiya soch aur ek nayi saadgi, ek nayi khoobsurat lakeer mili.

Ye Indo-Mughal kala us zamane ke mandiron se bilkul alag thi, jinme ek thakaan dikh rahi thi — har deewar par zyada nakashi, sone ka zyada paani, itna zyada ki aankh ko kahin aaram na mile.

Aur isi daur mein, imaaratiyon ne apne pyaar bhare haathon se ek imaarat khadi ki. Agra ka Taj Mahal.

Ab aakhri Grand Mughal ki baat kijiye.

Nehru yahan ek mohavra likhte hain. Aurangzeb ne ghadi ulti ghoomane ki koshish ki. Aur is koshish mein usne ghadi rok di — aur ghadi tod di.

Mughal badshah tab tak mazboot the, jab tak wo is desh ke mann ke saath chale. Jab tak unhone ek saajhi rashtriyata banane par kaam kiya. Jab tak unhone is desh ke saare hisson ko jodne ki koshish ki.

Jis din Aurangzeb is dhaare ke khilaaf khade hue, use dabane lage, aur khud ko Bharatiya raja se zyada ek Musalmaan raja ki tarah chalane lage — usi din Mughal saltanat bikharne lagi.

Akbar ka kaam mitne laga. Wo saari taaqatein, jinhe Akbar ne ek deewar ke peeche baandh rakha tha, deewar tod kar baahar aa gayin.

Nayi lehrein uthi. Unki soch tang thi, par unme ek ubharti rashtriyata thi. Wo itni mazboot nahi thi ki kuch khada kar sakein, aur waqt unke saath nahi tha. Par itni zaroor thi ki Mughal samrajya ko tod sakein.

## Part 9: Khola darwaza, band deewar

Jab bahar se log aaye, to ek cheez hui jo achhi thi. Is desh ke apne samaj ki buraayian saamne aa gayin.

Jaati ka patthar ho jaana. Chhua-chhoot. Apne aap ko doosron se alag rakhne ki wo zid, jo hadd se bhi aage chali gayi thi.

Aur Islam ka ek vichaar yahan bahut logon ko kheench gaya — bhai-chaare ka vichaar. Sabke baraabar hone ka vichaar.

Kise gaya? Us aadmi ko, jise baraabari ka koi chehra dikha hi nahi tha.

Isi soch se kai andolan uthe, jo dharmon ko jodna chahte the. Aur bahut log dharm badal bhi gaye. Inmein zyadatar neechi jaatiyon ke log the, khaas kar Bengal mein. Kuch oonchi jaati ke log bhi badle — kabhi sach mein yakeen badla tha, aur aksar isliye ki raja ka dharm apnane mein fayda tha.

Kashmir ka kissa suniye.

Wahan sadiyon tak dharm badalte rehne se, lagbhag poore log Musalmaan ban gaye. Par unhone apne purane Hindu riwaaz chhode nahi.

Unneesvin sadi ke beech mein, wahan ke Hindu raja ko pata chala ki bahut bade hisse ke log wapas Hindu ho jaana chahte hain — ek saath, poore samooh ke roop mein.

Raja ne Banaras ke panditon ke paas ek mandali bheji, aur poocha: kya ye mumkin hai?

Panditon ne mana kar diya. Aur baat wahin khatam ho gayi.

Ab ek aisi baat, jise Nehru dohrte hain.

Bahar se aaye Musalmaan yahan koi nayi technique nahi laaye. Koi naya aarthik dhaancha nahi laaye. Koi naya banane ka tareeqa nahi laaye.

Bhai-chaare mein yakeen ke bawajood, wo khud jaati mein bandhe hue the, aur feudal soch ke the. Utpaadan aur karkhane chalane ke maamle mein, is desh mein jo pehle se tha, wo us se kamzor tha.

Isliye is desh ki arthvyavastha par unka asar bahut thoda raha. Zindagi waisi hi chalti rahi. Aur sab log — Hindu ho ya Musalmaan — usi zindagi mein dhal gaye.

## Part 10: Khankhana ka tambu

To kya Hindu aur Musalmaan, dono saath rehne lage?

Do jawab hain. Aur dono sach hain.

Pehla jawab: doori.

Jaati ne gorakh-dhandha kiya. Aapas mein shaadiyan na ke barabar hui. Jo hui bhi, wo aksar ek Hindu aurat ka Musalmaan dharm mein jaana thi. Saath khaana-peena bhi us tarah nahi tha, jis tarah ek hi ghar ke log saath khaate hain.

Aur auraton ka parda. Ye deewar sabse oonchi thi. Musalmaan amiron mein parda aur bhi sakht tha. Hindu aur Musalmaan mard to roz milte the. Par dono taraf ki auraton ko ye mauka hi nahi milta tha.

Nateeja? Oonche gharon ki auratein ek-doosre se itni alag ho gayin ki dono taraf do alag soch ke samooh ban gaye.

Nehru ek kadwi baat bhi jodte hain. Aurat ko batlaya gaya ki uska sabse bada gun pavitrata hai, aur sabse bada paap uski pavitrata ka jaana. Par ye niyam aadmi ne khud par laagu nahi kiya. Aur Jahangir ke daur mein likhi Tulsidas ki Ramayan bhi, unki raay mein, aurat ki ek-taraf tasveer banati hai.

Ab doosra jawab. Gaon. Aur gaon mein is desh ke zyadatar log.

Wahan zindagi ka dhaancha hi saajha tha. Ek hi seema ke andar, Hindu aur Musalmaan ka rishta gehra tha. Jaati beech mein nahi aati thi. Hindu Musalmaan ko ek aur jaati maanta tha. Bas.

Aur Musalmaan bhi to kaun the? Zyadatar wahi log, jinhone dharm badla tha. Unhe apni purani virasat yaad thi. Unhe yahan ki kathayein, purane granth, bade epik — sab pata thi.

Dono ek hi tarah ka kaam karte the. Ek hi tarah ki zindagi, ek hi kapde, ek hi boli. Ek-doosre ke tyohaar mein jaate the. Kuch tyohaar to dono ke hi the. Lok-geet bhi saajhe the.

Zyadatar kisaan the, aur karigar.

Beech mein ek teesra giroh tha — vyapaari. Ye giroh zyadatar Hindu tha, aur arthvyavastha iske haath mein thi, chahe raajnaitik taaqat uske paas na thi. Isi giroh ka Musalmaanon se sabse kam mel tha. Bahar se aaye Musalmaan feudal soch ke the, vyapaar unhe pasand nahi tha, aur sood lene ki manaahi bhi beech mein thi.

Wo khud ko raaj karne wala giroh maante the — raja ke afsar, zameen ke pate lene wale, ya fauj ke afsar.

Sahitya mein ye mel dikhta hai.

Mughal daur mein bahut se Hindu log Farsi mein kitaabein likhte the — Farsi to darbar ki zubaan thi. Inmein kuch aisi kitaabein bani, jo aaj bhi classic maani jaati hain. Udhar Musalmaan vidwan Sanskrit ki kitaabon ka Farsi mein anuvaad karte the, aur Hindi mein likhte the.

Do naam yaad rakhiye.

Malik Mohammad Jaisi — Hindi ke ek bade kavi, jo Akbar se kuch pehle hue.

Aur Abdul Rahim Khankhana. Akbar ke darbar ke sabse bade amiron mein se ek. Unke pita Akbar ke sanrakshak the. Rahim Arabi, Farsi aur Sanskrit ke vidwan the, aur Hindi ke ek bahut acche kavi. Kuch samay ke liye wo Mughal fauj ke sarvocch senapati bhi the.

Ab sochiye.

Jis fauj ke wo senapati hain, wo Rana Pratap se lad rahi hai. Aur apne tambu mein wo usi Rana Pratap ki tareef likhte hain. Uske desh-prem, uski izzat, uski bahaduri.

Nehru kehte hain, Akbar ki neeti isi chivalry aur dosti par khadi thi, aur unke bahut se mantri ye unse seekhte the.

Unhe Rajput bahut pyaare the, kyunki unme wo baatein thi jo Akbar ke andar bhi thi. Bejhijhak bahaduri. Izzat. Aur zubaan ki pakki baat.

Unhone Rajputon ko jeet liya.

Par Rajput, apni saari acchi baaton ke saath, ek madhyakaal ke samaj ke pratinidhi the — ek aisa samaj, jo naye taaqaton ke aane par purana pad raha tha.

Aur Akbar? Nehru yahan ek gehri baat likhte hain. Akbar in nayi taaqaton se waaqif hi nahi the. Kyunki wo khud apni samajik virasat ke qaidi the.

## Part 11: Ghadi ulti ghoomti hai

Satrahvin sadi. Do shehar. Do mahal.

France mein Louis chaudahve. Wahan Versailles uthta hai. Aur madhya Europe ko ek lambi jung ujaad rahi hai.

Bharat mein Shah Jahan. Agra. Taj Mahal. Moti Masjid. Delhi ki Jama Masjid. Diwan-e-Aam aur Diwan-e-Khas.

Mayur Singhasan ka darbar itna shaandaar tha ki Versailles bhi peeche lagta tha. Par Nehru ek line jodte hain: ye dono gareeb, lut-ti hui praja par khade the. Gujarat aur Dekkhan mein bhayanak akaal pada tha.

Udhar England mein naye madhyam varg uth rahe the, jo vyapaar se bade hue the. Unke haath se raja ka sir kat gaya. Ek chhota sa ganraajya aaya aur chala gaya. Aur Parliament raja se oopar baith gaya.

Isi daur mein Aurangzeb gaddi par baithe — ek gharelu jung ke baad. Apne pita Shah Jahan ko kaid kar ke.

Nehru kehte hain, is waqt ko samajhne wala sirf ek Akbar hi ho sakta tha. Aur shayad wo bhi sirf der kar sakta tha, agar uski jigyasa use nayi technique aur badalte aarthik haalaat ka matlab samjha pati.

Aurangzeb ne aaj ko bhi nahi samjha, aur kal ko bhi nahi. Unhone apne se pehle walon ka kaam mitane ki koshish ki.

Wo kattar aur sakht puritan the, aur kala-sahitya unhe pasand nahi tha. Unhone Hinduon par phir se wo purana jaziya lagaya, aur unke kai mandir tudawe. Unhone un ghamandi Rajputon ko naraaz kiya, jo Mughal saltanat ke khambhe the.

Punjab mein unhone Sikhon ko chheda — ek shaant panth, jisme Hindu aur Islam ki baatein mili hui thi. Unke zulm ne use ek ladaku bhai-chaare mein badal diya.

Aur paschim mein? Unhone Marathas ko chheda — Rashtrakuton ke vanshaj, yaani pashchim Bharat ka ek purana rajavansh. Aur theek tab, jab unme ek kamaal ka neta uth raha tha.

Arthvyavastha bhi chatak rahi thi.

Sadi ke aakhir mein, Delhi ke paas hi, Jat kisaan baar-baar uthne lage. Aur ek doosra bagawat — Satnami, yaani aam kaam karne wale logon ka panth. Ek Mughal amir ne unhe hiqaarat se sunaar, badhai, safai wale aur mochi jaisa giroh kaha.

Aur yahi nayi baat thi. Ab tak bagawat raja aur nauwab karte the. Ab aam log kar rahe the.

Aur in sab ke beech, Sahyadri ki dhundh mein, ek naam ubharta hai.

Shivaji. Saal 1627 mein janme. Pahadi, sakht aadmiyon ke sardar, jo apni chhoti fauj se bade ilaaqe hila dete the. Unke ghudsawaar door-door tak pahunche. Unhone Surat ko luta, jahan Angrezon ki kothi thi, aur Mughal ilaaqon ke door ke hisson se chauth vasool kiya.

Nehru unhe ubharti Hindu rashtriyata ka prateek maante hain. Unhone purane granthon se prerna li. Bahadur the, aur neta ke gun unme the. Unhone Marathas ko ek mazboot, ek jut fauj banaya, use ek rashtriya background diya, aur aisi taaqat banaya jo Mughal samrajya ko tod de.

Unki maut 1680 mein hui. Par Maratha taaqat badhti rahi — itni, ki aage chalkar wo poore Bharat par chha gayi.

To ab wapas us dopahar mein chaliye. Fatehpur Sikri. Laal patthar. Dhoop ki lakeerein.

Ek chhapi hui kitaab, ek taraf rakhi hui. Aur ek sawal, jo hawa mein latak gaya.

Itna jigyasu samraat bhi Europe ki nayi taaqat ko kyun nahi dekh paaya?

Jawab ye hai. Kyunki uski jigyasa soch ki taraf thi, cheezon ki taraf nahi. Wo ye poochte the ki kaun sa dharm sach hai. Wo ye nahi poochte the ki ye cheez banti kaise hai.

Aur Akbar, jitna bada unka darbar tha, wo khud apne samaj ki virasat ke qaidi the. Isliye wo sawal utha hi nahi.

Aur ek peedhi baad, Aurangzeb ne us ghadi ko ulta ghumaya. Aur ghumaate hue tod diya.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, chhapi hui kitaab. Akbar ke haath mein aisi cheez aayi jo poori duniya badalne wali thi, aur sawal utha hi nahi. Aaj har nayi technology ke saath yahi pal laut kar aata hai. Hum use kharidte hain, chalaate hain, tareef karte hain. Kitne log use khol kar dekhte hain?

Doosri, jigyasa ka rukh. Nehru kehte hain ki koi sabhyata andar ki thakaan se girti hai, bahar ke hamle se kam. Ghar ki baat kariye, darbar ki baat kariye, ya desh ki baat kariye — sawal ek hi hai. Hum apne andar wo deewar dhoondh rahe hain, jise koi hamla nahi tod sakta?

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapke ghar mein koi ek cheez aisi hai, jise aap roz chalaate hain, par aaj tak khol kar nahi dekha? Aur agar dekha, to kya hua?

## Outro

Aurangzeb ke baad Mughal saltanat sirf ek parchhaai reh gayi. Us khaali jagah mein do nayi taaqatein khadi hui.

Ek samundar ke kinaare. Ek vyapaari company, jiske paas taaqat thi, par zimmedaari nahi.

Aur doosri, Rajasthan mein. Ek raja, jisne patthar ke vishaal yantra banwaye, aasman ke taare naape, aur ek din Portugal se aayi tables mein galti pakad li.

Naam yaad rakhiye. Agli baar hum Jaipur ki ek raat mein honge.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
