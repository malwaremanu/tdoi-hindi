## Ab tak

Ab tak: pichhli baar humne upar ki duniya dekhi thi. Takht badle, zubaan badli, libaas badla, naye geet bane. Par neeche, aam zindagi par kuch nahi hila. Uska kaaran ek dhancha tha — teen khambhon par khada: gaon ki panchayat, jaati, aur jukta parivaar. Aur usi zindagi mein ek reet badhi thi: parda. Aaj hum pehle us reet ko dekhenge, phir gaon ke darwaze par khade honge.

## Part 1: Ek rasam jo ghar ke andar aayi

Ek chhoti khidki. Us par jaali lagi hai. Jaali ke peeche ek chehra hai — ek aurat ka. Wo bahar dekh sakti hai. Bahar se koi use nahi dekh sakta.

Is reet ka naam parda hai.

Nehru yahan khud maante hain ki unhone ek chhalang lagayi hai. Kitaab beech mein aaj ke zamane par aa jaati hai. Par ye chhalang wo zaroori samajhte hain.

Unka faisla saaf hai. Is desh ki pichhle kuch sau saalon ki giraavat ke kaarnon mein parda ek bada kaaran hai. Aur jab tak ye reet poori tarah khatam nahi hoti, is desh ki samajik zindagi aage nahi badh sakti.

Aur nuksaan sirf aurat par nahi hai. Nehru likhte hain: nuksaan mard par bhi hai. Us bachche par bhi, jo apna zyadatar waqt parde mein ruki auraton ke beech bitata hai. Aur poore samaaj par bhi.

Ab ek sawal. Ye reet aayi kahan se?

Zyadatar log samajhte hain ki ye bahar se aane walon ke saath aayi. Kitaab ka jawab ulta hai.

Jinhone Dilli li — Afghan — unmein sakht parda tha hi nahi. Turk aur Afghan shahzadiyan ghode par sawaar hoti thi. Shikaar khelne jaati thi. Mehmaani par jaati thi.

Aur ek reet aaj bhi zinda hai: Haj ki yatra mein aurat chehra khula rakhti hai.

To parda aaya kahan se? Nehru ek andaaza dete hain — Byzantium ke darbar se. Wahan khwaja-sara auraton ke kamre ki chowkidari karte the. Wo tareeqa Russia tak gaya.

Bharat mein parda Mughalon ke zamane mein badha. Aur yahan usne ek naya rang liya: wo izzat aur rutbe ka nishaan ban gaya. Hindu ho ya Musalman — dono ke oopar ki classes mein.

Kahan phaila? Dilli, United Provinces, Rajputana, Bihar, Bengal — yani jahan naye asar sabse gehre the.

Kahan nahi phaila? Punjab. Frontier. Dono jagah log zyadatar Musalman the — aur wahan parda sakht nahi tha. Dakshin aur paschim mein to parda tha hi nahi.

Ek acchi khabar bhi hai. Hindu samaj mein ye reet tezi se mit rahi thi. Musalman samaj mein thodi dheere.

Aur ise todne mein sabse bada haath kiska tha? Congress ke andolanon ka. Unhone hazaaron middle-class auraton ko ghar se nikaal kar public kaam mein khada kiya.

Gandhi ji is reet ke sabse sakht khilafi the. Unhone ise ek ghinauna aur darindagi wala riwaj kaha.

Unki maang seedhi thi. Aurat ko wahi azaadi mile, aur khud ko banane ka wahi mauka, jo mard ko mila hai. Dono ke beech koi deewar nahi honi chahiye. Dono ka baartav swabhavik aur khud-ba-khud hona chahiye.

Aur Nehru jodte hain: Gandhi ji ne aurat ki barabari aur azaadi ke liye bade josh se likha, aur unki gharelu gulami ki sakht ninda ki.

Ab wapas. Yeh wahi jagah hai jahan Nehru khud likhte hain: main chhalang laga kar aaya, ab wapas aate hain.

Kyunki parda sirf ek reet nahi thi. Wo us bade dhanche ka ek hissa tha, jispar neeche ki zindagi khadi thi.

Aur us dhanche ke teen khambhe the. Pehla: gaon.

## Part 2: Gaon ki apni sarkar

Gaon ke andar ek chhoti si sarkar chalti thi. Uska naam panchayat. Aur us sarkar ki apni ek kitaab thi.

Nitisara — rajniti ka shastra — jise Shukracharya ke naam se joda jaata hai. Ek dasvin sadi ki kitaab, jo un dinon ka naqsha deti hai jab Turk ya Afghan hamla shuru bhi nahi hua tha: markazi sarkar ka dhancha, raja ki council, alag-alag mahkame, aur shehar aur gaon ki zindagi.

Gaon ki panchayat chuni hui council thi. Uske paas ammal ki taaqat thi, aur nyay ki bhi. Raja ke adhikari uske sadasya ki badi izzat karte the.

Zameen panchayat baantti thi. Kis ko kitni — ye wahin tay hota tha.

Aur lagaan? Panchayat fasal se hissa leti thi, aur us hisse mein se sarkar ka hissa sarkar tak khud pahuncha deti thi. Yani gaon apna tax khud wasool karta tha, aur apne hi aadmi ke haath sarkar ko deta tha.

Ek gaon ke oopar ek badi panchayat bhi thi — zaroorat pade to dekh sakti thi, dakhal de sakti thi.

Aur sadasya kaise chunte the? Ye sekdon saal purane pathar par khude hue lekh batate hain.

Har saal committee chuni jaati thi. Aur ek baat dhyan se suniye: auratein bhi usme baith sakti thi.

Agar koi sadasya galat nikle, to hata diya jaata tha. Aur ek shart aisi thi jo aaj sun kar ajeeb lagti hai: agar wo sarkari paise ka hisaab dene mein chook gaya, to wo bahar.

Ek aur shart ka pura maksad ek hi tha — bhai-bhatijavaad rokna. Kisi sadasya ke kareebi rishtedaar ko naukri nahi mil sakti thi.

[SFX: pathar par khudi hui likhaai ki si halki tik-tik; phir khatiya ki rassi ki chik-chik]

Aur panchayat apni azaadi par nakhra karti thi. Nitisara mein saaf likha hai: koi sainik raja ki likhit anumati ke bina gaon mein nahi ghus sakta.

Ab us raat ko yaad kijiye. Sadak par kaun khada tha? Ek sawaar. Uske paas ek mantri ki mohar thi. Raja ki anumati nahi thi. Aur gaon ka darwaza nahi khula.

Ye gaon ki zid nahi thi. Ye kanoon tha.

Aur isi baithak se ek line aati hai, jo yahan se aage poori kahani mein lauti hai. Nitisara kehta hai: jan-mat raja se bhi zyada taaqatwar hota hai.

Kitna taaqatwar? Ek tasveer suniye. Kai reshmon se bani ek rassi — ek sher ko bhi kheech le jaati hai.

Ek resha kamzor hota hai. Kai reshe — rassi.

Aur naukriyan kis aadhar par deni thi? Suniye: kaam, kirdaar aur kaabiliyat dekhi jaaye. Jaati nahi. Khandaan nahi.

Aur uske baad ek aur line, jo jaati ke zamane ke hisaab se bahut badi baat thi: na rang se, na purvajon se, wo aatma paida hoti hai jo ek Brahmin ka gaurav hai.

Yani wahi kitaabein, jo is dhanche ko sahi thehrati thi, khud keh rahi thi: asli cheez kaam hai.

Gaon ke andar ki baat ho gayi. Ab bahar dekhiye — us aadmi ki taraf jiske naam par ye sab chalta tha. Raja ka is zameen par haq aakhir kya tha?

## Part 3: Raja sirf lagaan leta tha

Bade shehron mein gaon se bhi zyada tarah ki sansthayein thi. Kaarigar, vyapaari, aur unke sangh — kaarigari ke sangh, vyapaar ke sangh, paisa lagane wale sangh. Har ek apne andar ka kaam khud dekhta tha.

Nehru maante hain ki ye jaankari tukdon mein mili hai. Par jo dikhta hai, wo ye: shehron aur gaon mein sangathanit swaraaj ka poora tanta tha. Markazi sarkar andar nahi ghusi — jab tak uska tax ka hissa aata raha.

Aur uski ek badi wajah thi: reet ka kanoon. Wo majboot tha. Siyasi ya fauji taaqat reet se bane haqon par kam hi haath daalti thi.

Yahan dharm-raj nahi tha. Yani raja dharm ka thekedaar nahi tha. Aur yahan ki rajneeti mein ek baat likhi thi, jo Europe mein kahin nahi milti: agar raja anyay kare, to uske khilaf bagavat ka haq banta hai.

Bharat ke bahar ye soch alag thi. Do hazaar saal pehle ek Chini darshanik, Mencius, likhte hain: jo raja apni praja ko ghaas-kaach samjhe, praja use daku aur dushman samjhe.

Ab Europe ko dekhiye. Wahan feudalism mein raja apne ilake ki zameen aur logon, dono ka maalik tha. Wo taaqat wo aage bade zamindaron ko saunpta tha, aur wo uske naam par qasam khaate the. Isse ek seedhi bani — aur wo seedhi Rome ke ek purane khayal par khadi thi.

Bharat mein aisa nahi tha. Raja ko ek hi haq tha: zameen se kuch lagaan lena. Aur lagaan wasool karne ka haq hi wo doosron ko de sakta tha.

Iska matlab ye tha: yahan ka kisan kisi ki jaageer nahi tha. Kisan apne maalik ka gulaam nahi tha.

Aur kyun nahi tha? Ek saadhaaran wajah: zameen bahut thi. Kisi kisan ko ujhaad kar uski zameen lene mein fayda hi nahi tha.

Isi wajah se yahan wo zamindari nahi thi jo Paschim mein thi. Aur na kisan apni zameen ka poora maalik tha.

Dono baatein — zamindari aur kisani milkiyat — Angrez yahan baad mein laaye. Aur dono ke nateeje bhayanak rahe.

Ab wapas us purane gaon par. Bahar se hamle aaye. Ladaiyan hui. Nayi ruling classes khadi hui, jo zyada tar hathyar ke bal par chalti thi. Gaon ki taaqat ghati. Aur lagaan ke tareeke badle.

Par ek baat dhyan dene layak hai. Afghan aur Mughal raja apni reet na tootne ke liye khaas dhyan rakhte the. Ghyas-ud-Din Tughlak ne to apne adhikariyon ko likh kar hidayat di — reet ka kanoon bachaye rakhna, aur raaj ke kaam ko dharm se alag rakhna, kyunki dharm har aadmi ki apni pasand hai.

Yani saltanat badli. Soch badli. Par gaon ki sarkar apni jagah rahi.

Wo gaon, jo apna darwaza khud band kar sakta tha, sadiyon tak azaad raha. Uska ant kab aaya? Jab Angrez aaye. Gaon ki khud-mukhtar sarkar Angrezon ke raaj mein tooti.

Ek khambha sadiyon khada raha. Ab doosre khambhe ki taraf. Par usse pehle ek sawal: itne alag-alag log ek hi samaaj mein saath kaise reh rahe the?

## Part 4: Kaam ka batwara, aur ek bada dhancha

Ek aadmi jaati chhod deta hai. Har group chhod deta hai. Kaam-kaaj ki duniya chhod deta hai — wo sanyasi ban jaata hai. Uske liye koi darwaza band nahi hota, kyunki wo saare darwaze chhod kar nikal chuka hai.

Ye us dhanche ki ek surat thi. Par dhancha khud kaisa tha?

Pehle samajhiye, sawal kya tha. Is desh mein log ek doosre se bahut alag the. Ek taraf wo log the, jinke dharm mein totem, jhaad-paudhe, har kism ka andhvishwas tha. Doosri taraf wo, jo soch ki sabse oonchi unchai tak pahunche the. Aur beech mein har darje ka roop tha.

Inko ek hi samaaj mein rakhna tha. Iska ek aasan raasta duniya mein hamesha raha hai: kamzor ko mita do, ya gulaam bana lo.

Yahan wo raasta nahi liya gaya.

Iske badle ek doosri tarah ka raasta chuna gaya: kai samooh, ek saath — jaise aaj ke desh apni minorities ka sawal suljhaate hain. America har aadmi ko sau feesadi American banata hai. Canada ka apna French samooh hai. Soviet Russia ne alag-alag samuhon ko ek multinational state banaya.

Nehru ye tulna khud karte hain, aur maante hain ki wo ise bahut door tak nahi khinch sakte.

Us dhanche mein har group ko apni zindagi jeene ki azaadi thi — bas ek shart par. Wo kisi doosre group ke kaam mein dakhal na de. Ye lachila dhancha tha. Naye group ban sakte the: naye logon se, ya kisi purane group ke bikhre hue logon se. Bas itna zaroori tha ki wo group apna kaam khud chala sake.

Aur har group ke andar barabari thi. Faisle milkar hote the. Group ke chune hue log poori sabha se poochh kar chalte the.

Ek cheez ye bhi thi: har group apne aadmi ko bachata tha. Jo kamai se haath dho baithe, uski madad hoti thi. Ek tarah ka bima.

Iska maksad ye tha: agar har group apna kaam theek kare, to poora samaaj theek chalega.

Aur ye groups aksar kaam ke hisaab se bane the — koi lohar ka, koi badhai ka. Isliye wo trade unions aur craft guilds jaise ho gaye.

In sab groups ko baandhne ke liye ek saanjha dhaaga banaya gaya — saanjhi sanskriti, saanjhi parampara, saanjhe heroes aur sant, aur teerth ki ek hi zameen, jahan log chaaron dishaon se chal kar jaate the.

Ye bond kaisa tha? Rajniti mein kamzor. Isi wajah se bahar ke hukmaran ke liye yahan jeetna aasan tha. Par samajik roop se bahut majboot. Isi wajah se hukmaran ko hazam karna, aur dobara khada ho jaana, aasan tha.

Kyunki is dhanche ke sir bahut the. Sab ke sab kaatna aasan nahi tha.

To jaati ek group system thi — kaam aur seva par khadi. Koi saanjha aqeeda zaroori nahi tha. Isi liye ek shaadi, kai shaadiyan, aur bina shaadi ka jeewan — teenon chalte the. Aur koi chhota samooh bade samooh ke saamne jhukta nahi tha. Wo apna alag, khud-mukhtar group bana sakta tha.

Aur vyakti? Vyakti sirf apne group ka sadasya gina jaata tha. Wo jo chahe kar sakta tha — bas group ka kaam na bigde.

Par ek raasta khula tha. Agar wo kaafi mazboot ho, aur apne saath kaafi log jod le, to naya group bana sakta tha.

Aur agar wo kisi bhi group mein fit na ho paaye? To phir uske saamne ek darwaza tha — sanyasi ka. Wo saare darwaze chhod kar bahar nikal jaata tha.

Yani is dhanche mein har ek ke liye ek jagah thi. Aur ek raasta bhi — bahar nikalne ka.

Par us dhanche mein kaun kaun tha?

## Part 5: Chaar jaatiyan, chhatha hissa

Pehle ek ulti baat suniye.

Is dhanche mein samaaj vyakti ko samooh ke neeche dabata tha. Par dharm ki soch hamesha vyakti ko oopar rakhti thi.

Mukti aur param satya ka gyaan sabke liye tha — har jaati ka, oonchi ho ya neechi. Ye mukti group ki cheez ho hi nahi sakti thi; wo pooree tarah apni thi. Aur uski khoj mein koi sakht aqeeda zaroori nahi tha.

Isi liye Bharat mein vyaktivaad ki ek dhaara hamesha rahi. Aur dono dhaaraon ke beech takraav bhi dikhta rehta hai.

Jo log jaati ki deewar ko chunauti dete the, wo aksar dharm ke sudhaarak hote the. Unka tark yahi tha: jaati ke bantware aatma ki unnati ke raste mein aa jaate hain.

Buddhism isi group-jaati ke aadarsh se ek toot tha — vyaktivaad ki taraf, aur sabko ek maanne ki taraf. Par us toot ke saath ek kamzori jud gayi: wo aam samajik kaam se kat kar peechhe hat jaane laga, aur usne jaati ke saamne koi doosra dhancha khada nahi kiya. Isliye jaati chalti rahi — us zamane mein bhi, aur uske baad bhi.

To is dhanche mein kaun kaun tha?

Jo log jaati se bilkul bahar maane jaate the, unhe ek taraf rakh lijiye. Baaki chaar the.

Brahmin: pujaari, shikshak, sochne-samajhne wale log.

Kshatriya: raja aur yodha.

Vaishya: vyapaari, karobar aur paisa lagane wale.

Aur Shudra: kheti aur doosre kaam karne wale.

Nehru kehte hain ki shayad ek hi jaati poori tarah band thi — Brahmin. Kshatriya apni ginti badhate rehte the: kai bahar se, kai yahan ke hi, jo taaqat mein badhe. Vaishya zyadatar vyapaar aur paisa lagane ka kaam karte the. Shudra zyadatar kheti aur ghar ke kaam mein.

Aur ek process chalta rehta tha. Naye peshe bante, to nayi jaatiyan bhi banti. Aur purani jaatiyan seedhi par oopar chadhne ki koshish karti rahti thi. Kabhi koi neechi jaati achanak wahi janeu pehen leti, jo sirf oopar ki jaatiyon ke liye tha. Izzat ka sawal tha, koi naya haq nahi mil jaata tha.

Par isse zyada farq nahi padta tha. Har jaati apne ghere mein apna kaam karti rehti. Poora jhagda izzat ka tha.

Kabhi-kabhi neechi class ka koi aadmi apni kaabiliyat se sarkar mein badi jagah par pahunch jaata tha. Par ye bahut hi kam hota tha.

Aur ek baat yaad rakhiye: us samaaj mein muqabla aur jyada kamai kaam nahi thi. Isi liye in lakeeron ka asar utna nahi tha, jitna aaj dekhne mein lagta hai.

Oopar Brahmin tha — gyaan par garv, logon ki izzat. Par daulat uske paas kam hi hoti thi. Vaishya ameer the. Par poore samaaj mein unki haisiyat bahut oonchi nahi thi.

Ab asli tasveer dekhiye.

Is poore dhanche mein zyadatar log kisan the.

Yahan zamindari nahi thi, aur kisan ki apni milkiyat bhi nahi. Kanoon ke hisaab se zameen kiski thi, ye kehna mushkil tha. Aaj ki tarah "maalik" ka khayal hi nahi tha.

Kisan ko ek haq tha: wo us zameen par kheti kar sakta tha. Asli sawal fasal ka batwara tha.

[SFX: fasal ke daanon ki halki khanak — anaj tola ja raha hai]

Fasal ka bada hissa kisan ke paas jaata tha. Raja ya sarkar ka hissa — aam taur par chhatha. Aur ek hissa gaon ke har us group ka, jo logon ki koi seva karta tha.

Ab us fehrist ko dekhiye. Pujaari aur shikshak. Vyapaari. Lohar. Badhai. Chamar. Kumhar. Raaj. Nai. Bhangi.

Sarkar se lekar safai karne wale tak — gaon ki fasal mein har ek ka hissa tha. Poora gaon ek hisse-daar company ki tarah tha.

Ab ek sawal bacha. Jo log is fehrist se bahar rakhe gaye, unka kya?

## Part 6: Chhua-chhoot, aur safai ka ulta sikka

Do aadmi ek hi gali mein rehte hain. Do ghar, do darwaze, ek deewar. Par wo saath nahi khaate.

Ye reet ek gali se lekar poore desh tak thi. Aur iske saath hi safai ke naam par jo cheez is samaaj mein thi, wo apne aap mein ek ulta sikka thi.

Ab un logon ki taraf aaiye, jinke paas fasal mein hissa nahi tha.

Unhe hum aaj "depressed classes" kehte hain. Naam naya hai. Ye un jaatiyon ke liye bola jaata hai jo seedhi ke sabse neeche paayedaan par hain. Unhe alag karne ki koi saaf lakeer nahi hai.

Jo saaf hai, wo untouchables hain. Uttar Bharat mein bahut thode log untouchable maane jaate the — sirf wo, jo gandagi ka kaam karte the.

Fa-Hsien — ek Chini tirthyatri, jo Nehru ke zamane se lagbhag pandrah sau saal pehle Bharat aaya tha — likhte hain ki us waqt wo log untouchable the, jo insaan ka mal hataate the. Dakshin mein unki ginti bahut badi thi.

Ye shuruaat kaise hui? Tay karna mushkil hai. Andaaza ye hai ki jo peshe gande maane jaate the, unhe karne wale untouchable kar diye gaye. Baad mein be-zameen khet mazdoor bhi isme jod diye gaye.

Ab wo ulta sikka dekhiye. Is samaaj mein safai ka khayal bahut gehra tha.

Ek achcha nateeja: roz nahana. Ye reet Bharat se England tak pahunchi. Aur sabse gareeb kisan bhi apne bartan chamakte rakhta tha. Us par usko garv tha.

Par is safai ki do hadd thi.

Pehli: ye vaigyanik nahi thi. Jo aadmi din mein do baar nahate the, wo wahi paani pi sakta tha, jismein aaj ka science germs batata hai. Nazar se saaf paani, aur saaf paani, ek cheez nahi hoti.

Doosri: ye poore gaon ki nahi thi. Apna ghar saaf, aur kachra padosi ke darwaze ke saamne. Gaon ki galiyan koode ke dher se bhari.

Aur sabse gehri baat: safai khud ek cheez nahi maani jaati thi. Wo kisi dharmik nishaan ka nateeja thi. Jahan wo nishaan gaya, wahan safai bhi giri.

Ab is sab ka bura nateeja dekhiye. Apni jaati ke logon ke saath khaana — doosri jaati ke saath nahi. Ye reet duniya mein kahin aur itni khatarnak shakal mein nahi mili. Kuch jaatiyan untouchable ho gayi, kyunki unka kaam zaroori tha, par ganda tha.

Ye khaana-saath-khaane ki reet izzat ka nishaan ban gayi. Aur ek ajeeb baat: neechi jaatiyan ise oopar ki kuch jaatiyon se bhi zyada sakhti se nibhati thi. Aaj ye oopar ki jaatiyon mein toot rahi hai, par neeche abhi bhi chalti hai.

Isse bhi badi rok shaadi par thi. Kabhi-kabhi mili-juli shaadiyan hoti bhi thi. Par Nehru likhte hain, bahut ajeeb hai ki har jaati itni sadiyon tak apne aap mein bandh rahi.

Nasal banaye rakhna ek bhram hai. Phir bhi itni sadiyon mein jaati ne kuch khaas shakalein bacha li — khaas taur par oonchi jaatiyon mein.

Aur ek aakhri baat. Koi group "jaati se bahar" nahi hai. Untouchables bhi nahi. Wo bhi apni jaati banate the, aur apne faisle apni panchayat se karte the. Iske bawajood, unmein se bahut logon ko gaon ki aam zindagi se bahar rakh kar takleef di gayi.

Wo dhanche ke bahar nahi the. Par gaon ki zindagi se bahar kar diye gaye the.

Ab teesra khambha.

## Part 7: Jukta parivaar

[SFX: chulhe ki halki aanch; bartan ki halki khanak]

Ek ghar sochiye. Us ghar mein teen peedhiyan rehti hain. Dada-dadi, unke bete-bahu, aur unke pota-poti. Saanjhi rasoi, saanjha aangan, saanjhi deewar.

Aur sampatti? Wo bhi saanjhi.

Is parivaar mein har sadasya us saanjhi sampatti ka hisse-daar tha. Viraasat maut se chalti thi, bantwar se nahi. Yani jaise ek sadasya jaata tha, uska hissa apne aap baaki sab mein chal jaata tha.

Ghar ka mukhiya? Dada, ya koi bada. Par wo maalik nahi tha. Wo manager tha. Rome ke us purane ghar ke mukhiya jaisa nahi, jiske paas apne bete-beti par bhi jaan-maar ka haq tha.

Bantwar hoti bhi thi — kuch shartein poori ho jaayein, aur sab khush ho, to. Par aam baat yahi thi: ghar ek rehta tha.

Aur saanjhi sampatti ka kaam kya tha? Sabki zaroorat poori karna. Kaam karne wale ki bhi. Aur us ki bhi, jo kaam nahi kar sakta — boodha, beemar, kamzor.

Iska matlab: sabke liye ek guaranteed minimum tha. Koi bhookha nahi soya.

Aur nateeja? Achcha kaam karne wale ko bada inaam nahi milta tha. Aur kam kaam karne wale ko bhookh nahi milti thi. Kaam aur inaam, dono ki ek hi seedhi thi.

Is dhanche mein zor tha — sabse bada, sabki bhalaai. Aadmi apni taraqqi ka soch bhi nahi sakta tha, jab tak ghar ka kaam na bigde.

Nehru ek baat alag se likhte hain. Bade parivaar mein bachcha bada hota hai, to uska main-main kam hota hai. Wo seekh jaata hai ki saath kaise rehna hai. Ye badi baat hai.

Aur ye sab America jaisi zindagi ka bilkul ulta tha. Wahan har aadmi apna fayda dekhta hai. Jo tez hai, wo aage. Jo kamzor hai, wo deewar se takra jaata hai.

To teenon khambhe khade the — gaon, jaati, parivaar. Aur teenon ek hi baat par khade the: samooh.

Ab hisaab ki baari.

## Part 8: Suraksha mili, taraqqi nahi

Teenon khambhon ka maksad ek hi tha: samaajik suraksha. Sthirta. Aur samooh ka chalta rehna.

Taraqqi maksad nahi thi. Aur isliye taraqqi ko nuksaan uthana pada.

Kyunki suraksha aur badlaav ek saath nahi chalte. Jo dhancha aapko har girawat se bachata hai, wahi aapko har nayi cheez se bhi bachata hai.

Par ab ek ajeeb baat suniye. Is sakht dhanche ke andar ek cheez zinda thi — aur wo thi faisle khud karne ki aadat.

Har group ke andar saanjhi zindagi thi. Barabari ka ehsaas tha. Aur faisle milkar hote the.

Nehru apna ek tajurba likhte hain.

Unhe hairaani hui thi ki ek gaon ka aadmi — kabhi padha-likha bhi nahi — chuni hui committee mein baithne ke liye kitna utaavla rehta tha. Wo jaldi seekh jaata tha. Aur jab uski zindagi ka koi sawal aata, to wo jhukta nahi tha.

Aaj bhi jaati ki panchayatein usi tarah faisle karti hain.

Aur ye dhang sirf jaati mein nahi tha. Gaon ke kaam mein tha. Vyapaari ke sanghon mein tha. Dharam ki sabhaon mein tha. Sab jagah bahut barik niyam the — chunav ke, behas ke.

Do hazaar saal se zyada purani Bauddh sabhaon mein to wo niyam the, jin ke nishaan aaj ki sansad mein bhi dikhte hain: ek adhyaksh ka beej, ek officer jo ginti poori karta hai, prastav ka teen baar aana, aur gupt matdan se faisla.

Ek badi baat. Ye sab ek aise dhanche ke andar tha, jiske peechhe ek saaf darshan tha. Insaan ko jodna. Kamane-ganthne se zyada achchhai, sundarta aur sach par zor dena.

Ek koshish ye bhi thi: izzat, taaqat aur daulat ek jagah jama na ho jaayein.

Aur ek cheez hamesha dohrai gayi: farz. Adhikaar nahi. Purane dharm-granth dharm, kaam aur farz ki fehrist dete hain. Unmein se kisi ek mein adhikaaron ki fehrist nahi hai.

Yahi is dhanche ki taaqat thi. Aur yahi uski bedi.

Kyunki ye ek bandh system tha. Andar thodi jagah thi — badlaav ki, apnapan ki, thodi azaadi ki. Par wo dhire-dhire sakht hota gaya.

Wo nayi pratibha ko apne mein lene ki taaqat kho baithta. Purane fayde jinse kai log jude the, unhone koi bada badlaav hone hi nahi diya. Shiksha doosri classes tak pahunch hi nahi payi. Jo andhvishwas oopar ki class jaanti thi ki andhvishwas hain, unhe bachaya gaya — aur naye bhi jod diye gaye.

[SFX: halki pankha ki chik-chik, phir ek lamba sannata]

Nateeja? Sirf arth-vyavastha nahi — soch khud band, paramparik, sakht, aur bina taraqqi ki ho gayi.

Aur ab is dhanche ka aakhri hisaab bacha hai. Wo kaun chukata hai, jiske paas kuch nahi tha?

## Part 9: Daam kaun chukata hai

Nehru is dhanche ki taraf insaaf karte hain. Wo kehte hain, ismein ek baat asal thi — oopar walon ka farz. Jiske paas zyada hai, uski zimmedari bhi zyada.

Par ek shart par. Log apni jagah par rahein, aur us vyavastha ko chunauti na dein.

Isi wajah se is desh ka har bada kaam oopar ki classes se aaya. Neeche walon ke paas mauke bahut kam the.

Aur phir bhi ye dhancha itni sadiyan chala. Kaise? Kyunki oopar wale chhote ghere nahi the. Wo ginti mein bade the, aur taaqat unmein banti hui thi. Isi liye wo itna lamba chal saka.

Uska aakhri aib yahi tha. Usne insaanon ki ek badi tadaad ko neeche daba diya, aur unhe nikalne ka koi raasta nahi diya — na padhai se, na kaam se, na paise se.

Aur ye dabne wali cheez sirf neeche tak nahi rahi. Uska asar oopar tak chadha. Poore samaaj ka dimaag sookhne laga.

Nehru ise ek lafz dete hain: petrification. Patthar ban jaana. Wo is desh ki arth-vyavastha aur zindagi ki sabse badi pahchaan ban gayi.

Aur ab wo line, jo sabse zaroori hai. Nehru likhte hain: is dhanche ke andar na rutbe ki barabari ho sakti hai, na mauke ki barabari. Na rajnitik loktantra. Aur aarthik loktantra to bilkul nahi.

Do soch hain. Aur in dono mein se ek hi zinda reh sakti hai.

[SFX: rassi ki chik-chik wapas aati hai; uske neeche ek tanpura ka lamba sur]

Ab wapas us baithak mein chaliye, jahan Nitisara rakhi thi.

Wahi line laut kar aati hai: kai reshmon se bani ek rassi, ek sher ko bhi kheech le jaati hai.

Wo sach thi. Us baithak ki aawaz asal mein raja se bhi taaqatwar thi. Us gaon ne apna darwaza ek raja ke liye band kar diya.

Par wahi rassi, wahi reshay, wahi taaqat — usne gaon ke andar ek doosra darwaza bhi bana diya.

Us gaon ka darwaza bahar se aane wale sipahi ke liye band hota tha. Par usi deewar ne naye vichaar ko bhi roka. Usi deewar ne ek aadmi ko ye kehne se roka: main kuch naya bana sakta hoon, main kuch naya seekh sakta hoon.

Ab is episode ka jawab.

Sawal tha: gaon, jaati aur parivaar — teenon ne suraksha di. To taraqqi kyun nahi mili?

Jawab yahi hai. Kyunki teenon ka maksad hi suraksha aur chalta rehna tha — badlaav nahi. Jo dhancha badlaav se bachata hai, wo ek din khud badal hi nahi paata. Aur uska daam sabse neeche wale chukate hain.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli: us dhanche ke teen khambhe aaj bhoot ki tarah nahi dikhte. Wo naye naam lekar lautte hain. Gaon ki jagah ab band-galiyan, society, colony — jo apna gate khud band kar leti hain, aur darwaze par ek guard khada rehta hai. Jaati ki jagah ab network hai — "hum apne logon" ka. Jukte parivaar ki jagah ab bhi wahi hisaab hai: khaandaan, biradari, aur wo adalat jo ghar ke andar baithti hai.

Nehru ka sawal aaj bhi utna hi taaza hai. Ek aadmi apni naukri isliye nahi chhodta ki risk hai. Ek parivaar apne bete ko wo kaam nahi karne deta, jo "log kya kahenge" ke dayre se bahar hai. Suraksha mili. Taraqqi ruk gayi.

Doosri: wahi purani rassi. Kai reshay, ek awaaz. Nehru ke zamane mein wo reshay gaon ki sabha mein jude the. Aaj wo reshay screen par jude hain. Taaqat wahi hai — bahut badi taaqat. Sawaal sirf itna hai: ye rassi kis taraf kheech rahi hai? Aapko bachane ke liye, ya aapko wahin khada rakhena ke liye?

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aapki zindagi mein wo kaun si cheez hai jise aap suraksha kehte hain — par jo aapko rok rahi hai? Aap use rassi kehte hain, ya zanjeer?

## Outro

Neeche ki zindagi apni jagah khadi rahi. Teen khambhe sadiyon tak use thaamte rahe — suraksha dete hue, badlaav ka darwaza band karte hue. Neeche ki zindagi apni jagah khadi rahi.

Par upar ek aadmi aaya, jo sach mein badalna chahta tha — jise duniya ki har cheez ke bare mein jaanna tha. Uske darbar mein ek din kaale chogon wale do Portuguese ek moti, chhapi hui kitaab rakhenge.

Badshah use kholenge. Phir band kar denge.

Aur us kitaab ke bare mein ek sawal koi nahi poochhega: ye chhapi kaise?

Agli baar: Akbar ka darbar, ek band kitaab, aur ek ghadi — jise kisi ne nahi khola.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
