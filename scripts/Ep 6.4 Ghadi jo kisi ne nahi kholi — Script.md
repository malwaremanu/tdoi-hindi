## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Ek qaidi, jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar hum neeche ki zindagi par gaye the — gaon ki panchayat, jaati, aur jukta parivaar. Teen khambhe, jinka maksad suraksha tha, taraqqi nahi. Aaj hum upar jaate hain, darbar mein.

## Part 1: Panipat ka ajnabi

Panipat ka maidan. Wahi maidan, jahan Bharat ki kismat kai baar tay hui hai. Saal 1526.

Ek fauj ne Delhi ki gaddi jeet li. Uska sardar Babar tha — Central Asia se aaya ek Turco-Mongol, Timur ke vansh ka.

Garmi ki dopahar hai. Ek shamiyana. Babar baithe hain, aur likh rahe hain.

Delhi mil gayi hai. Agra mein nayi rajdhani ban rahi hai, aur uske liye unhone Constantinople se ek mashhoor vaastukar bulwaya hai — udhar Suleiman the Magnificent ka daur tha, aur wahan imaaratein uthti thi.

Par Babar khush nahi hain.

Yahan ki mitti unhe ajnabi lagti hai. Wo apna watan yaad karte hain. Ferghana — Central Asia ki wo ghati, jahan unhone bachpan dekha. Wahan ki barf. Wahan ke phool aur phal. Wahan ki mehfil, aur doston se baatein.

Yahan unhe wo sab nahi milta.

Par phir bhi wo kalam utaarte hain, aur ek baat likhte hain: Hindustan ek kamaal ka accha desh hai.

Sochiye. Ek aadmi, jise yahan kuchh bhi apna nahi lagta — aur wo phir bhi is desh ki tareef likh raha hai.

Aur usi account mein ek doosri baat bhi hai, jo is episode ke liye bahut zaroori hai.

Babar likhte hain ki yahan hunarmand kaamgar aur karigaron ki koi kami nahi hai. Log apne haath se kamaal ki cheezein banate hain. Par machine banane mein, nayi cheez ijaad karne mein, yahan koi hunar nazar nahi aata. Aur zindagi ki nafasat aur aaram ke saamaan mein ye desh Iran se kaafi peeche hai.

Babar ne is desh ko theek se dekha bhi nahi. Chaaron taraf shatru the, aur unka saara waqt ladai aur Agra basane mein gaya. Par unke likhe hue se ek baat saaf dikhti hai: yahan ki kala ki jaan sookh rahi thi. Ek karan Timur ki tabahi thi. Doosra, bahut se vidvaan, kalakaar aur mashhoor karigar dakshin ki taraf chale gaye the. Aur teesra karan sabse gehri baat hai — is desh ke logon ki banane wali jaan khud sookh rahi thi.

Nehru yahan ruk kar khud se poochte hain: ye kyun? Kya ye is mitti ki aadat thi? Ya ye baad ki giraavat hai?

Kyunki purane daur ki kitaabein aur tasveerein kuchh aur kehti hain. Us waqt yahan nafasat thi, aur rehan-sehan ka ooncha, uljha hua tareeqa tha. Aur jab Babar yahan aaye, to dakshin mein ek shehar tha — Vijayanagar — jiske baare mein Europe ke yaatri likh kar jaate the: itni kala, itni nafasat, itna aish, jaise duniya mein kahin aur na ho.

To ye kaisa fark tha? Uttar ki mitti thaki hui thi, aur dakshin chamak raha tha.

Aur isi thaki hui mitti par aage ek nayi cheez giri. Par wo kiski thi? Us sawal ka jawab Babar ke pote ke paas hai.

## Part 2: Do thaki hui duniyaon ka milan

Pehle us cheez ko dekhiye jo is poore daur ko samajhti hai. Bahar se aaye log dheere-dheere andar ke ho gaye. Shuru mein ye ek neeti thi — logon ki nafrat kam karni hai, unhe apne saath lena hai, khud ko hamlavar nahi, is mitti ka beta dikhana hai. Phir ye neeti ek aadat ban gayi. Aur uske neeche se ek aur hawa uthi: soch aur jeene ke dhang ko milane ki koshish.

Aur ek baat samajh lijiye, jo Nehru har hamle par dohraate hain.

Hamla bura hota hai. Lekin uske andar ek cheez chhupi hoti hai. Wo band aadmi ka khol tod deta hai. Aadmi ko dekhna padta hai ki bahar ki duniya us se badi hai, aur us se ajeeb bhi.

Afghan aaye, aur unka asar is desh par pada. Mughal aaye, aur unka asar aur gehra tha — kyunki Mughal Afghanon se zyada sanskaari the, aur jeene ke dhang mein zyada aage.

Wo apne saath Iran ki nafasat laaye. Darbar chalane ka ek poora, sakht tareeqa — kya pehnna hai, kaise baithna hai, kis se kaise baat karni hai. Amiron ki zindagi ka dhang hi badal gaya. Dakshin mein Bahmani raajya ka Iran se seedha rishta tha, Calicut ke raaste.

Ye sab ek nayi hawa thi. Kala nayi hui. Imaaratein nayi hui.

Par Nehru yahan ek kadwi baat jodte hain.

Ye dono sabhyatayein nayi nahi thi. Dono purani thi. Dono apni jawaani jee chuki thi, aur dono sakht saanche mein dhal chuki thi.

Bharatiya sanskriti bahut purani thi, aur thak chuki thi. Arab-Irani sanskriti apni oonchaai paar kar chuki thi. Aur ek cheez jo kabhi Arabon ki pehchaan thi — nayi cheez jaane ki bechaini — wo ab kahin nahi thi.

To ye do thake hue logon ka milan tha. Aur ek baat gaur karne wali hai: agar yahan ke log un aaram-o-nafasat ko chahte, to Iran se laa sakte the. Dono deshon ke beech aana-jaana laga rehta tha. Isliye ye kisi deewar ki majboori nahi thi. Ye ek pasand thi — ya pasand ka mar jaana.

Aur is desh ka apna samaaj bhi hila nahi tha. Pakke vishwas, aur ek sakht dhaancha — jisne koshish aur taraqqi, dono ko rok diya tha.

To ab Babar ke pote ki baat kijiye.

## Part 3: Pachaas saal, ek daanv

Akbar. Mughal vansh ka teesra badshah, par asal mein samrajya inhi ne khada kiya. Saal 1556 se shuru, aur lagbhag pachaas saal.

Nehru unhe is kitaab ke sabse dilkash logon mein ginte hain. Bahadur aur bejhijhak. Kamaal ke senapati. Par saath mein narm-dil, daya se bhare hue. Ek sapne dekhne wale, aur ek kaam karne wale — dono ek hi aadmi mein. Aisa neta, jiske peeche log jaan de dete the.

Unke darbar ke Portuguese paadriyon ne unki aankhon ka haal likha hai: dhoop mein chamakte samundar jaisi.

Aur unke andar ek purana sapna phir se aakaar lene lagta hai. Ek Bharat — sirf naqshe par ek, nahi. Ek aisa Bharat, jo andar se ek hi log ho jaaye.

Ye baat yaad rakhiye. Yahi sapna aage chalkar Akbar ki taaqat bhi hoga, aur unki hadd bhi.

Unka asli daanv talwar nahi tha.

Is desh mein aise Rajput sardar the, jo kisi ke aage nahi jhukte the. Akbar ne unhe apne saath kiya. Shaadi ke rishte se bhi. Unhone ek Rajput rajkumari se byaah kiya.

Iska nateeja kya hua?

Unke bete Jehangir aadhe Mughal the, aadhe Rajput. Unke pote Shah Jahan ki maa bhi Rajput thi. Teen peedhi — aur ek Turco-Mongol khandan khoon se zyada Hindustani ho gaya.

Aur ye rishta sirf ghar ka nahi tha. Mughal amir dheere-dheere Hindustani hote gaye, aur Rajput log Iran ki sanskriti se rangte gaye. Rajput gharane saltanat ki reet aur fauj ki reedh ki haddi ban gaye.

Sirf ek aadmi nahi jhuka. Mewar ke Rana Pratap. Unhone jangal mein bhaagte rehna chuna, par ek baar bhi us aadmi ko salaam nahi kiya, jise wo baahar se aaya hamlavar maante the.

Aur Akbar apne aas-paas kamaal ke log jama kar lete hain. Do bhai — Faizi aur Abul Fazl. Aur Birbal. Unka darbar har dharm ka, aur har naye khayal ka, ek adda ban gaya. Koi naya tarika, koi nayi cheez — wo wahan pahunchti thi.

Unki sahansheelta itni chaudi thi ki kattar maulvi naraaz ho gaye. Akbar ne to ek naya dharm hi chalane ki koshish ki, jo sabko jod de.

Nateeja ye nikla ki wo Hinduon mein bhi utne hi lokpriya the, jitne Musalmaanon mein. Aisa na hua ki Mughal baahar waale rahe. Mughal vansh is desh ka apna vansh ban gaya.

Par isi jigyasu darbar mein ek cheez thi — jise kisi ne khola hi nahi.

## Part 4: Darbar mein ek ghadi

Fatehpur Sikri. Wahi daalan. Wahi chhapi hui kitaab.

Akbar kitaab ek taraf rakh dete hain, aur agle kaam par lag jaate hain. Koi nahi poochta ki ye panne chhape kaise.

Ye kitni ajeeb baat hai.

Kyunki Akbar ke baare mein paadri likhte hain ki wo sab kuch jaanna chahte the. Dharam bhi, jung bhi, aur mashin bhi. Unhe jangi hatyaar aur kaarigari ke tareeqe samajh aate the. Jangi haathi unhe bahut pyaare the.

Unki jigyasa ki misaal paadri aisi dete hain: wo sab kuch ek saath seekhna chahte the, jaise ek bhookha aadmi apna poora khana ek hi kaur mein nigal jaaye.

To phir sawal aur bada ho jaata hai. Chaar jagah dekhiye.

Pehli. Samundar.

Vasco da Gama 1498 mein Calicut pahunch chuka tha. Uske terah saal baad Portuguese ne Malacca le liya, aur Hind Mahasagar par apna raaj bana liya. Goa Portuguese ke haath mein tha.

Akbar zameen par jitne bade the, samundar par utne hi bebas. Wahi Portuguese, Haj ke liye samundar se jaane wale teerth-yatriyon ko pakad lete the, aur phir keemat maangte the — aur un yatriyon mein kabhi-kabhi unka apna khandan ya amir log hote the.

Par ek zameeni taaqat samundar ki ahmiyat nahi samajhti. Halanki is desh ki purani shaan ka ek bada hissa samundari raaston ke control se aaya tha. Akbar ne ek baar jahaaz banane ki baat sochi bhi, par use shauq samjha gaya, kaam nahi.

Doosri. Top-khana.

Mughal fauj ki topon ka bharosa videshi visheshagyon par tha — zyadatar Ottoman Turkon par. Top-khane ke mukhiya ka khitaab hi aisa tha: Rumi Khan. Rum, yaani poorab ka Rome — Constantinople. Ye videshi log yahan ke aadmiyon ko train karte the.

To phir koi apne aadmi ko baahar seekhne kyun nahi bhejta? Koi research kyun nahi karwaata?

Teesri. Chhapai.

Chhapi hui Bible to unke haath mein thi. Paadri ek-do aur chhapi kitaabein bhi laaye the. Ek samrajya ke liye ye kitni badi cheez thi — farmaan, hisaab, hukumnama, sab ek saancha, ek naap mein, hazaaron copies mein. Sarkar ka poora kaam badal sakta tha.

Phir bhi koi ye nahi poochta ki ye bana kaise. Chhapakhana mangvane ki baat koi nahi karta.

Chauthi. Ghadi.

Spring wali ghadiyan Portuguese laaye, aur baad mein Angrez. Mughal amiron ke ghar mein ye shaan ki cheez ban gayi. Aam log dhoop-ghadi, ret-ghadi aur paani-ghadi se kaam chalaate rahe.

Par ek bhi aadmi nahi utha, jisne ghadi khol kar dekha ho ki andar kya hai. Ye chal rahi hai kaise? Ye kamani kahan se aayi? Use yahan banane ki koshish kisi ne nahi ki.

Aur ye us desh mein hua, jahan duniya ke behtareen kaarigar the. Jahan lohar aur badhai apne haath se wo cheezein banate the, jinhe Europe ke log sirf sapne mein soch sakte the.

Ek kaarigar ki jigyasa aur ek samrajya ki jigyasa — dono ek hi darbar mein. Par dono kabhi mile nahi.

Aaj sochiye. Unn dino us darbar mein har dharm ki behas hoti thi. Har naye khayal ki. Par kaarigari ki ek behas nahi hoti thi.

To sawal ye hai: ye bimari sirf Bharat ki thi?

## Part 5: Ghadiyan jo gum ho gayi

Nahi. Nehru saaf kehte hain — ye sirf Bharat ki bimari nahi thi.

Poora pashchim aur madhya Asia isse zyada grasit tha. Shayad China bhi.

Ye log amaan nahi the. Bharat aur China, dono, pehle vigyaan ke kai hisson mein aage badhe the. Jahaazbani. Door-door ka samundari vyapaar. Ye cheezein machine mein sudhaar ke liye ek lagatar khinchaav deti thi.

Par sadiyon tak na yahan koi bada machine ka aavishkar hua, na wahan. Us waqt ki duniya, hazaar ya do hazaar saal pehle wali duniya se bahut alag nahi thi.

Aur Arabon ki kahani to aur bhi dukh bhari hai.

Khud Arabon ne sabse pehle amal wala vigyaan shuru kiya tha. Kaha jaata hai ki sabse shuruaati ghadiyan Arabon ne hi banayi thi — saatvin sadi mein. Damascus mein ek mashhoor ghadi thi. Aur Harun-al-Rashid ke Baghdad mein bhi.

Phir Baghdad gira, Arab taaqat kam hui. Aur dekhiye kya hua. Ghadi banane ka hunar in deshon se gaayab ho gaya.

Udhar Europe ke kuch deshon mein ye hunar badhta raha. Wahan ghadiyan aisi cheez hi nahi rahi — wahan ye aam hoti gayi.

Chhapai ki kahani suniye.

Yahan chhape hone se bahut pehle, Spain ke Moorish Arab, lakdi ke thappon se chhapai karte the. Unka raajya ise apne farmaan aur hukumnama guna karne ke liye istemaal karta tha.

Par wo thappa aage nahi badha. Aur baad mein wo bhi mit gaya.

Udhar Ottoman Turk. Wo Europe aur pashchim Asia mein sabse badi Musalmaan taaqat the. Unke darwaze par, unke bilkul paas, Europe mein kitaabein hazaaron ki tadaad mein chhap rahi thi.

Unhe iska pata tha. Par unhone sadiyon tak chhapakhana apnaya hi nahi.

Wajah? Ek, koi khinchaav hi nahi tha. Doosri, dharm ki bezatti ka darr. Kya pata chhape hue panne kisi ne utha liye, pairon ke neeche aa gaye, ya koode ke dher mein chale gaye. Apni pavitra kitaab ko chhapna unhone mana kiya.

Chhapakhana Misr mein baad mein koi bahar se le kar aaya. Aur wahan se wo dheere-dheere phaila.

To udhar Europe mein kya ho raha tha?

## Part 6: Europe ka naya josh

Europe us waqt kai maamlon mein pichhda tha. Par wo ek badi badlaav ki dehari par khada tha.

Ek nayi hawa chal rahi thi. Wo apne jahaaziyon ko samundar ke us paar bhej rahi thi, aur sochne walon ke dimaag nayi taraf mod rahi thi.

Ek baat ulte dimaag ki hai. Renaissance ne vigyaan ke liye kuchh khaas nahi kiya. Kuchh hadd tak usne logon ko vigyaan se door kiya. Aur uski universities mein jo purani, conservative shiksha shuru hui, usne wo baatein bhi phailne nahi di, jo pehle se maaloom thi.

Ek misaal suniye. Aathraahvin sadi ke beech tak, padhe-likhe angrezon mein zyadatar log ye maanne se inkaar karte the ki dharti ghoomti hai, ya sooraj ke chaaron taraf chakkar kaatti hai. Copernicus, Galileo aur Newton ko pata tha. Achhe telescope ban rahe the. Phir bhi wo Greek-Latin kitaabon mein pade rahe, aur Ptolemy wale purane naqshe ko pakde rahe — jisme dharti beech mein hai.

Unneesvin sadi mein England ke ek bade neta bhi hue, bahut padhe-likhe, par vigyaan unhe samajh nahi aata tha, aur kheenchta bhi nahi tha.

Aaj bhi dekhiye. Bahut se bade neta vigyaan ke baare mein kam jaante hain — aur unki duniya usi vigyaan ke sahaare chalti hai. Wahi vigyaan wo bade paimane par tabahi ke liye istemaal karte hain.

To phir Europe aage kaise nikla?

Kyunki Renaissance ne Europe ke mann ki kai purani zanjeerein tod di thi, aur kai aise buthaan gira diye the, jinhe log seene se laga kar baithaaye the.

Ek nayi aadat jag uthi. Cheezon ko gaur se dekhne ki. Poochne ki — ye sach hai kya? Ye kaam kaise karta hai? Ye baat kis buniyad par khadi hai?

Isi soch ko us sadi ke ek angrez darshanik ne aise likha: insaan ki taaqat aur insaan ka gyaan, dono ek hi raaste par chalte hain. Behtar ye hai ki vigyaan ko un buniyaadon par uthaya jaaye, jo amal se judi hain.

Saikde saal baad ek doosre angrez lekhak ne isi baat ko aage badhaya. Unka saar ye tha: gyaan ka sabse bada dushman hota hai — authority ko seedha maan lena, aur purane samay ko hukmar saabit kar dena. Log purane daur ko itni andhadhund shraddha se dekhte hain ki purane logon ka kehna, naye logon ke tark se bada ban jaata hai. Aur jo baat jitni purani, utni sach ke kareeb maani jaati hai.

Ab dhyaan dijiye. Akbar ki sadi kaun si thi? Solahvin.

Aur Europe mein solahvin sadi ne ek cheez ko janm diya — gati ka vigyaan. Ye koi chhoti baat nahi thi. Ye insaani zindagi mein ek inqilaab tha.

Us ek khoj ke saath Europe chal pada. Shuru mein dheere. Phir badhti raftaar se. Aur unneesvin sadi tak usne ek nayi duniya khadi kar di.

Udhar Asia? Asia statik tha. So raha tha. Purane dhang se apna kaam chala raha tha — insaan ki mehnat aur paseene ke sahaare.

## Part 7: Andar se toota

To ye hua kyun?

Nehru pehle ek chetavni dete hain. Asia itna bada aur itna tarah-tarah hai ki ek hi jawab saare deshon par nahi lagta. Har desh ko alag dekho.

Aur yahan ek baat hairaan karne wali hai. China us waqt, aur uske baad bhi, Europe se zyada sabhya tha — wahan logon ki zindagi Europe se zyada shaaleen thi. Aur Bharat? Bahar se dekho to yahan ek chamakta darbar tha, aur phoolta hua vyapaar, karigari, kaam-dhandha. Kai maamlon mein Europe ke desh ek Bharatiya yaatri ko pichhda aur ganwaar lagte.

Par ek cheez yahan bilkul nahi thi. Wo bechaini, jo Europe mein dikh rahi thi. Wo raftaar.

Aur Nehru ab apna asli jawab dete hain. Koi bhi sabhyata bahar ke hamle se kam girti hai, aur andar ki thakaan se zyada.

Thakaan kaise aati hai? Teen tareeqe hain.

Ek. Wo sabhyata apna kaam poora kar chuki hoti hai. Uske paas ek badalti hui duniya ko dene ke liye kuchh bacha hi nahi hota.

Do. Jo log us sabhyata ko uthaye hue hain, unki chaandi aa jaati hai. Wo uska bojh sambhal nahi paate.

Teen. Us samaj ka rehan-sehan hi ek deewar ban jaata hai. Uske aage badhna mumkin hi nahi, jab tak wo deewar gire na, ya koi aisi nayi cheez na aaye jo sab kuch hilaa de.

Aur ye girta hua dhaancha — ye Turk aur Afghan hamlon se bahut pehle hi dikh raha tha.

To kya in hamlon se kuchh naya hua?

Kuchh hua. Kala nayi hui. Imaaratein nayi hui. Chitrakari, sangeet, jeene ke dhang — sab par asar pada.

Par ye asar zameen tak nahi pahuncha. Ye upar hi reh gaya. Samaj wahi ka wahi raha. Aur kuchh maamlon mein to wo pehle se bhi sakht ho gaya.

Afghan koi nayi cheez apne saath nahi laaye the. Wo khud ek purane dhaanche ke log the — tabeli aur feudal.

Bharat mein Europe jaisa feudalism nahi tha. Par Rajput kul, jo is desh ki suraksha ki reedh ki haddi the, kisi tarah usi purane dhaanche mein bandhe the. Mughal bhi aadhe feudal the — fark ye tha ki unke beech mein ek mazboot raja khada tha. Aur us raja ne Rajputana ke aadhe-adhure feudalism ko kuchal diya.

Aur Akbar? Kya Akbar badlaav laa sakte the?

Nehru kehte hain, ho sakta tha.

Agar unki bechain jigyasa samaj ki taraf mud jaati, aur agar wo ye jaanna chahte ki duniya ke doosre hisson mein kya ho raha hai, to wo badlaav ki neev rakh sakte the.

Par wo apni saltanat jodne mein itne lage hue the.

Aur unke saamne ek badi gaath thi: Islam, jo naye logon ko jodna chahta hai — aur is desh ke logon ka apna dharm, apni reet. Dono ko ek saath kaise chalayein? Ek rashtra kaise banayein?

Isliye unhone dharm ko tark ki roshni mein samajhne ki koshish ki. Aur kuchh samay ke liye maamla badla hua bhi lagta tha.

Par ye seedha raasta nahi chala. Aur duniya mein kahin bhi aise seedhe raaste kam hi chalte hain.

Nateeja? Akbar ne bhi us samaj ke dhaanche mein koi buniyaadi fark nahi kiya. Unke baad wo badlaav ki hawa, wo dimaag ka adventure, dheemi pad gayi. Aur Bharat apni usi purani, sthirk zindagi par laut gaya.

## Part 8: Sau saal ki imaarat

Nateeja ye hua ki Akbar ne jo imaarat khadi ki, wo unke baad sau saal tak khadi rahi. Kamzor vaaris hone ke bawajood.

Kyunki Mughalon ke andar ek purani bimari thi. Har badshah ke baad, bhai aur rajkumar aapas mein gaddi ke liye ladte the. Har jung saltanat ki jaan kha jaati thi.

Phir bhi darbar ki raunak kam nahi hui. Grand Mughal ki shohrat Asia se Europe tak phaili.

Aur imaaratein banti rahi. Agra aur Delhi mein aisi imaaratein utthin, jinme purani Bharatiya soch aur ek nayi saadgi, ek nayi khoobsurat lakeer mili.

Ye Indo-Mughal kala us zamane ke mandiron se bilkul alag thi, jinme ek thakaan dikh rahi thi — har deewar par zyada nakashi, sone ka zyada paani, itna zyada ki aankh ko kahin aaram na mile.

Aur isi daur mein, imaaratiyon ne apne pyaar bhare haathon se ek imaarat khadi ki. Agra ka Taj Mahal.

Ab aakhri Grand Mughal ki baat kijiye.

Nehru yahan ek mohavra likhte hain. Aurangzeb ne ghadi ulti ghoomane ki koshish ki. Aur is koshish mein usne ghadi rok di — aur ghadi tod di.

Mughal badshah tab tak mazboot the, jab tak wo is desh ke mann ke saath chale. Jab tak unhone ek saajhi rashtriyata banane par kaam kiya, aur is desh ke saare hisson ko jodne ki koshish ki.

Jis din Aurangzeb is dhaare ke khilaaf khade hue, use dabane lage, aur khud ko Bharatiya raja se zyada ek Musalmaan raja ki tarah chalane lage — usi din Mughal saltanat bikharne lagi.

Akbar ka kaam mitne laga. Wo saari taaqatein, jinhe Akbar ne ek deewar ke peeche baandh rakha tha, deewar tod kar baahar aa gayin.

Nayi lehrein uthi. Unki soch tang thi, par unme ek ubharti rashtriyata thi. Wo itni mazboot nahi thi ki kuchh khada kar sakein, aur waqt unke saath nahi tha. Par itni zaroor thi ki Mughal samrajya ko tod sakein.

Us dhaanche ke saath ek doosri baat bhi chali. Jehangir aur Shah Jahan koi kamaal ke aadmi nahi the. Phir bhi unke raaj kaamyab rahe — kyunki unhone usi lakeer par chalna jaari rakha, jo Akbar ne itni mazbooti se khinchi thi.

Aur ek ajeeb baat: Aurangzeb ke baad bhi us dhaanche ke liye logon ke dil mein shraddha bachi rahi. Par ye shraddha sirf uttar aur madhya Bharat tak thi. Dakshin aur pashchim tak nahi pahunchi. Isliye is dhaanche ko chunauti paschim se aayi.

## Part 9: Khola darwaza, band deewar

Ise samajhne ke liye ek doosri taraf jaiye. Darbar ke bahar.

Satrahvin sadi. Do shehar. Do mahal.

France mein ek raja apna bada mahal bana raha tha. Madhya Europe ko ek lambi jung ujaad rahi thi.

Aur Bharat mein Shah Jahan. Agra mein Taj Mahal aur Moti Masjid. Delhi ki Jama Masjid.

Delhi ka Mayur Singhasan wala darbar France ke mahal se bhi zyada shaandaar tha. Par Nehru ek line jodte hain: ye dono gareeb, lut-ti hui praja par khade the. Gujarat aur Dekkhan mein bhayanak akaal pada tha.

Isi waqt ek aur cheez ho rahi thi, jispar kisi ne dhyan nahi diya.

Akbar ne sirf Portuguese dekhe the. Unke bete Jehangir ke zamane mein Angrezon ke jahaaz ne Hindustani samundar mein Portuguese ko hara diya. Angrezon ka ek doot, Sir Thomas Roe, 1615 mein Jehangir ke darbar mein aaya, aur kothiyan kholne ki ijazat maang kar le gaya.

Surat mein factory shuru hui. Madras 1639 mein basa.

Aur phir? Sau saal tak kisi ne Angrezon ko ahmiyat nahi di.

Ye baat gaur karne wali hai. Portuguese ab samundar par nahi the. Angrez samundari raaston ke maalik ban chuke the. Par Mughal darbar aur uske salahkaron ke liye iska koi matlab nahi tha.

Ek baar jab Mughal samrajya aankhon ke saamne kamzor pad raha tha — Aurangzeb ke daur mein — Angrezon ne jang se zyada ilaaqe lene ki koshish ki. Saal 1685.

Aurangzeb, kamzor hote hue bhi aur har taraf dushmanon se ghira hone ke bawajood, Angrezon ko hara diya.

France mein us raja ka lamba raaj chal raha tha, aur wahan aane wali kranti ke beej pade ja rahe the.

Isse pehle hi French bhi is desh mein apne paanv jama chuke the. Europe ki ubalti hui urja India aur poorab ki taraf phail rahi thi — theek us waqt jab is desh ki raajnitik aur aarthik halat tezi se gir rahi thi.

Darwaza samundar ki taraf khula pada tha. Aur deewar, jo sabse oonchi thi, wahi thi jo darbar ne apne aas-paas khadi ki hui thi.

## Part 10: Ghadi ulti ghoomti hai

Udhar Europe mein sirf vyapaar nahi badal raha tha. Satta badal rahi thi.

England mein naye madhyam varg uthe, jo vyapaar se bade hue the. Unke haath se raja ka sir kat gaya. Ek chhota ganraajya aaya aur chala gaya, aur aakhir Parliament raja se oopar baith gaya.

Isi daur mein Aurangzeb gaddi par baithe — gharelu jung ke baad, apne pita Shah Jahan ko kaid kar ke.

Nehru likhte hain: is waqt ko samajhne wala sirf ek Akbar hi ho sakta tha — aur shayad wo bhi sirf der kar sakta tha, agar unki jigyasa use nayi technique aur badalte aarthik haalaat ka matlab samjha pati.

Aurangzeb aaj ko bhi nahi samjhe, kal ko bhi nahi. Wo purane daur ke aadmi the — kattar puritan, kala-sahitya se bezaar.

Unhone Hinduon par phir wo purana jaziya lagaya, aur kai mandir tudawe. Unhone un ghamandi Rajputon ko naraaz kiya, jo Mughal saltanat ke khambhe the.

Punjab mein unhone Sikhon ko chheda — ek shaant panth, jisme Hindu aur Islam ki baatein mili thi. Zulm ne use ladaku bhai-chaare mein badal diya.

Aur paschim mein? Unhone Marathas ko chheda — Rashtrakuton ke vanshaj, pashchim Bharat ka purana rajavansh. Aur theek tab, jab unme ek kamaal ka neta uth raha tha.

Ek lehar uth rahi thi, jisme dharm aur rashtriyata mili thi. Ye saare Bharat ko apne daayre mein nahi leti thi. Rajput apne kul ki soch mein bandhe the. Sikh Punjab se bahar dekh hi nahi sakte the. Ek vidvaan ne likha hai ki Indo-European khandan mein sirf yahi ek hissa hai jisne ek bada rashtriya dharm aur ek bada vishwa-dharm diya.

Phir bhi is lehar mein dhaancha tha — yahi uski taaqat bhi thi, aur yahi uski sabse badi kamzori bhi; kyunki jo rashtriyata ek dharm ke ghere mein khadi ho, wo baaki logon ko bahar chhod deti hai.

Marathas hi ek giroh tha, jiske andar kuchh zyada chaudi baat thi. Ek angrez ne 1784 mein likha ki Hindustan aur Dekkhan mein sirf Marathas ke andar rashtriya lagav ka bhaav hai — koi bada khatra aaye to unke sardar ek ho jaayenge. Unke raajnitik aur fauji dhang khule the, aur andar ek tarah ka loktantra tha.

Arthvyavastha bhi chatak rahi thi.

Sadi ke aakhir mein, Delhi ke paas hi, Jat kisaan baar-baar uthne lage. Aur ek doosra bagawat — Satnami. Ek Mughal amir ne unhe hiqaarat se sunaar, badhai, safai wale aur mochi jaisa giroh kaha.

Aur yahi nayi baat thi: ab tak bagawat raja aur nauwab karte the; ab aam log kar rahe the.

In sab ke beech, Sahyadri ki dhundh mein, ek naam ubharta hai.

Shivaji. Saal 1627 mein janme. Pahadi, sakht aadmiyon ke sardar, jinki chhoti fauj bade ilaaqe hila deti thi. Unhone Surat ko luta, jahan Angrezon ki kothi thi, aur Mughal ilaaqon se chauth vasool kiya.

Nehru unhe ubharti Hindu rashtriyata ka prateek maante hain. Unhone purane granthon se prerna li, aur unme neta ke gun the. Unhone Marathas ko ek mazboot jut fauj banaya, use ek rashtriya background diya — aisi taaqat jo Mughal samrajya tod de.

Unki maut 1680 mein hui, par Maratha taaqat badhti rahi — itni ki aage chalkar wo poore Bharat par chha gayi.

To ab wapas us dopahar mein chaliye. Fatehpur Sikri. Laal patthar. Dhoop ki lakeerein.

Ek chhapi hui kitaab, ek taraf rakhi hui. Aur ek sawal, jo hawa mein latak gaya.

Itna jigyasu samraat bhi Europe ki nayi taaqat ko kyun nahi dekh paaya?

Jawab ye hai. Kyunki unki jigyasa soch ki taraf thi, cheezon ki taraf nahi. Wo ye poochte the ki kaun sa dharm sach hai. Wo ye nahi poochte the ki ye cheez banti kaise hai.

Aur Akbar, jitna bada unka darbar tha, wo khud apne samaj ki virasat ke qaidi the. Isliye wo sawal utha hi nahi.

Aur ek peedhi baad, Aurangzeb ne us ghadi ko ulta ghumaya. Aur ghumaate hue tod diya.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Pehli baat. Chhapi hui kitaab. Akbar ke haath mein aisi cheez aayi jo poori duniya badalne wali thi, aur sawal utha hi nahi. Aaj har nayi technology ke saath wahi pal laut kar aata hai. Hum use kharidte hain, chalaate hain, tareef karte hain. Kitne log use khol kar dekhte hain?

Doosri baat. Jigyasa ka rukh. Nehru kehte hain ki koi sabhyata andar ki thakaan se girti hai, bahar ke hamle se kam. Ghar ki baat ho, darbar ki baat ho, ya desh ki — sawal ek hi hai. Hum apne andar wo deewar dhoondh rahe hain, jise koi hamla nahi tod sakta?

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapke ghar mein koi ek cheez aisi hai, jise aap roz chalaate hain, par aaj tak khol kar nahi dekha?

## Outro

Aurangzeb ke baad Mughal saltanat sirf ek parchhaai reh gayi. Us khaali jagah mein do nayi taaqatein khadi hui.

Ek samundar ke kinaare. Ek vyapaari company, jiske paas taaqat thi, par zimmedaari nahi.

Aur doosri, Rajasthan mein. Ek raja, jisne aasman ke taare naape, aur ek din Portugal se aayi tables mein galti pakad li.

Naam yaad rakhiye. Agli baar hum Jaipur ki ek raat mein honge.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
