## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Pichhli baar hum ek aise daur ke aakhir par ruke the, jahan Mughal satta toot rahi thi aur naye jhande uth rahe the — ek pashchim mein, ek pahaadon mein. Us khaali jagah mein ek teesri cheez ghus aayi thi. Aaj wahi sawal: ye desh ek vyapaari company ka kaise ban gaya?

## Part 1: Chaar khiladi, ek khaali takht

18vi sadi ki shuruaat. Delhi mein takht hai. Badshah hai. Par badshahon ka wazan nahi raha.

Is khaali jagah ke liye maidan mein chaar khiladi the. Do Hindustani, do baahar ke.

Pehle, Marathas. Pichhli baar hum Shivaji se mile the. Unke banaye pahaadi savaar ab is desh ki sabse badi taaqat the.

Doosre, dakshin mein Haider Ali, aur unke bete Tipu Sultan. Haider Ali — dakshin ka wo sultan jisne Company ki fauj ko baar-baar haraya.

Teesre, Angrez. Aur chauthi, French. Ye dono yahan apni apni desh ki badi ladai ka ek chhota sa hissa lad rahe the.

Ab sochiye us sadi ki pehli aadhi ka manzar. Sabse aage kaun tha?

Marathas. Lagbhag tay dikhta tha ki ek din ye poore desh par raaj karenge. Aur Mughal samrajya ki jagah le lenge.

Ye khayaali baat nahi thi. 1737 mein Maratha savaar Delhi ke darwaazon par khade the.

Darwaazon par. Takht ke shehar ke.

Aur unhe rokne wali koi taaqat nahi thi.

To kahani yahin khatam ho jaani chahiye thi. Par nahi hui.

Kyunki is desh ka ek darwaza hai jo band nahi hota. Wo uttar-pashchim mein hai. Pahaadon ke us paar.

1739. Nadir Shah utar aaya.

Nadir Shah — Iran ka wo hamlavar jo lootne Delhi tak chala aaya.

Ye koi lambi ladai nahi thi. Ek aasan hamla. Delhi ke jo raja the, wo ladai bhool chuke the. Aaram-pasand ho gaye the.

Wo loot kar le gaya. Itna, ki uske saath woh mashhoor takht bhi chala gaya, jise Mor Sihasan kehte hain.

Aur ek ajeeb baat. Nadir Shah ka Marathas se koi takraav nahi hua.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki Marathas peeche hat gaye. Ulta. Uske jaane ke baad unhone Punjab tak apna phailaav badha diya. Ek baar phir unka poore desh par raaj dikhne laga.

Par Nadir Shah apne saath do tohfe chhod gaya. Dono bade.

Pehla. Delhi ke Mughal badshahon ka aakhri bharosa bhi chala gaya. Ab wo sirf parchhaiyan the. Naam ke badshah. Jinhe jo taaqatwar ho, apne haath se nachaye.

Aur ye khokhlaapan naya nahi tha. Purani reet ka jaadu itna gehra tha, ki Company khud, aur baaki sab, Plassey ke aane tak us badshah ko nazrana bhejte rahe. Plassey ke baad bhi bahut saal Company khud ko usi ka vakil maanti rahi.

Doosra tohfa. Afghanistan is desh se alag ho gaya.

Afghanistan bahut purane zamane se is desh ka hissa tha. Ab wo Nadir Shah ke raaj mein chala gaya. Kuch waqt baad wahan bagaavat hui, aur Nadir Shah ko uske hi afsaron ne maar diya. Afghanistan ek azaad rajya ban gaya.

Ek raat ne takht khokhla kiya. Aur ek sarhad tod di.

## Part 2: Panipat, aur ek daftar

[SFX: door se aati hui ghodeon ki gadgadahat, phir dhool mein dabti hui cheekhein]

Marathas ko Nadir Shah se koi farq nahi pada. Wo Punjab mein phailte rahe.

Unka asli imtihaan 1761 mein aaya. Panipat.

Panipat — wahi maidan jahan is desh ka faisla baar-baar hua hai. Is baar saamne tha Ahmad Shah Durrani, wo Afghan jo us waqt Afghanistan par raaj karta tha.

Us din Marathas ki poori taaqat khatam ho gayi. Unke sabse achhe savaar. Unke sabse achhe sardar. Sab wahin reh gaye.

Kuch saal unka samrajya ka sapna phika pad gaya.

Par wo uth khade. Aur apna raaj ek nayi shakal mein khada kiya. Ek sangh.

Poona mein Peshwa — is sangh ka mukhiya. Aur bade rajya: Gwalior ka Scindhia, Indore ka Holkar. Ye sangh pashchim aur madhya Bharat ke bade hisse par raaj kar raha tha.

Par theek usi waqt ek doosri taaqat zameen par pair jama rahi thi. Uska naam tha Company.

1757. Bengal. Plassey.

Clive — Company ka wo afsar jisne ye ladai jeeti. Par kaise? Zyada ladai se nahi. Zyada kaam bagaavat ne kiya, aur jhoothe kagaz ne.

Plassey ko log British samrajya ka shuru kaha karte hain. Par uski shuruaat aisi thi, jiska swaad khatta reh gaya.

Jaldi hi Company ke paas poora Bengal aur Bihar tha.

Aur iska pehla nateeja? Ek bhayanak akal. 1770 mein, is bade, bhare hue ilaake mein, ek tihai se zyada log mar gaye.

Dakshin mein Angrez aur French ki ladai khatam hui. Aur wo Angrezon ki jeet se khatam hui. French Bharat se lagbhag mit gaye.

Uttar mein, Punjab mein, ek Sikh rajya ug raha tha. Ranjit Singh ka. Ye rajya aage Kashmir aur Sarhad tak phaila. Par us waqt ye kinare ka rajya tha, asli ladai se alag.

To 18vi sadi ke aakhir mein desh mein sirf do taaqatein bachi thi. Marathas. Aur British.

Baaki sab inke aage-peechhe.

## Part 3: Kiraye ke sipahi

Ab ek baat samajhni zaroori hai. Ye Company asal mein ek daftar thi. Vyapaar ka.

Uske saath jo fauj thi, wo is vyapaar ki suraksha ke liye thi.

To ye Bengal tak pahunchi kaise?

Dheere-dheere. Aur aisa dheere, ki kisi ko pata bhi nahi chala.

Uska tareeqa ye tha: yahan ke rajaon ki aapas ki ladai mein ek ka saath dena. Kisi ek hone wale se mil kar doosre ko dabana.

Faayda? Iske sipahi kisi bhi taraf ke liye sabse achhe sipahi the. Aur Company apni is madad ka mota daam leti thi.

Yun uski taaqat badhti gayi. Uska lashkar bada hota gaya. Log inhe kiraye ke sipahi samajhte the — jise zaroorat ho, bhada de kar le jaye.

Jab samajh aaya ki Company kisi ka khel nahi khel rahi, sirf apna raaj chahti hai, tab tak wo apni jagah jam chuki thi.

Aur ek badi baat. Baahar walon ke khilaaf logon mein narazgi thi. Par wo rashtriya bhaavna nahi thi.

Us waqt wafadaari paas wale ka thanedar tak jaati thi. Jo sardar paisa ya loot ka mauka de, uske pichhe log chal padte the. Isi wajah se Company ki fauj mein zyadatar sipahi Hindustani the. Sepoy.

Sirf Marathas ke andar kuch aisi baat thi jo ek sardar se aage jaati thi — ek tarah ki rashtriya bhavna. Par wo bhi tang thi. Apne daayre se baahar nahi.

Aur wahi taaqat apne andar hi banti rah gayi.

Maratha sardaron mein aapas ki khincha-tan. Kabhi-kabhi to apni hi ladai. Peshwa ke neeche ek dheeli si ekta thi, bas. Aur jab zaroorat padi, tab koi kisi ke kaam nahi aaya. Ek-ek kar ke sab hare.

Rajputon ke saath unka bartav aisa tha ki wo unhe apna dushman bana baithe. Sahayak mil sakte the. Mili jagah, wo mili shikayatein.

## Part 4: Company kyun jeeti

Agar Marathas — jinme kaam ki taaqat bhi thi, ekta bhi thi — civil aur fauji intezaam mein peeche the, to baaki ka haal sochiye.

Rajputs. Bahadur. Par poori tarah purane feudaal dhang ke. Romaani, aur bilkul na-kaabil. Apas mein hi kabeelawi ladaiyan.

Bahut se Rajput sardar purani wafadaari mein Delhi ki dhalti hui taaqat ke saath ho gaye. Par Delhi itni kamzor thi ki uska faayda hi nahi utha saki. Rajputs khud khelona ban gaye. Aakhir Scindhia ke daayre mein chale gaye.

Uttar aur madhya Bharat ke Musalmaan raja bhi utne hi feudaal, utne hi peeche. Unse sirf uljhan badhi.

Aur Gurkhas? Nepal ke pahaadi sipahi. Sabse achhe. Shayad Company ke sipahiyon se bhi behtar. Feudaal, par apni mitti se aisa lagav ki apni raksha mein wo aise lade ki Angrezon ko darr lag gaya. Par asli ladai par unka koi asar nahi hua.

Aur Marathas? Jitni jagah jeeti, wahan tike nahi. Aaye, gaye. Jaden nahi pakdi.

To ab asli sawal. Ye sab jante hue bhi — Company jeeti kyun?

Pehli baat. Angrezon ka raajneetik aur fauji intezaam dono us zamane mein behtar the. Ek doosre se juda hua. Aur unke sardar kaabil the.

Doosri baat. Wo apne dushmanon se zyada jaankari rakhte the. Aur us jaankari ka poora faayda uthate the.

Unke saamne koi ek desh nahi tha. Alag-alag taaqatein thin. Jinme aapas mein khincha-tan thi. Har khincha-tan Company ke kaam aayi.

Teesri baat. Samundar.

Samundar par unka kabza tha. Iska matlab: surakshit adde. Naye sainik. Naya maal. Aur agar wo ek baar hare, to wo laut kar phir aa sakte the.

Chauthi baat. Paisa. Plassey ke baad Bengal ki daulat unke haath thi. Marathas se ladne ka paisa wahi tha. Har nayi jeet us paise mein aur izaafa karti thi.

Aur Marathas? Ek badi haar ka matlab tha girna. Uthne ka zariya nahi.

Nehru is poore daur ko dekh kar ek baat likhte hain: lagta hai jaise Angrezon ki jeet kuch ittefaqon aur khush-naseebi se hui ho. Kai baar wo hare bhi. Haider Ali ne haraya. Tipu ne haraya. Marathas ne haraya. Sikhon ne haraya. Gurkhas ne haraya. Thodi si badnaseebi hoti, to wo yahan se nikal jaate.

Par har baar wo wapas aaye.

Kyunki unke paas samundar tha, Bengal ka paisa tha, aur humari alag-alag taaqatein thin.

## Part 5: Us daur ka chehra

Ab zara us daur ko dekhiye, jisme ye sab ho raha tha.

Ladai, jeet, loot. Madhya Bharat, Rajputana, dakshin aur pashchim ke kuch hisse — ye jagah ujdi hui zameenein ban gayin. Wahin hinsa. Wahin dukh.

Faujein guzarti thin. Unke pichhe-pichhe raahzan chalte the. Aur us ilaake mein jo log rehte the, unki khabar kisi ko nahi thi — sirf itni, ki unka maal kaise le liya jaye.

Nehru likhte hain, is desh ke kuch hisse aise ho gaye the jaise Europe mein Tees Saal ki Jung ke baad hua tha.

Aur sabse buri haalat wahan thi jahan Company ka raaj tha, ya Company ke aashrit rajya the.

Edward Thompson — ek angrez lekhak, jisne us daur ki haalat likhi. Unka kehna tha ki Madras aur Company ke aashrit rajya Oudh aur Hyderabad aisa manzar the jaise dukhon ka bukhaar. Aur uske saamne Maratha neta Nana Farnavis ke ilaake — shaant suraksha ka ek nakhlistan.

Yahan ek baat sabse ajeeb hai. Angrezon ke paas taaqat thi. Daulat thi. Par jawabdaari nahi thi.

Company ke vyapaariyon ko apna hissa chahiye tha. Khazana chahiye tha. Unhe in logon ko sudhaarne ka, ya bachane ka, koi kaam nahi tha. Aashrit rajyon mein to taaqat aur jawabdaari ka naata hi toot gaya tha.

Jab Marathas ka kaam khatam hua, tab unhone dhyan civil raaj ki taraf diya. Tab kuch tarah ka intezaam aaya.

Aur humein baar-baar yaad dilaya jaata hai ki Angrezon ne is desh ko chaos se bachaya.

Aadha sach. Unke aane ke baad ek tarah ka order aaya. Us daur ko Marathas "khatre ka waqt" kehte the.

Par wo chaos kam se kam aadha Company ki apni neeti se paida hua tha. Aur ho sakta hai ki ye shaanti kisi aur tareeqe se bhi aa jaati — jaise is desh ke paanch hazaar saal ke itihaas mein aur bhi baar aayi thi.

## Part 6: Wo do aadmi

Is poore daur mein ek cheez kho gayi thi. Is desh ke mann mein kya ho raha tha.

Nehru ek shikayat likhte hain. Jo log is desh ka itihaas likhte hain, unhone ladaiyon aur rajaon ke baare mein bahut likha. Par kisi ke mann ke andar jhankne ki koshish kam ki.

Kabhi-kabhi ek jhalak mil jaati hai. Bas itni.

Aur us daur mein wo jhalak aisi thi: log thake hue. Toote hue. Kismat ke aage chup. Ek tarah ke bechaini mein — jaise sab kuch dekh rahe hain, samajh nahi rahe.

Kuch log zaroor jigyaasu the. Wo nayi taaqaton ko samajhna chahte the. Par halat ka sailab unke oopar se guzar gaya. Wo kuch badal nahi paaye.

Unme se ek aadmi ka naam Ranjit Singh tha.

Ranjit Singh — Punjab ka Maharaja, ek Jat Sikh, jisne apna rajya khada kiya, aur aage Kashmir aur Sarhad tak phailaya.

Usme kamzoriyan thin. Buree aadatein bhi. Phir bhi wo ek hairani-aavar insaan tha. Aur uski sabse badi baat ye thi ki wo poochhna band nahi karta tha.

Ek Francisi musafir, Jacquemont, uske baare mein likhta hai: ye bahut bahadur hai, aur maine pehli baar ek aisa jigyaasu Hindustani dekha hai. Wo kehta hai, inki jigyasa poori qaum ki susti ka hisaab kar deti hai.

Aur ek baat. Hindustani log aksar apne mann ki baat nahi rakhte. Aur padhe-likhe log to aur bhi kam. Us waqt ke padhe-likhe log bahar se aaye fauji sardaron se door hi rehte the. Unke kaam dekh kar unhe ghrina hoti thi.

To bahar walon se jo Hindustani milte the, wo aksar mauke-parast the. Ya darbaar ke wo mantri, jo apne hi faayde ke liye chalte the.

Ranjit Singh isme alag tha. Khuli baat.

Aur wo sirf jigyaasu nahi tha. Uska dil bada tha — us zamane mein, jab poora desh pathar ho gaya tha. Usne rajya banaya. Badi fauj banayi. Par khoon bahana use pasand nahi tha.

Ek angrez likhta hai: itna bada rajya itne chhote jurm par kisi ne khada nahi kiya.

Usne har jurm ke liye maut ki sazaa khatam kar di. Aur usi waqt England mein chhoti-chhoti chorion par bhi maut ki sazaa thi.

Ek aur angrez, jo usse milne gaya tha, likhta hai: asli ladai ke alawa unhone kabhi kisi ki jaan nahi li — jabki unki khud ki jaan par ek se zyada baar hamla hua. Aur unka raaj us zamane ke kai sabhya rajyon se zyada reham-dil tha.

## Part 7: Jaipur wala raja

Dusra aadmi ek alag tarah ka tha. Uski jigyasa ki shakal kuch aur thi.

Sawai Jai Singh. Jaipur ka. Rajputana.

Ye Ranjit Singh se pehle ka zamana hai. Aur ye wo daur hai jab Aurangzeb ke baad darbaar mein baar-baar hichkole aa rahe the.

Jai Singh in hichkolon mein bacha reh gaya. Kabhi Delhi ka aadhipatya maana. Jab Marathas ko rokna mushkil laga, to badshah ki taraf se unse samjhauta kar liya. Us waqt ke hisaab se chalak.

Par Nehru ko uski siyasat se matlab nahi. Nehru ke liye Jai Singh ki asli baat kuch aur hai.

Wo ek ganit-shastri tha. Khagol-vid. Vaigyanik. Shehar banane wala. Aur itihaas padhne wala.

[SFX: dhaatu ke yaantaron par ghoomti hui chhaya — ek dheemi, sargarm si ghoonjh]

Usne paanch badi vedhshalaayein banwayi. Jaipur, Delhi, Ujjain, Benares, Mathura.

Aur phir, ek din, Portugal se aaye padreson se usne suna ki Portugal mein khagol-vigyan kahan pahunch gaya hai.

To usne apne hi log bheje. Ek padre unke saath gaya. Gaya kahan? Portugal ke raja ke darbaar.

Wahan se raja ne apni tables bhejin.

Ab wo tables Jaipur pahunchi. Aur Jai Singh ne apni tables se unhe milaya.

Nateeja? Portugal ki tables kam sahi thin. Kai galtiyan thin.

Aur galti ka kaaran? Jai Singh kehte hain, unke yaantaron ka ghera chhota tha.

Ruk kar sochiye. Ye sirf "videshi cheez galat thi" ki kahani nahi hai. Ye is baat ki kahani hai ki yahan ginti ka kaam chal raha tha. Apna kaam.

Jai Singh ko Bharatiya ganit aata tha. Usne purane Yunani granth padhe the. Usne Europe ke naye kaam bhi jaane.

To usne wo kitabein mangwayi — aur Sanskrit mein anuvad karwayi. Yunani jyamiti ka wo purana vidvaan, Euclid. Trigonometry ke granth. Aur logarithm — bade ankdon se guna-bhag karne ka naya tareeqa.

Arabic kitabein bhi usne khagol-vigyan ki anuvad karwayi.

Nehru kehte hain, Jai Singh ke paas us zamane ka saara gyaan ikattha tha — apna bhi, bahar ka bhi.

Aur Jaipur shehar usne khud sajaya. Europe ke kai shehron ke naqshe ikattha kiye. Phir apna naqsha banaya.

Wo purane Europe ke naqshe aaj bhi Jaipur museum mein rakhe hain. Aur Jaipur aisa sajaya gaya ki aaj bhi use shehar-sajane ka ek namuna maana jaata hai.

Ab is aadmi ki teen baatein sun lijiye.

Pehli. Uski zindagi lambi nahi thi.

Aur uske poore jeevan mein ladaiyan chalti rahin. Darbaar ki chaalbaazi chalti rahi. Wo khud bhi in sab mein ghusa raha.

Doosri. Nadir Shah ka wo 1739 ka hamla uski maut se bas chaar saal pehle hua tha.

Sochiye, wo aakhri saal kaise honge. Ek taraf Delhi looti jaa rahi hai. Doosri taraf ek raja apne parchaun par ginti milaa raha hai.

Teesri. Aur ye sabse badi baat hai.

Jai Singh Rajputana ke us feudaal mahaul mein rehta tha, aur us daur mein jab is desh ke itihaas ka sabse andhera waqt tha. Wahan se ek vaigyanik nikla.

Ye ittefaq nahi. Ye is baat ka sabooot hai ki is desh mein poochhne ki aadat mari nahi thi.

Aur wo akela sochne wala aadmi bhi nahi tha. Wo apne zamane ki paidawar tha. Usne apne saath kaam karne wale bahut se vidvaan ikattha kiye. Unme se kuch ko wo Portugal gaye dooton ke saath bheja. Aur samaj ke niyam uske raaste mein nahi aaye.

Nehru ek badi baat likhte hain. Ho sakta hai is desh mein vaigyanik kaam ke liye khub maal tha. Sochne wale log the. Haath wale log the. Kami sirf mauke ki thi.

Wo mauka bahut der baad tak nahi aaya.

Aur aage chalkar jab wo dikkatein khatam ho gayin, tab bhi satta walon ne vaigyanik kaam ko koi protsaahan nahi diya.

To us daur mein is desh ki asli taaqat kahan thi? Jai Singh jaise logon mein. Aur unke bank, unke bazaar, unki karkhane mein.

## Part 8: Hundi, jahaaz aur daulat

Ab Bharat ke bazaar mein chaliye.

Ek angrez vidvaan ne likha hai: 18vi sadi tak is desh ke banane aur vyapaar karne ke tareeqe duniya mein kisi bhi jagah se kam nahi the.

Bharat ek bada banane wala desh tha. Uske bane maal Europe tak jaate the.

Uski bank-vyavastha majboot thi. Hundi — yaani ek shehar se doosre shehar tak paise bhejne ka kagaz. Ye hundi poori Hindustan mein hi nahi, Iran, Kabul, Herat aur Tashkent tak maani jaati thi.

Vyapaari daulat ubhar aayi thi. Dalaal, beech-wale, agent — ek poora jaal.

Aur jahaaz? Jahaaz banane ka kaam yahan phal-phool raha tha. Napoleon ki ladaiyon ke zamane mein ek Angrez admiral ke jahaaz bhi ek Hindustani firm ne banaye the.

Nehru ek saaf baat likhte hain: industry, vyapaar aur dhan-vyavastha mein Bharat us waqt kisi bhi desh se peeche nahi thi. Industrial kranti se pehle ki duniya mein jitna aage koi ja sakta tha, utna aage tha.

Par itna kuch kabhi nahi hota, agar is desh ne lambe-lambe shaant daur nahi dekhe hote, aur uske raaste vyapaar ke liye surakshit na hote.

Baahar wale yahan kyun aaye the?

Isliye ki ye cheezein sach mein achhi thin. Unka Europe mein bada bazaar tha.

Shuru mein Company ka asli dhanda yahi tha: yahan ka maal Europe le jaana. Aur is vyapaar mein munafa mota tha.

Yahi nahi. Is desh ke kaarigar itne kabil the ki wo England ki nayi machine-wali technique se bhi takkar le sakte the.

Aur jab machine-wala daur shuru hua, to bhi ye maal baha aa raha tha. Usko rokne ke liye bhaari mahsool lagane pade. Kuch jagah poori pabandi.

Adam Smith — angrez, jiski economics ki kitaab aaj bhi padhi jaati hai — is Company ke dhandhe par saaf likha: vyapaariyon ki ek aisi band company ka raaj, kisi bhi desh ke liye, sabse bura raaj hota hai.

Par ek cheez is desh ke paas nahi thi. Satta.

Vyapaari aur karkhane wale daulat-wale the. Desh ka economic dhaancha unke haath mein tha. Par raaj ki taaqat unke haath mein nahi thi.

Raaj despotik tha, aur bahut hadd tak feudaal — pehle se bhi zyada feudaal. Isliye yahan wo madhyam varg khada hi nahi ho paaya, jo pashchim ke kuch deshon mein satta chhinne ki soch rakhta tha.

Aam log thake hue the. Aashrit. Apne aap ko kuch samajhne wale nahi reh gaye the.

Ek khaali jagah thi. Aur us khaali jagah ko kisi cheez ne bharna tha.

## Part 9: Do England, do Bharat

Nehru kehte hain, shayad ye khaali jagah is desh ke thake hue samaaj ki hi den thi.

Wo samaaj badalne se inkaar karta raha, jabki duniya badal rahi thi. Aur Nehru ek saaf niyam likhte hain: jo sabhyata badlaav se inkaar karti hai, wo khatam hoti hai.

Us samaaj ke paas dene ke liye kuch naya nahi bacha tha. Badlaav ka waqt aa chuka tha.

Aur usi waqt England kahan tha?

England raajnitik taur par bahut aage tha. Wahan raaj-kranti ho chuki thi. Parliament ne raja par apna adhikaar jamaya tha. Uska madhyam varg apni nayi taaqat se wakif tha, aur phailna chahta tha.

Uski urja dikhti bhi thi. Kahan? Un khojon aur aavishkaron mein, jo industrial kranti ka elaan ban gaye.

Aur America? 1776 mein azaadi ka elaan. Chhah saal baad wo koloniyaan England se alag ho gayin — aur phir wahan asli badlaav shuru hua.

Zameen ka poora intezaam badla. Purane vishesh adhikaar mit gaye. Badi jagirein chhin kar chhote-chhote hisson mein baant di gayin. Aur ek jagriti ka daur shuru hua. Azaad America bade-bade qadam uthane laga.

France mein? Wahan ki badi kranti ne Bastille tod diya, us purane intezaam ka nishaan. Raja, feudalism, sab baha le gayi. Aur usne duniya ke saamne insaan ke adhikaaron ka elaan kar diya.

Aur England? America aur France ki in khunkhaar tabdiliyon se darr kar England aur bhi reactionary ho gaya.

Uska kaanoon aur bhi khunkhaar hua.

Sochiye. 1760 mein England ke takht par George III aaye. Us waqt wahan lagbhag ek sau saath aise jurm the, jinme maut ki sazaa hoti thi. Aur jab unka lamba raaj khatam hua, to us list mein kareeb sau naye jurm jud chuke the.

Aur wahan ka aam sipahi? Ek jaanwar se bhi bura haal. Maut ki sazaa aam baat thi. Aur usse bhi aam thi korein — khuli janta ke saamne, sau-sau korein, jab tak maut na aa jaye.

Aur India? Nehru saaf likhte hain: insaaniyat mein, insaan ki izzat mein, is desh us waqt England se kaafi aage tha. Yahan padhne-likhne wale log England se zyada the. Shehron ki suvidhaayein bhi zyada thin. Europe ke aam logon ki haalat bahut peeche thi.

Aaj sochiye. Ye baat humein chaunkati hai, kyunki hum aage-peeche ki sidhi ek seedhi lakeer samajhte hain. Nehru us lakeer ko todte hain.

Par ek farq tha, aur wo sabse bada farq tha. Pashchim ke Europe mein kuch nayi dhaaraayein chupke se kaam kar rahi thin. Wo badlaav la rahi thin. Is desh mein haalat wahin ki wahin khadi thi.

## Part 10: Kaun sa England?

Ab England ka doosra chehra dekhiye. Aur ye lambe waqt ka khel hai.

1600 mein Rani Elizabeth ne is Company ko sanad di. Us waqt Shakespeare zinda the, aur likh rahe the.

Kuch hi saal baad Angrezi Bible ka wo naya sanskaran chhapa. Usi ke aas-paas Milton ka janm hua.

Phir aayi wahan ki raaj-kranti. Hampden. Cromwell. Raja ki gardan.

Phir 1660 mein Royal Society — London ki wo sabha jahan naye vigyan par baat hoti thi, aur jiska kaam aage chalkar poora vigyan badalne wala tha.

Aur phir ek sau saal baad, 1760 mein, bunai ki ek chhoti si machine bani. Uske turant baad suut katne ki machine. Phir bhaap ka engine. Phir kapda bunne ki badi machine.

Ek ke baad ek. Aise ki duniya ka chehra badal gaya.

To sawal ye hai. Is desh mein kaun sa England aaya?

Wo England jisne Shakespeare aur Milton diye? Jahan ki baat aur lekhan mein nobility thi? Jahan raaj-kranti hui, aur azaadi ke liye ladai hui? Jahan vigyan aur technique aage badhte rahe?

Ya wo England, jiska kaanoon jaanwaron jaisa tha? Jahan aam sipahi ki haalat ek jaanwar se bhi buri thi? Jahan purani feudalism aur reaction baithi hui thi?

Nehru likhte hain: sach ye hai ki dono England the. Jaise har desh mein hote hain — har desh ke kirdaar ke do chehre.

Wahi Edward Thompson likhte hain: England mein sabse unche star aur aam zindagi ke beech jo khaai hai, wo itni gehri hai. Aisa kisi bhi aise desh mein nahi milega, jisse hum apni tulna chahenge. Aur wo khaai itni dheere bharti hai ki lagta hai bharti hi nahi.

Aur ye dono England saath-saath rehte hain. Ek doosre ko asar dete hain. Alag nahi kiye ja sakte. Jahan ek England India aaya, wo doosre England ko poora bhool kar nahi aa sakta tha.

Par har bade kaam mein ek England aage hota hai, doosre par bhaari padta hai. Aur Nehru kehte hain: yahan ye tay tha ki galat England hi aage aa jayega. Aur is desh mein aate hue wo yahan ke galat Bharat se milta, aur use aur uksata.

## Part 11: Dono parche

[SFX: cold open wali raat ki hawa, halki, laut kar aati hai]

Ab Jaipur ki us raat par lautta hoon.

Do parche. Ek bahar se aayi tables. Ek apne yahan ki ginti. Aur us raat, apni ginti sahi nikli.

Par is episode ka sawal ye nahi tha.

Sawal ye tha: ek vyapaari company is desh ki maalik kaise ban gayi?

Jawab do hisson mein hai. Company jeeti kuch ittefaq se, apni tayyari se, aur sabse zyada hamare aapas ke bantwaare se. Uske paas behtar fauj thi, behtar jaankari thi, samundar par kabza tha, aur Plassey ke baad Bengal ka paisa.

Par asli jawab us se gehra hai. Do England mein se jo yahan aaya, wo galat England tha. Aur usne yahan ke galat Bharat ko jagaya.

Ab aakhir mein ek tasveer. America ki azaadi aur is desh ki azaadi — dono lagbhag saath-saath ki baatein hain. Ek aazad hua, aur doosra ghulam.

Nehru likhte hain, ek Hindustani us daur ko dekhta hai. America ne jo tarakki ki, wo dekh kar uski chhaati mein ek halki si tamanna jaagti hai. Aur wo apne desh ko dekhta hai.

Sach ye bhi hai ki America ke paas ek khaali maidan tha. Ek saaf kaagaz. Aur humare paas bahut purani yaadein, purani reetiyan, purana bojh.

Par Nehru ek baat jodte hain.

Ho sakta hai, agar England ne is desh ka wo "bada bojh" apne oopar na liya hota — jise unhone humein sawaraj ka mushkil hunar sikhane ke liye uthaya hua tha — to Bharat na sirf azaad hota, na sirf zyada khush-haal hota. Wo vigyan aur kala mein bhi bahut aage hota. Aur un sab cheezon mein bhi, jo zindagi ko jeene laayak banati hain.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, do England. Nehru kehte hain har desh ke do chehre hote hain — ek jo aage kheenchta hai, ek jo peeche. Aur jab do desh milte hain, to ye tay hota hai ki dono taraf ka kaun sa chehra aage aata hai. Aaj duniya mein hamare paas bhi dono chehre hain. Sawal ye nahi ki hum kya hain. Sawal ye hai ki hum kya jagah de rahe hain.

Doosri, wo khaai. Thompson ne likha tha ki England mein unche aur aam star ke beech ki khaai kabhi bharti nahi. Wo baat Nehru ne 1944 mein likhi. Aaj wahi sawal kisi bhi desh ke liye poochha ja sakta hai — humare apne liye bhi. Humare unche star aur humare aam star ke beech kitni doori hai?

Aur ek teesri baat, jo is poore Ep 4-6 set ka nichod hai. Is desh ke paas kaarigar the. Bank the. Karkhane thin. Gyaan tha. Aur ek daur aisa aaya, jab poochhna hi band ho gaya. Aapke liye ek sawal: aap apni zindagi mein sabse aakhri baar kab apni ginti kisi bahar wali ginti se mila kar dekhi thi?
