Calcutta. Saal 1757. Dopahar dhal rahi hai.

Nadi ke kinaare ek kothi hai. Do manzil. Upar ek kamra, aur kamre mein ek lambi mez.

Mez par kaagaz hai. Bahut kaagaz.

Company ka writer — yaani wo clerk, jo sab kuch likh kar rakhta hai.

Kya likhta hai? Jo saamne aata hai. Jahaaz ka maal. Boriyon ka wazan. Bazaar ka bhaav. Raajneeti ki khabar.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Is set mein humne dekha ki Islam pehle dharm bankar aaya, phir talwar bankar. Pichhli baar raja badalte rahe, par neeche aam zindagi waisi hi rahi. Aur pichhle episode ke aakhir mein ek ghadi ulti ghoom kar toot gayi. Aaj us khaali jagah mein ek nayi taaqat khadi hoti hai.

## Part 1: Saanjhi gali

Aaiye, ek gali mein. Uttar Bharat. Kisi kasbe ki gali — tang si, dono taraf deewaarein.

Shaam hai, aur ek tyohaar hai. Gali mein roshni hai. Kahin mithai ki khushbu, kahin tel ke diye.

Ek ghar ke aangan se dholak ki awaaz aati hai. Bagal wale ghar se wahi dhun, thodi badli hui.

Wahan ek Musalmaan parivaar rehta hai. Dono ghar ek doosre ke tyohaar mein jaate hain.

Ek hi boli. Ek hi tarah ke kapde. Ek hi tarah ka khana.

Aur ye sab ittefaq nahi tha.

Is desh mein Musalmaanon ki bahut badi tadaad wahi log the, jinhone dharm badla tha. Aur jo apna dharm badalta hai, wo apni virasat bhool nahi jaata.

Zubaan ne dono ko jod diya. Aur zubaan ke saath sangeet, chitrakari, imarat, khaana, libaas, aur tyohaar — sab aa gaye.

Hindu aur Musalmaan sadiyon tak ek saath rehte. Ek doosre ke melon mein jaate the. Ek hi bhasha bolte the. Aur unke saamne ek hi tarah ke sawal khade the — lagaan, fasal, bhookh.

Upar ke log? Amiron aur jaageerdaron ne darbar se apna dhang liya.

Unhone ek aisi saajhi sabhyata khadi ki, jisme sab kuch milta-julta tha. Kapde. Khaana. Shauq. Shikar. Bahaduri. Polo ka khel. Haathiyon ki ladai.

Sochiye. Do dharm, aur ek shauk.

Aur ek baat. Ye amir log zameen ke maalik nahi the. Unhe kisi ilaake ka lagaan vasool karne ka haq milta tha — aksar zindagi bhar ke liye. Bas.

Par is saari gali mein ek deewar khadi thi.

Jaati ne milne se roka. Aapas mein shaadiyan na ke barabar hui. Jo hui, wo aksar ek Hindu aurat ka Musalmaan dharm mein chala jaana tha.

Khaana-peena bhi us tarah nahi tha, jis tarah ek hi ghar ke log saath khaate hain.

Aur sabse oonchi deewar — parda.

Musalmaan amiron mein parda aur bhi sakht tha. Hindu aur Musalmaan mard to roz milte the. Par dono taraf ki auraton ko ye mauka hi nahi milta tha.

Nateeja? Oonche gharon ki auratein ek doosre se itni alag ho gayin ki wo do alag soch ke samooh ban gayin — ek doosre se lagbhag anjaan.

To ye tasveer upar ki thi. Saaf, rangeen — aur usme ek darar.

Neeche? Neeche gaon mein?

Wahan zindagi ka dhaancha hi saajha tha.

## Part 2: Do jaati, ek zindagi

Gaon. Aur gaon mein is desh ke zyadatar log.

Yahan Hindu aur Musalmaan ka rishta gehra tha — aur jaati beech mein nahi aati thi.

Hindu Musalmaan ko ek aur jaati maanta tha. Bas.

Sochiye, ye baat kitni badi hai. Yahan do dharm aamne-saamne nahi the. Ek giroh saamne tha — jo alag tha, par usi gaon ka tha.

Aur Musalmaan bhi to kaun the? Zyadatar wahi log, jinhone dharm badla tha. Unhe yahan ki kathayein yaad thi. Purane granth yaad the.

Dono ek hi tarah ka kaam karte the. Ek hi zindagi. Ek hi kapde. Ek hi boli.

Ek-doosre ke tyohaar mein jaate the. Kuch tyohaar to dono ke hi the. Lok-geet bhi saajhe the.

Zyadatar kisaan the, aur karigar.

Beech mein ek teesra giroh tha — vyapaari.

Ye giroh zyadatar Hindu tha. Desh ka poora aarthik dhaancha lagbhag iske haath mein tha. Par raaj ki taaqat uske paas nahi thi.

Aur isi giroh ka Musalmaanon se sabse kam mel tha.

Kyun? Kyunki bahar se aaye Musalmaan feudal soch ke the. Vyapaar unhe pasand nahi tha. Aur sood lene ki manaahi bhi beech mein thi.

Wo khud ko raaj karne wala giroh maante the — raja ke afsar, zameen ke pate lene wale, fauj ke afsar, aur darbaron se jude vidvaan.

To teen giroh: upar amir, beech mein vyapaari, neeche gaon.

Par in teenon ke beech ek cheez chalti rahi, aur wo thi zubaan.

Mughal daur mein bahut se Hindu log Farsi mein kitaabein likhte the — Farsi darbar ki zubaan thi. Inmein kuch kitaabein aisi bani, jo aaj bhi classic maani jaati hain.

Udhar, Musalmaan vidvan Sanskrit ki kitaabein Farsi mein anuvaad karte the, aur Hindi mein likhte the.

Do naam yaad rakhiye.

Malik Mohammad Jaisi — Hindi ke ek bade kavi, jo Akbar se kuch pehle hue.

Aur Rahim Khankhana. Akbar ke darbar ke sabse bade amiron mein se ek. Rahim Arabi, Farsi aur Sanskrit ke vidvan the, aur Hindi ke ek bahut acche kavi. Kuch samay ke liye wo Mughal fauj ke sarvocch senapati bhi the.

Ab sochiye.

Jis fauj ke wo senapati hain, wo Rana Pratap se lad rahi hai. Aur apne tambu mein wo usi Rana Pratap ki tareef likhte hain.

Uska desh-prem. Uski izzat. Uski bahaduri.

Nehru kehte hain, Akbar ki neeti isi chivalry aur dosti par khadi thi. Aur unke bahut se mantri ye unse seekhte the.

To upar ka mel asli tha. Par ek cheez is daur mein isse bhi zyada asli thi.

Poore gaon, poore giroh, apna dharm badal rahe the. Aur badalne ke baad bhi wahi kaam karte rehte the.

## Part 3: Neeche ka chehra

Ye badlaav apne aap nahi aaya.

Jab bahar se naye log aaye, to unhone woh zakhm khol diye, jo is samaj mein pehle se pade the.

Jaati ka patthar ho jaana. Chhua-chhoot. Apne aap ko doosron se alag rakhne ki wo zid, jo hadd se bhi aage chali gayi thi.

Aur uske saamne Islam ka ek vichaar khada tha — bhai-chaare ka. Sabke baraabar hone ka.

Kise kheenchta tha ye vichaar? Us aadmi ko, jise baraabari ka koi chehra dikha hi nahi tha.

Isi wajah se bahut se log apna dharm badal gaye. Aur inmein zyadatar neechi jaatiyon ke log the — khaas kar Bengal mein.

Kuch oonchi jaati ke log bhi badle. Kabhi sach mein yakeen badla tha. Aur aksar isliye ki raja ka dharm apnane mein faayda tha.

Par is desh ka bada dharm wahi raha. Muzboot. Apne par yakeen se bhara. Apna ghera band kiye hue.

Upar wale giroh ko apne vichaaron par koi shak nahi tha; wo Islam ko darshan ki ek kaali si shuruat maante the.

Aur badlaav ka tareeqa bhi alag tha. Yahan alag-alag log nahi badle. Yahan giroh badle.

Ek jaati. Ya ek ilaake ka poora gaon. Ek saath.

Nateeja ye nikla ki unki zindagi wahi rahi, unke kaam ka khandaan wahi raha, sirf pooja ka tarika badal gaya.

Isi wajah se aaj is desh mein kuch kaam lagbhag poore ke poore ek hi giroh ke haath mein hain. Bunne wale zyadatar Musalmaan hain. Bahut se ilaakon mein to poori tarah. Joote banane wale, kapde silne wale, aur kasai — wahi kahani.

Aur us waqt is badlaav ka virodh bhi nahi hua. Jab tak zabardasti na ho, kisi ne kuch nahi kaha.

Aaj sochiye. Wahi sawal aaj poochha jaaye, to kitna shor hota hai.

Kyun? Kyunki ab dharm ek giroh ki ginti ban gaya hai. Alag-alag mazhabi electorate aa gaye. Har naya banda kisi ek giroh ka vote hai. Aur kuch logon ne to jangana ke aankde hi badalne ki koshish ki.

Kashmir ka kissa suniye.

Wahan sadiyon tak dharm badalte rehne se lagbhag poore log Musalmaan ban gaye. Par unhone apne purane Hindu riwaaz chhode nahi.

Unneesvin sadi ke beech mein wahan ke Hindu raja ko pata chala ki bahut bade hisse ke log poore giroh ke roop mein wapas Hindu ho jaana chahte hain.

Raja ne Banaras ke panditon ke paas mandali bheji: kya ye mumkin hai?

Panditon ne mana kar diya. Aur baat wahin khatam ho gayi.

Ab ek aisi baat, jise Nehru dohrte hain.

Bahar se aaye Musalmaan is desh mein koi nayi technique nahi laaye. Koi naya aarthik dhaancha nahi. Koi naya banane ka tareeqa nahi.

Bhai-chaare mein yakeen ke bawajood, wo khud jaati mein bandhe hue the, aur feudal soch ke the. Utpaadan aur karkhane chalane ke maamle mein, is desh mein jo pehle se tha, us se kamzor the.

Isliye is desh ki arthvyavastha par unka asar bahut thoda raha. Zindagi waisi hi chalti rahi. Aur sab log — Hindu ho ya Musalmaan — usi zindagi mein dhal gaye.

Aage chalkar jab karkhane aur gaon ki industry tooti, to bahut se karigar wapas kheti par chale gaye.

Dharm badla. Kaam nahi badla. Boli nahi badli.

Par is poore daur mein ek jagah thi, jahan cheezein sach mein badlin — aur wo jagah thi ghar ke andar.

## Part 4: Aurat ki jagah

Is daur mein aurat ki jagah neeche gayi.

Sach ye hai ki purane kanoon bhi aurat ke saath nyaay nahi karte the — jaageer, virasat, ghar ke haq, sab mein. Phir bhi wo kanoon us waqt ke angrezi kanoon se behtar the.

Ye kanoon jukta parivaar se aaye the. Unka maksad tha: parivaar ki saajhi jaageer ko kisi doosre parivaar mein jaane se bachana.

Kyunki shaadi hoti thi, to aurat ka parivaar badal jaata tha.

Aurarthik roop se wo apne pita, pati ya bete par aashrit maani jaati thi.

Par ye aashrit hona sirf kanooni baat thi. Wo apne naam se sampatti rakh sakti thi — aur rakhti thi.

Aur wo izzat-paati thi. Samajik aur sanskritik zindagi mein hissa leti thi. Is desh ka itihaas aisi auraton ke naam se bhara pada hai — sochne wali, darshan karne wali, raaj karne wali, aur ladne wali.

Par ye azaadi dheere-dheere kam hoti gayi.

Islam mein virasat ka kanoon zyada nyaay wala tha. Par wo Hindu auraton par laagu nahi hota tha.

Jo unke saath hua — aur Musalmaan auraton ke saath isse bhi zyada — wo tha parde ka sakht hoti jaana.

Ye reet uttar ke oonche gharon mein phaili, aur Bengal mein. Dakshin aur pashchim bach gaye.

Uttar mein bhi sirf oonche ghar. Aam logon par ye bojh nahi tha.

Aur iske saath ek doosri cheez aayi. Padhai ke mauke band ho gaye. Aurat ki duniya ghar ki deewaron tak simat gayi.

Ab jis aurat ke paas apne aage badhne ka koi doosra raasta nahi tha, use batlaya gaya: tera sabse bada gun teri pavitrata hai, aur tera sabse bada paap us pavitrata ka jaana.

Aur ye niyam kisne banaya? Aadmi ne. Aur aadmi ne ise khud par laagu nahi kiya.

Isi daur mein, Jahangir ke waqt, Tulsidas ne apni wahi mashhoor Ramayan likhi, jise log aaj bhi padhte hain. Usme aurat ki jo tasveer khinchi gayi, Nehru kehte hain, wo bahut hi be-insaaf aur pakshpati hai.

Ek taraf aurat ke haath mein jaageer ka kagaz hai. Doosri taraf, do peedhi baad usi ghar ki aurat ka naam kisi kagaz par nahi milega.

Ye dono baatein sach hain. Aur dono ek hi daur ki hain.

To ye duniya apne gaon, apne geet aur apne tyohaaron ke saath wahin khadi thi. Neeche ki zindagi apni jagah, aur upar ek takht.

Par 1707 mein wo takht khaali ho gaya.

## Part 5: Chaar khiladi, ek khaali takht

1707. Aurangzeb mar jaate hain.

Dilli mein takht hai. Par takht par baithne wala koi nahi.

Us din ke baad jo sau saal aaye, wo is desh ke sabse uljhe sau saal the.

Mughal samrajya tezi se tootne laga. Badshah ke bade afsar, jo door-door ke ilaaqon par raaj karte the, dheere-dheere apne hi raja ban gaye.

Par ek ajeeb baat thi. Ye naye raja khud ko azaad maante the, phir bhi Dilli ke us badshah ko salaam bhejte the — chahe wo badshah kamzor ho, chahe wo kisi aur ka qaidi ho.

Mughal naam ka jaadu itna gehra tha.

Aur ye naye rajya khud kuch nahi the. Na unme taaqat thi, na maayne. Wo sirf itne kaam ke the ki kisi bade khiladi ki madad kar sakein, ya uski raah rokein.

Unme sabse bada dikhne wala tha Hyderabad ka Nizam.

Dakshin mein uski jagah aisi thi ki shuruaat mein sab usse darr ke dekhte the.

Par jaldi pata chal gaya ki ye taaqat sirf dikhava thi.

Nehru ek tasveer dete hain. Wo likhte hain ki wo rajya bhusa bhar kar khada kiya hua ek parda tha — jise bahar ki taaqatein seedha khadi rakhti hai.

Uska poora dhang hi aisa tha. Padosiyon mein ladai bhadkana. Unki pareshani se faayda uthana. Khud kabhi maidan mein na utarna.

Aur jab ek baar usne apni aazadi dikhane ki koshish ki? Marathas ne uski fauj ko buri tarah bhaga diya. Wo log to Nizam ko apna chhota sardar maante the — jo unhe khiraj deta hai.

Bachne ka ek hi raasta bacha. Wo Company ke pankh tale chala gaya. Us aashrit raaj ke sahare wo bacha raha.

Aur aage chalkar uska ilaaka bina kisi mehnat ke badha — kyunki angrezon ne Tipu Sultan ko haraya, aur uska nuksaan Hyderabad ka faayda ban gaya.

Ek angrez afsar ne 1784 mein usi Nizam par likha. Saar ye: uska ilaaka chhota, aamadni kam, fauji taaqat tuchh. Wo apni zindagi mein kabhi bahaduri ya koshish ke liye nahi jaana gaya. Ulta, uski ek hi reet thi — padosiyon mein aag lagana, unki kamzori se faayda uthana, khud kabhi ladai mein na padna, aur shanti ke liye koi bhi zillat kabool kar lena.

To Dilli ke takht ke aas-paas chaar khiladi khade the.

Do Hindustani, do bahar ke. Marathas. Aur dakshin mein Haider Ali, aur unke bete Tipu Sultan — dakshin ka wo sultan jisne Company ki fauj ko baar-baar haraya. Bahar se: angrez, aur French.

Sadi ki pehli aadhi mein lagta tha ki faisla ho chuka hai. Marathas hi poore desh ke raja banenge. Wo Mughal samrajya ki jagah lenge.

Ye khayaali baat nahi thi. 1737 mein Maratha savaar Dilli ke darwaazon par khade the. Aur unhe rokne wali koi taaqat nahi thi.

To kahani yahin khatam honi chahiye thi.

Par ek darwaza tha jo band nahi hota — uttar-pashchim. Aur 1739 mein wo darwaza khul gaya.

## Part 6: 1739, phir 1761, phir 1757

1739. Nadir Shah. Iran ka wo hamlavar, jisne lootne Dilli tak ka raasta liya.

Ye koi lambi ladai nahi thi. Ek aasan hamla. Dilli ke jo raja the, wo ladai bhool chuke the. Aaram-pasand ho gaye the.

Wo loot kar le gaya. Itna, ki uske saath wahi mashhoor takht bhi chala gaya, jise Mayur Singhasan kehte hain.

Aur ek ajeeb baat. Nadir Shah ka Marathas se koi takraav nahi hua.

Ulta. Uske jaane ke baad Marathas aur phaile. Wo Punjab tak pahunche. Ek baar phir poore desh par unka raaj dikhne laga.

Par Nadir Shah apne saath do tohfe chhod gaya.

Pehla. Dilli ke Mughal badshahon ka aakhri bharosa bhi chala gaya. Ab wo sirf parchhaiyan the. Naam ke badshah, jinhe jo taaqatwar ho, apne haath se nachaye.

Aur ye khokhlaapan purana tha. Purani reet ka jaadu itna gehra tha ki Company khud, aur baaki sab, Plassey se pehle tak us badshah ko nazrana bhejte rahe. Plassey ke baad bhi bahut saal Company khud ko usi ka vakil maanti rahi.

Doosra tohfa. Afghanistan is desh se alag ho gaya.

Afghanistan bahut purane zamane se is desh ka hissa tha. Ab wo Nadir Shah ke raaj mein chala gaya. Kuch waqt baad wahan bagaavat hui — aur Nadir Shah ko uske hi afsaron ne maar diya. Afghanistan ek azaad rajya ban gaya.

Marathas ko Nadir Shah se koi farq nahi pada. Unka asli imtihaan 1761 mein aaya. Panipat.

[SFX: door se aati hui ghodeon ki gadgadahat, phir dhool mein dabti hui cheekhein]

Saamne Ahmad Shah Durrani tha — wo Afghan jo us waqt Afghanistan par raaj karta tha.

Us din Marathas ki poori taaqat khatam ho gayi. Unke sabse achhe savaar. Unke sabse achhe sardar. Sab wahin reh gaye.

Kuch saal unka samrajya ka sapna phika pad gaya. Par wo uth khade.

Ab unka raaj ek sangh ki shakal mein khada hua. Poona mein Peshwa — sangh ka mukhiya. Aur bade rajya: Gwalior ka Scindhia, Indore ka Holkar. Ye sangh pashchim aur madhya Bharat ke bade hisse par raaj kar raha tha.

Par ek baat yaad rakhiye. Panipat ne unhe theek us waqt kamzor kiya, jab Company is desh ki ek badi taaqat ban kar ubhar rahi thi.

1757. Bengal. Plassey.

Clive — Company ka wo afsar, jisne ye ladai jeeti. Par kaise? Zyada ladai se nahi. Zyada kaam bagaavat ne kiya, aur jhoothe kagaz ne.

Plassey ko log British samrajya ka shuru kaha karte hain. Par uski shuruaat aisi thi, jiska swaad khatta reh gaya.

Jaldi hi Company ke paas poora Bengal aur Bihar tha.

Aur iska pehla nateeja? Ek bhayanak akal. 1770 mein, is bade, bhare hue ilaake mein, ek tihai se zyada log mar gaye.

Dakshin mein angrez aur French ki ladai khatam hui — angrezon ki jeet se. French Bharat se lagbhag mit gaye.

Ab teen khiladi bache. Marathas. Haider Ali. Aur British.

Aur us waqt is desh mein kisi ne angrezon ko aane wala maalik nahi samjha. Jo aankh dekh rahi thi, wo pehla sthaan Marathas ko deti thi.

Haider Ali aur Tipu — ye wo do log the, jinhone Company ki fauj ko buri tarah haraya. Ek waqt aisa bhi aaya, jab Company ki taaqat tootne ke kareeb pahunch gayi.

Par dono dakshin mein band the. Poore desh ka faisla wahin nahi ho raha tha.

Aur us waqt dakshin mein ek aadmi tha, jo ek aisi cheez dekh raha tha, jise is desh mein baaki koi nahi dekh raha tha.

## Part 7: Do hi taaqatein

Us aadmi ka naam Haider Ali tha. Aur jis cheez par wo dekh raha tha, wo thi samundar.

Angrez kyun itne bade ho rahe hain? Kyunki unka samundar par kabza hai.

Haider Ali ek anokha aadmi tha. Uske andar ek desh ka khayaal tha, aur ek neta ki nazar.

Wo ek dard ki bimari se poora jeevan jhujhta raha. Phir bhi uski lagan, aur kaam karne ki shakti, hairan karne wali thi.

Usne saath mil kar angrezon ko bhagane ki koshish ki. Usne Marathas, Nizam aur Oudh ko sandesh bheje.

Kuch nahi hua.

To usne apni samundri fauj khadi karna shuru kiya. Usne Maldives ke jazeere apne kabze mein liye, aur unhe apna jahaaz banane ka adda bana liya.

Uska beta Tipu wahi kaam aage badhata raha.

Haider Ali ki maut fauj ke saath chalte hue, raaste mein hui.

Hindustan ke uttar mein, usi waqt, Punjab mein ek Sikh rajya ug raha tha. Ranjit Singh ka. Ye rajya aage Kashmir aur Sarhad tak phaila.

Par us waqt ye kinare ka rajya tha. Asli ladai se alag.

Aur asli ladai kya thi?

18vi sadi jab apne ant ki taraf badh rahi thi, to desh mein sirf do taaqatein bachi thi. Marathas. Aur British.

Baaki sab inke aage-peechhe.

1799 mein Tipu Sultan hara diye jaate hain. Ab maidan saaf tha.

Sadi badalte hi ek kaabil angrez afsar apni diary mein ye likh leta hai: is desh mein do hi badi taaqatein bachi hain — British aur Maratha. Baaki har rajya kisi ek ke asar ko maanta hai. Aur jitna hum peeche hatenge, utni jagah wo lenge.

Par Maratha sardaron ke andar aapas ki khincha-tan thi. Wo ladte rahe, aur alag-alag hare.

Kuch bade daanv unke the. 1804 mein Agra ke paas unhone angrezon ko buri tarah haraya.

Par 1818 tak sab khatam tha. Madhya Bharat ke bade sardar Company ke aage jhuk gaye, aur uski sarvochchta maan li.

Ab angrez is desh ke ek bade hisse ke nirvivad maalik the. Seedha raaj karte, ya putli rajaon ke zariye.

Punjab aur kuch kinare ke ilaake abhi bahar the. Unhe aage ki ladaiyon ne poora kiya — Sikhon se, Gurkhas se, aur Burma mein.

To ab ek sawal. Kitni chhoti shuruaat thi, aur kitna bada anjaam. Ek vyapaari company, aur poora desh.

Pehla jawab, jo sabse aasan lagta hai: ittefaq.

## Part 8: Ittefaq, ya majboori?

Ittefaq. Haan.

Nehru is daur ko dekh kar khud ruk jaate hain. Wo likhte hain: lagta hai jaise angrezon ki jeet kuch ittefaqon aur khush-naseebi se hui ho.

Thodi si mehnat mein itna bada inaam. Ek samrajya. Itni daulat, ki wo duniya ki sabse badi taaqat ban gaye.

Sochiye. Thoda sa palat jaata, to unki ummeed bhi khatam ho jaati.

Wo baar-baar haare. Haider Ali ne haraya. Tipu ne haraya. Marathas ne haraya. Sikhon ne haraya. Gurkhas ne haraya.

Thodi si kam khush-naseebi hoti, to wo yahan se nikal jaate. Ya zyada se zyada kinare ki kuch jagahon par tike rehte.

Par kya sirf ittefaq? Nehru aage ek line jodte hain, aur wahi is episode ki reedh hai.

Khush-naseebi thi. Par khush-naseebi ka faayda uthane ke liye kuch chahiye hota hai.

Kya chahiye? Ek aisa desh, jo apne ghar ko sambhal na sake.

Us waqt ye desh ek bahaav mein tha. Mughal samrajya tootne ke baad sau saal se iske beech mein koi khadi deewar nahi bachi thi. Sadiyon se ye itna kamzor nahi hua tha.

Aur jab sanyam toot jaata hai, to maidan khula reh jaata hai.

Us maidan mein sirf angrez nahi aaye the. Bahut naye khiladi aaye the. Par us waqt jis ek giroh ke paas jeet ke liye zaroori sab gun the, wo angrez the.

Unka sabse bada nuksaan? Wo ajnabi the. Door se aaye the.

Aur wahi nuksaan unka faayda ban gaya. Kyunki yahan koi unhe seriously hi nahi leta tha. Koi unhe is desh ke raja banne ki soch wala nahi maanta tha.

Aur ye dhoka Plassey ke bahut baad tak chala.

Company khud Dilli ke us parchhai badshah ki vakil bankar rehti, aur usse unke bare mein galat soch aur gehra hoti.

Bengal se jo loot wo le gaye, usne bhi wahi soch banayi. Ki ye log paise ke peeche hain, raaj ke nahi.

Ki ye ek aane-jaane wali musibat hai — jaise pichhle daur ke hamlavar, jo aaye, loota, aur apne desh laut gaye.

Aur yahan is daur ki asli baat aati hai.

Angrez baaki hamlavaron se alag the.

Wo ek aisi lehar laaye, jo pashchim mein sadiyon se banti aa rahi thi. Renaissance se. Mazhab ke tootne se. England ki raaj-kranti se. Aur ab karkhane ka daur shuru hone se.

Aage is lehar ko America aur France ki krantiyon se aur zor milega.

Par sabse badi baat ye thi. Angrez yahan bahar wale hi rahe. Khud ko ghusaana nahi. Mil jaana nahi. Koi koshish hi nahi.

Is desh ke itihaas mein pehli baar raaj bahar se chal raha tha. Aur arthvyavastha ka dil bhi kisi door desh mein tha.

Yaani is desh ko pehli baar ek colony banaya ja raha tha — ek aisa raajya, jo bahar se chalta hai, aur bahar ke liye.

## Part 9: Kiraye ke sipahi

Ab ek baat samajhni zaroori hai. Ye Company asal mein ek daftar thi. Dhandhe ka daftar.

Usne dhandha karne ke liye yahan kadam rakha tha. Uske paas jo fauj thi, wo is dhandhe ki suraksha ke liye thi.

To ye Bengal tak pahunchi kaise?

Dheere-dheere. Aur aisa dheere, ki kisi ko pata bhi nahi chala.

Uska tareeqa ye tha. Yahan ke rajaon ki aapas ki ladai mein ek ka saath dena. Kisi ek hone wale se mil kar doosre ko dabana.

Faayda? Iske sipahi kisi bhi taraf ke liye sabse achhe sipahi the. Aur Company apni is madad ka mota daam leti thi.

Yun uski taaqat badhti gayi. Uska lashkar bada hota gaya.

Log inhe kiraye ke sipahi samajhte the. Jise zaroorat ho, bhada de kar le jaaye.

Aur jab samajh aaya ki ye log kisi ka khel nahi khel rahe, sirf apna raaj chahte hain — tab tak wo apni jagah jam chuke the.

Bahar walon ke khilaaf narazgi thi. Par wo rashtriya bhaavna nahi thi.

Us waqt wafadaari paas wale ka thanedar tak jaati thi. Jo sardar paisa ya loot ka mauka de, uske pichhe log chal padte the.

Aur logon ke paas chunne ka mauka bhi kya tha? Jahan regular tankhwah milti, ya loot ka mauka, wahan wo chale jaate the.

Ye kamzori sirf yahan ki nahi thi. Jahan bhi koi samaj bikhar jaata hai, wahan aisa hi hota hai.

Isi wajah se Company ki fauj mein zyadatar sipahi Hindustani the. Sepoy.

[SFX: chhote dhol ki dheemi thaap, door kisi deewar ke peeche se]

Sirf Marathas ke andar kuch aisi baat thi, jo ek sardar se aage jaati thi. Par wo bhi tang thi — apne daayre se bahar nahi.

Aur unke andar hi unki taaqat banti rah gayi.

Maratha sardaron mein aapas ki khincha-tan. Kabhi-kabhi apni hi ladai.

Rajputon ke saath unka bartav aisa tha ki wo unhe apna dushman bana baithe. Sahayak mil sakte the. Mili jagah, wo mili shikayatein.

Aur marne wale lamhe mein koi kisi ke kaam nahi aaya. Ek-ek kar ke sab hare.

To ab sirf ek cheez bachi. Sab ke paas talwar thi. Company ke paas kuch aur bhi tha.

Kagaz.

## Part 10: Kagaz jeeta, talwar haari

Agar Marathas — jinme kaam ki taaqat bhi thi — civil aur fauji intezaam mein peeche the, to baaki ka haal sochiye.

Wo log amateur the. Kabhi-kabhi sirf adventure karne wale.

Aur Company? Nehru ek saaf baat likhte hain. Company ke angrez yahan theek se professional the.

Ek angrez lekhak ne Company ke daftar ke bare mein likha: us daftar mein aise log kaam karte the, jaisi kataar British samrajya ne apni poori umar mein ek saath kisi aur jagah nahi dekhi.

Aur unka kaam kya tha?

Ek. Rishwat. Company ke jo afsar yahan ke darbaron mein baithe rehte the, unka ek bada kaam hi ye tha: mantriyon aur afsar ko kharidna.

Do. Jaankari. Unki jasoosi ka jaal itna pakka tha, ki fauj ki khabar unhe pehle pahunch jaati thi. Unke dushman andhere mein the. Unhe pata hi nahi hota tha ki Company kya kar rahi hai, aur kya karne wali hai.

Teesri. Bhitar ka aadmi. Darbaron aur faujon ke andar unka ek jaal kaam karta rehta tha. Musibat ke waqt log unki taraf chale aate the.

Nateeja? Wo aksar ladai se pehle hi ladai jeet jaate the.

Plassey mein aisa hi hua. Aur uske baad yahi kahani dohrai gayi — Sikhon ki ladaiyon tak.

Ek misaal. Scindhia ke paas kaam karne wala ek uncha afsar chupke angrezon se samjhauta kar leta hai. Ladai ke theek waqt par wo apni poori fauj lekar unke saath chala jaata hai.

Inaam? Uske hi maalik ki zameen se ek naya rajya kaat kar usse de diya jaata hai. Wo rajya aaj bhi hai.

Aur uska naam? Uska naam dhokhe ka paryay ban gaya — jaise pichhli sadi mein kisi aur aadmi ka naam ban gaya tha.

Ab poori tasveer dekhiye.

Angrezon ke paas behtar raajneetik aur fauji intezaam tha, jo ek doosre se juda hua tha. Unke sardar kaabil the.

Unke paas jaankari thi — dushmanon se kahin zyada. Unhone hamare aapas ke bantwaare ka poora faayda uthaya.

Samundar par unka kabza tha. Iska matlab: surakshit adde. Naye sainik. Naya maal. Aur hare hue bhi wo laut kar, phir se taiyaar hokar aa sakte the.

Aur paisa. Plassey ke baad Bengal ki daulat unke haath thi. Marathas se ladne ka paisa wahi tha. Har nayi jeet us paise mein izaafa karti thi.

Aur hamare paas? Ek badi haar ka matlab tha girna. Uthne ka zariya nahi.

[SFX: register band hone ki dheemi thap; tanpura ka wahi sur, laut kar aata hai]

Wapas us kamre mein chaliye. Calcutta, 1757. Wahi lambi mez. Wahi kaagaz.

Ab register band hota hai.

Par us ek panna ko dekhiye, jispar ek naam do baar likha tha.

Ek baar jumlah mein. Ek baar kisi list mein.

Ab aap jaante hain ki wo kya tha. Wo kharide hue aadmiyon ki list thi — un aadmiyon ki, jo ladai shuru hone se pehle hi Company ke ho chuke the.

Is episode ka sawal ye tha: ek vyapaari company itne bade desh ki maalik kaise ban gayi?

Jawab: ittefaq se bhi. Apni tayyari se bhi. Par sabse zyada hamare aapas ke bantwaare se.

Company ki sabse badi taaqat uski top nahi thi. Uska daftar tha. Kagaz tha.

Wo ek aisa daftar tha, jise ek din talwar bhi mil gayi.

Hamare paas talwar thi. Daftar nahi.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, kagaz.

Company ke paas us waqt sabse badi top nahi thi. Uske paas jaankari thi — kiska rishta kis se bigda hua hai, kiska afsar kharida ja sakta hai, kahan kitna paisa bacha hai. Aur wo saari jaankari ek jagah likhi jaati thi.

Aaj ki duniya mein bhi yahi sach hai. Bada dushman wahi nahi hota, jo sabse zyada zor lagata hai. Bada dushman wahi hota hai, jo aapke bare mein aapse zyada jaanta hai.

Doosri, bantwaara.

Us daur mein ek hi maidan par chaar khiladi the. Aur har haar alag-alag hui. Haider Ali akele lade. Tipu akele lade. Marathas ek-ek kar ke lade. Sikh akele lade. Gurkha akele lade.

Kisi ne kisi ko waqt par madad nahi di.

Aur ab aapke liye ek sawal. Aapke kaam mein, aapke ghar mein, aapke desh mein — faisla kaun jeetta hai? Jo zor lagata hai, ya jo poora hisaab rakhta hai?
