## Cold open

[SFX: Jaipur ki chhat par raat — halki hawa, neeche soye hue shehar ki door ki dheemi awaaz]

Jaipur. Raat.

Ek chhat par kuch aadmi baithe hain. Saamne ek mez hai. Mez par do purane kaagaz. Aur ek deepak.

Ek parche par wo aankde hain jo Portugal se aaye the. Doosre par wo, jo yahin, isi mitti par, banaye gaye the.

Dono parche ek hi cheez ke baare mein hain. Aasmaan. Grah. Taare. Unke chalne ka waqt.

Ab dono parche saath rakhe jaate hain. Ek line. Phir doosri.

Aur jahan-jahan farq nikalta hai, unki nazar pehle wale parche par jaati hai. Wo parcha peechhe reh jaata hai.

[SFX: kaagaz par ungli phirne ki halki sarsaraahat; deepak ki lau ka dheema hilaav]

Ek aadmi ungli rakh kar ek line padhta hai. Doosra wahi line apne parche par dhoondhta hai. Aur dono ke beech ka farq chup-chaap ginti mein darj ho jaata hai.

Portugal ki tables kam theek hain. Kai jagah galtiyan hain.

Aur jo aadmi ye dono parche mila raha hai, wo galti ka kaaran bhi bata deta hai. Wo kehta hai: jin yaantaron se wo tables bani thin, unka ghera chhota tha.

[SFX: tanpura ka ek lamba, dheema sur, "Jai Singh" par shuru]

Ye aaj ki baat nahi hai. Ye aathravi sadi ki baat hai.

Ek Hindustani raja, apni chhat par, ek videshi tak ki ginti ko apni ginti se mila raha hai. Aur uski apni ginti sahi nikal rahi hai.

Us raja ka naam Sawai Jai Singh hai — Jaipur ka raja, jise taaron ko ginti mein badalna aata tha.

Neeche shehar soya hua hai. Upar sirf taare hain — aur ek raja, jo unhe ginti mein badalne ki koshish kar raha hai.

Us raat, us chhat par, ek baat saaf dikh rahi thi. Is desh mein jigyasa mari nahi thi. Yahan log abhi bhi dekh rahe the. Tol rahe the. Milaa rahe the.

To phir baat kahan bigdi?

[SFX: tanpura ghulta hai; ident se pehle sannata]

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 6.6: Kaun sa England aaya?

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo is desh ki kahani likhne ke liye andar band hai. Pichhli baar hum ek Company ke daftar par ruke the — ek aisa daftar jiske paas talwar bhi thi, par asli hathyar kagaz tha. Us jeet ne do sawal chhode the. Ek: kis England ka raaj hua? Doosra, jo hamara hai: kya hum sach mein peeche the? Aaj dono ka jawab.

## Part 1: Talwarein bahadur, naqshe khaali

Marathas ke paas kaam ke log kam nahi the. Statesman bhi the, savaar bhi. Aur unki aam fauj ka dil saaf tha — chowki chhod kar bhaagna unhe aata hi nahi tha. Maut saamne khadi ho, to bhi wo apni jagah par khade rehte the.

Ye unki sabse badi taaqat thi. Aur theek isi ke peeche unki sabse badi kamzori chhupi hui thi.

[SFX: sooraj nikalne se pehle paavde ki dheemi gadgadahat, phir ek hi jhatke mein toot padna]

Sochiye ek subah. Sooraj nikalne se pehle ek Maratha paavda tayyar hai. Ghode mitti kured rahe hain, saans le rahe hain. Sooraj nikalta hai — aur poora paavda saamne toot padta hai. Talwar ka poora zor. Sabse aage.

Yahi unka poora hunar tha. Tez chalna. Zor lagana. Dushman ko hairan kar dena.

Us subah dushman ko pehle hi pata tha ki ye aayega. Kab. Kahaan se. Kitne logon ke saath.

Isliye wo subah sasti ho gayi.

Kyun? Kyunki Marathas padhne mein hoshiyaar the, par poochhne mein nahi. Duniya ki khabar unke paas nahi pahunchti thi. Apne hi desh ka naqsha unhe poora yaad nahi tha. Aur dushman kya soch raha hai — ye poochhne ki zehmat unhone kabhi nahi ki.

Poochhne wale kaam ki kami aisi hoti hai. Bade faisle — kahaan ladna, kab ladna, kisse haath milana — andhere mein liye jaate hain. Aur dushman theek wahi jagah dekh leta hai.

Ye log bure savaar nahi the. Ulta. Wo sabse tez the, aur kabhi-kabhi is tezi se dushman ke pairon tale zameen khisak jaati thi. Par unke liye jung ka matlab ek ke baad ek bahaduri ka hamla tha. Bas itna.

Aage chalkar unhone apni fauj ko naye tareeqe se sajaya. Savaar bhaari kapde pehnne lage, dhaanche ban gaye. Nateeja? Jo dhaanchal mein aaya, wo raftaar mein chala gaya. Aur raftaar hi unki asli taaqat thi.

Nehru likhte hain, wo khud ko hoshiyaar maante the — aur the bhi. Par unki soch ek purane, date hue dhanche mein qaid thi. Wo us dhanche se bahar nikal hi nahi paate the.

Aur ek cheez is dhanche ko aur gehri kar gayi.

Indian rajaon ne bahut pehle dekh liya tha ki bahar se aayi faujein zyada tarbiyat-yafta hoti hain. To unhone khud bahar ke afsar rakhe — French aur Angrez. Ye dono apni-apni desh ki ladai yahan bhi lad rahe the, aur isi khincha-tan ne Hindustani faujon ko kuch faayda diya.

Par jab French hare, to unke afsar chale gaye. Jo bache, wo zyadatar Angrez the.

Aur wahi log theek ladai ke waqt apne maalik ko chhod kar doosri taraf chale jaate the. Poori fauj ke saath. Kabhi khazane ke saath.

Sochiye: aap ek aise aadmi par bharosa kar rahe hain, jo aapka afsar hai aur dushman ka beta.

Company is khaali jagah ko jaanti thi. Pichhle episode mein aapne dekha tha ki uske kagaz jeete the. Uske paas har bade darbaar mein kuch log the — kuch kharide hue, kuch apni ichha se.

Aur Marathas ne samundar ki taraf dekha bhi — par bahut der se. Haider Ali aur Tipu ne jahaaz banane ki koshish ki, wo bhi der se. Is desh mein jahaaz achhe bante the. Par ek navy saal-do saal mein khadi nahi hoti.

To sawal ye hai: sirf Marathas peeche the? Ya poore desh ka haal wahi tha?

## Part 2: Pahaadi, feudaal, aur dhalti hui Dilli

Agar Marathas — jinka khoon ek tha, jinka dhang ek tha — civil aur fauji intezaam mein peeche the, to baaki ka haal sochiye.

Rajputs. Bahadur, bilkul. Par poora dhang purana feudaal tha. Romantik. Aur kaam mein poori tarah na-kaabil. Apas mein hi kabeela-kabeela ki ladaiyan.

Bahut Rajput sardar purani wafadaari se Dilli ke saath ho gaye. Wo talwar uthate the us takht ke liye jiske paas ab kuch nahi bacha tha — sirf ek purana naam. Kyunki wafadaari unki reet thi.

Par Dilli itni kamzor thi ki us talwar ka faayda hi nahi utha saki. Nateeja? Rajputs khud khelone ban gaye. Aakhir wo ek Maratha sardar, Scindhia, ke daayre mein chale gaye.

Us daur ke Musalmaan raja aur sardar? Utne hi feudaal, utne hi peeche. Unse koi badlaav nahi aaya. Sirf uljhan badhi. Aur aam logon ka dukh.

Aur Gurkhas? Nepal ke pahaadi sipahi. Sabse achhe — Company ke sipahiyon se bhi behtar. Poori tarah feudaal, par apni mitti se itna lagav ki apni raksha mein wo aise lade ki Angrezon ko darr lag gaya.

Unki bahaduri ne Angrezon ko hilaya. Par asli faisle par koi asar nahi kiya.

Aur Marathas? Jitni jagah jeeti, wahan tike nahi. Aaye. Gaye. Jaden nahi pakdi.

Sach ye bhi hai ki us waqt ladai ka pahiya itna tez ghoom raha tha ki koi tik hi nahi sakta tha. Company ke ilaake bhi unhi halat mein the — wahan bhi uski sarkar jam nahi payi thi.

To aage badhiye. Un raaston par, jahan se ye saari faujein guzarti thin.

## Part 3: Aatank ka daur

Fauj jab guzarti hai, to uske peeche sirf dhool nahi aati.

Sochiye ek sarak. Madhya Bharat ki. Ek kuya hai, uske aas-paas gaadiyan ruki hui hain — kuch vyapaari, kuch kisaan, aur ek aurat, jiske haath mein lota hai.

[SFX: kuye par paani ka chhota shor, phir door se aati hui ghode ki taal]

Subah fauj aati hai. Ghode. Sipahi. Koi kisi se kuch nahi poochta, aur koi kuch rokta nahi — kyunki rokne wala koi nahi hai.

Shaam tak fauj chali jaati hai. Aur uske peeche?

Usi sarak par raahzan aate hain. Wo unhi logon se maal lete hain, jo us subah fauj se bach gaye the.

Nehru likhte hain: us daur mein madhya Bharat, Rajputana, aur dakshin aur pashchim ke kuch hisse ujdi zameenein ban gaye. Wahin hinsa. Wahin dukh. Aur wahan jo log rehte the, unki khabar kisi ko nahi thi — sirf itni ki unka maal kaise le liya jaye.

Wo likhte hain ki is desh ke kuch hisse aise ho gaye jaise Europe mein Tees Saal ki Jung ke baad hue the.

Aur haalat sabse buri wahan thi jahan Company ka raaj tha, ya jahan Company ke aashrit raja baithe the.

Edward Thompson — ek angrez lekhak, jisne us daur ki haalat likhi. Unka kehna tha: Madras, aur Company ke aashrit rajya Oudh aur Hyderabad — ye aise manzar the jaise dukhon ka bukhaar. Aur uske saamne ek Maratha neta, Nana Farnavis, ke ilaake — shaant suraksha ka ek nakhlistan.

To sawal ye hai: agar us daur mein kabhi sukoon mila, to kahan mila? Do jagah. Ek Bengal mein — thodi der ke liye. Aur ek Indore mein — poore tees saal ke liye.

## Part 4: Ek rani, aur ek loota hua Bengal

Pehle Bengal. Plassey se thoda pehle.

Us waqt Bengal par Allawardi baithta tha — Dilli ka vakil, jo naam se Mughal tha, par apne ilaake ka poora maalik. Uske lambe raaj mein sukoon tha. Vyapaar phalta-phoolta tha. Ilaka daulat-mand ho raha tha.

Uske marne ke kuch hi der baad Plassey hua. 1757.

Company ne khud ko Dilli ke badshah ka vakil bataya. Vakil — naam ka. Asal mein wo poori tarah azaad thi, aur jo chahe kar sakti thi. Aur usne kiya.

Bengal ka loot shuru hua. Naam par Company ka. Haath mein uske afsar, uske gumashte, uske dalaal.

Ab rukiye, aur doosri taraf dekh lijiye. Theek unhi saalon mein, madhya Bharat mein, Indore ki ek rani baithti hai. Ahalya Bai.

Uska raaj tees saal chala — 1765 se 1795 tak. Log kehte hain us daur mein poora intezaam tha, sukoon tha, aur log phal-phoole. Wo itni sacchi thi ki marne ke baad log usse sant maan gaye.

Aur Bengal aur Bihar? Naye raaj mein wo tootte gaye. Loot ka intezaam. Bhagdar. Akal.

Sochiye ye tulna. Jahan Company ka raaj tha, wahan barbadi. Jahan ek rani thi, wahan aman.

Aur asli baat ye hai. Angrezon ke paas taaqat thi. Daulat thi. Par jawabdaari nahi thi.

Company ke vyapaariyon ko apna hissa chahiye tha. Khazana chahiye tha. Unhe in logon ko sudhaarne se, ya bachane se, matlab hi nahi tha. Aashrit rajya mein to taaqat aur jawabdaari ka naata hi toot chuka tha.

Jab Marathas ka kaam khatam hua, tab unhone civil raaj ki taraf dhyan diya. Tab dheere-dheere kuch intezaam aaya.

Aur humein baar-baar yaad dilaya jaata hai: Angrezon ne is desh ko bhagdar se bachaya. Aadha sach. Unke aane ke baad ek tarah ka order aaya — sach. Us daur ko Marathas "khatre ka waqt" kehte the.

Par wo bhagdar kam se kam aadha Company ki apni neeti se paida hua tha. Aur ho sakta hai ki ye shaanti kisi aur tareeqe se bhi aa jaati. Is desh ke paanch hazaar saal ke itihaas mein aisi shaanti aur bhi baar aayi thi.

To is poore daur mein kya koi aisa aadmi tha jo poochhna jaanta ho? Do the.

## Part 5: Do aadmi

Ithaas ki kitaabein humein ladaiyon aur sardaron se bharti hain. Par is desh ke mann mein kya chal raha tha — uski khabar usme nahi hai. Kabhi-kabhi ek jhalak mil jaati hai. Bas itni.

Aur us jhalak ka jo chehra hai, wo thaka hua hai. Log toot chuke the. Kismat ke aage chup. Dekh rahe the, samajh nahi rahe the. Poochhna unke andar kahin so gaya tha.

Kuch log zaroor jigyaasu the. Wo nayi taaqat ko samajhna chahte the. Par halat ka sailab unke oopar se guzar gaya. Wo kuch badal nahi paaye.

Doosri taraf? Angrez apne desh ki raaj-kranti se, aur America aur France ki tabdiliyon se, hile hue the. Napoleon ki ladaiyon ne jung ka poora hunar badal diya tha. Unme sabse kam padha-likha aadmi bhi, jahaaz par chadh kar, duniya ke kai hisse dekh aata tha. Aur England ke andar khoj ka ek naya daur shuru ho chuka tha.

Nehru likhte hain: sabse bada farq ye tha ki Angrez ek aage badhte hue samaaj se aaye the, aur hum ek dhanche mein band the.

Un jigyaasuon mein se ek Ranjit Singh tha.

Ranjit Singh — Punjab ka Maharaja, ek Jat Sikh, jisne apna rajya khada kiya aur aage Kashmir aur Sarhad tak phailaya. Usme kamzoriyan thi. Buree aadatein bhi. Phir bhi wo ek hairani-aavar insaan tha.

Ek Francisi musafir, Jacquemont — us daur mein Punjab aaya hua — likhta hai: ye bahut bahadur hai; 'maine pehli baar ek aisa jigyaasu Hindustani dekha hai'; aur inki jigyasa poori qaum ki susti ka hisaab kar deti hai.

Aur yahan ek baat samajhni zaroori hai. Hindustani log apne mann ki baat nahi rakhte the. Padhe-likhe log to aur bhi kam. Wo bahar se aaye fauji sardaron se door rehte the — unke kaam dekh kar unhe ghrina hoti thi. To bahar walon se jo Hindustani milte, wo aksar mauke-parast the. Ya darbaar ke wo chalak mantri, jo apne hi faayde ke liye chalte the.

Ranjit Singh isme alag tha.

Aur wo sirf jigyaasu nahi tha. Us daur mein, jab poora desh pathar ho gaya tha, uska dil bada tha. Usne rajya khada kiya. Badi fauj banayi. Par khoon bahana use pasand nahi tha.

Ek angrez likhta hai: itna bada rajya, itne chhote jurm par, kisi ne khada nahi kiya.

Usne har jurm ke liye maut ki sazaa khatam kar di — chahe jurm kitna hi bhayanak ho. Aur usi waqt England mein chhoti-chhoti chorion par bhi maut ki sazaa thi.

Ek aur angrez, jo usse milne gaya tha, likhta hai: asli ladai ke alawa unhone kabhi kisi ki jaan nahi li — jabki unki khud ki jaan par ek se zyada baar hamla hua. Aur unka raaj us zamane ke kai sabhya rajyon se zyada reham-dil tha.

Dusra aadmi ek alag tarah ka tha. Usne bhi poochhna nahi chhoda. Par uske sawal kuch aur the.

## Part 6: Jaipur wale raja ki ginti

Sawai Jai Singh. Jaipur ka. Rajputana.

Ye Ranjit Singh se pehle ka zamana hai — wo daur jab Aurangzeb ke jaane ke baad darbaar mein baar-baar hichkole aa rahe the.

Jai Singh in hichkolon mein bacha reh gaya. Kabhi Dilli ka aadhipatya maana. Jab Marathas ko rokna mushkil laga, to badshah ki taraf se unse samjhauta kar liya. Us waqt ke hisaab se chalak.

Par yahan uski siyasat ki baat nahi hai. Uski asli baat kuch aur hai.

Wo ek ganit-shastri tha. Khagol-vid. Vaigyanik. Shehar banane wala. Aur itihaas padhne wala.

[SFX: dhaatu ke yaantaron par ghoomti hui chhaya — ek dheemi, sargarm si ghoonjh]

Usne paanch badi vedhshalaayein banwayi. Jaipur. Dilli. Ujjain. Benares. Mathura.

Aur phir, ek din, Portugal se aaye padreson se usne suna ki Portugal mein khagol-vigyan kahan pahunch gaya hai.

To usne apne hi log bheje. Ek padre unke saath gaya. Gaya kahan? Portugal ke raja ke darbaar.

Wahan se raja ne apni tables bhejin — wo aankde jinke saath ek doot wapas aaya.

Ab wo tables Jaipur pahunchi. Aur Jai Singh ne apni tables se unhe milaya.

Nateeja? Portugal ki tables kam sahi thin. Kai galtiyan thin.

Aur galti ka kaaran? Jai Singh kehte hain: unke yaantaron ka ghera chhota tha.

Ruk kar sochiye. Ye "videshi cheez galat thi" wali kahani nahi hai. Ye is baat ki kahani hai ki yahan ginti ka kaam chal raha tha. Apna kaam. Aur wo kaam videshi kaam se takkar le raha tha.

Jai Singh ko Bharatiya ganit aata tha. Usne purane Yunani granth padhe the — Euclid jaise. Usne Europe ke naye kaam bhi jaane.

To usne wo kitaabein mangwayi, aur Sanskrit mein anuvad karwayi. Trigonometry ke granth. Aur logarithm — bade ankdon se guna-bhag karne ka naya tareeqa. Arabic kitaabein bhi usne khagol-vigyan ki anuvad karwayi.

Aur Jaipur shehar usne khud sajaya. Europe ke kai shehron ke naqshe ikattha kiye. Phir apna naqsha banaya. Wo purane naqshe aaj bhi Jaipur museum mein rakhe hain. Aur Jaipur aisa sajaya gaya ki aaj bhi use shehar-sajane ka namuna maana jaata hai.

Ab is aadmi ki teen baatein sun lijiye.

Pehli. Uski zindagi lambi nahi thi. Aur uske poore jeevan mein ladaiyan chalti rahin. Darbaar ki chaalbaazi chalti rahi. Wo khud bhi in sab mein ghusa raha.

Doosri. Jai Singh ki maut 1743 mein hui. Nadir Shah ka wo 1739 ka hamla unki maut se bas chaar saal pehle hua tha.

Sochiye, wo aakhri saal kaise honge. Ek taraf Dilli looti jaa rahi hai. Doosri taraf ek raja, apne parchaun par, ginti milaa raha hai.

Teesri, aur ye sabse badi baat hai. Jai Singh Rajputana ke us feudaal mahaul mein rehta tha, aur us daur mein jab is desh ke itihaas ka sabse andhera waqt tha. Wahan se ek vaigyanik nikla.

Ye ittefaq nahi. Ye is baat ka saboot hai ki is desh mein poochhne ki aadat mari nahi thi.

Aur wo akela sochne wala aadmi bhi nahi tha. Usne apne saath kaam karne wale bahut se vidvaan ikattha kiye. Unme se kuch ko usne Portugal gaye dooton ke saath bheja. Samaj ke niyam uske raaste mein nahi aaye.

Nehru likhte hain: ho sakta hai is desh mein vaigyanik kaam ke liye khub maal tha. Sochne wale log the. Haath wale log the. Kami sirf mauke ki thi.

Aur wo mauka bahut der tak nahi aaya. Aur jab is daur ke hichkole khatam hue, tab bhi satta walon ne is kaam ko koi protsaahan nahi diya.

To phir is desh ki asli taaqat kahan thi? Jai Singh jaise logon mein. Aur unke bank, unke bazaar, unki karkhane mein.

## Part 7: Hundi, jahaaz aur daulat

Ab Bharat ke bazaar mein chaliye.

Sochiye ek vyapaari. Banaras mein baithta hai. Use Kabul mein paisa dena hai. Par wo poora raasta rupaya lekar nahi chalta. Wo ek kagaz likhwa deta hai — hundi.

Aur wo kagaz Kabul mein maana jaata hai. Iran mein bhi. Herat mein bhi. Tashkent mein bhi.

Ye kagaz kisi sarkar ka nahi tha. Ye bharose par chalta tha. Ek shehar ke vyapaari ka naam doosre shehar mein kaafi tha.

Yahi is desh ki bank-vyavastha thi. Majboot. Poori tarah organized. Aur uske oopar dalaal, beech-wale, agent — ek poora jaal.

Ek angrez vidvaan, Anstey, likhta hai: 18vi sadi tak is desh ke banane aur vyapaar karne ke tareeqe duniya mein kisi bhi jagah se kam nahi the.

Jahaaz? Jahaaz banane ka kaam yahan phal-phool raha tha. Napoleon ki ladaiyon ke zamane mein ek Angrez admiral ke jahaaz — uske flagship — ek Hindustani firm ne banaya tha.

Nehru likhte hain: industrial, commercial aur financial — teenon mein Bharat utna hi aage tha, jitna industrial kranti se pehle koi desh ja sakta tha.

Par itna kuch kabhi nahi hota, agar is desh ne lambe-lambe shaant daur nahi dekhe hote, aur uske raaste vyapaar ke liye surakshit na hote. Ye baat yaad rakhiye — kyunki aage jo hua, wo theek isi cheez ko todta hai.

Baahar wale yahan kyun aaye the? Isliye ki ye maal sach mein achha tha. Europe mein uska bada bazaar tha. Shuru mein Company ka asli dhanda yahi tha: yahan ka maal Europe le jaana. Aur is vyapaar mein munafa mota tha.

Yahi nahi. Is desh ke kaarigar itne kabil the ki wo England ki nayi machine-wali technique se bhi takkar le sakte the. Jab machine-wala daur shuru hua, to bhi ye maal baha aa raha tha. Usko rokne ke liye bhaari mahsool lagane pade. Kuch jagah poori pabandi.

Aur Clive — Bengal ka wo afsar jisne Plassey jeeti. 1757 mein, Plassey ke saal hi, usne Murshidabad ko dekh kar likha: ye shehar London jitna bada hai, itne log, itni daulat. Wo ye bhi keh gaya ki wahan kuch log London walon se bhi zyada daulat-mand hain.

Dacca. Uske baareek malmal ke liye mashhoor. Aur ye do shehar to kinare par the. Andar, poore desh mein, isse bade shehar the. Bade bazaar. Bade karkhane.

Aur ek cheez sabse ajeeb hai — khabar ka jaal. Bhaav ka jaal. Bade vyapaari gharon ko ladaiyon ki khabar Company ke apne afsaron se pehle pahunch jaati thi.

To sawal ye hai: ye poori economy khud aage badh sakti thi? Nehru likhte hain, shayad haan — agar halat normal rehte. Usme aage badhne ka beej tha. Par badlaav ke liye us dhanche ke andar ek kranti chahiye thi. Koi catalytic agent. Aur wo mauka nahi mila.

Kyunki foreign political domination pehle aa gayi. Usne is economy ko tez raftaar se tod diya — aur uski jagah kuch positive nahi rakha. Company yahan rajneetik taaqat bhi thi, aur economic taaqat bhi.

Aur isi baat par ek angrez ne 1776 mein likha: vyapaariyon ki aisi band company ka raaj, kisi bhi desh ke liye, sabse bura raaj hota hai. Uska naam Adam Smith tha.

To phir sawal banta hai: dono taraf ka hisaab khola jaaye, to kaun kis se aage tha?

## Part 8: Us paar ka kaanoon

Pehle is taraf ka hisaab.

Is desh ke vyapaari aur karkhane wale daulat-wale the. Poore desh mein faile hue the. Desh ka economic dhancha unke haath mein tha.

Par raaj ki taaqat unke haath mein nahi thi.

Raaj despotik tha, aur bahut hadd tak feudaal — shayad pehle se bhi zyada feudaal. Isliye yahan wo madhyam varg khada hi nahi ho paaya, jo pashchim ke kuch deshon mein satta chhinne ki soch rakhta tha.

Aur aam log? Thake hue. Aashrit. Apne aap ko kuch samajhne wale nahi reh gaye the.

Ek khaali jagah thi. Aur us khaali jagah ko kisi cheez ne bharna tha. Kyunki ye samaaj badalne se inkaar kar raha tha, jabki duniya badal rahi thi. Nehru ek saaf baat likhte hain: jo sabhyata badlaav se inkaar karti hai, wo khatam hoti hai. Badlaav ka waqt aa chuka tha.

Aur usi waqt England kahan tha?

England rajneetik taur par bahut aage tha. Wahan raaj-kranti ho chuki thi. Parliament ne raja par apna adhikaar jamaya tha. Uska madhyam varg apni nayi taaqat se wakif tha — aur phailna chahta tha. Wo urja dikhti bhi thi. Kahan? Un khojon aur aavishkaron mein, jo industrial kranti ka elaan ban gaye.

Par usi England ke raaj karne wale varg ka chehra kya tha? Do America ke itihaaskar, Beard aur Beard, uski ek lambi list dete hain. Ek jaanwar-jaisa kaanoon. Mehnat karne walon ke liye tiraskaar. Fauj aur navy mein berahmi. Padhai sirf khaas logon ke liye.

Aur America? 1776 mein azaadi ka elaan. Chhah saal baad wo colonies England se alag ho gayin. Aur phir wahan asli badlaav shuru hua. Zameen ka poora intezaam badla. Badi jagirein tod kar chhote hisson mein baant di gayin. Ek jagriti ka daur shuru hua.

France mein? Wahan ki badi kranti ne Bastille tod diya — us purane intezaam ka nishaan. Raja, feudalism, sab baha le gayi. Aur usne duniya ke saamne insaan ke adhikaaron ka elaan kar diya.

Aur England? America aur France ki in khunkhaar tabdiliyon se darr kar England aur bhi reactionary ho gaya. Uska kaanoon aur khunkhaar hua.

Sochiye. 1760 mein England ke takht par George III aaye. Us waqt wahan lagbhag ek sau saath aise jurm the, jinme maut ki sazaa hoti thi. Jab unka lamba raaj khatam hua, to us list mein kareeb sau aur jurm jud chuke the.

Aur wahan ka aam sipahi? Ek jaanwar se bhi bura haal. Maut ki sazaa aam baat thi. Aur usse bhi aam thi korein — khuli janta ke saamne, sau-sau korein, jab tak maut na aa jaye.

[SFX: kore ki ek tez awaaz, phir sannata]

Aur India? Nehru saaf likhte hain: insaaniyat mein, insaan ki izzat mein, insaan ke group ki izzat mein, is desh us waqt England se kaafi aage tha. Yahan padhne-likhne wale log England se zyada the. Shehron ki suvidhaayein bhi zyada thin.

Par ek farq tha. Aur wo sabse bada farq tha. Pashchim ke Europe mein kuch nayi dhaaraayein chupke se kaam kar rahi thin. Wo badlaav la rahi thin. Is desh mein haalat wahin ki wahin khadi thi.

To sawal ye nahi ki kaun kis se aage tha. Sawal ye hai: kaun sa England yahan aaya?

## Part 9: Kaun sa England?

England ka ek chehra lambe waqt mein dekhiye. Ye tareekhon ka beat hai — kyunki yahan do kahaniyan ek saath chal rahi hain.

1600. Rani Elizabeth ne is Company ko sanad di. Us waqt Shakespeare zinda the, aur likh rahe the.

Usi dashak mein Angrezi Bible ka wo naya sanskaran chhapa. Aur usi ke aas-paas Milton ka janm hua — wo shayar jisne aage Angrezi kavita ko naya aakaar diya.

Phir aayi wahan ki raaj-kranti. Hampden. Cromwell. Raja ki gardan. Parliament ki jeet.

Phir 1660. Royal Society — London ki wo sabha, jahan naye vigyan par baat hoti thi, aur jisne aage chalkar poora vigyan badal diya.

Aur phir ek sau saal baad, 1760 mein, bunai ki ek chhoti si machine bani. Uske turant baad suut katne ki machine. Phir bhaap ka engine. Phir kapda bunne ki badi machine.

[SFX: dhaatu ki machine ki tez, ghoomti hui ghoonjh; phir sannata]

Ek ke baad ek. Aise ki duniya ka chehra badal gaya.

To sawal ye hai. Is desh mein kaun sa England aaya?

Wo England jisne Shakespeare aur Milton diye? Jahan ki baat aur lekhan mein nobility thi? Jahan raaj-kranti hui, aur azaadi ke liye ladai hui? Jahan vigyan aur technique aage badhte rahe?

Ya wo England, jiska kaanoon jaanwaron jaisa tha? Jahan aam sipahi ki haalat ek jaanwar se bhi buri thi? Jahan purani feudalism aur reaction baithi hui thi?

Nehru likhte hain: sach ye hai ki dono England the. Jaise har desh mein hote hain — har desh ke kirdaar ke do chehre.

Wahi Edward Thompson likhte hain — wo angrez lekhak jise hum us daur ke manzar mein mil chuke hain: England mein sabse unche star aur aam zindagi ke beech jo khaai hai, wo itni gehri hai ki aisi khaai kisi bhi aise desh mein nahi milegi, jisse hum apni tulna chahenge. Aur wo khaai itni dheere bharti hai ki lagta hai bharti hi nahi.

Aur ye dono England saath-saath rehte hain. Ek doosre ko asar dete hain. Inhe alag nahi kiya ja sakta. Jahan ek England India aaya, wo doosre England ko poori tarah bhool kar nahi aa sakta tha.

Par har bade kaam mein ek England aage hota hai, doosre par bhaari padta hai. Aur Nehru kehte hain: yahan ye tay tha ki galat England hi aage aayega. Aur yahan aate hue wo yahan ke galat Bharat se milta, aur use aur uksaata.

Aur ye is episode ka jawab hai.

Sawal ye tha: hum peeche the, ya humein peeche kiya gaya?

Jawab: dono. Hamari soch ek purane, band dhanche mein qaid thi. Par jeet ke baad hamara bazaar aur karkhana jaan-boojh kar toda gaya. Aur do England mein se jo yahan aaya, wo galat England tha — aur usne yahan ke galat Bharat ko jagaya.

## Part 10: Dono parche

[SFX: cold open wali raat ki hawa, halki, laut kar aati hai]

Ab ek pal ke liye Jaipur ki us chhat par lautte hain.

Do parche. Ek bahar se aayi tables. Ek apne yahan ki ginti. Aur us raat apni ginti sahi nikli thi.

Do cheezein wahin mili thi: kaam karne ka saaman, aur mauka. Jai Singh ke paas saaman tha. Company ke paas mauka tha.

Ab aakhir mein ek tasveer.

America jab azaad hua, theek usi daur mein is desh ki azaadi chali gayi. Ek aazad hua, aur doosra ghulam.

Aur ek Hindustani, jo us daur ko dekhta hai, America ki tarakki dekh kar apne desh ko dekhta hai. Uski chhaati mein ek halki si tamanna jaagti hai.

Sach ye bhi hai ki America ke paas ek khaali maidan tha. Ek saaf kaagaz. Aur humare paas bahut purani yaadein, purani reetiyan, purana bojh.

Par Nehru ek baat jodte hain.

Shayad, agar England ne is desh ka wo bada bojh apne oopar nahi uthaya hota — jise unhone humein sawaraj ka mushkil hunar sikhane ke liye uthaya hua tha — to Bharat na sirf azaad hota, na sirf zyada khush-haal hota. Wo vigyan aur kala mein bhi bahut aage hota. Aur un sab cheezon mein bhi, jo zindagi ko jeene laayak banati hain.

Do parche. Do aage ke raaste. Aur dono aaj bhi mez par rakhe hue hain.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, do England. Nehru kehte hain har desh ke do chehre hote hain — ek jo aage kheenchta hai, ek jo peeche. Aur jab do desh milte hain, to ye tay hota hai ki dono taraf ka kaun sa chehra aage aata hai. Aaj hamare paas bhi dono chehre hain. Sawal ye nahi ki hum kya hain. Sawal ye hai ki hum kis chehre ko jagah de rahe hain.

Doosri, wo "peeche rehna". Is poore set ka sabse ajeeb sabak ye hai. Is desh ke paas kaarigar the, hundi thi, karkhane the, ginti thi — aur ek daur aisa aaya jab poochhna hi band ho gaya. Tarakki ka pehla kadam ye nahi ki aap zyada jaano. Pehla kadam ye hai ki aap poochho: ye cheez bani kaise?

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapne aakhri baar kab apni ginti kisi bahar wali ginti se mila kar dekhi thi — aur us din kaun sa parcha sahi nikla tha?

## Outro

Jai Singh ke paas tables thi, par mauka nahi tha. Company ke paas mauka tha.

Chapter chhah yahin band hota hai. Sabhyata bahar ke hamle se kam, andar ki thakaan se zyada tooti hai.

Aur set ka sawal wahin aata hai: jis desh ke paas duniya ke behtareen kaarigar the, wo nayi duniya ki daud mein peeche kyun reh gaya? Jawab ye hai ki wo peeche tha hi nahi — usse peeche rakha gaya. Aur peeche rakhne ka pehla hathyar tha: poochhna band kar dena.

Agla chapter wahi kholta hai: Bengal ki daulat ab England ki karkhanein chala rahi hai. Sawal ye: jab ek desh ka paisa doosre desh ki charkhi chalane lage, to ye sauda kitna sasta hota hai?

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
