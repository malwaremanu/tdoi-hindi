## Cold open

Hyderabad. Nizam ka mahal. Unnisvi sadi ki shuruaat.

Nizam ke man mein ek shauq jaagta hai. Wo dekhna chahte hain ki Europe ki mashinein kaisi hoti hain. Angrez Resident unke liye do cheezein mangwata hai. Ek hawa nikalne wala pump. Aur ek chhapakhana, yaani printing press.

Nizam dekhte hain, khush hote hain, aur baat khatam. Dono mashinein baaki tohfon ke saath ek kamre mein rakh di jaati hain. Dhool khane ke liye.

Par Calcutta mein baithi Company sarkar ko khabar milti hai, aur wo naraaz hai. Pump se nahi. Chhapakhane se. Resident ko daant padti hai: ek Hindustani riyasat mein printing press kyun pahunchaya?

Resident ka jawab sunne layak hai. Wo kehta hai, agar hukum ho, to main use chupchaap tudwa doon.

Ek mashin jo kitaab chhaapti hai. Aur ek hukumat jo usse darti hai. Aaj ki kahani isi dar ki kahani hai.

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 7: Aakhri daur, pehla hissa. Angrezi raaj ki pakad, aur ek naye Bharat ki pehli awaazein.

## Part 1: Ek nayi jaat

Nehru chapter ki shuruaat ek seedhi baat se karte hain. Jab ek desh par baahar ki taaqat raaj karti hai, to jung kabhi poori tarah khatam nahi hoti. Goli na chale, tab bhi ek khinchaav hamesha bana rehta hai.

Purane zamane mein jeetne wala apni jeet ka bahana nahi dhoondhta tha. Jeet li, bas. Par naye zamane mein har kabze ko achha dikhana padta hai. Aur yahin se itihaas ko tod-marod ki shuruaat hoti hai. Kabhi jaan-boojhkar, kabhi anjaane mein.

Bharat itna bada aur itna ulja hua desh hai ki yahan har baat ke saboot mil jaate hain. Jo dhoondhoge, wahi milega. Nehru kehte hain ki Angrezon ne yahi kiya. Pehle ek raay banayi, phir uske liye tathya chun liye.

Nateeja ye hua ki jinhe Angrez khalnayak kehte, wo Bharat walon ke liye nayak the. Aur jinhe Angrezon ne khitaab aur inaam diye, unhe aam log gaddar maante the. Ab socho, ek Hindustani bachcha school mein wahi itihaas padh raha hai jo uske apne buzurgon ko bura kehta hai. Nehru is gusse ko American kranti se samjhate hain, jiska itihaas Angrez aur American aaj bhi alag-alag likhte hain.

Gokhale ne ek baar halke vyang mein likha tha ki ye Angrezi rishta to Bhagwan ki koi gehri marzi hogi. Marzi jo bhi rahi ho, do qaumein paas aayin, par mili nahi. Aur jo Angrez yahan aaye, wo krantikari nahi the. Wo England ke sabse purane-khayal tabqe se aate the.

Ab ek misaal. Bombay mein cricket ka ek bada mukabla hota tha, chaar teamon ke beech. Hindu, Muslim, Parsi aur European. Teen teamein apne dharm ke naam se khelti thi. Aur European team ka naam tha "Bombay Presidency". Yaani Bombay ki asli numaindgi gore karte the. Baaki sab mehmaan the.

Nehru ko bachpan ke Angrezi akhbaar bhi yaad hain. Unme sarkari tabadle hote, polo aur ghudsawari ki khabrein, naach ki partiyan. Hindustani logon ka zikr lagbhag nahi. Padhkar lagta hi nahi tha ki is desh mein Hindustani rehte bhi hain.

Railway ke dibbe, station ke waiting room, park ki benchein. Un par likha hota tha: sirf Europeans ke liye. Koi bhi gora, chahe German ho ya Polish, apne aap raaj karne wali nasl ka hissa tha.

Aur yahan Nehru apna sabse tez vaar karte hain. Jaatiyon ke is desh mein Angrezon ne, khaaskar Indian Civil Service ne, apni ek nayi jaat bana li. Sabse oonchi, sabse band. Hindustani afsar wardi to pehen lete, par us jaat mein kabhi shaamil nahi hote. Is jaat ko apni zaroorat par dharm jaisa yakeen tha. Aur jab yakeen ke saath kamai bhi judi ho, to sawal poochne wale par gussa aana tay hai.

## Part 2: Bengal ki loot, bunkaron ki barbaadi

Angrezi raaj ka pehla poora anubhav Bengal ko hua. 1757 ke baad. Aur wo anubhav shuru hua seedhi loot se.

Lagaan aisa vasoola gaya ki zinda kisaan to kya, mare hue kisaanon se bhi paisa nikala gaya. Nehru do Angrez itihaaskaron, Edward Thompson aur G.T. Garrett, ka hawala dete hain. Unhone is daur ki sone ki bhookh ki tulna Spain ke un vijetaon se ki hai jo America ko lootne gaye the. Aur is aarthik bebaaki ka bada zimmedar Clive ko maana.

Nateeja aaya 1770 mein. Bengal aur Bihar mein akaal. Abaadi ka ek-tihaai se zyada hissa khatam ho gaya.

Aur ye paisa gaya kahan? Nehru ek American lekhak Brooks Adams ka hawala dete hain, jinka kehna tha ki Bengal ki daulat ne England ki audyogik kranti ko pehla dhakka diya. Nehru yahan kadwa tanz karte hain: Bengal ko garv hona chahiye ki usne England mein mashino ka yug laane mein itni madad ki.

Ab doosri taraf dekhiye. Bharat sadiyon se kapda banata tha, duniya bhar ko bhejta tha. Naye mashini kapde se takkar to honi hi thi. Par Angrezon ne is takkar ko aur tez kiya. Bharat mein mashin mangwana mana tha. Yahan ka kaarigar naya hunar seekh hi nahi sakta tha. Jo khaali jagah bani, use England ke maal ne bhar diya.

1834 mein Governor-General Lord Bentinck ne khud ek report likhi. Usme Bharat ke bunkaron ki haalat ka zikr hai, aur wo tasveer itni darawni hai ki Nehru use poora uddhrit karte hain. Saar itna hai ki vyapar ke itihaas mein aisi museebat ki misaal milna mushkil hai.

Karodon kaarigar. Purana kaam chhin gaya, naya kaam milne nahi diya gaya. To wo kahan jaate? Kuch mar gaye, dasiyon laakhon mein. Baaki zameen ki taraf bhage. Par zameen pehle se bhari thi.

Aur yahan ek ulti ganga behi. Duniya ke har aage badhte desh mein log gaon se shehar ja rahe the, kheti se kaarkhane ki taraf. Bharat mein ulta hua. Unnisvi sadi ke beech mein lagbhag pachpan pratishat log kheti par tike the. Jung se pehle ye ginti chauhattar pratishat ho gayi.

Nehru kehte hain, Bharat ki gareebi ka asli raaz yahi hai. Aur ye raaz purana nahi, naya hai. Beemari aur anpadhta is gareebi ke kaaran nahi, nateeje hain. Abaadi ki baat bhi wo seedhe kaat dete hain: Britain ki abaadi ka ghanatva Bharat se dugna hai.

Khet chhote hote gaye, tukdon mein bant-te gaye. Karz badha. Zameen sahukaar ke paas jaane lagi. Bina zameen ke mazdooron ki ginti laakhon mein badhi.

Ek aur baat Nehru ko chubhti hai. England ne mashini yug mein jaane ki keemat chukayi, par kam. Kyunki baaki keemat Bharat, China aur doosre ghulam deshon ne chukayi. Akaal se, maut se, berozgari se.

Aur aaj bhi, jab Bharat bade udyog ki yojana banata hai, Angrez dost salaah dete hain ki kheti ko mat bhoolna. Nehru jhunjhla kar kehte hain, Bharat ka kisaan hi to Bharat hai. Use kaun bhoolega? Par kheti ka sankat udyog ke sankat se paida hua hai. Dono ko alag karke nahi suljhaya ja sakta.

## Part 3: Pehli baar, kisi aur ka pucchalla

Bharat par pehle bhi hamle hue the. Par jo aaye, wo yahin bas gaye. Yahin ki zindagi ka hissa ban gaye. Jaise England mein Normans, ya China mein Manchu.

Angrez alag the. Unka kendra Bharat ke bahar tha. Faisle London mein hote the. Raaj karne wale hamesha paraaye rahe. Nehru ek lekhak ke shabdon ke sahare kehte hain ki Bharat ne pehli baar apni azadi kho di.

Gaon, jo hazaaron saal se Bharat ki arthvyavastha ki neev tha, toot gaya. 1830 mein ek Angrez afsar, Sir Charles Metcalfe, ne in gaon ko chhote-chhote ganrajya jaisa bataya tha, jo har toofan ke baad bhi tike rehte hain. Nehru unki ye mashhoor baat yaad karte hain. Par ab wo gaon na kamaai ka kendra rahe, na prashasan ka.

Angrezon ne apni pasand ke zameendaar banaye. Laakhon kisaanon se nipatne ke bajaye kuch ameeron se nipatna aasaan tha. 1829 mein Lord Bentinck ne saaf likha ki Permanent Settlement aur jagah bhale fail hua ho, ek fayda zaroor hua. Ameer zameendaaron ka ek tabqa ban gaya jo Angrezi raaj ke tikne mein apna fayda dekhta hai.

Har zile mein ek Collector. Lagaan bhi wahi, police bhi wahi, adaalat bhi wahi. Apne zile ka chhota badshah.

Phir fauj. 1858 ki ek sarkari report mein khule taur par likha gaya ki Hindustani sipahiyon ko ek-doosre ke khilaaf santulit rakha jaye. Fauj ka asli kaam tha desh par kabza banaye rakhna.

Aur bill? Sab Bharat ne bhara. Apni hi jeet ka kharcha. Company se Crown ko hone wale tabadle ka kharcha. Burma aur Africa ki muhimon ka kharcha. England se Bharat tak telegraph line ka poora kharcha. Bhoomadhya Sagar wale Angrezi jahaazi bede ka kuch hissa. Aur hadd ye ki London mein Turkey ke Sultan ke swagat ki daawat bhi Bharat ke khaate mein gayi.

Railway bani. Zaroori thi. Par har lagaaye paise par paanch pratishat byaaj ki guarantee thi, isliye kisi ko kharch dekhne ki fikr nahi thi. Saara saamaan England se aaya.

Taleem ke baare mein ek Angrez lekhak, Kaye, ne Metcalfe ki jeevani mein likha ki shuru mein sarkar ko gyaan ke failne se dar lagta tha. Chhapakhana aur Bible, dono se. Yaad kijiye Nizam wala kissa.

Nehru is hisse ko imaandari se khatam karte hain. Ye itihaas Bharat walon ko gussa dilata hai. Par isme Angrezon ki taaqat bhi dikhti hai, khaaskar humari phoot ka fayda uthane ki. Jo qaum kamzor ho aur waqt se peechhe reh jaye, wo museebat ko khud bulati hai.

## Part 4: Chhe sau ek riyasatein

Ab ek ajeeb cheez. Bharat ki riyasatein. Ginti: chhe sau ek.

Kuch France jitni badi. Kuch ek aam kisaan ke khet jitni. Kareeb pandrah badi riyasatein: Hyderabad, Kashmir, Mysore, Travancore, Baroda, Gwalior, Jaipur, Patiala aur kuch aur. Baaki saikdon itni chhoti ki naqshe par sui ki nok barabar. Zyadatar Kathiawar aur Punjab mein.

Mysore udyog mein sabse aage tha. Mysore, Travancore aur Cochin padhai mein Angrezi Bharat se bhi aage. Par zyadatar riyasatein peechhe aur samanti thi. Sab mein ek aadmi ka raaj. Hyderabad mein nagrik azadi lagbhag thi hi nahi.

Ye riyasatein aayin kahan se? Nehru ka jawab: zyadatar Angrezi raaj ki shuruaat ke saath hi bani. Kuch Mughal subedaariyan thi. Kuch Maratha sardaar the jo haar kar Angrezon ke adheen ho gaye. Sirf kuch Rajput riyasatein Mughalon se pehle ki hain. Travancore ka itihaas hazaar saal purana hai. Udaipur ke Maharana apni vanshavali Suryavansh se jodte hain.

Hyderabad pehle chhota tha. Tipu Sultan ki haar aur Maratha yudhon ke baad Angrezon ne use bada kiya, is shart par ki Nizam unke neeche rahega. Dilchasp baat: Tipu ki zameen pehle Peshwa ko di gayi thi. Peshwa ne aisi shart par lene se mana kar diya.

Kashmir ko Company ne Sikh yudhon ke baad bech diya, aaj ke raja ke par-dada ko. Mysore ki aaj ki riyasat bhi Tipu ke baad Angrezon ne banayi.

In riyasaton mein asli taaqat Angrez Resident ki thi. Kuch Resident imaandar the. Zyadatar nahi. Taaqat poori, zimmedari zero. Angrez sahsik riyasat ka khazana khaali karte rahe. Oudh aur Hyderabad ke qisse lagbhag yakeen se bahar hain.

Phir 1857 aaya. Raja-maharajaon ne bagawat se doori rakhi, kuch ne to Angrezon ki madad ki. Angrezon ne sabak seekha. Riyasaton ko mitane ki neeti chhod di. Ab inhe bachaya jayega, mazboot kiya jayega.

1935 ke qanoon mein pehli baar riyasaton ko seedhe Viceroy ke neeche rakha gaya, "Crown representative" ke roop mein. Nehru kehte hain ki Crown se khaas rishte ki baat nayi hai. Asli treaty to sirf chaalees riyasaton ke saath thi.

Kuch raja achhe the, kuch bure. Par achhe bhi har mod par roke jaate. Nehru ek lekhak, Shelvankar, se sehmat hain, jinhone riyasaton ko Bharat mein Britain ka "fifth column" kaha.

## Part 5: Chhapakhana, Ram Mohan aur nayi padhai

Ab cold open wale chhapakhane par lautte hain. Sarkar private press se darti thi, par khud ka kaam bina chhapai ke nahi chalta tha. To Calcutta aur Madras mein sarkari press khule.

Pehla private press Serampore mein Baptist missionariyon ne lagaya. Pehla akhbaar 1780 mein Calcutta mein ek Angrez ne nikala.

Pashchim ka asar sabse zyada vichaar par nahi, roz ki zindagi par pada. Rail, press, mashinein, ladne ke behtar tareeqe. Aur sabse bada badlaav: zameen bikne wali cheez ban gayi. Jo cheez sadiyon riwaaj se bandhi thi, use paise ne khol diya.

Isi daur mein ek aadmi khada hota hai. Raja Ram Mohan Roy. Sanskrit, Arabi, Farsi ke vidwan. Islam ka asar, phir thoda Isaai dharm ka. Par apne dharm ki jad nahi chhodi. Use saaf karna chaha.

Sati ke khilaaf unki muhim ki wajah se Angrez sarkar ne us par rok lagayi. Nehru yahan saaf karte hain ki sati kabhi aam riwaaj nahi tha. Purane Sanskrit granthon mein iski ninda hai. Akbar ne ise rokne ki koshish ki thi, Marathe bhi iske khilaaf the.

Ram Mohan Bharatiya press ke sansthapakon mein bhi the. Shuru ke Angrez akhbaar sarkar ki khoob aalochna karte the, aur censorship lagi. Azad press ke pehle himayati kuch Angrez hi the. Unme se ek, James Silk Buckingham, ko desh se nikaal diya gaya.

1818 mein pehla Hindustani akhbaar nikla. Usi saal Serampore se Bangla ki pehli patrika. Aur usi saal ek aur cheez aayi: Regulation III. Bina mukadme ke qaid ka qanoon. Nehru likhte hain ki ye 126 saal purana qanoon ab bhi chal raha hai, aur log aaj bhi iske tahat jail mein hain.

Ram Mohan ne Farsi mein ek saaptahik nikala, taaki poore desh ke padhe-likhe padh sakein. 1823 ke press qanoon ke baad wo band ho gaya. Ram Mohan ne England ke Raja tak arzi bheji. Baad mein wo Delhi ke Badshah ki taraf se England gaye, aur Bristol mein unki maut hui.

Isaai missionariyon ki ek den Nehru khule dil se maante hain. Bible ka har bhasha mein anuvaad karne ki dhun mein unhone chhoti-chhoti bhashaon ke vyakaran aur shabdkosh banaye. Pahadi aur jangli kabeelon ki boliyon ko likhit roop diya.

1830 mein Calcutta ke Hindu College ke kuch chhaatron ne maang rakhi: Company ki taaqat par lagaam, aur sabke liye muft, zaroori padhai. Company ka dar sahi nikla.

Aur purani padhai ka kya hua? Bengal mein hazaaron school maafi ki zameen par chalte the, yaani lagaan-mukt zameen. Company ko jaldi paisa chahiye tha. To pura kagaz maanga gaya. Purane sanad kho chuke the, ya deemak kha gayi thi. Zameen chheen li gayi. School band. Teacher berozgaar.

Isse sabse zyada maar Musalmanon par padi, kyunki wahi in zameenon ke sabse bade haqdar the. Hindu madhyam varg vyapar aur peshon mein tha. Usne Angrezi padhai jaldi apnayi aur sarkari naukri mein aage nikal gaya. Ye fark aage chalkar bahut bhaari pada.

Bengal mein Angrezi padha naya varg England ki taraf umeed se dekhta tha. Nehru Tagore ke aakhri janmdin ke sandesh ka zikr karte hain, May 1941 ka, jisme Tagore ne us purane bharose ko aur uske tootne ko yaad kiya.

## Part 6: 1857, aur uske baad ka tareeqa

Mai 1857. Meerut. Sipahiyon ne bagawat kar di.

Plan chupke se aur achhe se bana tha. Par waqt se pehle phoot pada. Aur jaldi hi ye sirf fauji bagawat nahi rahi. Delhi, aaj ke UP, Madhya Bharat aur Bihar ke hisson mein ye aam logon ki ladai ban gayi. Hindu aur Musalman, dono kandhe se kandha milakar lade. Sabki nazar Delhi ke mahal mein baithe boodhe, kamzor Mughal badshah par thi.

Nehru isse azadi ki pehli jung ki tarah dekhte hain, par aankh moond kar nahi. Ye asal mein samanti sardaaron ki bagawat thi. Logon ki hamdardi unke saath thi, par na unke paas sangathan tha, na koi aage ka sapna. Bahut se sardaar door khade dekhte rahe ki kaun jeetta hai. Raja-maharaja zyadatar Angrezon ke saath rahe. Gorkhe aur, hairat ki baat, Sikh bhi, jo kuch hi saal pehle Angrezon se haare the.

Nehru ka nateeja: tab tak rashtra ki bhavna bani hi nahi thi. Azadi mare hue samanti dhaanche ke liye ladkar nahi aani thi.

Phir bhi is bagawat ne kuch shandaar log diye. Tantia Topi ne mahino Angrezon ko chhakaya. Aakhir mein Narmada paar kar Maratha ilaqe mein gaye, apno se madad ki umeed mein. Madad nahi mili. Dhokha mila.

Aur ek naam jo aaj bhi har zubaan par hai. Jhansi ki Rani Lakshmi Bai. Nehru ke mutabik bees saal ki umar. Ladte hue shaheed hui. Jis Angrez general ne unka saamna kiya, usne khud unhe baagiyon mein sabse bahadur maana.

Ab dabane ka hissa. Nehru kehte hain ki iska sach kam hi chhapa hai. Savarkar ne is jung par kitaab likhi, wo turant ban ho gayi. Kuch imaandar Angrez itihaaskaron ne parda uthaya, aur jo dikha wo dil dehla deta hai. Delhi mein ek hafte ki loot ki ijazat di gayi, jo asal mein ek mahina chali. Saath mein qatl-e-aam. Log Timur aur Nadir Shah ko yaad karne lage.

Nehru ke apne shehar Allahabad mein General Neill ne apni "adaalatein" lagayi. Nehru ek Angrez lekhak ka byora dete hain. Kanpur, Lucknow, har jagah yahi hua.

Ye sab yaad karna Nehru ko achha nahi lagta. Par wo kehte hain ki wo soch 1857 ke saath khatam nahi hui. Jab bhi sankat aata hai, wo laut aati hai. Duniya Jallianwala Bagh ko jaanti hai. Par uske alawa bhi bahut kuch hua hai. Unka ek vaakya yaad rakhne layak hai, apne shabdon mein: samrajya bura hai, nasalwaad bhi bura hai, par dono mil jaayein to sabka patan hota hai.

1883 mein Ilbert Bill aaya. Ek chhoti si baat: kya Hindustani jaj kisi gore par mukadma chala sakta hai? Gore bhadak uthe. Nehru ek purane bade Angrez afsar, Seton Kerr, ka bayaan quote karte hain, jo bataata hai ki Angrez apne aap ko kya samajhte the.

1857 ke baad sab badla. Company gayi, Crown aaya. Fauj nayi tarah se bani. Aur raaj ke teen pakke tareeqe tay hue. Ek: aise log aur tabqe banao jinka fayda Angrezi raaj se juda ho. Do: ek gut ko doosre ke khilaaf taraazu mein rakho. Teen: har darar ko chauda karo.

## Part 7: Seth, Chetty aur Marwari

Dheere-dheere Bharat 1857 se ubhra. Ek nayi chetna jaagi. Ek taraf nazar Pashchim par thi, doosri taraf apne ateet par. 1885 mein Indian National Congress bani.

Ooper ka ek patla tabqa paisa jod raha tha. Wahi udyog mein utra. Aur Nehru ek dilchasp baat pakadte hain: udyog mein wahi log aaye jinke khoon mein sadiyon se vyapar tha.

Ahmedabad, kapde ka naya kendra, Mughal daur mein bhi mashhoor tha. Wahan ke seth apne jahaaz Africa aur Faras ki khaadi tak bhejte the. Paas ka bandargaah Bharuch to Unani aur Roman zamane se jaana jaata tha.

Gujarat, Kathiawar aur Kutch ke log hamesha se vyapari aur samudri yatri rahe. Dharm badla, dhanda nahi. Parsi, jo terah sau saal pehle Gujarat aaye, unki bhasha bhi Gujarati hai. Musalmanon mein Khoja, Memon aur Bohra, sab Gujarat aur Kutch se. Ye log Burma, Ceylon aur Africa tak phaile.

Rajasthan ke Marwari poore desh ka andaroni vyapar aur len-den sambhalte the. Bade financier bhi, gaon ke chhote sahukaar bhi. Kisi bade Marwari ghar ki hundi desh mein kahin bhi chal jaati thi.

Sindhi Shikarpur aur Hyderabad se nikal kar Asia bhar mein phaile. Nehru kehte hain, jung se pehle duniya mein shayad hi koi bandargaah ho jahan ek Sindhi dukaan na mile.

Madras ke Chetty. Shabd aata hai Sanskrit ke "Shreshthi" se, yaani vyapari sangh ka mukhiya. Aur yahi shabd "Seth" ki jad bhi hai.

Har gaon mein ek baniye ki dukaan. Namak, tel, aur udhaar. Gareebi badhi, karz badha, aur dheere-dheere sahukaar hi zameendaar ban gaya.

Brahman aur Kshatriya vyapar ko neeche nazar se dekhte the. Unke liye izzat zameen mein thi, ya vidya mein. Angrezi raaj mein sarkari naukri sabse badi izzat ban gayi. ICS ko "heaven born service" kaha jaata tha. Nehru chutki lete hain: wo swarg London ke daftaron ki dhundhli parchhaai bhar tha. Aur vakeel, jo nayi adaalaton mein khoob kamaate the, wahi rajneeti aur samaj sudhaar mein aage aaye.

Bombay Hindustani udyog ka gadh bana. Parsi, Gujarati, Marwari aage. Marathe peshon aur vidya mein aage, achhe sipahi, mill mazdoor bhi. Nehru ye fark bhugol se samjhate hain: Maharashtra pathreela aur sakht, Gujarat upjaau aur naram.

Aur sabse zaroori baat. Ye fark ilaqe ke hain, dharm ke nahi. Ek Bengali Musalman Punjabi Musalman se zyada Bengali Hindu ke kareeb hai. Do Bengali kahin bhi milein, dharm koi bhi ho, turant ghul-mil jaate hain. Punjabi bhi.

Musalman, khaaskar Bengal aur uttar mein, Angrezi padhai aur udyog dono se door rahe. Kuch samanti soch ki wajah se, kuch byaaj par Islam ki rok ki wajah se. Haalaanki Nehru muskura kar yaad dilate hain ki sabse mashhoor sahukaaron mein ek Pathan kabeela bhi hai.

## Part 8: Sudhaar ki awaazein

Unnisvi sadi mein Hindu samaj ke andar bechaini thi. Kattar riwaaj, jaat-paat, andhvishwas. Sudhaar ki kai dhaaraein uthi.

Ek Gujarati sanyasi, Swami Dayananda Saraswati, ne Arya Samaj shuru kiya. Par uski jadein Punjab mein jami. Naara tha: Vedon ki taraf lauto. Matlab, Vedon ke baad jo kuch juda, sab hatao. Moorti puja bhi, baad ka darshan bhi. Arya Samaj ne jaat-paat ke khilaaf aur ladkiyon ki padhai ke liye kaam kiya.

Phir Bengal se ek bilkul alag awaaz. Swami Vivekananda. Ramakrishna Paramhansa ke shishya. Wo Advaita Vedanta ko bhavishya ka dharm maante the, jo tark aur vigyaan dono se mel khaata hai. Insaan paapi nahi, ishwar ka ansh hai.

Rajneeti se wo door rahe. Par baar-baar kehte ki desh ki umeed aam logon mein hai, ooper ke tabqe mein nahi. Wo chahte the ki Bharat Pashchim ki mehnat, barabari aur josh seekhe, par apni aatma na chhode. Duniya se kat kar rehna hi Bharat ke patan ki wajah thi, aisa unka maanna tha.

Ek baar unhone khud ko samajwadi bhi kaha. Is wajah se nahi ki wo koi sampoorn vyavastha hai, balki isliye ki baaki sab aazmaye ja chuke hain.

Unke har bhaashan mein ek hi sur tha. Abhay. Daro mat, mazboot bano. Kamzori hi sabse bada paap hai. Kanyakumari se Himalaya tak wo yahi bolte rahe, aur khud ko ghisa daala. 1902 mein unki maut hui. Umar sirf untalees saal.

Phir Nehru do mahaan logon ki tulna karte hain: Tagore aur Gandhi. Tagore, ameer kalakar jo dheere-dheere aam logon ke hamdard bane. Zindagi ko geet aur naach ke saath poora jeene ki parampara. Gandhi, kisaan jaise, tyaag aur tapasya ki parampara. Tagore soch ke aadmi, Gandhi lagataar karm ke. Dono duniya ko dekhte the, aur dono poori tarah Hindustani the. Ek hi Bharat ke do alag par mel khaate chehre.

Vivekananda ne chetavni di thi ki ateet mein mat atko, aage dekho. Par jo ateet unhone jagaya, uski chamak se bachna mushkil tha.

## Part 9: Aligarh, Al-Hilal aur Iqbal

Ab Musalmanon ki taraf. 1857 ke baad sabse zyada maar unhi par padi thi. Angrez unhe shak ki nazar se dekhte the. Angrezi padhai se wo door the.

Is khaai ko paatne aaye Sir Syed Ahmad Khan. Wo chahte the ki Musalman Angrezi padhein aur Islam ko vigyaan ke saath jodein. Unhone Aligarh mein college khola. Unke saath Shibli Nomani, Nazir Ahmad, aur Urdu ke bade shaayar Hali jaise log jude.

Par Sir Syed ka asar sirf ooper ke tabqe tak tha. Aam Musalman, jo zyadatar gareeb Hindu jaatiyon se aaye the, apne ameeron se zyada aam Hindu ke kareeb the. Aur Sir Syed ne Musalmanon ko Congress se door rakhne ki koshish bhi ki.

Phir bhi kai bade Musalman Congress mein gaye. Nayi peedhi mein rashtravaad jaagne laga. Use mod dene ke liye 1906 mein Muslim League bani, Angrezi sarkar ki sarparasti mein, Aga Khan ki agwaai mein. Do maqsad: Angrezon ke prati wafadari, aur Musalmanon ke hit.

Nehru yaad dilate hain ki us daur ke saare bade Musalman neta purani taleem se nikle the. Urdu ke sabse bade shaayar Ghalib 1857 se pehle hi apne shikhar par the.

Phir aata hai 1912. Do naye saaptahik. Urdu mein "Al-Hilal", aur Angrezi mein "The Comrade".

Al-Hilal ke sampadak the ek naujawan, Maulana Abul Kalam Azad. Unki bhasha nayi thi, tez aur jaandaar. Purane maulana unse naraaz the, par behas mein unse jeet nahi paate the, kyunki Azad dharm-granth unse zyada jaante the. Azad ka kehna tha ki Islam aur Hindustani rashtravaad mein koi takraav nahi. 1914 mein sarkar ne press zabt kar liya. Unhone "Al-Balagh" nikala. 1916 mein wo bhi band, aur Azad nazarband. Lagbhag chaar saal. Bahar aaye to seedhe Congress ke bade netaon mein. Do baar Congress adhyaksh bane.

The Comrade ke sampadak the Maulana Mohammad Ali. Islami parampara aur Oxford ki padhai ka ajeeb mel. Nehru halki chutki lete hain ki unke lekh chhote kabhi nahi hote the. 1911 mein Bengal ka batwara radd hua, aur unka Angrezon par bharosa hil gaya. Balkan yudh ne unhe Turkey ki taraf kheencha. Turkey ke pehle vishwa yudh mein utarne par unhone ek bahut lamba lekh likha. Akhbaar band. Unhe aur unke bhai Shaukat Ali ko nazarband kiya gaya. 1919 ke aakhir mein chhoote, aur seedhe Congress mein.

Ali bhaiyon ki ye yatra poore Musalman samaj ki yatra thi. 1913 mein Muslim League ne bhi wafadari chhod kar swaraj ki maang apna li.

Turkey kyun itna maayne rakhta tha? Kyunki wo lagbhag akela azaad Musalman desh bacha tha. Hindustani Musalman uski purani shaan mein sukoon dhoondhte the. Hindu bhi Turkey se hamdardi rakhte the, Europe ke hamle ka shikaar ek Asiyai desh samajhkar. Phir Mustafa Kemal Pasha aaye. Unhone khilafat khatam ki, aur Turkey ko ek aadhunik, dharm-nirpeksh desh banaya. Puri Asia mein rashtravaad ki ek nayi lehar uthi.

Aur phir Iqbal. Bade shaayar. Pakistan ke shuruaati himayatiyon mein gine jaate hain. Par Nehru ek dilchasp baat batate hain. Edward Thompson ke mutabik, Iqbal ne baatcheet mein maana ki unhone Pakistan ki baat Muslim League ke adhyaksh ki haisiyat se ki thi. Unhe khud lagta tha ki ye poore Bharat ke liye, aur khaaskar Musalmanon ke liye, nuksaandeh hoga. Nehru ki raay mein batwara Musalmanon ka pichhdapan door nahi karega, balki samanti tabqe ki pakad aur lambi karega.

## Part 10: Tilak, Gokhale aur alag nirvachan

Beesvi sadi ki shuruaat. Bharat mein bhaari udyog ki neev padi. Jamshedpur mein Tata ka loha aur steel ka kaarkhana. Angrez sarkar ne ise khushi se nahi, majboori mein hone diya.

Saath mein ek naya mazdoor varg bana. Kamzor aur bina sangathan ke. Peeche karodon berozgaar khade the, to hadtaal kaamyaab hona mushkil tha. 1920 ke aas-paas pehli Trade Union Congress bani. Kisaanon ke samandar mein mazdoor ek balti bhar the. Par Russi kranti ke baad sab unki taraf dhyan dene lage.

Rajneeti mein do raaste the. Gopal Krishna Gokhale, naram aur dheere sudhaar wale. Aur Bal Gangadhar Tilak, garam, jo seedhe swaraj ki maang karte the aur Maharashtra ke aam logon tak pahunche. 1905 mein Bengal ka batwara hua, aur poora desh bhadak utha. Swadeshi andolan. 1907 mein Surat mein Congress do hisson mein bant gayi. Tilak ko lambi qaid mili, Mandalay mein.

Aur phir wo cheez aayi jise Nehru is chapter ka sabse bada zehar maante hain. Alag nirvachan. Yaani Musalman vote sirf Musalman ummeedwar ko denge, alag list se.

Lord Morley, jo London mein Bharat ke Secretary of State the, iske khilaaf the. Unhone apni diary mein iske khatre likhe. Par Viceroy ke dabaav mein maan gaye. Nehru ka andaaza hai ki Viceroy aur unke saathi yahi chahte the: ki asli loktantra der se aaye.

Nehru ki list lambi hai. Alag nirvachan ne kamzor tabqon ko aur kamzor kiya, kyunki wo apne pairon par khade hone ke bajaye sahaare dhoondhne lage. Ekta ki jagah alagaav badha. Ye loktantra ka ulta tha. Asli sawal, roti aur rozgaar, jo sabke saajhe the, peeche chale gaye. Behas sarkari naukriyon ke chhote-chhote padon par simat gayi.

Sahi raasta kya tha? Pichhde tabqon ko taleem do, aage badhao. Musalmanon ke liye, dalit samaj ke liye, sabke liye. Ye kabhi nahi kiya gaya.

Aur ye zehar phailta gaya. Pehle Musalman, phir doosre samudaay. Bharat chhote-chhote alag dibbon ka desh ban gaya. Nehru saaf kehte hain ki isse thoda bhi fayda hua ho to unhe dikhta nahi. Aur isi se aage chalkar Bharat ko todne ki maang nikli.

1918 ki Montagu-Chelmsford report ne bhi khud yahi chetavni di thi, ki dharm aur jaat par bante vote log ko naagrik nahi, guton ka sipahi banate hain. Chetavni likhi gayi, aur file mein rakh di gayi.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, Regulation III. Nehru ne 1944 mein likha ki 126 saal purana bina-mukadma qaid ka qanoon ab bhi chal raha hai. Azad Bharat mein bhi preventive detention ke qanoon bane rahe. Ye behas abhi khatam nahi hui.

Doosri, chhapakhane ka dar. Nizam ke press ko chupchaap todne ki peshkash, aur aaj internet band karne ke faisle. Mashin badli hai, dar wahi hai.

Teesri, Bombay ka cricket. "Bombay Presidency" naam ki team mein sirf gore. Aaj wahi shehar Bharatiya cricket ka gadh hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru kehte hain ki ek Bengali Musalman, Punjabi Musalman se zyada Bengali Hindu ke kareeb hai. Aapki apni pehchaan mein ilaqa zyada bhaari hai ya dharm?
