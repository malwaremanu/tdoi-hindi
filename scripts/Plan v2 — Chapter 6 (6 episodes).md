# Plan v2 — Chapter 6, "New Problems" → 6 episodes

Oct 17, 2026 · @Manupal Choudhary

**What this document is.** The phase A–E sheet for the re-split of `book/chapters_v2/ch06.txt` (29,799 words, the Internet Archive full text) into **six** episodes, `Ep 6.1 … 6.6`. It follows `scripts/Ep 4+ — Chapter-to-Episodes Method.md` (R1–R18). No script is written from anything else.

---

## 0. What changed, and why

Chapter 6's episodes were written from a lossy copy (`book/chapters/ch06.txt`, 17,363 words) that was missing whole passages — the Kabir / Guru Nanak cluster, the second half of the caste section, the feudalism-and-land block, the sea-power passage, the Warren Hastings letter, Allawardi's Bengal and Ahalya Bai's Indore, Adam Smith, and the chapter's own closing lines. The source has now been rebuilt from the Internet Archive full text as `book/chapters_v2/ch06.txt` — **29,799 words, 16 sub-section headings** (the chapter's own empty opener among them; the old copy was missing whole passages, and one real heading — "Aurangzeb puts the Clock Back" — lost its `## ` mark in the rebuild and must still be counted as a sub-section) — so the facts the old copy never carried now have to be told. At the owner's decision, chapter 6 becomes a **set of six episodes** (Ep 6.1 … 6.6) at roughly **5,000–5,200 narration words each**, a ratio of about **1.0× of source words** (the old single episode ran at 0.39× and read as a fact blizzard; the old four-episode set at about 0.65×). The chapter's own line — Arabs → Mahmud → Afghans → Mughals → Marathas → British, with one long social digression in the middle (village, caste, joint family) and the economic verdict at the end — is the skeleton; the digression gets two episodes instead of one, because in the real source it is **~5,600 words** (S07 1,247 + S08 886 + S09 3,481), not the ~2,900 the lossy copy showed. It is carried by 6.2's last two beats and all of 6.3.

**Chapter 6 ka set sawal (Ep 6.1–6.6):** *Jis desh ke paas duniya ke behtareen kaarigar the, wo nayi duniya ki daud mein peeche kyun reh gaya?* — kept from the existing set, because the rebuilt source answers it much more fully (S11's clock-and-Bible block, S15's organization-and-technique block, S17's economy block all grew).

**Set answer, built across the six payoffs:** (6.1) the door was open and the account was unequal; (6.2) the throne kept changing and everyday life did not; (6.3) everyday life rested on three pillars whose aim was security, not progress; (6.4) the one ruler who could have asked "how is this made?" never asked it, and his great-grandson broke the clock; (6.5) the Company won by luck, by organization, and above all by our own disunity — its real weapon was paper; (6.6) India was not behind until it was held behind — the workshops, banks and hundis matched the world's, and the wrong England came.

### 1.2 Episode count (R2 arithmetic, for the record)

`E = max( ceil(29,799 / 4,600), ceil(U / 75) ) = max(7, …)` at the method's 4,600-word rule. The owner has set **6** with a narration ceiling of 5,200 (≈32 min) instead of 5,000, i.e. 4,970 source words per episode at 1.05×. That is the whole logic of this split: six episodes at ~32 minutes, not seven at ~29. The U side is comfortable everywhere (largest episode ≈60 of 75).
