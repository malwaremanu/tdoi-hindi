## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar wo zameen ke neeche gaye, ret mein dabe Mohenjo-daro tak, aur Sindhu tak, jisne is desh ko naam diya. Par us shehar ki likhaai koi padh nahi paaya. Aaj hum un shabdon tak chalenge jo padhe ja sakte hain.

## Part 1: Pothi ko insaan ki tarah padhna

Kalpana kijiye, us kothri ki mez. Lalten ke paas kitaabon ka ek chhota dher. Upanishad. Gita. Aur unke peechhe, sabse purani, Ved.

Mohenjo-daro ki khudai se pehle yahi Ved Bharat ki sanskriti ke sabse purane likhe hue saboot maane jaate the.

Par kitne purane? Is par lambi behas chali.

Europe ke vidvaan aam taur par inhe baad ka bataate the. Bharat ke vidvaan bahut pehle ka.

Nehru ko ye josh thoda ajeeb lagta hai. Jaise apni sanskriti jitni purani saabit ho, utni hi badi ho jaayegi.

Ek European vidvaan Vedon ki shuruaat ko bahut peechhe le gaye. Do hazaar, ya shayad dhai hazaar saal Isa poorv. Yaani lagbhag Mohenjo-daro ke zamane tak.

Zyadatar vidvaan Rigved ke geeton ko lagbhag pandrah sau saal Isa poorv ka maante hain. Aur khudai ke baad se, ye tareekh peechhe khiskaane ka jhukaav badha hai.

Tareekh jo bhi ho, ek baat lagbhag tay hai. Ye sahitya Yunan aur Israel ke sahitya se bhi pehle ka hai. Insaan ke mann ke sabse purane likhe hue dastavezon mein se ek.

Yahan ek naam aata hai, jo aaj baar-baar lautega. Max Müller — ek German vidvaan, Vedon ke bade paarkhi.

Unhone Vedon ko Arya insaan ka bola hua pehla shabd kaha tha.

Ye shabd Arya apne saath laaye the, jab wo is upjaau desh mein utre. Unke vichaar ek saajhe khazane se aaye the. Usi khazane se Iran mein Avesta bana — wahi purana Irani granth, jo pichhli baar "Hindu" shabd ke saath mila tha.

Aur dono ki bhasha itni milti hai, ki ek baat kahi gayi hai. Avesta Ved ke zyada kareeb hai, jitna Ved apni hi baad ki Sanskrit ke.

Jaise do chachere bhai, jinki boli aapas mein jitni milti hai, utni apne hi poton se nahi.

Ab Nehru ek mushkil sawal ke saamne khade hain. Dharm-granthon ko padhein kaise?

Unhe insaan ki likhi kitaab maan kar parkhiye, to sachche bhakt naraz hote hain. Par Nehru kehte hain, doosra koi raasta hai hi nahi.

To wo apna niyam banate hain.

Wo dekhte hain ki unhe kya chhodna hai. Gehri tattva-charcha. Karm-kaand ke byore. Lambi-lambi teekayein. Jo panne unki zindagi ke sawalon se nahi jude, unhe wo palat dete hain.

Bahut kuch wo samajh nahi paate. Kabhi doosri baar padhne par roshni milti hai. Kabhi nahi.

Par asli baat ye hai. Agar ye kitaabein "aasmaani hukm" hain, jinhe bina sawal ke poora maanna hai, to Nehru ka mann band ho jaata hai.

Aur agar inhe insaanon ne likha hai, to mann khul jaata hai. Bahut samajhdaar, door tak dekhne wale insaan. Par insaan hi.

Nehru ke liye ye kahin badi baat hai. Ek insaan apne bal par oonchaai tak pahunche, aur phir doosron ko bhi upar uthaye. Kisi aur ki awaaz ka bhonpu bhar na bane.

Kai dharm-sansthapak sach mein adbhut log the. Par Nehru kehte hain, unhe insaan na maano, to unki saari chamak bujh jaati hai. Ummeed insaan ke badhte mann se milti hai.

Aur ek shart. Har pothi ko uske zamane mein rakh kar padhna hai. Uske mahaul, uske samaaj ke saath. Karm-kaand ka lapeta hata kar.

Kuch sawal hamesha ke hain. Isliye ye kitaabein aaj bhi khinchti hain. Kuch sirf apne zamane ke the. Wo ab bas itihaas hain.

Ab Nehru sabse purani pothi kholte hain.

Usme unhe kya milta hai?

## Part 2: Jaanne ka jhola

Ek khula aasmaan sochiye. Neeche ek nadi. Kinaare par aag jal rahi hai. Aur kuch log gaa rahe hain.

Kiske liye? Suraj ke liye. Hawa ke liye. Barsaat, bhor aur aag ke liye. Har us cheez ke liye, jo unhe hairaan karti hai.

Yahi Ved hain.

"Ved" shabd "vid" se bana hai. Vid, yaani jaanna. Nehru ke hisaab se Ved apne zamane ke saare gyaan ka ek sangrah the. Ek jhola, jisme sab kuch bhar diya gaya.

Isme kya-kya hai? Geet. Prarthanayein. Yagya ki vidhiyan. Thoda jaadoo-tona. Aur prakriti par kamaal ki kavita.

Nehru ko afsos hai ki bahut se Hindu inhe sirf aasmaan se utri vaani maante hain. Isse inki asli keemat chhoot jaati hai. Ye insaan ke mann ke pehle khilne ki kahani hain. Aur kya mann tha wo!

Dhyan dijiye. Vedon mein murti-puja nahi hai. Devtaon ke mandir nahi hain.

Aur zindagi ke liye ek ajeeb sa josh hai. Shuruaati Arya jeene mein itne doobe the, ki aatma ki taraf unka dhyan kam hi gaya. Maut ke baad kuch hota hai, ye wo maante the. Par dhundhle dhang se.

Phir sadiyon mein soch badalti hai. Pehle bahut se devta, aasmaan wale Yunani devtaon jaise. Phir ek Ishwar. Aur phir usme ghulta hua ek aur vichaar, ki sab kuch asal mein ek hi hai.

Prakriti ke rahasya par chintan shuru hota hai. Khojne ka mann jaagta hai. Aur Ved ke ant tak aate-aate, Vedanta — "Ved ka ant" — hum Upanishadon tak pahunch jaate hain.

Rigved, pehla Ved, shayad insaaniyat ki sabse purani kitaab hai. Ek vidvaan ne isme ek safar ki shuruaat dekhi. Duniya aur insaan ka matlab dhoondhne ka safar. Aur unke shabdon mein, Bharat ne wo safar kabhi roka nahi.

Par ye pehli kitaab hai, pehli sabhyata nahi. Iske peeche sadiyon ki basi hui zindagi thi. Sindhu ke shehar, Mesopotamia, aur bhi.

Isliye Rigved mein ek samarpan hai, jo Nehru ko bahut sateek lagta hai. Rishiyon ke naam. Humare poorvajon ke naam. Jo sabse pehle raasta dhoondhne nikle.

Rabindranath Tagore ne in geeton ko ek poori kaum ki hairat ka dastavez kaha. Ek seedha, bahadur aur khushi bhara vishwas. Jisme rahasya zindagi ko bojhil nahi, jaadui banata tha.

Aur beech-beech mein ek chamak bhi. "Sach ek hai. Gyaani use alag-alag naamon se bulaate hain."

Ye is kahani ka pehla padaav hai. Hairat.

Par phir dheere-dheere ek chinta andar aati hai. Ek kavi pukaar uthta hai: "Ae Shraddha, hume vishwas de."

Sochiye. Vishwas maangna padta hai, matlab vishwas kam pad raha hai. Shak aa chuka hai.

Aur phir ek geet aata hai, jo srishti ke baare mein gehre sawal uthata hai. Use "Srishti ka geet" kehte hain. Max Müller ne use ek aur naam diya. "Agyaat devta ke naam."

Yaad hai pichhli baar ka vaada? Duniya ki sabse purani kitaabon mein se ek, aur usme pehli baar koi poochta hai: ye sab aaya kahan se?

Yahi wo geet hai. Hairat ke baad doosra padaav. Shak.

To kya ye log duniya se bhaagne wale the? Ya duniya mein doobe hue log, jo duniya ke baare mein sawal pooch rahe the?

Nehru yahan apne hi logon ka ek pyaara bhram todne wale hain.

## Part 3: Ek khatarnak khel

Kalpana kijiye, qile ka aangan. Shaam ki tehal. Ek saathi qaidi kehta hai, "Hum gareeb sahi, ghulam sahi. Par aatma mein to aaj bhi duniya ke guru hain."

Nehru ruk jaate hain. Ye baat unhe chubhti hai.

Wo likhte hain ki ghulam desh aksar aaj se bhaagta hai. Beete zamane ke sapnon mein chhupta hai. Purani shaan ki yaad se tasalli dhoondhta hai.

Nehru ise bewakoofi kehte hain. Aur khatarnak bhi. Aur bahut se log ye khel khelte hain.

Usse bhi zyada shak wali ek doosri baat. Ye maanna, ki hum baaki har cheez mein gir gaye, par aatma mein ab bhi mahaan hain.

Nehru ka jawab seedha hai. Bhookh aur ghulami ki neev par koi mahaanta nahi tikti. Na aatmik, na koi aur.

Aur ye "duniya se pare" wali tasveer bani kahan? Kai pashchimi lekhakon ne ise khoob hawa di.

Phir Nehru ek kadwi baat kehte hain. Har desh ke gareeb aur badkismat log thode "duniya se pare" ho hi jaate hain. Jab tak wo kraanti na karein. Kyunki ye duniya saaf taur par unke liye bani hi nahi. Ghulam kaumon ke saath bhi yahi hota hai.

Aaj sochiye. Jab koi kehta hai "hum to hamesha se aadhyatmik the", to wo kis cheez se nazar chura raha hai?

Ab Nehru ek tasveer dete hain. Do nadiyan.

Insaan bada hota hai, to bahar ki duniya se poora santosh nahi milta. Wo andar ka matlab bhi dhoondhta hai. Sabhyatayein bhi aise hi badi hoti hain.

Ek nadi bahar ki zindagi ki. Ek andar ki. Jab dono paas-paas behti hain, santulan rehta hai. Jab door ho jaati hain, mann aur aatma ke sankat shuru hote hain.

Rigved mein bahar wali nadi lehraati hai. Prakriti ki sundarta. Jeene ki khushi. Devta bhi bilkul insaanon jaise, Yunan ke Olympus parvat ke devtaon ki tarah. Wo neeche aate, logon mein ghulte-milte. Dono ke beech koi pakki lakeer nahi.

Phir andar wali nadi gehri hoti hai. Ankhon se pare ki duniya ki khoj. Ek tarah ki virakti. Aur sawal. Is sab ka maqsad kya hai? Agar hai, to insaan uske saath taal kaise milaye?

Dono nadiyan saath-saath behti hain. Zor kabhi ek par, kabhi doosri par. Par buniyaad kabhi "duniya bekaar hai" wali nahi thi.

Maya ka vichaar bhi poora bhram nahi tha. Wo asli sach ke muqable mein apekshik thi. Kuch-kuch Plato ki parchhai jaisi. Duniya ko jaisi hai waisi le kar, uski sundarta ka mazaa lena, yahi dhang tha.

Nehru tulna karte hain. Kai Semitic dharm, aur shuruaati Isai dharm, kahin zyada duniya se pare the. T. E. Lawrence — wo angrez lekhak-sipahi, jo barson Arab registaan mein rahe. Unke hisaab se in sab ki saajhi neev ek hi khayal tha: duniya bekaar hai. Aur isse aksar bhog aur tyaag ke beech jhoolna aata hai.

Ab Bharat ke khilte hue daur dekhiye. Har baar wahi rang. Kala, sangeet, kavita, naach, natak. Yahan tak ki prem aur sharir ke rishton par baareek adhyayan.

Nehru poochte hain, duniya ko bekaar maanne wali sanskriti ye sab paida kar sakti thi? Aur hazaaron saal zinda reh sakti thi?

Par wo imaandaar hain. Bauddh aur Jain dharm ne duniya se door rehne par zor diya. Kuch daur mein log jhund ke jhund viharon mein chale gaye. Kyun? Nehru kehte hain, unhe nahi pata.

Madhyakaal ke Europe mein bhi log maan baithe the ki duniya khatam hone wali hai. Shayad aisa tyaag raajnaitik aur aarthik haar ki ghutan se aata hai.

Aur Buddh dharm bhi beech ka raasta hai. Nirvana khaali-pan nahi, ek sakaaraatmak avastha hai. Bas shabdon se pare hai, isliye "na" mein bataayi gayi. Bauddh desh, khaas kar China, zindagi se pyaar ka jeeta-jaagta saboot hain.

To phir ye bhram aaya kahan se?

## Part 4: Poori taaqat se kaam, phal se upar

Kalpana kijiye, Nehru ek line ke neeche kalam se lakeer kheench rahe hain.

Bhram ki jad ek hi hai. Bharatiya soch hamesha zindagi ke aakhri maqsad par zor deti rahi. Wo us "pare" ko kabhi bhooli nahi.

Isliye wo zindagi ko poora apnaati thi. Par uski ghulam banne se mana karti thi.

Uski seekh ek vaakya mein samaa jaati hai. Sahi kaam karo, apni poori taaqat se. Par us kaam se upar raho. Nateeje ki zyada fikr mat karo.

Ye vaakya yaad rakhiye. Agle episode mein ye ek yuddh ke maidan mein laut kar aayega.

Iska matlab hai virakti, kaam ke beech. Kaam se bhaagna nahi.

Ek kisaan sochiye. Wo poori mehnat se beej boota hai. Barsaat uske haath mein nahi. Phir bhi wo beej bo deta hai. Aur aasmaan ki taraf dekh kar haath par haath rakh kar nahi baith jaata.

Nehru kehte hain, ye vichaar duniya ke zyadatar darshanon mein hai. Bas kehne ka dhang alag hai. Dikhti duniya aur andekhi duniya ke beech santulan.

Dikhti duniya se zyada chipko, to doosri duniya bhool jaati hai. Aur tab kaam ka bhi koi aakhri matlab nahi bachta.

Aur ek cheez in purane khojiyon mein saaf dikhti hai. Sach ke liye junoon.

Rudhi aur aasmaani faisle? Wo un logon ke liye chhod diye gaye, jo unse upar uth nahi sakte. Khud ka anubhav, khud ka prayog, yahi raasta tha.

Ab Nehru ek imaandaar shak jodte hain. Andekhi duniya ka anubhav shabdon mein nahi bandhta. Wo sach ki jhalak thi, ya kalpana ka dhokha? Nehru nahi jaante. Shayad aksar khud ko dhokha hi tha.

Par unhe nateeje se zyada dhang pasand hai. Koi hukm nahi. Koi rudhi nahi. Khud jaa kar dekho, parde ke peechhe kya hai.

To ye khud jaa kar dekhne wale kaun the?

## Part 5: Andhe ke peeche andhe

Kalpana kijiye, ek bada yagya. Dhuaan. Mantron ki goonj. Ghee ki aahuti.

Aur kinaare par ek rishi, jo halke se muskura raha hai.

Ye Upanishadon ka zamana hai. Lagbhag aath sau saal Isa poorv. Arya kab ke bas chuke hain. Ek sthir, khushhaal sabhyata ban chuki hai. Naye aur purane ka mel. Upar Arya vichaar, neeche puraani puja ke dhang.

Aur is zamane ke rishi Vedon ki baat izzat se karte hain. Par halki si chutki ke saath.

Purane devta ab unhe santosh nahi dete. Pujaariyon ke karm-kaand ka mazaak udta hai.

Ek Upanishad kehta hai, jo bina samjhe vidhiyan karte hain, aur khud ko gyaani samajhte hain, wo bhatakte hain. Jaise andhe ko andha raasta dikha raha ho.

Jaadoo-tone mein dilchaspi ko sakhti se roka jaata hai. Yahan tak ki Vedon ko "nichli vidya" kaha jaata hai. Oonchi vidya andar ke mann ki hai.

Par dhyan dijiye. Koi purane se rishta nahi todta. Ateet ek seedhi hai. Uspar khade ho kar aage chadhna hai.

Upanishadon mein khojne ka josh hai. Mann ka safar. Sach jaanne ki pyaas. Ye aaj ke vigyaan ke tareeqe nahi hain. Par usme vigyaan ka ek ansh hai. Koi rudhi raaste mein nahi aane di jaati.

Nehru saaf kehte hain, isme bahut kuch bekaar bhi hai. Aaj ke liye bematlab.

Asal zor khud ko jaanne par hai. Ek mera apna aatma. Ek sabse bada, poorn aatma. Aur Upanishad kehte hain, dono asal mein ek hain.

Aur ye duniya? Jhooth nahi. Apne dang se sach. Andar ke bade sach ka ek roop.

Upanishadon mein kai jagah do arth nikalte hain. Isliye vyakhyaayein bhi anek hui. Par jhukaav ek hi taraf hai, ki sab ek hai. Shayad us zamane ki tez bahason ko thanda karne ke liye. Jodne ka raasta.

Gyaan ke saath aacharan bhi chahiye. Bina sanyam ke vidvatta bekaar hai. Aur zindagi ke saare farz nibhane hain, par virakti ke saath.

Phir Nehru ek kamzori pakadte hain. Zor vyakti ke poorn banne par itna tha, ki samaaj peechhe chhoot gaya.

Upanishad kehte hain, insaan se upar kuch nahi. Samaaj ko sthir maan liya gaya. Aur mann aasmaan aur dil ki gehraiyon mein ghoomta raha.

Ye soch sankari raashtravaadi nahi thi. Haan, Bharat khud ko duniya ki dhuri samajhta tha, jaise China, Yunan aur Rome ne bhi samjha. Par Mahabharat kehta hai, insaanon ki poori duniya ek hi deh hai, jiske ang ek doosre par tike hain.

Par ye rishi asal mein bolte kaise the? Unki awaaz kaisi thi?

## Part 6: Charaiveti

Wapas kothri mein chaliye. Raat gehri hai. Nehru ek panna halke se, apne aap se padh rahe hain.

Wo maante hain ki Upanishadon ki gehri tattva-charcha unki pakad mein nahi aati. Par unhe inki dhaar pasand hai. Sawal karne ki aadat. Tark ki zameen. Ye dharm ka nahi, darshan ka raasta hai.

Roop chhota, kasa hua. Aksar shishya poochta hai, guru jawab deta hai. Kuch log kehte hain, ye shayad kisi guru ke lecture ke notes the. Ya shishyon ke likhe.

Ek angrez vidvaan ko inme do doston ki gehri baatcheet ki sachchai sunai deti hai.

Aur Rajaji — C. Rajagopalachari, Nehru ke saathi aur Congress ke bade neta. Unke liye ye duniya ki sabse purani pavitra kitaab hai. Phir bhi sabse aadhunik. Sach ki pyaas mein lagbhag laaparwah khoj.

Upanishadon ki reedh sach hai. Ek line kehti hai, sach hi jeetta hai, jhooth nahi. Is line ko sambhaal kar rakhiye. Episode ke aakhir mein ye hume aaj tak le aayegi.

Aur ek prarthana, jo shayad aapne suni hai. Asatya se satya ki or. Andhere se roshni ki or. Mrityu se amrit ki or.

Phir bechain mann jhaankta hai. Kiske kehne par mann apni daal par baithta hai? Aankh aur kaan ko kaun chalata hai?

Aur phir wahi sawal, jo cold open mein ladke ne poocha tha.

[SFX: cold open ki dhaara, bahut halki, ek pal ke liye]

Hawa ruk kyun nahi sakti? Mann ko chain kyun nahi? Paani kis talaash mein behta hai?

Nehru likhte hain ki ye insaan ka safar hai. Raaste mein koi padaav nahi. Safar ka koi ant nahi.

Ek aur purani pothi mein, isi lambe safar par ek geet hai. Har chhand ek hi shabd par khatam hota hai.

Charaiveti. Charaiveti.

Chalte raho, musafir. Chalte raho.

Yahi hai aaj ka naam. Wo dhaara jo rukti nahi.

Is khoj mein kisi sarvashaktimaan ke aage jhukne wali vinamrata nahi hai. Ye mann ki jeet hai, apne mahaul par.

Ek rishi kehta hai, ye sharir raakh ho jaayega, saans hawa mein mil jaayegi. Par main, aur mere karm, nahi. Ae mann, ye yaad rakhna.

Ek subah ki prarthana mein to suraj se kaha gaya hai, tujhe tu banane wala main hi hoon. Nehru likhte hain: kya zabardast aatmavishwas!

To aatma kya hai? Use seedhe bataya nahi ja sakta. Sirf "na" mein. Ye nahi. Ye bhi nahi. Ya ek doosre dhang se: wo tum ho.

Har aatma ek chingaari hai. Ek dahakti aag se nikli, aur usi mein laut jaane wali.

Aag ek hai. Par jo jalti hai, usi ki shakal le leti hai. Waise hi andar ka aatma, jis cheez mein jaata hai, waisa dikhta hai. Khud uska koi roop nahi.

Agar har jeev mein wahi ek chingaari hai, to logon ke beech ki deewaron ka kya hoga?

## Part 7: Aadhyatmik loktantra

Pichhli baar humne ek deewar ko uthte dekha tha. Arya aur baaki logon ke beech. Phir Aryon ke apne beech.

Ab usi zamane ka ek vichaar us deewar ke upar se paar chala jaata hai.

Jo sab mein ek hi tattva dekh le, uske liye deewarein gir jaati hain. Insaan se, prakriti se, ek apnapan jaagta hai. Upanishad poochte hain, jo sab mein ek dekhta hai, use kaisa dukh, kaisa bhram?

Nehru yahan ek virodh dekhte hain. Ek taraf Indo-Arya ki kattar alag-thalag soch. Doosri taraf ye vichaar, jo jaati, varg aur har bhed ko laangh jaata hai.

Nehru ise ek naam dete hain. Aadhyatmik loktantra.

Ek line kehti hai, jo sab mein ek aatma dekhe, wo kisi jeev ko neecha nahi dekh sakta.

Ye sirf siddhant tha. Nehru maante hain. Par zindagi par iska asar zaroor pada hoga.

Iske chalte sahansheelta aayi. Aastha ke maamle mein khula vichaar. Jiyo aur jeene do. Ye Bharatiya sanskriti ki pehchaan bani, jaise Chini sanskriti ki bhi.

Dharm aur sanskriti mein koi sab par thopa hua ek hukm nahi tha. Nehru ise ek purani, samajhdaar sabhyata ka nishaan maante hain, jiske mann ke bhandaar khatam nahi hote.

Ek sawal bhi poocha gaya. Ye brahmaand kya hai? Kahan se uthta hai? Kahan jaata hai?

Jawab ajeeb hai. Azaadi mein uthta hai. Azaadi mein tikta hai. Aur azaadi mein hi ghul jaata hai.

Nehru saaf kehte hain, iska poora matlab wo nahi samajhte. Par itna dikhta hai, ki in rishiyon ko azaadi se junoon tha. Swami Vivekananda isi baat par baar-baar zor dete the.

Nehru ye bhi maante hain ki us zamane ke mann mein utarna mushkil hai. Likhne ka dhang ajeeb, anuvaad kathin, zindagi bilkul alag.

Phir bhi Upanishadon ki baat har daur mein suni gayi. Isne desh ke mann aur charitra ko gadha. Ek vidvaan ke hisaab se, Hindu soch ka koi bada roop aisa nahi, jiski jad yahan na ho. Bauddh dharm bhi nahi.

Aur ye vichaar seemaon mein nahi ruke.

Pehle Iran ke raaste Yunan pahunche, aur wahan kuch soch-wichaar karne walon ko chhua.

Bahut baad mein Plotinus — ek Yunani darshanik — Iran aur Bharat ka darshan padhne poorab aaya. Upanishadon ka rahasyamay paksh use khaas taur par bhaaya.

Kehte hain, Plotinus se ye vichaar St. Augustine tak gaye — Isai dharm ke ek bade shuruaati vichaarak. Aur unke zariye us zamane ke Isai dharm tak.

Phir pichhle dedh sau saal mein Europe ne in kitaabon ko dobara khoja. Aur wahan halchal mach gayi.

Sabse mashhoor naam hai Schopenhauer — ek German darshanik, jo apni nirasha ke liye jaane jaate the.

Sochiye. Zindagi ko dukh maanne wala aadmi. Aur wo kehta hai, Upanishadon ka adhyayan meri zindagi ki tasalli raha hai. Aur meri maut ki bhi tasalli hoga.

Aur hamare Max Müller, teesri baar. Wo kehte hain, Schopenhauer bina soche tareef karne wale aadmi nahi the. Aur wo bhi unke josh mein shareek hain. Unke sabse khushi ke ghante Vedanta padhte hue beete. Subah ki roshni jaise. Pahaad ki saaf hawa jaise.

Ek Irish kavi ne to yahan tak kaha. Europe aur America ke bade aadhunik kavi jo keh gaye, wo sab Gita aur Upanishadon mein pehle se hai. Aur usse bhi zyada.

Itni oonchi soch. Par sawal ye hai. Us jungle ki basti mein, ise samajhte kitne log the?

## Part 8: Chhota sa ghera

Cold open ka wo ped yaad kijiye. Guru, aur do-teen shishya.

Ab zara peechhe hattiye. Ped ke aage poora gaon hai. Koi khet mein hai. Koi gaay dooh raha hai. Koi bachche ko sula raha hai.

Ped ke neeche ki baatcheet unke kaanon tak nahi pahunchti.

Pehle wo baat, jo sab tak pahunchi. Upanishad sharir ki taiyaari aur mann ki saaf nazar par zor dete hain. Gyaan ho ya koi bhi kaamyaabi, keemat chahiye. Sanyam. Kasht. Tyaag.

Ise tapasya kehte hain. Aur ye vichaar oopar ke vidvaanon mein bhi tha, aur neeche ke anpadh logon mein bhi. Hazaaron saal pehle bhi, aur 1944 mein bhi.

Gandhiji ke netritva mein jan-aandolan Bharat ko hila rahe hain. Nehru kehte hain, unka mann samajhna ho, to tapasya samajhni padegi.

Par Upanishadon ki baaki soch? Wo ek chhote se ghere tak simit rahi. Chune hue log, jo use samajh sakte the. Aam janta ki pahunch se bahut door.

Nehru yahan ek niyam batate hain. Har sabhyata mein ek chhota rachnatmak varg hota hai. Wo bahumat ke saath taal mein rahe, use upar kheenche, faasla kam kare. Tab sanskriti tikti bhi hai, badhti bhi.

Aisa varg na ho, to sabhyata sad jaati hai. Aur agar ye rishta toot jaaye, tab bhi. Phir wo varg khud banjar ho jaata hai. Ya koi nayi taaqat uski jagah le leti hai.

Nehru ka andaaza hai ki Upanishad ke zamane mein koi khaai saaf nahi dikhti thi. Samaaj darjon mein bata tha. Mann ke darje bhi maan liye gaye the. Isse ek tarah ki shaanti thi.

Par keemat bhi thi. Naye vichaaron ko aam andhvishwason ke saanche mein dhaala gaya. Unka asli matlab ghis gaya. "Sab ek hai" puja ke liye "ek Ishwar" ban gaya. Neeche ki puja ko sirf saha nahi gaya, badhaava diya gaya.

Nateeja? Vichaar neeche nahi utre. Aur khaai badhti gayi.

Phir nayi lehrein uthin. Bhautikvaad. Shak karne wale. Ishwar ko na maanne wale. Unhi se Bauddh aur Jain dharm nikle. Aur Ramayan, Mahabharat jaise mahakavya, jo phir se jodne ki koshish the.

Itihaas aise hi chalta raha. Rachna ke ubaal, phir kalah. Aakhri bada ubaal chauthi sadi Isvi ka shastriya yug tha.

Lagbhag 1000 Isvi tak andar ki thakaan saaf dikhne lagi. Kala phir bhi chalti rahi. Phir bahar se naye log aaye, aur thake mann mein nayi jaan daali.

Par wo thakaan andar se aayi kahan se?

## Part 9: Jab log chalna bhool gaye

[SFX: cold open ki dhaara phir laut kar aati hai, aur dheere-dheere tham jaati hai]

Wo dhaara phir sochiye. Jo ek pal ke liye bhi nahi rukti thi.

Ab dekhiye, wo kaise rukti hai.

Jis vyaktivaad ne rishiyon ko aasmaan tak pahunchaya, usi ne samaaj ko bhula diya. Har insaan ki zindagi bant gayi. Apne chhote se khaane mein farzon ki ek gathri.

Poore samaaj ke prati koi farz nahi. Sab ek hain, ye ehsaas jagaane ki koi koshish nahi.

Nehru imaandaar hain. Ye vichaar shayad aadhunik hai. Kisi bhi purane samaaj se iski ummeed karna theek nahi. Phir bhi, Bharat mein ye kami kahin zyada dikhti thi.

Aage chal kar yahi soch logon ke mann ki jail ban gayi. Sabse zyada neechi jaatiyon ke liye. Par oonchi jaatiyon ke liye bhi.

Jaise-jaise jaati sakht hui, mann bhi sakht hua. Aur kaum ki rachnatmak urja bujhti gayi.

Phir ek baat ulti lagne lagti hai. Har vishwas, har andhvishwas, har bewakoofi ko sehne ki aadat. Isne bahut si buri reetiyon ko zinda rakha.

Badhte pujaari varg ne iska poora fayda uthaya. Janta ke andhvishwas par apne swaarth ki imaarat khadi ki.

Nehru nyay se kehte hain, ye varg kuch Church shaakhaon jitna taaqatwar shayad kabhi nahi bana. Har daur mein aise aadhyatmik neta the jo iske khilaaf bole. Aur chunne ke liye kai raaste the. Par janta ko jakadne ke liye ye kaafi tha.

Khule vichaar aur kattarpan saath-saath rahe. Unse kitaabi bahas ka jaal bana. Aur kadi, sookhi vidhiyon ka zor.

Har baar purane granthon ki duhaai di gayi. Par badalte zamane ke hisaab se unka matlab dobara samjhne ki koshish nahi hui.

Charaiveti, chalte raho, ek rataa hua mantra ban gaya. Log chalna bhool gaye.

Aur jo itna zinda tha, uska sirf chhilka bacha.

Aurobindo Ghose — krantikari se yogi bane Bengal ke vichaarak — ne isi par ek tasveer di. Upanishad, Buddha, ya shastriya yug ka koi Bharatiya aaj ke Bharat mein utaar diya jaaye. To wo kya dekhega? Apni kaum ko purane roopon aur chhilkon se chipka hua. Asli matlab ka nau hissa gavaaya hua. Mann ki gareebi, thahraav, vigyaan ka ruk jaana. Wo hairaan reh jaayega.

To aaj ka sawal. Kya Bharat sach mein duniya se mooh modne wala "aadhyatmik" desh tha?

Nahi. Wo zindagi se pyaar karne wala, sawal poochne wala mann tha. "Duniya se udaasin" Bharat baad mein bana, jab sawal kuch logon tak simat gaye, aur baaki ke paas sirf chhilka bacha.

Hairat. Shak. Khoj. Ek chhota ghera. Aur phir chhilka.

Par 1944 ki us kothri mein, lalten ab bhi jal rahi hai. Ek qaidi dhai hazaar saal purane sawal apni copy mein utaar raha hai.

Shayad dhaara ruki nahi thi. Bas uske kinaare baithne wale kam ho gaye the.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Yaad hai Upanishad ki wo line? Sach hi jeetta hai, jhooth nahi. Sanskrit mein: Satyameva jayate. Aaj ye humara rashtriya vaakya hai. Har sarkaari mohar par, Ashok ke sheron ke neeche.

Par Nehru ki chetaavni bhi yaad rakhiye. Shabd bach jaayein, aur unka matlab chhoot jaaye, to chhilka hi bachta hai.

Doosri baat, wo "chhota sa ghera". Aaj humare paas bhi ek rachnatmak varg hai. Vaigyanik, visheshagya, technology banane wale. Unki bhasha aksar aam aadmi tak nahi pahunchti. Aur aam aadmi tak pahunchta hai unka sasta, toota hua roop. Forward kiya hua message.

Nehru ki kasauti seedhi thi. Ye varg bahumat ko saath le kar chale, to sabhyata badhti hai. Rishta toote, to dono sookhte hain.

Aapke liye ek sawal: aap kis ghere mein hain? Aur aapne pichhli baar kis puraane "sach" se sawal poocha tha?
