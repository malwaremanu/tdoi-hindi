## Cold open

[SFX: raat ke jhingur, bahut dheeme; beech mein door se ek mandir ki ghanti]

Ahmadnagar ka qila. Raat. Aangan mein chaandni phaili hui hai. Poornima hai — poore chaand ki raat.

Ek kothri ki khidki se roshni aati hai. Andar ek aadmi baitha hai. Saamne kaagaz hai, aur ek kalam.

Wo kalam uthata hai. Par likhne se pehle wo ruk jaata hai.

Kyunki usne aaj ka din gina hai.

Aaj Vaishakh ki poornima hai. Vaishakh — garmi ka mahina, May ya June ke aas-paas. Aur aaj ka din hai do hazaar chaar sau athaasi.

Ye ginti aaj ki nahi hai. Ye ek aadmi ke guzarne se shuru hui.

Aur us aadmi ke baare mein teen baatein kahi jaati hain.

Ki wo issi poornima ko paida hua tha.

Ki issi poornima ko usse wo samajh mili, jiske liye wo barson bhatka tha.

Aur issi poornima ko, bahut saal baad, usne aankhein band kar li.

Ek hi din. Ek hi chaand. Janm, gyaan, aur ant.

## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye kitaab likh raha hai. Pichhli baar hum ek naujawan ke saath paidal Takshashila pahunche the. Us safar ke aakhir mein Banaras ke paas ek hiran-van ka zikr tha. Wahan ek aadmi apni pehli baat kehne wala tha. Wahi aadmi aaj humare saamne hai.

## Part 1: Bagi rajkumar

Paanch sau saal Isa se pehle, uttar Bharat mein ek chhota raajya hai.

Uske rajkumar ka naam hai Siddhartha. Siddhartha — Buddha ka bachpan ka naam.

Ghar mein sab kuch hai. Mahal. Baag. Aaram.

Par ye naujawan khush nahi hai. Uske andar kuch aur chal raha hai. Nehru likhte hain, wo andar ki badi ladaiyan lada.

Phir ek din wo mahal chhod deta hai. Apne shareer ko satata hai. Bhookha rehta hai.

Aur ek ped ke neeche baithkar usse wo cheez mil jaati hai jise wo dhoondh raha tha. Us din ke baad wo Siddhartha nahi rehta. Buddha ban jaata hai.

Wo assee saal ki umar tak jeete hain, aur book kehti hai ki unka dehaant 544 BC mein hua. Usi saal se Buddhist era shuru hota hai.

Itihaas-kar pehle 487 BC kehte the. Par aaj wahi log purani tareekh ko zyada sahi maan rahe hain.

Buddha ek Kshatriya the — yaani usi varg se, jisme raja aur yoddha aate hain. Aur usi varg ka ye aadmi khada hokar pujariyon ke khilaaf bolta hai.

Wo lok-priya dharm par hamla karta hai. Andhvishwas par. Karmkand par. Aur us pujari-varg par, jiske saath in sab ka apna swarth juda hua tha.

Wo metaphysics chhodta hai. Chamatkar. Aasman se aaye sandesh. Devtaon aur bhoot-pret se lena-dena.

Uske sahare teen cheezein hain: tark, reason, aur apna anubhav. Zor naitikta par hai.

Uske dekhne ka dhang hai man ka vishleshan — ek man, jisme aatma naam ki koi cheez nahi.

Nehru kehte hain ki uski poori baat aisi hai. Jaise band kamre ki basi hawa ke baad pahadon se aayi taazi hawa.

Buddha ki kahani ne unhe bachpan se khincha. Ek angrez ki likhi lambi kavita unki pasandeeda kitaab thi.

Bade hokar wo apne praant mein Buddha se judi jagahon tak raasta ghuma lete the.

Kahan paida hue — Nepal ki sarhad ke paas. Kahan baithkar unhe gyaan mila — Gaya, us Bodhi ped ke neeche. Kahan unhone pehla updesh diya. Aur kahan unhone aankhein hamesha ke liye band kar lin.

Par asli sawal yahi hai. Us zindagi mein unhone kaha kya?

Aur ek baat to aur bhi ajeeb thi. Buddha chahte the ki log unki baat maanein hi nahi. Pehle parkhein.

## Part 2: Sona aag mein

Kalpana kijiye ek sunaar ki dukaan.

Aag jal rahi hai. Ek aadmi sone ka tukda aag mein pakad kar rakhta hai. Wo use tapata hai, thanda karta hai, phir dekhta hai ki andar asli sona bacha kya.

Buddha apne raaste ke liye yahi tasveer dete hain.

Unka kehna tha: meri baat ko sirf shraddha se mat maano. Pehle parkho, jaise sone ko aag mein parkha jaata hai.

Hazaar aadmiyon ko yuddh mein harane se bada vijeta wo hai jo khud ko jeet le.

Aadmi jaati se neech ya Brahman nahi banta. Wo apne karm se banta hai.

Kisi ne paap kiya hai, to uske ghaav par namak kaun chhidkega?

Aur jeet? Jeet nafrat ko janm deti hai. Kyunki hara hua aadmi dukhi rehta hai.

Aur ye sab unhone kaha — bina kisi dharm ki chhaya, bina God ka zikr, bina doosri duniya ka laalach.

Wo kehte hain: sach apne mann mein dhoondo. Raah tark par chalti hai, anubhav par chalti hai. Aur Ignorance saare dukh ka jad hai.

Aur Bhagwan? Kya wo hai, kya nahi?

Buddha iska jawab dete hi nahi. Na haan, na na. Jahan gyaan mumkin nahi, wahan hamara faisla latka rehna chahiye.

Ek shishya ne poocha: aap is absolute ki baat kyun nahi karte? Jawab saaf tha. Jo cheez har jaani hui cheez se juda hui ho, uska saboot kisi jaane hue tark se nahi mil sakta. Poori duniya, jaise hum use jaante hain, aapas ke rishton ka jaal hai.

To hum wahan tak chalte hain, jahan tak dekh sakte hain.

Sochiye. Us zamane mein Bharat ka mann aatma se bhara tha — monism, poorva-janm, parlok. Aur is aadmi ne in sab par chupchaap parda daal diya.

Iske badle wo ek aur cheez par zor dete hain: niyam. Ek aisa niyam, jo sab jagah chalta hai. Kaaran aur nateeja.

Nehru ek hairat ki baat likhte hain. Ye soch aaj ki physics ke naye khayalon ke itna kareeb kyun hai?

Aur mann? Buddha mann ko shareer ka hissa maante hain. Aadmi man ki halaton ka guchha hai. Self ek dhaara hai — vichaaron ki dhaara.

Ek line mein unhone yahi rakha: hum jo kuch hain, wo humare sochne ka nateeja hai.

Par ek sawal bacha hua hai. Agar sach yahan hai, to dukh ka jawab kya hai?

Buddha ka jawab ek ginti se shuru hota hai. Aansuon ki ginti se.

## Part 3: Chaar samundar se zyada aansu

Wo apne shishyon se kehte hain: is lambi yatra mein tum bahut roye ho.

Tumhara jo hissa tha, wahi chhinta raha. Aur jise tum pyaar karte the, wo tumhara nahi tha.

Aur us dukh mein tumne itne aansu bahaye hain — itne ki chaaron bade samundaron ka saara paani kam pad jaaye.

Chaaron samundar. Aur ek aadmi ke aansu.

Buddha yahi dukh le kar aate hain, aur uske chaar hisse karte hain.

Pehla: dukh hai. Ye sach hai.

Doosra: dukh ka ek kaaran hai — hamari galat ichha.

Teesra: ye dukh ruk bhi sakta hai.

Chautha: aur rukne ka ek raasta hai.

Is aakhri jagah ka naam hai nirvana.

Nirvana — log ise bujh jaana samajhte hain. Jaise ek deepak bujh jaye. Buddha ne ise mana kiya.

Unka kehna tha, bujhti hui cheez ek hai: galat ichha. Par iska matlab mit jaana nahi. Wo ek tez kriya hai.

Aur dikkat ye hai ki hum ise kya kahen. Hamari bhasha iske liye bani hi nahi. Isliye hum ise sirf "aisa nahi, waisa nahi" keh sakte hain.

Ab raasta kya hai? Do kinaare, aur beech mein ek raasta.

Ek kinaara hai khane-peene aur bhog-vilas mein doob jaana. Doosra kinaara hai shareer ko tarasa kar thukra dena.

Buddha ne khud doosra kinaara chuna tha. Wo bhookhe rehte, shareer satate. Aur wahi anubhav sikhata hai: jo apni shakti kho chuka hai, wo sahi raah par nahi chal sakta.

Isliye unka raasta beech ka raasta hai.

Us beech ke raaste ke aath hisse hain. Aath "sahi".

Sahi vishwas. Sahi ichha. Sahi bhasha. Sahi karm. Sahi kamai. Sahi koshish. Sahi dhyan. Aur sahi thahraav.

Yahan koi devta kuch nahi deta, koi kripa nahi barasti. Sab kuch ek cheez par tika hai: apne aap ko sudhaarna.

Aur agar koi is raah par chalta hua khud ko jeet leta hai, to uske baad kuch nahi bachta.

Buddha ki apni ek line hai. Us aadmi ki jeet ko devta bhi haare mein nahi badal sakta, jo khud ko jeet chuka ho.

To jawab mil gaya? Dukh hai, kaaran hai, ant hai, raasta hai.

Par ek baat hai jo Buddha ne jaan-boojh kar nahi batayi. Aur jab unse poochha gaya, to unhone ek mutthi bhar patte utha liye.

## Part 4: Mutthi bhar patte

[SFX: sookhe patte — ek mutthi, halke se girte hue]

Sharad ka mausam hai. Patte jhad rahe hain.

Buddha ke haath mein sookhe patte hain. Wo apne sabse pyaare shishya se poochhte hain:

Ananda — Buddha ka sabse pyaara shishya. In patton ke alawa, aur patte hain?

Ananda kehte hain: bhagwan, autumn ke patte har taraf gir rahe hain. Inhe gina nahi ja sakta.

Phir Buddha kehte hain: bilkul waise hi, maine tumhe mutthi bhar sach diye hain. Inke alawa hazaaron sach aur hain, jinhe gina nahi ja sakta.

Ek mutthi bhar. Baaki sab sach wahi the, jo wo ji sakte the, keh nahi sakte the.

Ab kahani se hatt kar, ek aadmi apni aankhon se ye sab dekh raha hai.

Nehru likhte hain ki kai vihaaron mein unhone ek shaant, adhyayan mein doobi hawa dekhi. Chehron par sukoon tha, ek gambhirta, duniya ki fikron se halki doori.

Unke mann mein sawal utha: kya ye zindagi ke saath chalta hai? Ya ye bhaagne ka raasta hai?

Nehru ka jawab imaandaar hai. Buddha ka udasi-bhara rukh unke swabhav se match nahi karta tha; zindagi se mooh modna unhe pasand nahi thi.

Wo andar se kisi hadd tak ek "pagan" hain. Zindagi ki bharpoori unhe pasand hai, aur jhagde se wo bahut peeche nahi hat-te.

Kya Buddha ka raasta nakaaraatmak tha? Bahut log kehte hain.

Par Nehru kehte hain: Buddha ke baare mein sochte hue mujhe aisa kuch mehsoos nahi hota. Aur jis dharm ki jadein sirf nirasata mein hoti, wo karod logon ko itne mazbooti se bandh nahi sakta tha. Aur un logon mein apne zamane ke sabse pratibha-shaali log the.

Aur ye baat unhone ek murti ko dekhte hue mehsoos ki.

Buddha ki murti. Kamal par baethe. Shaant, ichha se upar. Itne door lagte hain, ki pahunchna namumkin lage.

Par phir dobara dekhiye. Un shaant chehre ke peeche kuch aur hai. Ek josh. Aankhein band hain, par unn aankhon se kuch jhaankta hai.

Jaise wo keh rahe hon: daud kar mat bhaago, shaant aankhon se saamna karo. Zindagi mein badhne ka mauka dekho.

Nehru ek angrez lekhak ka saar bhi dete hain. Unke hisaab se Buddha "shaant aur meethi garima ka poora namuna" hain — jisme har saans lene wale ke liye komalta hai.

Jis kaum ne aisa aadmi diya, uske andar gyaan aur andar ki shakti ke gehre bhandar honge.

Buddha ki baat dheere-dheere phail rahi thi. Wo shuruaat mein ek Kshatriya aandolan tha — raja aur pujari-varg ke beech ki takkar. Par uska naitik rukh aam logon ko chhoo gaya. Wo ek lok-priya sudhaar ka aandolan ban gaya. Kuch Brahman bhi uski taraf aaye.

Par zyadatar Brahmins uske khilaaf the. Wo Bauddhon ko bidhrmi kehte the.

Sawa sadi baad, iss naye dharm ko ek samraat mila. Uska naam tha Ashoka.

Par usse pehle, ek doosri cheez poori tarah taiyaar ho chuki thi.

Talwar.

## Part 5: Seema ka ek sardar

[SFX: loha loha par — ek tez, chhoti tankar]

Uttar Bharat apne hathyaron ke liye mashhoor tha. Khaaskar apne steel ke liye. Apni talwaron aur khanjaron ke liye.

Paanch sau saal Isa se pehle, ek badi Bharatiya fauj Fars ki sena ke saath Greece tak pahunchi. Ghudsawar bhi, aur paidal bhi.

Sadiyon baad, ek Farsi maha-kavita mein ek line hai. Jab Sikandar ne Fars par hamla kiya, to Farsi logon ne apni talwaron ke liye jaldi-jaldi Bharat se maang bheji.

Aur Arabi mein talwar ka ek purana shabd hai: muhannad. Matlab — "Hind se aaya hua". Ye shabd aaj bhi chalta hai.

Loha unke haath mein kuch aur bhi tha. Dilli ke paas aaj bhi lohe ka ek bahut bada khamba khada hai.

Aaj ke scientists iska jawaab nahi dhoondh paaye ki wo kaise banaya gaya. Do hazaar saal ki hawa, barish aur garmi us par kuch nahi kar paayi. Zang wahan nahi lagti.

Us khambhe par ek likhaai hai, jo Gupta script mein hai. Par kuch vidvaanon ka kehna hai ki khamba khud us likhaai se bhi purana hai.

Ab Sikandar. Yahan ek baat chaukanne wali hai.

Jise hum duniya ka bada vijeta maante hain, uska Bharat par hamla sena ke hisaab se koi bada kaam nahi tha.

Ye sarhad paar ka ek dhava tha. Aur uske liye bhi safal nahi.

Raste mein ek sarhad ka chhota-sa sardaar aisa lada ki aage badhne ka poora khayal dobara sochna pada.

Aur yahi sawal uski fauj ke dimaag mein aaya. Agar sarhad ka ek chhota raja itna lad sakta hai, to aage ke bade raajya kya karenge?

Isliye uski fauj ne aage jaane se inkaar kar diya. Wo laut gayi.

Par Sikandar ke jaane ke baad jo hua, wo iss episode ka doosra hissa hai.

Ek naujawan, aur ek buzurg guru. Dono ek saath khade hote hain. Aur ek aisa naara lagate hain, jo iss desh mein baar-baar laut kar aata hai.

## Part 6: Guru aur shishya

Ye hai 323 BC. Sikandar Babylon mein mar gaya.

Aur uski khabar pahunchte hi, Chandragupta aur Chanakya ne wo purana naara lagaya, jo har zamane mein naya hota hai. Nationalism.

Chandragupta — Maurya samrajya ka pehla samraat, jisne desh ko ek kiya.

Chanakya — uska guru, jise Kautilya bhi kaha jaata hai.

Dono ne logon ko bahar se aaye hamlavar ke khilaaf khada kiya.

Yunani fauj ka thikana ujaad diya. Taxila chhin liya. Aur nationalism ka sahara mila, to sahayak bhi milte hain.

Chandragupta ne sahayakon ke saath uttar Bharat paar kiya, aur Pataliputra ja pahuncha. Sikandar ki maut ke do saal ke andar wo shehar aur uska raajya uske haath mein tha. Maurya samrajya khada ho chuka tha.

Ab Sikandar ka apna senapati aata hai. Seleucus.

Seleucus — Sikandar ka senapati, jo uske marne par Asia Minor se Bharat tak ka hissa le baitha.

Usne Sindhu paar karke uttar-pashchim Bharat par apna raaj dobara jamane ki koshish ki.

Wo haar gaya. Aur haarke usne Afghanistan ka ek bada hissa, Kabul aur Herat tak, Chandragupta ko de diya. Usne apni beti bhi Chandragupta se byaah di.

Ab samrajya dekhiye. Arab saagar se Bengal ki khaadi tak. Uttar mein Kabul tak. Sirf dakshin Bharat chhoot gaya.

Book kehti hai: itihaas mein pehli baar itna bada raajya khada hua, jo ek jagah se chalta tha.

Maurya sena ke paas ek cheez thi, jo doosron ke paas nahi thi — trained war-elephants. Nehru unhe aaj ke tanks ke barabar rakhte hain.

302 BC mein Seleucus ne apne aage ke yuddh ke liye Bharat se paanch sau hathi liye. Itihaas-kar kehte hain, faisla inhi hathiyon ne kiya.

Par samrajya ek aadmi se nahi chalta. Uska dimaag ek aur tha.

Chanakya ke baare mein ek purane Sanskrit natak se ek tasveer nikalti hai.

Bahadur aur chaalbaaz. Ghuroor wala, badle ki bhaavna se bhara. Ek chhoti si beizzati bhi kabhi nahi bhoolta. Dushman ko dhokha dene ke liye har jugaad karta.

Samrajya ki lagaam uske haath mein thi — par wo samraat ko apna maalik nahi, apna pyaara shishya maanta tha.

Nehru likhte hain ki log usse Bharat ka Machiavelli kehte hain. Machiavelli — Italy ka purana raajneeti-lekhak. Par wo usse kahin bada aadmi tha: soch mein bhi, kaam mein bhi.

Aur uski apni zindagi? Bilkul saral. Thaat-baat mein koi dilchaspi nahi. Jis din usne apna wada poora kiya, us din wo sab chhod kar sant ki tarah baith jaana chahta tha.

Kitaab ka naam hai Arthashastra — Chanakya ki likhi hui, raaj chalane ki vidya. Ye sirf ek kitaab nahi hai. Ye ek samrajya chalane ka poora naqsha hai.

## Part 7: Ek kitaab mein poora raaj

Saal 321 BC. Samrajya uttar mein Kabul tak, aur almost poora Bharat.

Sabse oopar ek autocracy thi — ek dictator.

Par uske neeche, gaon aur shehron mein, bahut si khud-mukhtari thi — chune hue buzurgon ki. Aur ye itni keemti thi, ki koi raja usme haath nahi daalta tha.

Par centre ki hukumat har jagah thi, uske haath bahut lambay. Nehru kehte hain, kuch tarah se ye Maurya raajya aaj ki dictatorship ki yaad dilata hai.

Par phir wo sacchai jodte hain: us zamane ki kheti wali arthvyavastha mein aaj jaisa control mumkin hi nahi tha. Aur ye raajya sirf ek police state bhi nahi tha.

Niyam zyadatar shehron par lagte the, gaon par kam. Practice theory se peeche chalti thi.

Arthashastra mein raja ke kartavya hain, mantriyon ke, sarkari vibhaagon ka kaam, kootniti, jang aur shanti.

Usme fauj ka poora bayan hai — paidal, ghudsawar, rath, hathi. Par Chanakya jodte hain: anushasan ke bina badi fauj bojh ban jaati hai.

Phir wo kitaab rok-roj ki zindagi par aati hai:

Vyapaar. Bazaar. Adalat. Kanoon. Shaadi. Talaq. Aurton ke adhikaar. Vidhwa ka doosra vivaah. Khanan. Jansankhya. Paasport. Jail.

Ek zikr China ke resham ka bhi hai — chinapatta. Aur kitaab China ke resham aur Bharatiya resham mein farq batati hai. Yaani chauthi sadi BC tak China se vyapaar chal raha tha.

Nehru ko ek cheez khaas lagti hai — raja ka shapath.

Taj pehna jaate waqt raja ko kehna padta tha: agar main tum par zulm karun, to mujhe swarg se haath dhona pade. Jeevan se bhi, aur santaan se bhi.

Aur aage: raja ki khushi apni praja ki khushi mein hai. Jo raja ko achha lage, par praja ko na lage, wo achha nahi.

Ek aur line hai: jo raja urja wala ho, uski praja bhi urja wali hogi. Aur jan-hit ka kaam raja ke aaram par nahi ruk sakta tha.

Aur agar raja galat kare, to logon ko haq tha ki wo use hata dein, aur doosra baitha dein.

Sichai ka ek poora vibhaag tha. Aur ek navigation ka vibhaag — bandargah, ghaat, pul, naav. Inhi naavon se maal Burma tak jaata tha. Aur ek navy bhi thi.

Yunani duniya se sambandh the — Seleucus ke vaaris, aur Misr ke Ptolemy. In rishton ki neev thi dono taraf ka vyapaar.

Ek Yunani lekhak ne likha ki Central Asia ki Oxus nadi ek lambi zanjeer ki kadi thi. Usi raaste se Bharatiya maal Caspian aur Black Sea hote hue Europe pahunchta tha. Teesri sadi BC mein ye raasta chala.

Us zamane ka Central Asia hara bhara tha. Hazaar saal baad wo sookhne laga.

Arthashastra mein ek chhota-sa zikr hai. Raja ke astabal mein "Arabi ghode" khade the.

Ab sawal yahi hai. Ek kitaab mein poora raaj tha — par us raaj ka sabse bada shehar aankhon se kaisa dikhta tha?

Us shehar ke dwar par ek pardesi khada hai. Aur wo sab kuch dekh kar likh raha hai.

## Part 8: Lakdi ka shehar

Pataliputra. Aaj ka Patna.

Ye shehar do nadiyon ke sangat par baitha hai — Ganga aur Sone.

Kalpana kijiye ek Yunani doot. Megasthenes — Seleucus ka bheja doot, jisne Pataliputra apni aankhon se dekha.

Wo shehar ke bahar wali khai par khada hai. Uski nazar deewar par jaati hai.

Lakdi ki deewar. Usme jhiriyan hain — teer chalane ke liye. Deewar par burj hain, aur dwar. Book kehti hai: paanch sau se zyada burj, chausath dwar.

Shehar ki lambai lagbhag nau meel hai. Aur saamne wali khai sirf suraksha ke liye nahi hai — usme shehar ki gandagi bhi girti hai.

Deewar hi nahi, ghar bhi lakdi ke hain. Iske peeche ek soch hai — bhookamp.

Wo ilaaka bhookamp ke liye mashhoor tha. Aur 1934 mein aaye Bihar ke bade bhookamp ne aaj bhi ye baat yaad dila di.

Aur kyunki sab lakdi ka tha, aag se bachne ke niyam sakht the. Har ghar mein seedhi, aankde, aur paani se bhari matke.

Par sabse badi baat deewar nahi hai.

Shehar mein ek nagar-sabha thi — logon ne chuni hui.

Tees sadasya. Chhe samitiyaan. Paanch-paanch log ek samiti mein.

Ek samiti karigari aur karkhane dekhti hai, ek janm-mrityu ki ginti, ek banayi hui cheezon ki nigrani, aur ek yaatriyon ke intezaam ki.

Aur poori sabha? Paisa, safai, paani, sarkari imaarat, aur baag.

Aaj sochiye. Shehar ki safai, paani aur bazaar ka kaam aaj bhi koi chuni hui sabha dekhti hai. Do hazaar saal pehle wahi kaam isi tarah bantta tha.

Samrajya ke door-door ke hisse sadakon se jude the, aur raaston mein musafiron ke liye aaraam-ghar the. Sabse badi sadak, King's Way, rajdhani se uttar-pashchim ki sarhad tak jaati thi.

Pardesi vyapaariyon ka khaas dhyan rakha jaata tha. Unhe kuch aise haq milte the, jaise ek doosre desh ke mehmaan ko.

Aur vyapaar ka phailav? Misr mein mummy ko Bharatiya muslin mein lapeta jaata tha, aur kapde par Bharatiya indigo ka rang chadta tha. Kaanch bhi mila.

Yahan luxury bhi badh rahi thi — saraayein, bhojan-ghar, jua-khana, guild ki dawatein. Aur manoranjan? Naachne wale, gaane wale, nat — gaon-gaon tak jaate the.

Arthashastra ke likhne wale ko ye pasand nahi tha — ek common hall zindagi ko bitaa deta hai. Par public entertainment mein jo help na kare, use jurmana lagta hai.

Raja apni taraf se manch banwata tha — naatak, mukka-yuddh, jaanwar-chaudharon ke muqable. Tyohaar par raaton ko galiyaan roshan hoti thin.

Megasthenes ek chhoti baat bhi note karta hai. Bharat ke log sajne-dhajne aur sundarta se pyaar karte the. Wo joote tak batate hain, jo aadmi ki lambai badha de.

Aur usi samrajya ke ek aadmi ne ek din ek faisla liya. Usne use patthar par khudwakar duniya ko suna diya.

## Part 9: Kalinga ke baad

Chandragupta ke bete Bindusara ke zamane mein bhi Pataliputra mein pardesi doot aate rahe — Misr ka Ptolemy, aur Antiochus.

Unka beta tha Ashoka — Chandragupta ka pote.

Ashoka ke zamane mein Bharat ek bada international centre ban gaya — kaaran tha Buddha dharm ka phailav.

Aur Ashoka ki baat hume kaise pata chali? Usne khud likhwaya.

Uske edict patthar par khude, dhaatu par khude — aur aaj bhi hamare paas hain.

Wo sirf apne logon ke liye nahi the — aane wale logon ke liye bhi.

Aur ek edict mein ek line hai, jo do hazaar saal baad bhi hila deti hai.

Usme Ashoka likhte hain: ab wo koi hatya nahi sahega. Kalinga mein maare gaye aur bandi banaye gaye logon ke sauven ya hazaarven hisse jitna bhi nahi.

Kalinga — aaj ka Odisha.

Ab suniye, asli jeet kya hai. Edict kehta hai: asli vijay dilon ki hoti hai, aur wo kartavya ke dharm se.

Aur Ashoka jodte hain: aisi asli jeetein unhone pehle hi jeet li hain. Apne raajya mein bhi, door ke raajyon mein bhi.

Yahin cold open ke do shabd lautte hain.

Pehla: hazaar aadmiyon ko yuddh mein harane se bada vijeta wo hai jo khud ko jeet le.

Doosra: jeet nafrat ko janm deti hai.

Ashoka ne ye samjha nahi tha kitaab se. Usne ye talwar se seekha tha.

Par Ashoka bhaag kar bhikshu nahi bane the — wo ek samraat the jo pahle se zyada kaam kar raha tha.

Uske apne shabdon mein: jab main kha raha hoon, jab antahpur mein hoon, jab apne kamre mein, apni gaadi mein, apne baag mein hoon — sarkari report likhne wale mujhe logon ke kaam ki khabar dete rehna.

Kyunki — har samay, har jagah, mujhe janta ke kaam karne hain.

Pashchim mein wo wahan tak gaye, jahan Sikandar ki apni duniya thi. Misr tak. Macedonia tak. Poorab mein Burma aur Siam tak.

Aur dakshin mein Lanka? Wahan koi doot nahi gaya — apne bete aur beti ko bheja, Mahendra aur Sanghamitra.

Kahin bhi kisi par zor nahi dala gaya. Baat ki gayi mann se, aur dil se. Aur Bauddh hokar bhi usne baaki dharmon ka poora aadar rakha.

Isi liye Buddha dharm Kashmir se Ceylon tak phailta hai. Nepal tak. Aur baad mein Tibet, China aur Mongolia tak.

Bharat ke andar dheere-dheere ek badlaav aata hai. Maans kam hua, sharaab kam hui, pashu-bali band ho gayi. Aur us zamane mein Brahman aur Kshatriya dono maans khaate the, sharaab peete the.

Vyapaar phir se phailta hai. Central Asia ke Khotan mein ek Bharatiya basti banti hai, aur Taxila mein bahar se vidyarthi badhte hain.

To is episode ka sawal. Asli jeet kya hai — talwar ki, ya apne aap ki?

Ashoka ke paas dono the. Ek taraf wahi talwar, jisne duniya ka pehla bada samrajya khada kiya. Doosri taraf wo mutthi bhar sach, jo Buddha sookhe patte le kar aaya tha.

Usne talwar se sab jeet kar ye seekha ki jeet hi apne andar nafrat bo deti hai.

Jo jeet dilon par hoti hai, wahi tikti hai. Baaki sab ek din patthar ke khandhar ban jaati hai.

Par Ashoka ne ek aur cheez chhodi, jo patthar ki nahi thi. Wo lakdi ki thi.

## Part 10: Do hazaar saal ki lakdi

[SFX: sookhi lakdi par ungli — halki, chikni khurak]

Pehle Ashoka ka doosra chehra. Wo ek bada banane wala aadmi tha.

Kuch vidvaanon ka kehna hai ki usne badi imaartein banane ke liye bahar se karigar bulaye. Uske kuch khambhe Fars ke shahron jaise hain. Par usi kaam mein Bharatiya kaarigari ki chhaap dikhti hai.

Kareeb tees saal pehle, khudai karne wale ek angrez afsar ne uske Pataliputra wale mahal ka ek bahu-khamba hall nikaala.

Usne report mein likha: ye shah-bal aise chikne aur poore the, jaise aaj hi rakhe gaye hon. Do hazaar saal se zyada purane. Jodon ki lakeerein tak alag nahi dikhti, itni bariki ki aaj bhi usse behtar banaya jaana mumkin nahi.

Aise shah-bal doosre ilaakon mein bhi mile — bahut achhi halat mein.

India mein ye zyada hairani ki baat hai: yahan hawa lakdi ko kha jaati hai, keede us par toot padte hain. Zaroor koi khaas treatment hua hoga — wo kya tha, aaj tak raaz hai.

Patna aur Gaya ke beech Nalanda hai, jo aage mashhoor hui. Ashoka ke zamane mein uska zikr nahi.

Ashoka ne 232 BC mein dehaant diya — ek-chalis saal ka raaj.

Ek angrez itihaas-kar, H G Wells, ne likha: itihaas ke panno par hazaaron rajaon ke naam bhare hain. In sab ke beech Ashoka ka naam akela chamakta hai.

Volga se Japan tak uska naam aaj bhi izzat se liya jaata hai. Uski yaad utne log rakhte hain, jitne Constantine ya Charlemagne ka naam sun nahi paaye.

Ab lautiye us jagah, jahan se ye set shuru hua. Set shuru hua tha Mohenjo-daro ki ret se, aur un deewaron se jo har peedhi thodi oonchi hoti gayi. Set khatam hota hai us lakdi par, jo do hazaar saal se nahi gali.

To Nehru ne jo khoj ki, usme mila kya?

Ek jaati? Nahi. Jaati tooti, aur badalti rahi.

Ek dharm? Nahi. Dharm hazaaron rukhon mein phaila.

Mila ek sabhyati, jo aane wale har naye log ko apne andar sama leti hai. Aur phir apne hi andar deewarein bana leti hai. Ek mann, jo har cheez par sawal poochta rehta hai. Ek janta, jo kaam karti hai, gaati hai, nachti hai.

Aur ek samrajya, jisne talwar se sab jeet kar seekha, ki jeet apne aap ki hoti hai.

Ret sookhi rahi. Deewar oonchi hoti rahi. Aur lakdi nahi gali.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai.

Aaj humare desh ke jhande par ek pahiya hai. Aur sarkari muhar par ek stambh ke oopar baethe chaar sher. Ye Ashoka ke hi kaam ki nishaniyan hain.

Ek samraat, jisne patthar par khudwaya tha: asli vijay dilon ki hoti hai.

Aur hum? Hum apni jeet ka hisaab lagane baithein, to hum kya ginnte hain? Kitne hare, kitne maare?

Ya hum ye ginnte hain ki humne apne aap par kya jeeta?

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aaj aapki zindagi mein jo sabse badi jeet hui hai — usme aapne kisi ko hara, ya khud ko?

## Outro

Maurya samrajya dheere-dheere bikhar gaya. Par uske baad ek cheez aisi hui, jo Nehru ko bahut ajeeb lagti hai.

Uttar-pashchim se phir Yunani aaye. Par is baar ek Yunani doot ne, Sanskrit mein, ek khamba khada kiya.

Kyun?

Yahin se shuru hota hai Chapter 5 — "Through the Ages". Agli baar hum wahi khamba dekhenge. Aur usse poochenge: itne hamlavar aaye, itni takkarein huin — phir bhi Bharat, Bharat kaise bana raha?

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
