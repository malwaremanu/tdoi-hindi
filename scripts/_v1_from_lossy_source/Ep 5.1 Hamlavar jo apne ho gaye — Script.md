## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar hum Maurya samrajya ke ant par ruke the, Ashoka ke baad, jab ek bada raaj ek chhote raaj mein badal gaya tha. Us episode ke aakhir mein ek Yunani doot ne Sanskrit mein apni baat likhwayi thi. Aaj hum usi khamba se shuru karte hain.

## Part 1: Sookhi shaakha, naya beej

Maurya samrajya dheere-dheere phika pad gaya. Uski jagah Sunga raajvansh aaya. Par Sunga raja ek chhote se ilaake ke maalik the. Na wahi seema, na wahi shaan.

Ek bada raaj chala gaya, aur ek chhota raaj baith gaya. Ye khaalipan kuch saalon ka nahi tha. Ye sadiyon ka tha.

Isi khaali waqt mein do cheezein dikhti hain.

Ek taraf, dakshin mein badi riyasatein utth rahi thi.

Doosri taraf, uttar-pashchim mein Kabul se Punjab tak ek nayi taaqat phail rahi thi. Bactria ke log. Inhe Indo-Greek kehte hain. Ya aasan bhasha mein, is seema par base hue Yunani.

Ye log kabhi is desh ke bahar the. Ab wo is desh ke andar the, aur aage badh rahe the.

Ek naam samne aata hai. Menander — un Yunanion ka raja, jo apni sena lekar Pataliputra tak pahunch gaya.

Pataliputra. Mauryon ki rajdhani. Us par khatra khada ho gaya tha.

Par Menander ko haraya gaya. Hata diya gaya.

Aur phir wahi hua, jo is chapter mein baar-baar hota hai. Haar kar lautne wale par is desh ka mahaul aise chha gaya, ki wo khud badal gaya.

Menander Bauddh ho gaya. Aur Bauddh parampara mein wo ek bade naam ban gaye. Buddha kathaon mein wo "Milinda" hain. Logon ne unhe lagbhag ek sant maan liya.

Ek raja, jisne is desh ko jeetna chaha. Aur aage chalkar wahi is desh ka ho gaya.

[SFX: gehri ghanti ki ek lambi goonj]

Dharm hi nahi badla. Do kalaayein bhi mili. Yunani shaili aur Bharatiya bhaav se ek nayi kala utthi. Gandhara ki kala. Afghanistan aur us Sarhad wale ilaake ki kala.

Ab Besnagar ka khamba yaad kijiye. Wo sirf ek purani cheez nahi hai. Wo isi kahani ki pehli tasveer hai.

Aur ye akeli misaal bhi nahi thi. Aage-peechhe aur bhi log aaye, aur unke saath bhi wahi hua.

Par ek videshi raja ka Bauddh ban jaana sirf ek baat thi. Doosri baat zyada badi thi. Buddh dharm khud do hisson mein toot gaya.

## Part 2: Do raah

Kushan kaal mein ek badi phoot padi. Buddh dharm do hisson mein baant diya gaya. Mahayan aur Hinayan.

Do naam, aur ek hi dharm. Par ab ye dharm ek nahi raha. Jo ek baar do hisson mein bant gaya, use phir jodna aasan nahi hota.

Nehru yahan ek baat par rukte hain. Ye ladai talwaron se nahi ladi gayi.

Bharat ka apna dhang tha. Vivaad ko badi sabhaon mein rakha jaata tha. Door-door se log aate the. Sunte the. Bolte the. Phir faisla hota.

Kashmir us samrajya ke lagbhag beech mein tha. Isliye wahi us behes aur sanskritik kaam-kaaj ka kendra ban gaya.

Behes ek jagah shuru hoti thi. Par uski goonj door tak jaati thi.

Ek naam is behes se sabse oopar nikalta hai. Nagarjuna — pehli sadi ke mahan Bauddh vidvaan, jinhone Mahayan ke siddhant ghaale.

Unki buddhi tez thi, aur himmat bhi badi. Nehru likhte hain ki Bharat mein Mahayan ki jeet ka bada hissa unhi ka hai. Unke apne darshan ki kahani hum aage, Ep 5.4 mein sunenge.

Nateeja? Do raaste do deshon mein baant gaye. Mahayan China tak pahuncha. Aur Lanka aur Burma Hinayan par tik gaye.

Aur raja kaun the? Kushan — wo raajvansh, jo bahar se aaya tha, par khud ko poori tarah Bharatiya bana chuka tha.

Wo kala ke bade aashraydaata ban gaye. Vihar, murteein, vidya — sab unke daur mein badhe.

Ek videshi raajvansh, aur kala ko saambhalne wala haath. Ye bhi wahi purana dhang tha.

Par Nehru ek doosri baat bhi likhte hain. Isi ke saath, unke raaj ke khilaaf ek chhupa hua khichaav bhi chalta raha. Ek desh-prem ka swar.

Wo swar abhi dheema tha. Par gaayab nahi hua.

Aur jab naye kabile pahaadon ke us paar se aaye, to wahi swar ek aandolan ban gaya.

## Part 3: Chautha Chandra

Chauthi sadi ki shuruaat mein naye kabile pahaadon se utar kar aane lage.

Is baar log chup nahi baithe. Bahar se aane walon ke khilaaf ek bhaav aakaar le raha tha.

Uska neta ek raja bana. Us raja ka naam bhi Chandragupt tha.

Usne naye hamla-varon ko bhaga diya. Aur ek bada, phaila hua samrajya khada kiya.

Yahin se Gupta samrajya ka daur shuru hota hai. 320 AC. Gupta — Chandragupt ka wahi naya raajvansh, jisne dedh sau saal tak uttar Bharat par raaj kiya.

Nehru likhte hain ki Gupta kaal mein ek ke baad ek bade raja aaye. Jang mein kaamyaab, aur aman ki kalaon mein bhi.

Par ye raaj sirf taaqat se nahi bana tha. Dar se bhi bana tha.

Talwar se raaj khada hota hai. Par us raaj ko andar se dar chalata hai.

Ek lamba waqt tak hamla hota raha. Isse logon ke andar ek gehra bhaav bana. Bahar wala khatra hai.

Sabse zyada asar purane Brahman aur Kshatriya vargon par pada. Unhe do cheezein bachani thi. Apni mitti, aur apni sanskriti.

Ab dhyan dijiye. Jo log pehle aa chuke the, ghul chuke the, unhe apnaya jaata tha. Par jo ab aata tha, usse ladha jaata tha.

Aur isi soch se ek koshish shuru hui. Poore desh ko ek jaisa banana. Purane Brahmanik aadarshon par.

Yahi soch aage chalkar is daur ki sabse badi taaqat banegi. Aur isi soch se iski sabse badi kamzori bhi aayegi.

Jutaane ke saath-saath isme kuch aur bhi aa gaya. Wo cheez aane wali thi. Dheere-dheere.

Is koshish mein ek cheez theek hui. Aur ek cheez bigad gayi.

## Part 4: Shell

Jo cheez bigdi, wo sabse andar ki thi.

Nehru likhte hain ki purana bharosa ja raha tha. Aur jab bharosa jaata hai, to aadarsh sakht ho jaate hain. Wo sakhti is desh ke swabhaav ki nahi thi. Par wo aa gayi.

Iske saath ek aur cheez hui. Bharat apne shell mein simatne laga. Bahar se nahi, andar se. Zameen par bhi, aur mann mein bhi.

Shell ka matlab kya hai? Ek ghar, jiska darwaza andar se band ho jaaye.

Aur jo ghar apna darwaza khud band karta hai, wo bahar ki duniya ko bhoolne lagta hai.

Nehru ka ye shabd, shell, apni jagah rakhiye. Wo Part 11 mein laut kar aayega.

Par wo shell kaisa tha?

Nehru saaf likhte hain. Wo itna gehra tha, aur itna chaura, ki usme sab samaa jaaye.

Kyunki is dharm aur darshan ka swabhaav udarta tha. Anek maton aur anek jaatiyon ke log isme jagah paate the. Samaane ka kaam chalta rehta tha.

Par ek baat badal gayi. Bahar wale ke khilaaf ye soch ab pehle se zyada tez ho gayi. Bachav ki zid ban gayi.

Aur jab rashtra-prem taaqat pakadta hai, to wo aksar samrajyvaad jaisa dikhne lagta hai.

Gupton ka daur tez tha. Roshan tha. Kala mein bhara hua tha. Par wahi daur is samrajyvaad ki taraf bahut jaldi mud gaya.

Unke ek raja ka naam Samudragupta tha. Unhe Bharat ka Napoleon kaha gaya hai.

Sahitya aur kala ki taraf se ye ek chamakdaar daur tha. Log ise chamak ka daur kehte hain, aur sahi kehte hain. Par isi chamak ke saath khatra bhi andar aa chuka tha.

## Part 5: Safed Hoon

Gupton ne uttar Bharat par lagbhag dedh sau saal raaj kiya. Phir unke baad aane wale raja aaye. Par ab wahi raja bachav par the. Samrajya sikudta gaya. Chhota hota gaya.

Kyunki pahaadon ke us paar se phir koi aa raha tha.

Unhe Safed Hoon kehte hain — Madhya Asia se aaye ek jangli lashkar, jo jahan pahuncha wahan lutiya daali.

Inhone is desh mein waisa hi kahar dhaya, jaisa Europe mein Attila kar raha tha.

Kahar ki koi sarhad nahi hoti. Jo Europe mein ho raha tha, wahi yahan ho raha tha.

Sochiye, kaisa daur tha. Ek desh, jise baar-baar log tod rahe hain. Aur ek desh, jo phir bhi tikta hai.

Unke kaale kaam jab poore desh tak pahunche, to ek cheez jaagi. Ek saath. Ek jut.

Yashovarman ke neeche riyasaton ka ek sangh bana — wo sardar, jiske neeche rajaon ka sangh Hoonon par toota.

Unn par ek saath hamla hua. Hoonon ki taaqat toot gayi. Aur unka sardaar Mihiragula bandi bana liya gaya.

Ab is kahani ka sabse ajeeb mod aata hai.

Gupta vansh ka ek raja zinda tha. Baladitya. Usne Mihiragula ke saath waisa vyavhaar kiya, jaisa is desh ki reet kehti thi. Udaarta.

Usse jaane diya gaya.

Par Mihiragula ne badle mein kya kiya?

Wo chupchaap laut kar aaya. Aur apne hi meherbaan par ek chhupa hua hamla kiya.

Ye chhota waqia hai. Isme is desh ke bharose ka poora itihaas hai.

Ek aadmi ki udaarta. Aur usi aadmi ka dhokha.

Phir bhi ek baat yaad rakhiye. Hoonon ka raaj yahan bahut lamba nahi chala. Aadhi sadi, ya usse bhi kam.

Aur unmein se bahut se yahin reh gaye. Chhote-chhote sardar ban kar. Kabhi upadrav karte. Aur dheere-dheere is desh ke samundar mein ghul gaye.

## Part 6: Kanauj ka raja

Saatvin sadi ki shuruaat mein wahi bache hue sardar phir sarkash ho gaye.

Is baar unhe Kanauj ke raja ne kuchla. Harshavardhana — Kanauj ka raja, jisne uttar aur madhya Bharat ko mila kar ek lamba samrajya khada kiya.

Wo dharm mein Bauddh the. Par unka Bauddh dharm Mahayan tha. Wahi dhaara, jo kai maayno mein Hindu parampara ke kareeb thi.

Yahan do dharmon ko lada nahi gaya. Dono ko jagah di gayi. Dono ko badhaya gaya.

Do dharm, ek raaj. Ye bhi is desh ka purana dhang tha.

Unke zamane mein ek mehmaan door se aaya. Ek Chinese yaatri. Hsuan Tsang. 629 AC.

Uska aana aage ek lambi sadak kholta hai, jis par hum is set mein baad mein chalenge.

Harsha khud kavi the. Natak likhte the. Unke aas-paas kalaakaar aur kavi ikattha the. Unki rajdhani, Ujjayini, sanskritik kaam ka ek bada kendra ban gayi.

Aur 648 AC mein Harsha chale gaye.

Nehru yahan ek baat par ruk jaate hain. Theek usi waqt, Arab ke registaan se ek nayi taaqat utth rahi thi. Islam. Wo aage Africa aur Asia mein tezi se phailne wali thi.

Zara sochiye, kya waqt tha. Ek taraf ek raja kala aur sahitya ko ikattha kar raha tha. Doosri taraf, ek naya dharm apna raasta bana raha tha.

Ek hi zamane ki do kahaniyan. Ek andar ki, ek bahar ki.

Uttar Bharat ka darwaza dheere-dheere band ho raha tha. Aur bahar ek poora naya daur khul raha tha.

Par ab hum ek pal ke liye uttar chhod kar dakshin chalte hain. Wahan kahani bilkul alag hai.

## Part 7: Samundar ki taaqat

Dakshin mein Maurya samrajya ka koi ant nahi aaya. Wo wahan kabhi poora phaila hi nahi tha.

Ek hazaar saal se zyada tak, dakshin mein badi riyasatein phalti-phoolti rahi. Ek hazaar saal. Ye chhota waqt nahi hai.

Is lambi avadhi mein dakshin ne apna ek poora jahan bana liya.

Pehle Andhras — dakshin ki wo badi taaqat, jisne Shakon ko haraya. Baad mein Kushan unke samkaalin ban gaye.

Phir pashchim mein Chalukya samrajya aaya. Uske baad Rashtrakuta.

Unse aur dakshin mein Pallav — dakshin ki wo riyasat, jisne samundar paar ja kar bastiyan basayi.

Aur phir Chola samrajya. Uska phailaav poore peninsula par tha. Lanka tak. Aur dakshin Burma tak. Unka aakhri bada raja Rajendra tha. Unki mrityu 1044 AC mein hui.

Par ye taaqat kheti se nahi, samundar se aayi thi. Samundar is riyasat ki sadak thi.

[SFX: samundar ki lehrein; door se ek jahaaz ki rassi kheenchne ki aawaaz]

Dakshin apne badhiya utpaadon ke liye jaana jaata tha. Aur apne samundari vyapaar ke liye. Ye sea-power the. Unke jahaaz door-door deshon mein maal le jaate the.

Yahan Greekon ki bastiyan bhi thi. Yahan Roman sikke bhi mile hain. Aur Chalukya rajaon ne Persia ke Sassanid rajaon se doot bheje. Doot dono taraf se aate-jaate rahe.

Ab uttar ki taraf laut kar ek sawal dekhiye.

Uttar mein hamla ke baad hamla hota raha. Rajvansh badle. Bazaar jalte rahe.

Dakshin par in hamlon ka seedha asar nahi pada.

Par "seedha nahi" ka matlab "koi asar nahi" nahi hota.

Jab uttar mein aag lagti thi, to log bhaagte the. Kaun bhaagta tha? Karigar. Mistri. Chitrakar.

Wo dakshin mein ja bas gaye.

Nateeja? Dakshin purani kala ki parampara ka ghar ban gaya. Aur uttar, naye logon aur nayi hawaon ke saath, badalta raha.

Aage aane wali sadiyon mein ye kaam aur tez hua. Aur dakshin kattar Hindu parampara ka bada gadh ban gaya.

## Part 8: Do sau saal ki sarkar

Yahan Nehru humein rok kar ek baat kehte hain.

Hamlaon ki lambi fehrist padh kar ek galat tasveer ban jaati hai. Jaise is desh ka itihaas sirf jang aur toot-phoot ka itihaas ho.

Wo tasveer galat hai. Ye daur ek hazaar saal se zyada lamba hai. Aur is lambi avadhi mein is desh ne lambe-lambe saal aman aur intezaam ke saath guzare.

Ek ginti dekhiye. Maurya. Kushan. Gupta. Aur dakshin mein Andhra, Chalukya, Rashtrakuta.

Inmein se har ek do-so-so-tees sau saal chala.

Nehru ek tulna karte hain, jo aaj bhi chamakti hai. Inmein se har ek, is desh mein Angrezon ke raaj se zyada lamba chala. Angrezon ka raaj tab tak utna purana nahi hua tha.

Aur inmein se lagbhag saare raajvansh isi mitti ke the.

Nateeja? Raaj badalte the, par zameen wahi rehti thi.

Jo bahar se aaye, jaise Kushan, wo bhi jaldi is desh ke dhang mein dhal gaye. Wo yahan ke raja ban kar raaj karte the. Unki jadein isi mitti mein thi.

Seema par chhoti-moti jhankiyan hoti rahi. Bagal ki riyasaton mein kabhi takraar hoti rahi. Par aam haal aman ka hi tha.

Aur raja log kis baat par garv karte the? Kala par. Sahitya par. Vidya par.

Ye kaam ek seema tak mehdood nahi rehta tha. Kyunki poore Bharat ka sanskritik aur sahityik aadhaar ek hi tha.

Isi liye koi bhi dharmik ya daarshnik vivaad turant phail jaata tha. Poorab se pashchim tak. Uttar se dakshin tak. Sab jagah usi par behes hoti.

Aur jang bhi waisi nahi thi jaisi hum sochte hain. Jang ke waqt bhi aam aadmi ka kaam band nahi hota tha. Sena nikal jaati thi, aur gaon ka kaam chalta rehta tha.

Yahan tak ki likhit samjhautey bhi mile hain. Ladne wale raja, aur gaon ki khud-hukumat wali sabhaon ke sardar. Dono ke beech likha jaata tha: fasal ko nuksaan nahi pahunchayenge. Aur galti se pahunch gaya, to bharpaayi denge.

Zara sochiye. Ek likha hua vaada, jisme jang ke pakshe kisaan ki fasal ki fikar karte hain.

Par is niyam ki ek seema bhi thi. Ye desh ke andar ki ladaiyon ke liye tha. Bahar se aane wali senaon ke liye nahi. Aur jahan asli satta ki ladai thi, wahan bhi nahi.

## Part 9: Jang ke niyam

In jangon ke niyam kahan se aaye? Is desh ki purani soch se.

Jo jang dharm ke liye hai, wo dharm se ladni chahiye. Isme koi galat tareeka nahi chalega.

Kitna amal hua, wo alag sawal hai. Par niyam likhe hue the, aur bahut saaf. Niyam likhna aasan hai. Un par chalna mushkil.

Zeher wale teer mana the. Chhupi hui hathiyar mana thi.

Jo so raha hai, use nahi maro. Jo bhaag kar sharan mein aaya hai, use nahi maro. Jo haath jod kar khada hai, use nahi maro.

Aur ye sun kar aaj hairat hoti hai. Achhi imaaratein girayi nahi jaayengi. Ek jang, jisme sundarta ko bachaya jaata tha.

Par ye soch apne waqt se aage nahi chal rahi thi. Chanakya ke waqt mein isme daraar aa chuki thi. Chanakya — Maurya rajaon ke mahamantri, Arthashastra ke rachayak.

Wo saaf likhte hain. Agar shatru ko harane ke liye zyada tabahi wale, chhal-pooran tareeke zaroori hain, to unhe apnaya ja sakta hai.

Aur yahi nahi. Arthashastra mein wo aise yantra bhi batate hain, jo ek saath sau aadmiyon ko maar saken. Kuch aise vismakaari padarthon ka bhi zikr hai. Aur trench jang ka bhi.

Ye asli mein kya the, Nehru kehte hain, aaj batana mushkil hai. Shayad ye sirf purani kathaon ke jaadui karname the. Ye maanne ka koi aadhaar nahi ki iska matlab barood tha.

To ye daur jang ka tha ya aman ka?

Nehru yahan ek badi baat likhte hain. Is desh ke itihaas mein bure daur aaye. Aag. Talwar. Akaal. Andar ka intezaam toot gaya.

Par poore itihaas par nazar daaliye, to ek baat dikhti hai. Is desh ne Europe se kahin zyada lambe daur aman aur intezaam mein guzare.

Ye Turk aur Afghan hamlon ke baad ki sadiyon par bhi laagu hoti hai. Aur us waqt tak bhi, jab Moghul samrajya toot raha tha.

Aaj sochiye. Ek soch aaj bhi chalti hai. Ki Angrezon ne pehli baar is desh ko aman aur intezaam diya. Nehru ise sabse ajeeb bhram kehte hain. Pax Britannica, yaani Angrezon ka aman.

Sach kya hai? Jab Angrezon ka raaj shuru hua, tab ye desh apne sabse neeche nuqte par tha. Andar sab bikhar chuka tha.

Aur yahi ek wajah thi, ki wo raaj khada ho paaya.

## Part 10: Soch, aur mila-jula khoon

Bharat ko soch se mohabbat rahi hai.

Nehru ek kavi ka zikr karte hain, jinki lines aaj bhi dohrai jaati hain. Aur soch ki is mohabbat ka ek ajeeb pehlu wo batate hain.

Ye mohabbat aksar un baaton ke liye hoti hai, jinhe vyavhaarik log bekaar kehte hain. Faltu. Absurd. Aisi soch, jisme roti nahi banti.

Par is desh ka swabhaav yahi raha. Yahan soch ka aadar hua. Sochne wale logon ka aadar hua. Nehru unhe highbrows kehte hain.

Aur is desh ne unhe do cheezon se oopar rakha. Talwar se. Aur paise se.

Ye badi baat hai. Kyunki zyadatar duniya mein talwar aur paisa hi sabse oopar rehte hain.

Aur jab is desh ke bure din aaye, jab haalat sabse girti hui thi, tab bhi isne soch ka saath nahi chhoda. Usme thodi tasalli dhoondhi.

Is baat ko yaad rakhiye. Ye aage laut kar aayegi.

Kyunki yehi desh, jisne soch ko talwar se oopar rakha, khud ko deewaron ke andar bhi baandh raha tha.

Nehru likhte hain, hum ek aise log rahe hain, jo apne ateet par garv karte hain, apni virasat par garv karte hain, aur us virasat ko bachane ke liye deewarein aur baadhayein banate rehte hain.

To sawal yahi hai. Agar hamari soch itni badi thi, to ye deewarein kis cheez ke liye thi?

Iska jawab Nehru hamare khoon se nikaalte hain. Aur hamare naam se.

Dekhiye is desh ke logon ko.

Nehru likhte hain, hum nasl ki shuddhta par garv karne wale log hain. Jaati bhi sakht ho chuki hai. Aur phir bhi, isi desh ka khoon duniya ki sabse mili-juli cheez hai.

Arya, Dravid, Turani, Semitic, Mongol. Sab isme hain.

Arya baar-baar ki lehron mein aaye, aur Dravidon ke saath unka milan hua. Uske baad hazaaron saal tak, ek ke baad ek giroh aate rahe.

[SFX: door ki ghode ki taap, dheemi]

Shak, jinhe Scythian bhi kehte hain — Madhya Asia ke wo ghoomne wale log, jo yahan bas gaye. Yunani. Kushan. Aur unke peeche Turk, Turco-Mongol, aur aur bhi.

Kuch bade jhund mein aaye. Kuch chhote. Kuch akela. Sabne yahan ghar banaya.

Ek angrez itihaaskar ne iska ek achha vaakiya likha hai. Nehru use dohraate hain.

Kahani aisi hai. Bahut jangli, ladaku kabile baar-baar uttar ke maidanon par aate hain. Rajaon ko haraate hain. Shehar jalate hain. Naye raaj aur nayi rajdhaniyan banate hain. Aur phir wo gayab ho jaate hain. Ek badi lehar mein ghul kar.

Peechhe sirf do cheezein chhodte hain. Thoda sa mila-jula khoon. Aur kuch reetiyon ke chithe, jo dheere-dheere apne hi mahaul se milte jaate hain.

To ye mahaul kya tha?

Nehru ek sawal poochte hain. Kuch hissa mausam aur mitti ka hai. Is desh ki hawa ka. Bhugol ka.

Par usse bada kuch aur hai. Ek tez khinchaav. Ek ichha. Zindagi ke matlab ka ek vichaar, jo is desh ke andar uski sabse shuruaati umar mein hi chhap gaya tha. Itna gehra, ki wo tik gaya. Aur jo bhi is desh ke sampark mein aaya, wo usi taaqat se andar aa gaya. Chahe wo kitna bhi alag ho.

Yahan Nehru ek baat likhte hain, jo unki imaandari dikhati hai.

Wo kehte hain, kisi sabhyata ke neeche ek ichha ya soch dhoondhna khokhla lagta hai. Ek insaan ki zindagi sau jagah se ras aati hai. Ek desh ki zindagi, usse bhi zyada jagah se.

Satah par ideein tairti rehti hain. Kuch ek doosre ki dushman. Aap koi bhi giroh utha lijiye, apni baat saabit kar lijiye. Doosra giroh utha lijiye, wahi baat tor dijiye. Purane bade deshon mein ye aur aasan hai, kyunki wahan murde zinda logon se chipke rehte hain.

Isliye Nehru kehte hain, aasan baatein aur aasan vargeekaran khatarnak hote hain. Yahan soch seedhe-seedhe do hisson mein nahi bantti. Ek soch doosri mein milti rehti hai. Purane shabd wahi rehte hain, andar ka maal badal jaata hai. Aur kabhi kabhi wo waqt se peechhe reh jaate hain, aur bojh ban jaate hain.

## Part 11: Hamlavar jo apne ho gaye

Phir wo ichha sirf ek khayaal thi?

Aur phir bhi ek baat dikhti hai. Hum badalte rehte hain. Har daur pichhle daur se alag hai. Aaj hum, nasl aur sanskriti dono se, purane Bharat se bahut alag hain.

Par Bharat aur China ki sabhyataon mein ek ajeeb si jaan bachi hai. Bahut badlaav aaye, bahut sankat aaye, par dono apni buniyadi pehchaan bachaye rakh paaye. Ye tabhi mumkin tha, jab wo zindagi aur prakriti ke saath taal mein hon.

Jo cheez unhe unki purani jadein pakde rakhti hai, wo achhi ho ya buri ya dono ka mel, wo taaqat ki cheez hai. Warna itne saal nahi tikti. Ho sakta hai ki uski upyogita bahut pehle khatam ho gayi ho, aur wo tab se bojh ban gayi ho. Ya ho sakta hai ki aage ke dauron ne uske andar ka bhala daba diya ho, aur ab sirf khaal bachi ho. Ek jeevashm ka khaali shell.

Aur is sab ke beech ek cheez thi, jo is desh ne apne mein sambhali rakhi. Lachak.

Yahan koi niyam aisa nahi tha, jo riwaaz se na badal sake. Ek naya samooh apne naye riwaaz, naye vishwas, naye tareeke le kar aata, aur phir bhi bade samooh ka hissa mana jaata.

Isi lachak ne bahar se aane walon ko sokhna aasan banaya.

Iske peeche kuch tha. Kuch buniyadi naitik niyam. Zindagi ko dekhne ka ek darshanik tareeka. Aur doosron ke tareeke ke liye sahishnuta.

Jab tak is dhaanche ka maksad suraksha aur sthirta tha, ye chalta raha. Aarthik badlaav aaye, to wo khud ko badalta raha, aur chalta raha.

Asli chunauti kisi sena se nahi aayi. Asli chunauti ek nayi soch se aayi. Aage badhne ki soch. Social progress. Jo purane thahre hue saanche mein fit hi nahi hoti thi.

Nehru kehte hain, ye soch poorab ko bhi ukhaad rahi hai, aur pashchim ko bhi. Pashchim aaj bhi progress ko sabse oopar rakhta hai, par wahan suraksha ki maang badh rahi hai.

Aur Bharat mein? Yahan suraksha hi nahi thi. Isi liye yahan ke log apni purani khoai se nikal kar aise progress ke baare mein sochne lage, jo zyada suraksha de.

Ab is episode ke do siron ko milaiye.

Ek khamba, Besnagar mein. Ek Yunani ka khamba, jis par Sanskrit likha hai.

Aur aaj is desh ke wo log, jo sabse zyada Bharatiya hain, jo Bharat ki parampara par sabse zyada garv karte hain.

Rajput. Jinke baare mein Nehru likhte hain ki unmein se kai Indo-Scythian ke vanshaj hain, aur kuch Hoonon ke bhi.

Jat. Kathiawad ka wo mazboot kisan, jo mitti se juda hai, aur apni zameen mein dagi nahi sehta. Unka bhi Scythian mool hai.

Aur Kathi. Kathiawad ka wahi lamba, khoobsurat kisan. Unka bhi wahi mool.

Kuch logon ka mool pakka bataya ja sakta hai. Kuch ka nahi. Par asli baat kya hui?

Yahi. Ki mool jo bhi raha ho, ye sab poore-poora Bharatiya ban gaye. Sab is sanskriti mein saajhi bhagedaar hain. Aur is desh ke ateet ko apna ateet kehte hain.

[SFX: Besnagar wali sookhi hawa, bahut halki, laut kar aati hai]

To Besnagar ka wo khamba ab ek sawal ban jaata hai.

Jisne use khada kiya, wo bahar se aaya tha. Usne yahan ki bhasha chuni. Yahan ki baat likhwayi. Aur yahin ka ho gaya.

Phir jo bahar se aaya, aur apne mein hi reh gaya, usne yahan koi lamba asar nahi chhoda. Wo mit gaya. Aur mitne mein usne apna nuksaan kiya, aur kabhi kabhi is desh ka bhi.

Aur jisne khud ko bachane ke liye darwaza band kar diya, wo bhi haar gaya.

To jawab yahi hai.

Bharat Bharat bana raha, kyunki usne har aane wale ko andar samaa liya.

Aur usne darwaza band kiya us waqt, jab samaa lene ki jagah bachav hi uski pehchaan ban gayi.

Wahi waqt Gupta kaal ki chamak ke andar chhupa hua tha. Chamak aur darwaza, dono saath aaye.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, Nehru ka wo bhram. Wo likhte hain, ye maanna ki bahar se aaye raja ne pehli baar is desh ko aman aur intezaam diya, sabse ajeeb bhram hai. Aaj usi bhram ke naye roop hain. Kabhi wo kisi padosi par thop diya jaata hai, kabhi kisi bade bazaar par, kabhi kisi nayi technology par. Sach ye hai ki jab bahar ka raaj aaya, to desh sabse kamzor tha, aur isi liye aaya. Ye baat aaj bhi yaad rakhne layak hai. Ye bhram chhota nahi hai. Ismein hum apni hi mitti ko kam samajhne lagte hain.

Doosri, Nehru ki lachak wali baat. Is desh ne sab kuch apne mein samaa liya. Log, riwaaz, bhasha, dharm. Cold open ka khamba isi ki gawahi hai. Aur Rajput aur Jat ka waakiya bataata hai ki sabse pakke rakhwale aksar unhi khandaanon se aate hain, jinhe kabhi bahar wala kaha gaya tha.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aaj aapke aas-paas "apna" kaun hai? Aur ye faisla aapne khud kiya hai, ya kisi ne aapke liye kar diya?
