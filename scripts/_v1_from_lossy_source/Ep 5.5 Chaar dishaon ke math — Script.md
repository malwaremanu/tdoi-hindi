## Ab tak

Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila, aur ek qaidi jo Bharat ko samajhne ke liye peechhe lautta hai. Pichhli baar hum ruke the ek aise dharm par, jo bina jung ke apni hi mitti se gayab ho gaya. Wahan ek naam khula tha: Shankara. Aaj hum uske peeche chalenge.

## Part 1: Chaar kone, chaar ghar

To wo aadmi kaun tha?

Uska naam Shankara tha. Shankaracharya.

Ek pandit. Ek darshanik. Dakshin ke ek chhote se ilaake se. Brahmin parivar se.

Us zamane mein aisa aadmi gaon mein baith kar shastra padh sakta tha. Poore desh ka safar uski majboori nahi thi.

Nehru likhte hain ki Shankara ne is desh ke chaar kone par chaar math banaye. Math, yaani monastery. Wahan sanyasi rehte the, aur vidya padhi jaati thi.

Ek dakshin mein. Sringeri, Mysore ke paas.

Ek poorab mein. Puri, samundar ke kinaare.

Ek pashchim mein. Dvaraka, Kathiawad mein.

Aur ek uttar mein. Badrinath, Himalaya ke beech.

Zara naqsha kholiye aur chaaron jodiye. Dakshin. Poorab. Pashchim. Uttar.

Ek aadmi ne poori zameen ka naqsha apne gharon se bhar diya.

Aur is naqshe ko banane wale ke baare mein Nehru ek ajeeb baat likhte hain. Duniya se mooh modne ka darshan unhone khada kiya, par wo khud kisi ghaar mein bhaag kar nahi baithe.

Wo ek hi aadmi mein kai log the. Darshanik bhi, vidvaan bhi. Agyani bhi, aur mystic bhi. Kavi, sant, sudhaarak, aur ek kabil manager.

Wo Malabar ka tha, dakshin ke us chhor ka. Zindagi bhar poore desh mein chalta raha, behes karta raha, apni aag logon mein bharta raha.

Brahmin dhaanche ke andar unhone pehli baar dus religious orders khade kiye. Unme se chaar aaj bhi zinda hain.

Aur ye ghar aise hi nahi chune gaye. Ye chaar jagah pehle se hi teerth the. Poore desh se log wahan jaate the. Aaj bhi jaate hain. Shankara ne wahan ghar banaya, to wahan jaane wali bheed aur badh gayi.

Phir wo Kedarnath gaya. Himalaya ki un unchaiyon mein, jahan saal bhar barf rehti hai. Aur battis saal ki umar mein wahan mara.

Nehru ek baat note karte hain. Ye safar khud ek baat hai. Us zamane mein raste kachche the, sawari dheemi aur purani. Ek jagah se doosri jagah pahunchna mahino ka kaam tha.

Phir bhi wo chala. Baar-baar chala.

Kyun?

Pehle ye samajhna zaroori hai ki wo apne saath kya le ja raha tha.

Wo ek soch le ja raha tha. Darshan.

Aur ye darshan ek nahi tha.

Chhe the.

## Part 2: Darshan sabke paas hota hai

Ek darshan kya cheez hai?

Ye bade sawalon ka guchha hai. Duniya kya hai. Main kaun hoon. Sahi kya hai, galat kya hai. Jeena kaise chahiye.

Nehru ek baat maante hain. Ye sawal sabse mushkil sawal hain. Unhe samajhne ke liye dimaag ko kadi mehnat se baandhna padta hai. Bahut logon ke liye ye samajh se bahar hi rehte hain.

Aur soch ek jagah ruk kar nahi baithti. Purani aur nayi soch saath-saath chalti hain, ek doosre se ladti hui. Ek aadmi ka dimaag hi contradictions ka guchha hota hai, uski ek baat doosri se nahi milti. Ek poore desh mein atit ke kai daur ek saath khade rehte hain.

Nehru kehte hain, ye sirf peeche reh gaye deshon ki baat nahi. Jahan aadmi nayi se nayi cheez istemaal karta hai, wahan bhi aise vishwas zinda hain jinhe tarak nahi maanta. Ek scientist apne laboratory se baahar nikalte hi science ka tareeka bhool jaata hai.

Par yahan ek ajeeb baat hai.

Darshan sirf darshanikon ke paas nahi hota.

Har aadmi ke paas zindagi ka koi na koi darshan hota hai. Kabhi sochkar banaya hua. Aur kabhi socha hi nahi hua, bas mehsoos kiya hua.

Aur agar humne khud nahi socha, to humne wo kisi aur se liya. Maa-baap se, muhalle se, guruji se.

Ya phir hum ek doosra raasta lete hain. Sochne ke jhamele se bachne ke liye hum kisi vishwas mein baith jaate hain. Koi dharm. Koi kattar vichaar. Koi desh ki kismat wali baat. Ya koi dhundhla sa, achha lagne wala insaaniyat wala bhaav.

Aur aksar ye sab ek saath chalte hain, bina kisi jodne wali cheez ke.

Nateeja?

Humare andar ek nahi, kai aadmi baith jaate hain. Har ek apne alag kamre mein. Darwaza band. Kabhi ek baahar aata hai, kabhi doosra.

Nehru ise split personality kehte hain. Kisi ek sawal par aap poora insaan nahi hote. Aap us kamre ki soch hote hain, jiska darwaza us waqt khula hai.

Aur Bharat mein? Nehru likhte hain, yahan duniya ke kahin bhi se zyada, purane samaaj ka dhaanche wala vichaar aur uske peeche ki soch aaj tak tik gayi hai.

Kahin aur ye purani soch toot kar bikhri. Yahan wo chalte-jaate logon ke andar bani rahi.

Iska matlab ye badi cheez thi?

Ya iska matlab kuch aur hai?

## Part 3: Jo kaam kare, wahi zinda

Nehru yahan jaldi faisla nahi sunaate. Wo pehle ek tarazoo rakhte hain.

Ye purani soch itne saal kyun tiki? Kisi cheez mein taaqat tabhi hoti hai, jab usme koi gun ho — wo gun jo samaaj ko jode rakhe aur zindagi ke saath dhal jaaye.

Aur wo soch aakhir mein bojh kyun ban gayi? Kyunki usi ke andar jo bura tha, wo us gun se bada ho gaya. Soch zindagi se alag ho gayi.

Aur us soch ko aaj akele nahi dekh sakte. Use poori duniya ke saamne rakhna padega.

Ab is par ek angrez vidvaan ka saar aata hai. Uska kehna tha:

Bharat mein dharm koi sakht dogma nahi hai. Wo insaan ke chalne ka ek niyam hai, jise har aadmi ki haalat ke hisaab se badla ja sakta hai.

Dogma aap deewar par chipka kar rakh sakte hain, zindagi se alag. Par chalne ka niyam zindagi se alag nahi reh sakta. Ya wo kaam karega, ya wo raaste mein khada ho jaayega.

Jis din wo zindagi ki lakeer se hat jaata hai, us din wo bekaar ho jaata hai.

Darshan bhi aisa hi hai. Wo zindagi ke badalte maal par baat nahi karta. Wo uske peeche ki sthir sachai par baat karta hai, agar aisi koi sachai hai. Isliye usme ek sthirta rehti hai, jo bahar ke badlaav se nahi hilti.

Par darshan bhi usi mahaul mein paida hota hai. Jis zameen par wo ugta hai, uski mitti usme lagti hai. Jis dimaag ne use socha, us dimaag ki haalat usme dikhti hai. Aur agar uski hawa phail jaaye, to wo poori kaum ki zindagi wali soch badal deta hai.

Aur Bharat mein yahi hua.

Nehru likhte hain, yahan darshan oopar ke thode se chune hue logon tak hi rehta tha. Par kahin aur se zyada, yahan wo nicha tak pahuncha. Usne is desh ke dekhne ka tareeka ghaala, aur ek khaas soch ka swabhaav banaya.

Isme Buddh darshan ka bada hissa tha. Madhyakaal mein Islam ka asar bhi pada, aur kuch naye panth uthe jo Hindu aur Islami jeevan-dhang ke beech pul banana chahte the.

Par sabse bada asar kis cheez ka tha?

Nehru ka jawab saaf hai. Chhe darshanon ka.

Inhe yahan darshana kaha jaata hai. Hum inhe chhe darwaze ki tarah kholenge. (Darwaze wali tasveer humari hai, book ki nahi.)

Pehla darwaza ab khulega. Aur pehla darwaza sabse sakht hai.

Kyunki wahan sirf tarak hai.

## Part 4: Pehla darwaza — Nyaya

Pehle do baatein. Ek: ye darshan naye nahi hain — inki shuruaat Buddh dharm se bhi pehle ki hai.

Do: ye alag-alag nahi, jude hue badhte hain. Brahmin wale darshan ek taraf, Bauddh wale doosri taraf. Kabhi tanak jaate the, kabhi udhaar lete the.

Aur Isai sadi shuru hone se pehle, bahut se darshanon mein se chhe ghaale ja chuke the.

Har ek apna raasta hai, apni dalil. Phir bhi wo kate hue nahi — wo ek bade plan ke hisse hain.

Chhe naam, chhe darwaze: Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, Vedanta.

Koi anek devtaon ko maanta hai, koi ek Ishwar ko, koi sirf ek ko. Aur ek aisa hai, jo Ishwar ko bilkul nahi maanta, aur sirf evolution par khada hai. Kuch idealist hain, kuch realist.

Phir bhi Nehru kehte hain, inke beech bahut si baatein ek jaisi hain.

Kaun si baatein? Ye sab maante hain ki brahmaand ek niyam se chalta hai, usme ek bada lay hai. Ki har cheez ka apna kaaran hai, phir bhi aadmi apni kismat kuch khud banata hai. Ki janm baar-baar hota hai. Aur ki niswarth prem aur bina matlab ke kiye kaam ki badi jagah hai.

Aur ek baat sabse ajeeb hai. Ye saare darshan shastra ko maante hain, par apne sawal wo insaani tareekon se suljhaate hain. Nehru ek vidvaan ka saar likhte hain: shastra ka kaam sirf nateejon ko pavitra banana reh jaata hai.

Ab pehla darwaza. Nyaya.

Nyaya ka matlab hi hai tarak. Sahi sochne ka vidhi.

Nehru ek tulna karte hain. Ye Yunani darshanik Aristotle ke tarak jaisa hai. Bahut milta hai, par kuch gehra fark bhi hai.

Aur dekhiye — Nyaya ke usool baaki saare darshanon ne maan liye. Koi usse bahas nahi karta.

Ye sirf kitaabon mein bhi nahi rehta. Purane aur madhyakaal, dono dauron mein Nyaya Bharat ke pathshalaon mein padhaya jaata tha, aur aaj bhi padhaya jaata hai. Nayi padhai ne usse hata diya, par jahan Sanskrit purane tareeke se padhai jaati hai, wahan Nyaya aaj bhi syllabus ka hissa hai.

Uska darja kya tha? Ye darshan padhne ki tayyari thi, aur har padhe-likhe aadmi ki zaroori dimaagi kasrat.

Nehru kehte hain, purani Bharatiya padhai mein Nyaya ki jagah kam se kam utni badi thi, jitni European padhai mein Aristotle ke tarak ki.

Iska tareeka aaj ke vigyaan jaisa nahi tha, par wo andhvishwas par bhi nahi chalta tha. Wo gyaan ki har cheez ko chhaanta tha, aur tarak se saabit karta tha.

To kya usme vishwas bilkul nahi tha?

Tha. Aur wahi is darwaze ki kamzori hai — ya uski hadd.

Nyaya pehle kuch baatein maan leta hai — aisi baatein, jinhe tarak se saabit nahi kiya ja sakta. Aur unhe maan lene ke baad, poora mahal unhi neevon par khada hota hai.

Kaun si baatein? Ki zindagi aur prakriti mein ek lay aur ek jut hai. Ki ek Ishwar hai. Ki har aatma alag hai. Ki ye brahmaand anuon se bana hai.

Aur insaan? Wo na sirf aatma hai, na sirf shareer — dono ke jude hone se banta hai. Aur ye sach, aatmaon aur prakriti ka mila-jula jaal.

Pehla darwaza khul gaya. Isme tarak tha, aur ek chhupi hui neev bhi.

Agla darwaza isi neev se shuru hota hai. Aur wahin ek darshan aisa bhi khada hai, jo har cheez ko alag-alag dekhta hai.

## Part 5: Doosra darwaza — alag-alag, aur anu

Doosra darwaza. Vaisheshika.

Vaisheshika bahut hadd tak Nyaya jaisa hi hai. Par usme ek zor alag hai.

Wo kehta hai: har cheez alag hai. Har aatma, har vastu.

Aur wo brahmaand ka anu-siddhant khada karta hai. Sab kuch chhote-chhote anuon se bana hai.

Iske beech ek cheez sabse oopar rakhi jaati hai. Dharm. Yaani naitik niyam. Nehru kehte hain, poora brahmaand isi dharm se chalta hai, aur poora darshan isi ke aas-paas ghoomta hai.

Aur Ishwar? Yahan Ishwar ko saaf-saaf maana nahi gaya. Uska darwaza khula chhod diya gaya hai.

Nehru ek baat jodte hain. Vaisheshika aur Nyaya ka shuruaati Bauddh darshan se gahara rishta hai. Aur dono ka tareeka, poore maayne mein, realistic hai.

Ab teesra darwaza. Aur yahan kahani badalti hai.

Teesra darshan. Samkhya.

Nehru likhte hain ki ise Kapila naam ke ek purane darshanik ne ghaala. Kapila ka samay saatvi sadi Isai poorv ke aas-paas maana jaata hai. Unhone bahut si purani dhaaraon ko samet kar ise ek aakaar diya.

Ek vidvaan ne is darshan par ek badi baat likhi. Uska kehna tha, duniya ke itihaas mein pehli baar, insaan ke dimaag ki poori azaadi aur uska apne bal par poora bharosa — ye yahan dikhta hai.

Samkhya ki shakal Buddh dharm ke uthte waqt pakki hui.

Iska tareeka kya tha? Ye sirf dimaag se nikla ek siddhant tha, baahar ki cheezon ko naap kar nahi bana tha. Aur jo cheezein iski pahunch se bahar thin, unhe naapna mumkin hi nahi tha.

Par isme ek baat kamaal ki hai. Jaise Buddhism, wahi Samkhya bhi sirf tarak ki line par chala. Usne Buddhism ko uske hi maidanon mein lalkara — bina kisi adhikari ke sahare.

Aur isi wajah se Ishwar ko hataana pada.

Samkhya mein na ek Ishwar hai, na koi niraakaar Ishwar. Na ek-Ishwar-wad, na ek-tattva-wad. Uska raasta atheist tha, aur isne us dharm ka neev hila diya jo oopar wale kisi bal par khada tha.

Yahan duniya ko kisi Ishwar ne banaya nahi. Yahan ek lagatar evolution chalti hai. Wo chetna aur prakriti ke takraav se banti hai. Par wo prakriti bhi, apne aap mein, ek urja hai. Aur ye evolution kabhi rukti nahi; ye chalti rehti hai.

Isi wajah se Samkhya ko dvaita kaha jaata hai. Dvaita, yaani do. Kyunki wo do mool kaaranon par khada hai.

Ek: Prakriti. Wo prakriti jo kabhi na ruke, jo badalti rahe. Doosra: purusha — wo chetna jo badalti nahi. Purush anant hain. Aur purusha khud kuch karta nahi. Bas uska asar prakriti par padta hai, aur prakriti banti chali jaati hai.

Ab tak humne chhe mein se teen darwaze khole. Aur sab kuch dimaag ka khel tha.

Chautha darwaza isse hatkar hai. Wahan dimaag nahi, shareer khada hai.

## Part 6: Chautha darwaza — Patanjali ka Yoga

Chautha darshan. Yoga.

[SFX: kothri mein ek gehri, lambi saans; phir chup]

Yoga ka naam aaj ghar-ghar mein hai. Par Nehru kehte hain, ise bahut kam log samajhte hain.

Yog ka purana vidhi kaafi tha. Use Patanjali naam ke ek vidvaan ne ghaala, aur saaf shakal di.

Patanjali sirf Yoga ke darshanik nahi the. Unhone Panini ke Sanskrit vyakaran par ek badi teeka bhi likhi — Mahabhashya. Nehru kehte hain, wo teeka bhi Panini ke kaam jitni badi classic hai.

Aur is darshan mein ek baat baaki sab se alag hai. Yoga pehle se socha hua siddhant nahi hai, ye khud jaanchne ka tareeka hai. Isliye koi bhi ise apna sakta hai. Nireeshwar Samkhya wale bhi ise chalate the. Iska matlab, Ishwar is darshan ka zaroori hissa nahi hai.

Naam ka matlab bhi dekhiye. Yoga ka matlab hai jodna — jaise angrezi ke "yoke" mein.

Yoga ka maksad kya hai? Shareer aur mann ko saadhna, aur us saadhna ko aisi gehrai tak le jaana jo shareer se upar hai.

Nehru yahan imaandari se ek sawal chhodte hain. Yoga ke aakhri padav ek aisi samajh tak le jaate hain, jahan shabd nahi pahunchte.

Par Nehru kehte hain: mujhe nahi pata ki ye upar ki haalat hai, ya sirf khud ko hi dhoka dena. Aur ek baat wo saaf jodte hain. Bina rehnumai ki gayi Yoga se kabhi-kabhi dimaag par bura asar bhi pada hai.

Par us aakhri padav se pehle, ek kaam hai. Shareer ka kaam.

Shareer mazboot ho. Halka ho. Sundar ho. Sakht ho. Aur lachak wala ho.

Iske liye kuch shareer ki kasratein hain, aur saans lene ke tareeke — saans ko lamba karna, gehri karna, aur uspar thoda kaabu paana.

Par Nehru ek shabd pakadte hain. "Kasrat" — ye galat shabd hai, kyunki isme koi zor hai hi nahi. Koi daud, koi kood, koi jhatka. Ye asana hai — baithne, khade hone, mudne ke khaas aakaar.

Aur theek se kiye jaayein, to ye shareer ko aaram dete hain, andar se taaza karte hain, thakate nahi.

Nehru ek tulna karte hain. Baaki tareeke — daud-dhoop, jhatke, uchhal-kood — aadmi ko haanfta hua chhod dete hain. Saans phool jaati hai, thakaan reh jaati hai.

Ye tareeke Bharat mein bhi the. Kushti. Tairna. Ghudsawari. Talwar. Teerandazi. Indian club. Aur ju-jitsu jaisa ek khel bhi.

Par asana wala purana tareeka Bharat ka apna lagta hai — Nehru kehte hain, wo is desh ke darshan ke swabhaav se milta hai.

Usme ek thehraav hai. Shareer chal raha hai, par mann shant. Taaqat milti hai, par na urja barbaad hoti hai, na mann mein halchal.

Aur ek faayda. Ye har umar ke liye hai. Kuch asana to boodhe log bhi kar sakte hain.

Aur is darwaze ke peechhe ek aadmi khada hai, jise hum jaante hain. Wo Ahmadnagar ki kothri mein hai. Aur ye kaam wo roz karta hai.

## Part 7: Qaidi ki asana

Ab Nehru apne baare mein likhte hain.

Kai saalon se unhone aise hi kuch chune hue, aasan asana roz kiye hain, jab bhi mauka mila. Aur unhe koi shak nahi — unhe inka bada faayda hua.

Kyunki unki zindagi aise mahaul mein guzri, jo shareer aur mann, dono ke liye achha nahi tha — kothri, sifar, bhaddi hawa, kam neend.

Unka poora riyaaz — thode asana aur kuch saans ki kasratein — bas itna hi tha. Wo shareer ke shuruaati padav tak hi pahunche. Aur unka mann? Wo aaj bhi ek aisa sadasya hai, jo sunta nahi. Bahut baar hadd se bahaar chala jaata hai.

Yoga sirf shareer ka kaam nahi hai. Uske saath khana-peena aata hai — sahi cheezein khaana, galat cheezon se bachna.

Aur uske saath ek naitik tayyari aati hai — ahimsa, satya, sanyam.

Ahimsa par wo ruk kar ek baat saaf karte hain. Ahimsa ka matlab sirf haath rok lena nahi hai — dil mein kisi ke liye bura bhaav na rakhna, nafrat na, jalan na.

Ye sab kis liye? Indriyon par kaabu ke liye. Phir dhyan, ekagrata. Aur uske aage, wo samajh jo tarak se nahi aati.

Yahin Nehru ek aadmi ko saamne laate hain. Vivekananda.

Vivekananda — aadhunik Bharat ke sabse bade Yoga aur Vedanta ke prachaarak. Wo baar-baar ek baat par zor dete the: yoga andhvishwas nahi hai. Wo ek experiment hai, aur tarak par khada hai.

Vivekananda kehte the, koi bhi Yoga aapse tarak chhodne ko nahi kehta. Koi nahi kehta ki aankh band kar lo, aur apna dimaag kisi pujari ke haath mein de do. Sab ek hi baat kehte hain — apne tarak ko pakde raho.

Aur wo tarak kitni door tak jaata tha? Nehru unka ek vakya likhte hain. Tarak se atheist ban jaana behtar hai, kisi ke kehne par do sau crore devtaon ko maan lene se.

Nehru unki do aur baatein sunaate hain.

Pehli. Soch bhi ek kaam hai. Aur soch ki keemat sirf kaam se milti hai.

Doosri. Inspiration. Vivekananda ka kehna tha ki inspiration tarak ke khilaaf kabhi nahi ja sakti. Jise hum inspiration kehte hain, wo tarak ka hi aage badha hua roop hai.

Aur uske saath ek shart thi. Inspiration kisi naam, fame ya apne fayde ke liye nahi — wo sab ke bhale ke liye ho, poori tarah niswarth.

Ab Nehru ek sawal utthate hain. Vivekananda ka tarak par ye zor kahan se aaya? Wo maante the ki mann azaad hona chahiye. Aur unka apna desh? Nehru unka ek chhota vakya sunaate hain. Vivekananda kehte the, main us desh mein paida hua, jahan adhikar ki hadd hi paar ho chuki thi.

Aakhir mein Nehru ek saavdhani likhte hain. Yoga ek experiment hai, par uske ilaake aam aadmi ki pahunch se bahar hain. Aur ye sirf Bharat mein nahi hui. Aise anubhav Isai sadhuon, Parsi Sufiyon aur doosron ke likhe hue milte hain.

Nehru ek baat par hairaan hote hain — ye anubhav ek doosre se itne milte hain. Ek Francisi lekhak ne likha tha, ye dikhata hai ki manushya ka bana hua saara maal ek hi hai.

Aur Nehru ek koshish sujhaate hain — shayad aaj ka mann ka science is experiment se kuch le sake. Koshish karni chahiye.

Aaj sochiye. Aaj yog ki koi kami nahi — koi studio, koi video, koi app. Par usme se kitna asana hai, aur kitna wo cheez hai jo Vivekananda maangte the — tarak ko pakde rakhna?

## Part 8: Paanchwa darwaza — Mimamsa

Paanchwa darwaza. Mimamsa.

Ye reet ka darshan hai. Vidhi. Puja. Yagya. Aur iska jhukav anek devtaon wali soch ki taraf hai.

Iska asar aaj bhi dikhta hai. Nehru likhte hain, aaj ka aam lok Hindu dharm, aur Hindu Law — dono par is darshan ka bada asar hai. Kyunki isi ne jeene ka wo dharm khada kiya. Sahi jeene ka poora nizam.

Isi reet wale darshan ne aam logon ke ghar tak apna nizam pahunchaya — puja ka samay, vrat, daan, snan, aur kaun sa kaam kis din ho.

Ab yahan Nehru ek ajeeb baat par rukte hain.

Hindu anek-devta-wad ek ajeeb kism ka anek-devta-wad hai.

Devta, yaani "chamakte hue". Unke apne khaas bal hain. Phir bhi wo insaan se neechi paidish maane jaate hain.

Aur ye soch Hindu aur Bauddh, dono mein hai. Yahan devta ko chhota nahi kiya gaya, sammaan diya gaya. Par insaan ka darja usse oopar rakha gaya.

Dono maante hain ki insaan ka janm hi sabse oonchi seedhi hai, jahan tak Being pahuncha hai. Khud ko jaanne ka raasta yahin se jaata hai. Devta bhi aage nahi badh sakte — unhe bhi insaan ka janm lena padta hai.

Sochiye, kaisa anek-devta-wad hai ye, jo devtaon ko bhi insaan ka janm lene par majboor kar deta hai.

Bauddh to ye bhi kehte hain: sirf insaan hi Buddh ki sabse oonchi haalat tak pahunch sakta hai.

Nehru kehte hain, ye baat aam anek-devta-wad se bahut door hai. Is soch mein devta oopar nahi baithte. Wo insaan se neeche khade hain.

Aur aakhri darwaza isi seedhi ke sabse oopar ek hi cheez rakhta hai.

## Part 9: Chhetta darwaza — Vedanta

Chhetta darshan. Vedanta.

Nehru kehte hain, ye Upanishadon se nikla. Saalon mein isne kai shaklein li. Par ek baat kabhi nahi badli — ye hamesha ek-tattva par khada raha.

Samkhya ke purusha aur Prakriti yahan alag cheezein nahi rehti — wo ek hi sachai ke do roop ban jaate hain: the absolute.

Aur yahin Shankara aate hain.

Hamari kahani ke wo aadmi, jo Kedarnath ki barf mein gaya — unhone is purane Vedanta ki neev par apna darshan khada kiya. Advaita Vedanta. Advaita, yaani do nahi, ek.

Nehru likhte hain, aaj Hindu dharm ka sabse bada darshanik nazariya yahi hai.

Is darshan ka dil kya hai? Sirf ek. Ek hi aakhri sachai hai — Atman, wo poora, bada, absolute soul. Atman subject hai, baaki sab object.

Ab ek sawal. Wo ek, jo sab mein hai, anek kaise dikhne lagta hai? Nehru kehte hain, iska jawab tarak nahi de sakta — humara dimaag iss finite duniya se bana hai.

Yahan Upanishad ki ek chhoti line hai.

Poora wo hai, poora ye hai. Poore se poora nikalta hai. Poore mein se poora hataaiye — to bhi poora bacha rehta hai.

Nehru kehte hain, Shankara ne gyaan ka ek baareek nizam banaya — maanyataon se shuru kiya, kadam-kadam par tarak se aage badhe, aur poora Advaita khada kiya.

Us nizam ki ek tasveer kamaal ki hai. Sochiye ek ghada — andar ki jagah wahi humari alag aatma, baahar ki poori jagah wahi Atman.

Dono alag lagte hain. Kaam-kaaj ke liye alag maane bhi jaate hain. Par ye alag hona sirf dikhta hai — sach mein alag nahi. Aur azaadi? Ye samajhna ki wo dono ek hain.

To phir ye duniya? Nehru kehte hain, ye us sachai ki parchhaayi hai — ek saaya, jo is duniya par pada hai. Iska naam Maya hai. Log Maya ko "illusion" kahte hain, yaani aisa jhooth jo hai hi nahi. Nehru kehte hain, ye tarjuma galat hai.

Maya ka matlab gayab hona nahi. Maya "hai" aur "nahi hai" ke beech baithi hai — ek aadhi sachai. Nehru ek shabd sujhaate hain: relativity.

To phir achha aur bura? Nehru yahan saaf ho jaate hain. Aakhri sachai kuch bhi ho, is duniya mein achhe aur bure ka fark kaam karta hai — wahin, jahan hum alag-alag insaan ban kar jeete hain.

Hum finite log infinite ko soch hi nahi sakte, bina usko chhota kiye. Humari soch usse seema de deti hai. Phir bhi ye chhote aakaar usi infinite mein rehte hain.

Nateeja? Dharm ka roop relative ban jaata hai. Aur har aadmi ko azaadi hai ki wo us sachai ka jitna aakaar bana sakta hai, banaye.

Shankara ne samaaj ke caste wale dhaanche ko maan liya tha — usse kaum ka jama hua anubhav kaha. Par unka ek saaf vichaar tha: kisi bhi caste ka koi bhi aadmi sabse oonchi vidya tak pahunch sakta hai.

Aur unke darshan par ek chhaap hai — duniya se mooh modna, uske kaam-kaaj se hat jaana. Aur baar-baar ek hi baat: apna tyaag.

Chhe darwaze khul gaye. Ab wapas us aadmi ke paas chaliye, jo barf mein gaya tha.

## Part 10: Chaar dishaon ke math

Nehru ek baat likhte hain, jo Part 1 mein chhoot gayi thi. Shankara ke ye lambe safar — us zamane mein safar mushkil tha, sawari dheemi aur purani. Ye safar khud kuch keh rahe hain.

Wo poore desh mein ghoomta hai. Har jagah usse aise log milte hain jo usi tarah sochte hain, aur wo unse Sanskrit mein baat karta hai.

Sanskrit us zamane mein poore Bharat ke padhe-likhe logon ki sajhi bhasha thi — bas yahi baat is desh ka jut dikha deti hai.

Log safeer-chaar hote rahe, phir bhi aate-jaate rahe. Nayi kitaabein safar karti rahi. Har nayi soch poori zameen par tezi se phailti rahi, aur us par garam behes hoti thi.

Ye sirf padhe-likhe logon ki baat nahi thi. Aam log bhi teerthon ki taraf chalte rahe — us zamane ke teerth, jo itne purane the ki unka zikr maha-kathaon tak jaata hai.

Nateeja? Ye chalta-phirta rehna ek hi zameen aur ek hi sanskriti ka bhaav badhata raha.

Ye safar sirf oopar ke logon ka nahi tha. Teerthon mein har caste, har varg ke mard aur auratein milte the. Teerth ka jo bhi dharmik matlab raha ho, log usse ghoomne aur mauj ke din ki tarah bhi lete the.

Ek teerth par jaaiye, to wahan poora Bharat ek jagah dikhta hai — alag kapde, alag rasm, alag bhasha — aur phir bhi sab jaante hain ki hum ek hain. Uttar aur dakshin ki bhasha ka fark bhi samvaad ke aade nahi aaya.

Nehru ka andaaza hai, wo is jut ke bhaav mein kuch jodna chahte the. Unhone do jagah kaam kiya. Ek, oopar ki duniya mein — soch, darshan, dharm: wahan unhone poore desh ki soch ko ek karne ki koshish ki. Doosra, aam logon ki duniya mein: wahan unhone kai dogme tod diye, aur apne darshan ka darwaza har us aadmi ke liye khol diya jo andar aane laayak tha.

Aur chaar math? Wo chaaron kone par the. Nehru kehte hain, ye jaan-boojh kar aise lagaye gaye. Wo chaar jagah khud teerth thin, aur ab aur bhi badi teerth ban gayin.

Purane Bharatiya log apne teerth dhoondhne mein kitne achhe the. Unke teerth lagbhag hamesha khoobsurat jagahon par hain — Amarnath, Rameshwaram, Benares, Hardwar jahan Ganga maidan mein utrti hai, Prayaga jahan Ganga Jamna se milti hai — aur dakshin mein itni jagah.

Ye teerth sirf pooja ki jagah nahi the — dakshin ke purane mandiron mein kamaal ki moortiyan aur kala hai, to teerthon se art ki ek jhalak bhi milti hai.

[SFX: Kedarnath wali tez hawa, bahut halki, laut kar aati hai]

Ye bhi kaha jaata hai ki Shankara ne is desh mein Buddhism ko badi dharm ki tarah khatm karne mein haath diya, aur uske baad Brahminism ne usse apni baahon mein samaa liya. Par Buddhism us se pehle hi sikud chuka tha.

Nehru ek ajeeb baat jodte hain. Shankara ke kuch Brahmin virodhak unhe kehte the — ek chhupa hua Buddhist. Aur Nehru kehte hain, isme sach bhi hai. Buddhism ka asar unke darshan par gehra tha.

Ab is episode ke do siron ko milaiye. Ek taraf, Kedarnath ki barf mein ek aadmi, jisne is desh ke chaar kone par ghar banaye. Doosri taraf, Ahmadnagar ki kothri ka ek qaidi, jo subah farsh par kambal bichhata hai.

Aur beech mein chhe darwaze.

Jawab yahi hai.

Ye chhe darshan alag-alag the — koi Ishwar ko maanta tha, koi nahi. Par wo alag kamron mein band nahi the.

Wo ek hi ghar ke chhe darwaze the. Aur darwazon ke beech raaste the.

Un raaston par sirf pandit nahi chalte the — kisan chalte the, auratein, karigar.

Isliye jab ye log sochte the, to wo ek hi zameen par sochte the. Aur ek hi zameen par sochne wali soch door nahi reh sakti.

Isliye itne alag-alag darshan ek hi desh ki soch ban gaye.

Aur wo aadmi? Apne chaar gharon se barf tak chala gaya. Kyunki darwaze banane wale ko khud rasta chalna padta hai.

## Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, kamre wali baat. Nehru likhte hain ki hum split personality wale log ban jaate hain — har sawal par ek alag kamre ka darwaza khulta hai. Aaj ye aur saaf dikhta hai: ek hi din mein hum ek jagah ek soch bolte hain, aur doosri jagah uski ulti. Nehru ki kasauti aaj bhi kaam ki hai: aapki soch aapki zindagi ke saath chal rahi hai, ya wo zindagi se alag ho kar sirf kaagaz par zinda hai?

Doosri, Yoga wali. Nehru ne likha tha ki asana ko kasrat nahi kehna chahiye, kyunki usme zor nahi hota. Aur Vivekananda ne kaha tha — tarak ko pakde raho. Aaj yog duniya mein badi cheez ban gaya hai. Par ek sawal bacha rehta hai: hum shareer ka hissa to le lete hain, par uske saath jo dimaag wali shart aayi thi, wo aksar chhod dete hain.

Aapke liye ek sawal. Aapke paas zindagi ka jo bhi darshan hai — kya wo aapke saath badal raha hai, ya aap itne saalon se usi neev par khade hain, jispar aapne kabhi dekha hi nahi?
