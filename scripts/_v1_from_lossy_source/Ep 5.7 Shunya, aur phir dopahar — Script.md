## Ab tak
Ab tak: 1944, Ahmadnagar ka qila. Ek qaidi, Jawaharlal Nehru, wahan is desh ki khoj mein ek kitaab likh raha hai. Pichhli baar humne dekha tha ki is desh ki soch pahaad aur samundar, dono paar gayi. Us episode ka aakhri drishya tha, samundar ke us paar, patthar mein tarasha ek chehra. Aaj wahi chehra humare saamne khada hai.

## Part 1: Wahi muskaan, bar-bar
Chaliye us chehre ke paas lautte hain.

Sadiyon pehle yahan ek shehar tha. Patthar ka. Iske mandir khade the, aur unke dwar par ye chehre.

Ye sab kahan se aaya? Nehru kehte hain: Bharat se.

South-East Asia ke in deshon mein Bharatiya sabhyata ne jadein pakdi. Uska saakshya aaj bhi har taraf milta hai.

Pehla saakshya: naam. Yahan ke jo bhi raja uthe, unke naam poore Bharatiya the. Sanskrit ke. Iska matlab ye nahi ki wo log poore Bharatiya the. Iska matlab ye hai ki wo Indianized ho gaye the.

Doosra saakshya: rajdarbar. Rajya ke samaroh Bharatiya the, aur bhasha Sanskrit thi. Sarkar ke har afsar ke paas koi purana Sanskrit ka pad tha. Hairat ki baat: ye pad aur ye naam aaj tak chal rahe hain. Thailand mein bhi. Aur Malaya ke Musalmaan riyason mein bhi.

Teesra saakshya: kala. Indonesia ke purane sahitya Bharatiya puran-kathaon se bhare hain. Java aur Bali ke mashhoor naach Bharat se aaye. Bali ne to apni purani Bharatiya sanskriti aaj tak sambhali — yahan tak ki Hindu dharm bhi wahan zinda raha. Aur likhne ki kala? Wo Philippines tak gayi.

Cambodia ko dekhiye. Uska lipi dakshin Bharat se aaya. Sanskrit ke shabd wahan gaye, halke badlaav ke saath. Aur kanoon? Cambodia ka kanoon is desh ke purane kanoon-granthakar Manu ke kanoonon par khada hai. Bauddh asar se kuch badlaav ke saath, wo aaj ke Cambodian kanoon mein dhal gaya.

Nateeja? Yahan sirf mala aur dhupaal nahi gayi. Rajdarbar gaya, bhasha gayi, lipi gayi, kanoon gaya. Aur upar se upar — patthar gaya.

Us patthar par phir ek naam milta hai. Ek Cambodian raja, jisne ye bada mandir banwaya. Uska naam tha Jayavarman VII. Ek theek-thaak Bharatiya naam.

Par ek sawal khada hota hai. Jo cheez yahan se gayi, wo wahan itni badi kaise ho gayi?

## Part 2: Jahan mitti upjaau thi
Java mein imaaraton ka ek dher hai. Borobudur.

Uska poora varnan ek vakya mein: wahan Buddha ki saari jeevan-katha patthar mein tarashi gayi hai. Aur doosri jagahon par deewaron par ubhare hue chitra. Vishnu ki katha. Ram ki. Krishna ki.

Nateeja? Ye kala wahan ja kar mari nahi. Wo wahan ki mitti mein utar gayi.

Ek angrez lekhak ne Angkor par likha: ye aaj duniya ka sabse bada ajooba hai. Patthar mein insaan ki pratibha ka ek shikhar. Aur phir unhone ek baat jodi. Ye ek aisi sabhyata ke nishaan hain, jo chhah sau saal tak apne pankh faila kar chami. Aur phir itni poori tarah mit gayi, ki uska naam bhi insaan ke hothon se mit gaya.

Nehru kehte hain: pehla jhatka Bharat se aaya. Par use aage badhaya Khmer logon ki apni pratibha ne.

Ek aur angrez vidvaan, Quaritch Wales, ise aage le jaate hain. Unka kehna hai: jab Bharat ka haath yahan se hat gaya, tab bhi uski prerana bhooli nahi gayi. Khmer pratibha azaad hui, aur usne naye, bade vichaar gade.

Wo ek teekhi baat jodte hain. Khmer sanskriti ki jadein Bharatiya prerana mein hain. Bina us prerana ke, Khmer log zaada se zaada wahi bana paate, jo Central America ke Maya logon ne banaya. Par ye prerana aisi upjaau mitti par giri.

Ek French vidvaan, Sylvain Levi, ne is phailav ko ek line mein baandha. Persia se Chinese samundar tak — is desh ne apna vishwas, apni kahaniyan aur sabhyata phailayi. Aur insaan ki chauthai nasal par aisi chhaap chhodi jo aaj tak miti nahi.

Sochiye, ye tareef yahan se aayi hai. To phir yahin, is mitti par kya hua?

Nehru isi par rukte hain. Unka kehna hai: Bharat mein wo pehli aag dheere-dheere bujh gayi. Kyunki mann aur mitti, dono thak gaye. Naye vichaar aane band ho gaye.

Phir unka vyakyaa. Jab tak Bharat ne apna mann khula rakha — bahar apni daulat di, aur bahar se wo liya jiski use zaroorat thi — tab tak wo taaza, mazboot aur zinda raha. Par jitna zyada usne khud ko bachane ke liye apne khol mein ghusna shuru kiya, utni hi uski wo aag dheemi padi. Aur sundar cheez banane ki kala jo chali gayi, to bachche usse pehchaanne ki kabiliyat bhi kho baithe.

Wo kala miti nahi — par uski khoj kisi ne ki.

In khandaron ki khudai European vidvaanon ne ki. Khaas taur par French aur Dutch vidvaanon ne. Nehru kehte hain, itni badi imaaratein aur shehar abhi bhi zameen ke neeche pade honge. Aur Malaya ke kuch purane khandar? Wo khudai ke kaam mein, ya sadak ke liye patthar nikaalne mein, nasht kar diye gaye.

## Part 3: Aadha itihaas
Ab ek angrez ka naam aata hai. Sir John Marshall — angrez puratatvavid, jinhone in khandaron ko sabse kareeb se dekha.

Marshall ki baat hai: agar aap is art ko sirf India mein dekhte hain, to aap uski aadhi kahani hi jaante hain. Poori tarah samajhne ke liye uske peeche Buddha dharm ke raaste chaliye — Central Asia tak, China tak, Japan tak. Aur Cambodia aur Java ki un imaaraton par to sirf hairan hi huiye.

Har desh mein ise ek alag nasal ki pratibha mili, aur ek alag mahaul. Unke asar se uska roop badalta gaya.

Yahan Nehru ek imaandaar baat likhte hain. Mujhe art ki samajh nahi hai. Main aise dekhta hoon, jaise ek aam aadmi dekhta hai. Koi murti ya tasveer mujhe khushi deti hai, koi nakaraar ho jaati hai.

Unhe Ceylon ke Anuradhapura ka Buddha ki murti ne gehri tarah chhua. Us murti ki ek tasveer barson tak unke saath rehti hai. Par dakshin Bharat ke kuch mashhoor mandir, jahan tarashai ki koi kami nahi, unhe bechaini se bhar dete hain.

Aur phir ek angrez lekhak aate hain, jo India ke saare ajoobe maante the. Phir bhi likhte hain: Bharat ka khayal khud unhe ghinona lagta tha. Aur Hindu kala par, unke shabdon mein, ek chipchipi, ghinoni chhaap thi.

Nehru ka jawab shaant hai. Unhe aisa lagta hai, to lagna unka haq hai. Main bhi is desh ki bahut cheezon se ghin karta hoon. Par poore Bharat se nahi. Kyunki main khud se nafrat nahi kar sakta.

Par ye art ki raay ka sawal nahi hai. Ye ek poore desh ke liye chupi hui dushmani hai. Aur Nehru ek sawal poochte hain: jinhe humne nuksaan pahunchaya, unse hum nafrat karte hain?

Isi hisse mein do aur angrez aate hain. Lawrence Binyon aur E. B. Havell. Dono is kala ki qadar karte the.

Havell ki baat gehri hai. Ek desh ki kala uski soch aur kirdaar ka khaas izhaar hoti hai. Par use tab hi samjha ja sakta hai, jab uske peeche ke aadarsh samjhe jaayein. Jo rajya unhe na samjhe, wo soch ki nafrat ke beej bo deta hai.

Aur ek baat, jo is episode ki spine hai. Havell kehte hain: ye kala kisi chhote giroh ke liye nahi bani thi. Uska maksad tha, dharm aur darshan ke bade vichaar aam logon tak pahunchein. Aur wo kaamyaab rahi.

Iska saakshya Havell khud dete hain: is desh ka kisaan, pashchimi matlab mein padha-likha nahi hota. Phir bhi wo apne varg ke sabse sanskrit logon mein gina jaata hai.

Sochiye. Jo kala zaroorat ki cheez thi, wo kaamyaab hui. Aur jo kala apne dekhne walon ko dhoondhne chali — wo apna hi desh nahi dekh paayi.

## Part 4: Ajanta ki auratein
[SFX: gaar ke andar gehri sannata, door paani tapakne ki dheemi boond; phir ek halka saans]

Art mein kalakar ko chaahiye tha ki wo khud ko prakriti ke har mausam mein utaar de — isi se insaan aur brahmaand ka mel dikhta hai. Ye poore Asia ki kala ka mool-raag hai. Isi wajah se itni vividhta ke baad bhi Asia ki kala mein ek ekta hai.

Bharat mein purani painting bahut kam bachi hai. Sirf Ajanta ke wo pyaare frescoes. Is desh ki asli chhap murti aur imaaraton mein thi, jaise China aur Japan painting mein aage the. Sangeet bhi apne dhang se aage tha, aur usne Asia ke sangeet par chhapa chhodi.

Ab ek ajeeb rukavat. Is desh mein murti-puja ka virodh tha — Vedon ne murat ki pooja ke khilaaf kaha tha. Isliye Buddha ke dharm mein bhi bahut der se Buddha ke roop ko patthar mein tarasha gaya. Phir wo waqt aaya jab roop bana. Mathura ke museum mein ek bada patthar ka Bodhisattva hai, taaqat aur bal se bhara hua. Wo Kushan kaal ka hai, yaani Isa ke janm ke aas-paas. Us kala mein prakriti ki sidhi nakal hai, aur thodi Chini chhaap bhi ho sakti hai. Aur ulta bhi hua: Bharatiya aadarshvaad China aur Japan gaya, aur wahan ke bade daur mein unhe badal diya.

Ab sone ka daur. Gupta kaal. Chauthi se chhathi sadi tak. Jise is desh ka Golden Age kaha jaata hai. Usi waqt Ajanta ki gaarein khodi gayi, aur un par frescoes bane. Bagh aur Badami ka waqt bhi yahi tha.

In frescoes ko Bauddh bhikshuon ne banaya. Unke guru ne kaha tha: auraton se door raho. Unhe dekho bhi nahi. Wo khatarnak hain.

Aur deewaron par kya hai? Auratein bharpoor. Sundar auratein. Raajkumariyan. Gaayika. Nartaki. Baithi hui, khadi hui, apne aap ko sajaati hui, juloos mein chalti hui. Ajanta ki auratein mashhoor ho gayi.

Saatvi-aathvi sadi mein Ellora ki gaarein thos patthar mein khodi gayi, aur beech mein wo Kailasa mandir khada hua. Elephanta ki gaarein bhi isi kaal ki hain, aur usme gehri Trimurti hai.

Wahan ek tooti hui murti hai. Shiv Nataraja. Havell kehte hain, tootne ke baad bhi wo shaan ki kalpana hai, titaan-shakti ka roop. Par us naach ke beech us sir par wahi shaant bhaav hai, jo Buddha ke chehre par hota hai.

British Museum mein ek doosri Nataraja murti hai. Ek aadhunik shilpi, Epstein, ne likha: is murti mein prem aur maut ka bhayankar saar hai, aur hamari European kahaniyan iske saamne bekaar lagti hain.

Aur yaad hai cold open wala chehra? Java se Borobudur ka ek Bodhisattva ka sir ab Copenhagen ke ek museum mein hai. Havell: wo sundar hai, par usme kuch gehri baat hai — wo chehra, jaise ek aaine mein, Bodhisattva ki shudh aatma dikhata hai.

Havell ki aakhri baat: Java ki kala ka apna swabhav hai. Usme bhi wahi shaanti hai, par Elephanta aur Mamallapuram ki kadi bhavaheenata wahan nahi. Java ki kala mein insaani santosh aur khushi zyada hai — wo us shaanti ka chehra hai, jo in upniveshiyon ko apne pyaare dweep par mili.

## Part 5: Shunya
[SFX: ek sookhi lakdi ke takhte par ek kadam jaisa halka thap; phir sannata]

Ab hum mudte hain us cheez ki taraf, jo dekhne mein kuch bhi nahi lagti.

Pehle ek purani baat. Nehru likhte hain: aaj se lagbhag dedh sau saal pehle, Napoleon ke zamane mein, ek French vidvaan La Place ne likha tha — das nishaane, har nishaane ki do keematein: apni aur jagah ki. Aur ye khayal Archimedes aur Apollonius se bhi chuk gaya.

Par baat ye hai: geometry, ank-ganit aur algebra — teeno ka janm is desh mein bahut purane zamane se juda hai.

Shuruaat shayad vedik vedia ke saath hui. Purani kitaabon mein ek niyam milta hai: ek varg ko ek aayat mein badalna, jiski ek bhuja tay ho. Aur ye aakar aaj bhi Hindu samaroh-mandap banane mein kaam aate hain.

Par ek sach bhi hai. Geometry mein Greece aur Alexandria aage nikal gaye. Lekin ank-ganit aur algebra mein Bharat aage raha.

Ye kaise hua? Kalpana kijiye ek chhota sa nishaan.

Kisi ne zameen par ek bindu banaya. Ek chhota sa nukta. Itna chhota, ki dekhne mein lagta hai wahan kuch hi nahi hai. Phir wo bindu bada hua. Ek chhote gol ki tarah. Aur us cheez ka naam pada — shunya. Yaani kuch nahi.

Yahi wo cheez thi, jise is desh ne bekaar maan kar phenk nahi diya. Usne use ek ank ki tarah gina, kisi bhi doosre ank ki tarah.

Aur is aavishkaar ko kisne kiya? Nehru saaf kehte hain: humein nahi pata. Naam kisi ka nahi milta.

Jo sabse purana zero ka nishaan ab tak mila hai, wo ek dharm-granth mein hai. Lagbhag Isa se do sau saal pehle. Aur jagah ke hisaab se ank likhne ka tareeqa — dashamlav padhti — wo shayad Isa ke janm ke aas-paas shuru hua.

Ek vidvaan ne likha: is khoj ki ahmiyat kabhi badhaa-chadhaa nahi ki ja sakti. Kisi bekaar hawa jaisi cheez ko ek naam, ek nishaan dena — aur usme kaam karne ki taaqat bharna.

Ek doosre vidvaan ne likha: paanch hazaar saal tak ginti ke auzaar mein ek bhi sudhaar nahi hua — phir us anjaan Hindu ka kaam ek vishwa-ghatna ban gaya.

Ek aur vidvaan ko hairat hui. Greece ke bade ganit-shastri is khoj par kyun nahi pahunche? Unka jawab: Greece ke log kaam-kaaj ke vigyaan ko halka maante the. Wo apne bachchon ko padhaane ka kaam bhi gulamon par chhod dete the. To jo desh duniya ko geometry de kar itna aage gaya, usne algebra ka shuruaati roop kyun nahi banaya?

Iska jawab ek British vidvaan, Professor Hogben, dete hain: tarakki ko kuch pratibhaon ki roshni samajhna dhoka hai — asli cheez wo samaaj hai, jo sabse bade dimaag ko bhi ghere hai. Ye aavishkaar kisi ek bhramit pratibha ki pal-bhar ki roshni nahi thi. Ye us samay ke samaaj ka paida kiya hua nikla. Ek zaroorat thi, aur us zaroorat ne raasta maanga.

Purani Sanskrit ki ganit-kitaabein uthaiye. Wo bhari hui hain vyapaar aur rishton ki mushkil samasyason se: kar, Rin, sud, saajhedari, barter, aur sone ki shudhta ka hisaab.

Yaani samaaj bada aur ulajha ho gaya tha, aur bahut se log bina aasaan tareeqe ke sarkari kaam aur door ke vyapaar chala rahe the.

## Part 6: Ginti ka jangla
Aur jo darwaze khule, wo dekhne layak hain.

Bhinn-ank aa gaye. Unka guna aur bhaag. Teen cheezon ka niyam khoja gaya, aur poora kiya gaya. Warg aur wargmool — aur wargmool ka apna nishaan bhi. Ghan aur ghanmool. Ghataane ka chinh. Sine ki tables. Aur pi ki keemat — teen point ek chaar ek chhe tak.

Ek aur badi baat. Algebra mein akshar aa gaye — jo cheez humein nahi pata, uske liye ek akshar. Aasaadharan aur varg-samikaran aaye.

Aur shunya ka ganit khud. A se a ghata karne par shunya. A mein shunya jodne par a. A mein shunya guna karne par shunya. Aur a ko shunya se bhaagne par — anant. Ghata-ank bhi aaye. Yaani chaar ka vargmool do bhi ho sakta hai, aur ghata do bhi.

Ye sab kis kis ne likha?

Paanchvi se baaraahvi sadi tak, ek ke baad ek bade ganit-shastri uthe. Inse pehle geometry ki kitaabein thi. Baudhaayan. Aapastamb. Kaatyaayan. Teen naam, teen kitaabein — warg, aayat aur tribhuj ke sawal.

Aur algebra ki jo sabse purani kitaab bachi hai, wo ek mashhoor khagol-shastri ne likhi. Aryabhata. Aryabhata, wo ganit aur taaron ke vidvaan, jinka janm 476 mein hua. Unhone ye kitaab sirf teis saal ki umar mein likhi. Kuch log unhe algebra ke aavishkarak kehte hain. Par Nehru kehte hain, wo apne se pehle walon ke kaam par khade the.

Agla bada naam Brahmagupta ka hai. Brahmagupta, ek khagol-shastri, jinhone 628 mein shunya ke niyam likhe.

Aakhri bada naam Bhaskara II ka hai. Unka janm 1114 mein hua. Unhone teen kitaabein likhin: khagol-shastra, algebra, aur ank-ganit. Unki ank-ganit ki kitaab ka naam hai Lilavati.

Ek ganit ki kitaab ka aisa naam? Ye ajeeb hai, kyunki Lilavati ek aurat ka naam hai. Kitaab mein baar-baar ek jawan ladki se baat hoti hai — usse "hey Lilavati" kaha jaata hai — aur phir usi ke saamne sawal rakhe jaate hain. Mana jaata hai, bina pakke saakshya ke, ki Lilavati Bhaskara ki beti thi. Aur kitaab ki bhoomika saaf aur simple hai. Ye aaj bhi Sanskrit pathshalaon mein padhaayi jaati hai.

Sadiyon tak kitaabein aati rahin. Naraayan ne 1150 mein likha. Ganesh ne 1545 mein. Par ye sirf wahi dohrai thi, jo pehle ho chuka tha. Baaraahvi sadi ke baad, is desh mein ganit par bahut kam naya kaam hua.

## Part 7: Hindsa
[SFX: door se ek dheemi ghanti; uske neeche kagaz ki halki sar-sarahat]

Aathvi sadi. Baghdad.

Us waqt Baghdad duniya ke sabse bade gyaan-kendron mein se ek tha.

Wahan Khalif Al-Mansur ka raaj tha, aur uske zamane mein — 753 se 774 ke beech — Bharat ke kuch vidvaan Baghdad gaye. Unke saath ganit aur khagol-shastra ki kitaabein thi. Ye uski pehli sansthit yatra thi.

Aryabhata aur doosre vidvaanon ki kitaabein Arabic mein anuvaad ki gayi. Isne Arab duniya ke ganit aur khagol-shastra ko badal diya. Us waqt Baghdad mein Greek aur Jewish vidvaan bhi jama the, apne saath Greek darshan, geometry aur vigyaan laaye hue.

Nateeja? Baghdad ki chhaap pure Musalmaan sansaar par phaili — Central Asia se Spain tak. Aur Arabic anuvaadon ke saath Bharatiya ganit bhi.

Arabon ne in gintiyon ko ek naam diya: "Hind ki gintiyaan". Aur Arabic mein ginti ke liye jo shabd hai — Hindsah — uska matlab hi hai, Hind se aaya hua.

Aur phir ek doosra safar shuru hua. Is Arab duniya se ye naya ganit Europe tak pahuncha, shayad Spain ke Moor vaishwavidyalaya ke raaste. Aur wahan ja kar ye Europe ke ganit ki neev ban gaya.

Europe ne ise turant nahi apnaya. Wahan ke logon ne in ankdon ko begane dharm ka chinh maana. Ismein saikdon saal lag gaye. Sabse purana saakshya jahan mila, wo ek Sicilian sikka hai. Saal 1134. Britain mein pehla istemaal 1490 mein.

Aaj sochiye. Aaj duniya ke har calculator mein, har mobile mein, do cheezein hain. Shunya. Aur ank ki jagah. Dono yahan paida hui, aur dono ne Baghdad ka raasta liya.

To yahan ki cheez ghar chhod kar duniya ki ho gayi. Aur yahan, jahan wo paida hui thi, ek aadmi sirf ek daftar ke kone mein baithkar usse khel paaya.

## Part 8: Madras ka ek clerk
Us aadmi ka naam Srinivasa Ramanujan hai.

Ramanujan — dakshin Bharat ke ek gareeb Brahman parivaar ka ladka, jise theek se padhne ka mauka hi nahi mila. Wo Madras Port Trust mein ek clerk ban gaya.

Par uske andar ek aisi pratibha chal rahi thi, jo ruki nahi jaati thi. Wo apna daftar ka kaam khatam karta, aur khaali waqt mein numbers aur equations ke saath khelne lagta.

Aur ek din ek ittefaq hua. Ek ganit-shastri ki nazar uske kaam par padi. Unhone uske amateur kaam ko Cambridge bhej diya. Yahan ke log hakke reh gaye.

Cambridge se ek scholarship aayi. Ramanujan ne apni clerk ki naukri chhodi, aur England chala gaya. Wahan bahut thode waqt mein usne bahut gehri aur bilkul nayi tarah ka kaam kiya. England ki Royal Society ne use apna Fellow maan liya.

Aur do saal baad wo mar gaya. Shayad TB se. Wo teis saal ka tha. Us waqt ke ek bade vaigyanik ne, kahin, usse is sadi ka sabse bada ganit-shastri kaha.

Nehru yahin ruk kar ek baat likhte hain. Ramanujan ki chhoti si zindagi aur maut, is desh ke haalaat ka ek nishaan hai.

Karodon log. Inmein kitne ko thodi si bhi padhai mili? Kitne bhookh ke kinare par zinda hain? Aur jinhe thodi padhai mil bhi gayi, unmein kitne aise hain, jinke saamne kisi daftar mein ek clerk ki naukri hai — aur wo bhi itni kam tankhwah par, jo England ke unemployment dole se bhi kam ho?

Nehru aage ek sawal rakhte hain. Agar is jeevan ne apne darwaze khol diye — khana, sehet, padhai, aur badh jaane ka mauka — to in karodon mein se kitne bade vaigyanik, kitne shikshak, kitne lekhak aur kalaakar honge?

Sochiye. Ek desh jo shunya khoj sakta tha. Aur uske ek sabse pratibhashali bete ko apni pratibha dikhane ke liye duniya ke doosre kinare jaana pada.

Ab hum wapas mudte hain. Un sadiyon ki taraf, jab ye thakaan shuru hui.

## Part 9: Dopahar shuru hoti hai
Dhyan dijiye, patan ek din mein nahi aaya.

Sone ka daur khatam hone se pehle hi, kamzori ke nishaan dikhne lage.

Uttar-pashchim se Safed Hoon aaye. Jhund ke baad jhund. Unhe baar-baar khada kar diya gaya. Par wo phir aaye. Dheere-dheere wo Uttar Bharat mein andar tak ghus gaye. Aadhi sadi tak unhone poore uttar par raaj kiya.

Phir ek bada prayaas hua. Gupt vansh ke aakhri bade raja ne, Central India ke ek shasak Yashovarman ke saath mil kar, Hoonon ko bahar khada kar diya.

Par is lambi ladai ne Bharat ko raajnitik aur sainya roop se kamzor kar diya.

Aur ek gehri baat. Ye Hoon log sokh liye gaye — bilkul waise hi, jaise is desh ne har videshi tatwa ko sokha tha. Par wo apna asar bhi chhod gaye. Aur purane Indo-Aryan aadarsh kamzor pad gaye. Unke baare mein purane varnan kehte hain ki unmein aisi nirmamta dikhti thi, jo is desh ke yuddh aur shasan ki tameezon ke bilkul khilaf thi.

Aur phir, chahe bahut si achhi baatein hui, ek andar ki kamzori is desh ko pakad leti hai. Ye sirf raajnitik pehchaan par asar nahi daalti. Ye rachna ka kaam bhi apne jaal mein le leti hai.

Iska koi tareekh nahi hai. Ye dheema, raingta hua badlaav tha. Uttar mein pehle aaya, dakshin mein baad mein.

Aur ab dakshin — raajnitik aur sanskritik, dono taraf — zyada zaroori ban gaya. Shayad isliye ki dakshin us aseem ladai se bacha raha, jo uttar hamesha lad raha tha. Aur shayad isliye ki bahut se lekhak, kalaakar aur bade shilpi uttar ke bechain halaat se bhaag kar dakshin chale gaye, jahan ke taaqatwar rajya aur chamakte darbar unhe wahi mauka dete the, jo kahin aur nahi mila tha.

Gyaan abhi poori tarah nahi bujha tha. Sabse bada naam tha Nalanda — wahan pahunchna khud ek sanad thi; daakhla sabko nahi milta tha. China, Japan, Tibet se log padhne aate the. Bahar tak jo sabhyata gayi, wo zyadatar Nalanda ke vidvaanon ka kaam tha. Vikramshila — aaj ke Bihar mein Bhagalpur ke paas. Vallabhi — Kathiawar mein. Ujjayini — jo Gupta kaal mein upar utthi. Aur dakshin mein, Amravati.

## Part 10: Sabhyata ki dopahar dhalti hai
[SFX: dhoop mein ek lamba, sust saans; door kuch pakshi]

Jab ye hazaar saal apne ant ki taraf badhne lage, to ye poora daur ek sabhyata ki dopahar lagne lagta hai.

Subah ki wo roshni kabhi ki bujh chuki thi. Dopahar chadh chuki thi. Aur ab dhal rahi thi.

Dakshin mein abhi bhi jaan thi. Wo josh kuch sadiyan aur chala. Aur samundar ke us paar, apni bastiyon mein, wo khoon-garam zindagi agle sahasrabdi ke beech tak chalti rahi.

Par is dhadakti duniya ka dil jaise patthar ban gaya. Uske dhadke dheeme pad gaye. Aur dheere-dheere ye patthar-pan haathon-paun tak phail gaya.

Darshan mein. Aathvi sadi mein Shankara ke baad koi bada darshanik nahi. Sirf ek lambi kataar — tika-kaar aur tark-kaar. Khoj karne ka tevar, aur dimaag ke adventure ka josh — un dono ki jagah ek sookhi, sakht tark aur ek sooni bahas aa gayi. Aur dono dharm bigadte hain. Brahmanism bhi, aur Buddhism bhi. Pooja ke gire hue roop upar aate hain — khaas taur par Tantrik pooja ke kuch roop, aur Yoga ka bigada hua gyaan.

Sahitya mein aathvi sadi ke Bhavabhuti is desh ka aakhri bada naam hain. Uske baad kitaabein likhi jaati rehti hain, par unki bhasha jaise-jaise ulajhti jaati hai. Ganit mein baaraahvi sadi ke Bhaskara II aakhri bada naam hain.

Aur kala mein? Ek vidvaan ise thoda aur aage le jaate hain. Unke hisaab se saatvi ya aathvi se chaudahvin sadi tak is desh ki kala ka mahan daur tha — theek waise hi jaise Europe mein Gothic kala ka daur tha. Aur chhahvi sadi mein us purani kala ki rachna ki taaqat kam hone lagi. Aur Nehru ka khayal hai, kala mein bhi dakshin ne uttar se zyada der tak purani parampara sambhali.

Aur bahar jaane ka aakhri bada safar bhi dakshin se hi hua. Nauvin sadi mein. Uske baad Chola vansh gyarahvin sadi tak samundar par taaqatwar raha. Aur gyarahvin sadi mein usne Sri Vijaya ko takkar de kar hara diya.

## Part 11: Thakaan kahan se aayi
To tasveer saaf hai. Ye desh sookh raha tha. Ye process dheema tha — sadiyon tak chala, uttar se shuru hua, aur aakhir mein dakshin tak pahuncha.

Aur ab wo sawal, jiske liye ye poora set khada tha. Kyun?

Teen jawab diye jaate hain. Shayad ye sirf umar thi — wahi umar, jo sabhyataon par hamla karti hai, jaise insaan par. Ya shayad ye ek samundari lehar thi, jiska aage-peeche ka dole chaal tha. Ya shayad bahar ke hamle zimmedaar the.

Ek vichaarak kehte hain: is desh ke darshan ne apna josh kho diya, kyunki pehle raajnitik azaadi gayi. Wahi French vidvaan, Sylvain Levi, likhte hain: Sanskrit sanskriti Bharat ke saath khatam ho gayi.

Nehru kehte hain: ye sab sahi hai. Par ek sawal wo aage rakhte hain: raajnitik azaadi kyun gayi? Ek chhota desh badi taaqat se dab sakta hai. Par Bharat jaisa bada desh — itna sabhya — bahar ke hamle se tab tak nahi jhukta, jab tak andar se wahi sada na ho gayi ho. Aur wo sada, is hazaar saal ke ant mein, saaf dikhti hai.

Sikhne ka mauka sirf upar walon ke paas tha. Kshatriya ka kaam tha ladna. Baaki logon ko ijazat hi nahi thi. Brahman aur Kshatriya vyapaar ko neecha maante the, aur nichli jaatiyon se padhai chhin li gayi thi. Shehron ka viksit arthvyavastha tha, phir bhi rajya ki banawat jaageerdaari thi.

Nehru yahin ek shisha uthate hain. Rome bhi andar ki kamzori se gira: arthvyavastha sikudi, kaarigar kanoon se apne kaam se baandh gaye, aur kaam jaati ban gaye.

Aise haalaat mein bada badlaav mumkin nahi tha — jab tak banawat badle, aur pratibha ke naye srot khulein. Aur yahin jaati-pratha khadi thi. Wo badlaav ke raaste mein ek deewar thi. Apne saare gunon aur di hui sthirta ke saath, uske andar apne vinash ke beej the.

Isne is sabhyata ko sthirta di, par yahi cheez aage badhne se roki. Isne hunar aur vyapaar khada kiya — par hamesha har giroh ke andar alag-alag. Kaam virasat mein milne lage, aur naya sochna band ho gaya.

Jab tak is banawat ne raaste diye, wo badhti rahi. Aur hadd par pahunch kar wo thahar gayi — phir peeche hatne lagi. Patan hua: soch mein, darshan mein, rajniti mein, ladai ke tareeqon mein, bahar ki duniya se rishton mein. Aur arthvyavastha sikud gayi.

Par Nehru ek aakhri baat jodte hain. Phir bhi us purani banawat mein jaan baaki thi. Kuch lachak, aur badalne ki salahiyat. Isi se wo bachi rahi, naye sampark se faayda uthaya, kuch tarakki bhi ki — par wo tarakki hamesha baandhi hui thi, ateet ki zanjeerein uske pairon mein thi.

Chaliye, ab wapas Bayon chalte hain. Wahi ek chehra, patthar mein baar-baar dohraya hua. Ek waqt tha, jab ye prerana yahan se bahar gayi, doosre logon ke haathon mein padi, aur wahan aur badi ho gayi. Aur yahan? Yahan kaarigar ki dukaan chup ho gayi. Kyunki usse sabse zyada chahiye naye haath, aur naye vichaar, jo uske darwaze se andar aate rahein.

Ab hum dono cheezein jod sakte hain. Wo bindu, jo shunya bana. Aur wo muskaan, jo patthar mein dohrayi gayi.

Ek chhota sa bindu yahan paida hua, aur ghar se nikal kar duniya ki ginti ban gaya. Aur yahan ka hunar ghar se nikal kar doosre deshon ke mandir ban gaya.

To jis desh ke paas itna tha, wo thak kyun gaya?

Kyunki usne apna darwaza band kar liya. Jab tak usne diya aur liya, tab tak wo taaza raha. Mazboot raha. Zinda raha. Aur jis din usne khud ko bachane ke liye apne khol mein ghusna shuru kiya, us din wo aag dheemi padne lagi. Aur wo vyavastha, jisne is samaaj ko sadiyon tak tikaya, wahi nayi pratibha ko upar aane ka raasta rok baithi.

Hogben ki baat yaad rakhiye: har sabhyata apna ant andar liye chalti hai — jab tak wo aam logon ki padhai ko utni jagah na de, jitni khaas pratibha ko.

Ramanujan us waqt bhi kahin baithkar ginti se khel raha hoga. Aur dopahar dhal rahi thi.

## Aaj ke hisaab se
Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Do baatein.

Pehli, shunya. Aaj hum jo ank likhte hain, jisse hisaab karte hain, wo is mitti mein paida hue. Phir ghar chhod kar Baghdad gaye, wahan se Spain, aur wahan se poori duniya. Sawal ye hai: kya hum wahi darwaza khula rakhte hain, jisse hawa andar aati hai?

Doosri, Ramanujan wali baat. Nehru ka sawal 1944 mein tha, aur aaj bhi utna hi taaza hai. Aaj kitne bachche apne daftar ki mez par, dukaan ke peeche, khet ke kinare, kisi pratibha ke saath khel rahe honge — aur koi unhe dekhne wala nahi? Ek desh ki sabse badi daulat uske log hain. Aur uski sabse badi barbadi ye hai, ki wo apni pratibha ko dekh hi nahi paata.

Aapke liye ek sawal: aapke aas-paas koi aisa kaam hai, jise koi kar sakta hai, par uska darwaza band hai? Aur agar wo darwaza aap khol sakte hain — to aap kis ke liye kholenge?

## Outro
Chapter paanch yahin khatam hota hai.

Set ka sawal tha: itni chamak ke baad, thakaan kahan se aayi? Jawab: thakaan bahar se nahi aayi. Us desh ne apna darwaza andar se band kar liya. Aur jis din usne khud ko bachane ke liye apne khol mein ghusna shuru kiya, us din wo aag dheemi padne lagi.

Par wo shunya ghar par nahi rukha. Aathvi sadi mein wo Baghdad pahuncha — aur Baghdad us waqt ek nayi duniya ka darwaza tha. Usi duniya se ek naya dharm is desh ki taraf badhne wala tha.

Chapter chhah wahin se shuru hota hai.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
