Cold open

Purab ka samundari kinara. Aaj ka Odisha. Tab iska naam tha Kalinga.

Ladai khatam ho chuki hai. Kheton mein laashein hain. Gaon khaali hain. Ek lakh log maare gaye hain. Dedh lakh ko qaidi banakar le jaya gaya hai. Aur jo bache hain, unme se bahut se bhookh aur beemari se mar rahe hain.

Jeetne wala raja Magadh ka samrat hai. Uska raaj Kabul se Bengal tak phaila hai. Kalinga uski aakhri badi jeet hai. Ab use jashn manana chahiye.

Par wo jashn nahi manata. Wo pachhtata hai. Aur phir ek ajeeb kaam karta hai. Apna pachhtava pattharon par khudwa deta hai, taaki poora desh padhe. Ki ab aur khoon nahi bahega. Ki asli jeet talwar se nahi, dil jeetne se hoti hai.

Is raja ka naam hai Ashoka. Saal hai lagbhag 261 isa poorv. Aur ye patthar aaj bhi khade hain.

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 4: The Discovery of India. Bharat ki khoj, Sindhu ki ghati se Ashoka tak.

Part 1: Mitti ke neeche ke shehar

Pichhle episode mein Nehru ne poocha tha ki Bharat hai kya. Is chapter mein wo jawab dhoondhne ateet mein utarte hain. Aur shuruaat sach mein zameen ke neeche se hoti hai.

Sindh mein Mohenjo-daro. Punjab mein Harappa. Dono ek-doosre se kaafi door. Dono jagah khudai se hazaaron saal purane shehar nikle. Is sabhyata ke nishaan Kathiawar se Punjab ke Ambala zile tak mile hain, shayad Ganga ki ghati tak bhi. Yaani ye sirf Sindhu ghati ki sabhyata nahi thi. Ek dukh bhi hai: khudai mein jo nikla, wo dhoop aur baarish mein dheere-dheere toot raha tha.

Is sabhyata ki likhaai abhi tak poori padhi nahi gayi. Phir bhi itna saaf hai ki ye shehar kisi shuruaati koshish jaise nahi lagte. Pakki eenton ke ghar. Achhe snaanghar. Naaliyon ka intezaam. Aur hairat ki baat ye ki sabhyata zyadatar duniyavi thi. Dharm tha, par chhaaya hua nahi tha. Nehru Sir John Marshall ka hawala dete hain, jo Bharat ko Iran, Mesopotamia aur Misr ke saath duniya ki sabse purani sabhyataon mein ginte hain.

Yahan ke log Mesopotamia ke Sumer se vyapaar karte the. Akkad mein shayad Bharatiya vyapaariyon ki basti bhi thi. Aur Mohenjo-daro ke log Arab saagar ke kinaare se machhli mangwate the.

Nehru ko sabse zyada ye baat chhooti hai ki Mohenjo-daro ki kuch lok-reetiyan, kaarigari, aur pehnaave ke tareeqe aaj bhi Bharat mein dikhte hain. Beech mein bahut se khaali panne hain. Par ek dhaaga chhe-saat hazaar saal se juda hua hai.

Aur Bharat ki kahani jab pehli baar dikhti hai, to wo rota hua bachcha nahi hai. Wo pehle se bada ho chuka hai. Sapnon mein khoya hua nahi, balki naaliyan aur snaanghar banata hua.

Part 2: Aarya aaye, aur "Hindu" shabd ki kahani

Ye log kaun the? Kahan se aaye? Nehru saaf kehte hain: pata nahi. Shayad ye isi mitti ki sabhyata thi. Kuch vidvaan isme aur dakshin Bharat ki Dravid sabhyata mein gehri samanta dekhte hain.

Aur ye khatam kaise hui? Gordon Childe jaise kuch log maante hain ki koi achanak aafat aayi. Sindhu ki baadh gaon ke gaon baha le jaati hai. Ya mausam badla aur registan kheton mein ghus aaya. Mohenjo-daro mein ret ki parat pe parat jami hai, aur log purani neev par baar-baar oonche ghar banate rahe. Ret ne in shehron ko dhaka bhi, aur bachaya bhi.

Iske kareeb hazaar saal baad uttar-pashchim se Aarya aaye. Lahar dar lahar. Nehru isse Bharat ka pehla bada sangam kehte hain: aane wale Aarya, aur yahan ke Dravid. Dono ke mel se Bharat ki buniyaadi sanskriti bani.

Phir aur log aaye. Irani, Yunani, Shak, Hoon, Islam se pehle ke Turk, shuruaati Isai, Yahudi, Parsi. Har koi aaya, kuch badla, aur isi mein ghul gaya. Nehru ek angrez lekhak ka hawala dete hain jo Bharat ko sab kuch sokh lene wala samundar kehta hai. Aur Nehru ko ye ajeeb bhi lagta hai: jis samaj mein jaati ki itni deewarein hain, usme bahar walon ko apnane ki itni taaqat kaise?

Yahin wo ek shabd par ruk jaate hain: Hindu. Purane Bharatiya granthon mein ye shabd milta hi nahi. Ye Sindhu se nikla hai. Iran aur Madhya Asia ke log Sindhu ke paar rehne walon ko Hindu kehte the. Isi Sindhu se Hindu aur Hindustan bane, aur Indus aur India bhi. Saatvi sadi mein Chini yaatri I-tsing ne likha ki asli naam Aaryadesh hai.

Purana shabd tha dharma. Aur dharma ka matlab mazhab se bada hai. Iski jad ka matlab hai thaam kar rakhna. Kartavya, naitikta, zimmedari, sab isme aate hain.

Nehru ki baat seedhi hai. Bauddh aur Jain Hindu nahi hain, par sau pratishat Bharatiya hain. Ek Isai ya Musalmaan apna mazhab badle bina Bharatiya jeevan apna leta hai. Isliye Bharat ki sanskriti ko "Hindu sanskriti" kehna galat hai. Is sanskriti ki pehchaan ek hi rahi hai: alag-alag cheezon ko jodkar kuch naya banana.

Part 3: Ved, aur zindagi ko haan

Mohenjo-daro milne se pehle Ved hi sabse purane granth maane jaate the. Zyadatar vidvaan Rigved ko lagbhag 1500 isa poorv ka maante hain. Phir bhi ye shayad Yunan aur Israel ke sahitya se purana hai. Iski bhasha Iran ke Avesta se itni milti hai ki dono ek hi parivar ke lagte hain.

Ab Nehru ek imaandar baat kehte hain. Jail mein unhone Ved, Upanishad aur Gita padhe. Kuch samajh aaya, kuch nahi. Par wo inhe aisa "pavitra granth" maankar nahi padh sakte the jise bina sawal maanna ho. Aise padhne se unka man band ho jaata tha. Jab unhone inhe bahut samajhdaar insaanon ka likha maana, tab man khula. Unke liye ye zyada badi baat hai ki ek insaan khud oonchai tak pahunche aur doosron ko bhi upar uthaye.

Puraanon ke saath bhi yahi. Sach ghatna maano, to bekaar lagte hain. Kalpana maano, to inki khoobsurti dikhne lagti hai.

"Ved" shabd "vid" se bana hai, yaani jaanna. Us waqt ka saara gyaan ek jagah: prarthana, yagya ke niyam, jaadu-tona, aur kudrat par shaandaar kavita. Inme moorti-pooja nahi, mandir nahi. Aur zindagi ke liye zabardast utsaah. Shuru ke Aarya jeene mein itne mast the ki aatma ki zyada parwaah hi nahi karte the.

Dheere-dheere sawal aate hain. Pehle kai devta, phir ek Ishwar, phir ye khayal ki sab kuch ek hi hai. Rigved ke ek bhajan mein srishti par hi sawal uthta hai: shuru mein kya tha, aur kya kisi ko sach mein pata hai? Nehru yahan Tagore ke shabdon ka sahara lete hain. Ved ke aakhir mein aate hain Upanishad, yaani Vedanta, Ved ka ant.

Yahan Nehru ek galatfehmi par seedha vaar karte hain. Kaha jaata hai ki Bharat "aadhyatmik" desh hai, is duniya se udaasin. Unka jawab: ghulam aur bhookhe desh ki aadhyatmik mahaanta ka koi matlab nahi. Gareeb aur ghulam log aksar parlok ki taraf dekhte hain, kyunki ye duniya unke liye bani hi nahi lagti.

Jab-jab Bharat phala-phoola, tab sangeet, naach, natak, chitrakala aur sahitya bhi phale. Jo sanskriti zindagi ko bekaar samajhti, wo ye sab kaise rachti? Aur hazaaron saal tikti kaise? Nehru ke hisaab se Bharatiya soch ka saar sannyas nahi, anaasakti hai. Poori taaqat se kaam karo, par phal ki chinta mein mat ulajho.

Part 4: Varna, ashram aur dharma

Ab ek kadwa sawal: jaati kaise bani? Shuru mein deewar Aaryon aur gair-Aaryon ke beech thi. Aaryon mein khud koi bantwara nahi tha. "Arya" shabd ki jad ka matlab hai jotna. Wo kisaan the. Wahi kisaan pujari bhi, sainik bhi, vyapaari bhi. Phir kaam bante, kaam se varg bane, aur varg jaatiyon mein badal gaye. Dheere-dheere "Arya" ka matlab nasl nahi, "shreshth" ho gaya.

Samaj ko chaar hisson mein baanta, to insaan ki zindagi ko bhi. Pehle padhai aur sanyam. Phir grihasth. Phir samaj ki seva, bina swarth ke. Aur aakhir mein sannyas. Zindagi ko apnane aur chhodne, dono ke liye jagah.

Ek parampara ye bhi thi ki sabse ooncha aadar paise ya talwar ka nahi, gyaan aur tyaag ka ho. Nehru kehte hain ki isi wajah se Gandhi, jo Brahmin nahi hain, bina paise aur bina pad ke karodon dilon par raaj karte hain. Kisi desh ko samajhna ho, to dekho wo kis tarah ke neta ke peeche chalta hai.

Is sabhyata ke beech mein dharma tha, aur dharma ek bade niyam ka hissa tha, Rita, jo poore brahmand ko chalata hai. Zor haq par nahi, farz par tha. Aaj ki duniya ulta karti hai.

Isi daur mein, Nehru dukh ke saath kehte hain, "chhoo-mat" wali soch bhi shuru hui. Jo vyavastha samaj ko sthir rakhne ke liye bani thi, wo dheere-dheere samaj aur dimaag dono ki jail ban gayi. Suraksha mili, par aage badhne ki keemat par.

Phir bhi is sanskriti ka dum dekhiye. Hamle aaye, sadiyan beeti, par dhaara nahi tooti. Nehru ek angrez vidvaan ka hawala dete hain, jinke hisaab se China ke alawa koi aur desh apni bhasha aur reetiyon ko teen hazaar saal se lagataar nahi jod sakta.

Part 5: Upanishad: sawal, sawal aur sawal

Lagbhag 800 isa poorv. Aarya bas chuke hain. Samaj sthir aur khush-haal hai. Aur ab aate hain Upanishad.

Inme Ved ka aadar hai, par halka mazaak bhi. Purane devta ab santusht nahi karte. Pujariyon ke karmkaand par vyang hai. Ek jagah unhe andhon ki qataar kaha gaya hai, jinhe andhe hi raah dikha rahe hain. Par purane se naata nahi toda jaata. Use seedhi banakar aage chadha jaata hai.

Upanishad sach ki talaash hain. Ved ko bhi "neeche ka gyaan" kaha gaya hai. Ooncha gyaan andar ka hai. Zyadatar hissa guru aur shishya ke sawal-jawab mein hai, shayad class ke notes jaisa.

Nehru ko inki metaphysics poori samajh nahi aati, aur wo ye kehte bhi hain. Par inka tareeqa unhe bhaata hai. Hukm nahi, sawal. Man kis ke kehne par chalta hai? Hawa ruk kyun nahi sakti? Paani behta kyun rehta hai? Aitareya Brahmana ka ek geet har pankti ke baad ek hi shabd dohrata hai: "charaiveti", yaani chalte raho.

Aur isme ghutne tekne wali vinamrata nahi hai. Ek subah ki prarthana mein insaan suraj se kehta hai ki tujhe roshan karne wala main hi hoon. Nehru is aatmvishwas par hairaan hain.

Aatma kya hai? Use bataya nahi ja sakta. Bas itna kaha ja sakta hai: ye nahi, ye nahi. Ya phir: tum wahi ho. Agar sab mein ek hi tatva hai, to kisi se nafrat kaise? Nehru ise "aadhyatmik loktantra" kehte hain. Ye soch jaati ki deewaron ke bilkul khilaaf jaati hai. Aur isi se Bharat mein "jiyo aur jeene do" ka mizaaj bana.

Ek sawal ka jawab Nehru ko hairaan karta hai. Brahmand kahan se aaya, kahan jaata hai? Jawab: azadi se uthta hai, azadi mein tikta hai, azadi mein hi ghul jaata hai. Nehru maante hain ki iska poora matlab unhe nahi pata. Bas itna ki in rishiyon ko azadi se gehra pyaar tha. Schopenhauer aur Max Müller jaise Europe ke vichaarakon ne bhi Upanishadon ki khul kar tareef ki, aur Nehru unke shabd darj karte hain.

Par doosra paksh bhi hai. Upanishad ki soch kuch chune hue logon tak simti rahi. Aam logon ke liye ise andhvishwas mein ghol diya gaya. Har insaan ko apni mukti ki fikr thi, samaj ki nahi. Sab kuch bardasht karne ki aadat ne buri reetiyon ko bhi zinda rakha, aur pujari varg ne logon ke andhvishwas par apni dukaan chalayi. Nehru Aurobindo ka ek khayal yaad karte hain: Upanishad ke zamane ka koi aadmi aaj aa jaye, to dekhega ki uske log purane chhilkon se chipke hain, aur gooda kho chuke hain.

Phir ek khoya hua adhyaay. Upanishad ke baad bhautikvaad ki ek tez lehar aayi. Ye log Ved, pujari aur parlok, kisi ko nahi maante the. Par inki ek bhi kitaab nahi bachi. Hum inhe sirf inke dushmanon ki kitaabon se jaante hain. Nehru ko shak hai ki ye kitaabein mitayi gayi. Phir bhi chauthi sadi isa poorv ka Arthashastra ise Bharat ke bade darshanon mein ginta hai.

Isi uthal-puthal se Jain darshan, Bauddh darshan, mahakavya aur Gita nikle. Nehru ise Athens ke patan ke baad ke Yunan se jodte hain. Fark ye ki Yunan sambhal nahi paya. Bharat sambhal gaya, aur agle hazaar saal sirjan se bhare rahe.

Part 6: Ramayan, Mahabharat aur Vikram ki paheli

Nehru ka bachpan. Maa aur ghar ki badi auratein unhe Ramayan aur Mahabharat ki kahaniyan sunati thi, jaise Europe ka bachcha pariyon ki kahaniyan sunta hai. Har saal unhe Ramleela dikhane le jaaya jaata. Khule maidan mein hazaaron log. Abhinay kachcha tha, par kisi ko fark nahi padta. Kahani sabko zabaani yaad thi.

Baad mein Joan of Arc, Alice, Akbar-Birbal, Sherlock Holmes aur Jhansi ki Rani bhi aaye. Par sabke peeche wahi pauranik parda tha jo bachpan mein bana.

Nehru kehte hain ki duniya mein in do granthon jaisa asar shayad kisi kitaab ka aam logon par nahi raha. Ek hi kahani vidvaan ke liye bhi, aur anpadh kisaan ke liye bhi. Ye kathaein sach, vachan nibhane, himmat aur tyaag ki seekh deti hain. Ho sakta hai ye na bataayein ki kya hua tha. Par ye bataati hain ki log kya maante the, aur kya banna chahte the.

Yahin Nehru ek kami par ungli rakhte hain. Yunani, Chini aur Arab itihaas likhte the. Purane Bharatiya nahi. Itihaas ke naam par bas ek kitaab hai: Kalhan ki Rajatarangini, baarahvi sadi ka Kashmir ka itihaas. Nateeja? Darshan mein jo dimaag itna tez tha, wo tathyon ke maamle mein bhola rehta hai.

Misaal taaza hai. April 1944, yaani pichhle hi mahine, Vikram Samvat ke do hazaar saal poore hue, aur desh bhar mein jashn hua. Par 57 isa poorv ke aas-paas Vikram naam ka koi raja milta hi nahi. Ek Vikramaditya chauthi sadi isvi mein the, jinhone Hoonon ko khadeda. Shayad Malwa ka ek purana samvat baad mein unke naam se jod diya gaya. Nehru ko hairat hai ki samajhdaar log bhi is kahani ko itihaas banane mein jut gaye.

Ab Mahabharat. Ramayan ek kasi hui kavita hai, Mahabharat poora vishvakosh. Badi ladai shayad chaudahvi sadi isa poorv ke aas-paas maani jaati hai, haalaanki ye tareekh bhi dhundhli hai. Isi se poore Bharatvarsh ka khayal pakka hota hai, jisme Gandhar, yaani aaj ke Afghanistan ka hissa, bhi shaamil tha. Gandhari wahin ki thi. Rajdhani Hastinapur aur Indraprastha, aaj ki Dilli ke aas-paas.

Mahabharat mein samaj ki bhalaai par zor hai. Safalta sach, sanyam aur ahimsa se hai, jaati ya khaandaan se nahi. Nehru ki pasandeeda seekh: asantosh hi pragati ki prerna hai. Aur ek tathya bhi: Mahabharat mein mehmaanon ko gomaans parosne ke zikr hain. Us par rok baad mein lagi.

Mahabharat ke beech mein hai Bhagavad Gita. Saat sau shlok. Tilak, Aurobindo aur Gandhi, sabne iski apni vyakhya likhi. Gandhi isse ahimsa nikalte hain, doosre nyay ke liye hathiyar uthana.

Nehru ke liye Gita sankat ki kavita hai, jab kartavya aapas mein takra rahe hon. Sandesh hai: haath par haath rakhkar mat baitho, kaam karo, par phal ke peeche mat bhaago. Aur ye har jaati ke liye hai. Pachchees sau saal mein Bharat bahut badla, par har pidhi ko Gita mein kuch zinda mila.

Part 7: Purane Bharat mein roz ki zindagi

Darshan bahut hua. Ab bazaar chalte hain.

Nehru shikayat karte hain ki itihaaskaron ne raajaon aur darshan par bahut likha, par aam log kaise jeete the, is par kam. Do khazaane hain. Ek, Buddha se pehle ke daur ki Jataka kathaein. Doosra, Kautilya ka Arthashastra.

Jataka ki duniya mein raja ka pad pehle chunav se aata tha, phir khaandaani ho gaya. Zulmi raaja ke khilaaf baghawat ke kisse bhi hain. Gaon apna kaam khud chalate the. Lagaan upaj ka lagbhag chhatha hissa tha.

Bazaar mein aam, anjeer, angoor, kela aur khajoor bikte. Phoolon ki maala tab bhi utni hi pyaari thi jitni aaj. Chawal, gehun aur doodh khaas the. Maans bhi khaya jaata tha, aur sharaab ki dukaanein bhi thi. Resham, oon aur sooti kapda. Chandan ka itr. Aur peshe: vaid, shalya-chikitsak, abhineta, nat, kathputli wale. Andhere paksh bhi the. Gharelu daas the, aur chandaal kahe jaane wale achhoot, jinka kaam laashein uthana tha.

Kaarigaron ki shreniyan, yaani guild, taaqatwar thi. Seth, yaani shreshthi, bada aadmi tha. Aur ek anokhi baat: ek hi kaam karne walon ke poore gaon. Badhaiyon ka ek gaon, hazaar parivaron ka. Nehru maante hain ki shayad jaati vyavastha aise hi phaili.

Vyapaar door tak tha. Paanchvi sadi isa poorv mein Misr ke Memphis mein Bharatiya vyapaariyon ki basti thi. Bharuch se jahaaz Babylon jaate. Banaras aur Patna se nadiyon ke raaste Lanka tak. Purani Arabi mein talwar ko "muhannad" kehte the, yaani Hind ki. Aur Dilli ka lohe ka stambh aaj tak jang nahi khata.

Sikandar ka hamla, Nehru ke hisaab se, ek chhota sa chhapa tha. Seema ke ek chhote sardar ne itni takkar di ki uski fauj ne aage badhne se mana kar diya.

Panini ne chhathi-saatvi sadi isa poorv mein Sanskrit ka vyakaran likha, jo aaj bhi maanak hai. Shunya, dashamlav ki sthaan-maan paddhati, aur algebra mein ajnaat sankhya ke liye akshar, ye Bharat ki den hain. Yunani das hazaar se aage ki ginti ka naam nahi rakhte the. Bharat mein das ki ghaat atharah tak ke naam the. Isliye jab Darwin ke baad Europe hil gaya, Bharat ko jhatka nahi laga. Uski kathaon mein pehle se karodon saal ke yug the.

Aaj ke Peshawar ke paas Takshashila tha, jahan khaas taur par chikitsa padhayi jaati. Ameer gharon ke ladke akele, bina hathiyar, wahan padhne jaate.

Manu ke qanoon mein aurat hamesha pita, pati ya bete par nirbhar thi. Par kahaniyon mein unka maan hai, aur vidushi auraton ka zikr baar-baar aata hai. Nehru jodte hain ki tab bhi ye Yunan, Rome aur madhyakaal ke Europe se behtar tha.

Aur Sikandar ke itihaaskar Arrian ki ek baat Nehru ko bahut pasand hai: gaane aur naachne ka itna shauk kisi aur qaum ko nahi tha.

Part 8: Mahavir aur Buddha

Chhathi sadi isa poorv. Do Kshatriya rajkumar, ek hi zamane ke. Mahavir, jinse Jain dharm aaya, aur Gautam Buddha.

Dono ne Ved ka adhikaar nahi maana. Srishti ke kisi pehle kartaa par ya to inkaar kiya, ya chuppi saadhi. Dono ne ahimsa par zor diya. Jain darshan ki ek baat Nehru ko achhi lagti hai: sach is par nirbhar karta hai ki aap kahan khade hokar dekh rahe hain.

Paramparagat hisaab se Buddha assi saal ki umar mein 544 isa poorv mein gaye. Aur ek ittefaq: Nehru jis din ye panna likh rahe hain, wo Vaishakh ki poornima hai, Bauddh naya saal 2488. Kaha jaata hai isi poornima ko Buddha janme, isi ko gyaan mila, aur isi ko nirvaan hua.

Buddha ne pujariyon, karmkaand, chamatkaar aur andhvishwas, sab par hamla kiya. Nehru kehte hain ki unki baatein band kamre ke baad pahaadon ki taaza hawa jaisi lagti hain.

Wo kehte the ki meri baat aadar ki wajah se mat maano. Jaise sone ko aag mein parakhte hain, waise parakho. Ishwar hai ya nahi? Na haan, na naa. Aatma? Is par bhi chuppi. Jahan gyaan mumkin nahi, wahan faisla rok do. Par kaarya aur kaaran ka niyam atal hai.

Dukh hai, dukh ka kaaran hai, dukh ka ant hai, aur us ant tak pahunchne ka raasta hai. Ye hain chaar arya satya. Raasta beech ka hai. Na bhog mein doobo, na shareer ko sukhao. Kripa se nahi, apni mehnat se. Aur nirvaan bujh jaana nahi, jhoothi ichhaon ka khatam ho jaana hai.

Ek kissa Nehru ko pyaara hai. Buddha ne mutthi mein kuch sookhe patte liye aur Anand se poocha, kya bas yahi patte hain? Anand ne kaha, patjhad mein to anginat patte gir rahe hain. Buddha ne kaha, maine tumhe bhi mutthi bhar sach diye hain. Baaki sach anginat hain.

Jaati par Buddha ne seedha hamla nahi kiya, par apne sangh mein use maana bhi nahi. Unke baad sadiyon tak jaati lachili rahi. Bahar se aane wale do-teen peedhi mein Kshatriya ban jaate. Sikkon par dikhta hai: dada ka naam videshi, pote ka Sanskrit. Kai Rajput khaandaan Shakon aur Hoonon se nikle. Aur aaj, Nehru kehte hain, Gandhi jaati ke jhaad-jhankhaad ko kaat rahe hain, aur aaj ki zindagi uski jad hila rahi hai. Saath mein chetavni: Pashchim mein nasl ke naam par nayi jaatiyan ban rahi hain.

Nehru ka Buddha se rishta bachpan ka hai. Edwin Arnold ki "Light of Asia" unki pasandeeda kitaab thi. Bade hokar wo Buddha se jude thikanon par jaate, kabhi raasta badal kar bhi. Par wo saaf kehte hain ki Bauddh dharm ki niraasha unhe nahi jachti. Wo khud ko andar se "pagan" kehte hain, jise zindagi aur kudrat ki bharmaar pasand hai.

Phir bhi Buddha ki moorti unhe rok leti hai. Kamal par baithe, aankhein band, shaant. Pehli nazar mein door, pahunch se bahar. Par dobara dekho, to us shaanti ke peeche ek gehri aag hai. Aur Nehru ko lagta hai ki wo kehte hain: ladai se bhaago mat, shaant aankhon se uska saamna karo.

Part 9: Chandragupta aur Chanakya

323 isa poorv. Babylon se khabar aati hai ki Sikandar mar gaya. Chandragupta aur uske guru Chanakya mauqa pehchaante hain. Wo bahar walon ke khilaaf logon ko uthate hain. Yunani fauj Takshashila se khadedi jaati hai. Do saal ke andar Chandragupta Pataliputra par kabza kar leta hai. Maurya samrajya ki neev padti hai.

Sikandar ka senapati Seleucus wapas aata hai, Sindhu paar karta hai, aur haarta hai. Use Kabul aur Herat tak ka ilaqa chhodna padta hai, aur Chandragupta uski beti se vivaah karta hai. Dakshin ko chhodkar, Arab saagar se Bangal ki khaadi aur Kabul tak, pehli baar itna bada kendriya raajya banta hai.

Chanakya, yaani Kautilya, ko Bharat ka Machiavelli kaha jaata hai. Nehru kehte hain ki wo usse kahin bade the. Mudrarakshasa naatak mein unki tasveer hai: chaalak, ghamandi, apmaan kabhi na bhoolne wale. Saath hi saadi zindagi, aur kaam poora hone par sab chhodkar chale jaane ki ichha. Samrat unke liye maalik nahi, pyaara shishya tha.

Unka Arthashastra raajya chalane ki poori kitaab hai. Kootneeti, yudh, kheti, khadaan, bazaar, jail, passport, janganana, sinchaai. Vidhva-vivah ko maanyata hai, aur kuch haalat mein talaaq ko bhi. Raja rajyabhishek par praja ki seva ki kasam khaata tha. Aur zulm kare, to log use hata sakte the.

Ye taanashahi thi, Nehru maante hain. Kuch maayno mein aaj ki dictatorships jaisi. Par gaon apna kaam khud chalate the. Ek Raajpath rajdhani se uttar-pashchim seema tak jaata tha, jagah-jagah dharamshalaon ke saath. China ke resham ka bhi zikr hai.

Aur Pataliputra, aaj ka Patna, Ganga aur Sone ke sangam par. Yunani doot Megasthenes ke hisaab se lagbhag nau meel lamba. Lakdi ki deewar, 570 burj, 64 darwaze, aur chaaron taraf gehri khaai. Ghar bhi zyadatar lakdi ke, shayad bhukamp ki wajah se. Nehru 1934 ke Bihar ke bhukamp ko yaad karte hain. Aag se bachne ke liye har ghar mein seedhi aur paani ke bartan zaroori the.

Aur sabse hairat ki baat: shehar ki chuni hui nagarpalika. Tees sadasya, paanch-paanch ki chhe samitiyan. Udyog, janm-mrityu ka hisaab, yaatriyon ki dekhbhaal. Saath milkar paani, safai, baag aur imaaratein.

Part 10: Ashoka

Chandragupta ke baad beta Bindusara, aur phir pota Ashoka. Wahi Ashoka jisse humne shuruaat ki thi.

Kalinga ke baad Ashoka ne apne man ki baat shilalekhon par khudwayi. Usne likhwaya ki Kalinga mein jitne mare, unka sauva ya hazaarva hissa bhi ab use manzoor nahi. Asli vijay dharma se dil jeetna hai.

Aur ye sirf baatein nahi thi. Usne likhwaya ki khaate waqt ho, sote waqt ho, ya rath mein, praja ka kaam use turant bataya jaye. Wo dhyaan mein khoya bhikshu nahi, din-raat kaam karne wala raja tha.

Uske doot Syria, Misr, Macedonia tak gaye. Madhya Asia, Burma aur Siam tak. Apne bete Mahendra aur beti Sanghamitra ko usne Lanka bheja. Kahin zabardasti nahi. Wo pakka Bauddh tha, par doosre panthon ka bhi aadar karta tha.

Bauddh dharm Kashmir se Lanka tak phaila, phir Nepal, Tibet, China aur Mongolia tak. Bharat mein shaakahaar badha, sharaab kam hui, aur pashu-bali par rok lagi.

Pataliputra mein Ashoka ke mahal ka sainkdon khambhon wala hall kareeb tees saal pehle khoda gaya. Lakdi ke latthe do hazaar saal baad bhi itne chikne aur saabut the ki jod tak nahi dikhte the. Bharat ki garmi aur deemak ke beech ye kaise bacha, ye aaj bhi raaz hai.

Ashoka 232 isa poorv mein gaya, iktalis saal raaj karke. Nehru H.G. Wells ka hawala dete hain, jinke hisaab se itihaas ke hazaaron raajaon ke beech Ashoka akela taare ki tarah chamakta hai.

Kalinga ke maidan se shuru hui ye kahani ek sawal par khatam hoti hai. Ek samrat jo jeet kar pachhtaya. Kitne aur ne aisa kiya?

Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, Sindhu lipi. Nehru ne 1944 mein likha ki ye abhi poori padhi nahi gayi. Assi saal baad bhi ye baat sach hai. Padhne ke kai daave hue, par koi bhi sabko manzoor nahi hua.

Doosri, "Hindu" shabd. Nehru ka kehna tha ki Bharat ki sanskriti ko "Hindu sanskriti" kehna galat hai, kyunki Bauddh, Jain, Isai aur Musalmaan bhi isi ke hisse hain. Ye behas aaj bhi utni hi garam hai.

Teesri, Vikram Samvat wala kissa. Nehru ki shikayat thi ki hum parampara ko bina jaanche itihaas maan lete hain. WhatsApp ke zamane mein ye chetavni aur bhi kaam ki lagti hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru kehte hain ki kisi desh ko samajhna ho, to dekho wo kaisa neta chunta hai. Aaj ke Bharat par ye kasauti lagayein, to kya dikhta hai?

Outro

Agle episode mein Maurya ke baad ka Bharat. Gupta yug, Sanskrit natak, dakshin Bharat, aur China aur Dakshin-purvi Asia tak pahunchi Bharatiya sanskriti. Phir ek mushkil sawal: itni chamak ke baad zang kahan se lagi?

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
