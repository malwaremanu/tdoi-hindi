Cold open

Saatvin sadi. China ki rajdhani. Ek jawan Bauddh bhikshu lambe safar par nikalne wala hai. Manzil hai Bharat, jise Chini log "pashchim ka desh" kehte hain. Raasta registaan, barfeele darre aur daakuon se bhara hai.

Vidaai ke waqt samraat khud aate hain. Ek pyala mangwaate hain. Phir apni muthi mein China ki thodi si mitti lekar us pyale mein ghol dete hain. Bhikshu se kehte hain: ise pee lo. Paraye desh ke dher saare sone se apni zameen ki ek muthi mitti zyada keemti hoti hai.

Bhikshu ka naam hai Hsuan Tsang. Wo pyala peeta hai aur nikal padta hai. Saal 629 mein wo Bharat pahunchta hai. Barson yahan ghoomta hai, Nalanda mein padhta hai, aur jab lautta hai to uske saath sainkdon pothiyan hain.

Socho, ek aisa desh jahan pahunchne ke liye log apni jaan daav par lagate the. Wo desh kaisa tha? Aur wahi desh, kuch sadiyon baad, andar se sookhne kyun laga?

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 5: Yugon ke aar-paar.

Part 1: Guptaon ka sunehra daur

Pichhle episode mein humne Ashoka ko dekha tha. Uske baad Maurya samrajya dheere-dheere bikhar gaya. Uski jagah Shunga vansh aaya, jiska ilaqa kaafi chhota tha.

Uttar-pashchim se Yunani raja aage badhe, jinhe Indo-Greek ya Bactrian kehte hain. Kabul se Punjab tak phail gaye. Unka ek raja, Menander, Pataliputra tak chadh aaya, par haarkar lautna pada. Phir ek ajeeb baat hui. Menander Bharat ke rang mein aisa ranga ki Bauddh ban gaya. Bauddh kahaniyon mein use Raja Milinda ke naam se yaad kiya jaata hai, lagbhag sant ki tarah.

Madhya Bharat mein, Sanchi ke paas Besnagar mein, pehli sadi isa poorv ka ek pathar ka khambha khada hai. Use Heliodorus stambh kehte hain. Us par Sanskrit mein lekh hai, jo ek Yunani ne likhwaya tha. Ye saboot hai ki bahar se aaye log kaise Bharat ke ho jaate the. Isi mel se Gandhara ki kala bani, jisme Yunani shaili aur Bauddh vishay ek saath dikhte hain.

Phir Kushan aaye. Unke zamane mein Bauddh dharm do hisson mein bant gaya: Mahayana aur Hinayana. Behas badi-badi sabhaon mein hui, jahan desh bhar se log aaye. Is behas ka sabse bada naam hai Nagarjuna, pehli sadi isvi ke vidwan. Unhi ki wajah se Bharat mein Mahayana jeeta. Mahayana China pahuncha. Lanka aur Burma ne Hinayana pakde rakha.

Kushan bhi Bharatiya rang mein dhal gaye the, phir bhi bahari raaj ke khilaaf andar hi andar ek gussa pal raha tha. Chauthi sadi ki shuruaat mein ye gussa ek andolan ban gaya. Ek aur Chandragupta uthe, naye hamlawaron ko bahar kiya, aur saal 320 isvi mein Gupta samrajya ki neev padi.

Nehru is daur ko ek naam dete hain: rashtravaad, jo badhte-badhte samrajyavaad ban jaata hai. Baar-baar ke hamlon ne logon ko apni zameen aur apni sanskriti bachane ki fikr mein daal diya. Jo pehle se andar aa chuke the, unhe apna liya gaya. Par naye aane walon ke liye darwaze band. Purane aadarsh sakht hone lage. Bharat jaise apne khol mein simatne laga. Haan, wo khol bahut bada aur gehra tha.

Gupta raja ladai mein bhi tez the aur kala mein bhi. Samudragupta ko to "Bharat ka Napoleon" tak kaha gaya. Sahitya aur kala ke liye ye chamakdaar zamana tha.

Kareeb dedh sau saal Gupta poore uttar Bharat par majboot raaj karte rahe. Phir Madhya Asia se Shwet Hun aaye, wahi Hun jo Attila ke saath Europe ko raund rahe the. Unki krurta ne aakhirkaar logon ko ek kar diya. Yashovarman ki aguwai mein ek sangh bana aur Huno ko haraya gaya. Unka sardar Mihiragula pakda gaya. Gupta vanshaj Baladitya ne Bharat ki reet ke mutabik use maaf karke jaane diya. Badle mein Mihiragula laut kar aaya aur apne hi upkaari par dhokhe se hamla kar diya.

Uttar mein Hun raaj lagbhag pachaas saal chala. Bahut se Hun yahin bas gaye aur dheere-dheere Bharat ke samandar mein ghul gaye. Saatvin sadi mein kuch Hun sardar phir sar uthane lage. Unhe Kanauj ke raja Harshavardhan ne kuchla aur uttar se madhya Bharat tak bada raajya banaya. Harsha khud kavi aur natakkar the. Mahayana Bauddh the, par Hindu dharm ko bhi sahara dete the. Inhi ke zamane mein Hsuan Tsang aaye. Harsha ki maut 648 mein hui. Theek usi waqt Arab ke registaan se Islam uth raha tha.

Ab dakshin. Maurya ke baad hazaar saal se zyada dakshin mein bade raajya phalte-phoolte rahe. Andhra, phir pashchim mein Chalukya, phir Rashtrakuta. Aur neeche Pallava, jinhone samudra paar basne ke abhiyaan sabse zyada chalaye. Phir Chola, jinka raaj Lanka aur dakshini Burma tak gaya. Aakhri bade Chola raja Rajendra ki maut 1044 mein hui.

Dakshin samudri taaqat tha. Wahan ke jahaaz door desh tak maal le jaate. Wahan Yunaniyon ki bastiyan thi, Roman sikke mile hain. Chalukya darbar aur Iran ke Sassanid raja ek doosre ke paas doot bhejte the.

Uttar ke hamle dakshin tak seedhe nahi pahunche. Par uttar se kaarigar, shilpi aur raajmistri bhaagkar dakshin gaye. Is tarah dakshin purani kala ka garh ban gaya, aur aage chalkar kattar paramparaon ka bhi.

Nehru yahan ek galatfehmi door karte hain. Raja aur samrajya ki is lambi list se lagta hai ki Bharat hamesha jung mein tha. Aisa nahi tha. Maurya, Kushan, Gupta, Andhra, Chalukya, har vansh do-teen sau saal chala. Nehru chutki lete hain ki ye angrezi raaj se lamba hai. Zyadatar shanti thi, aur raja kala ko badhava dene mein garv karte the.

Jung ke bhi niyam the. Kuch purane samjhaute mile hain, jinme ladne wale raja gaon ki panchayaton se vaada karte hain ki fasal ko nuksaan nahi hoga, aur galti se hua to bharpaai hogi. Purane shastra kehte the ki zehar bujhe teer nahi, chhupe hathiyar nahi, sote hue ya sharan maangne wale par vaar nahi, sundar imaraton ko todna nahi. Chanakya ke zamane tak ye soch badalne lagi thi. Unke Arthashastra mein aisi mashinon ka zikr hai jo ek baar mein sau logon ko maar dein. Nehru maante hain ki ye shayad purani kahaniyan hain, baarood ka koi saboot nahi.

Aur ek kadwa nishkarsh. Ye kehna ki angrez aakar pehli baar shanti laaye, Nehru ke hisaab se sabse ajeeb bhram hai. Sach ye hai ki angrez tab aaye jab Bharat sabse kamzor tha. Aur wahi kamzori unke aane ki wajah bani.

Part 2: Sab ko apna lene wala desh

Ek kavi ki panktiyan hain, jo Nehru quote karte hain, ki Poorab ne duniya ki hulchal dekhi aur phir apne vichaaron mein doob gaya. Nehru maante hain ki Bharat ko sochna hamesha pyara raha. Usne talwar waale ya paise waale ko vidwan se upar nahi rakha. Bure din mein bhi wo vichaar se sukoon dhoondhta raha.

Phir ek ulti baat. Hum khud ko alag rakhne wale log rahe, apni nasl par ghamand karne wale. Aur phir bhi hum nasl ka ek ajeeb mishran ban gaye. Arya, Dravid, aur unke baad lehar par lehar: Irani, Yunani, Shak, Kushan, Turk, Mongol. Sab aaye, raaj kiya, shehar basaye, aur phir isi bheed mein kho gaye.

Nehru Rajputon ki misaal dete hain. Aaj unse zyada Bharatiya aur apni parampara par garv karne wala koi nahi. Par kaha jaata hai ki kai Rajput Shakon se, kuch Huno se bhi, nikle hain. Jat kisaan, jo apni zameen mein dakhal bardaasht nahi karta, uski jad bhi Shakon mein hai. Kathiawad ke lambe-chaude Kathi bhi. Par ab sab Bharatiya hain.

Ye kaise hua? Nehru ka andaaza hai ki shuruaat mein hi Bharat ke man mein zindagi ka koi gehra vichaar baith gaya, jo itna taaqatwar tha ki jo bhi aaya, uske rang mein ranga gaya. Saath hi wo khud ko tokte hain. Itne bade desh ko ek vichaar mein baandhna khatarnaak hai. Yahan har baat ke ulat baat bhi mil jaati hai.

Phir bhi ek sach hai. Bharat aur China, dono hazaaron saal tak badalte rahe, phir bhi apni pehchaan bachaye rahe. Ye kudrat se mel ke bina mumkin nahi. Shayad wo taaqat kab ki chuk gayi aur ab sirf khol bacha hai. Par taaqat thi.

Samaj mein lachak bhi thi. Riwaaj badalne se niyam badal jaata. Naya group apni reet ke saath bada parivar ban jaata. Jab tak maqsad sthirta aur suraksha tha, ye dhaancha chala. Asli chunauti tab aayi jab tarakki ka naya vichaar aaya, jo ruke hue dhaanche mein fit nahi hota tha. Nehru ek mazedaar ulat-pher dikhate hain. Pashchim mein tarakki ke baad ab log suraksha maang rahe hain. Bharat mein suraksha ki kami ne logon ko tarakki ki taraf dhakela hai.

Niyam saaf hai. Jo bahar se aaya aur ghul-mil gaya, usne diya bhi aur paaya bhi. Jo alag raha, wo mit gaya.

Ab Iran. Bharat ka sabse purana aur sabse lamba rishta Iran se hai. Dono ek hi jad se nikle. Ved aur Zarathushtra ka dharm milte-julte hain. Vedic Sanskrit aur Avesta ki bhasha bhi. Rigved mein Iraniyon ko "Parshava" kaha gaya, jisse aage chalkar "Parsi" shabd bana.

Chhathi sadi isa poorv mein Darius ka samrajya Sindh aur Punjab ke hisse tak tha. Yunani itihaaskar Herodotus likhte hain ki Bharat wala soobha uska sabse ameer aur sabse aabaad soobha tha, aur wo 360 talent sona-dhool kar mein deta tha. Socho, Sindh tab registaan nahi tha. Kehte hain Ashoka ki imaraton par Iran ke Persepolis ki chhaap hai.

Kabul, Kandahar aur Seistan wo ilaqe the jahan Bharatiya aur Irani milte the. Ek zamane mein unhe "Safed Hind" kaha jaata tha.

Mughal daur mein ye rishta sabse gehra hua. Babar Samarkand ke us mahaul se aaye jahan Irani kala chamak rahi thi. Humayun ne haarkar Iran ke Safavi shah ke yahan panaah li, aur unki madad se lautkar Delhi jeeti. Phir Iran se kavi, kalakaar aur vidwan Mughal darbar aate rahe. Persian sadiyon tak darbar ki bhasha rahi. Dakshin ki Dravid bhashaon mein bhi Farsi shabd hain. Ek French vidwan ne Taj Mahal ko Bharat ke shareer mein Iran ki aatma kaha.

Afsos ki is lambi dosti ki aakhri yaad Nadir Shah ka bhayanak hamla hai, kareeb do sau saal pehle. Phir angrez aaye aur unhone Asia ki taraf ke saare raaste band kar diye. Samandar ka raasta England ki taraf khula, par Iran, Madhya Asia aur China se zameen ka rishta kat gaya. Nehru ise angrezi raaj ka sabse dukhad nateeja maante hain.

Book likhne se do mahine pehle, Allahabad mein ek Irani sanskritik dal aaya. Uske neta ne ek purani Farsi katha sunayi: do bichhde bhai, jinke parivar sadiyon baad sirf apni bansuri ki purani dhunon se ek doosre ko pehchaan lete hain.

Part 3: Greece, aur Bharat ka rangmanch

Europe ke kai vidwan maante the ki duniya mein jo kuch achha hai, Greece se aaya. Nehru ko Poorab aur Pashchim ke "buniyaadi fark" wali baat hi samajh nahi aati. Unki nazar mein purane Bharat aur Greece mein bahut kuch saajha tha. Dono zindagi ko poora jeete the, aur dono andar ki zindagi ko bhi bada maante the. Dono jagah mausam ke hisaab se tyohaar the. Hamare yahan aaj bhi Diwali, Holi, fasal ke tyohaar, aur Raas-leela ke naach hain.

Greece ki ek cheez anokhi thi: prayog wala vigyaan. Ye Alexandria mein sabse zyada phala, 330 se 130 isa poorv ke beech. Uske jaisa kuch Bharat mein nahi tha. Bharat ki apni den thi ganit, shunya aur sthaan-maan ki pranaali. Nehru maante hain ki dono samaj ki zaroorat se nikle: Alexandria mein samudri safar, Bharat mein vyapar.

Kuch fark bhi. Purane Bharat mein auraton ka parda nahi tha, sirf raaj gharane mein kuch had tak. Vidushi auratein behas mein hissa leti thi. Greece mein shaadi ek anubandh thi, Bharat mein sanskaar.

Yunani maal Bharat mein khoob aata tha. Bharuch ke bandargah par sharaab aati thi. Ek Tamil kavi Yavanon ke jahaazon mein aayi thandi, khushbudaar sharaab ka zikr karta hai. Aur ek mazedaar kissa. Pataliputra ke raja Bindusara, Ashoka ke pita, ne Yunani raja Antiochus ko chitthi likhi: meethi sharaab, sookhe anjeer, aur ek darshanik bhejo. Jawab aaya: anjeer aur sharaab bhej denge, par hamare qanoon mein darshanik becha nahi ja sakta.

Ujjain ki vedhshala ka Alexandria se rishta tha. Ek Yunani kitaab kehti hai ki kuch Bharatiya Socrates se milne gaye the. Pythagoras par Bharatiya darshan ki gehri chhaap maani jaati hai. Gyarahvin sadi mein Alberuni aaye, Sanskrit seekhi, aur apni kitaab mein dono darshanon ki tulna ki.

Ek chaunkane wali baat. Murti-puja shayad Greece se aayi. Vedic dharm mein na murti thi, na mandir. Shuru ka Bauddh dharm bhi Buddha ki murti banane ke khilaaf tha. Par Afghanistan ke paas Yunani kala ka asar bada. Pehle Bodhisattva ki Apollo jaisi murtiyan bani, phir khud Buddha ki. Farsi aur Hindustani mein murti ko aaj bhi "but" kehte hain, jo Buddha se nikla shabd hai.

Ab natak. Jab Europe ko Sanskrit natak mila, to turant kaha gaya ki ye Greece ki nakal hoga. Unnisvi sadi bhar behas chali. Aakhir maana gaya ki Bharat ka rangmanch poori tarah apna hai. Uski jadein Rigved ke samvaadon mein hain, Ramayan-Mahabharat mein natak ka zikr hai, aur Krishna-leela ke naach-gaane mein uska roop bana.

Natya Shastra teesri sadi isvi ka maana jaata hai. Chhota Nagpur ki Ramgarh pahadiyon mein doosri sadi isa poorv ka ek purana rangmanch khoda gaya hai, jo Natya Shastra ke varnan se mel khaata hai. Bhasa ke terah natak isi sadi ki shuruaat mein mile. Sabse purane natak Ashvaghosha ke hain, jo taad-patron ke tukdon mein Gobi registaan ke kinaare Turfan mein mile.

Europe ko Sanskrit natak ki khabar 1789 mein mili, jab William Jones ne Kalidas ka "Shakuntala" angrezi mein chhapa. Hangama mach gaya. German, French, Danish, Italian mein anuvaad aaye. Goethe ne iski bahut tareef ki, aur kehte hain "Faust" ki prastaavna ka khayal yahin se aaya.

Kalidas Sanskrit ke sabse bade kavi aur natakkar maane jaate hain. Shayad chauthi sadi ke aakhir mein Ujjain mein, Chandragupta Vikramaditya ke darbar mein the. Nehru unhe zindagi ke laadle beta jaisa batate hain, jinhone zindagi ki komalta zyada dekhi, kathorta kam. Unki "Meghdoot" mein ek bichhda premi baarish ke mausam mein baadal se kehta hai ki meri priya tak mera sandesh le ja. Ek American vidwan ki tareef ka Nehru zikr karte hain.

Aur bhi naam. Shudraka ka "Mrichhakatika", yaani mitti ki gaadi. Vishakhadatta ka "Mudrarakshasa", jo poori tarah rajneetik hai. Iska hero Chanakya hai, aur Nehru ko ye aaj ke zamane ke liye bhi sateek lagta hai. Harsha ke teen natak. Aur saatvin-aathvin sadi mein Bhavabhuti, jinki khoobsoorti bhasha mein hai, isliye anuvaad mein kho jaati hai. "Mrichhakatika" ka angrezi roop 1924 mein New York mein manch par khela gaya.

Afghan, Turk aur Mughal daur mein bhi Bharatiya sangeet aur kavita ko sahara mila. Bijapur ke Ibrahim Adil Shah ne Hindi mein sangeet par granth likha. Sanskrit natak ka patan unse bahut pehle shuru ho chuka tha. Wajah thi ki uski bhasha aur logon ki bolchaal mein faasla badhta gaya. Phir bhi natak likhe jaate rahe. 1892 mein Shakespeare ke ek natak ka Sanskrit roop aaya. 1890 ki ek list mein 189 lekhakon ke 377 natak the. Ek nayi list mein 650 hain.

Sanskrit natak mein padhe-likhe kirdar Sanskrit bolte hain, aam log, aksar auratein, Prakrit. Greece ke ulat yahan abhinetriyan manch par aati thi. Par sabse bada fark ye hai. Yunani natak ka dil tragedy hai: insaan kyun dukh jhelta hai, burai kahan se aati hai. Sanskrit mein dukhant ki ijazat hi nahi thi. Karm aur punarjanm ke vishwas mein andha sanyog tha hi nahi. Phir bhi nayak kismat ke aage ghutne nahi tekta. Mudrarakshasa mein Chanakya taaron ke bharose baithne walon ka mazaak udaata hai.

Part 4: Sanskrit, jo mari hi nahi

Sanskrit bahut bhari-poori bhasha hai. Phailti gayi, sajti gayi, par Panini ka vyakaran, jo kareeb chhabbees sau saal pehle bana, kabhi nahi chhoda. Patan ke daur mein lekhak shabdon ko jodkar kai-kai line lambe shabd banane lage, sirf chaturai dikhane ke liye.

1784 mein William Jones ne kaha ki Sanskrit, Greek aur Latin teeno kisi ek bhooli hui jad se nikli lagti hain. Isse ek nayi vidya shuru hui, tulnatmak bhasha-vigyaan. Unnisvi sadi mein German vidwan sabse aage the. Lagbhag har German university mein Sanskrit vibhag tha. Bharatiya bhi seekhne Germany gaye. Unka fayda ye tha ki wo granth ki aatma mehsoos kar sakte the. Nuksaan ye ki purani maanyataen nishpaksh hone nahi deti thi.

Nehru ka kehna hai ki bhasha vyakaran se kahin badi cheez hai. Wo ek qaum ki kavita hai. Isliye Sanskrit ka sahi anuvaad kavi-vidwan hi kar sakta hai, aur aise log kam hote hain. Ek French vidwan ne kaha tha ki bahut shabdik hone ke chakkar mein anuvaad bewafa ho jaate hain. Nehru ke hisaab se angrezi mein Sanskrit ka koi anuvaad mool ke laayak nahi. Ye poori duniya ka nuksaan hai.

Sanskrit aaj bhi zinda hai. Hindi, Bangla, Marathi ya Gujarati jaanne wala saral Sanskrit samajh leta hai. Urdu mein bhi shayad assi pratishat shabd Sanskrit se nikle hain. Dakshin ki Dravid bhashaon mein lagbhag aadhe shabd Sanskrit se jude hain. Thailand ne naye sarkari aur vaigyanik shabd Sanskrit se banaye. Pashto aur Sinhala bhi isi parivar ki hain. Europe mein Sanskrit ke sabse kareeb Lithuanian hai.

Purane log aawaaz ko bahut mahatva dete the. Poori kitaabein zubaani yaad karke peedhi dar peedhi aage di jaati. Vedon ka paath aaj bhi unhi purane niyamon se hota hai.

Par ek ulta asar bhi. Sanskrit ki pakad ne lok-bhashaon ko pichhda rakha. Padhe-likhe log unhe ganwaar maante the. Phir bhi praanteey bhashaen sadiyon mein khadi hui aur apna sahitya banaya. Nehru kehte hain ki asli bhasha ka sawal sirf Hindi-Urdu ka hai, jo bolne mein ek hain, likhne mein do. Unhe Hindustani mein raasta dikhta hai.

Part 5: Buddh ka raasta, aur uska ghul jaana

Buddha ne logon ki bhasha, Prakrit, mein updesh diya. Usi se Pali bani, jisme Lanka, Burma aur Siam ka Hinayana chalta hai. Baad ke Bauddh vidwanon ne Sanskrit mein likha, aur wo granth China, Japan, Tibet pahunche.

Buddha ka zamana poori duniya mein vichaaron ki halchal ka tha. Wahi daur Lao-tse, Confucius, Zarathushtra aur Pythagoras ka tha. Bharat mein usi se bhautikvaad bhi nikla, Gita bhi, Bauddh bhi, Jain bhi.

Buddha ke darshan par kisi ko aitraaz nahi tha. Purane dharm mein koi bhi vichaar chal jaata tha, bas wo vichaar rahe. Jhagda tab hua jab Bauddh dharm ne samaj ke ritiriwaaz ko chhua. Soch mein azaadi thi, amal mein sakhti.

Mahayana mein Buddha devta ban gaye, unki murti aayi, unki bhakti hone lagi. Hinayana purani shuddhata se chipka raha. Aaj bhi dono ek doosre ko thoda neecha dekhte hain.

Nagarjuna ko Nehru Bharat ke sabse bade dimaagon mein ginte hain. Unka tark itna bebaak tha ki apne hi vishwas ko kaat de. Unke hisaab se kuch bhi asli nahi, duniya sirf dikhawa hai. Par uske peeche ek "param" ki taraf ishara hai, jo soch ke bahar hai. Ise shunyata kaha gaya. Aur dhyan dijiye, shunya hi wo shabd hai jo baad mein zero ka naam bana. Par shunyata khaali-pan nahi hai. Ek Bauddh vidwan ke shabdon ka Nehru zikr karte hain, jinke mutabik isi ke kaaran sab kuch sambhav hai.

Ek mazedaar kissa. Soviet Union ke Buryat ilaqe mein sarkar ne dharm ke khilaaf prachaar kiya ki vigyaan to bhautikvaadi hai. Wahan ke Mahayana bhikshuon ne parcha chhapa: bhautikvaad hamare liye naya nahi, hamare ek school ne khud ye siddhant banaya tha.

Itni gehri baaton ke baad bhi ek baat pakki rakhi gayi: is duniya mein naitikta ka mol hai. Achhi zindagi jiyo. Anant ki baat anant par chhodo.

Ab Bauddh dharm ka Hindu samaj par asar. Bali aur yagya ka zor ghata. Ahimsa badhi, jaanwaron par daya badhi. Par saath mein zindagi se udaasi bhi aayi. Duniya chhodne ki chahat, brahmacharya, shakahaar. Purane Arya aadarsh mein ashram the: brahmacharya, grihasth, vanprasth, aur sanyas sirf budhape mein. Ab har jagah bade-bade math aur vihar ban gaye. Bihar ka naam hi vihar se nikla hai. Balkh mein ek hazaar bhikshuon ka "Nava-vihar" tha, jo Farsi mein "Naubahar" ban gaya.

China aur Japan mein Bauddh dharm ne aisi udaasi kyun nahi phailayi? Nehru ka andaaza hai ki wahan Confucius aur Lao-tse ki parampara majboot thi, aur Mahayana kam niraash tha. Bharat par Jain dharm ka asar bhi tha, jo sabse zyada duniya se mooh modta tha.

Ek aur ulta asar jaati par pada. Buddha ke zamane mein jaati lachkeeli thi. Janm se zyada guni aur kaam dekha jaata. Chhandogya Upanishad ki kahani hai. Satyakaam naam ka ladka guru ke paas jaane se pehle maa Jabala se apna gotra poochta hai. Maa sach-sach kehti hai ki jawani mein wo naukrani thi, use nahi pata. Bas itna kaho ki tum Jabala ke bete Satyakaam ho. Ladka guru ko yahi bata deta hai. Guru kehte hain: itna sach sirf sachcha Brahmin bol sakta hai. Jao, lakdi laao, main tumhe shishya banata hoon.

Par ahimsa par zor ka nateeja ye hua ki kheti neechi maani jaane lagi, kyunki hal chalane mein jeev marte hain. Jo kheti kabhi Aryon ka garv thi, uske karne wale samaj mein neeche gir gaye. Nehru iska dosh Bauddh dharm par nahi daalte. Wo kehte hain ki jaati vyavastha mein hi ye jhukaav tha.

Ab wo sawal jo is chapter ka sabse dilchasp sawal hai. Aath-nau saal pehle Paris mein French lekhak André Malraux mile. Baat shuru hote hi unhone poocha: Hindu dharm ne bina kisi dharm-yudh ke, Bauddh dharm jaise vishaal dharm ko Bharat se kaise hata diya? Wo kaun si andar ki taaqat thi? Aur kya wo taaqat aaj bhi hai?

Nehru ke paas tab poora jawab nahi tha. Par ab wo kuch baatein rakhte hain. Koi bada qatl-e-aam nahi hua. Hindu dharm kabhi poori tarah hata hi nahi tha. Bauddh dharm Bharat ki hi upaj tha, Upanishadon ki soch se juda. Dono ne ek doosre ko badla. Brahminon ne Buddha ko avatar bana liya. Mahayana sab se samjhauta karta gaya. Math ameer hue, anushasan dheela pada, jaadu-tona ghus aaya. Fa Hien paanchvi sadi mein aaye to Bauddh dharm khil raha tha. Hsuan Tsang saatvin sadi mein aaye to girawat dikh rahi thi. Aur aathvin sadi mein Shankaracharya ne Bauddh sangh ki tarah Hindu sanyasiyon ke math banaye. Yani Brahminvaad ne Bauddh dharm ko pachaya, aur pachaate hue khud badal gaya.

Part 6: Chhe darshan, aur ek yaatri

Nehru kehte hain ki hum sabka koi na koi zindagi ka falsafa hota hai, chahe soch kar banaya ho ya virasat mein mila ho. Bharat mein ye falsafa aam logon tak zyada gehra pahuncha. Ek angrez kala-vidwan Havell ke shabdon ka wo zikr karte hain, jinka saar ye hai ki Bharat mein dharm koi jadd siddhant nahi, balki jeene ka ek kaam-chalau niyam raha hai. Aur aisa niyam tabhi tak kaam ka hai jab tak zindagi se mel khaaye.

Isa ke janm se pehle chhe darshan pakke ho chuke the: Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Sankhya, Yoga, Mimansa aur Vedanta.

Nyaya yaani tark ka shastra. Aristotle ke tark jaisa. Sadiyon tak har padhe-likhe aadmi ki zaroori taaleem raha. Vaisheshika anuon ki baat karta hai, aur kehta hai ki duniya dharm, yaani naitik niyam, se chalti hai.

Sankhya ko Kapil se joda jaata hai. Ek German vidwan ka Nehru zikr karte hain, jinke hisaab se insaani dimaag ne pehli baar poori azaadi se apni taaqat par bharosa yahin dikhaya. Sankhya mein Ishwar hai hi nahi. Duniya kisi ne banayi nahi. Wo lagataar viksit ho rahi hai, prakriti aur purush ke mel se.

Yoga Patanjali ka hai. Wahi Patanjali jinhone Panini ke vyakaran par "Mahabhashya" likha. Pashchim mein yoga ka matlab naak ki nok dekhna samjha jaata hai. Nehru batate hain ki asli yoga sharir aur man ka anushasan hai. Aasan thakaate nahi, shareer ko dheela aur taaza karte hain, aur har umar mein ho sakte hain. Aur yahan ek niji baat. Nehru barson se kuch saral aasan aur saans ke abhyaas karte aaye hain, khaas kar jail mein. Unhe isse bahut faayda hua. Par maante hain ki unka man ab bhi ziddi hai aur baar-baar behak jaata hai.

Vivekananda yoga ko tark se jodte the. Unke vichaaron ka Nehru zikr karte hain, jinka saar hai ki sachchi prerna kabhi tark ke khilaaf nahi jaati.

Mimansa karmkaand wala darshan hai. Hindu qanoon par iska gehra asar hai. Ek dilchasp baat: Hindu aur Bauddh, dono maante hain ki devta bhi insaan se neeche hain. Mukti ke liye unhe bhi insaan ka janm lena padta hai.

Aur chhatha, Vedanta. Shankaracharya ka Advaita aaj Hindu darshan ki mukhya dhaara hai. Sab kuch ek hi Atman hai. Ghade ke andar ki khaali jagah aur bahar ka aakaash alag dikhte hain, par hain ek. Duniya ko "maya" kaha gaya, jiska anuvaad "bhram" kiya jaata hai. Nehru kehte hain ye galat hai. Maya hone aur na hone ke beech ki cheez hai, kuch-kuch saapekshta jaisi.

Shankara ki kahani khud ek yaatra hai. Dakshin mein Kerala ke ek Brahmin ne poore desh ka safar kiya, us zamane mein jab safar bahut mushkil tha. Chaar konon par chaar math banaye: dakshin mein Shringeri, poorab mein Puri, pashchim mein Dwarka, aur Himalaya mein Badrinath. Battees saal ki umar mein Kedarnath ki barf mein unki maut hui.

Nehru ke liye ye safar Bharat ki ekta ka saboot hai. Har jagah unhe Sanskrit bolne wale saathi mile. Naye vichaar poore desh mein phail jaate the. Aur sirf vidwan nahi, har jaati ke mard-aurat teerth karte the. Teerth chhutti aur mele jaise bhi the. Har teerth par poora Bharat ikattha hota tha. Aur Nehru ko lagta hai ki purane logon ne teerth bahut sundar jagahon par chune: Amarnath ki barfeeli gufa, Kanyakumari, Haridwar jahan Ganga pahadon se maidan mein utarti hai, Prayag ka sangam.

Part 7: Samandar paar Bharat

Ab cold open wali kahani par lautte hain. Bharat aur China ka rishta isvi 67 mein shuru hota hai, jab Kashyapa Matanga samraat Ming Ti ke zamane mein China pahunche. Unke baad Kumarajiva, Bodhidharma, Paramartha, Jinagupta. Chhathi sadi mein akele Lo-yang mein teen hazaar se zyada Bharatiya bhikshu aur das hazaar Bharatiya parivar rehte the. Kumarajiva 401 mein gaye. Unki saintalees kitaabein bachi hain, aur unki Chini bhasha ki tareef hoti hai.

China se bhi log aaye: Fa Hien, Hsuan Tsang, I-tsing. Fa Hien jab nikle, to unke guru Kumarajiva ne kaha ki sirf dharm mat padhna, Bharat ke logon ka rehan-sehan bhi dekhna.

Hsuan Tsang ne apni kitaab mein Bharat ki shiksha ka varnan kiya. University mein paanch vishay: vyakaran, shilp, chikitsa, tark aur darshan. Unhe logon ka padhai ka shauq bahut bhaaya. Kar halke the, begaar nahi thi, vyapari khulkar aate-jaate the. Nehru khud Nalanda ke khandahar dekhne gaye aur uski vishalta dekh kar dang reh gaye.

I-tsing ne bhi Nalanda mein lamba waqt bitaya. Wo batate hain ki uttar mein gehun, dakshin aur poorab mein chawal khaya jaata tha. Ghee, doodh, malai har jagah. Bharatiyon ko shuddh-ashuddh ka bahut khayal tha. Bacha khana rakhna yahan theek nahi maana jaata tha. I-tsing ne Bharat ki tareef ki, par saaf kaha ki pehla sthaan uske China ka hai. Bharat "Arya desh" hai, par China "divya bhoomi".

Aathvin sadi ki China ki sabse purani chhapi kitaabon mein Sanskrit ki kitaabein hain. Nehru hairaan hain ki itne len-den ke baad bhi Bharat mein chhapai nahi aayi. Unhe afsos hai ki Bharat ne China se uski vyavaharik samajh nahi seekhi, jo hamari udaan bharti kalpanaon par lagaam laga sakti thi. Ek Chini kahawat bhi hai: sarkar pakde to maar-maar ke maar degi, Bauddh pakdein to bhookha maar denge.

Phir ek lambi judaai. Aur Nehru ki umeed ki ab phir dono desh ek doosre ki taraf dekh rahe hain.

Ab Dakshin-poorvi Asia. Nehru kehte hain ki Bharat ko samajhna hai to waqt mein peeche jao, aur nakshe mein bahar bhi. Rabindranath Tagore ki ek baat ka wo zikr karte hain. Kareeb pandrah saal pehle unhone is ilaqe ka itihaas padha to wo romanchit ho gaye. Champa, Cambodia, Angkor, Srivijaya, Majapahit, sab achanak zinda ho uthe.

Shuruaat vyapar se hui. Sanskrit granthon mein in deshon ko Swarnabhoomi aur Swarnadweep kaha gaya, sone ki zameen. Java ka naam "Yavadweep" se aaya. Andhra ke sikkon par do mastool wala jahaaz bana hai. Ajanta mein haathi dhote jahaaz dikhaye gaye hain. 1088 ki ek Tamil shilalekh mein "Pandrah Sau" naam ke vyapari sangh ka zikr hai, jo zameen aur samandar se ghode, haathi, ratn, itr aur dawaiyan bechte the.

Pehle vyapari gaye, wahan base, ghul-mil gaye. Phir raajkumar gaye. Shuruaat Kalinga se hui, par sangathit koshish Pallavon ne ki. Kaha jaata hai Malay naam Malwa ke logon se juda hai.

Ye bastiyan pehli-doosri sadi se pandrahvi sadi tak, terah sau saal se zyada chali. Sabse bada Shailendra ya Srivijaya samrajya tha, jo aathvin sadi tak Malaya, Sumatra, Java ka hissa, Borneo aur Philippines tak phaila tha. Ek historian likhte hain ki uske sansthaapak ka naam aaj ki encyclopedia mein dhoondhe nahi milta. Gyarahvin sadi mein Cholon ne nau-sena bhejkar Srivijaya ko jeeta, aur pachaas saal se zyada wahan dabdaba rakha.

Phir Java ka Majapahit aaya, 1292 mein. Ye bahut sangathit samrajya tha: vyapar vibhag, upnivesh vibhag, swasthya vibhag, sena vibhag, aur ek sarvochch adalat. Iski mashhoor shaasak thi Rani Suhita. Majapahit ne Kublai Khan ke doot ka apmaan kiya, aur China ki sena ne sabak sikhaya. Shayad wahi se Javaniyon ne baarood seekha, jisse unhone Srivijaya ko haraya.

Aakhir mein samandar ka raaj Arabon ke paas gaya. Malacca uthaa, Islam phaila, aur pandrahvi sadi ke aakhir mein Majapahit khatam hua. Aur sirf kuch saal baad, 1511 mein, Portugal ka Albuquerque Malacca pe kabza kar leta hai. Europe Poorab tak pahunch chuka tha.

Par sanskriti reh gayi. Rajaon ke naam Sanskrit the. Sarkari padon ke Sanskrit naam aaj bhi Thailand aur Malaya mein chalte hain. Java aur Bali ke naach Bharat se nikle. Bali mein to Hindu dharm aaj bhi hai. Philippines mein likhne ki kala Bharat se gayi. Cambodia ki lipi dakshin Bharat se aayi, aur uska qanoon Manu par tika hai.

Sabse badi nishani hai pathar. Java ke Borobudur mein Buddha ki poori jeevan-katha pathar mein tarashi hai. Cambodia ke Angkor mein Vishnu, Ram aur Krishna ki kathaen hain. Angkor ke darwaze par ek vishaal chehra hai, jiski muskaan ko "Angkor smile" kehte hain. Ise Jayavarman Saptam ne banwaya, ek theth Bharatiya naam. Ek angrez lekhak ne Angkor ko duniya ka sabse bada ajooba bataya, Nehru ye bhi quote karte hain.

Nehru yahan ek gehri baat kehte hain. Prerna Bharat se gayi, par Khmer logon ne use apni pratibha se naya roop diya. Aur Bharat mein wahi prerna dheere-dheere sookh gayi. Jab tak Bharat ne darwaze khule rakhe, lena-dena kiya, wo taaza raha. Jitna khud mein simta, utna murjha gaya. Sundarta banane ki kala gayi, to use pehchaanne ki aankh bhi chali gayi.

Part 8: Pathar, masale aur shunya

Ek angrez puratatvavid John Marshall ke hawaale se Nehru kehte hain ki Bharat ki kala ko sirf Bharat mein dekhna aadhi kahani hai. Poori kahani Tibet, Burma, Siam, Cambodia aur Java tak jaati hai.

Phir Nehru imaandari se kehte hain ki unhe kala ki koi samajh nahi. Wo aam aadmi ki tarah dekhte hain: koi cheez dil chhoo jaati hai, koi nahi. Lanka ke Anuradhapura ki Buddha murti ne unhe itna chhua ki uski tasveer barson unke saath rahi. Aur dakshin ke kuch mashhoor mandir, nakkaashi se lade hue, unhe bechain karte hain.

Ek angrez lekhak ne Bharatiya kala ko ghinauna kaha tha. Nehru gussa nahi karte. Kehte hain ki unhe bhi Bharat ki bahut si baatein buri lagti hain, par wo poore Bharat se nafrat nahi kar sakte, aakhir wo khud Bharatiya hain. Phir ek tez sawal poochte hain: kya hum unhi se nafrat karte hain jinhe humne chot pahunchayi ho?

Havell jaise angrez bhi the, jinhone Bharatiya kala ko samjha. Unka kehna tha ki ye kala vidwanon ke liye nahi, aam logon tak dharm aur darshan pahunchane ke liye bani. Isiliye anpadh kisaan bhi sanskaari hota hai.

Ajanta ki gufaen Gupta daur mein bani. Inhe Bauddh bhikshuon ne chitrit kiya. Unke guru ne auraton se door rehne ko kaha tha. Phir bhi deewaron par auratein hi auratein hain: rajkumariyan, gaayika, nartaki, shringaar karti hui. Nehru hairaan hain ki un bhikshuon ne duniya ko itne pyaar se kaise dekha. Saatvin-aathvin sadi mein Ellora ka Kailash mandir ek hi chattaan kaatkar bana. Usi daur ki Elephanta ki Trimurti, aur Mahabalipuram. Elephanta mein toote hue Nataraj mein bhi naach ki gati aur chehre par Buddha jaisi shanti hai.

Ab vyapar. Isvi san ke pehle hazaar saal Bharatiya vyapari Poorab ke samandar par chhaye rahe aur Bhoomadhya Sagar tak pahunche. Kali mirch Rome jaati thi. Kehte hain Goth sardar Alaric Rome se teen hazaar pound kali mirch le gaya. Roman lekhak rote the ki Rome ka sona Bharat ja raha hai.

Rasayan shastra mein Bharat aage tha. Ispaat ko pakka karna yahan jaldi aaya, aur Bharatiya loha bahar mashhoor tha. Chikitsa mein tarakki hui. Khagol vidya university mein padhai jaati thi, aur ek bahut sahi calendar bana jo aaj bhi chalta hai. Nehru tokte hain ki pujariyon ne grahan ke gyaan ko andhvishwas phailane ke liye bhi istemaal kiya.

Ek aur fayda: Bharat mein gulaami nahi thi. Jaati vyavastha ki saari buraiyon ke bawajood, Nehru ke hisaab se wo gulaami se behtar thi. Har jaati apne kaam mein maahir hoti gayi.

Aur ab ganit, shayad is chapter ka sabse garv bhara hissa. Napoleon ke zamane ke French ganitagya Laplace ki baat ka Nehru zikr karte hain, jo das ankon se har sankhya likhne ki Bharatiya tarkeeb par hairaan the.

Vedi banane ke liye shuruaati jyamiti bani. Jyamiti mein Greece aage nikla, par ankganit aur beejganit mein Bharat aage raha. Shunya aur sthaan-maan kisne banaya, koi nahi jaanta. Shunya pehle ek bindu tha, phir chhota gola bana. Sabse purana zikr lagbhag 200 isa poorv ka hai. Ek angrez ganitagya ke shabdon ka Nehru zikr karte hain, jinke hisaab se shunya se zyada asardaar koi ganitiya khoj nahi hui.

Itni badi khoj Greece mein kyun nahi hui? Nehru ka jawab hai: samaj ki zaroorat. Sanskrit ki ganit ki kitaabein vyapar ke sawalon se bhari hain: kar, karz, byaaj, saajhedari, sone ki shuddhata. Itne bade vyapar ke liye aasaan hisaab chahiye tha.

Phir sab khul gaya. Bhinn, trairashik niyam, varg aur vargmool, ghan, minus ka nishaan, sine ki taalika. Pi ka maan 3.1416. Agyaat sankhya ke liye akshar. Aur shunya ke niyam: kisi sankhya se shunya ghatao to wahi sankhya, shunya se guna karo to shunya, shunya se bhaag do to anant.

Naam yaad rakhiye. Aryabhata, janm 476. Teyees saal ki umar mein khagol aur ganit par kitaab likhi. Brahmagupta, 628, jinhone shunya ke niyam likhe. Aur Bhaskara Dwitiya, janm 1114, jinki ankganit ki kitaab ka naam hai "Lilavati". Ek ladki ka naam. Kitaab mein baar-baar "he Lilavati" kehkar sawal samjhaaye gaye hain. Maana jaata hai ki wo Bhaskara ki beti thi, haalaanki saboot nahi. Ye kitaab aaj bhi Sanskrit paathshalaon mein padhai jaati hai. Baarahvi sadi ke baad, aadhunik daur tak, Bharat mein koi naya ganit nahi hua.

Ye ank duniya mein kaise phaile? Aathvin sadi mein Khalifa Al-Mansur ke zamane mein Bharatiya vidwan Baghdad gaye, kitaabein saath le gaye. Aryabhata ka Arabi mein anuvaad hua. Arab inhe "Hind ke ank" kehte the. Baghdad se Spain, aur Spain se Europe. Europe mein inhe kaafiron ke nishaan kehkar roka gaya. Sabse purana istemaal Sicily ke ek sikke par hai, 1134 ka. Britain mein 1490 mein.

Aur ganit ki baat Ramanujan ke bina adhoori hai. Dakshin ke ek gareeb Brahmin parivar mein janme, theek padhai nahi mili, Madras Port Trust mein clerk bane. Khaali waqt mein sankhyaon se khelte rahe. Kisi ne unka kaam Cambridge bheja. Wahan unhe bulaya gaya. Thode hi waqt mein unhone kamaal ka kaam kiya. Royal Society ne unhe Fellow banaya. Do saal baad, taintees saal ki umar mein, shayad tapedik se, unki maut ho gayi.

Nehru ke liye Ramanujan Bharat ki haalat ki tasveer hain. Karodon mein se kitnon ko padhai milti hai? Kitne bhookh ke kinaare jeete hain? Aur jo padh lete hain, unke aage kya hai, ek clerk ki naukri? Agar sabko mauka mile, to inme se kitne Ramanujan niklenge?

Part 9: Dopahar ke baad

Ab chapter ka aakhri aur sabse kadwa sawal. Sunehra daur khatam hone se pehle hi kamzori dikhne lagi thi.

Huno ke saath lambi ladai ne Bharat ko thaka diya. Hun bhi pacha liye gaye, par apni chhaap chhod gaye. Unki krurta Bharatiya reeti se bilkul alag thi.

Phir dheere-dheere ek andar ki kamzori. Koi ek tareekh nahi. Pehle uttar mein, baad mein dakshin mein. Dakshin kuch sadiyan aur chamakta raha, shayad isliye ki wo hamlon se bacha raha aur uttar ke kalakaar wahan pahunch gaye. Videsh ki bastiyan pandrahvi sadi tak josh mein rahi.

Nehru ek tasveer dete hain. Pehli hazaar saal ke aakhir tak ye ek sabhyata ki dhalti dopahar thi. Subah ki roshni kab ki ja chuki thi. Dil dhadakna dheema kar raha tha, aur ye jakdan haath-pair tak phail rahi thi.

Saboot har jagah hain. Shankara ke baad koi bada darshanik nahi, sirf teekakaar. Bhavabhuti ke baad koi bada kavi nahi. Bhaskara Dwitiya ke baad koi bada ganitagya nahi. Jigyasa ki jagah sukha tark aa gaya. Tantra aur yoga ke bigde roop phaile. Kala mein dakshin ne parampara zyada der sambhali.

Kyun? Ek vidwan kehte hain ki darshan ne raajnaitik azaadi ke saath apni taaqat khoi. Nehru maante hain ki ye sach hai. Par phir poochte hain: azaadi khoyi hi kyun? Itna bada, itna viksit desh bahar ke hamle se tabhi haarta hai jab andar se khokhla ho, ya dushman ke paas behtar jung ki takneek ho.

Unka jawab hai jaati vyavastha. Ladna sirf Kshatriya ka kaam reh gaya. Brahmin aur Kshatriya vyapar ko neecha maante. Neechi jaatiyon ko padhai aur tarakki ka mauka nahi diya gaya. Har kaam peedhi dar peedhi ek group mein band ho gaya. Naya kaam, naya prayog, naya vichaar, sab ruk gaya. Is vyavastha ne Bharat ko sthirta di, ye Nehru maante hain. Par jab tak usme badhne ki jagah thi, wo aage le gayi. Jab jagah khatam hui, wo ruk gayi, aur phir peeche khinchne lagi.

Aur nateeja: har maidan mein girawat. Soch mein, rajneeti mein, jung ki takneek mein, bahar ki duniya ki jaankari mein. Poore Bharat ki jagah chhote-chhote ilaqai lagaav.

Phir bhi Nehru yahin baat khatam nahi karte. Purane dhaanche mein ab bhi jaan thi, ajeeb zid thi, thodi lachak thi. Isiliye wo bacha, aur naye vichaaron se kuch seekha bhi. Bas har kadam par ateet ke bahut saare bojh uske pairon mein bandhe rahe.

Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, Nalanda. Nehru ne likha ki uska sirf ek hissa khoda gaya hai. 2016 mein Nalanda ke khandahar UNESCO ki World Heritage list mein aaye, aur paas hi ek nayi Nalanda University bhi bani hai.

Doosri, Iran aur Asia se kate rishte wali shikayat. Nehru ne umeed ki thi ki hawai jahaaz purane padosiyon ko phir jodenge. Aaj Delhi se Tehran ya Beijing ki udaan kuch ghanton ki hai. Par kya dil ki doori kam hui?

Teesri, Ramanujan wala sawal. Nehru ne poocha tha ki agar karodon logon ko padhai aur roti mile, to kitne Ramanujan niklenge. Ye sawal aaj bhi utna hi zinda hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru kehte hain ki Bharat tab tak taaza raha jab tak usne darwaze khule rakhe. Aapki apni zindagi mein, kab darwaza khulne se kuch naya mila?

Outro

Agle episode mein ek naya mod. Arab se uthi ek nayi lehar Bharat ke darwaze par aati hai. Mahmud Ghaznavi ke hamle, Afghan aur Mughal, aur do sanskritiyon ka takraav aur mel. Kabir aur Guru Nanak ki awaaz, aur Akbar ka sapna.

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
