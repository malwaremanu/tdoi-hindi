Cold open

Bihar. Jharia ka koyla ilaqa. Ek aadmi zameen ke neeche utar raha hai.

Upar dhoop thi. Neeche andhera hai, garmi hai, aur hawa itni bhaari ki saans atakti hai. Koyle ki kaali deewaron ke beech kuch log kaam kar rahe hain. Unme auratein bhi hain. Jhuki hui kamar, sir par bojh.

Ye aadmi Jawaharlal Nehru hain. Isse pehle wo shehron ki mazdoor bastiyon mein bhi gaye the. Tang kothriyan, ek ke upar ek. Wahan se bahar nikle to sir ghoom raha tha, aur man mein dar aur gussa, dono saath.

Barson baad, Ahmadnagar ke qile mein baithkar, wo likhte hain ki Jharia ki wo tasveer unke man se kabhi nahi gayi. Insaan se aisa kaam karwaya ja sakta hai, ye jhatka bhi nahi gaya.

Phir ek aur baat jodte hain. Baad mein qanoon bana ki auratein zameen ke neeche kaam nahi karengi. Par ab, jung ke naam par, unhe wapas khadaan mein bhej diya gaya hai. Sarkar kehti hai, mazdoor kam pad gaye. Aur usi waqt desh mein laakhon aadmi bhookhe aur bekaar baithe hain.

Kami aadmiyon ki nahi hai. Kami mazdoori ki hai, aur izzat ki.

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 8: Aakhri daur, doosra hissa. Rashtravaad banaam samrajyavaad.

Part 1: Ek thaka hua desh

Pichhle episode ki kahani beesvi sadi ki shuruaat tak aayi thi. Ab pehla vishwa yudh shuru hota hai, 1914 mein.

Rajneeti us waqt dhimi thi. Congress do hisson mein bati hui thi, garam dal aur naram dal. Upar se jung ke qanoon aur paabandiyan. Par ek nayi baat dikh rahi thi. Musalmanon ka padha-likha naya madhyam varg desh ke baare mein zyada sochne laga tha. Wo Muslim League ko Congress ke kareeb dhakel raha tha. Aur kuch waqt ke liye dono ne haath bhi mila liye.

Ab gareebi ki tasveer. Nehru do sarkari ya aadhi-sarkari reporton ka zikr karte hain. 1928 mein Britain ke trade unionon ka ek dal Bharat aaya. Usne Assam ke chai baagaanon ke baare mein likha ki wahan har saal laakhon logon ka paseena, bhookh aur nirasha chai mein ghulti hai. Aur Bengal ke public health director ne 1927-28 ki report mein likha ki wahan ka kisaan jo kha raha hai, us par ek chooha bhi kuch hafton se zyada zinda na rahe.

1918 mein jung khatam hui. Logon ko laga ab kuch raahat milegi. Mila ulta. Sakht qanoon aaye, aur Punjab mein martial law laga. Desh mein apmaan aur gusse ki lehar daud gayi.

Upar se sudhaar ki baatein chalti rahi. Kitne Hindustani afsar banenge, constitution mein kya badlega. Nehru ko ye sab mazaak lagta tha. Jab desh ki jawaani kuchli ja rahi ho, aur loot din-ba-din gareebi ko gehra kar rahi ho, tab ye baatein beimaani hain. Unke shabdon ka saar: hum ek toota hua, bebas desh ban chuke the.

Kisaan dara hua tha, jhuka hua. Mill mazdoor bhi wahi. Aur madhyam varg, jo andhere mein raasta dikha sakta tha, khud andhere mein doob gaya tha. Kai maamlon mein uski haalat kisaan se bhi buri thi. Hazaaron padhe-likhe log apne purane tabqe se toot chuke the, aur naya koi thikana nahi tha.

To kiya kya jaye? Nehru yahan ek misaal dete hain. Bharat ki beemari TB jaisi thi. Dheere-dheere andar se khaati hui. Kabhi-kabhi lagta tha ki haija ya plague jaisi koi tez beemari behtar hoti, kam se kam saamne to hoti. Par wo khayal jaldi chala jaata. Josh mein uthaya gaya koi bhi jokhim kahin nahi le jaata. Aur purani, gehri beemari ka ilaaj neem-hakeem se nahi hota.

Aur tab Gandhi aaye.

Part 2: Daro mat

Gandhi kisi toofan ki tarah nahi aaye. Wo hawa ke ek taaze jhonke ki tarah aaye, jisne logon ko lambi saans lene diya.

Nehru batate hain ki purane Bharat ke granthon mein ek shabd baar-baar aata hai: abhay. Yaani nidarta. Sirf sharir ki bahaduri nahi, man se dar ka nikal jaana. Itihaas ki shuruaat mein Janak aur Yajnavalkya ne kaha tha ki neta ka kaam hai apne logon ko nidar banana.

Par Angrezi raaj mein Bharat ki sabse badi bhavna dar thi. Fauj ka dar. Police ka dar. Khufiya vibhaag ka dar. Afsar ka dar. Jail ka dar. Zameendaar ke kaarinde ka dar. Sahukaar ka dar. Aur sabse bada, berozgaari aur bhookh ka dar, jo hamesha darwaaze par khada rehta tha.

Isi dar ke khilaaf ek dheemi, pakki awaaz uthi. Daro mat.

Kya baat itni seedhi thi? Poori tarah nahi. Par Nehru kehte hain ki dar apne bhoot khud banata hai, jo asli khatre se bhi zyada darawne hote hain. Aur jab aadmi thande dimaag se dekh le ki bura se bura kya hoga, aur use sweekar kar le, to aadha dar wahin khatam ho jaata hai.

Aur achanak, jaise kisi ne kandhon se kaala kambal utha liya ho. Poora nahi, par hairat ki had tak. Log seedhe khade hone lage.

Dar aur jhooth saath chalte hain, Nehru kehte hain. To jab dar ghata, jhooth ki zaroorat bhi ghati. Log raaton-raat sant nahi ban gaye. Par chhupne aur jhooth bolne ki majboori kam ho gayi. Nehru ise psychoanalysis jaisa batate hain. Jaise kisi doctor ne mareez ke ateet mein utarkar uski ganth dhoondh li, use dikha di, aur bojh utar gaya.

Saath mein ek sharm bhi jaagi. Itne lambe waqt tak paraaye raaj ke aage jhuke rehne ki sharm. Aur ek zid: ab aur nahi, chahe jo ho.

Phir sach ki baat. Sach kya hai? Nehru imaandari se kehte hain, mujhe pakka nahi pata. Har aadmi ka sach uski parvarish aur mizaaj se rang leta hai. Par kam se kam ek aadmi ke liye sach wahi hai jo wo andar se sach maanta hai. Is paimane par, Nehru kehte hain, unhone Gandhi jaisa koi nahi dekha. Aur saath mein chutki lete hain: neta ke liye ye khatarnaak gun hai. Gandhi apne man ki baat bol dete the, aur uske badalte rang bhi logon ko dikha dete the.

Gandhi ka asar sab par alag tha. Kuch logon ki poori zindagi badal gayi. Kuch par thoda pada, ya utar gaya. Par poori tarah koi chhoot nahi paya. Yahan Nehru ek purane Greek kirdaar, Alcibiades, ki ek baat yaad karte hain, jo usne apne guru Socrates ke baare mein kahi thi.

Part 3: Congress gaon chali

Ab Gandhi Congress ke andar aate hain, aur uska dhaancha hi badal dete hain.

Congress pehle bhi loktantrik thi, par chhote daayre mein. Upar ke tabqe ki party. Gandhi ne darwaaze khol diye. Kisaan bhar-bhar ke aaye. Congress dekhte-dekhte ek vishaal kisaan sangathan jaisi dikhne lagi, jisme madhyam varg ka achha-khaasa hissa tha. Mill mazdoor bhi aaye, par alag-alag, apni union ke roop mein nahi.

Ab tak do hi raaste the. Ya to bhaashan aur prastaav paas karna. Ya hinsa aur bam. Gandhi ne dono ko kinaare kiya. Hinsa ki khule taur par ninda hui. Naya tareeqa aaya: bilkul shaant, par galat cheez ke aage jhukna nahi, aur iski jo bhi saza mile, use khushi se jhelna.

Nehru kehte hain, Gandhi ajeeb kism ke shaanti-premi the. Unme zabardast urja thi. Kismat ke aage jhukna unke swabhav mein tha hi nahi. Unka virodh shaant tha, tameez wala tha, par virodh tha.

Kaam ki pukaar do taraf thi. Ek, videshi raaj ka saamna. Do, apni hi samajik burayiyon se ladai. Azadi aur ahimsa ke saath Congress ke khambhe the: desh ki ekta, alpsankhyakon ka sawal suljhana, dalit samaj ko upar uthana, aur chhuachhoot ka khatma.

Gandhi ne Angrezi raaj ki neev pehchani. Dar, rutba, logon ka sahyog, aur kuch tabqon ka swarth. Unhone in sab par chot ki. Angrezon ke diye khitaab lautane ki apeel hui. Bahut kam logon ne lautaye. Par aam logon ki nazar mein wo khitaab izzat nahi, sharm ki nishani ban gaye. Viceroy ke darbar aur rajaon ki shaan-o-shaukat, jo pehle aankhein chaundhiyati thi, ab gareebi ke beech bhaddi aur hasyaspad lagne lagi. Ameer log apni daulat dikhane se katrane lage. Kai to kapdon mein aam aadmi jaise dikhne lage.

Purane neta is bheed se ghabraaye. Par lehar itni tez thi ki zyadatar beh gaye. Kuch alag ho gaye. Unme ek the Mohammad Ali Jinnah. Nehru saaf karte hain ki Jinnah Hindu-Muslim sawal par nahi gaye. Wo is nayi, garam soch mein fit nahi hue. Aur unhe wo bheed pasand nahi thi: maile kapdon mein, Hindustani bolte log. Unki rajneeti council ke kamre ki rajneeti thi. Kuch saal wo itne alag-thalag mehsoos karte rahe ki England jaakar bas gaye.

Kaha jaata hai ki Bharat ka mizaaj shaant baithne ka hai. Purani qaum, lamba darshan. Par Gandhi, jo poori tarah Hindustani the, iske bilkul ulte the. Kaam ke deewane. Khud bhi daudte, sabko daudate.

Unhone padhe-likhe naujawanon ko gaon bheja. Aur yahan Nehru apni kahani batate hain. Pehli baar unhone kisaan ko uski kachchi jhopdi ke andar dekha. Bhookh ki parchhaai uske peechhe-peechhe chalti thi. Nehru kehte hain, Bharat ki arthvyavastha humne kitaabon se zyada in gaon se seekhi. Uske baad purani zindagi mein lautna mumkin nahi raha.

Gandhi ke apne pakke vichaar the, arthvyavastha par, samaj par. Unhone sab Congress par nahi thopa. Kuch cheezein aage badhayi, kabhi zyada aage chale gaye, to laut bhi aaye. Par do baatein sab par chhaa gayi. Har cheez ki kasauti ye ki usse sabse gareeb ko kya mila. Aur ye ki saadhan bhi utne hi zaroori hain jitni manzil, kyunki galat raasta manzil ko hi badal deta hai.

Gandhi gehre dharmik the, andar tak Hindu. Par unka dharm kisi riwaaj ya kattarpan se nahi bandha tha. Unhone Bharat ki sanskriti ko kisi ek dharm ki jaagir nahi maana. Wo likhte hain ki unhe har desh ki hawa apne ghar mein chahiye, par wo kisi hawa mein ud nahi jaayenge. Apni jadein kabhi nahi chhodi.

Congress par Gandhi chhaaye rahe, par ajeeb tareeqe se. Congress behes wali, bagawati party thi. Gandhi kai baar jhuke, kabhi apne khilaaf faisla bhi maana. Kuch baaton par ade rahe, aur alag bhi hue. Par jab bhi ladai ka waqt aata, wo Bharat ki azadi ka chehra ban jaate. Baaki matbhed peechhe chale jaate.

Aur Nehru ki ek baat jo poore chapter ka saar hai. Rashtravaad aur samrajyavaad ke beech pakki sulah ho hi nahi sakti. Thode samjhaute ho sakte hain. Barabari ka rishta sirf azad Bharat aur England ke beech ho sakta hai.

Part 4: Sooba sarkarein

1935 mein Government of India Act aaya. Naam bada, kaam chhota. Paisa, fauj aur videsh neeti, teeno Angrezon ke haath mein. Viceroy pehle se bhi zyada taaqatwar.

Isi daur mein Burma ko Bharat se alag kiya gaya. Nehru ek dilchasp baat batate hain. Burma mein Angrez, Hindustani aur Chini vyapariyon ke beech khinchtaan thi. To Angrezon ne Burmiyon ke man mein Hindustaniyon aur Chiniyon ke khilaaf bhavna bhadkayi. Par saath mein Burmiyon ko azadi bhi nahi di. Nateeja? Burma mein Japan-samarthak lehar uthi, jo 1942 mein Japan ke hamle ke waqt saamne aa gayi.

Congress ne 1935 ka qanoon kabhi nahi maana. Phir bhi faisla hua ki sooba sarkarein chalayi jaayengi. Do kaam saath-saath. Azadi ki ladai jaari, aur jitna ho sake, kisaan aur mazdoor ke liye qanoon.

1937 ke chunaav hue. Congress zyadatar soobon mein jeeti. Ek ehsaas hamesha bana raha ki koi sankat kabhi bhi aa sakta hai. Isliye jaldi thi: jitna ho sake utna kaam, sankat se pehle. Raste mein rode bhi bahut the. Upari sadan, jinke vote sirf ameeron ke paas the. Governor ke vishesh adhikaar.

Ek bada faisla hua: doosri partiyon ke saath milkar sarkar nahi banegi. Sirf Assam aur Frontier mein chhoot. Nehru maante hain ki is par baad mein bahut behes hui. Unhe aaj bhi lagta hai ki us waqt ye faisla theek tha. Par wo imaandari se jodte hain ki iska asar Hindu-Muslim sawal par bura pada. Kai Musalmanon ko laga ki unhe bahar rakha gaya. Aur is ehsaas ka fayda purane-khayal netaon ne uthaya.

Ab Punjab aur Bengal dekhiye. Wahan mantrimandal kuch mahine pehle hi ban gaye the. Koi takraav nahi hua. Punjab mein to zyadatar mantri wahi purane bade afsar the. Angrezi hukumat se unka koi jhagda tha hi nahi.

Fark sabse saaf dikha rajneetik qaidiyon ke maamle mein. Punjab aur Bengal mein police aur khufiya raaj waisa hi chalta raha. Qaidi nahi chhoote. Bengal mein, jahan sarkar aksar gore vidhayakon ke vote par tiki thi, hazaaron log saalon se bina mukadme ke band the.

Congress soobon ne pehla kaam yahi kiya: rajneetik qaidi chhode. Kuch qaidi hinsa ke maamlon mein the. Governor ne unhe chhodne se mana kiya. Shuru 1938 mein baat itni badhi ki do Congress sarkaron, United Provinces aur Bihar, ne istifa de diya. Governor peechhe hate, aur qaidi chhoote.

Part 5: Mantri teesre darje mein

Nehru ke man mein ek sawal tha. Doosre desh kar sakte hain, to hum kyun nahi? Gareebi hai, pichhdapan hai, phoot hai. Sab pata hai. Keemat bhaari hogi. Par aaj jo keemat roz chuka rahe hain, usse bhaari nahi.

Par andar ka kaam shuru kaise ho, jab har kadam par bahar ka raaj raasta rokta ho?

Sooba sarkarein ek chhota mauqa thi. Mantri kaam mein doobe rehte. Mantri kam the, aur purane afsaron se mel nahi baithta tha. Congress ne tay kiya tha ki mantri saadgi se rahenge. Tankhwah kam. Ab tamasha dekhiye. Mantri ka ICS secretary mantri se chaar-paanch guna zyada kamata tha. Aur Civil Service ki tankhwah chhoone ka haq sarkar ke paas tha hi nahi. Usi train mein mantri doosre ya teesre darje mein baitha hai, aur uska matahat pehle darje mein, ya apne shahi saloon mein.

Angrez aksar kehte the ki Congress ka high command upar se hukum chalata hai. Nehru ise galat batate hain. Rozmarra ke kaam mein koi dakhal nahi tha. Bas itna chahte the ki bade rajneetik sawalon par, khaaskar Governor aur Delhi ke saath rishton par, sab soobe ek line par rahein.

August 1937 mein Congress Working Committee ne ek prastaav paas kiya. Soobe aapas mein mil kar kaam karein, khaaskar udyog aur arthvyavastha mein. Nadiyon ka survey, jo soobon ki seema nahi dekhti. Ganga ghaati ka survey, aur ek Ganga River Commission, jo UP, Bihar aur Bengal ke saath ke bina ban hi nahi sakta. Nehru likhte hain ki ye kaam aaj tak baaki hai.

Isi prastaav mein badi rashtriya yojana ka beej tha. Uska poora roop tab tak mumkin nahi tha jab tak Delhi ki sarkar logon ke haath mein na ho. Par neev daali ja sakti thi. 1938 ke aakhir mein National Planning Committee bani, aur Nehru uske chairman bane. Uski kahani thodi der mein.

Nehru khud in sarkaron ke aalochak the. Kaam dhima lagta tha. Par peechhe mudkar dekhte hain to hairaan hote hain. Sirf sava do saal mein kitna hua. Kisaan aur mazdoor dono mazboot hue. Aur ek badi cheez: basic education, yaani buniyaadi taleem. Nayi soch par bani, aur Bharat ke gaon ke hisaab se gadhi gayi. Kai kaam poore hone ko the jab sarkarein hat gayi, aur baad mein Governor ne unhe thande baste mein daal diya.

Aur Civil Service ka bharam bhi toota. Uski kaabiliyat ka dhol zyadatar usi ne khud peeta tha. Purane dhaanche ke bahar wo bebas nikli. Logon se darti bhi thi, unhe neecha bhi samajhti thi. Badi aur tez yojanaon ki kalpana usme thi hi nahi. Laal feeta hi uska hathiyaar tha. Nehru kehte hain ye baat gore aur Hindustani, dono afsaron par lagu thi, kuch apvaadon ko chhodkar.

Aur ek chhoti, chubhti baat. Har chitthi khuli jaati thi, har phone suna jaata tha. Congress ke paas chhupane ko kuch nahi tha. Phir bhi ye ehsaas ki koi kandhe ke upar se jhaank raha hai, rishton mein bhi kadwahat bhar deta tha.

Part 6: Teen kone ka khel

Ab sabse naazuk sawal. Alpsankhyak, Muslim League, aur Jinnah.

Bees aur tees ke dashak mein kai chhote Musalman sangathan the. Aapas mein ladte, aam logon se door. Unki ahmiyat utni hi thi jitni Angrez sarkar unhe deti. Kaam ek hi tha: vidhan sabha aur naukriyon mein Musalmanon ke liye khaas hissa. Iske peechhe ek asli dar bhi tha. Padhai, naukri aur udyog mein Hindu aage the, aur ginti mein bhi zyada. Jinnah tab England mein the.

Frontier mein Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan poori tarah Congress ke saath the. Kai padhe-likhe Musalman bhi. UP jaise soobon mein Congress ke kisaan program ka asar Musalman kisaanon par bhi tha. Par aam Musalman dheere-dheere apne purane zameendaar aur nawab netaon ki taraf laut raha tha, jo ab Musalmanon ke rakhwaale bankar aaye the.

Samjhaute ki koshishein baar-baar hui. Har baar beech mein teesra kona tha: Angrez sarkar. Use asli sulah nahi chahiye thi. Wo kabhi is taraf ko kuch deti, kabhi us taraf ko. Nehru maante hain ki Hindustani agar samajhdaar hote to ye rukawat bhi paar kar lete. Par wo samajh nahi thi. Jab bhi sulah kareeb aati, sarkar koi chaal chalti aur palda palat jaata.

Congress ne kya diya? Har insaan aur har samudaay ke haq ki poori hifaazat. Pichhde tabqon ko jaldi aage laana, khaaskar dalit samaj ko. Auraton ko har baat mein mardon ke barabar haq. Aur ek anokhi peshkash: agar kisi dharmik alpsankhyak ke khaas maamle par jhagda ho, to faisla bahumat ke vote se nahi hoga. Wo ek nishpaksh adaalat ko jaayega, chahe antarrashtriya adaalat hi kyun na ho.

Alag nirvachan ke baare mein Nehru phir wahi kehte hain jo pichhle episode mein suna. Zyada seatein dene se bhi alpsankhyak bahumat nahi ban jaata. Ulta, bahusankhyak voter ki usme dilchaspi khatam ho jaati hai. Saanjhe chunaav mein ummeedwar ko har tabqe ke paas jaana padta hai. Alag chunaav mein ye zaroorat khatam.

Nehru Congress ki galtiyan maante hain, par chhoti, tareeqe ki. Congress ko sulah ki zaroorat thi, kyunki uske sadasya har dharm ke the. Wo poore desh ki soch sochne par majboor thi. Sirf ek samudaay ke sangathanon ke liye jhagda hi rozi-roti tha.

Phir ek kadwi sachchai. Sabko kisi baat par raazi karna kabhi mumkin nahi. Agar ek chhota gut har badlaav par veto laga sake, to badlaav kabhi nahi aayega. Aur jab hukumat khud aise guton ko paal rahi ho, to badlaav sirf kranti se aayega.

Musalmanon ke andar bhi sab League ke saath nahi the. Shia apna alag sangathan rakhte the, aur saanjhe chunaav ke paksh mein the. Nehru yahan Sunni aur Shia ke purane batwaare ka itihaas bhi samjhate hain, Arab mein khilafat ki gaddi ke jhagde se. Ahrar aur Jamiat-ul-Ulema jaise sangathan aksar Congress ki line par chalte the, aur isliye sarkari maar bhi jhelte the. In sab ne milkar 1940 mein Delhi mein Azad Muslim Conference ki, League ke khilaaf, aur wo bahut kaamyaab rahi.

Hindu taraf Hindu Mahasabha thi. Nehru ise League ka joda kehte hain, utni hi sankeern, bas rashtriya shabdon ka libaas pehne. Dono taraf ke kuch neta zeher ugalte the. Aur bolna kaam ki jagah le leta tha. Punjab aur Sindh ke Hindu alpsankhyak, aur Punjab ke Sikh, bhi kai baar sulah ke raaste mein aaye.

Ab ek kasauti: chunaav. 1937 mein Hindu Mahasabha ka safaya ho gaya. League ki haalat bhi kharab rahi, khaaskar Musalman bahul soobon mein. Punjab aur Sindh mein bilkul fail. Bengal mein aadhi-adhuri. Frontier mein baad mein Congress ki sarkar bani. Jahan Musalman alpsankhyak the, wahan League kuch behtar rahi.

Iske baad League ka rang badla. Nehru kehte hain ki unhone Hitler ke prachaar ke tareeqe dhyan se padhe the, aur Bharat mein wahi dekhkar chaunk gaye. 1938 mein Czechoslovakia ka Sudetenland sankat aaya. League ke kuch netaon ne Sudeten Germanon aur Hindustani Musalmanon ki tulna ki. Bhaashanon aur akhbaaron mein bhadkaav badha. Ek Congress Musalman mantri ko chaaku maara gaya, aur League ke kisi neta ne ninda tak nahi ki.

Asar hua. League upchunaav jeetne lagi. Par jeet mein bhi bahut se Musalman voter Congress ke kisaan program ke saath khade rahe. Aur pehli baar League aam logon ki party banne lagi. Nehru ko ye dukhi bhi karta hai, par ek umeed bhi deta hai. Shayad isse purane zameendaar netaon ki jagah naye, aage ki soch wale log aayenge.

Phir Jinnah ke saath patra-vyavahaar. Nehru tab Congress adhyaksh the. Unhone kai chitthiyan likhi. Seedha sawal: League chahti kya hai? Congress sarkaron se shikayat kya hai? Jinnah ke lambe jawaab aaye. Par kisi mein saaf baat nahi. Nehru ko hairat hoti hai, aur thodi bebasi bhi. Unhe lagta hai ki Jinnah na koi waada karna chahte the, na sulah.

Jinnah ki ek hi zid thi: pehle Congress maane ki League hi Musalmanon ki akeli numainda hai. Uske bina baat aage nahi. Nehru ka nateeja: Jinnah ko lagta tha ki maamla latakne se Angrezon se zyada milega.

Isi se do-qaumi siddhant nikla, aur usse Pakistan ka khayal. Nehru poochte hain, isse "do qaumon" ka sawal kaise suljhega, jab wo har gaon, har shehar mein saath rehti hain? Aur ek ulta asar bhi hua. Jab desh ki ekta par sawal utha, to bahut se logon mein ekta ke liye josh jaaga. Kabhi-kabhi todne ki koshish hi jodti hai.

Part 7: Ek yojana jo jail mein band hui

Ab National Planning Committee. Isme taraah-taraah ke log the. Udyogpati, arthshaastri, vaigyanik, Congress ke log, kuch soobon ke mantri. Nehru ko khud nahi pata tha ki ye khichdi kaise pakegi. Jhijhak ke saath chairman bane, kyunki kaam unke dil ka tha.

Har mod par mushkil. Aankde nahi the. Delhi ki sarkar ne madad nahi ki. Soobe dost the, par apne kaam mein uljhe. Congress ke andar bhi kuch log ise anchaaha bachcha maante the. Aur bade vyapari shak mein the. Nehru ka andaaza hai ki wo isliye jude ki andar baithkar apne hit behtar bacha sakein.

Pehla sawal: lakshya kya hai? Jawab: har aadmi ko ek theek-thaak zindagi. Arthshaastri kehte the ki ek aadmi ko kam se kam pandrah se pachchees rupaye mahina chahiye. Ye jung se pehle ke aankde hain. Aur asal mein kya tha? Ek Hindustani ki saalana ausat aamdani lagbhag painsath rupaye. Saal ki. Isme ameer bhi shaamil, shehar wale bhi. Gaon wale ki saalana aamdani shayad tees rupaye.

To kitna badhna hoga? Hisaab laga: desh ki daulat paanch se chhe guna badhe. Ye chhalaang bahut badi thi. To lakshya rakha das saal mein do se teen guna. Das saal ki yojana, alag-alag hisson ke liye alag nishaane.

Neeti ki kuch baatein. Desh jitna ho sake apne pairon par khada ho. Videshi vyapar band nahi, par hum kisi ke aarthik ghulam nahi banenge, aur kisi ko banayenge bhi nahi. Pehle apne logon ka pet, phir bahar ka bazaar. Sarkari udyog ek swatantra public trust chalaye. Cooperative bhi ho. Kheti ki zameen, khaanein, nadiyan aur jangal, sab poore desh ki saanjhi daulat. Taluqdar aur zameendaar jaise beech wale dheere-dheere hatenge, unke haq khareed kar. Banjar zameen par sarkar saamuhik kheti shuru kare. Bank aur bima par sarkar ka niyantran.

Nehru ek darr ka jawab bhi dete hain. Yojana mein niyantran hoga, to kya azadi kam hogi? Unka kehna hai, Bharat ke paas khone ko azadi hai hi kitni? Humein to sirf paani hai. Loktantra aur cooperative ka saath rahe, to bahut se khatre tal sakte hain.

Kaam ka tareeqa bhi dilchasp tha. Bahut si sub-committees. Har ek ki pehli report, uspar behes, wapas bhejna, phir aakhri report. Nehru ko is kaam mein desh ki kaabiliyat dekhkar bahut hausla mila. Shiksha par do faisle khaas the. Har umar ke ladke-ladkiyon ke liye sharirik fitness ke paimane. Aur atharah se baais saal ke har jawaan ke liye ek saal ki zaroori samaj seva. Kheti, kaarkhane, sadak, kahin bhi. Koi chhoot nahi, sirf sharirik ya maansik beemari mein.

Phir September 1939. Doosra vishwa yudh. Kuch logon ne kaha committee rok do. November mein Congress sarkaron ne istifa de diya. Ab soobon mein Governor ka raaj tha, aur unhe is kaam se koi matlab nahi tha. Vyapari jung ke theke se paisa kamaane mein lage the.

Committee ne faisla kiya ki kaam chalega. Ulta, jung mein iski zaroorat aur badh gayi. Us waqt mashin, transport, chemical aur kaarkhane wali reportein saamne thi. Jung ke liye sab zaroori. Par sarkar ko pasand nahi tha. Bhaari udyog ki manzoori nahi milti thi, aur manzoori ke bina koi mashin bahar se aa nahi sakti thi.

October 1940 mein Nehru giraftaar hue. Lambi saza. Committee ke kuch aur sadasya bhi jail gaye. Nehru chahte the ki saathi kaam jaari rakhein. Wo nahi maane. To Nehru ne kaha, kaagaz jail mein bhej do, main wahin draft likh doonga. Government of India ne rok diya. Na kaagaz aaye, na is par kisi se mulaqat ki ijazat mili.

Saari mehnat daftar ki almaariyon mein padi rahi. December 1941 mein Nehru chhoote. Par tab Pacific mein jung chhid chuki thi, Bharat par hamle ka khatra tha. Purane dhaage jodne ka waqt nahi tha. Aur phir wo wapas jail chale gaye.

Part 8: Charkha ya tractor

Ab ek purani behes. Bade kaarkhane ya gaon ke chhote udyog?

Pehle do kisse. Imperial Chemical Industries, yaani ICI, Britain ki ek vishaal company. Use baar-baar Hindustani udyog ki keemat par fayda diya gaya. Punjab ki khanij sampatti ke liye use lamba lease mila. Sharten kabhi bataayi nahi gayi.

Doosra kissa power alcohol ka. UP aur Bihar mein cheeni mill bahut thi. Unse sheera nikalta tha, jo bekaar phenk diya jaata. Congress sarkaron ne kaha, isse power alcohol banao, yaani gaadiyon ka eendhan. Tareeqa aasaan tha. Bas ek dikkat thi. Shell aur Burma Oil ke hit takrate the. Delhi ne mana kar diya. Jung ke teesre saal, jab Burma haath se gaya aur tel ruk gaya, tab akl aayi. 1942 mein ek American dal, Grady Committee, ne bhi yahi salaah di.

Ab Gandhi aur mashin. Gandhi ki zindagi ki samajh hi alag thi, aur ye unki bhasha aur kaam dono mein dikhti thi. Naitikta sabse upar. Galat raasta kabhi sahi nahi hota.

Nehru apni raay saaf rakhte hain. Wo tractor aur badi mashin ke paksh mein hain. Unka maanna hai ki zameen par bojh kam karne, gareebi mitane, aur desh ki suraksha ke liye tez audyogikaran zaroori hai. Par bina yojana ke nahi. China aur Bharat jaise purane deshon mein, jinki apni gehri parampara hai, aur bhi soch-samajhkar.

China mein Nehru ne Industrial Cooperatives dekhe the, jinhe "Indusco" kehte the. Unhe laga ye Bharat ke liye bilkul theek hai. Chhote udyog ko loktantrik aadhar milega, aur saath kaam karne ki aadat padegi. Bade udyog jitni tezi se badhein, chhote udyog ki jagah hamesha rahegi. Bijli chhote kaam ko bade se takkar lene layak banati hai. Henry Ford tak ne vikendrikaran ki baat ki thi. Aur kuch vaigyanik kehne lage the ki bade shehar insaan ko mitti se kaat dete hain.

To Congress ne khadi aur gaon ke udyog kyun chune? Nehru ka jawab vyavaharik hai. Angrezi raaj aur uske swarthon se bandhe hum aur kya kar sakte the? Gareeb ko kuch kamaai aur khud par bharosa dena tha. Nehru galti bhi maante hain. Cooperative shuru se apnane chahiye the. Gaon ki chhoti mashinon ko behtar banane ke liye vaigyanikon ki madad leni chahiye thi.

Gandhi bhi badle. Nehru unke kuch bayaan dete hain. Saar ye ki Gandhi ko mashin se nahi, mashin ke junoon se dikkat thi. Har gaon ke ghar mein bijli ho, to unhe aitraaz nahi. Asli paap ye hai ki mashin se daulat aur taaqat kuch haathon mein simat jaaye. Wo kai bhaari udyogon ko bhi maanne lage, sharat ye ki wo sarkar ke hon. Aur unhone kaha ki aarthik barabari ke bina unka poora program ret par bana mahal hai.

Nehru ke liye ye behes ab purani ho chuki hai. Duniya bade udyog ki taraf ja chuki hai. Bharat bhi. Bina yojana ke audyogikaran ki burayiyan sab jaante hain. Par wo burayiyan mashin ki hain ya uske peechhe ke aarthik dhaanche ki? Agar dhaanche ki, to dhaancha badlo. Mashin ko kya kosna.

Aur asli baat. Ye sirf utpaadan ka hisaab nahi hai. Ye soch ka badlaav hai. Bharat bahut der se purane dharre mein atka hai. Naye anubhav naye vichaar laate hain. Man chalne lagta hai, khojne lagta hai.

Isliye Nehru chahte hain ki har bachcha physics, chemistry aur khaaskar biology ki buniyaadi baatein seekhe. Wo khud sahitya ke premi hain, purane granth unhe pyaare hain. Par vigyaan ke bina aaj ki duniya samajh nahi aayegi. Aur unhe vigyaan mein ek chamatkar dikhta hai. Nazuk aur taaqatwar mashinein, kudrat ke karkhane mein jhaankne ki khidki. Aur sabse badi baat, ye sab insaan ke dimaag se nikla hai.

Part 9: Viceroy jo company ka director bana

Aakhri hissa sabse seedha hai. Sarkar ne Bharat ke udyog ko kaise roka.

Bhaari udyog ke naam par Bharat mein ek hi cheez thi. Jamshedpur ka Tata Iron and Steel. Baaki workshop marammat ki dukaanein thi. Pehle vishwa yudh mein engine aur railway dibbon ki kami hui. Tata ne railway engine banane ka socha, shayad mashin bhi mangwa li. Jung khatam hui. Sarkar aur Railway Board ne phir se England ke engine khareedne shuru kiye. Railway sarkari thi ya Angrezi companiyon ki. Doosra koi khareedaar tha hi nahi. Tata ko iraada chhodna pada.

Jamshedji Tata ne Bangalore mein Indian Institute of Science shuru kiya tha. Nehru kehte hain, America aur Soviet Union mein hazaaron research sansthan hain. Bharat mein Bangalore ke alawa lagbhag kuch nahi tha.

Jung ke dauran saikdon railway engine aur hazaaron dibbe Middle East bhej diye gaye. Kahin-kahin patri tak ukhaad kar bhej di gayi. Bharat mein transport ka sankat aur badh gaya. Nehru ko aage ki soch ki is kami par hairat hoti hai.

1939 ke aakhir ya 1940 ki shuruaat mein Bharat mein hawai jahaaz banane ki koshish hui. Ek American company se baat tay thi. Sarkar aur army headquarters ko taar bheje gaye. Koi jawaab nahi. Baar-baar yaad dilane par jawaab aaya: mana. Jahaaz England aur America se khareed lo, yahan banane ki kya zaroorat.

Dawaiyon ka kissa aur bhi sunne layak hai. Jung se pehle bahut si dawaiyan aur vaccine Germany se aati thi. Jung ne ye rasta band kiya. Suggestion aaya ki kuch zaroori vaccine sarkari sansthanon mein bana lo. Sarkar ne kaha, sab ICI se mil jaayega. Phir kaha gaya ki yahan banana sasta padega, fauj aur aam janta dono ke kaam aayega, kisi ka niji munafa nahi. Ek bade afsar bhadak gaye. Unka jawaab tha ki sarkar koi dukaan nahi chalati.

Nehru ka tanz yahan sabse tez hai. Sarkar dukaan nahi chalati thi, par dukaanon mein bahut dilchaspi rakhti thi. Khaaskar ek dukaan mein: ICI. Lord Linlithgow, jo 1936 se 1943 tak Viceroy rahe, Bharat chhodne ke kuch hi mahino baad ICI ke director ban gaye. Nehru kehte hain, ye London ke bade vyapar aur Delhi ki sarkar ke rishte ka saboot hai. Ho sakta hai Viceroy rehte hue bhi wo uske share-holder rahe hon.

Ab aankde. December 1942 mein Linlithgow ne bataya ki jung ke liye Bharat ka yogdaan kitna bada hai. Sarkari theke chha mahine mein unteesh crore rupaye se badhkar ek sau saintees crore tak pahunche. Lagta hai udyog chhalaang maar raha hoga. Par udyog ka index dekhiye, 1935 ko sau maankar. 1938-39 mein ek sau gyarah. 1939-40 mein ek sau chaudah. Beech mein December 1941 mein ek baar ek sau sattaais tak gaya. April 1942 mein ek sau nau. July 1942 mein ek sau solah. Yaani hathiyaaron ko chhodkar, kul udyog jung se pehle se bas thoda sa upar.

Matlab saaf hai. Jung ka utpaadan naya utpaadan nahi tha. Wo aam logon ke liye bannewaale maal ko mod kar jung ki taraf bhej raha tha.

Kuch log zaroor ameer hue. Kapda, loha aur jute mill. Jung ke thekedaar, jamakhor, munafakhor. Crorepatiyon ki ginti badhi. Par mazdoor neta N.M. Joshi ne Central Assembly mein kaha ki jung mein mazdoor ki haalat aur kharab hui hai. Punjab aur Sindh ke bade kisaan khush the. Baaki kisaan pis gaye. Aur mehngaai ne har khareedaar ki kamar todi.

Grady Committee ki report kabhi chhapi nahi. Shayad sarkar ne rok di. Kuch salaahein bahar aayi. Power alcohol, zyada steel, zyada bijli, aluminium, aur ek taaqatwar utpaadan board, American tareeqe par. Committee ko Delhi ki sust chaal pasand nahi aayi. Par do cheezon ki unhone khule dil se taareef ki. Tata Steel ka intezaam, jo poori tarah Hindustani chala rahe the. Aur Hindustani mazdoor. Haath ka hunarmand, aur achhi haalat mile to bharose layak.

Chapter ek umeed ke saath khatam hota hai. Kuch naye udyog uge hain, chemical, jahaaz, chhota sa hawai jahaaz udyog. Naye udyog mein paisa lagane par jo rok thi, wo thodi dheeli hui hai. Aur bade vyapari badi-badi yojanaein bana rahe hain. Bharat, jiska vikaas itne lambe samay tak roka gaya, ab audyogikaran ke darwaaze par khada hai.

Par Nehru ek line yaad dilate hain jo poore chapter ka dard hai. Aankde badlein ya na badlein, tasveer wahi hai. Wahi tukdon wale ilaaj, wahi bina yojana ka kaam, Angrezi udyog ke liye wahi pakshpaat. Aur is beech, log bhookh aur beemari se marte rahe.

Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, Planning Committee. Jo sapna jail ki almaari mein band raha, wahi 1950 mein Planning Commission bana, aur 1951 mein pehli Panchvarshiya Yojana aayi. Das saal ki jagah paanch saal, par soch wahi.

Doosri, Viceroy se company director. Aaj bhi ye sawal zinda hai ki bade afsar riti hote hi kis company mein jaate hain. Ise "revolving door" kaha jaata hai.

Teesri, ek saal ki zaroori samaj seva. 1939 ki ek committee ne har jawaan ke liye ye suggest kiya tha. Bharat mein ye kabhi lagu nahi hua. NSS aur NCC jaise swechha wale program zaroor bane.

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru kehte hain ki Gandhi ki sabse badi den logon ke man se dar nikalna thi. Aaj aapki zindagi mein kaun sa dar hai jo asli khatre se bada hai?

Outro

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
