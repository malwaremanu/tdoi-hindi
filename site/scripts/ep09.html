Cold open

Bombay. 8 August 1942. Shaam dhal chuki hai, raat hone wali hai.

Ek bade pandaal mein hazaaron log baithe hain. Manch par Congress ke neta hain. Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, Congress ke president. Aur unke paas ek dubla-patla buzurg, Mahatma Gandhi.

Ek prastaav padha ja raha hai. Usme likha hai ki ab desh ko roka nahi ja sakta. Ki angrezon ko Bharat chhodna hoga. Aur agar wo nahi maante, to ek bada, ahinsak jan-andolan chalega, Gandhiji ki aguai mein.

Der shaam prastaav paas ho jaata hai. Log ghar laut jaate hain. Neta bhi so jaate hain. Kal se baat-cheet ki koshish honi hai. Viceroy ko chitthi jaani hai.

Par kal ki subah kuch aur hi lekar aati hai. Sooraj nikalne se pehle police darwaze khatkhatati hai. Bombay mein, aur poore desh mein, ek saath. Ek-ek karke saare bade neta utha liye jaate hain.

Unme ek hain Jawaharlal Nehru. Aur unki manzil hai wahi jagah jahan se humne ye safar shuru kiya tha. Ahmadnagar ka qila.

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 9: Aakhri Daur, teesra hissa. Doosra vishwa yudh. Aaj hum dekhenge ki baat us raat tak pahunchi kaise.

Part 1: Ek ghulam desh ki videsh niti

Kahani 1927 se shuru hoti hai. Jung abhi baarah saal door hai.

Europe mein us waqt shaanti ki baatein ho rahi thi. Germany League of Nations mein aa chuka tha. Locarno ke samjhaute ko hamesha ki shaanti ki neenv bataya ja raha tha, aur Britain ki badi jeet bhi. Par ek doosra nazariya bhi tha. Ki ye sab Soviet Russia ko akela karne ki chaal hai.

Russia ne apni kranti ke das saal poore kiye the. Turkey, Iran, Afghanistan aur Mongolia se uski dosti ho gayi thi. Udhar China mein rashtravaadi fauj aadhe desh par qabza kar chuki thi, aur bandargahon par angrezon se takra rahi thi.

America in sab se door tha. Communism se use nafrat thi, isliye Russia ke saath nahi. Britain ke kaarobar se muqabla tha, isliye uske saath bhi nahi. Aur upar se Europe ke jhagdon mein na fansne ki zid.

Bharat ki hamdardi kis taraf thi? Russia aur Asia ke deshon ki taraf. Iska matlab communism ki tareef nahi tha. Bas China ki har jeet mein log apni azadi ki aahat sunte the. Indonesia, Indo-China, Misr ke andolanon mein dilchaspi badhi.

Aur kuch nishaan chinta ke the. Singapore ko ek bada navy adda banaya ja raha tha. Ceylon mein Trincomalee ka bandargah taiyar ho raha tha. Nehru ko lagta tha ki ye aane wali jung ki taiyari hai.

Isi mahaul mein 1927 mein Congress ne apni videsh niti ki neenv rakhi. Do baatein. Bharat kisi samrajyawadi jung ka hissa nahi banega. Aur Bharat ko uske logon ki marzi ke bina kisi jung mein nahi dhakela jayega. Ye baat baar-baar dohraayi gayi. Desh mein kisi ne iska virodh nahi kiya.

Phir Hitler aaya. Congress ne turant uski ninda ki. Kyunki Nehru ko Hitler mein wahi samrajyawad aur nasl-parasti dikhti thi, jiske khilaaf Bharat lad raha tha, bas zyada nangi shakl mein. Manchuria par Japan ka hamla, Abyssinia, Spain, Czechoslovakia, Munich. Har ghatna ke saath gussa badha.

Nehru yahan ek kadwa sabak darj karte hain. Sankat ke waqt har desh sirf apna fayda dekhta hai. Munich ke dino mein Europe ke saikdon antarrashtriya sangathan, fascism-virodhi manch, sab achanak gunge ho gaye. Wo London ke Economist akhbaar ki ek tippani ka zikr karte hain, jo yahi baat maanti thi.

Aur ghulam desh? Uska poora dhyaan apni azadi par hota hai. Nehru ise sharir mein pal rahe ek rog jaisa batate hain, jo har soch ko rang deta hai. Aur azadi milne ke baad bhi ilaaj dheere hota hai. Man ke ghaav sharir ke ghaavon se der mein bharte hain.

Phir bhi Bharat mein nafrat kam rahi. Iska shrey Nehru Gandhiji ko dete hain. Gandhi kattar rashtravaadi the, par unki nazar poori duniya par thi. Wo azaad deshon ke ek vishwa-sangh ka sapna dekhte the. Nehru unke kuch shabd bhi yaad karte hain, jinme rashtravaad ko insaaniyat ki seva se joda gaya hai.

Ek kaanta South Africa tha, aur Kenya. Wahan Hindustaniyon ke saath rang ke naam par bhed-bhaav hota tha. Ajeeb baat ye ki Canada, Australia aur New Zealand ke logon se, ek-ek insaan ke taur par, Hindustani khoob ghul-mil jaate the.

Congress ki azadi ka matlab alag-thalag rehna nahi tha. Wo kehti thi ki hum doosre deshon ke saath milkar apni azadi par kuch seemayein maan lenge, kisi duniya-bhar ke dhaanche ke andar.

Desh mein kuch log is par naraaz bhi the. Wo kehte the: Italy, Germany aur Japan jaise taaqatwar deshon se dushmani kyun? Angrez ka dushman humara dost. Rajneeti mein aadarsh ki jagah nahi. Par Congress ke banaye jan-mat ke aage ye log khulkar bol nahi paaye. Aur Muslim League in sab sawalon par chupchaap rahi.

Part 2: Ek aadmi ne 40 crore ko jung mein jhonk diya

Congress ki ek aur maang thi. Hindustani fauj ko bina logon ki marzi ke desh ke bahar na bheja jaye. Central Legislative Assembly ne bhi yahi kaha tha.

Iske peeche purana dard tha. Hindustani sipahi Burma, China, Iran, Middle East aur Africa mein angrezon ke liye lade the. Unhi logon ke khilaaf, jinse Bharat ka koi jhagda nahi tha. Nehru ek Misri aadmi ka kadwa taana yaad karte hain. Uska matlab tha ki tum khud to ghulam ho hi, doosron ko ghulam banane mein bhi angrez ki madad karte ho.

Nehru maante hain ki Congress ki niti mein ek ulajhan thi. Ek taraf fascism ka virodh, doosri taraf angrez samrajya ka bhi. Par ye ulajhan halaat ki thi, Congress ki banayi hui nahi. Fascism door tha, akhbaaron mein padha jaata tha. Samrajyawad roz darwaze par khada tha. Fark darje ka tha, kism ka nahi. Aur ek baat saaf thi. Hamle ke khilaaf hathiyar uthane mein ahinsa kabhi aade nahi aayi.

1938 ki garmiyon mein Nehru England aur Europe mein the. Bhashanon mein, lekhon mein, niji baaton mein wo yahi samjhate rahe. Sudetenland ke sankat ke beech pareshaan Czech log unse poochte the: jung hui to Bharat kya karega? Unke paas baarikiyon ka waqt nahi tha. Phir bhi unhone Nehru ki baat ka tark samjha.

Phir 1939 aaya. Sarkar ne logon se poochhe bina faisle lene shuru kar diye. Congress ki Working Committee ne kaha ki ye Congress aur Assembly, dono ki baat ko jaan-boojhkar thukrana hai. August 1939 ki shuruaat mein virodh ka pehla qadam uthaya gaya. Central Assembly ke Congress sadasyon se kaha gaya ki agle session mein na jaayein.

Ye jung shuru hone se teen hafte pehle ki baat hai.

Logon ko dar tha ki 1914 dohraya jayega. Ki angrez phir jung Bharat par thop denge, aur jung ke bahane jo thodi-bahut azadi mili hai, wo bhi chheen lenge. Par pacchees saal mein desh badal chuka tha. Log poochte the: kya bees saal ki ladai aur qurbani ka koi mol nahi? Kya hum chupchaap ye beizzati sah lein?

Phir 3 September 1939 aaya. Europe mein jung ka elaan hua. Aur usi waqt Viceroy ne elaan kiya ki Bharat bhi jung mein hai.

Nehru ki chubhan yahan saaf hai. Ek aadmi. Wo bhi videshi. Ek nafrat kiye jaane wale system ka numainda. Aur usne chaalees crore logon ko, bina unse ek shabd poochhe, jung mein utaar diya. Canada aur Australia jaise dominion mein ye faisla chune hue logon ne lamba behes karke liya tha. Bharat mein nahi. Aur ye baat dil par lagi.

Part 3: Sahyog ka ek shabd, do matlab

Jab Europe mein jung shuru hui, Nehru China mein the, Chungking mein. Congress president ka taar aaya: turant lauto. Wo bhaage-bhaage wapas aaye.

Lautkar dekha ki Working Committee ki baithak chal rahi hai. Mohammad Ali Jinnah ko bhi bulaya gaya tha, par wo nahi aa paaye. Is beech Viceroy ne kai ordinance nikaal diye the. London ki Parliament ne bhi ek naya qanoon paas kiya tha, jisse sooba sarkaron ke haath baandh diye gaye. Kisi chune hue numainde se kuch nahi poocha gaya.

14 September 1939. Kaafi soch-vichaar ke baad Working Committee ne ek lamba bayaan jaari kiya. Usme Poland par Nazi hamle ki saaf ninda thi, aur jo log lad rahe the, unse hamdardi. Saath mein sahyog ki peshkash bhi. Par kuch sawal bhi. Ye jung kis cheez ke liye hai? Democracy ke liye? To kya wo democracy Bharat par bhi lagu hogi?

Ek chutki bhi thi. Riyasaton ke raja Europe mein democracy bachane ke liye madad ka vaada kar rahe the. Committee ne kaha ki achha ho, ye log pehle apni riyasaton mein democracy le aayein, jahan unka poora ekchhatra raaj hai.

Nehru maante hain ki ye peshkash aasaan nahi thi. Desh ke zyadatar log antarrashtriya baarikiyan nahi samajhte the. Wo bas angrezon se naraaz the. Phir bhi umeed thi ki sankat ke dabav mein London ke neta purane dhaanche se bahar niklenge.

Aisa nahi hua. Jawab mein saaf inkaar mila. Congress ko samajh aaya ki angrez humein dost ya saathi nahi, hukm maanne wale ghulam ki tarah chahte hain. Shabd ek hi tha, "sahyog". Par Congress ke liye uska matlab tha barabar ke saathiyon ka kaam. Angrezon ke liye matlab tha: hum hukm denge, tum bina sawal maano.

To Congress ki sooba sarkarein istifa de gayi. Bengal, Punjab aur Sind mein istife nahi hue. Wahan pehle se Governor aur bade afsar hi asli maalik the. Phir bhi baad mein Bengal ke Governor ne apne hi Prime Minister ko hata diya, kyunki wo pasand nahi the. Sind ke Prime Minister ne Viceroy ko chitthi likhkar angrez niti ki aalochana ki, aur virodh mein sarkar ka diya hua ek sammaan lauta diya. Istifa nahi diya. To Viceroy ne Governor se unhe barkhaast karwa diya.

Nehru jab ye likh rahe hain, tab tak lagbhag paanch saal ho chuke hain. Sooba-sooba Governor ka ek-aadmi raaj. Unke shabdon ka saar ye hai ki jung ki dhundh mein desh unneesvi sadi ki taanashahi mein wapas pahunch gaya. Congress sarkaron ka kiya hua kaam mita diya gaya. Bas kisaanon ke kuch qanoon bache, aur unhe bhi aksar kisaan ke khilaaf padha jaata.

Aur kuch soobon mein sarkarein banane ka ek seedha tareeqa nikala gaya. Assam, Orissa aur Frontier mein kai vidhayakon ko jail bhejo. Alpmat apne aap bahumat ban jayega. Bengal ki sarkar poori tarah European vidhayakon ke sahare tiki thi.

Part 4: Doosri peshkash, aur Churchill

Istifon ke baad afsar apne-apne ilaqe ke chhote taanashah ban gaye. Police puraani aadaton par laut aayi. Jung ki dhundh mein sab chhip jaata tha.

Ajeeb baat ye hui ki Congress sarkaron ke aalochak bhi pachhtaane lage. Ab unhe un sarkaron ki khoobiyan yaad aane lagi. Kehne lage ki istifa nahi dena chahiye tha. Muslim League ke kuch log bhi chintit the.

Congress ne desh ko yaad dilaya ki agar civil disobedience ho bhi, to poori tarah shaant ho. Satyagraha ka matlab sabke liye sadbhav hai, khaaskar virodhi ke liye. Nehru saaf karte hain ki ye ahinsa angrezon ke khilaaf azadi ki ladai ke liye thi. Desh ki raksha ya jung se iska koi lena-dena nahi tha.

Ye Europe mein jung ka "phoney" daur tha. Poland ke girne ke baad sab shaant tha. Bharat mein aam aadmi ko jung bahut door lagti thi. Communist Party us waqt jung mein angrezon ki madad ke sakht khilaaf thi, aur June 1941 tak rahi. Party par pabandi thi, asar kam tha. Par wo jawanon ke beech josh bhadkane wale gut ki tarah kaam karti thi.

Andar halaat bigad rahe the. Congress walon ko roz ke kaam ke liye jail bheja ja raha tha. Gaon mein chhote afsar aur police jung ke naam par kisaanon se vasooli kar rahe the. Defence of India Act ke tahat bina mukadme giraftariyan ho rahi thi.

March 1940. Bihar ke Ramgarh mein Congress ka saalana adhiveshan hua, Maulana Azad ki adhyakshata mein. Faisla hua ki ab civil disobedience hi raasta bacha hai. Par turant shuru nahi kiya gaya. Logon se kaha gaya: taiyar raho.

Phir jung ne karvat li. Denmark aur Norway par hamla. Phir France ka hairatangez tarah se gir jaana. Dunkirk. England par hawai bambaari. Bharat mein England ke liye hamdardi ki lehar uthi. Kuch log kehte the ki England ki museebat Bharat ka mauqa hai. Congress ke netaon ne ise saaf thukra diya. Jab azaad England ka astitva hi daav par ho, tab andolan nahi. Civil disobedience ki baat band kar di gayi.

Aur Congress ne ek nayi peshkash ki. Is baar choti aur seedhi. Bharat ki azadi ko maan lo. Kendra mein ek rashtriya sarkar banao, jisme alag-alag dal hon. Naye qanoon ki zaroorat nahi, Viceroy maujooda dhaanche mein hi ye kar sakte hain. Badle mein jung mein poora sahyog.

Nehru khud ise maanne mein hichkichaye. Unhe lagta tha ki aisi sarkar kamzor aur bebas hogi. Congress ke andar bhi virodh tha. Par wo maan gaye, kyunki wo chahte the ki agar izzat ke saath mumkin ho, to Bharat fascism ke khilaaf ladai mein poori tarah utre.

Is beech desh ki maali haalat bhi bigad rahi thi. Faayda sirf jung ke munafakhoron, thekedaron aur moti tankhwah wale angrez afsaron ko ho raha tha. Bhrashtachar aur bhai-bhatijawaad khulkar tha. Aur aalochana karna jung ke kaam mein rukawat maana jaata.

London mein bhi badlav hua. Neville Chamberlain chale gaye. India Office se Lord Zetland bhi gaye, aur Nehru vyang karte hain ki kisi ne aansu nahi bahaye. Unki jagah aaye Leopold Amery. Unke baare mein ek baat pata thi. Unhone Parliament mein China par Japan ke hamle ka bachaav kiya tha. Daleel ye di thi ki Japan ki ninda karoge, to Bharat aur Misr mein Britain ne jo kiya, uski bhi karni padegi. Nehru kehte hain: daleel sahi, istemaal galat.

Par asli aadmi the naye Prime Minister, Winston Churchill. Bharat ki azadi par unki raay saaf thi, aur baar-baar dohraayi gayi thi. Nehru unke 1930 aur 1931 ke kai bayaan gina dete hain. Ek mein Gandhi aur Congress ko kuchalne ki baat hai. Ek mein Bharat ko British taaj ka sabse keemti heera kaha gaya hai. Aur ek mein ye ki "dominion status" bas kehne ki baat hai, jo kisi nazdeeki waqt mein lagu nahi hogi.

Nehru ke liye yahi jad thi. Bharat hi samrajya tha. Usi par qabza England ko bada banata tha. Churchill England ko samrajya ke bina soch hi nahi sakte the. To Bharat ko azaad kaise sochte?

Peshkash ka jawab jaldi aaya. August 1940 mein. Poora inkaar. Aur aisi bhasha mein, jisse lagta tha ki angrez desh ko baantne wale har tatva ko badhava denge, chahe Bharat barbaad ho jaye.

Nehru ko gehra jhatka laga. Unhone us waqt ek lekh likha, jiska naam rakha "The Parting of the Ways", yaani raaste alag. Wo hamesha azadi chahte the, par England se dosti ki umeed bhi rakhte the. Ab pehli baar laga ki saath chalne ka koi raasta nahi bacha.

Part 5: Ek-ek karke jail

Azadi ka nasha, jo desh ki poori taaqat ko jung mein jhonk deta, wo nahi mila. Mili inkaar ki tees. Aur saath mein ghamand bhari bhasha, aur aisi sharton ki lambi list jo shayad kabhi poori na ho sakein. Nehru ko lagne laga ki London ki saari behes aur bhaari-bharkam bhashan bas ek parda hai. Iraada ek hi hai: jab tak ho sake, Bharat ko pakde raho.

Burma ne bhi ek chhoti si maang rakhi thi, jung ke baad dominion status ka bharosa. Wo bhi thukra di gayi. Nehru likhte hain ki Burma mein iska nateeja jaldi dikh gaya.

Ab chup baithna mumkin nahi tha. Nehru ek baat yaad dilate hain. Angrez sarkar Bharat mein rashtravaadi aur mazdoor andolan se hamesha ek jung ladti rehti hai. Kabhi tez, kabhi dheemi, par kabhi band nahi.

To October 1940 mein vyaktigat satyagraha shuru hua. Ye bahut chhote paimane par tha. Har aadmi ko pehle ek tarah ki pareeksha deni padti, ijazat leni padti. Chune hue log koi sarkari hukm todte, giraftaar hote, saza paate. Sabse pehle upar ke log chune gaye: Working Committee ke sadasya, purane mantri, vidhayak. Dheere-dheere ginti badhti gayi, pacchees se tees hazaar tak. Inme vidhan sabhaon ke speaker bhi the.

Sandesh ye tha. Agar humari chuni hui sabhaon ko chalne nahi doge, to uske sadasya taanashahi ke aage nahi jhukenge. Wo jail chun lenge.

Nehru jaldi giraftaar hue. Ek bhashan ke liye chaar saal ki saza.

Ek saal se zyada sab jail mein rahe. Jo thodi-bahut khabar milti, usse jung ko samajhne ki koshish karte. President Roosevelt ki chaar azadiyon ke baare mein padha. Atlantic Charter ke baare mein suna. Aur phir Churchill ki safai suni, ki ye Charter Bharat par lagu nahi hota.

June 1941 mein Hitler ne achanak Soviet Russia par hamla kiya. Jail mein sab saans rokkar jung ka badalta rukh dekhte rahe.

4 December 1941 ko kai log reha kar diye gaye. Teen din baad, 7 December ko, Pearl Harbour hua. Japan jung mein utar aaya. Ab jung Bharat ke darwaze ki taraf badh rahi thi.

Part 6: Gandhi aur ahinsa ki kashmakash

Yahan Nehru ek galatfahmi door karte hain. Congress ne kabhi nahi socha tha ki ahinsa fauj, navy, air force ya police par lagu hogi. Ye maani hui baat thi ki ahinsa sirf azadi ki ladai ka tareeqa hai. Haan, iska asar ye zaroor hua ki Congress hathiyar ghataane aur jhagdon ke shaantipoorn hal ki himayati bani.

Ek misaal dete hain. Jab sooba sarkarein thi, kai sarkarein college aur university mein military training shuru karna chahti thi. Rok lagayi Government of India ne.

Phir Gandhi ke mizaaj ki baat aati hai. Nehru likhte hain ki Gandhi apne kuch usoolon par chattan ki tarah ade rehte hain. Par unme doosron ke saath, aur badalte halaat ke saath, dhalne ki bhi gazab ki kshamta hai. Wo dekhte hain ki aam log kitna kar sakte hain. Phir kabhi achanak rukte hain, jaise dar gaye hon ki samjhauta zyada ho gaya, aur wapas apne khoonte par laut jaate hain. Andolan ke beech wo janta ke man ke saath chalte hain. Baaki waqt zyada siddhantwadi lagte hain. Unke kaam aur unke lekhon mein bhi ye fark dikhta hai. Isse unke apne log bhi uljhan mein pad jaate hain.

Par Nehru ek baat saaf kehte hain. Gandhi Bharat ke sabse bade neta ahinsa ya arthshastra ki wajah se nahi hain. Aam Hindustani ke liye wo azadi ki zid ka nishaan hain. Taaqat ke ghamand ke aage na jhukne ka. Kabhi beizzati na maanne ka. Log sau baaton par unse asehmat ho sakte hain. Par jab azadi daav par ho, sab unhi ke paas laut aate hain.

December 1941 mein ek purana jhagda phir uth khada hua. Gandhiji chahte the ki Congress poori tarah ahinsa par tike, jung mein bhi. Maulana Azad aur kai doosre neta ye nahi maan sake. Gandhiji ke kuch pakke chele bhi asehmat the. Halaat itni tezi se badal rahe the ki Gandhiji ne apni baat par zor nahi diya, haalaanki wo Congress ke rukh se jude bhi nahi.

Iske baad unhone ye sawal Congress mein kabhi nahi uthaya. Cripps ke prastaav par baat sirf rajneeti ki hui, ahinsa ki nahi. Aur aage chalkar, August 1942 tak, Gandhiji is had tak maan gaye ki agar Bharat azaad ho, to Congress jung mein hissa le sakti hai.

Nehru ke liye ye hairat ki baat thi. Gandhi ke liye ahinsa unki zindagi ka khoon thi. Aur Bharat ki azadi unka sabse bada junoon. Jab dono takraaye, palda azadi ki taraf jhuka. Ahinsa mein unka vishwas kam nahi hua. Par wo maan gaye ki is jung mein Congress use lagu na kare. Nehru ke shabdon ka saar: kattar paigambar par vyavaharik rajneta bhaari pada.

Gandhi ke man ke is baar-baar ke sangharsh ko samajhne ke liye Nehru angrez sainya-lekhak Liddell Hart ki ek kitaab ka ek ansh yaad karte hain.

Part 7: Jung darwaze par

1942 ki shuruaat. Jung tezi se Bharat ki taraf aa rahi thi.

Japan ne angrezon ke do bade jangi jahaaz, Prince of Wales aur Repulse, duba diye the. Nehru ek kadwi baat darj karte hain. Angrezon ko jahaaz doobne se zyada is baat ka dukh laga ki unhe ek Asian desh ne duboya. Germany duboti to shayad itna na chubhta.

Isi daur mein China ke neta Generalissimo Chiang Kai-shek aur Madame Chiang Kai-shek Bharat aaye. Ye badi ghatna thi. Sarkari niyamon ki wajah se wo aam logon se mil nahi paaye. Par unki maujoodgi, aur Bharat ki azadi ke liye unki khuli hamdardi ne desh ko apne khol se bahar nikaala. Bharat aur China ka rishta aur gehra hua. Nehru kehte hain ki khatre ne rashtravaad aur antarrashtriyavaad ko paas la diya. Beech mein bas ek cheez thi: angrez sarkar ki niti.

Ek aur vyang. London mein yudh mein haare deshon ki nirvasit sarkarein baithi thi. Bharat ko bhi ek "tohfa" mila. Doosri angrez colonies se bhaage hue nirvasit afsar. Nehru kehte hain ki ye log Bharat ke angrez dhaanche mein bilkul fit baith gaye.

Gandhiji is sab se bahut pareshaan the. Hamlavar fauj ke saamne civil disobedience ka koi matlab nahi tha. Chupchaap hamla sah lena bhi nahi. To kya karein? Congress ne hamle ke khilaaf ahinsa ka raasta thukra diya tha, aur Gandhi maan chuke the ki unhe aisa karne ka haq hai. Par khud, ek insaan ke taur par, wo kisi hinsak raaste mein shamil nahi ho sakte the. Aur wo sirf ek insaan nahi the. Koi pad ho ya na ho, unki baat karodon log sunte the.

Nehru yahan Gandhi ki samajh ki tareef karte hain. Gandhi ko itihaas mein khaas dilchaspi nahi thi. Par wo Bharat ke logon ki jadon ko bahut kareeb se jaante the. Kisi bhi samasya ka saar pakad lete the, faaltu baaton ko chhodkar. Har cheez ko naitik nazar se dekhte, aur isse unhe lambi door ki nazar milti. Yahan Nehru Bernard Shaw ki ek raay ka zikr karte hain, ki Gandhi chhoti-chhoti chaalon mein galti kar sakte hain, par unki badi ranneeti sahi rehti hai.

Part 8: Cripps aaye

Is beech Malaya aur Burma se bhaage hue log Bharat pahunch rahe the. Bhookhe, thake hue. Aur apne saath rang-bhed ki darawni kahaniyan la rahe the. Ki raaste mein gore aur kaale ke liye alag niyam the. Ye kahaniyan poore desh mein phail gayi.

Isi waqt, March 1942 mein, British War Cabinet ke sadasya Sir Stafford Cripps ek prastaav lekar Bharat aaye.

Nehru maante hain ki is par likhna unke liye mushkil hai, kyunki wo khud baat-cheet mein the, aur kuch baatein abhi kehne ki nahi. Par itna batate hain ki prastaav pehli baar padhkar wo gehre udaas ho gaye. Jitna padha, utni udaasi badhi. Unhe Cripps se, aur us sankat ki ghadi se, kahin zyada ki umeed thi.

Kisi bahar wale ko lag sakta tha ki prastaav bahut kuch de raha hai. Par andar jhaankne par har jagah bediyan thi. Sabse bada khatra ye tha ki koi bhi sooba chahe to naye Bharat se alag reh sakta tha. Nehru kehte hain ki ye sirf ek batwaare ki baat nahi thi. Ye anginat batwaaron ka darwaza tha.

Phir riyasatein. Unme lagbhag nau crore log rehte the. Unke bhavishya par unki koi aawaaz nahi thi. Faisla raja karte. Gandhiji rajaon ke dushman nahi the. Par wo saaf keh chuke the ki British Bharat ki azadi ke liye bhi riyasaton ki janta ke haq ka sauda nahi karenge. Nehru batate hain ki Commonwealth qanoon ke bade jaankar Professor Berriedale Keith bhi is mudde par Gandhi ke saath the.

Nehru ko poora naqsha ek shatranj ki bisaat jaisa lagta hai. Darjanon naam ke azaad tukde. Kai tukde apni taanashahi bachane ke liye Britain ki fauj par tike. Na rajneetik ekta, na aarthik. Aur upar se Britain ka dabdaba.

Nehru maante hain ki Cripps khud Bharat ka bhala chahte the. Par baat kisi ek aadmi ki neeyat ki nahi thi. Saamne ek sarkari dastavez tha, jaan-boojhkar gol-mol. Aur kaha gaya: lo to poora lo, chhodo to poora chhodo.

Congress ne kaha ki bhavishya ki baat chhod do. Abhi sankat hai, abhi ki baat karo. Ek rashtriya sarkar bane, jo poori taaqat se desh ki raksha kare. Ahinsa ka sawal kahin nahi tha. Ulta, Congress ek Hindustani Defence Minister maang rahi thi.

Behes ke baad Cripps maan gaye ki ek Defence vibhag kisi Hindustani ke haath mein ho sakta hai. Par us vibhag ke zimme kya hota? Public relations. Petrol. Canteen. Stationery aur chhapai. Videshi mehmanon ki khaatirdaari. Sipahiyon ki suvidhayein. Nehru kehte hain ki is list ne Hindustani Defence Member ko mazaak bana diya.

Phir bhi baat aage badhi. Dono paksh thoda paas aate dikhe. Nehru ko pehli baar laga ki samjhauta ho sakta hai.

Aur theek usi waqt ajeeb cheezein hone lagi. America mein Lord Halifax ne Congress par zordar hamla bola. Delhi mein sabko pata tha ki Viceroy Lord Linlithgow aur bade afsar kisi samjhaute ke khilaaf hain, kyunki isse unki taaqat ghatti.

Congress chahti thi ki Hindustani fauj ko rashtriya fauj jaisa mehsoos ho. Ki jaldi se militia aur home guard banaye jaayein, Commander-in-Chief ke neeche hi. Jawab mila: nahi. Hindustani fauj British fauj ka hissa hai. Use rashtriya fauj kaha bhi nahi ja sakta.

To nateeja ye nikla. Sarkar ka dhaancha wahi rahega. Viceroy ki taaqat wahi rahegi. Aur kuch Hindustani unke vardi-dhaari naukar bankar canteen sambhalenge. Nehru kehte hain ki ye Amery ki atthaarah mahine purani peshkash se zara bhi alag nahi tha.

Ise maanna namumkin tha. Waise bhi, baad mein jab logon ko pata chala ki baat-cheet mein Congress ne kitni riyayatein di thi, to desh mein hungama hua.

Do baatein Nehru zor dekar kehte hain. Pehli, poori baat-cheet mein Hindu-Muslim ya alpsankhyak ka sawal ek baar bhi nahi utha. Sawal sirf ye tha ki asli taaqat rashtriya sarkar ko milegi ya nahi. Doosri, ye kehna jhooth hai ki Gandhiji ki zid se baat tooti. Wo apni patni ki beemari ki wajah se pehle hi chale gaye the. Aakhri daur ki baat-cheet unke bina hui.

Cripps hawai jahaaz se England laut gaye. Jaane se pehle aur lautkar unhone kuch aise bayaan diye jo Nehru ke hisaab se tathyon ke khilaaf the. Bharat mein inka bahut bura maana gaya.

Prastaav sirf Congress ne nahi, desh ke har dal ne thukraya. Sabse narm netaon ne bhi. Muslim League ne apni aadat ke mutabik pehle sabki sunne ka intezaar kiya, phir apne kaaranon se mana kiya.

Nehru ka nichod ye hai. Jung samjhaute mein rukawat nahi thi. Jung to visheshagya ladte. Asli sawal wahi purana tha: rashtravaad banaam samrajyawad. Aur us sawal par angrez shasak varg tas se mas nahi hona chahta tha. Unke peeche Churchill khade the.

Part 9: Hatasha

Cripps ka achanak chale jaana sabko chaunka gaya. Kya War Cabinet ka ek mantri itni door bas yahi kamzor peshkash dohraane aaya tha? Ya ye sab America ke logon ko dikhane ke liye tha? Desh mein gussa aur kadwahat phail gayi.

Phir bhi chup baithna mumkin nahi tha. Hamla kabhi bhi ho sakta tha. To Congress ne aam logon ko salah di. Angrez ya mitra fauj ke kaam mein rukawat mat daalo, warna isse dushman ko madad milegi. Par hamlavar ke aage kabhi mat jhuko. Uska hukm mat maano. Usse koi ehsaan mat lo. Agar wo tumhare ghar aur khet chheene, to jaan dekar bhi virodh karo. Shaanti se, poore asahyog ke saath.

Nehru imaandari se maante hain ki shaant asahyog kisi aage badhti fauj ko rok nahi sakta. Zyadatar aam log virodh kar bhi nahi paate. Par umeed thi ki kabze wale gaon aur shahar mein kuch log zaroor ade rahenge. Unhe saza milti, shayad maut. Par unki misaal se desh mein muqable ki bhaavna jaagti.

Ye yojanayein bani, thoda kaam bhi hua. Par Nehru ko lagta tha ki hum bas satah khurach rahe hain. Asli hal tabhi tha jab sarkar aur janta saath kaam karein. Aur wo namumkin ho chuka tha.

Ye dil todne wali haalat thi. Sankat pukaar raha tha. Kuch karne ki bechaini thi. Par haath bandhe the. Tabahi tezi se paas aa rahi thi, aur Bharat bebas, naraaz aur khamosh pada tha, videshi fauj ka maidan bankar.

Jung aayi thi, par koi josh nahi laayi. Wo josh jisme dard aur maut bhool jaate hain, aur bas azadi ka sapna dikhta hai. Hisse mein aaya sirf dukh, aur aane wali aafat ka ehsaas, jise rokne mein bhi koi haath nahi bata sakte the.

Nehru saaf karte hain ki ye haar-jeet ka sawal nahi tha. Wo nahi chahte the ki Axis desh jeetein. Wo nahi chahte the ki Japan Bharat mein ek inch bhi ghuse. Par sawal ye tha ki is jung ka sakaaratmak maqsad kya hai? Kya ye bas puraani galtiyon ka dohraav hai?

Yahan Nehru ko Rabindranath Tagore ka aakhri sandesh yaad aata hai, jo unhone maut se pehle, ek saal pehle diya tha. Nehru uska ansh dete hain. Aur khud ko samjhate hain: insaan par se vishwas nahi khoya ja sakta. Ishwar ko koi nakaar de, par insaan ko nakaar diya to bacha kya? Phir bhi ye maanna mushkil tha ki sach ki jeet tay hai.

Thake hue sharir aur bechain man ke saath Nehru kuch din ke liye Himalaya ki ek andar ki ghaati mein chale gaye. Kulu.

Part 10: Bharat chhodo

Kulu se lautkar ek sawal Nehru ko pareshaan karta raha. China ke log itne saal itni himmat se kyun lade? Aur Soviet Union ke log itne poore man se kyun lad rahe the?

Unka andaaza tha ki fark bhavishya par bharose ka tha. Doosre desh bhi bahaduri se lade, jaise Dunkirk ke waqt England. Par sankat talte hi kuch dheelapan aa jaata, jaise kal ke baare mein shak ho. Soviet Union mein, jitna bahar se dikhta tha, aisa koi shak nahi tha. Nehru ye bhi jodte hain ki wahan behes ko badhava diya bhi nahi jaata tha.

Bharat mein ulta tha. Na aaj par bharosa, na kal par. Bas ek bhaari, khamosh hatasha.

Isi waqt Gandhiji ne kuch lekh likhe. Unhone logon ki dhundhli soch ko ek shakl de di. Gandhiji ke liye ab chup baithna asahya tha. Unka kehna tha: Bharat ki azadi maano, aur azaad Bharat mitra deshon ke saath milkar hamle ka saamna karega. Agar ye nahi hota, to kuch karna hoga. Logon ko us neend se jagana hoga, jo unhe har hamlavar ka aasaan shikaar bana rahi hai.

Nehru ko ye tark jacha. Jo log aaj angrez ke aage sir jhukaate hain, wo kal Japan ke aage bhi jhuka denge. Agar aaj ke manmaane hukm na maanne ki aadat bane, to wahi kal hamlavar ke khilaaf himmat banegi.

Gandhi aur Nehru ke beech ke kai purane matbhed mit gaye. Par ek mushkil bachi rahi. Koi bhi andolan jung ke kaam mein rukawat banega. Aur hairat ki baat ye ki Gandhiji ab bhi maante the ki angrezon se samjhauta ho sakta hai. Wo kehte the ki main poori koshish karunga. Isliye wo "action" ki baat to bahut karte, par ye nahi batate ki karenge kya.

Udhar sarkar ka mizaaj bhi garm ho raha tha. Nehru kehte hain ki iske liye use kisi josh ki zaroorat nahi thi. Ye uska swabhav tha, kisi ghulam desh par qabza kiye baithi videshi hukumat ka swabhav. Use ye mauqa achha laga, ek baar mein har virodhi ko kuchal dene ka. Aur usne taiyari kar li.

Phir Bombay mein All India Congress Committee baithi. Prastaav mein kaha gaya ki Bharat ki azadi poore Asia ki azadi ki shuruaat hogi. Azaad deshon ka ek vishwa-sangh bane, aur uski neenv United Nations ke saath rakhi jaye. Committee ne saaf likha ki wo China aur Russia ki raksha mein koi rukawat nahi daalna chahti. Us waqt sabse bada khatra unhi do deshon ko tha.

Aur phir prastaav ka dank. Committee ne kaha ki ab wo desh ko rok kar nahi rakh sakti. Isliye Bharat ke azadi ke haq ke liye ek ahinsak jan-sangharsh ki manzoori di jaati hai, Gandhiji ki aguai mein. Par ye manzoori tabhi lagu hogi, jab Gandhiji tay karenge. Aur aakhri baat: Congress ko apne liye taaqat nahi chahiye. Taaqat aayegi, to Bharat ki poori janta ki hogi.

Aakhri bhashanon mein Maulana Azad aur Gandhiji ne agla qadam bhi bata diya. Pehle Viceroy se baat. Phir United Nations ke bade netaon se apeel, ek izzatdaar samjhaute ke liye. Aisa samjhauta jo Bharat ko azadi de, aur Axis ke khilaaf ladai ko bhi mazboot kare.

Wo agla qadam kabhi uthaya hi nahi ja saka.

8 August 1942 ki der shaam prastaav paas hua. Kuch hi ghante baad, 9 August ki tadke, Bombay aur poore desh mein giraftariyan shuru ho gayi.

Aur yahin se raasta Ahmadnagar ke qile ki taraf mudta hai. Wahi qila, jahan Nehru phawda chhodkar kalam uthayenge, aur ye kitaab likhenge.

Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, Cripps prastaav ka wo "sooba alag ho sakta hai" wala hissa. Nehru ne ise anginat batwaaron ka darwaza kaha tha. Paanch saal baad, August 1947 mein, desh sach mein bata. Aur riyasaton ka sawal bhi aazadi ke baad saalon tak uljha raha.

Doosri, Defence Member ki wo list. Canteen, stationery, petrol. Ye ek chhoti si list thi, par isne ek badi baat khol di. Sawal hamesha ye hota hai ki faisla kaun karega, kursi kis naam ki hai, ye nahi.

Teesri, Nehru ki imaandari. Wo ye nahi chhupate ki unke andar khud ladai thi. Ek taraf fascism se nafrat, doosri taraf angrezon se gussa. Wo maante hain ki ye ulajhan thi. Aaj kitne neta khulkar maante hain ki unki apni niti mein virodhabhas hai?

Aapke liye ek sawal: Gandhi aur Nehru, dono maante the ki jo aaj ek maalik ke aage jhukega, wo kal doosre ke aage bhi jhukega. Kya aapko ye baat aaj bhi sach lagti hai?

Outro

Agle episode mein hum wapas Ahmadnagar ke qile mein honge. 9 August ke baad desh mein kya hua. Jan-andolan, goliyan, giraftariyan. Duniya ne kya kaha. Bengal ka akaal phir se, kareeb se. Aur Nehru ka wo bada sawal: azaad Bharat kaisa ho, ek majboot rashtra, ya tukdon mein bata hua?

Tab tak ke liye, namaskar.
