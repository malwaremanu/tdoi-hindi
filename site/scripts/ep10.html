Cold open

Calcutta. Saal 1943.

Subah footpath par ek aurat baithi hai. Uski godi mein bachcha hai, jo ab hilta nahi. Thodi door ek aadmi pada hai. Log uske paas se guzar jaate hain. Kal raat yahan aur bhi log the. Ab wahan sirf unke kapde hain.

Usi shehar mein, kuch kilometer door, race course par bheed lagi hai. Topiyan, chhatriyan, champagne. Ghode daud rahe hain.

Ye ghode doosre shehron se aaye hain. Train se. Khaas dibbon mein. Usi waqt, jab gaon tak anaaj pahunchane ke liye gaadi nahi mil rahi.

Raat ko clubon mein naach hota hai. Daawatein hoti hain. Rashan card abhi tak shuru nahi hue. Is mehfil mein angrez bhi hain aur Hindustani bhi. Jung ne dono ki jeb bhari hai. Kuch ne to paisa usi anaaj ki jamaakhori se banaya hai, jiski kami se log sadak par mar rahe hain.

Ye sab Nehru apni aankhon se nahi dekh rahe. Wo ek qile mein band hain, akhbaar padh rahe hain, aur likh rahe hain.

Namaskar, main hoon Asha, aur ye hai "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein". Aaj Episode 10, aakhri episode: Phir Ahmadnagar ka qila.

Part 1: Do saal ka sapna

Hum wahin laut aaye hain jahan se shuru kiya tha. Ahmadnagar ka qila. Par ab August 1944 hai. Nehru ko yahan aaye do saal se thoda zyada ho gaya hai.

Do saal. Wahi gine-chune chehre. Wahi deewarein. Har din ek jaisa. Nehru ise ek lamba sapna kehte hain, jo ek hi jagah jad pakad chuka hai.

Wo sochte hain ki jab bahar niklenge to duniya badli hui milegi. Log pehchaane lagenge, par ajnabi bhi. Aur wo khud bhi wahi aadmi nahi rahenge. Phir ek ajeeb sawal: kahin bahar ki wo roz ki zindagi hi sapna to nahi? Asli kaun sa hai, jaagna ya sona?

Pichhle chaar mahine wo likhte rahe hain. Bharat ke ateet par. Ab peeche mudkar dekhte hain to likha hua unhe adhoora lagta hai. Bikhra hua. Aur jahan wo nishpaksh rehna chahte the, wahan bhi unka apna man ghus aaya. Wo maante hain ki kai baar unhone use roka. Kai baar lagaam dheeli chhod di.

Ateet ke baare mein likhkar unhe laga tha ki uska bojh halka hoga. Hua bhi. Par aaj ka bojh, aur aane wale kal ka andhera, wo waise ke waise hain. Wo un logon se thodi irshya karte hain jinke man mein kabhi shak ne ghar nahi kiya. Unki zindagi kitni seedhi hoti hai.

Phir ek baat jo poore chapter ki chaabi hai. Koi bhi ghatna akeli nahi hoti. Hum use alag karke dekhte hain, jaise wahi shuruaat ho aur wahi ant. Par wo ek lambi zanjeer ki kadi hai. Usme anginat logon ki ichhayein milti hain, takraati hain, aur nateeja wo nikalta hai jo kisi ek ne nahi chaha tha. Bada neta bhi isi zanjeer ki paidaish hai. Wo bheed ko modta hai, par bheed ka itihaas use bhi modta hai.

Isi nazar se Nehru August 1942 ko dekhte hain.

Part 2: Ab aur nahi

9 August 1942 ko Gandhi, Nehru aur Congress ke baaki neta pakde gaye. Uske baad desh mein jo hua, us par bahut likha gaya. Kisi ne gaali di, kisi ne safaai.

Nehru kehte hain ki zyadatar log asli baat chook gaye. Wo ise rajneeti ke taraazu par tol rahe the. Jabki ye rajneeti se gehri cheez thi. Logon ke andar ek hi bhaav tha: videshi raaj ab bardaasht nahi. Baaki sab sawal chhote pad gaye. Is raaj mein sudhaar ho sakta hai ya nahi, takraane se nuksaan zyada hoga ya kam, kisi ko parwah nahi thi.

Ye bhaav naya nahi tha. Barson se tha. Bas pehle uspar lagaam thi. Aur jung ne dono kaam kiye. Usne sapne bade kiye, ki azaadi ab door nahi. Aur usne haath bhi baandhe, kyunki Bharat fascism ke khilaaf ladai mein rukawat nahi banna chahta tha.

Par Mitra deshon ke neta peeche dekh rahe the, aage nahi. Churchill ke liye ye jung bas purani duniya ko wapas jamaane ki ladai thi. England ka samaj waisa hi rahe, samrajya waisa hi rahe. Roosevelt ki baatein badi thi, par niti mein zyada fark nahi tha. Phir bhi duniya bhar mein log unki taraf umeed se dekhte the.

Bharat mein ye bhaav kitna faila tha? Ginti mumkin nahi. Karodon log gareebi mein itne pise the ki unke paas sochne ki taaqat hi nahi bachi thi. Kuch log naukri ya fayde ke lalach mein bik chuke the. Par jahan bhi bhaav tha, uske kai rang the. Kuch log jaan par khelne ko taiyar. Kuch door se sirf hamdardi rakhne wale. Kuch ka dum ghut raha tha. Kuch ne halaat ke saath jeena seekh liya tha.

Part 3: Bina hathiyaar ki baghawat

Neta jail mein the. Logon ko koi yojana nahi mili thi. Congress ka aakhri sandesh, jo Nehru ko yaad hai, ahinsa par hi zor deta tha.

Par ek baat ka asar shayad hua. Congress ne kaha tha ki videshi hamlawar ke khilaaf hathiyaar uthana jaayaz hai. To log sochne lage: jo hamla pehle se ghar mein baitha hai, uske khilaaf kyun nahi? Aam aadmi ke liye ye baareek fark samajhna mushkil tha. Aur kuch log aise bhi the jinka ahinsa mein kabhi yakeen tha hi nahi.

Josh ke pal mein log sochte nahi. Barson ka daba hua gussa unhe aage dhakelta hai. Aur 1857 ke baad pehli baar itni badi taadaad mein log angrezi raaj ke saamne khade hue. Taaqat ke saath. Par aisi taaqat jiske paas hathiyaar nahi the.

Nehru saaf kehte hain ki ye bewaqoofi bhari aur galat waqt ki chunauti thi. Fauj aur bandookein doosri taraf thi, itihaas mein pehle se kahin zyada. Bheed kitni bhi badi ho, fauj se nahi jeet sakti, jab tak fauj khud paala na badal le. Par bheed ne ye ladai chuni nahi thi. Wo un par aa padi thi. Aur unhone jo kiya, us mein azaadi ka pyaar aur ghulami se nafrat saaf dikhi.

Ahmedabad mein kapda mill ke mazdooron ne hadtaal ki. Teen mahine se zyada chali. Shaant rahi. Bahar se ek paisa nahi mila. Aur ye wo waqt tha jab tankhwah achhi thi, yaani nuksaan unka apna tha. Doosre shehron mein hadtaalein kuch din chali. Cawnpore mein badi hadtaal nahi hui, kyunki wahan ke communist netaon ne use rok diya.

Ab daman. Sarkari aankde kehte hain ki police aur fauj ki goli se 1,028 log mare aur 3,200 ghayal hue. Nehru in aankdon ko bahut kam maante hain. Khud sarkar ne maana ki kam se kam 538 baar goli chali. Iske alawa chalti lorryon se bhi goli chalayi jaati thi. Logon mein charcha thi ki pacchees hazaar mare. Nehru ise badha-chadha maante hain. Unka andaaza das hazaar ke aas-paas hai.

Sabse zyada maar padi gaon ke gareeb kisaanon par. Peedhiyon se dukh jhelne wale log, jinhone pehli baar sar uthakar behtar kal ka sapna dekha tha. Kai jagah poore gaon ko kode lagane ki saza mili.

Bengal ke Midnapore ilaake mein, Tamluk aur Contai, 1942 mein ek bhayanak toofan aaya tha. Sab ujad gaya tha. Phir bhi sarkari fauj ne wahan Congress ke 193 camp aur ghar jala diye. Ye baat khud Bengal sarkar ki taraf se kahi gayi.

Aur phir jurmana. Poore-poore gaon par samoohik jurmana laga. Bharat Sachiv Amery ne British sansad mein bataya ki kul nabbe lakh rupaye ka jurmana laga, aur usme se saadhe athattar lakh vasool bhi ho gaye. Bhookhe logon se ye paisa kaise nikala gaya, ye alag kahani hai. Nehru kehte hain ki goli aur aag se bhi zyada dard is vasooli ne diya. Aur aksar jitna jurmana tha, usse zyada vasoola gaya. Beech ka paisa raaste mein gayab ho gaya.

Nateeja? Angrez jeet gaye. Kam se kam us waqt. Nehru maante hain ki Bharat us aakhri imtihaan mein haara, jahan sirf taaqat ginti hai. Sirf angrezi bandook ki wajah se nahi. Isliye bhi ki bahut se Bharatiya wo aakhri qurbani dene ko taiyar nahi the jo azaadi maangti hai.

Part 4: Bahar kya dikha, andar kya hua

Duniya ko ye sab dikha hi kitna? Censor ne mota parda daal diya tha. Bharat ke akhbaar bhi bahut kuch chhaap nahi sakte the. Bahar jaane wali khabron par aur sakht nazar thi. Upar se sarkari prachaar chhoda gaya. Sabse zyada America mein, kyunki wahan ki raay ka wazan tha. Saikdon lecturer bheje gaye, angrez bhi, Hindustani bhi.

America ki raay bati hui thi. Wahan log doosron ke samrajya ko pasand nahi karte the. Wo Japan ke khilaaf Bharat ki madad bhi chahte the. Par lagataar ek-tarfa prachaar ka asar hua. Log sochne lage ki Bharat ka maamla bahut uljha hua hai, aur apne dost Britain ke ghar ke maamle mein kya bolein.

Russia ki raay ka kisi ko pata nahi tha. Wo apni zameen se dushman ko nikaalne mein jute the. Par wo door ki sochte hain, aur Bharat unki Asian seema se sata hai. Nehru bas itna zikr karte hain ki November 1942 mein, Soviet kranti ki pacchisvi saalgirah par, Stalin ne apni niti ke baare mein kya kaha tha.

China ki hamdardi poori tarah Bharat ki azaadi ke saath thi. Kuch purane rishton ki wajah se. Par zyada isliye ki China samajhta tha: Bharat ghulam raha to China ki azaadi bhi khatre mein hai. Aur sirf China nahi. Poore Asia, Misr aur Middle East mein Bharat ki azaadi ek kasauti ban gayi thi. Yahan Nehru American neta Wendell Willkie ki kitaab "One World" ka zikr karte hain.

Ab desh ke andar. Nehru ek kadwi baat kehte hain. Videshi raaj ko hamesha samaj ke kamzor tabke par tikna padta hai. Jo log aadarshwadi hain, khuddaar hain, wo ya to door rehte hain ya takraate hain. Bachte hain mauqaparast. Aur jab sarkar aur janta ke beech khuli ladai ho, to upar wahi pahunchte hain jo apne hi logon ko kuchalne mein sabse aage hon.

Aise mahaul mein ghoos, berehmi aur laaparwahi phalti hai. Aam Hindustani ko sabse zyada ghin un Hindustaniyon se aati hai jo raja se bhi zyada rajbhakt ban jaate hain. Log unki tulna France ki Vichy sarkar se karte hain, ya Germany aur Japan ki kathputli sarkaaron se. Aur ye bhaav sirf Congress walon ka nahi. Muslim League ke log aur naram-dal ke neta bhi yahi kehte hain.

Jung ki aad mein naye tareeqe nikle. Paisa dekar naqli mazdoor sangathan banaye gaye. Kaagaz ki kami thi, phir bhi Gandhi aur Congress ko gaali dene wale akhbaar chalaye gaye. Videsh mein prachaar daftar khule. Anjaan logon ke dal America bheje gaye. Aur jo sarkar se asahmat tha, use na passport mila, na jahaaz mein seat.

Upar se sab shaant dikhta tha. Par Nehru ise ek jwalamukhi kehte hain, jiska munh kuch der ke liye band kar diya gaya hai. Sarkar ke purane dost bhi keh rahe the ki angrezon ke khilaaf aisi kadwahat unhone pehle kabhi nahi dekhi.

Part 5: Bimaar Bharat

Bharat bimaar tha. Tan se bhi, man se bhi. Aur is bimari ka sabse bhayanak lakshan tha akaal. Bengal, poorvi aur dakshini Bharat.

Nehru ke hisaab se ye angrezi raaj ke 170 saal ka sabse bada akaal tha. Iski tulna sirf 1766 se 1770 ke us akaal se ho sakti hai, jo Bengal aur Bihar mein angrezi raaj ki shuruaat mein pada tha. Peechhe-peechhe haiza aur malaria aaye, aur doosre soobon tak faile. Laakhon log mare.

Ye kudrat ka kehar nahi tha. Na dushman ki naakebandi thi. Har samajhdar observer maanta hai ki ye insaan ka banaya hua akaal tha. Ise pehle se dekha ja sakta tha, aur roka ja sakta tha. Aakhri ghadi tak, jab sadkon par roz hazaaron mar rahe the, sarkar maanti hi nahi thi ki akaal hai. Akhbaaron mein iska zikr censor kaat deta tha.

Ek tareekh dekhiye. Europe mein jung September 1939 mein shuru hui. Bharat sarkar ne khaane ka alag vibhaag sawa teen saal baad banaya. Japan ki jung shuru hone ke bhi ek saal baad. Sabko pata tha ki Burma par Japan ka kabza Bengal ki rasoi par chot karega. Phir bhi 1943 ke beech tak koi food policy thi hi nahi.

Nehru ka vyang yahan tez hai. Ye sarkar har kaam mein nikammi hai, siwaay ek ke: apne virodhiyon ko kuchalna. Uska dimaag bas isi mein laga hai ki wo khud kaise tiki rahe.

Aur jung ke hisaab se bhi socho. Akaal theek usi ilaake mein pada jo jung ke morche ke sabse kareeb tha. Nehru kehte hain ki sarkar ne "scorched earth" ki baat bahut ki thi, par yahan jo mila wo jhulse aur bhookhe insaan the.

Par jad aur gehri thi. Nehru 1933 ki ek sarkari report ka hawala dete hain. Indian Medical Service ke mukhiya Major General Megaw ne dispensary doctoron se poochha tha ki log kitna khaate hain. Poore Bharat mein sirf 39 pratishat log theek se poshit maane gaye. 41 pratishat kam poshit. 20 pratishat bahut bure haal mein. Bengal mein to sirf 22 pratishat theek the, aur 31 pratishat bahut bure haal mein. Yaani akaal se das saal pehle bhi Bengal bhookha tha.

Rahat ka kaam hua. Desh bhar ki gair-sarkari sansthaon ne kiya, England ke Quakers ne kiya. Aakhir mein sarkar jaagi, aur fauj ko lagaya. Is sankat ne logon ki achhai bhi dikhayi aur kamzori bhi. Har tabke ke log sewa mein jute. Par kuch aapas ki jalan mein uljhe rahe, aur kuch ko koi fark hi nahi pada.

Nehru kehte hain ki Bengal toot chuka hai. Ek kamzor peedhi bachi hai. Aur yahan wo Rabindranath Tagore ki ek baat yaad karte hain, jo Tagore ne marne se pehle kahi thi: angrez jab jaayenge, to peeche kya chhodkar jaayenge?

Part 6: Zinda nadi, ruka hua paani

Itne dukh ke baad bhi zindagi chalti rehti hai. Kal ke maidan-e-jung par aaj ghaas ugti hai. Nehru ko Bharat mein ek ajeeb tasveer dikhti hai, jisme har cheez ka ulta bhi saath khada hai.

Baatein azaadi ki ho rahi hain, aur desh par shayad itihaas ka sabse sakht raaj hai. Ek taraf akaal, doosri taraf kuch haathon mein jamta hua paisa. Gareebi aur ameeri saath-saath. Purani soch aur nayi soch saath-saath. Aur in sab ke neeche ek zinda taaqat, jo dabaye nahi dabti.

Jung mein Bharat ki factoriyan zyada chali. Par Nehru poochte hain: kya naye udyog bane, ya purane hi khinche? Economist John Mathai, jo Tata ke director bhi the, maante the ki jung aur angrezi niti ne udyog ko aage badhne ke bajaye roka hai.

Phir ek baat jo Nehru ko chubhti hai. Paanch saal mein Bharat ne bahut bada paisa jama kiya. Par wo paisa sterling ke roop mein London mein pada hai. Bharat use chhoo nahi sakta. Ye wo kharcha hai jo Bharat sarkar ne Britain aur America ki taraf se kiya. Nehru kehte hain ki is paise ke peeche bhookh hai, akaal hai, bimari hai, aur laakhon maut hain.

Angrezon ki ek aadat bhi unhe khatakti hai. Wo apne kaam ki tulna sau-do sau saal purani duniya se karte hain. Bhool jaate hain ki pichhle pachaas saal mein technology ne raftaar kitni badal di. Aur ye bhi ki jab wo aaye, Bharat jungli desh nahi tha. Ek viksit sabhyata thi, jo kuch der ke liye ruk gayi thi.

Japan ne Manchuria mein aath saal mein itna udyog khada kiya ki wahan Bharat se zyada koyla nikalne laga. Par uske peeche ghulami thi, zulm tha. Ab angrez kehte hain, dekho, humne Japan aur Germany jaisa zulm to nahi kiya. Nehru poochte hain: kya ab yahi kasauti bachi hai?

Phir wo apni sabhyata par bhi ungli uthate hain. Bharat ne bahut kuch paaya, par paate-paate wo jam gaya. Jo usool kabhi zinda khoj the, wo maani hui baatein ban gaye. Aur jab khoj band hoti hai, to sach, sundarta aur azaadi bhi murjha jaate hain.

Paschim mein ulta hai. Wahan raftaar hai, bhookh hai, haq ki baat hai, farz ki kam. Wo zinda hai, par us zindagi ko bukhaar chadha hai, jo badhta hi jaata hai. Aur har kuch saal mein wo khud ko tabah karta hai. Nehru ka nuskha saaf hai: Bharat aur China ko paschim se seekhna hoga. Par paschim ko bhi zindagi ke kuch gehre sabak seekhne honge.

Aur Bharat ki asli sachchai? Angrezi fauji kabza. Ek fauji Viceroy ke daur mein ye baat ab chhupayi bhi nahi jaati. Par Nehru yaad dilate hain ki taaqat ki ek seema hoti hai. Wo apne khilaaf naye virodhi paida karti hai.

Part 7: Dharm, darshan aur vigyaan

Ab Nehru ek kadam peeche hatkar sochte hain. Sach kya hai?

Agar koi aakhri sach hai, to wo hamesha ke liye hoga. Par insaan ka dimaag chhota hai. Wo uska ek tukda hi pakad paata hai, apne yug aur apni soch ke hisaab se. Isliye sach ko baar-baar dhoondhna padta hai, naye shabdon mein kehna padta hai. Tabhi wo zinda rehta hai.

Darshan ne sawal poochna sikhaya. Par wo aksar apne haathi-daant ke mehal mein raha, roz ki zindagi se kata hua.

Dharm ka tareeqa alag hai. Wo bhaavna aur antargyaan par chalta hai. Dikkat tab hoti hai jab wo yahi tareeqa un cheezon par bhi lagata hai jinhe parkha ja sakta hai. Sangathit dharm, Nehru kehte hain, aksar aatma se zyada apni jaaydaad ki fikr karta hai. Wo man ko tang karta hai, andhvishwas badhata hai, aur insaan ko doosron par nirbhar banata hai.

Vigyaan ka koi kinara nahi dikhta. Par Nehru maante hain ki har anubhav uski pakad mein nahi aata. Kala, kavita, sundarta ka ehsaas, achhai ki pehchaan. Ek botany ka vidwan phoolon ki khoobsurti se anjaan reh sakta hai. Yahan Nehru French vichaarak Pascal ka zikr karte hain, jinke hisaab se tark ka aakhri kaam ye maanna hai ki bahut kuch uske paar hai.

Isliye Nehru ka zor vigyaan ke tathyon par nahi, vigyaan ke mizaaj par hai. Khula dimaag. Saboot maangna. Galti maanne ki taiyaari. Ye mizaaj wo pahaadon ki choti par bhi saath le jaana chahte hain, jahan darshan rehta hai.

Aur ek umeed ki baat. Paschim mein vigyaan ka raaj hai, par vigyaan ka asli mizaaj wahan bhi poora nahi aaya. Bharat ko lamba safar karna hai. Par Nehru maante hain ki Bharat ki purani soch ki jad, baad ki rasmon ko chhodkar, isi mizaaj se mel khaati hai. Nidar hokar sach ki khoj. Insaanon ki ekta. Har jeev mein divyata.

Part 8: Rashtra ka khayal

Nehru ka maanna hai ki rashtra ka bhaav bahut gehra hota hai. Jo use nazarandaaz kare, wo logon tak nahi pahunch sakta.

Iska udaharan wo Bharat ki Communist Party ko dete hain. Unke hisaab se party desh ki parampara se poori tarah kati hui hai. Uske liye duniya ka itihaas November 1917 mein shuru hua. Bharat jaise bhookhe desh mein communism ki pukaar honi chahiye thi. Par party aisi bhasha bolti hai jo logon ke dil tak nahi pahunchti. Josh hai, jad nahi.

Doosra udaharan aur bhi tez hai. Bade shehron ke wo log jo "modern" hone ka dikhava karte hain. Paschim ke kapde pehente hain, paschim ki buraaiyan apnate hain, aur apni sanskriti ka kuch pata nahi. Na wo poorab ke hain, na paschim ke.

Phir Nehru Bharat, China aur Europe ki tulna karte hain. Bharat mein soch ki gazab ki azaadi thi, par samaaj ke dhaanche sakht the. Dheere-dheere un sakht dhaanchon ne soch ko bhi jakad liya. China ka dimaag zyada lachila raha. Wahan dharm ki kattarta kam thi, naitikta aur samajh zyada.

Kuch log purane yug mein laut jaana chahte hain. Koi Vedon ke zamane mein, koi Islami raaj mein. Nehru ise khayali pulao kehte hain. Waqt ki sadak ek-tarfa hai. Peeche koi nahi ja sakta.

To kya badalna hoga? Nehru kehte hain ki yug barabari maangta hai. Purani ghulami gayi, jahan ek aadmi doosre ka maal hota tha. Par ek nayi ghulami aa gayi, jahan insaan ko saamaan ki tarah bechte-khareedte hain. Aur poore desh abhi bhi doosre desh ki jaaydaad hain.

Bharat ki mushkilon ki jad ye hai ki hum purana dhaancha bachaakar aage badhna chahte hain. Ye mumkin nahi. Munaafe par tika samaj badalna hoga. Nehru yaad dilate hain ki Bharat mein paise wale se log jalte to hain, par izzat use dete hain jo tyaag kare.

Unka sapna ek aisa loktantra hai jisme gaon ki apni sarkar ho, aur sahkari sansthaon ki bhi awaaz ho. Unhe lagta hai ki aisa Bharat padosiyon se ladega nahi. Wo duniya ke liye shaanti ki taaqat banega.

Part 9: Batwara ya ekta

Ab sabse naazuk sawal. Bharat ek rahega ya batega?

Nehru pehle khud ko tokte hain. Insaan ki ichha uski soch ko rang deti hai. Wo santulan dhoondhne ki koshish karte hain, aur maante hain ki shayad kaamyaab nahi ho paate.

Phir wo karm ki baat karte hain. Jo hum karte hain, wahi hamara kal banata hai. Insaan ka bhi, desh ka bhi. Angrez aur Bharat ke beech do sau saal ka zabardasti ka rishta hai. Usne dono ka karm likh diya hai. Angrez ko usme achhai zyada dikhti hai, Hindustani ko buraai. Par Nehru kehte hain ki zabardasti ka rishta kaisa bhi ho, usse nafrat hi paida hoti hai.

Batwara hua to kya hoga? Desh kamzor hoga, ye to seedhi baat hai. Par usse bura hoga man ka takraav. Ek taraf jodne wale, doosri taraf todne wale. Naye swaarth paida honge jo badlaav rokenge. Ek galat kadam doosre galat kadam ki taraf le jaata hai.

Par Nehru imaandaari se maante hain ki zabardasti ki ekta bhi dhokha hai. Barood ka dher. Ekta dil ki honi chahiye. Unhe yakeen hai ki Bharat mein wo gehri ekta hai, bas kuch der ke liye upar dhool jam gayi hai.

Galti hamari bhi hai, wo maante hain. Par angrez hukumat ne jaan-boojhkar jo phoot daali, use wo maaf nahi kar paate. Yahan unhe Ireland aur China yaad aate hain.

Yahan Nehru Irish lekhak Jim Phelan ki ek kitaab ka zikr karte hain, jo jail ke baare mein hai. Baat ye hai ki jail insaan ki har chhoti kamzori ko bada kar deti hai. Nehru kehte hain ki videshi raaj bhi desh ke saath yahi karta hai. Jail mein qaidi jasoos hote hain. Ghulam desh mein chaaplus.

Ek hisaab bhi wo saamne rakhte hain. Bengal aur Punjab mein Musalman lagbhag 54 pratishat hain. Agar sirf unhe vote ka haq mile, to wo baaki 46 pratishat ki kismat tay karenge. Kuch soorton mein, Nehru ke hisaab se, 28 pratishat log 72 pratishat ka faisla kar sakte hain. Aur jab bantwaare ki kasauti dharm ho, to koi tarkik hal nikalta hi nahi. Batwara hua to riyasatein bhi alag rehne ka bahana dhoondh lengi.

Nehru ka hal ye hai: kuch zaroori maamle poore Bharat ke saath. Baaki mein ikaiyon ko poori azaadi. Beech mein ek saajha daayra. Bas koi zabardasti na ho, aur kisi ek aadmi ka vote doosre ke do-teen vote ke barabar na ho.

Aur bada naqsha? H.G. Wells kehte the ki chhote-chhote rashtron ka daur khatam ho raha hai. G.D.H. Cole sochte the ki Bharat ek bade, desh se upar wale ilaake ka kendra banega. Nehru Indian Ocean ke aas-paas ke deshon ke ek kshetriya samooh ki baat karte hain: Iran, Iraq, Afghanistan, Ceylon, Burma, Malaya, Siam, Java. Aise samooh mein aaj ke alpsankhyak sawal alag roop le lenge. Unka saaf kehna hai: chhote rashtra ka zamana gaya.

Part 10: Do mahashaktiyaan aur samrajya

August 1944 mein Paris azaad ho chuka hai. Germany peeche hat raha hai. Ab sawal shaanti ka hai, jo jung se bhi mushkil hai. Sab keh rahe hain: ab dobara nahi. Nehru yaad dilate hain ki 1918 mein bhi yahi kaha gaya tha.

Wo dekhte hain ki jaise-jaise jeet paas aayi, Mitra deshon ke neta aur purane-khayaal hote gaye. Atlantic Charter aur chaar azaadiyan peeche chali gayi. Ladai ab fascism ki soch ke khilaaf nahi, sirf fauj ke khilaaf fauj reh gayi. Spain mein Franco ko badhawa mila. Yahan Nehru George Bernard Shaw ki ek chutki ka zikr karte hain, Churchill aur "empire" shabd ke baare mein.

Phir wo "geopolitics" ko pakadte hain. American professor Spykman ki baat, ki America ko gher liye jaane ka khatra hai, isliye wo kinaare ke deshon se dosti kare. Nehru ko ye chaalaaki bhi lagti hai aur moorkhata bhi. Unka tark seedha hai. Zameen gol hai. Har desh kisi na kisi se ghira hai. Is dar ka ek hi ilaaj power politics ke paas hai: poori duniya jeet lo. Aur abhi-abhi duniya ne dekha ki aisi koshish ka anjaam kya hota hai.

Britain ki majboori bhi wo samajhte hain. Jung se pehle Britain videshi nivesh ki kamai par chalta tha. Ab ulta hoga. Lord Keynes ke andaaze se jung ke ant tak Britain par jame hue sterling karz teen arab pound tak pahunch jayenge. Bharat, Misr aur doosre deshon ka. Isliye Britain apna samrajya chhodna nahi chahta. Usi ke sahaare wo America aur Russia ke barabar khada hona chahta hai.

America ki niti alag hai. Use khule bazaar chahiye, Asia ka tez audyogikaran chahiye. Daya se nahi, apna maal bechne ke liye. Isliye Britain aur America mein takraav tay hai. Soviet Union apni seema par dost aur nirbhar desh chahta hai. Nehru ko dar hai ki kal America ek naya samrajya na ban jaye.

Phir Nehru dono mahashaktiyon ko saath rakhkar dekhte hain. Dono ki kamiyan ulti hain. America mein sirf rajnaitik loktantra ki buraaiyan hain. Russia mein rajnaitik loktantra na hone ki. Par dono mein bahut kuch saajha hai. Raftaar, vigyaan par bharosa, logon ki padhai, aur madhyayug ka bojh nahi. Dono ke paas doosre deshon ke saath purani dushmani ka hisaab bhi kam hai. Nehru maante hain ki America mein kaale logon ka sawal unke loktantra par daag hai. Par wo ye bhi kehte hain ki Russi logon mein naslwaad lagbhag nahi hai.

England ka bojh sabse bhaari hai. Lamba samrajya, aur aise afsar jo doosron ko chidhane mein maahir hain. Nehru ek kissa batate hain. Kuch mahine pehle Bharat sarkar ke ek secretary ne qaid mein band Gandhi ko ek chitthi likhi, jo sochi-samjhi badtameezi thi. Desh ne ise apna apmaan maana. Kyunki Gandhi Bharat ka prateek hain.

Aakhir mein Nehru dhai hazaar saal peeche jaate hain. Athens. Wahan loktantra bhi tha aur samrajya bhi. Athens ne Melos naam ke dweep ko jeeta, mardon ko maar diya, auraton aur bachchon ko ghulam bana diya. Nehru yahan itihaaskar Thucydides aur neta Pericles ki baaton ka zikr karte hain. Aur unka nateeja: azaadi aur ghulami ek saath zyada der nahi chal sakti. Ek doosre ko kha jaati hai. Aaj azaadi baanti nahi ja sakti.

Part 11: Mitti se toota rishta

Ab ek aisa vishay jo pehli nazar mein ajeeb lagta hai. Aabadi.

Jung ne karodon logon ko ghar se ukhaad diya hai. Par Nehru ko isse gehri ek baat dikhti hai. Paschimi Europe mein janm-dar gir rahi hai. France ki aabadi barson se badhna band ho chuki hai, ab ghat rahi hai. England mein 1880 ke dashak se janm-dar lagataar gir rahi hai. Hitler aur Mussolini ne zyada bachche paida karwane ki koshish ki, par asar thodi der raha. Andaaza hai ki Russia ko chhodkar Europe ki aabadi 1955 ke aas-paas apne shikhar par hogi, phir ghategi.

Ulti taraf Bharat, China, Java aur Soviet Union hain, jahan aabadi badh rahi hai. Nehru saaf kehte hain ki Bharat ko zyada log nahi chahiye. Kam log hon to behtar.

Iska matlab? Europe ka duniya par dabdaba ghatega. Technology kuch deshon ki jaagir nahi rahegi.

Par janm-dar kyun gir rahi hai? Nehru maante hain ki kisi ko theek se pata nahi. Shaayad shehri zindagi ka tanaav. Shaayad hamesha ki hod. Aur shaayad mitti se toota rishta. Wo ek aankda dete hain: America mein bhi khet-mazdooron ki janm-dar padhe-likhe professional logon se do guna se zyada hai. Rome ke patan ke waqt bhi yahi hua tha.

Unhe lagta hai ki zameen se, kudrat se kat jaana insaan ke liye bura hai. Yahan wo Kashmir ki baat karte hain, jahan kudrat ka jaadu bina bulaye bhi chhoo leta hai. Aur ek French vidwan Foucher ka zikr karte hain, jinhone Kashmir par likha.

Vigyaan har din nayi cheez bana raha hai. Bhaap ke baad bijli, ab electronics. Kuch log keh rahe hain ki Magnesium-Aluminium ka yug aane wala hai, aur dono dhaatu itni hain ki kisi ko kami nahi hogi. Par Nehru ko ek shak hai. Humein taaqat ki kami nahi. Kami us taaqat ke sahi istemaal ki hai. Vigyaan taaqat deta hai, par ye nahi batata ki use kahan lagayein.

Phir wo ek purane sawal par aate hain, jo Upanishadon mein bhi hai. Jaanne wale ko kaun jaanega? Jo aankh sab dekhti hai, wo khud ko kaise dekhe? Nehru kehte hain ki aaj ka sachcha vaigyanik purane zamaane ke rishi aur darshanik jaisa hai. Yahan wo Einstein ka zikr karte hain, jo kehte the ki insaan ka bhavishya ab uski naitik taaqat par tika hai, aur jo tyaag aur sanyam ki baat karte the. Nehru ko isme Bharat ki purani awaaz sunayi deti hai.

Shaayad hum insaaniyat ke kisi bade daur mein jee rahe hain, aur uski keemat chuka rahe hain. Bade daur hamesha uthal-puthal wale hote hain. Poori sthirta ka matlab hai maut. Zindagi lagataar ban rahi cheez hai.

Bharat ke liye Nehru ek chetavni dete hain. Hum kabhi apni tareef mein doob jaate hain, kabhi khud par taras khaate hain. Dono bekaar hain. Sach ka saamna himmat se karna hoga. Is hisse mein Nehru Confucius, Spinoza, Tagore, Lao Tzu aur Aristotle, sabko yaad karte hain. Sabki baat ka saar ek hai: andar aur bahar ke beech santulan. Kudrat ke saath kudrati, samaaj ke saath insaan. Aur apne andar ke sabse achhe hisse ke saath jeene ki koshish.

Part 12: Kaagaz khatam

Ab chapter ka ant. Aur kitaab ka bhi.

Nehru ko likhte hue lagbhag paanch mahine ho gaye hain. Haath se likhe hazaar panne. Is beech duniya mein bahut kuch hua. Jung jeet ki taraf badh rahi hai. Desh mein bhi bahut kuch hua, jise wo sirf door se dekh sake. Kabhi-kabhi udaasi ki lehar aayi, aur chali gayi.

Aur ab ruknaa hoga. Kyun? Ek bahut seedhi wajah se. Kaagaz khatam ho raha hai. Jo kaagaz badi mushkil se juta tha, wo lagbhag poora bhar chuka hai. Aur naya milna aasaan nahi.

Socho. Duniya ki sabse mashhoor kitaabon mein se ek, aur uska aakhri panna isliye aaya kyunki jail mein kaagaz nahi bacha.

Aakhri pannon mein Nehru Bharat ko ek purani aurat ki tarah dekhte hain, jo lambi neend se jaag rahi hai. Wo badlegi. Badalna hi hoga. Kapde badlenge. Par uska purana jaadu rahega, aur uski samajh use sach aur sundar cheezon ko pakde rehne mein madad karegi.

Wo jung par ek baat kehte hain. Jung ka sabse bada nuksaan maut nahi hai. Maut to sabki aani hai. Sabse bada nuksaan hai nafrat aur jhooth, jo dheere-dheere logon ki aadat ban jaate hain. Bharat mein bhi nafrat hai, wo maante hain. Baar-baar ka apmaan bhoolna aasaan nahi. Par Hindustani der tak gussa nahi paalte.

Phir Emerson ka zikr aata hai. Sau saal pehle Emerson ne Americanon se kaha tha ki Europe ki nakal mat karo, apni zameen se taaqat lo. Nehru ye baat Bharat ke liye uthate hain.

Aur aakhir mein, wo apni peedhi ka hisaab karte hain. Hamara daur mushkil tha. Jaldi hi hum hat jaayenge aur naye log aayenge. Humne apna kaam kaisa kiya? Pata nahi. Aane wale log tay karenge. Kis kasauti par? Wo bhi pata nahi.

Par shikayat koi nahi, kyunki ye raasta humne khud chuna tha. Zindagi ka swaad wahi le paate hain jo uske kinaare par khade hote hain, jinhe maut ka dar nahi chalata. Galtiyan hui hongi. Par hum ochhepan se bache, andar ki sharm aur kaayarta se bache. Nehru ke liye yahi kaafi hai.

Aaj ke hisaab se

Ye hissa Nehru ka nahi, humara hai. Teen baatein.

Pehli, batwara. Nehru ne 1944 mein likha ki shayad Bharat ka kuch batwara zabardasti ho jaye. Teen saal baad, August 1947 mein, wahi hua. Aur Punjab aur Bengal hi bate, jinke aankde wo gin rahe the.

Doosri, do mahashaktiyaan. Jung khatam hone se pehle Nehru ne dekh liya tha ki duniya America aur Soviet Union ke beech bategi. Agle chaalees saal yahi hua. Aur azaad Bharat ne kisi khemme mein na jaane ka raasta chuna.

Teesri, aabadi. Nehru ne kaha tha ki Bharat ko kam log chahiye. Aaj Bharat duniya ka sabse zyada aabadi wala desh hai. Aur Europe ki girti janm-dar, jiski baat wo 1944 mein kar rahe the, aaj wahan ki sabse badi chinta hai.

Aapke liye ek sawal: Nehru ne apni peedhi ke baare mein kaha ki aane wale log tay karenge ki humne kaisa kaam kiya. Aap us peedhi ko kis kasauti par tolenge?

Outro

Yahin "The Discovery of India" khatam hoti hai. Ek qaidi, ek qila, aur paanch mahine ki likhai, jo kaagaz khatam hone par ruki.

June 1945 mein Nehru Ahmadnagar se nikle. Do saal baad wo azaad Bharat ke pehle Pradhan Mantri bane.

Is safar mein saath dene ke liye shukriya. Main thi Asha, aur ye tha "The Discovery of India, Hindi mein".

Namaskar.
